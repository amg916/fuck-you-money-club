## 2. Install an idempotent ledger with evidence holds and atomic posting  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The six reference modules below form one executable Python namespace when their code cells are concatenated in order. They use only SQLite and Python standard-library components. Provision one database per contracting firm on a supported local filesystem; do not place the live journal on a shared network mount. Initialize once, then allow separate worker processes to open independent connections. Every business mutation begins with BEGIN IMMEDIATE, checks its command fingerprint inside that transaction, and commits the journal, state transition, and replay result together. SQLite serializes writers, eliminating read-check-write races within this interface. A crash before commit leaves nothing posted; a crash after commit permits the same command to return its stored result. Reusing a command key with different arguments raises an error rather than accepting a second interpretation. A busy timeout may still expire; retry the identical command after bounded delay, never invent a replacement key. UNIQUE constraints protect source fingerprints, evidence attestations, journal identities, and transition identities. The reference deliberately excludes bank transfers and court submissions: database atomicity cannot make an external service execute exactly once. Those actions require their own provider identifiers and independently reconciled acknowledgments.

Intake accepts an immutable source snapshot and derives its fingerprint from the source locator plus content digest. Scoring combines anomaly, corroboration, and provenance inputs on a transparent hundred-point scale; it ranks work rather than estimating legal success. Store full source material in encrypted object storage and put only digests, opaque identifiers, and verification events in this demonstration database. The evidence function records hashes of signed review artifacts. Its caller must already be authenticated and authorized by the surrounding application; a hash proves consistency, not truth or professional status. Verification requires both source and counsel attestations. The escrow_hold state means documentary release restrictions on a potential award, not permission for this business to hold client money. Release requires award payment evidence, an allocation worksheet, and an accounting review. Ordinary retainer billing can continue during that award hold because its consideration comes from delivered services. Keep statutory filing decisions outside the scoring function. Counsel handles FCA complaints under seal, SEC Form TCR, and IRS Form 211. Store sealed materials in separately restricted counsel-controlled repositories; ordinary customer dashboards receive only authorized operational status and no restricted allegations or docket attachments.

Use integer cents throughout the ledger. An invoice debits receivables and credits service revenue only after the corresponding work is delivered; a collection debits cash and credits receivables without creating revenue again. A credit reverses an uncollected invoice amount, while a paid expense debits operating expense and credits cash. The example forbids collections or credits above the matter's outstanding balance and posts eligible award receipts only after documentary release. It does not implement advance deposits, taxes, payroll liabilities, refunds, or client trust accounting; add their complete balanced-entry paths before using those transaction types. Access to direct database writes must remain restricted because SQL privileges can bypass application invariants. The reconciliation view separately exposes earned fees, received awards, cash receipts, paid expenses, receivable movement, and operating cash movement. QA checks journal balance, minimum posting completeness, foreign keys, and negative matter receivables within one consistent transaction. Reconcile cash postings to imported bank statement identifiers and actual bank balances outside this compact reference. Run restored-backup tests and independent access reviews before production. The technical objective is narrow and testable: repeated authorized requests cannot duplicate postings, and concurrent requests cannot overspend an outstanding receivable.

## 3. Price legal attrition explicitly and keep award upside outside payroll  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use a probability tree to expose attrition without dressing assumptions as observed DOJ rates. For sensitivity testing, begin with one hundred investigative prospects. Assume sixty fail counsel's combined public-disclosure and original-source assessment, leaving forty; assume half of those lack sufficient representative-claim support, leaving twenty potential filings. These are planning assumptions, not published dismissal frequencies. Among filed matters, assume forty percent intervention and sixty percent non-intervention. Apply an additional ten percent government-dismissal assumption within the non-intervened branch, then assume recoveries in fifty percent of intervened matters and twenty percent of surviving non-intervened matters. Conditional recovery probability becomes 0.40 × 0.50 + 0.60 × 0.90 × 0.20 = 30.8 percent per filed matter. Consequently, 69.2 percent of filed matters and 93.84 percent of initial prospects produce no modeled recovery. Expected branch counts may be fractional because this is a sensitivity model, not an actual docket inventory. Replace assumptions with the operator's observed cohorts and dated DOJ statistics where comparable denominators exist. Granston-style dismissal consideration is distinct from non-intervention: declining intervention does not itself dismiss an action. Track dismissal motions, orders, withdrawals, settlements, and judgments separately.

Award percentages require program-specific arithmetic. Under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(d), an ordinary eligible FCA relator generally receives fifteen to twenty-five percent when the government proceeds and twenty-five to thirty percent when it does not, subject to statutory qualifications and reductions. A $1 million qualifying recovery therefore supports illustrative gross relator ranges of $150,000 to $250,000 or $250,000 to $300,000, not an automatic analytics-company entitlement. SEC awards generally range from ten to thirty percent of monetary sanctions collected in qualifying actions exceeding $1 million; CFTC percentages also generally run ten to thirty percent. IRS section 7623(b) generally provides fifteen to thirty percent of collected proceeds when its eligibility and amount thresholds apply, including the individual-taxpayer income condition. Thus fifteen to thirty percent is not a universal cross-program rate. Maintain a separate allocation worksheet showing the statutory recipient, collected award, permissible deductions, other owners' interests, and operator amount actually earned. Where the operator acts as agent, ASC 606 presentation reflects the retained service consideration rather than gross custodial receipts. Have the accountant identify the applicable recognition framework for each award; do not assume every statutory payment is customer-contract revenue.

Plan for FCA investigations that may remain under seal for two to five or more years through extensions, while recognizing that SEC and IRS processes have different confidentiality structures rather than an identical court-seal mechanism. Section 3730(b)(2) establishes the initial FCA seal and government service requirements; counsel manages extensions, permitted disclosures, and court directions. A seal breach can cause severe sanctions or dismissal and destroy economic upside, but automatic dismissal is not the universal rule after State Farm Fire & Casualty Co. v. United States ex rel. Rigsby. Keep deadline alerts free of sealed allegations and restrict contractor access to the minimum authorized material. Model a zero-award year as the ordinary operating budget. The Base business remains viable because $487,500 of delivered retainer work covers $268,125 of annual operating costs and leaves $219,375 owner profit. A delayed complaint, declined intervention, or failed award application therefore changes the contingent register rather than payroll funding. If a firm stops purchasing analytical work, reduce its next service allocation and replace that commercial demand independently of litigation outcomes. Never discount invoices against an anticipated relator share or borrow against a probability tree presented as if it were an enforceable receivable.
