## 1. Sell Defensible Evidence Production, Not a Fraction of Suspected Fraud  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Data-mined whistleblower analytics is a specialist evidence-production business serving plaintiff and relator law firms. The operator acquires permitted datasets, resolves entities, tests specific hypotheses and delivers reproducible findings under a written engagement. A buyer purchases an analytical work product: source extracts, executable transformations, comparator selection, an exception register and a documented explanation of alternative causes. The buyer does not purchase a successful prosecution or an assured whistleblower award. CMS Part B utilization, Part D prescribing, Open Payments transfers, SAM.gov notices and lawfully obtained import manifests supply screening inputs, not verdicts. A provider billing three times its specialty median may have a different referral population, coding mix or reporting period. The service therefore earns its fee by separating those explanations from discrepancies that survive testing. The core play, fca_qui_tam_and_sec_whistleblower_forensic_analytics, can begin with a $7,500 fixed-scope package containing one frozen dataset, three tested hypotheses and a counsel-facing evidence memorandum. If corroboration fails, deliver the supported negative finding, stop the allegation and retain only fees earned under the engagement. Counsel controls legal strategy; the analyst controls reproducibility, source discipline and analytical accuracy.

The legal pathways must remain separate from the first intake interview. False Claims Act work under 31 U.S.C. §§ 3729–3730 concerns false or fraudulent claims involving government money and requires attention to falsity, knowledge, materiality and payment mechanics. Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 9(b) requires particularity for fraud allegations; counsel must determine whether representative claims or other reliable claim-level indicia meet the controlling circuit's pleading standard. Public-data pattern recognition alone does not supply every element. The FCA public-disclosure bar in § 3730(e)(4) also requires analysis of the disclosure source, substantially similar allegations and any original-source exception. SEC eligibility under 15 U.S.C. § 78u-6 instead includes qualifying original information that can arise from independent analysis, subject to regulatory exclusions and submission requirements. IRS awards under 26 U.S.C. § 7623 depend on tax-program rules and collected proceeds, not securities sanctions or FCA damages. An operator may be a paid expert without being an eligible claimant in any program. Document those roles separately, avoid purchasing supposed claims, and never convert the service invoice into a share of counsel's legal fees. Each prospective award needs its own eligibility record.

Use eleven mapped operating plays without pretending that every adjacent evidence lane is a separately supplied catalog identifier. Two are the specified forensic desk and multi_inbox_free_workspace_outbound_clusters acquisition concept; nine chapter-level lanes are Part B utilization, Part D prescribing, Open Payments relationships, procurement representations, import-manifest corroboration, SEC disclosure analysis, IRS factual reconciliation, FOIA corroboration and sealed-matter support. Each lane needs a purchasable unit and a failure fallback. A $2,000 Part B screen or $3,000 Part D review buys a bounded comparison, not a fraud conclusion. A $1,500 relationship map, $4,000 procurement package and $2,500 manifest reconciliation end with documented exceptions when the hypothesis fails. A $5,000 SEC memo and $4,500 IRS reconciliation stop where lawful source access or eligibility evidence ends. FOIA support can cost $1,200 per request package plus approved agency charges; sealed support can cost $2,000 monthly for defined maintenance tasks. These are illustrative service prices, not statutory fee schedules or extra revenue added to the scorecard. The five archetypes below describe delivery specialization. A small team should select one primary archetype and sell adjacent modules only when it can preserve the same evidence standards and turnaround discipline.

## 2. Recognize Operator Revenue Without Capitalizing the Award Story  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The revenue boundary is paid forensic litigation-support retainers from plaintiff or relator firms plus realized net statutory award proceeds actually belonging to the operator. Government recoveries, alleged loss, provider reimbursements, procurement contract value and underlying client GMV are not operator revenue. Under ASC 606, a customer deposit generally remains a contract liability until the promised service is transferred; cash receipt alone does not complete performance. A stand-ready monthly service may be recognized over time when its contract and performance satisfy the applicable criteria. A discrete evidence package may instead be recognized upon delivery or another supported transfer point. Document the performance obligation, acceptance terms, refund rights and treatment of reimbursable costs. Principal-versus-agent assessment determines whether controlled services appear gross or only an arranging fee appears net. Do not apply an invented retained_share multiplier to the entire business. For a $10,000 customer charge containing $2,000 collected solely as an agent for another supplier, the illustrative revenue is $8,000; supplier collections are not extra margin. If the operator controls the complete service as principal, the conclusion can differ. Statutory awards require separate accounting analysis rather than automatic treatment as ordinary ASC 606 customer revenue.

## 4. Build the Evidence Chain and Budget for Dismissal  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build each dossier as a reconstruction that another qualified analyst can repeat without trusting your narrative. Record source URLs, acquisition dates, release identifiers, license conditions, cryptographic hashes and transformation versions. Python hashlib can fingerprint preserved source files; sqlite3 can store normalized observations; decimal can prevent binary rounding drift in monetary calculations. Retain the unmodified source separately from cleaned tables and label derived fields explicitly. Every chart should resolve to a query, population definition and exclusion rule. Counsel should receive a claim-element matrix separating observed facts, reasonable inferences, missing support and contrary evidence. Preserve coding changes, merger effects, duplicate records and plausible innocent explanations instead of filtering them away. Public data does not authorize acquisition of patient records, employee accounts or confidential tax returns. Additional access must come from a lawful, authorized source within the engagement. Before sensitive material enters a cloud service, verify contractual permission, access restrictions and retention settings; privilege is not created merely by copying a lawyer. For high-impact findings, assign a second analyst to reproduce the key result and actively search for counterexamples. The resulting evidence chain is both the commercial deliverable and the team's strongest defense against expensive analytical rework.

FCA complaints go through licensed relator counsel under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(b)(2), with the required seal, government service and disclosure of substantially all material evidence and information. The initial statutory seal period is at least sixty days, and extensions can make the process much longer. Do not serve the defendant or publicize the filing contrary to the governing procedure and court orders. A seal breach creates serious dismissal and award risk, although dismissal is not automatic in every case under State Farm Fire & Casualty Co. v. United States ex rel. Rigsby. Maintain a counsel-owned docket calendar and an access list that includes contractors, backups and external service providers. The analyst must not assume that non-intervention ends the case or validates its merits; counsel decides whether continued litigation is supportable and funded. DOJ dismissal under § 3730(c)(2)(A), including concerns associated with the Granston memorandum, is distinct from non-intervention. The government's dismissal authority and procedural requirements also reflect United States ex rel. Polansky v. Executive Health Resources. SEC Form TCR and IRS Form 211 submissions should likewise be routed through specialist counsel, but their confidentiality arrangements must not be described as the FCA court-seal process.

Model procedural attrition explicitly without manufacturing observed dismissal rates. For an illustrative cohort of ten prospective FCA matters, assume three interventions and seven non-interventions: a 70% non-intervention planning rate. Assume counsel abandons five of the seven after evaluating evidence and funding. If the government seeks and obtains dismissal in one of the two remaining matters, that is a 50% dismissal assumption within that narrow branch, not a measured DOJ or Granston policy rate. The four surviving matters can still produce zero collections. Maintain a separate all-zero outcome in which public-disclosure problems, pleading defects, adverse merits or collection failure eliminate every award. These inputs are sensitivity tests, never sales claims or forecasts supported by nonexistent statistics. Replace them only with dated, consistently defined evidence and preserve the denominator, procedural stage and observation window. In every branch, recurring service obligations remain funded by collected customer fees and available working capital. Establish a delivery reserve, written change-order pricing and a stop-work threshold for overdue invoices. Time to first deal of four to twelve months makes early customer validation essential. Durable operators can lose the award thesis, preserve the factual record and still operate a profitable evidence-services business.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Contract the Evidence Unit
   - Define the analytical question, permitted sources, deliverables, acceptance terms and fee milestones.
   - Separate counsel's litigation decisions from the operator's factual services; record award eligibility as an independent question.
2. Freeze and Reconstruct Sources
   - Preserve source files, calculate hashes and build a versioned transformation register.
   - Document release dates, excluded observations, identifier collisions and every monetary field's meaning before computing an anomaly score.
3. Challenge the Finding
   - Reproduce material results and test the strongest competing explanation.
   - Deliver a fact-and-inference matrix with missing support and contrary evidence; counsel determines whether lawful further corroboration is warranted.
4. Deliver and Reconcile
   - Transfer the approved package securely, record service performance and invoice earned milestones.
   - Update the restricted procedural register separately; recognize no speculative award and retain the all-zero recovery scenario.
