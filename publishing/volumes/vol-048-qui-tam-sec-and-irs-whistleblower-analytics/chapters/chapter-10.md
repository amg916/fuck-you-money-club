Write each source into an immutable landing directory with acquisition time, release date, license reference, schema version, and SHA-256 digest. Never interpret an absent or suppressed CMS cell as zero activity. Separate provider identifiers from names, preserve historical addresses, and attach a confidence score to every proposed join. Partition records by reporting period before calculating peer comparisons so revised releases cannot silently rewrite an earlier dossier. The owner reviews exceptional joins; one contractor maintains ingestion, while another reconstructs source evidence. A failed download produces a stale-source warning and pauses dependent scoring rather than recycling yesterday's results as fresh evidence.

## 2. Automate corroboration and dossiers without manufacturing evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Play five is the FOIA and primary-source corroboration builder. Convert each candidate into a question ledger: alleged mechanism, relevant period, missing records, possible innocent explanation, and the exact record that could resolve the uncertainty. FOIA under 5 U.S.C. § 552 provides access procedures and exemptions, not an immediate production promise or a substitute for discovery. Generate draft requests naming custodians, date ranges, record types, and preferred electronic formats; a person approves scope and submission because the request itself communicates information outside the firm. Track acknowledgment, tracking number, fee correspondence, production batches, and appeal dates without treating the statutory determination period as a delivery deadline. For each produced file, preserve the original, extract text into a separate derivative, and log redactions and missing attachments. Budget $2 of the $12 machine batch for retrieval orchestration and indexing; fees and licensed records are separately authorized expenses inside the engagement budget. If an agency withholds documents, record the asserted exemption and route the decision to counsel. Switch to accessible primary sources, narrow the hypothesis, or close the candidate rather than filling evidentiary holes with an LLM's plausible reconstruction of records it has never seen.

Play six is citation-bound extraction and dossier assembly. A model receives only approved document excerpts, a constrained field schema, and instructions to return quoted spans with page or row locators. Treat embedded document instructions as hostile content: they cannot request secrets, change destinations, invoke tools, or amend the extraction contract. Parse returned JSON with strict size and type checks; validate every quoted span against stored text before it enters the evidence ledger. Python's stdlib handles orchestration, JSON, and hashing; PDF parsing and OCR require separately selected services or local executables rather than pretending they exist in the stdlib. Budget $4 for model extraction, $3 for approved OCR or parsing, and $3 for storage, scoring, and orchestration, including the $2 corroboration allocation already described. Those categories total $12 rather than adding a second retrieval charge. Prepare an evidence packet containing source inventory, chronology, entity map, competing explanations, and unresolved questions. A deterministic template supplies headings and source links when the model fails. No model invents missing invoices, beneficiary details, contract clauses, interviews, or representative claims. Counsel receives an explicitly incomplete packet when decisive documents remain unavailable, with each unsupported proposition removed from the factual narrative.

Play seven is counsel-fit qualification, with an explicit separation between statistical interest and filing sufficiency. The full-time owner checks practice focus, jurisdiction, conflicts routing, available evidence, engagement economics, and willingness to pay for bounded support work. Counsel assesses the FCA public-disclosure bar under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(e)(4), including the statutory channels of disclosure, substantially similar allegations, original-source requirements, and the government's ability to oppose dismissal. Publicly accessible data does not automatically create a viable relator, and neither does it automatically resolve the statutory analysis. Build a Rule 9(b) matrix linking who, what, when, where, and how to actual source passages; representative-claim demands and acceptable alternatives vary by circuit and factual context. Aggregate CMS summaries often cannot establish claim submission, falsity, or knowledge. The model flags missing elements but never declares them satisfied. For four accepted packets from forty candidates, the illustrative $272 fully loaded batch costs $68 per accepted packet; rejection of all forty is a valid outcome, not permission to lower standards. If counsel declines, retain the reason, stop external circulation, and reuse only nonconfidential method lessons rather than shopping an identifiable allegation across a mailing list.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the authorized evidence inputs
   - Create a batch manifest and hash every acquired source.
   - Record acquisition authority, reporting period, source version, and permitted recipients before extraction begins.
2. Generate bounded factual derivatives
   - Run deterministic joins, constrained extraction, and quotation validation.

3. Conduct adversarial human examination
   - Review alternative explanations, disclosure history, and claim-specific gaps.
   - The analyst verifies sources; licensed counsel evaluates filing sufficiency and program eligibility.
4. Release only the authorized packet
   - Approve the named recipient and export a versioned evidence dossier.
   - Log the export digest and decision, reconcile delivery, and preserve a replayable record without placing matter content in general telemetry.

## 3. Fund the service with retainers and isolate contingent recovery  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Do not synchronize protected matter titles into ordinary CRM records, public issue trackers, shared calendars, model telemetry, or marketing analytics. A reminder uses an opaque identifier and points authorized counsel to the protected record. Retain the actual sealing order because restrictions are matter-specific. A seal breach creates serious litigation and recovery risk, but dismissal or share loss is not mechanically automatic in every case. Route SEC Form TCR and IRS Form 211 through counsel under this operating model, with separate program-specific intake and deadline rules. Never transplant the FCA seal process onto those programs. The often-cited 2–5+ year horizon describes possible investigation and recovery delays, not a universal seal duration or an expected payment date. If instructions conflict or access fails, suspend exports and seek counsel's clarification while preserving the audit trail.
