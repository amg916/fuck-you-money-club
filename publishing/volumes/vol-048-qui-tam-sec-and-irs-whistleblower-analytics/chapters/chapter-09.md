## 1. Establish Counsel-Led Eligibility Before Pricing an Evidence Dossier  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a service engagement, not a promise to manufacture a whistleblower claim. The law firm hires the operator to reconcile datasets, preserve source records, test alternative explanations, and document reproducible findings. Counsel decides whether facts support liability under 31 U.S.C. § 3729 and whether a proposed relator can proceed under § 3730. An unusually high CMS payment ratio is a research lead, not proof that a false claim reached the government. Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 9(b) requires particularity for allegations of fraud; circuit approaches differ on when reliable indicia can substitute for representative claim details. Build a claim-level worksheet recording provider identity, service date, billing code, payer, challenged representation, corroborating record, and competing explanation. Leave unsupported propositions out of the deliverable rather than filling gaps with statistical confidence. Record whether each fact came from CMS Part B/D, Open Payments, SAM.gov, import manifests, an authorized witness, or a client-owned record. A $7,500 monthly retainer purchasing 30 analyst hours implies $250 per hour before overhead; its invoice becomes payable for delivered analysis regardless of whether counsel files anything. That separation makes negative findings commercially useful and removes pressure to characterize every anomaly as fraud.

Public-source mining creates a specific eligibility problem under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(e)(4), not a blanket prohibition on public information. Counsel must compare substantially similar allegations or transactions against the statute's enumerated public-disclosure channels and assess the original-source exception and government's opposition authority. Preserve publication dates, acquisition dates, independent knowledge, and any qualifying voluntary disclosures to the government. Also screen the first-to-file restriction in § 3730(b)(5), limitations under § 3731(b), and materiality under Universal Health Services v. Escobar. All FCA complaints in this operating model are filed exclusively through licensed relator counsel. Section 3730(b)(2) requires an initial seal of at least 60 days, service on the government of the complaint and written disclosure of substantially all material evidence, and no service on the defendant until ordered. Extensions can produce long investigations; a 2–5+ year filing-to-payment horizon does not mean every case remains sealed throughout that period. Never place a sealed caption, target name, allegation, or case-specific search query into an enrichment vendor. A seal breach can produce serious sanctions and jeopardize recovery, but dismissal or loss of an award is not an automatic statutory consequence of every breach.

Separate each award program before discussing percentages. FCA shares generally run 15%–25% when the government proceeds and 25%–30% when it does not, subject to statutory qualifications and reductions. SEC awards generally run 10%–30% of monetary sanctions collected in qualifying covered and related actions, with covered actions requiring sanctions exceeding $1 million. IRS § 7623(b) generally provides 15%–30% when its proceeds and taxpayer thresholds are met; discretionary § 7623(a) claims follow a different framework. CFTC awards likewise generally use 10%–30%, not a universal 15%–30% band. Route SEC Form TCR and IRS Form 211 submissions through counsel as a firm operating requirement, without implying counsel is universally mandated by those programs. Anonymous SEC submissions seeking eligibility require attorney representation under the applicable rules. A vendor does not earn a statutory award merely by selling a dossier, and its entity eligibility cannot be assumed across programs. Document the actual claimant, qualifying knowledge, submission timing, assistance duties, and payment recipient. Have counsel assess prior reporting obligations and original-information exclusions before acceptance. If eligibility fails, retain the ordinary paid analytics engagement only where independently valuable; remove contingent upside from that matter's forecast and stop describing the vendor as an entitled whistleblower.

## 2. Contract for Deliverables, Lawful Access, and State-Specific Boundaries  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a master services agreement plus a matter-specific statement of work signed by the retaining law firm. Define the service as forensic data analysis and litigation support, expressly reserving legal opinions, representation, claim selection, and filing decisions to licensed counsel. List included datasets, permitted joins, deliverable formats, analyst hours, acceptance tests, and excluded investigative activity. A defensible acceptance test reproduces five sampled calculations from preserved source files; it does not require counsel to agree that fraud occurred. State the fee trigger as a monthly service period or accepted milestone, with a specified invoice date, payment term, and change-order mechanism. Prohibit percentage compensation tied to attorney fees unless applicable professional rules expressly permit the arrangement; California Rule of Professional Conduct 5.4 illustrates the nonlawyer fee-sharing problem. Contingent litigation-support or expert compensation raises additional state and evidentiary concerns. Do not disguise a referral fee as an analytics success fee. Any separately eligible claimant award requires its own counsel-reviewed treatment and must never be assumed assignable to the vendor. Require the client to identify its contracting entity, authorized instructor, billing contact, and evidence-transfer channel so payment disputes cannot become leverage over testimony, regulatory reporting, or access to a client's own records.

State boundaries follow the actual work, not the contract's chosen label. California Business and Professions Code § 6125 and New York Judiciary Law § 478 restrict unauthorized practice; a vendor should not tell a prospective relator which claims to plead or negotiate representation as though it were counsel. Investigative licensing needs a separate analysis. California Business and Professions Code § 7521 broadly describes private-investigator activities, while Texas Occupations Code Chapter 1702 and Florida Statutes Chapter 493 impose their own structures and exceptions. Desk research is not automatically exempt because its inputs are public, and retention by a lawyer does not create a universal exemption. Before witness contact, surveillance, identity tracing, or evidentiary investigation, obtain a written jurisdiction-and-activity assessment from counsel or use an appropriately licensed subcontractor. Add separate assessments for state false-claims statutes, local filing procedures, and sector restrictions; state remedies do not simply inherit federal eligibility or seal rules. For healthcare matters, distinguish published aggregate CMS information from identifiable patient records. Access to the latter requires a lawful basis and role-specific analysis under HIPAA and applicable state law. Neither a public billing anomaly nor a whistleblower label authorizes downloading an employer's entire clinical database.

Allocate intellectual property and custody explicitly. The client receives its source records, matter-specific work product, and a documented license or ownership interest in paid deliverables; the operator retains preexisting parsers and generalized methods without retaining confidential matter facts for resale. A confidentiality clause should preserve legally protected government reporting and must not require prior notice or permission where that would impede SEC communications under 17 CFR § 240.21F-17. Contracts governing confidential information with employees and covered contractors should include the immunity notice contemplated by 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), while making clear that its conditions do not authorize unrestricted acquisition. Specify security controls, breach notification, subcontractor access, retention, litigation holds, and return or deletion procedures. Counsel-directed work may support privilege or work-product protection, but a legend alone does not establish either. Negotiate a liability cap with explicit treatment of confidentiality breaches, intentional misconduct, and data misuse, and confirm enforceability under selected state law. Dispute clauses should identify forum, escalation, and preservation duties while allowing protected reporting and necessary emergency relief. Narrow anti-circumvention to identified commercial introductions for a defined period; never restrict government cooperation, witness access, a client's counsel choice, or a whistleblower's statutory communications.

## 4. Use Outreach That Sells Analysis Without Soliciting Restricted Evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Aim initial outreach at law firms, using public practice descriptions and firm-level contact details rather than purchased lists of alleged victims or employees. Commercial B2B email remains subject to 15 U.S.C. § 7704: accurate routing and subject information, required advertising identification, a valid physical postal address, a clear opt-out mechanism, and timely suppression are operational requirements. The sender should append its actual business identity and postal address before sending; an example script is not a substitute for those particulars. Use this body verbatim after confirming its factual accuracy: 'I provide reproducible forensic analysis of public CMS, Open Payments, procurement, and trade records for plaintiff-side counsel. The engagement purchases source-linked analysis, including explanations that weaken a theory; it does not purchase a promised claim or recovery. Would a 20-minute discussion of your team's research backlog be useful? Please do not send client identities, sealed allegations, or confidential documents in reply. Reply stop to end these commercial messages.' Use the subject 'Forensic research capacity for counsel.' Honor opt-outs within ten business days and keep the suppression record separate from prospect enrichment. Avoid target-specific accusations, recovery projections, misleading government affiliations, and statements suggesting that counsel must act before an invented deadline.

Discovery should establish authority, scope, and payment before facts migrate across systems. Use this opening: 'Which attorney will supervise the engagement, which jurisdiction governs the intended work, and what decision should the analysis support? Please describe the dataset categories without naming a sealed matter. Are you requesting public-source analytics, review of records your client may lawfully provide, or licensed investigative activity? We price a defined service milestone independently of any filing or award.' Then ask: 'What existing disclosures or proceedings might affect eligibility, what representative claim evidence can counsel authorize us to examine, and what innocent explanation would change your assessment?' Do not ask employees to retrieve records beyond their access rights, bypass controls, or upload another person's credentials. If someone volunteers sensitive material, use this response: 'Please stop transmitting documents here. A licensed attorney should assess your authority to possess and disclose them and provide an appropriate channel. We can discuss our service without receiving those materials.' For phone outreach, assess 47 U.S.C. § 227, FCC rules, state calling restrictions, and applicable recording law before using automation or recording. California Penal Code § 632 and Florida Statutes § 934.03 illustrate why a single nationwide recording script cannot replace jurisdiction-specific analysis.

Close with a written discovery recap that the supervising attorney can correct before access is provisioned. Use this scope confirmation: 'The proposed pilot costs $2,500 for ten analyst hours, a source register, one reproducible anomaly test, and a competing-explanation memorandum. It excludes legal opinions, witness contact, complaint drafting, and submission. Payment purchases the work whether the result supports, weakens, or eliminates the proposed theory. Additional work requires a written scope change.' This is an illustrative pilot quote, not a replacement for the annual scorecard. If asked for an award split, respond: 'Our service fee is independent of attorney compensation and government recovery. Counsel must assess any separately eligible claimant interest; we do not sell eligibility or promise a percentage.' If a prospect asks for a sealed-case example, respond: 'We can demonstrate methods using public or synthetic records, not restricted matter details.' Maintain one outreach register with source provenance, message version, suppression status, and authorized follow-up date. Keep it segregated from the evidence repository. Review sample messages quarterly for unsupported claims and provider-derived personal information. A defensible growth channel produces paying counsel relationships while keeping allegations, claimant identities, and confidential evidence out of prospecting systems entirely.
