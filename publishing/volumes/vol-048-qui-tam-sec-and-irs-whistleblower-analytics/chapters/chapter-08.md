## 1. Build an official-source spine before purchasing enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a source register, not a scraper collection. Give every dataset an owner, published release date, observation period, access route, permitted use, identifier vocabulary, and reproducible retrieval recipe. CMS Medicare Physician and Other Practitioners and Medicare Part D Prescribers support peer comparisons, but their public files are aggregated, delayed, and subject to suppression. They do not supply the claim-level encounters needed to establish individual false submissions. Preserve both service year and publication date so an apparent utilization jump does not become a fabricated contemporaneous signal. Use NPPES for NPI identity resolution, not proof of active licensure or specialty competence. Match HRSA OPAIS registrations using identifiers and effective periods rather than organization names alone. Open Payments adds reported manufacturer relationships; it does not independently establish improper inducement. Build comparisons within specialty, geography, site of service, beneficiary mix where available, and dataset coverage. A physician with 400 observed services against a peer median of 100 has a fourfold screening ratio, not fourfold recoverable damages. Record the denominator, suppressed observations, and plausible clinical explanations beside that ratio before selling any research memo to counsel.

The first eight entries below anchor a sixteen-source directory continued in Section 2. Each access method names a real entry point while avoiding guessed dataset identifiers or fabricated commercial prices. For CMS APIs, obtain the dataset UUID and version from the catalog's API documentation, then store that resolved endpoint in the acquisition manifest. Prefer published bulk files for full-population work; use filtered API requests for incremental validation. Annual CMS utilization releases cannot support daily surveillance merely because a downloader runs nightly. NPPES monthly dissemination files and weekly updates have different jobs: initialize from a full snapshot and reconcile documented changes afterward. Open Payments publication and correction cycles also require version comparisons rather than blind replacement. For SAM.gov, distinguish entity registrations, contract opportunities, and exclusion records; access permissions and API quotas differ. USAspending award transactions are not invoices or proof of payment for particular defective goods. Read source-specific documentation before joining dates, amounts, and recipient identifiers. Where an API fails, retain the last successful snapshot with an explicit age field and switch to the listed official download rather than silently interpreting an empty response as zero activity.

Use a conservative acquisition scheduler built with urllib.request, json, csv, sqlite3, hashlib, and datetime. Keep secrets in environment variables, never in evidence exports. Apply per-host concurrency limits, bounded retries, exponential backoff with jitter, and Retry-After handling; do not evade throttling through rotating accounts or addresses. Identify the client where the publisher requires it. Preserve response status, retrieval time in UTC, content type, request parameters, final URL, and SHA-256 digest alongside the original bytes. A digest proves that a stored object matches its recorded digest, not that the publisher's assertions were true. Separate immutable source objects from normalized tables and analyst annotations. Validate expected columns, identifier lengths, row counts, date ranges, and duplicate keys before releasing a snapshot to analysis. Set a quarantine state for schema drift or truncation, with a human-readable reason and last working release. Replaying a 10,000-row sample through yesterday's and today's parser should produce explainable differences, not unexplained entity churn. Budget staff time for source changes even when access costs nothing; a free portal with brittle exports can cost more than a narrow, well-documented commercial lookup.

## 2. Purchase narrow corroboration instead of indiscriminate enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run enrichment as an evidence waterfall: official identifier lookup, official relationship verification, targeted commercial corroboration, then counsel-directed primary records. SEC EDGAR provides filings and structured company facts; its reporting perimeter is public issuers and other filers, not every private supplier. GLEIF links legal-entity identifiers and reported parent relationships, but an absent LEI does not imply a fictitious business. State corporate registries, county land records, and professional boards remain jurisdiction-specific corroboration sources. Preserve the exact registry record and its effective date rather than copying a search-engine snippet. Commercial tools such as Panjiva and ImportGenius can identify shipment patterns and intermediaries, but manifest coverage varies by transport mode, jurisdiction, confidentiality treatment, and vendor processing. A consignee-name match needs address, timing, and product corroboration before becoming an ownership or origin assertion. Never infer customs underpayment from a shipment estimate alone. Request lawful entry documents, classification support, and duty calculations through counsel when those facts become material. Source licensing also matters operationally: a subscription may permit internal analysis while restricting redistribution of raw records in client exhibits. Resolve that distinction before promising a downloadable evidence bundle.

Set purchase thresholds against research yield rather than marketing coverage. An illustrative $600 monthly enrichment budget allocated to twenty disputed entity matches costs $30 per resolved inquiry if all twenty resolve; if only five resolve, the effective cost becomes $120. Track accepted matches, rejected matches, unresolved matches, analyst minutes, and downstream evidentiary use separately. Do not treat an available email address as a useful litigation fact. SEC acquisition requires an identifying User-Agent and observance of the published fair-access ceiling of ten requests per second; choose a lower local ceiling and honor later publisher changes. CourtListener and RECAP provide useful docket discovery, but coverage depends on contributions and does not replace the official court record. PACER commonly charges $0.10 per page, subject to applicable caps, exceptions, and quarterly waivers; confirm the current fee schedule before bulk retrieval. Sealed material is not available for public enrichment. Do not probe sealed docket endpoints, infer permission from an exposed URL, or send client matter names to commercial search systems without authorization. A documented inability to obtain a primary record is a research limitation, not permission to invent its contents.

FOIA is a corroboration channel, not a claim-level data vending machine. Build narrow requests around agency, record type, office, date interval, contract identifier, and desired electronic format. The statutory twenty-working-day determination framework is not a promise of completed production; consultations, exemptions, litigation, and backlogs can extend the actual timeline. Record the tracking number, acknowledged scope, fee category, fee ceiling, correspondence, and appeal deadline. Expect privacy and law-enforcement exemptions to affect healthcare and investigative records. IRS return information is restricted by 26 U.S.C. § 6103; public tax-exempt organization records do not open private taxpayer accounts. Use IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search to verify status and retrieve available filings, then distinguish reported organizational revenue from the operator's own fees. Where requests stall, narrow the requested record set, seek already published agency material, or suspend the hypothesis pending lawful corroboration. The directory intentionally mixes free official sources, metered court access, and quote-based commercial services. Their roles are complementary, not interchangeable. Require each paid source to identify a specific missing fact, a permitted use, and a cheaper fallback before renewing the subscription.

## 3. Convert anomalies into claim-ready evidence, including the 340B mapped play  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Implement the pipeline as four separately testable layers: acquisition, identity resolution, hypothesis testing, and evidence packaging. SQLite is sufficient for a small desk when indexes cover NPI, UEI, award identifier, NDC, and effective dates. Use decimal.Decimal for currency and explicit units for quantities; a dollar obligation, prescription count, dosage unit, and drug package cannot share an unlabeled amount column. Track source-to-output lineage through deterministic transformation versions and parameter files. Diagnostic tests should detect join multiplication, identifier collisions, stale registrations, unexpected null fields, and unexplained distribution shifts. A join that turns 1,000 prescriptions into 1,300 matched rows requires reconciliation before any margin calculation. Produce an exception ledger with source references, reason codes, reviewer disposition, and correction history. For every shortlisted allegation, construct a claim matrix identifying actor, conduct, date, payment request, alleged falsity, supporting record, and competing explanation. Rule 9(b) requires particularity in alleging fraud; jurisdiction-specific pleading standards determine what representative claims or reliable indicia suffice. Public aggregate outliers cannot substitute for that assessment. The paid product is a reproducible research package with explicit evidence gaps, not a machine-generated accusation dressed as a complaint.

Unpack section_340b_contract_pharmacy_spread_arbitrage as a research and controls subject, not presumed misconduct. HRSA-qualified covered entities include eligible health centers, Ryan White entities, and qualifying hospitals; eligibility depends on the applicable statutory category. The play combines TPA split-billing, specialty fulfillment, and referral capture, but none establishes a lawful patient relationship by itself. The often-cited 25–50% or greater acquisition discount is a screening assumption, not a universal statutory percentage. Actual 340B ceiling prices follow statutory pricing mechanics and are not generally available as a public NDC price feed. Illustratively, $10,000 of collected reimbursement less $6,000 acquisition cost, $250 dispensing expense, $150 administration, and $100 transaction expense leaves $3,500 before other costs and reversals. One hundred eligible paid fills produce $350,000 of illustrative program contribution, not analytics-desk revenue. A separately contracted $150 administrative fee per fill would generate $15,000 for that service provider before its expenses, but does not belong in this volume's retainer scorecard. Flat fees do not automatically resolve anti-kickback concerns, payer restrictions, or referral issues. Counsel must evaluate the actual services, remuneration, and relationships.

Test the 340B hypothesis with dated OPAIS relationships, authorized dispensing transactions, encounter evidence, payer terms, acquisition records, and Medicaid billing treatment. CMS Part D summaries alone cannot identify which fills used 340B inventory or establish duplicate discounts. Separate diversion risk under 42 U.S.C. § 256b(a)(5)(B) from duplicate-discount risk under § 256b(a)(5)(A), and distinguish fee-for-service Medicaid identification from managed-care arrangements. Manufacturer contract-pharmacy restrictions and litigation require current, jurisdiction-specific review. The operational fallback is an eligible entity-owned specialty pharmacy alongside permitted contract pharmacies, not a workaround that changes patient eligibility. Where applicable manufacturer arrangements use 340B ESP, automate only authorized claims submissions, reconcile acknowledgments, and verify whether affected NDC access actually resumes; uploads do not assure unrestricted supply. Enforce EHR encounter checks before replenishment and isolate patient records from public analytics storage. Tele-referrals require documented clinical relationships and lawful referral arrangements, not automated capture for margin alone. If access or eligibility fails, stop affected replenishment, investigate exceptions, and price the engagement as controls analysis rather than projecting recoveries. This preserves a billable service even when the suspected abuse does not survive corroboration.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the acquisition manifest
   - Record source version, observation period, access authority, retrieval parameters, and object digest.
   - Quarantine incomplete files and retain the last validated release without presenting stale information as current.
2. Resolve identities and test joins
   - Match stable identifiers with effective dates, then reconcile every unexpected change in transaction counts.
   - Keep ambiguous candidates separate and require independent review before combining organizations or attributing conduct.
3. Build the claim and counter-explanation matrix
   - Connect each allegation to representative records, payment mechanics, statutory theory, and a plausible alternative explanation.
   - Label unsupported fields as explicit research tasks in narrative notes rather than manufacturing transaction evidence.
4. Deliver through the authorized matter workspace
   - Export only the contracted evidence package, with lineage, access restrictions, and a reproducible calculation appendix.
   - Counsel controls filing decisions and sealed material; remove matter identifiers from outside enrichment queries.

Use an award sensitivity ledger outside the operating budget. FCA relator shares generally span 15–25% when the government proceeds and 25–30% when it does not, subject to statutory qualifications and reductions. SEC awards generally span 10–30% under their qualifying framework; IRS mandatory-program awards generally span 15–30% subject to thresholds and reductions; CFTC generally uses 10–30%. These regimes do not share one universal percentage. An analytics vendor receives no statutory entitlement merely by finding an anomaly or supporting a law firm. Model only the operator's established entitlement, after applicable deductions, and distinguish it from lawyer compensation. FCA investigations can involve 2–5+ years under seal; other award programs also have long timelines but do not use the identical court-seal mechanism. An illustrative $2 million collected recovery times a 20% eligible share equals $400,000 before deductions, not current revenue. A 25% assumed probability of reaching that outcome gives $100,000 of undiscounted sensitivity value, still zero spendable cash before collection. Keep probability-weighted research economics separate from recognized revenue, bank balances, and staffing commitments.

Model legal attrition with explicit branches rather than a universal win rate. Screen 31 U.S.C. § 3730(e)(4) public disclosure and original-source issues, Rule 9(b) pleading sufficiency, intervention, non-intervention, government dismissal, merits outcomes, and collection separately. For an illustrative stress test, assume 70% non-intervention and a 10% dismissal branch within that cohort: 100 candidate matters then yield 70 non-intervened matters and seven modeled dismissals, before other losses. These are deliberately hypothetical rates, not measured DOJ or Granston-era frequencies. Replace them with dated, cohort-defined observations where available; annual DOJ statistics alone may not supply matched outcome denominators. Government dismissal under § 3730(c)(2)(A) is distinct from declining intervention. Zero collection must remain a supported scenario after years of work. FCA complaints go exclusively through licensed relator counsel under § 3730(b)(2); counsel also routes SEC Form TCR and IRS Form 211 submissions. Maintain matter-specific access, sealed-document isolation, and counsel-controlled communications. A seal violation can cause serious sanctions and jeopardize recovery, but automatic forfeiture is not a universal rule. Stop external sharing whenever the permitted disclosure scope is unresolved.
