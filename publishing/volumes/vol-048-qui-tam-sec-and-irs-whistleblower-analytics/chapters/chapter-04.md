## 1. Protect Half the Capital Before Buying the First Subscription  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Treat startup capital as a survival allocation, not an equipment shopping list. The three budgets below preserve exactly 50% as working capital: $5,000 from $10,000, $12,500 from $25,000, and $22,500 from $45,000. Keep that money in a separate business deposit account, reconcile it weekly, and release it only against the thirteen-week cash forecast. The remaining half buys five bounded deliverables: entity and engagement documents, a secured workstation, insurance, narrowly scoped source access, and initial delivery capacity. These are planning envelopes, not vendor quotations; reallocate within the deployment half when a bindable insurance quote or counsel estimate differs. Do not reduce the reserve percentage to accommodate an annual data contract. The $10,000 configuration assumes an existing usable computer, home-office operation, and a founder who performs extraction and analysis personally. Contractors receive task budgets rather than open-ended availability retainers. The $45,000 configuration purchases deeper source corroboration and delivery capacity, not executive furniture or additional management. Separate personal living reserves from business float: owner withdrawals consume cash even when an income statement classifies them outside operating expense. Record every committed annual renewal in the forecast on its actual payment date rather than smoothing it into an imaginary monthly invoice.

Construct three monthly cash envelopes before opening a matter. A $1,500 lean envelope contains $100 for software and storage, $150 for insurance accruals, $200 for bookkeeping and administrative services, $250 for source acquisition, $300 for counsel consultation, and $500 for contractor work. A $4,500 delivery envelope allocates those categories $200, $250, $350, $700, $1,000, and $2,000 respectively. A $9,500 capacity envelope allocates $350, $450, $700, $1,500, $2,000, and $4,500. Actual policies may require upfront premiums, so distinguish the expense accrual from the payment schedule and avoid counting an initial premium twice. These envelopes exclude founder living costs, income taxes, and incremental payroll; they are operating controls rather than complete annual scenario expenses. At the lean burn, the three reserves last approximately 3.33, 8.33, and 15 months without receipts. At $4,500, they last 1.11, 2.78, and five months. Because first deals can take four to twelve months, the smallest budget cannot support even the earliest expected close without additional funding or lower burn. Use fractional paid engagements, phased purchases, or additional owner capital; never replace the missing runway with an estimated relator award. Stop discretionary acquisition when projected unrestricted cash falls below eight weeks of committed payments.

Establish an entity whose contracts describe forensic data analysis and litigation support rather than legal representation or guaranteed recoveries. Obtain an EIN directly from the IRS without paying an intermediary, open an entity-owned bank account, and maintain a separate tax reserve. Have counsel review the operating agreement, confidentiality terms, contractor invention assignments, and applicable restrictions on sharing legal fees. A services entity does not automatically acquire an individual's statutory award rights. Bill law firms for defined analytical deliverables using ordinary fees; do not disguise an impermissible referral payment as a success-based consulting charge. Request professional liability and cyber coverage that expressly addresses litigation support, sensitive records, subcontractors, and incident response. Compare exclusions for intentional acts, contractual liability, regulatory investigations, and prior-known circumstances before binding. Maintain vendor payment verification through a known telephone number, not a changed bank account in an email thread. Require two-person verification for material transfers even when the second person is an external bookkeeper. Keep client funds out of operating accounts; if an engagement requires custodial handling, route that arrangement through the appropriate licensed professional. The operating objective is simple: every dollar has an owner, purpose, authorization record, and cash date before it leaves the reserve.

## 2. Assemble Twelve Replaceable Layers Around Reproducible Evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the first production pipeline on Python, DuckDB, and immutable source files. CMS Part B and Part D aggregates identify unusual utilization, prescribing, and reimbursement patterns; Open Payments contributes reported manufacturer relationships; SAM.gov contributes entity and procurement context. None independently proves a false claim, knowledge, or materiality. Store a retrieval manifest containing dataset release, original URL, retrieval time, license terms, byte count, SHA-256 digest, and transformation commit. Preserve raw downloads unchanged and write normalized outputs to new versioned locations. Resolve entities with NPI, UEI, addresses, and documented name aliases rather than names alone. Record suppressed cells, lagged publication periods, and population differences before calculating outlier scores. Use DuckDB locally until concurrent workloads justify PostgreSQL; a server is not a prerequisite for credible evidence. Keep public-source analytics separate from privileged or sealed matter storage so a developer cannot accidentally publish a confidential attachment with a harmless-looking benchmark. A GitHub repository contains code, synthetic fixtures, and schemas only. Configure automated secret scanning where available, but do not mistake scanning for permission to upload client material. Every analytical result should reproduce from its source manifest and pinned environment without relying on an analyst's memory.

The twelve-layer matrix gives exact planning tiers and usage rates, not a claim that every supplier will preserve today's terms. Verify regional availability, checkout pricing, taxes, contract duration, and supported security controls before purchase. Microsoft 365 Business Basic is budgeted at $6 per user monthly on an annual commitment; two users therefore consume $12 monthly equivalent, with the payment schedule separately recorded. Bitwarden Teams is budgeted at $4 per user monthly when billed annually, adding $8 for two users. For Amazon S3 Standard in US East, budget $0.023 per GB-month for the first storage tier: 200 GB costs $4.60 before requests, retrieval traffic, replication, and tax. One customer-managed AWS KMS key adds $1 monthly, with eligible symmetric requests budgeted at $0.03 per 10,000 before allowances. The resulting $25.60 monthly subtotal is only a software-and-storage floor, not total operating cost. The remaining $74.40 within the lean $100 envelope absorbs modest usage, domain costs, and recovery media accruals. Requote rather than silently exceeding the envelope. Free software still consumes patching, configuration, monitoring, and recovery labor; assign that work explicitly to the owner.

Design evidence custody around distinct public, confidential, privileged, and sealed classifications, with counsel deciding the last two. Encryption does not make a storage location suitable for a court-restricted matter. Before uploading, document authorized users, permissible jurisdiction, retention instructions, subcontractor access, and the applicable order. Use individual identities, multifactor authentication, device encryption, automatic locking, and time-bounded access. Microsoft 365 Business Basic does not supply every advanced conditional-access or endpoint-management capability; upgrade deliberately when a matter requires controls outside the selected tier. Disable public links and keep subject identifiers out of task titles and email subjects. Back up approved evidence repositories with restic using a separately protected recovery key. Test restoration to an isolated device, validate source digests, and record elapsed time. S3 versioning is useful but does not replace a separately administered backup; an attacker with broad permissions can destroy multiple versions. Apply immutable retention only after reconciling counsel's hold instructions with deletion and access obligations. A confidentiality footer does not create privilege, and copying counsel does not automatically protect an ordinary business analysis. Budget for these distinctions through restricted workflows and documented ownership rather than an expensive all-purpose platform.

The monthly infrastructure envelopes are subsets of these expense totals, not substitutes for delivery costs, compensation, and other scenario expenses. Where the operator is an agent rather than principal, apply the ASC 606 principal-agent assessment to service revenue. The supplied scorecard contains no numerical retained_share factor, so do not invent one or treat client recoveries as operator billings.

Use a zero-award operating forecast as the default treasury view. A separate upside schedule can track two-to-five-plus-year recoveries, but FCA court seals do not describe every SEC or IRS proceeding. The statutory recipient, eligibility rules, deductions, and actual collection determine what belongs in operator revenue; a filed tip or complaint creates neither cash nor an automatic receivable. Under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(d), qualifying FCA shares generally run 15%–25% with government intervention and 25%–30% without, subject to statutory qualifications and reductions. SEC and CFTC qualifying awards generally range from 10%–30%; qualifying IRS awards under 26 U.S.C. § 7623(b) generally range from 15%–30%. Do not apply a universal 15%–30% multiplier across all programs. A hypothetical $2 million FCA recovery at a 20% recipient share produces $400,000 before applicable reductions, fees, and other adjustments, not $2 million of operator revenue. If the analytics firm has no lawful entitlement to that award, its award revenue is zero regardless of its contribution to the dossier. Counsel should document ownership and permissible arrangements before any upside appears in an internal model. Keep speculative proceeds outside borrowing capacity, contractor commitments, and minimum cash coverage calculations.

Model legal attrition before ranking anomaly leads. In an explicitly illustrative cohort of 100 leads, reject 40 after counsel's public-disclosure assessment and another 30 after representative-claim evidence review, leaving 30 for further development. Section 3730(e)(4) requires analysis of the disclosure source, substantially similar allegations, and any applicable original-source exception; a public dataset alone does not resolve that inquiry. Rule 9(b) requires particularity, with controlling circuit standards determining whether identified claims or reliable indicia suffice. Stress the remaining cohort with a hypothetical 70% DOJ non-intervention rate: 21 non-intervened and nine intervened matters. Apply a separate hypothetical 20% government-dismissal stress to that 30-matter cohort, producing six expected dismissals, not six observed cases. These assumptions are sensitivity inputs, not reported DOJ or Granston-era rates; dismissal powers predate that memorandum, and non-intervention itself is not dismissal. Track observed rates by closed decision cohort, decision date, intervention status, and motion denominator rather than copying a headline percentage. Government dismissal under § 3730(c)(2)(A) involves procedural protections and the intervention framework addressed in Polansky. Finally run complete zero recovery across every surviving matter. If that branch cannot pay committed expenses from service collections and reserves, reduce spending before accepting another investigation.

## 4. Test the Customs Edge and Complete the Pre-Flight Evidence Chain  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

For customs_first_sale_rule_valuation_arbitrage, distinguish lawful valuation design from evidence of an actionable false declaration. Under 19 CFR § 152.103 and the Nissho Iwai framework, an eligible first sale requires substantiation of a bona fide sale, export destination, and the applicable transaction-value conditions; an inexpensive factory invoice is insufficient. At 100,000 units, an $18 middleman invoice yields $1.8 million of declared value, while a qualifying $8 factory transaction yields $800,000 before required additions. At a uniform illustrative 25% ad valorem duty, the amounts are $450,000 and $200,000, a $250,000 difference or 55.56%. That simple endpoint is slightly above the mapped 35%–55% annual savings range; adjustments and partial eligibility must be modeled rather than rounding away the difference. If only 80% of those units qualify, mixed duty becomes $250,000 and savings become $200,000, or 44.44%. Do not project those percentages indiscriminately across $10 million–$250 million import portfolios. Tariff classification, origin, additional duties, assists, royalties, freight treatment, and related-party conditions change the result. Importer duty savings are client economics, not analytics revenue. A separately scoped $6,000 invoice-reconciliation engagement produces $6,000 of service consideration as the contracted work is performed, regardless of the importer saving $200,000.

Build the customs proof packet from purchase orders, payment records, factory invoices, middleman invoices, shipping documents, entry summaries, and the applicable valuation adjustments. Public manifests can support shipment linkage but rarely establish the full price, payment, or arm's-length evidence required for first-sale treatment. Preserve the difference between a missing public record and a contradictory primary record. The mapped fallback is an encrypted, confidential customs-broker escrow vault that protects the middleman's margin from routine buyer access while allowing CBP auditors full authorized access. Document retention, release authority, audit response timing, and chain of custody; the importer remains responsible for required substantiation and reasonable care. Obtain a formal customs-counsel opinion addressing the actual transaction chain, not a template referring only to the rule's name. If adequate evidence cannot be produced, abandon first-sale treatment and use the otherwise supportable valuation method. For whistleblower analytics, counsel must separately evaluate whether conduct supports an FCA theory involving a government payment or obligation; a lawful duty reduction is not fraud. Never promise confidentiality arrangements that obstruct an audit or conceal records the importer must obtain. Quote document extraction separately from legal analysis, and prohibit the analyst from signing a customs opinion or directing unsupported entry changes.

Before launch, run one complete matter simulation using synthetic records and one authorized public-source sample. Demonstrate retrieval, digest verification, transformation, entity matching, exception annotation, counsel export, access revocation, and restoration. Require a second person to reproduce a claimed anomaly without verbal instructions. The evidence packet should identify the alleged conduct, relevant period, responsible entities, source limitations, and the specific missing facts needed for legal assessment. For FCA matters, licensed relator counsel files the complaint under the seal procedures of 31 U.S.C. § 3730(b)(2), including the initial statutory period and any extensions ordered by the court. Calendar court-specific instructions and prohibit public case references, sales anecdotes, or publication of sealed allegations. Seal violations can bring serious consequences, including dismissal in appropriate circumstances; do not represent loss of a share as automatic in every case. Route SEC Form TCR and IRS Form 211 submissions through counsel under this operating design, while respecting their distinct procedures. Finally, collect a signed services agreement, confirm insurer scope, verify the receiving account independently, and show a thirteen-week forecast with every statutory award set to zero. Launch a bounded paid pilot only when these artifacts are complete and named owners have accepted ongoing monitoring responsibilities.
