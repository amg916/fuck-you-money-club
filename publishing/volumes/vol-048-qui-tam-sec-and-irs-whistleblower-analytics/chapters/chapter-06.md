## 1. Mine eleven sourcing lanes, but sell an evidence workflow rather than an accusation  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build two separate queues: a signal queue containing reproducible anomalies, and a buyer queue containing firms with demonstrated demand for forensic capacity. Use eleven mapped sourcing plays: CMS Part B cohort analysis; CMS Part D prescribing analysis; Open Payments relationship mapping; federal procurement performance analysis; state and municipal procurement analysis; import-manifest reconciliation; SEC disclosure reconciliation; IRS referral preparation; FOIA corroboration services; relator-counsel demand mining; and government_rfp_subcontractor_brokerage. These are working acquisition lanes, not eleven independent promises of recoveries. Start with two adjacent lanes, such as Part B and Open Payments, so the same entity resolution and specialty taxonomy support multiple assignments. Download CMS public-use files with their documentation, preserve release dates, and distinguish provider identifiers from corporate parents. Compare like specialties, places of service, geography, and patient mix before ranking billing intensity. A high percentile is a screening result, not proof of a false claim. Suppression, beneficiary complexity, ownership changes, and coding revisions can create false positives. Sell a fixed-scope cohort validation and source-provenance package that counsel can evaluate without receiving patient records or any sealed material. Record negative findings because eliminating weak leads is itself a billable deliverable when the engagement specifies it.

For federal procurement, join SAM.gov notices and amendments to USAspending award records, then verify performance allegations against the underlying contract, modification history, and acceptance documents. A contract ceiling is neither actual expenditure nor operator revenue. State and municipal boards reveal rebids, repeated extensions, specialized translation needs, courier routes, and understaffed IT projects; each may support either analytics demand or a separate service opportunity. Import manifests require special restraint: commercial access terms, confidential treatment, incomplete consignee information, and freight-forwarder identities limit what can be concluded. Reconcile a shipment hypothesis with authorized invoices and customs records before estimating duty exposure. SEC lanes begin with EDGAR filings and dated disclosure changes; IRS lanes begin with a concrete tax issue and records lawfully available to the prospective source. Neither lane justifies soliciting stolen files. FOIA requests should target specific record series, custodians, and date ranges, with an explicit budget for fees and delay. An illustrative $2,500 source-validation sprint capped at twenty delivery hours yields $125 billed per delivery hour before overhead. If corroboration fails, deliver an auditable rejection memorandum instead of stretching a pattern into an allegation. Counsel still receives a usable decision and a reusable research trail.

The required government_rfp_subcontractor_brokerage play has a different economic engine and must remain separate from whistleblower award assumptions. Scan SAM.gov, state portals, and municipal bid boards for janitorial, grounds, courier, translation, and IT staffing solicitations with thin documented competition. Automated extraction should capture insurance, wage requirements, site visits, bonding, evaluation criteria, and subcontracting restrictions; a human verifies the solicitation and every amendment. On a $100,000 service contract, a $75,000 subcontractor quote leaves a 25% management spread before supervision, insurance, rework, and financing costs. It is not a 25% net margin. A 15–30% spread means fulfillment consumes 85–70% of the customer price before those additional costs. Under covered small-business service set-asides, 13 CFR § 125.6 generally limits payments to subcontractors that are not similarly situated entities to 50% of the relevant government payment, subject to applicable exclusions and contract specifics. Do not force the illustrative $75,000 arrangement into an incompatible award. The fallback is an unrestricted commercial or municipal opportunity, with its own rules checked, or qualified similarly situated teaming. Resolve conflicts before investigating an entity connected to a fulfillment client. This optional brokerage lane contributes no revenue to the chapter's forensic scorecard reconciliation.

## 2. Qualify counsel demand and separate interesting anomalies from viable assignments  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the buyer list from firm practice pages, publicly unsealed docket entries, published opinions, attorney conference appearances, and analyst hiring notices. CourtListener helps discover matters; confirm important procedural facts in the official docket and never attempt to uncover sealed identities. Select firms that repeatedly handle the same subject matter as the proposed dataset, rather than every firm mentioning whistleblowers. A recent unsealed healthcare matter, a procurement practice expansion, or a posting for a damages analyst is a demand trigger, not evidence of available budget. Maintain separate fields for public source, date observed, practice fit, named business contact, and outreach status. Use a one-hundred-point qualification score: twenty-five points for domain fit, twenty for lawful evidence access, twenty for a concrete paid scope, fifteen for budget and decision authority, ten for delivery timing, and ten for security readiness. Advance scores of seventy-five or more, nurture scores from fifty-five through seventy-four, and archive lower scores. Hard exclusions override the number: requested seal disclosure, unauthorized access, witness pressure, fabricated claims, or payment conditioned on improper legal-fee sharing. A small firm with an immediate bounded research need may outrank a prestigious firm without an identified buyer.

Evidence qualification belongs beside commercial qualification, not after the sales call. Under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(e)(4), substantially similar public disclosures can require dismissal unless the government opposes dismissal or an applicable exception, including original-source status, applies. Counsel must evaluate the disclosure categories, timing, and the prospective relator's independent knowledge; a public dataset anomaly alone does not establish eligibility. Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 9(b) requires fraud allegations to be pleaded with particularity, and circuit standards differ concerning representative claims and reliable indicia. Build a representative-claim worksheet where available: actor, date, service or contract obligation, claim identifier, amount, alleged falsity, payment connection, and supporting source. Do not invent missing claim details from aggregate CMS totals. Record whether the client can lawfully supply supporting documents and whether counsel considers the available specificity adequate. For each lead, maintain an alternative-explanation column covering coding changes, contract amendments, statistical suppression, and entity-resolution errors. Charge for testing those explanations. The fallback for a weak case hypothesis is a paid methodology or data-quality assignment only if the firm independently values that work; otherwise close the lead. A failed theory should not become a recurring invoice without a useful contracted purpose.

Budget statutory upside as a separate, long-duration scenario tree. FCA investigations may extend 2–5+ years under seal, although the statutory initial seal period is at least sixty days. Under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(b)(2), counsel files the complaint under seal and follows service restrictions and subsequent court orders. Non-intervention does not itself end a case: a relator may continue under the governing provisions. Government dismissal under § 3730(c)(2)(A), associated with the Granston policy and later case law, is a separate branch. Track observed rates only with dated, comparable cohorts and explicit denominators. For sensitivity testing, assume seventy of one hundred hypothetical matters receive non-intervention, then seven of those seventy face government dismissal: 70% and 10% conditional rates, not published DOJ statistics. Apply independent zero-recovery outcomes to failed merits, collection problems, eligibility defects, and unsuccessful continued litigation without double-counting branches. For operating cash planning, set every uncollected award to zero. Seal breaches create severe litigation and eligibility risks, but their consequences are case-specific rather than an automatic universal forfeiture rule. SEC Form TCR and IRS Form 211 submissions also go through counsel in this workflow, with program-specific eligibility and timing checks before any filing.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four checks before accepting sensitive material or a paid mandate**
> 
> Complete these checks with the retaining firm before moving from public demonstrations to matter-specific work.
> - Confirm source ownership, access permission, applicable restrictions, and whether any court order limits possession, use, service, or disclosure.
> - Have counsel assess § 3730(e)(4), Rule 9(b) particularity, prospective relator eligibility, and whether representative-claim detail can be supported.
> - Document the client, economic buyer, deliverables, payment terms, conflicts, and absence of unauthorized referral compensation or legal-fee splitting.
> - Separate paid service revenue from contingent awards, restrict matter access, and budget zero collections from every pending statutory filing.

## 3. Run a restrained multichannel cadence that converts research into paid mandates  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use a six-touch, twenty-one-day sequence aimed at a named attorney or litigation operations contact. On day one, send a short email linking a public methodology note to the firm's demonstrated practice and offer a fifteen-minute scoping conversation. On day four, make one manual business call or leave a concise voicemail. On day seven, send a LinkedIn message only where platform rules and the contact's preferences permit it. On day eleven, share one relevant public-data limitation the firm may encounter. On day sixteen, offer a bounded paid validation sprint with a sample deliverable index. On day twenty-one, close the loop and stop unless invited to continue. Do not attach accusations, name prospective relators, or describe sealed matters. Commercial email must satisfy applicable CAN-SPAM requirements under 15 U.S.C. § 7704, including accurate headers, an appropriate postal address, and a working opt-out honored within the statutory period. Avoid automated calling or texting without the required permissions. Inbound publishing should show reproducible methods, provenance standards, and anonymized data-quality lessons, not target lists. A focused webinar for healthcare litigators can generate better questions than broad claims about discovering fraud. Track each channel separately to learn where actual paid buyers originate.

Treat conversion benchmarks as planning hypotheses until actual cohorts mature. An illustrative first campaign begins with three hundred raw firm records, resolves duplicates and practice fit to one hundred twenty qualified accounts, and reaches one hundred named contacts. Twenty positive responses produce twelve discovery calls, eight scoped proposals, four paid pilots, and three signed recurring mandates. The stage rates are 40%, 83.3%, 20%, 60%, 66.7%, 50%, and 75%, respectively; the end-to-end raw-record conversion is 1%. These are testable targets, not market averages. Define a positive response as interest in discussing a relevant paid assignment, excluding courtesy replies and unsubscribes. A win requires a signed scope and initial payment, not merely a favorable call. The four pilots and three resulting mandates represent four customer relationships if mandates upgrade existing pilots; do not report seven distinct clients. Repeating an equally productive cohort three times would yield nine recurring clients, but capacity and market saturation can change the result. Use months one and two for methodology and list validation, months three through six for pilots, and months seven through twelve for renewals and referrals. First paid work may arrive within the specified 4–12-month window, while award collection remains outside that acquisition promise.

Handle objections by reducing uncertainty, not by predicting recoveries. When counsel says, 'We already have analysts,' offer a bounded overflow task using their preferred output schema and a measurable turnaround. When they say, 'Public data cannot make our case,' agree that screening is not pleading support and propose a paid source-gap assessment with an explicit stop condition. When they request contingency-only work, explain that the operating product is forensic delivery purchased on ordinary service terms; counsel must separately assess any lawful award interest, eligibility, professional-conduct restrictions, and conflicts. When price is challenged, reduce cohort size or source count rather than quietly removing provenance checks. A $2,500 pilot with twenty delivery hours costs $1,200 in illustrative labor at $60 per hour plus $300 in data and quality assurance, leaving $1,000 contribution before acquisition costs and general overhead. Fifty-percent upfront billing yields $1,250 cash, not the entire project margin. Define acceptance around reproducibility, completeness against the scope, and timely delivery rather than a favorable case conclusion. Ask for a recurring mandate only after the firm identifies repeated work worth purchasing. With permission, request an introduction that describes service quality without exposing matter facts or implying a paid referral arrangement.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Assemble a traceable prospect cohort
   - Research three hundred public firm records and select one hundred twenty domain-qualified accounts.
   - Preserve the trigger source, observation date, practice fit, business contact, and exclusions for each account.
2. Run the six-touch outreach sequence
   - Contact one hundred relevant people using restrained email, manual calls, and permitted professional messages.
   - Keep client facts out of outreach, record opt-outs immediately, and stop after the twenty-one-day sequence.
3. Sell and deliver a bounded pilot
   - Propose a $2,500 validation sprint with twenty delivery hours and explicit acceptance criteria.
   - Collect the agreed initial payment and deliver source manifests, reproducible calculations, limitations, and a decision memorandum.
4. Convert demonstrated utility into recurring scope
   - Offer a retainer only when counsel identifies repeated research worth purchasing.
   - Track customer upgrades separately from new clients and renew against accepted work rather than predicted recoveries.

## 4. Reconcile cash economics, capacity, and award risk before expanding acquisition  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Reconcile every scenario to Formula v2.0 without treating government recoveries, client damages, procurement value, or alleged tax underpayments as operator revenue. The Conservative case is $95,000 annual operator revenue, $64,600 operating costs, and $30,400 owner net profit at 32%, supported by one full-time operator and two contractors. An illustrative service-only bridge is five clients paying $1,500 monthly for twelve months, plus $5,000 of separately earned project work. The Base case is $487,500 revenue, $268,125 costs, and $219,375 owner profit at 45.0%, with one full-time operator and two contractors. Ten clients paying $3,750 monthly generate $450,000; another $37,500 of completed project work reaches the canonical total. The Scaled case is $880,000 revenue, $369,600 costs, and $510,400 owner profit at 58%, with two full-time staff and three contractors. Sixteen clients paying $4,250 monthly generate $816,000; $64,000 of additional earned projects completes the bridge. These mixes illustrate annualized operating states, not a claim that every client signs on January first. A midyear start produces fewer billed months. Keep optional procurement brokerage outside these bridges and maintain a separate ledger for any lawful, realized net statutory award interest.

Protect margin by specifying workloads before selling subscriptions. For the Base bridge, cap each $3,750 monthly mandate at twelve scheduled delivery hours plus defined automated monitoring, with separately priced expansions. Ten clients consume one hundred twenty scheduled hours monthly before quality assurance, acquisition, administration, and project work. Two contractors are scheduled resources, not unlimited capacity; document their availability, specialties, and access permissions. For the Scaled bridge, sixteen mandates at eight scheduled hours consume one hundred twenty-eight hours monthly, leaving the larger team room for deeper project work and control functions. Higher pricing must reflect demonstrated monitoring value and standardized outputs, not unexplained hourly inflation. Treat these caps as illustrative package designs, then measure actual delivery time and renewal behavior. Require written scope changes for new datasets or expanded entity populations. Publish the startup capital envelope of $10,000–$45,000 and the monthly operating overhead range of $1,500–$9,500, but do not mistake that overhead range for total scenario expense. Canonical annual costs must also absorb applicable labor, delivery, acquisition, and other operating costs. Manage runway from collected service cash, staged purchases, and receivable aging. An invoice awaiting payment cannot fund payroll merely because a pending matter has a large alleged recovery.

Statutory awards require program-specific arithmetic. FCA relator shares generally range from 15–25% when the government proceeds and 25–30% when it does not, subject to statutory qualifications and exceptions. SEC awards generally range from 10–30% of qualifying monetary sanctions collected when statutory conditions, including the covered-action threshold, are met. IRS § 7623(b) generally provides 15–30% of collected proceeds subject to its eligibility rules, including the amount-in-dispute threshold and the additional gross-income condition for individual taxpayers. Do not advertise a universal 15–30% rate across these programs. For illustration, a qualifying $10 million FCA recovery at a 20% share yields $2 million to the relator before relevant deductions, not automatically to the analytics company. If the operator has no lawful entitlement, operator award revenue is zero. If an entitlement exists, model only the operator's documented retained share after applicable allocations; never gross up another party's recovery. Apply ASC 606 principal-versus-agent analysis to service billings where relevant and obtain appropriate accounting treatment for award interests rather than assuming identical recognition rules. Release no uncollected award into operating cash projections. Expand acquisition only when paid delivery, repeat purchases, receivable collection, and documented capacity jointly support the next cohort.
