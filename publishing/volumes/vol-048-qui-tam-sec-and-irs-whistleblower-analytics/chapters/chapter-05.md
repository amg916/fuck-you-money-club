## 1. Kill defective evidence before it becomes a defective complaint  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The first trap is treating an outlier as a false claim. CMS Part B utilization, Part D prescribing, Open Payments transfers, and SAM.gov awards describe different units, reporting periods, and legal relationships. A high reimbursement total may reflect specialty mix, a referral center, or reassigned billing rather than fabricated services. Build cohorts by specialty, geography, service code, and reporting year before ranking providers. Preserve downloaded bytes, release notes, retrieval timestamps, and SHA-256 digests with Python hashlib; never overwrite an earlier release with a corrected file. Require a second analyst to reproduce every material total from the frozen source. An illustrative investigation trigger is a peer-adjusted result above the ninety-ninth percentile with at least thirty comparable observations, but that trigger supports research, not an allegation. Stop dossier delivery when a material identifier join lacks documented corroboration or a suppressed cell has been treated as zero. Track reproducibility as accepted calculations divided by tested calculations, targeting 100% for cited exhibits. The operator sells a defensible analytical process; counsel decides whether the facts support falsity, knowledge, materiality, and a federal payment nexus. A clean chart without those elements is an expensive visualization, not litigation inventory.

The second trap is assuming public-data synthesis automatically creates an eligible relator. Under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(e)(4), substantially the same allegations or transactions disclosed through specified public channels can expose an FCA action to dismissal unless an exception applies or the Government opposes dismissal. The original-source analysis is substantive: timestamped downloading alone does not establish independent knowledge that materially adds to disclosed allegations. FOIA production is not a magic originality certificate. Maintain a disclosure chronology separating public records, independently obtained observations, voluntary government disclosures, and the proposed complaint date. Counsel should also evaluate first-to-file exposure under § 3730(b)(5), recognizing that sealed competing cases may prevent definitive clearance. The third trap is pleading aggregate suspicion without the circumstances required by Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 9(b). Build a representative-claim matrix with claimant, date, payment program, alleged false statement, supporting record, and missing factual links. Circuit standards differ; representative claim examples are not a universal mechanical prerequisite. Measure claim-link completeness against counsel's jurisdiction-specific checklist rather than inventing missing invoices. If public records cannot support the required inference, stop the complaint workstream and offer a bounded research engagement instead of manufacturing certainty.

The fourth trap is treating a seal as an ordinary confidentiality label. FCA complaints must be filed through licensed relator counsel under the statutory seal, served with the required disclosure on the Government, and withheld from the defendant until the court orders otherwise. The initial statutory period is sixty days; extensions can produce investigations lasting years. Keep matter names out of shared sales systems, invoices visible to unrelated customers, public repositories, and external AI prompts. Use counsel-controlled storage, role-based access, encrypted transfer, and a named recipient list for every export. One unauthorized external disclosure triggers an immediate distribution stop, evidence preservation, and counsel-led response. A seal breach can cause sanctions and imperil participation, but State Farm v. Rigsby rejects automatic dismissal for every violation; do not promise either inevitable forfeiture or automatic forgiveness. The fifth trap is acquisition misconduct: borrowed credentials, unauthorized portal access, restricted patient exports, and fabricated corroboration can overwhelm an otherwise useful analysis. Record acquisition authority separately from factual relevance. A record's usefulness does not authorize obtaining it. Quarantine questionable material without further circulation, preserve access logs, and let counsel determine lawful handling and any required remedial steps before analysis resumes.

## 2. Reconcile cash without borrowing against a sealed hypothesis  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The sixth trap is booking alleged damages, Government recoveries, or projected awards as operator revenue. Formula v2.0 fixes annual gross operator revenue at $95,000, $487,500, and $880,000; corresponding cost ceilings are $64,600, $268,125, and $369,600. Subtraction produces $30,400, $219,375, and $510,400 of annual owner net profit at 32%, 45.0%, and 58% margins. A transparent illustrative allocation uses no award receipts: Conservative has five $1,500 monthly retainers plus $5,000 of completed projects; Base has eight $5,000 monthly retainers plus $7,500 of completed projects; Scaled has ten $7,000 monthly retainers plus $40,000 of completed projects. These allocations explain the supplied totals without claiming the scorecard prescribed customer counts. Each assumes twelve paid months and earned service delivery. An advance retainer can create a contract liability until performance occurs. Under ASC 606, principal-versus-agent analysis determines gross or net presentation for pass-through services. Never include client claim value, merchant turnover, or federal contract value in this revenue line. Maintain separate ledgers for earned retainers, collected cash, refundable advances, reimbursable costs, and realized award proceeds; reconcile them monthly before describing the business as profitable. Revenue visibility and available cash are related measurements, not interchangeable balances.

The seventh trap is financing payroll with contingent percentages. FCA shares generally run 15%–25% when the Government proceeds and 25%–30% when it does not, subject to statutory adjustments and exclusions. SEC and CFTC programs generally use 10%–30% ranges for qualifying awards; IRS § 7623(b) generally uses 15%–30%, with eligibility thresholds and reduction rules. These are distinct programs, not one universal 15%–30% entitlement. An analytics vendor has no automatic right to a relator's award or counsel's legal fee. State professional-conduct rules, including restrictions on sharing legal fees with nonlawyers, must shape engagement design. Price ordinary research as documented services rather than assuming a percentage arrangement is permissible. For sensitivity analysis only, a $1,000,000 recovery multiplied by a 20% eligible share equals a $200,000 award before applicable deductions. If counsel's valid fee agreement absorbs 40% and reimbursable case costs equal $20,000, the illustrative retained amount is $100,000 before taxes. That amount belongs only to the legally entitled recipient. It contributes zero to this operator's budget unless entitlement, measurement, and receipt are established. An unfavorable judgment, dismissal, ineligibility determination, or failed collection instead produces zero award revenue after years of work.

The eighth trap is hiding delivery leakage inside attractive headline margins. In the illustrative Base allocation, eight clients consuming thirty analyst hours monthly require 240 hours before selling, management, and quality control. At a $75 blended delivery cost, that is $18,000 monthly, leaving $4,343.75 from the Base monthly cost ceiling of $22,343.75 for all other expenses. The canonical 1 FT + 2 C team therefore needs explicitly contracted capacity; headcount alone does not prove deliverability. An extra eight hours per client each month adds $57,600 annually, cutting owner profit from $219,375 to $161,775 if prices remain unchanged. Trigger scope revision when delivery hours exceed 110% of the signed allowance for two consecutive months. Suspend discretionary enrichment when an undisputed invoice exceeds forty-five days, subject to the engagement's notice terms. Require a thirteen-week cash forecast excluding all prospective awards. Three months of the Base total cost ceiling is $67,031.25, above the $10,000–$45,000 startup range; deposits, staged hiring, or a smaller initial footprint must bridge that gap. The stated $1,500–$9,500 monthly operating-expense range does not replace fully loaded scenario costs. Refund obligations and committed contractor payments remain cash exposures even when revenue collection stops.

## 3. Price dismissal, award denial, and platform exclusion explicitly  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The ninth trap is interpreting DOJ non-intervention as either vindication or automatic defeat. Non-intervention leaves a relator to proceed under § 3730(c)(3), usually with heavier financing and litigation burdens; it is not itself a merits judgment. Government dismissal under § 3730(c)(2)(A) is a separate event. The Granston memorandum describes dismissal considerations, not a statutory recovery entitlement or a portable probability table. United States ex rel. Polansky v. Executive Health Resources addresses Government dismissal after intervention, including later intervention, and the applicable procedural framework. Build an illustrative cohort of twenty counsel-screened matters: six stop for public-disclosure or pleading defects; fourteen proceed; three receive intervention and eleven do not. Assume two of those eleven later face Government dismissal, leaving nine privately pursued matters. The model's non-intervention rate is 11/14, or 78.6%; its assumed Government-dismissal rate among non-intervened matters is 2/11, or 18.2%. These are planning inputs, not reported DOJ or Granston rates. If one intervened and one privately pursued matter ultimately collect, eighteen of twenty produce zero recovery. Replace every assumption with counsel's documented cohort experience when available, preserving the denominator and observation window so incompatible rates cannot be multiplied together.

The tenth trap is treating submission as award eligibility. SEC Form TCR and IRS Form 211 serve different statutory programs and require distinct evidence, identity, timing, and award procedures. Route both through counsel under this operating model. An anonymous SEC submission requires attorney representation and additional identification procedures before payment; a generic anonymous email does not substitute for the prescribed process. Monitor SEC Notices of Covered Action because an award application generally must arrive within ninety calendar days of the notice. IRS mandatory-award eligibility generally requires proceeds in dispute exceeding $2,000,000 and, for an individual taxpayer, gross income exceeding $200,000 for at least one taxable year at issue. Tax return information remains subject to statutory confidentiality restrictions. Track each program's filing receipt, declaration, supplemental submission, notice date, and application deadline independently. Distinguish denial before payment from recoupment after payment. False statements, disqualifying conduct, revised proceeds, invalid entitlement, or contractual disputes can create reductions or repayment exposure depending on the governing rule or agreement. Never assert a universal clawback period. Preserve award determinations, fee calculations, cost invoices, and correspondence so counsel can reconcile actual retained proceeds rather than reverse-engineering them from an optimistic press release.

The eleventh trap is converting data access into an adversarial contest with the source platform. Repeated rate-limit violations, shared API credentials, scraping behind revoked access, and concealed account replacement can cause permanent account exclusion or contractual liability. Use documented CMS downloads, approved API access, permitted Open Payments exports, licensed import-manifest datasets, and authorized SAM.gov interfaces. Cache immutable public releases instead of repeatedly retrieving identical records. Set a local operational pause after three consecutive HTTP 429 responses, honor Retry-After, and resume only within published access conditions. A revoked credential or explicit access denial triggers an immediate stop, not credential rotation to defeat the restriction. Separately, monitor false-positive burden: if more than 20% of a twenty-item reviewed sample fails basic entity or arithmetic validation, disable that scoring rule until corrected. Keep safety thresholds distinct from legal conclusions. A 19% defect rate still does not justify forwarding a known error. External enrichment also creates disclosure risk when query strings contain sealed defendant names or protected identifiers. Strip sensitive fields before authorized enrichment and use a counsel-approved offline workflow where stripping would destroy context. The fallback is slower legitimate research, not a disguised replacement account or a purchased credential.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight verifications before a dossier leaves the workspace**
> 
> A failed verification stops external delivery. Preserve the work already performed, identify the defect, and resume only after the responsible person documents its resolution.
> - Verify acquisition authority, immutable source copies, and reproducible exhibit totals; quarantine every restricted record lacking a documented access basis.
> - Obtain counsel's public-disclosure, original-source, first-to-file, and jurisdiction-specific pleading assessment without claiming inaccessible sealed dockets have been cleared.
> - Verify recipient permissions, seal-sensitive fields, and the exact filing or award-application deadline before transferring any matter package.
> - Confirm the engagement remains solvent with zero award receipts, documented contractor capacity, and cash reserved for refunds and committed delivery.

## 4. Remove procurement pass-through defects and rehearse the shutdown  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The required mapped play, gsa_municipal_set_aside_facility_ghost_brokering, is workable only as disclosed coordination between an eligible facility-service prime and qualified subcontractors. The name must not become an operating instruction to conceal who performs the work. Consider an illustrative $100,000 eligible service contract: a documented $5,000 coordination fee less $3,200 of delivery costs leaves $1,800 contribution before overhead. The operator records only the earned $5,000 service fee, not the prime's $100,000 contract revenue. A cost-of-personnel or similarly situated entity analysis under applicable federal limitations on subcontracting must follow the actual solicitation and 13 CFR § 125.6; the label coordinator does not excuse prohibited pass-through performance. Federal service set-asides generally restrict payments to nonsimilarly situated subcontractors to 50% of the amount paid by the Government for contract performance, subject to applicable rules and exclusions. Municipal programs can impose different certification, performance, and disclosure requirements. Verify the specific local ordinance and solicitation rather than transplanting federal percentages. Match capability, licenses, staffing, and required status before quoting. The forensic business must also screen conflicts before accepting investigative work involving its own coordination customers, subcontractors, or counterparties with overlapping records.

The twelfth trap is disguising a success commission as routine coordination while ignoring procurement restrictions. For covered federal contracts, FAR Subpart 3.4 and applicable clauses address contingent fees for obtaining Government contracts, with defined exceptions that counsel must evaluate. A fee is not permissible merely because an invoice says consulting. False eligibility certifications, fabricated subcontractor capacity, sham invoices, and undisclosed kickbacks can expose participants to payment recovery, termination, exclusion, and other liability. Track eligibility evidence by bid date, subcontractor status by performance period, and performance payments against the applicable limitation. An unverified certification or projected prohibited payment allocation stops the bid. If the $5,000 coordination fee is refundable upon a valid contract defect and $3,200 has already been spent, returning the fee changes the engagement from $1,800 contribution to a $3,200 loss. That $5,000 adverse swing must fit the refund reserve. The required fallback is to withdraw noncompliant bids and operate as a disclosed subcontractor on unrestricted work. Do not repackage the same ineligible arrangement under another entity. Keep this play's fee ledger separate from forensic retainers and award proceeds; its illustrative economics are not additional revenue silently added to the canonical volume scenarios.

The thirteenth trap is continuing because sunk research costs feel recoverable. Implement a weekly exception meeting using one ledger for evidence defects, another for delivery economics, and a third for deadlines and access incidents. Assign an accountable person and a next review date to every exception. Red conditions include unauthorized disclosure, missing acquisition authority, fabricated support, a prohibited procurement arrangement, and insufficient cash for committed obligations. Amber conditions include disputed scope, declining paid utilization, and evidence needing corroboration. Counsel owns legal determinations; the operator owns collection pauses, cost freezes, and accurate reporting. Rehearse a zero-recovery quarter in which the largest retainer pauses and no contingent proceeds arrive. In the Base allocation, losing one $5,000 monthly client for three months removes $15,000 revenue before any cost adjustment. If costs remain fixed, annual owner profit falls to $204,375. Reforecast immediately instead of waiting for an annual margin report. Close an engagement with a source inventory, delivered-work acceptance record, outstanding invoice schedule, refund calculation, and counsel-directed retention instructions. Never delete records to make an incident disappear. The durable advantage is surviving enough ordinary paid engagements to pursue exceptional matters without pressuring analysts to turn uncertainty into allegations.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the defective workstream
   - Pause affected collection, external delivery, or bidding immediately.
   - Preserve source files, permission records, correspondence, and access logs. Do not broaden the pause to unrelated authorized engagements unless the incident affects their evidence or systems.
2. Assign authority and quantify exposure
   - Send the factual incident record to the responsible counsel and operator.
   - List affected recipients, deadlines, earned fees, refundable balances, committed contractor costs, and potential award dependencies. Separate established facts from unresolved hypotheses.
3. Rebuild the zero-award cash forecast
   - Update thirteen weeks of collections, refunds, payroll, and contracted spending.
   - Remove every prospective statutory award. Apply the actual engagement terms to service pauses and refunds, and identify the funding needed before any new commitment.
4. Resume with evidence or execute the fallback
   - Document corrected authority, calculations, recipients, and engagement scope.
   - Resume only the corrected activity. If a procurement bid remains noncompliant, withdraw it and pursue disclosed unrestricted work; if evidence remains insufficient, close the allegation workstream.
