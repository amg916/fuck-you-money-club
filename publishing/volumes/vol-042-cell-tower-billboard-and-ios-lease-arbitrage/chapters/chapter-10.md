## 1. Build a parcel evidence pipeline before automating qualification  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Give every opportunity a durable identity before buying software. Use county FIPS, assessor parcel number, instrument number, and an internal opportunity identifier; keep tower registration numbers and yard entrances as linked assets rather than interchangeable addresses. A Python scheduler fetches permitted FCC Antenna Structure Registration downloads, state DOT annual average daily traffic files, county parcel exports, and authorized recorder indexes. Store source URL, retrieval time, effective date, checksum, coordinate reference system, and license restrictions beside every record. Python's urllib.request, csv, json, sqlite3, hashlib, and logging support acquisition, normalization, deduplication, and audit trails without sibling modules or third-party packages. For spatial joins, call an authorized ArcGIS REST endpoint or use precomputed parcel crosswalks; do not treat latitude and longitude rounding as a property boundary test. An ASR match identifies a registered structure, not ownership of its ground lease, and rooftop telecom sites may never appear in that inventory. A reviewer resolves parcel splits, mailing-address collisions, and boundary uncertainty before a record becomes contactable. Route failed downloads into a dated retry queue, preserve the last usable snapshot, and mark its age prominently instead of silently presenting stale records as fresh opportunities.

Separate evidence extraction from eligibility decisions. The model receives a bounded document excerpt and returns typed fields for owner entity, lease term, renewal options, escalation language, assignment restrictions, mortgage references, and quoted supporting text. Validate the response with explicit Python checks: required keys, allowed enumerations, numeric ranges, and offsets that actually match the source. Treat every filing and lease as untrusted content; instructions embedded inside those documents cannot change the extraction schema or trigger external actions. A second pass compares critical numbers against the original image or PDF rendering. Low confidence, conflicting dates, and missing exhibits go to a person rather than receiving invented values. DOT AADT is a demand proxy, not proof of digital-sign eligibility, visibility, or available advertiser demand. Connect traffic segments to route direction, measurement year, truck share where published, and actual sightlines. For IOS, join zoning use categories, flood layers, access geometry, and available pavement records, then require heavy-axle and operating-hours confirmation locally. Under 47 CFR 17.4, registration obligations attach to qualifying antenna structures; an inventory screen cannot certify every installation. Likewise, 23 U.S.C. § 131 and state spacing rules require corridor-specific billboard analysis rather than a national distance shortcut.

Instrument automation as a labor experiment, not a count of model calls. Define one eligible task as a repeatable record transformation that previously took two minutes, and freeze the definition before measurement. An illustrative batch of 1,000 tasks has a 2,000-minute manual baseline. If 740 finish without intervention, 260 require two minutes each, and sampled review plus incident recovery consumes another 60 minutes, total human time becomes 580 minutes: a 71% reduction. This does not mean 740 parcels qualify, nor that negotiations become autonomous. At assumed prices, 1,000 extraction calls costing $0.02 each, $8 of data access, $4 of compute, and $3 of storage total $35, or $0.035 per task. Valuing all 580 human minutes at $45 per hour adds $435, producing a fully loaded $470 batch cost, or $0.47 per task. Track correction frequency separately from throughput because fast erroneous joins create expensive mail and diligence. The full-time principal owns exception priorities; three contractors cover research operations, document coordination, and field or accounting work within their competence. Use qualified specialists when necessary, with their costs inside the operating budget. Recalculate savings weekly and suspend straight-through processing when a source schema changes.

## 3. Connect RFID access, NNN billing, and recovery into one auditable loop  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat the yard as a physical operating system whose software can fail without trapping people or destroying billing evidence. The RFID reader sends an event to a site controller, which records tag identifier, reader identifier, direction, controller sequence, and timestamp. The cloud endpoint authenticates the sender, validates the message schema, and places the event in a durable queue before acknowledging receipt. Use a stable event identifier and a unique database constraint to make retries idempotent. A tag read proves detection, not necessarily a completed vehicle movement; correlate entry and exit sensors and route contradictions to review. Contracted capacity and lease terms govern recurring rent, while separately agreed usage rules govern excess occupancy or additional services. Keep access authorization separate from invoice computation so a duplicate message cannot create duplicate charges or revoke a tenant's credentials. During connectivity loss, the local controller follows a reviewed cached-access policy and buffers events for later synchronization. Emergency egress and manual release follow equipment specifications and applicable local requirements, never an improvised software rule. Limit retained vehicle identifiers to operational need, restrict staff visibility, and log credential enrollment and revocation. Test outage behavior on site before marketing the facility as unmanned.

Encode each IOS agreement as a versioned billing schedule with commencement date, licensed or leased area, base charge, escalation formula, deposit treatment, and allocated NNN obligations. Triple-net wording alone does not establish which party owes every repair, tax adjustment, environmental cost, or utility expense. Attach the controlling clause to each allocation rule. Python produces an invoice draft; an authorized reviewer checks first invoices, amendments, extraordinary pass-throughs, credits, and disputed usage. A billing provider such as Stripe or an accounting platform such as QuickBooks receives only the approved invoice payload through its documented API. Webhook signatures are checked against the raw request body, timestamps receive replay screening, and payment events are reconciled against provider records rather than trusted as standalone proof of settlement. Maintain an outbox table so database changes and intended external actions can be recovered consistently after a process crash. Failed payment creates a collection task, not an automatic lockout. The principal follows the agreement and applicable remedies before restricting access. For an illustrative site collecting $12,000 monthly and paying $8,500 head rent plus $1,500 of directly assigned site costs, retained management spread is $2,000. Do not report $12,000 as the scorecard's retained IOS contribution or subtract those same site costs again.

Budget event processing by useful business action rather than by the largest telemetry counter. Suppose a yard sends 30,000 monthly events and the assumed queue, compute, and storage cost is $0.0002 per event: processing costs $6. Add $24 of monitoring and backups plus $20 of controller connectivity for $50 monthly technology expense, or roughly $0.00167 per event. This excludes hardware, installation, payment processing, field visits, and labor, which remain separate budget lines. Twenty reviewed exceptions at three minutes each and $45 per hour add $45, making the illustrated monitored workflow $95 monthly before those other costs. Use deterministic rules for access and money; a language model may summarize an exception but cannot invent a lease amendment or issue a bank instruction. Set alerts for missing controller heartbeats, impossible occupancy, repeated payment events, and unexplained ledger variance. Every alert has an owner and a bounded response window matched to severity. Restore backups into a separate test environment and replay recorded events to prove invoices remain unchanged. When automated recovery fails, move to a numbered manual access log and approved invoice worksheet, then reconcile both before returning to unattended operation.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Enroll the contract and controller
   - Link the executed agreement, authorized contacts, billing version, and site controller.
   - Test credential scope, emergency release, local buffering, and event identity before enabling routine tenant access.
2. Capture and reconcile operating events
   - Authenticate events, reject duplicates, and compare movement evidence with capacity records.
   - Queue contradictions for the field contractor; telemetry alone must not create an unagreed usage charge.
3. Review money before external transmission
   - Generate invoice drafts and obtain the required review for new or changed charges.
   - Transmit approved payloads through the outbox and reconcile provider settlement records with the internal ledger.
4. Practice outage recovery and controlled resumption
   - Restore a backup, replay events, and reconcile manual operating records.
   - Resume unattended processing only after balances, credentials, and field equipment pass documented checks.
