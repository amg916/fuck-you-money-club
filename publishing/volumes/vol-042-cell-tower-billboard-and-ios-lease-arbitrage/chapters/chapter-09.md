## 1. Establish principal capacity before pricing another party's rights  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start every engagement by identifying the asset, the contracting entity, and whose economic interest the operator represents. Buying an easement for your own account differs from negotiating its sale for an owner in exchange for compensation. California Business and Professions Code section 10131 describes compensated brokerage activities for another; section 10133 contains specific exclusions rather than a universal option trader exemption. Florida Statutes section 475.01 also reaches activities performed for another for compensation, while section 475.011 supplies defined exemptions. Counsel should classify the actual sequence, including solicitation, negotiation, advertising, assignment, and payment, in each operating state. An option signed immediately before reselling an owner's transaction does not settle that classification. Document genuine purchase obligations, capital exposure, assignability, and the operator's disclosed capacity. If the activity requires a license, retain an appropriately licensed firm under a lawful compensation arrangement or change the transaction before solicitation. Do not rename a commission a consulting fee and continue unchanged. For probate leads, obtain the fiduciary's appointment and any required court authority; for entity owners, verify signing authority and property ownership separately. An assessor contact is a research lead, not proof that the person can convey an easement.

Build a jurisdiction sheet that separates federal constraints, state programs, county records, and municipal use permissions. Under 23 U.S.C. section 131 and 23 CFR Part 750, highway advertising controls operate through federal requirements and state implementation; a commercially zoned parcel is not automatically eligible for a digital display. State rules and local ordinances can impose additional spacing, illumination, change-interval, height, and transfer restrictions. Obtain a written determination identifying the controlled route, measurement method, existing competing signs, permit holder, and proposed display format. For telecom assets, 47 U.S.C. section 332(c)(7) preserves local siting authority subject to federal limitations; 47 U.S.C. section 1455(a) addresses qualifying modifications of existing facilities, not unrestricted permission for new construction. FCC Antenna Structure Registration under 47 CFR Part 17 concerns structures meeting registration criteria and does not establish land ownership, carrier tenancy, or transferable rent rights. Keep each regulator's answer attached to the exact parcel and design revision. When an authority will not confirm transferability, price a conditional option instead of a completed permit assignment. When digital conversion is refused, evaluate lawful static use only if its economics work independently.

Keep the scorecard's revenue perimeter visible in every contract approval memo. Formula v2.0 uses annual operator revenue of $120,000, $535,000, and $950,000, producing owner net profit of $33,600, $214,000, and $494,000 at 28%, 40.0%, and 52% respectively. Corresponding total operating costs are $86,400, $321,000, and $456,000; teams remain 1 FT plus 3 contractors, 1 FT plus 3 contractors, and 2 FT plus 4 contractors. Those revenue figures represent retained easement spreads, earned billboard assignment fees, and retained IOS master-lease spreads, not institutional purchase principal or total fleet spending. An illustrative tower rent stream of $24,000 annually bought at 16 times rent costs $384,000; a supported exit at 20 times rent produces $480,000 consideration and a $96,000 spread before transaction costs. Neither multiple is an entitlement to an institutional bid. Confirm whether the quoted 16-to-22-times range references current rent, escalated rent, or another underwriting basis. With $12,000 to $60,000 startup capital, this example requires a conditional assignment or separately committed acquisition funding. Have the accountant assess ASC 606 principal-agent presentation for applicable fees and spreads; lease accounting follows the applicable lease guidance rather than a universal netting assumption.

## 2. Make the contract transfer a usable right, not merely a signature  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Define the asset through exhibits rather than broad promises about tower rights or yard access. A telecom easement package should identify the surveyed area, access route, utility corridors, term, permitted equipment, expansion rights, rent assignment, escalators, and existing carrier termination provisions. Specify whether the seller retains development rights that could obstruct access or impair the contracted use. For billboard transactions, distinguish ownership of the structure, the ground lease, the state permit, the local entitlement, and advertising contracts; an assignment of one does not convey the others. For IOS, define usable acreage, vehicle classes, parking density, pavement obligations, operating hours, drainage maintenance, and prohibited materials. Attach the governing site plan and a condition baseline with dated photographs. A triple-net allocation must identify taxes, insurance, maintenance, and extraordinary capital work instead of relying on the abbreviation alone. Set inspection access, test permissions, restoration duties, and refundable consideration before diligence begins. Tie the obligation to close or commence rent to enumerated deliverables, with an outside date and a clear termination mechanism. A diligence extension should state its price and length, not depend on another verbal discussion after an agency misses its response target.

Treat mortgage priority as a closing dependency, not an administrative task assigned after signing. Obtain a title commitment, review recorded mortgages and restrictions, and identify the lender's consent requirements for easements, lease assignments, and subleases. A subordination, nondisturbance, and attornment agreement must address the actual interest being protected; a tenant form may not protect a perpetual easement buyer without tailored language or separate priority arrangements. Nondisturbance should cover the agreed use after foreclosure while the protected party performs its obligations. Include notice addresses, cure periods, successor recognition, and limits on amendments requiring lender consent. Seek an estoppel confirming rent, defaults, deposits, side agreements, and termination rights from the relevant party. Check a carrier's purchase rights, assignment restrictions, and early termination rights against the buyer's underwriting rather than assuming rent continues forever. For an IOS master lease, confirm express subletting rights and whether foreclosure could extinguish customer occupancy arrangements. If the lender delays, extend the option through a priced amendment or terminate before exposure becomes nonrefundable. Do not substitute a seller indemnity for survival of the purchased right; an unsecured claim against the seller is a different asset with different recovery risk.

Use a payment clause that separates earned compensation, closing mechanics, and post-closing adjustments. For an illustrative permit assignment priced at $35,000, a $5,000 refundable escrow deposit leaves $30,000 due only after the listed assignment approvals and closing deliverables occur. The deposit is not incremental revenue, and refundability ends only as the contract specifies. Anti-circumvention language should identify introduced counterparties, the covered opportunity, duration, documented preexisting relationships, and an enforceable remedy; it cannot manufacture an otherwise unlawful commission. Allocate inaccurate title statements, preexisting contamination, physical damage during inspections, and unauthorized representations to named parties. Negotiate a measurable liability cap for ordinary contractual claims, while counsel addresses enforceability and appropriate exceptions for fraud, willful misconduct, indemnities, and payment obligations. Specify governing law, venue or arbitration rules, notice methods, interim relief, and responsibility for disputed escrow funds. For data and work product, distinguish public source records, purchased licensed material, original analysis, and customer confidential documents. Give the closing buyer defined use rights without promising exclusive ownership of public facts. Require return or controlled deletion of confidential files when a transaction ends, subject to specified retention duties and dispute preservation.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Prevent the four expensive closing surprises**
> 
> The strongest negotiating leverage exists before the deposit hardens and before a buyer builds its investment committee package.
> - Send the proposed easement exhibit and lender protection form together; a generic lender consent can omit survival after foreclosure.
> - Obtain billboard permit transfer instructions for the named legal entities before marketing a freely assignable package.
> - Ask the carrier or seller for every amendment and rent-direction letter; an old lease abstract can miss a termination concession.
> - Attach an IOS maintenance baseline identifying rutting, culverts, and pavement limits so triple-net language does not silently transfer deferred capital work.

## 3. Condition IOS exposure on environmental, access, and operating evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

An empty yard is not an empty risk file. Before executing an unconditional IOS master lease, obtain written confirmation that the proposed outdoor storage, fleet parking, trailer drops, and any ancillary maintenance fit the applicable zoning district and site approvals. Identify vehicle weights, axle configurations, turning radii, queueing capacity, emergency access, and restrictions on truck routes or bridge crossings. A permitted industrial use does not establish that a driveway connection or pavement section can support loaded tractor-trailers. Use the local planning office, road authority, fire official, and a qualified civil engineer for their respective determinations. Order a Phase I environmental site assessment from a qualified environmental professional and investigate recognized environmental conditions before hardening the lease. CERCLA liability under 42 U.S.C. section 9607 can affect current owners and operators; contractual allocation does not erase governmental rights. Where a purchaser seeks a statutory landowner defense, evaluate all appropriate inquiries under 40 CFR Part 312 and continuing obligations with counsel. A tenant cannot assume that buying a Phase I report alone establishes a defense. Match report reliance, user responsibilities, timing, and transaction structure to the protection actually being pursued.

Determine stormwater obligations from actual industrial activity and exposure, not the marketing label fleet yard. Under 40 CFR section 122.26, covered industrial discharges require the applicable permit pathway unless a valid exclusion or other determination applies. Transportation facilities with vehicle maintenance shops, equipment cleaning, or airport deicing operations receive specific treatment; a parking-only operation requires its own factual classification. State-delegated programs and municipal separate storm sewer requirements can add operating duties. Where required, prepare the stormwater pollution prevention plan, identify outfalls, assign inspections, retain monitoring records, and budget corrective work. Evaluate whether fuel storage creates obligations under 40 CFR Part 112 using the rule's capacity and discharge-potential criteria rather than assuming every tank triggers the same program. Contractually prohibit fueling, washing, repairs, waste storage, or hazardous materials unless specifically authorized and supported by site controls. Record preexisting staining, drainage patterns, fill history, and neighboring uses before customer entry. If testing identifies a material issue, obtain a scoped remediation plan and responsible funding or abandon the site. Moving contaminated soil around the yard to create parking capacity can expand exposure instead of solving an operational problem.

Underwrite the lease commencement date as carefully as the quoted rent. In an illustrative IOS month, $18,000 collected from fleet customers less $11,000 master rent and $2,000 pass-through site charges leaves a $5,000 retained spread before other operator costs. Annualized, that contribution basis is $60,000, not the $216,000 customer collection total. If customer receipts fall to $12,000 while those obligations remain $13,000, the site loses $1,000 monthly before overhead. Structure commencement around documented possession, permitted use, functioning access, and contracted delivery conditions; do not promise customers entry before those rights exist. Negotiate staged acreage where the landlord will support it, objective expansion pricing, and restoration obligations that distinguish ordinary wear from damage. Require certificates and policy review for commercial general liability, automobile exposure where relevant, and pollution coverage suited to the operation. Verify additional insured status and exclusions instead of treating a certificate as coverage. RFID access and billing software reduce administration but do not inspect drainage or enforce vehicle restrictions without assigned operators. Maintain an incident escalation owner, periodic physical inspection schedule, and cash reserve calibrated to fixed rent and the negotiated exit period.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the proposed use
   - Create one site plan naming acreage, vehicles, activities, and operating hours.
   - Send the same revision to planning, roads, environmental reviewers, the landlord, and the insurer.
2. Resolve physical and environmental findings
   - Obtain zoning findings, access review, and a qualified Phase I assessment.
   - Track material findings with responsible parties, priced remedies, decision dates, and termination rights.
3. Align the contractual dependencies
   - Tie deposits, possession, rent commencement, and customer access to objective deliverables.
   - Confirm subletting permission and lender protection before promising uninterrupted occupancy to customers.
4. Activate controls and verify the first billing cycle
   - Test RFID permissions, incident routing, inspection assignments, and invoice reconciliation.
   - Compare actual retained spread with the downside model and stop expansion when fixed obligations outrun collections.

## 4. Turn public records into verified, permission-based owner conversations  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Implement public_record_api_intelligence as a provenance-controlled queue, not a bulk contact-harvesting exercise. County dockets, probate registries, unclaimed property ledgers, FCC records, and DOT traffic feeds can reveal ownership transitions or site-selection triggers, but none establishes an uncontested claim by itself. Match parcel identifiers, recorded instruments, entity names, and event dates; route ambiguous matches to human review. Unclaimed property entries are research signals here, not permission to solicit recovery fees under a separate state's finder regime. Respect source terms, access limits, privacy restrictions, and redistribution conditions. For an illustrative monthly queue, 300 source events at $2 each cost $600; verifying 60 candidates for ten minutes each at $45 hourly costs $450. Total research cost is $1,050. If that queue yields 15 documented conversations, three conditional offers, and one completed $20,000 retained spread, research-adjusted contribution is $18,950 before closing costs and overhead. Those conversion assumptions are a test model, not additional scorecard revenue. If access breaks or permitted request limits tighten, switch to scheduled bulk FOIA/public-record exports, official data feeds, and low-frequency cached queues. Federal FOIA covers federal agencies; county records generally require the applicable state public-records process.

Use an opening that identifies the principal, source, and limited purpose without suggesting agency affiliation. Telephone script: 'Hello, this is Morgan with Cedar Parcel Acquisitions. We buy property interests for our own account. A recorded document suggests you may control the telecom lease at this parcel. Are you the right person to discuss whether a purchase proposal would be useful? If you prefer no further contact, I will record that now.' Replace the example identity with the actual caller and entity before use. Discovery script: 'Who owns the land interest, who receives rent, and who must consent to an assignment? May we review the lease, amendments, mortgage restrictions, and your preferred timing through a secure link?' Do not ask an unverified contact to email bank details. Before dialing, classify the number, technology, purpose, and state requirements under the TCPA, 47 U.S.C. section 227, 47 CFR section 64.1200, and applicable solicitation rules. A manual business call is not blanket permission for automated texts or prerecorded messages. Maintain suppression records, permitted calling windows, and required screening. Disable recording unless the applicable interstate consent requirements have been resolved and the required consent obtained.

Use commercial email with truthful routing, a specific subject, a physical postal address, and a working opt-out; where CAN-SPAM applies, follow 15 U.S.C. section 7704, including applicable advertising identification and opt-out processing within ten business days. Email script: 'Subject: Purchase inquiry concerning your highway sign ground lease. This is a commercial inquiry from Cedar Parcel Acquisitions, a prospective principal buyer. We are reviewing whether your ground lease and associated permit interests can support an assignment. We have not confirmed transferability or obtained agency permission. Would you like a short document list? Reply stop to end our marketing emails.' Append the actual sender's valid postal address and identity before sending. Billboard discovery script: 'Who holds the state permit, does the municipality allow this display format, and what written approvals would an assignment require?' IOS discovery script: 'Which vehicle classes and activities are authorized, what environmental reports exist, and when would rent begin if access work remains unfinished?' When an owner declines, answer: 'Understood. I have marked this contact for no further marketing from us.' Apply that suppression before the next campaign. Keep acquisition offers conditional and avoid statements implying guaranteed permit issuance, lender consent, or a committed institutional exit.
