## 1. Build the rights-and-events spine before buying enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Organize the desk around eleven mapped operating plays, each requiring a different evidence packet: tower easement acquisition, rooftop telecom rights acquisition, easement resale, billboard ground-lease control, billboard permit assignment, digital-sign conversion screening, IOS master-lease acquisition, fleet-yard subleasing, lender SNDA resolution, unmanned yard access and billing, and municipal_code_violation_trigger_arbitrage. These are operating workstreams, not eleven additive revenue forecasts. Give every opportunity a durable internal identifier linked to county, parcel identifier, physical address, geometry, asset type, and instrument references. An FCC Antenna Structure Registration number identifies a registered structure; it does not identify every antenna tenancy, establish land ownership, or prove an assignable lease. Many rooftop installations will have no ASR record because registration requirements do not cover every structure. Start with ASR for applicable structures, then join assessor parcels and recorder instruments, retaining separate confidence scores for spatial proximity and legal identity. A tower point inside a parcel is a research lead, not proof that the parcel owner controls the relevant easement. Store original source coordinates and the transformed project coordinates so an analyst can reproduce a disputed match. Reject automated joins across adjacent parcels until a survey, recorded exhibit, or reliable site document resolves the boundary.

Acquire public records through documented bulk downloads, published APIs, or the portal's permitted export function. For FCC ASR, preserve the registration number, status, coordinates, structure height, registrant, and source retrieval date; retain the download documentation alongside the file. Use ULS separately when a relevant radio authorization needs corroboration, recognizing that spectrum authorization and property control answer different questions. County assessor systems contribute tax ownership and parcel geometry, while recorder systems supply deeds, mortgages, easements, assignments, releases, and recorded notices. Search the recorder by both parcel-linked names and instrument references because indexing practices vary and parcel search may omit older documents. A recently formed special-purpose entity can hold title while an operating company occupies the site. Do not substitute a commercial contact record for that distinction. Build an evidence chain from current vesting deed to controlling lease or easement, then to each amendment and assignment. For billboard prospects, add state DOT outdoor-advertising records and the local sign file before pricing an assignment. For IOS, request the actual zoning district, permitted-use text, site approvals, and conditions governing truck storage, surfacing, operating hours, and access. Record the issuing office whenever a portal omits an attachment.

Implement municipal_code_violation_trigger_arbitrage as an event service, not an indiscriminate address scrape. Poll municipal building portals and county recorder dockets daily where supported for failed structural or fire inspections, expired permits, code citations, and newly filed mechanic's liens. Normalize event type, event date, publication date, case number, parcel, responsible office, and current disposition. A lien filing is an allegation requiring document review; it is neither a judgment nor proof that the owner needs a specific contractor. Deduplicate by jurisdiction and case or instrument number, then suppress resolved, withdrawn, duplicate, or mislocated events. Route exclusive, timestamped remediation opportunities to an authorized contractor territory and send attorney-relevant records only under a permitted arrangement that avoids paid legal recommendations or fee sharing. Illustratively, 120 monthly events multiplied by 50% verified relevance and 25% contractor acceptance produce 15 accepted leads; at $80 each, contractor lead revenue is $1,200 before collection costs and $300 of review expense, leaving $900 before other overhead. That separate service example does not increase this volume's canonical forecast. If daily access is unavailable, use low-frequency scheduled API or bulk exports, disclose record age, and identify every outreach message as private commercial contractor or remediation assistance, never municipal enforcement.

## 2. Enrich only after jurisdiction, geometry, and permitted use reconcile  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run enrichment as a waterfall with explicit stop conditions. First establish the parcel and jurisdiction from official geometry; second establish current vesting and recorded burdens; third establish the asset's permit and use status; fourth purchase missing ownership normalization or contact research. Commercial parcel coverage from Regrid or property records from ATTOM can accelerate joins, but neither should silently overwrite a conflicting official instrument. Save provider identifiers, source fields, retrieval timestamps, and match confidence independently. For an illustrative 200-parcel batch, a free jurisdiction screen rejecting 120 parcels leaves 80 for ownership review; a second screen rejecting 50 leaves 30 for licensed enrichment. At an assumed $2 per enriched record, purchased data costs $60 instead of $400. Those are budgeting assumptions, not vendor prices. Include analyst time: thirty files at twelve minutes each require six hours even when API retrieval takes seconds. Use a weekly exception queue to resolve ambiguous ownership, missing amendments, and parcel splits rather than repeatedly repurchasing the same records. For contact verification, prefer a counterparty's published business channel or a documented introduction. Do not infer permission for automated calling from the mere presence of a telephone number in a licensed dataset. Preserve opt-outs and channel restrictions with the contact record.

Use GIS to establish questions that a site visit and specialist review must answer. In QGIS, load parcels, ASR points, roadway centerlines, AADT stations, sign locations, zoning polygons, FEMA flood hazards, wetlands indicators, and known environmental facilities into a common projected coordinate system. Document units before buffering; a distance expressed in degrees is not a defensible spacing measurement. For billboards, state rules may measure spacing along a roadway or distinguish direction, interchange proximity, and controlled routes, so a simple circular buffer is only an initial filter. AADT counts vehicles, not verified advertising impressions, and the count station may represent a segment with different visibility or turning movements. For IOS, inspect flood exposure, drainage paths, nearby sensitive uses, access geometry, pavement condition, and recorded access rights. FEMA mapping does not describe every drainage problem, the Wetlands Mapper does not determine jurisdiction, and an empty EPA ECHO result does not establish clean property. Escalate potentially viable yards to an environmental professional and an appropriate Phase I scope before executing the master lease. Preserve the layer vintage, the actual source feature, and a screenshot of the analytical view so another reviewer can distinguish source facts from the analyst's interpretation.

Treat access reliability as an operating specification. The directory's refresh intervals are desk targets, not promises about government publication latency; every connector must record both source effective date and retrieval time. For ArcGIS REST FeatureServer services, inspect layer metadata for supported query formats, pagination, object identifiers, spatial reference, and maximum record count before choosing a batch strategy. Where supported, request identifiers first and fetch bounded groups with only necessary fields. Validate returned counts against the expected identifier set because a successful HTTP response can still contain a truncated result. Use conditional requests when a service supports them, honor Retry-After, back off after throttling, and stop when access terms prohibit automation. A portal login, CAPTCHA, or blocked response is a reason to request an export, not rotate identities. Budget for records requests, certified copies, and commercial coverage gaps instead of assuming every useful field has an API. Keep acquired personal information out of general-purpose screenshots and restrict contact exports to the people handling outreach. When a vendor contract restricts redistribution, send the contractor a permitted event summary rather than the underlying licensed dataset. Review official provider documentation at implementation time because endpoints, authentication methods, geographic coverage, and commercial entitlements change.

## 3. Turn evidence into spread math without inflating operator revenue  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Maintain separate ledgers for underlying transaction consideration, recognized operator revenue, and owner profit. The scorecard calls its revenue line gross revenue, but the supplied basis is net easement buyout flip spreads, billboard permit assignment fees, and retained NNN IOS master-lease spreads. It excludes institutional REIT principal face values and does not treat tenant business turnover as desk revenue. Reproduce Formula v2.0 exactly: Conservative revenue of $120,000 less $86,400 of implied costs yields $33,600 at 28%; Base revenue of $535,000 less $321,000 yields $214,000 at 40.0%; Scaled revenue of $950,000 less $456,000 yields $494,000 at 52%. Staffing remains one full-time operator plus three contractors for Conservative and Base, and two full-time operators plus four contractors for Scaled. These annual costs encompass the model's total cost burden, not merely software subscriptions or the stated monthly operating range. The $12,000 to $60,000 startup range, $2,000 to $12,000 monthly operating range, and two-to-six-month first-deal window remain separate planning constraints. Carry an explicit retained-share field where a transaction requires it, but do not apply a second haircut to revenue already expressed as retained spread. Have the accountant determine contractual gross-versus-net presentation rather than labeling every pass-through receipt operator revenue.

Price telecom rights from verified cash flows, not a multiple pasted onto gross rent. An illustrative easement producing $24,000 in annual eligible rent costs $384,000 at sixteen times rent and resells for $528,000 at twenty-two times rent. The gross difference is $144,000; if $24,000 of transaction-level deductions are assigned to the revenue bridge, the illustrative net spread is $120,000 before the model's remaining overhead. This example explains mechanics and is not the Conservative scenario's assumed deal schedule. Document where legal, title, brokerage, financing, and closing charges sit so none are deducted twice. Sixteen-to-twenty-two-times pricing describes a screening range, not an available buyer commitment. For sensitivity, $24,000 received annually for twenty years discounted at 10%, without escalation or terminal proceeds, has a present value of approximately $204,326. A perpetual easement does not make the underlying tenant payment obligation perpetual. Model termination rights, renewal options, escalators, revenue sharing, access obligations, and removal provisions separately. Package the lease, amendments, estoppel, recorded rights, mortgage information, and proposed SNDA for lender review. If priority or nondisturbance cannot be resolved, reduce exposure through a conditional option or abandon the purchase; do not compensate for defective rights with a more optimistic exit multiple.

For billboard assignments, distinguish the private ground-lease interest from the public permit. Illustratively, a $30,000 assignment receipt less $8,000 of acquisition and transaction-level deductions produces a $22,000 net spread before overhead, provided the receiving party can actually take the intended rights. State DOT permission, local sign permission, digital-conversion authority, and landlord assignment consent are separate questions. A favorable traffic count cannot repair a prohibited location or a nontransferable permit. For IOS, an illustrative occupied yard collecting $18,000 monthly and paying $12,000 master rent plus $2,000 of unrecovered site obligations retains $4,000 monthly, or $48,000 annually before remaining overhead. NNN language does not erase vacancy, defaults, drainage repairs, environmental exposure, or expenses the subtenant cannot reimburse. Maintain occupancy and collection sensitivities beside the rent schedule. If collections fall to $15,000 while those costs remain unchanged, retained spread falls to $1,000 monthly. Check whether reimbursements have already been netted before applying any retained-share calculation. Route zoning, heavy-axle access, stormwater, and environmental exceptions to the relevant specialist before execution. Where digital conversion fails, test a lawful static-sign assignment; where a yard fails the intended truck use, reject it or re-underwrite a specifically permitted use rather than assuming a variance will arrive.

## 4. Operate a small, auditable pipeline with diagnostic failure paths  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A small desk can maintain the evidence pipeline with Python's urllib.request, csv, json, sqlite3, hashlib, decimal, and logging modules, supplemented by QGIS for spatial review. Keep raw source files immutable and store each retrieval in a manifest containing source URL, retrieval time, source effective date, content hash, file size, and access basis. Separate ingestion, normalization, matching, scoring, and outreach into distinct stages so a parser failure cannot send an unreviewed message. Use SQLite tables for parcels, assets, instruments, permits, events, counterparties, rights, and exceptions, with source references attached to every material claim. Model parcel splits and multiple leases explicitly instead of assuming one parcel equals one opportunity. Apply Decimal to currency, store original text beside normalized dates, and quarantine records with ambiguous date formats or incompatible units. Idempotent imports should update an existing source record rather than generate another lead. Compare record counts, required-field completeness, and geographic bounds with the previous successful batch. A sharp change should suspend downstream delivery while preserving the raw file for diagnosis. Publish a compact daily report covering fresh records, unresolved exceptions, stale sources, duplicate suppression, and opportunities advanced. The operator needs actionable exceptions, not a dashboard celebrating how many rows were downloaded.

Build diagnostics around failures that create expensive decisions. A successful download containing a portal login page must fail the parser's content and schema checks. A county export with a changed parcel field must enter quarantine rather than break joins silently. Coordinates landing outside the county suggest a spatial reference or axis-order problem; repeated owner names across unrelated parcels suggest an overaggressive entity match. Compare a small sample against source documents after every connector change and keep known test fixtures for each jurisdiction. Use a dead-letter queue for records requiring human interpretation, with an assigned person, reason, next action, and due date. If a portal becomes unavailable, retain the last verified record but display its age and stop describing the opportunity as current. For yard operations, reconcile RFID access events with authorized vehicles, lease dates, invoiced spaces, and payment status. A tag read proves a reader observed a credential, not that a particular truck occupied a billable space all month. Flag duplicate credentials and anomalous dwell patterns for review; do not automatically invoice penalties from noisy telemetry. Gate-control outages require a documented local access procedure that preserves emergency egress and authorized tenant access without exposing remote credentials to contractors.

Give the one full-time operator ownership of exception prioritization, counterparty communication, and economic reconciliation; use the three contractor roles for title and transaction support, local entitlement verification, and environmental or site diligence as their scopes require. The Scaled case adds a second operator and a fourth contractor, not an assumption that more automation eliminates specialist judgment. Track verified opportunities per analyst hour, source-to-verification latency, false-positive rate, evidence completeness, consent turnaround, and collected spread per closed transaction. For illustration, a queue of forty exceptions at fifteen minutes each consumes ten hours; reducing that queue to sixteen through better identifiers releases six hours without relaxing diligence. Measure whether the released time improves verified deal flow rather than merely increasing outreach volume. Maintain jurisdiction-specific checklists for principal transactions and brokerage licensing because dealing in one's own contractual interest and arranging another person's transaction can receive different treatment under state law. Retain written contractor service terms and separate attorney routing from paid legal recommendations. Review source permissions, vendor entitlements, access credentials, and outreach suppression regularly. The operating edge is a reproducible decision trail: what right is available, what evidence supports it, what condition could defeat it, who must resolve that condition, and what spread remains after resolution.

**Six Diagnostic Controls That Protect Evidence Quality, Tenant Operations, and Revenue**

| Failure signature requiring operator attention | Automated diagnostic and retained evidence | Assigned response and safe fallback | Economic or operational consequence prevented |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A downloaded public-record file contains a login page instead of the expected dataset. | Check content type, required columns, and sample records; retain response status and the original retrieved bytes. | Pause that connector and request an authorized export; never bypass the portal's authentication or access restrictions. | Prevents stale or empty records from being reported as a newly verified acquisition or contractor opportunity. |
| Parcel joins suddenly multiply after a county changes identifiers or publishes parcel splits. | Compare uniqueness, match counts, and identifier formats with the previous release; preserve old-to-new mapping evidence. | Quarantine affected joins for GIS and recorder review; retain separate parcel histories until the relationship is established. | Prevents duplicate outreach, incorrect ownership assumptions, and purchase analysis tied to the wrong legal parcel. |
| A billboard appears eligible because an analyst used the wrong distance units or roadway geometry. | Validate coordinate units and measurement method; attach state rule text and the source roadway feature. | Seek DOT district review and appropriate measurement support; stop digital-conversion pricing until the conflict is resolved. | Prevents paying for a location whose spacing, corridor status, or permit conditions defeat the intended assignment. |
| An IOS yard shows frequent RFID reads but the monthly occupancy invoice cannot be reconciled. | Match credentials, authorized vehicles, lease periods, occupancy evidence, and billed quantities; flag unexplained differences for review. | Have the operator inspect exceptions with the tenant; preserve local access procedures during reader or network outages. | Prevents unsupported penalties, interrupted authorized access, billing disputes, and false conclusions about collected yard revenue. |
| An enforcement lead remains active internally after the source case has been resolved or withdrawn. | Recheck source disposition before delivery and retain the verification timestamp; suppress previously delivered event identifiers. | Withdraw the lead from distribution and notify the recipient of the correction using the agreed service channel. | Prevents misleading outreach, duplicate lead charges, and contractor effort directed toward an obsolete remediation opportunity. |
| The reporting ledger treats a buyer's total acquisition payment as the desk's retained operator revenue. | Reconcile settlement proceeds, acquisition basis, transaction deductions, retained-share treatment, and remaining overhead in separate fields. | Have the operator and accountant correct the bridge before publication; keep illustrative cases outside canonical actuals. | Preserves the $120,000, $535,000, and $950,000 scorecard denominators and prevents double deduction of transaction costs. |

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the jurisdiction and permitted source route
   - Choose one county and its relevant municipality and DOT district; document each source's permitted access method.
   - Record public portal URLs, export procedures, license terms, source vintage, planned refresh intervals, and fallback office contacts before scheduling retrieval.
2. Ingest and reconcile a bounded sample
   - Import a small parcel-and-event batch, preserve raw files, and manually verify identifiers against source records.
   - Test duplicate handling, dates, coordinates, instrument references, permit status, and event disposition; quarantine contradictions rather than overwriting them.
3. Purchase only the missing decision evidence
   - Advance surviving parcels through ownership, permit, contact, lender, and environmental review in that order as applicable.
   - Track paid lookups and specialist hours per verified opportunity; require written resolution of title, use, access, and assignment exceptions before commitment.
4. Release verified work and reconcile outcomes
   - Deliver authorized leads or transaction packets, then reconcile collections, retained spreads, source errors, and recipient feedback.
   - Apply exclusivity and suppression rules, log corrections, preserve an audit trail, and report actual operator revenue separately from underlying transaction consideration.
