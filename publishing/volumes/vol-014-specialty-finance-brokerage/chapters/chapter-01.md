## 1. Define the intermediary, the deliverable and the revenue boundary  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Specialty finance brokerage is the business of arranging commercial financing that requires more product knowledge, collateral analysis, or lender selection than a generic bank introduction. The operator translates a borrower's purpose, repayment capacity, collateral, ownership, and timing into a lender-ready package, then manages the transaction through documented funding. Equipment loans, accounts-receivable facilities, SBA loans, acquisition debt, and property bridge loans are different products, not interchangeable sources of easy capital. An equipment lender values identifiable machinery and resale liquidity; a factor evaluates invoice eligibility and account-debtor credit; a bridge lender studies property value, title, and a credible repayment exit. The broker normally neither advances principal nor earns the lender's interest spread. Its deliverable is a properly matched, documented placement under an enforceable compensation agreement. A $2 million facility therefore represents arranged financing, not $2 million of brokerage sales. This distinction separates an intermediary from a balance-sheet lender, debt purchaser, servicer, or securities placement agent. Each adjacent role changes capital needs and operating duties. Before accepting an assignment, identify the actual product, relevant jurisdictions, borrower entity, compensation payer, and activities the engagement authorizes. Do not describe ordinary commercial-debt placement as permission to sell investment interests or handle customer funds.

For this volume, recognized operator revenue means retained origination or referral fees only; loan principal, borrower sales, collateral value, and client transaction volume remain outside the revenue ledger. Consider a $1 million closing with a contractual $20,000 commission and a $5,000 referral share belonging to an outside originator. The scorecard revenue basis is the $15,000 retained fee, not the funded principal and not an inflated figure that counts another party's entitlement. Keep separate fields for contractual gross commission, partner allocation, retained fee, earned date, payment date, and reversal exposure. Contract terms determine when the service is complete and compensation becomes enforceable; cash receipt alone does not resolve revenue recognition. Under an applicable accrual framework, evaluate performance obligations, collectibility, and constraints on variable consideration rather than automatically recognizing every signed term sheet. Refundable advances remain liabilities until earned, while a disputed commission requires separate treatment from an unconditional receivable. The operational retained-fee measure should reconcile to the accountant's gross-versus-net presentation, including any principal-agent assessment. Track clawback estimates consistently without subtracting the same exposure twice. A lender dashboard showing funded volume is a production report; only the contract, closing evidence, and fee reconciliation establish what the brokerage actually earned.

Five archetypes explain where a small brokerage can build repeatability: equipment placement, receivables placement, SBA packaging and placement, property bridge placement, and acquisition or cash-flow debt placement. Choose one initial borrower profile and one primary repayment logic rather than advertising every financing product. A practical starting wedge might be established manufacturers replacing equipment, staffing firms financing approved invoices, or owner-operators purchasing occupied commercial premises. The canonical launch envelope is $20,000 to $100,000 of startup capital, $4,000 to $18,000 of monthly operating expense, and three to nine months to the first deal. Those ranges describe planning inputs, not a promise that their lowest endpoints work together. Nine months at $18,000 consumes $162,000 before startup costs, so that combination needs additional runway or a lower burn rate. Licensing, insurance, secure document handling, experienced underwriting support, and delayed commissions consume cash before marketing produces dependable closings. Two full-time roles can cover origination and credit operations while two contractors supply bounded specialist work. Specify contractor hours and deliverables; a headcount label does not purchase unlimited capacity. The initial moat is a narrow, accurate credit process that lenders trust enough to review quickly and borrowers trust enough to use again.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Verify the business before accepting a live mandate**
> 
> Complete these four checks for the actual product and jurisdictions before collecting a sensitive borrower package.
> - Document whether the activity requires a state commercial-loan broker license, an exemption, or a separately authorized specialist; California Financial Code section 22100 is one concrete starting point.
> - Execute compensation terms identifying the payer, retained fee, outside referral allocation, earning event, payment deadline, and every contractual clawback trigger.
> - Confirm borrower authority, approved recipients, secure upload access, retention rules, and a working process for independently verifying changes to payment instructions.
> - Establish at least two plausible lender paths with current product criteria; decline or restructure requests whose repayment source, collateral ownership, or permitted use cannot be substantiated.

## 2. Build a lender matrix that predicts execution rather than promises  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A useful lender matrix stores constraints that change placement decisions: geography, transaction size, industry exclusions, operating history, collateral eligibility, lien position, guarantor requirements, minimum coverage, documentation, and realistic closing duration. Add the last verification date and the person who confirmed each material criterion. A website advertising equipment financing cannot establish appetite for a particular used machine, cross-border seller, or startup borrower. The named organizations in the comparison matrix are research starting points, not representations of active broker agreements, available products, or published compensation schedules. Every fee structure shown is a negotiation category requiring written confirmation. Build primary and backup routes before submitting documents, but obtain borrower authorization and avoid uncontrolled application distribution. For accounts receivable, model advance rates against eligible invoices rather than total revenue. For example, $500,000 of invoices less $100,000 of excluded balances leaves $400,000 eligible; an illustrative 80% advance produces $320,000 before reserves and other deductions. For equipment, distinguish invoice price from lender collateral value and borrower cash contribution. For bridge debt, distinguish initial funding from holdbacks and future draws. These calculations prevent a nominal approval from becoming a closing failure when the borrower discovers that usable proceeds cannot satisfy the actual transaction.

The underwriting package should answer the lender's next question before a credit analyst asks it. Use Salesforce or HubSpot for relationship stages, ShareFile or Box for controlled document exchange, and a restricted Airtable or relational database for non-sensitive lender criteria. Do not scatter tax returns, identity records, or bank statements across unrestricted marketing systems. Capture source dates, document owners, reconciliations, and unresolved discrepancies so automation flags missing evidence instead of manufacturing credit conclusions. A receivables package should connect aging totals to the ledger and explain credits, offsets, concentrations, and related-party invoices. An acquisition package should reconcile seller adjustments to source evidence and distinguish historical earnings from unsupported projections. UCC searches and lien analysis belong early in secured transactions: UCC section 9-322 generally establishes priority rules, but state enactments, perfection methods, collateral classes, and exceptions determine the actual result. A filing receipt alone does not prove first priority. Use experienced closing professionals to resolve releases, intercreditor arrangements, and perfection issues. Maintain a dated exception register, assign every unresolved item, and stop describing a file as complete while essential repayment or collateral evidence remains contradictory. Lender trust comes from accurate exceptions, not cosmetically perfect folders.

Durable brokerage economics emerge when knowledge accumulates faster than headcount. Application checklists reduce repeated document requests; lender matching removes unsuitable submissions; renewal tracking creates timely repeat conversations before facilities expire. Those are the canonical automation levers, and each needs a measurable operating result. Track submission-to-indication conversion, indication-to-close conversion, days awaiting borrower documents, days awaiting lender decisions, and retained revenue per funded transaction. Separate lender rejection from borrower withdrawal and closing failure; otherwise a weak credit process can hide behind a single aggregate conversion rate. Monitor lender concentration by retained fees and qualified pipeline, because a dominant lender can change appetite faster than the brokerage can rebuild its distribution. Maintain backup relationships with genuinely different funding sources rather than multiple contacts at one institution. A moat also includes documented loss lessons: machinery categories that resell poorly, invoice disputes that recur, acquisition adjustments lenders reject, and property exits that fail under current rates. Borrower permission and contractual restrictions govern reuse of customer information; process knowledge can compound without republishing private files. Beginner hype advertises maximum financing amounts and instant approvals. A professional brokerage sells accurate expectations, cleaner execution, and credible alternatives when the first lender declines.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Make lender trust compound across transactions**
> 
> The strongest tactics improve decision quality and fee collectibility rather than merely increasing application traffic.
> - Ask each lender for three recent decline patterns, record them as dated matrix rules, and verify them again before submitting an edge-case file.

## 3. Reconcile retained-fee production to the three canonical profit cases  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The Conservative case is $120,000 of annual retained-fee revenue at a 25% net margin, producing $30,000 of annual owner net profit with two full-time staff and two contractors. One illustrative production bridge is twelve funded transactions averaging $10,000 in retained fees. At an illustrative 2% retained yield, each transaction would involve $500,000 of principal and the annual arranged principal would be $6 million; neither principal figure is revenue. The corresponding annual expense allowance is $90,000, or $7,500 monthly on an averaged basis. That allowance must cover the costs included in the scorecard, with a documented treatment for owner compensation and contractor time. It cannot silently assume four market-rate employees receiving full-year salaries. If the owner fills a full-time role, distinguish cash owner profit from any economic adjustment for replacement labor; do not overwrite the canonical result with a differently defined metric. Assuming a 25% qualified-opportunity closing rate, twelve closings require forty-eight qualified opportunities annually. This is a planning assumption, not observed conversion evidence. Measure acquisition expense per qualified file and per funded deal separately, then preserve enough working capital to survive delayed closings without accepting unsuitable borrowers.

The Base case is $560,000 of annual retained-fee revenue at a 35.0% net margin, producing $196,000 of annual owner net profit with two full-time staff and two contractors. An illustrative production bridge is twenty-eight closings averaging $20,000 retained, equivalent to a little over two closings monthly rather than a constant weekly rhythm. At a hypothetical 2% retained yield, the corresponding principal is $28 million annually. The canonical expense allowance is $364,000, averaging about $30,333 monthly across all modeled costs. This exceeds the quoted $4,000 to $18,000 monthly operating range, so that range cannot describe the complete Base-case expense stack. Treat it as an initial planning envelope or partial overhead category only after defining its contents. Reconcile payroll, contractor support, acquisition spending, systems, insurance, professional services, and other modeled expenses explicitly rather than forcing incompatible figures together. At a hypothetical 35% qualified closing rate, eighty qualified files generate twenty-eight closings. Capacity depends on package complexity and rework, not just file count. A brokerage processing acquisition packages may need fewer simultaneous mandates than one placing standardized equipment transactions. Preserve the canonical revenue and profit while testing whether the chosen mix can actually fit the stated team.

The Scaled case is $1,000,000 of annual retained-fee revenue at a 45% net margin, producing $450,000 of annual owner net profit with three full-time staff and three contractors. Forty closings averaging $25,000 retained provide one illustrative production bridge. At a hypothetical 2% retained yield, arranged principal reaches $50 million, but the brokerage still recognizes only $1 million on the defined retained-fee basis. Annual expenses are $550,000, averaging approximately $45,833 monthly across the complete modeled cost stack. The higher margin requires better fee yield, repeat relationships, reduced rework, or a more productive mix; automation alone does not prove those improvements. Stress the plan before adding fixed costs. If one quarter of projected closings disappear while average fees remain unchanged, revenue falls to $750,000. Holding the $550,000 expense assumption constant leaves $200,000 of owner profit before any additional downside, not the canonical $450,000. Separately model a lender exit, sixty-day commission delays, and reversals under actual contract terms. Do not merge these stress cases into the published scorecard. Weekly management should reconcile qualified pipeline, expected funding dates, earned retained fees, receivables, cash collections, and reversal exposure so headline production never substitutes for an executable cash plan.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Qualify the actual credit request
   - Record borrower authority, financing purpose, repayment source, collateral, timing, and jurisdiction.
   - Use a product-specific checklist and reject unsupported representations before sending the file to a lender.
2. Match and document compensation
   - Select a primary and backup lender using recently verified criteria, then execute compensation terms.
   - Identify the retained share, fee payer, earning event, invoice procedure, and reversal triggers before substantial placement work.
3. Control the closing package
   - Resolve exceptions, reconcile usable proceeds, verify closing instructions independently, and obtain funding evidence.
   - Leave movement of principal to the authorized lender and closing parties; retain the documents supporting the brokerage's earned fee.
4. Reconcile and schedule the next event
   - Post the retained fee, reconcile collections and partner allocations, and schedule renewal and clawback reviews.
   - Review aged receivables weekly and preserve a separate record of arranged principal that never enters recognized brokerage revenue.

## 4. Use adjacent mapped plays without importing their economics or unlawful assumptions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The mapped play public_filing_trigger_mail_arbitrage uses Secretary of State formations and county deed or mortgage recordings as timing signals, not proof that a recipient wants financing. Where access terms permit, ingest daily records, deduplicate entities and addresses, suppress unsuitable contacts, and send clearly private offers through Lob or Postalytics. Never imitate government seals, official citation numbers, or mandatory invoices; 39 U.S.C. section 3001 addresses deceptive mailed solicitations, and California Business and Professions Code section 17537.9 regulates specified recorded-document solicitations. Illustratively, 1,000 pieces at an assumed $1.10 delivered cost consume $1,100. A 2% response rate produces twenty responses; if five qualify and one eventually closes for a $10,000 retained fee, contribution before sales labor and all other costs is $8,900. Those assumed rates require cohort testing across the entire closing cycle. The play's broader monetization includes genuine workplace-update subscriptions, property-tax appeal services, and exclusive new-mover or business leads, each under its own applicable operating requirements. Those receipts are separate service economics and excluded from this brokerage scorecard. The fallback is unambiguous private branding and a useful standalone service, not more aggressive official-looking copy. Use recorded events to improve relevance while preserving accurate identity, truthful pricing, suppression controls, and a direct explanation of what the customer actually receives.

The mapped play erisa_self_funded_specialty_drug_importation_arbitrage targets self-funded employers with 100 to 5,000 employees, but its international sourcing premise requires a major boundary: ERISA status does not authorize importing otherwise unlawful prescription products. Foreign pharmacy licensing, manufacturer-sealed packaging, and zero employee copays do not establish lawful United States distribution. The proposed $6,000 to $15,000 monthly domestic list-price range and $800 to $2,200 foreign range are diligence hypotheses, not verified obtainable prices or employer net costs. At the play's illustrative $50,000 to $100,000 annual verified savings per patient, a 25% to 35% service fee spans $12,500 to $35,000. Do not calculate savings against list price when rebates, benefit design, or actual plan payments establish a different baseline. These amounts are benefit-service compensation, not retained loan-origination revenue, and enter none of the three canonical cases. The workable fallback starts with independent TPAs, transparent pass-through PBMs, lawful domestic specialty channels, and manufacturer assistance programs where actual eligibility permits. Maintain domestic bridge-fill coverage and licensed clinical oversight so administrative savings never depend on interrupted treatment. Any international channel needs a product-specific lawful pathway under the FDCA and applicable FDA rules; section 804 programs have defined limits and do not create blanket permission for employer-arranged biologic imports. Treat this as an adjacent specialist partnership, not an automatic finance-broker expansion.

The mapped play out_of_statute_zombie_debt_portfolio_settlement concerns purchasing or servicing distressed receivables, not arranging new commercial loans. Separate the period for filing collection litigation from credit-reporting limits, and exclude obligations extinguished by applicable law, discharged in bankruptcy, already paid, or unsupported by ownership and balance records. Regulation F, 12 CFR section 1006.26, prohibits covered debt collectors from suing or threatening suit on time-barred debt; it does not create a universal permission to solicit every old account. State rules can further restrict communications, require disclosures, or affect revival. Illustratively, $1 million face value purchased for $5,000 remains a $5,000 acquisition outlay, not $1 million of revenue. If verified lawful voluntary collections total $30,000 and servicing costs total $12,000, cash surplus before overhead and other expenses is $13,000; accounting presentation still requires its own analysis. No part belongs in this volume's retained-origination scorecard. A self-service portal must identify the creditor accurately, support disputes, avoid invented urgency, and never imply that payment erases accurate reporting automatically. Apply the Fair Credit Reporting Act's reporting periods separately from litigation limitation periods. The fallback is to suppress affected accounts, correct reporting, and suspend collection until counsel approves remediation. Across all three mapped plays, the transferable moat is disciplined verification; public records, healthcare savings, and face-value receivables are not substitutes for collectible brokerage fees.
