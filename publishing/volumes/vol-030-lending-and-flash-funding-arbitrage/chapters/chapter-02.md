## 1. Identify the Debtor, the Economic Buyer, and the Person Authorized to Release Cash  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The check writer is usually a wholesaler, acquisition entity, or business-purpose borrower whose signed purchase contract outruns available liquidity. The acute pain is a contractual deadline: losing an assignment spread, missing a simultaneous resale, or forfeiting an earnest-money deposit after a contingency expires. Statutes do not generally force this customer to buy transactional funding; contract terms create urgency, while lending and settlement rules constrain the solution. Separate three identities during intake: the entity owing repayment, the individual authorized to bind that entity, and the settlement professional authorized to disburse its closing proceeds. A title officer may accept written instructions without becoming a guarantor. Obtain formation records, an operating agreement or corporate resolution, signer identification, and the purchase and resale agreements before quoting executable terms. Verify entity status through the relevant Secretary of State and reconcile names against the escrow file. For a $200,000 acquisition followed by a $220,000 resale, a separately agreed $2,000 funding fee buys a specific liquidity bridge, not an interest in the entire $220,000 sale. The borrower evaluates the fee against the remaining spread after transfer costs, commissions, taxes, and other debt. A large headline spread cannot substitute for a complete settlement statement.

Build the sales conversation around control rather than speed alone. Ask who can approve the price, who signs the obligation, who can alter escrow instructions, and whose funds remain after senior disbursements. Experienced buyers pay for a reliable closing sequence and a precise document list; distressed buyers often seek an exception to every verification step. Quote a written fee schedule with the funding amount, permitted duration, extension mechanics, cancellation treatment, and lawful maximum charges. Do not describe a fee as earned merely because an invoice exists. A borrower paying $2,000 on $200,000 for two days faces a 1% period charge; a simple annualized comparison is 182.5%, which signals why state usury analysis must consider substance rather than the invoice label. Some jurisdictions exempt specific lenders, entities, amounts, or transactions; commercial purpose alone is insufficient. California Financial Code section 22100 establishes licensing requirements subject to applicable exemptions, while Florida Statutes section 687.03 illustrates a different interest-limit framework. Record the governing facts and reviewed legal treatment before offering repeat terms. If an operator cannot explain why the product is permitted in the relevant state, deadline pressure does not justify sending principal or disguising the charge as consulting revenue.

The mapped play earnest_money_gator_escrow_lending advances $2,500 to $25,000 into title escrow under documented JV or promissory protections. On a $10,000 deposit supporting a legitimately earned $20,000 assignment fee, a negotiated 20% share produces $4,000 of operator revenue; the returned $10,000 remains principal. The broader negotiating range is 15% to 30% of an earned assignment fee, or a lawful fixed return under the actual transaction structure. Specify whether the split uses gross assignment compensation or a defined net amount, then enumerate every permitted deduction. A refundable contingency is a conditional recovery mechanism, not cash-equivalent collateral: notice deadlines, delivery methods, disputes, and escrow release language determine access to the deposit. Record or perfect security only where appropriate to the asset and governing law; a UCC filing does not create a real-property mortgage. Under this volume's release rule, funds go only to verified bonded settlement agents after unconditional end-buyer cash is confirmed on deposit. Many early-stage EMD requests therefore fail eligibility despite attractive economics. Freeze additional advances when conditions deteriorate, seek timely authorized cancellation, and pursue counsel-reviewed escrow release or contractual remedies rather than assuming the title agent can refund disputed money immediately.

## 2. Select the Rail That Matches Finality, Custody, and the Closing Deadline  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat a payment rail as a combination of transport, custody, and enforceable instructions. Fedwire is transport; escrow is custody; a payoff authorization determines who receives money and when. Fedwire settlement between participating banks is final under its governing framework, but that does not make a fraudulent beneficiary instruction safe or eliminate separate recovery claims. Before the first transfer, call the settlement firm using a number independently obtained from its verified public listing, establish the officer's identity, and confirm the bank, beneficiary, account details, and escrow reference. Obtain required bond evidence and independently check the agent's license or appointment where applicable; insurance and bonding are different protections with different exclusions. Any changed bank details require a fresh callback and a second reviewer, never a reply to the change-request email. Use bank-supported security procedures consistent with UCC section 4A-202 and retain the agreed procedure. A small test wire can confirm an account receives funds but cannot establish that the recipient is entitled to them. Record the bank confirmation, settlement ledger acknowledgment, and exact release conditions together. If the bank cutoff leaves insufficient review time, reschedule the closing instead of treating urgency as authentication.

ACH suits predictable collections with enough time for settlement, return handling, and account validation; it should not be treated as equivalent to available wire cash for a same-day resale. Return and dispute exposure varies by entry type, account type, authorization, and applicable rules. An ACH credit arriving in an account is not automatically unconditional end-buyer cash for this operating policy: the bank and settlement agent must establish availability and remaining restrictions. Platform checkout adds processor underwriting, card or bank funding rules, settlement delays, reserves, and reversal exposure. Use it only when the provider expressly permits the underlying lending-related transaction. Retainers, statutory disbursement, factoring, and milestone holds are commercial structures rather than independent banking networks; each still settles through an actual transfer method. A retainer may fund a clearly described intake service but cannot silently become a nonrefundable lending charge. Statutory disbursement means following the specific jurisdiction's trust-account or settlement requirements, not asserting a universal payment priority. Factoring requires a transferable receivable and documented account-debtor treatment; anticipated assignment income is not automatically an existing invoice. Milestone holds preserve leverage only when accepted instructions make release conditions objective and do not conflict with superior rights.

For the core 24-to-72-hour double-close, create a two-closing cash map before principal moves. Identify the acquisition payment, resale proceeds, taxes, title charges, prior lien payoffs, funding principal, and the separately earned operator fee. On the illustrative $200,000 advance, a $2,000 fee creates a $202,000 repayment instruction; it does not convert the entire $202,000 receipt into revenue. Require the settlement agent to confirm unconditional end-buyer cash on deposit and the absence of unresolved funding conditions before release. A lender commitment, proof-of-funds letter, screenshot, or pending transfer is weaker evidence and does not meet that standard. Document whether the settlement agent may apply resale proceeds to the acquisition, because contract terms, lender restrictions, and local settlement rules can prohibit that sequence. Corporate 0%-APR credit is a funding source, not an escrow rail or permission to manufacture purchases. Verify whether the offer covers purchases, balance transfers, or cash advances; many cash transactions incur immediate fees and interest. Never route a sham merchant sale to extract cash. If a permitted $20,000 transfer costs 3%, its $600 cost consumes 30% of a $2,000 fee before operations, loss reserves, or financing beyond the promotional period.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Map the money and signers
   - Identify the borrower, authorized signer, end buyer, settlement agent, and every proposed beneficiary.
   - Attach purchase agreements, entity authority, settlement estimates, and a principal-versus-fee cash map to one controlled deal record.
2. Authenticate the destination
   - Independently call the verified settlement firm and confirm account instructions with dual review.
   - Record the callback source, officer identity, bank details, bond verification, and any instruction-change history before preparing the wire.
3. Confirm cash and release conditions
   - Obtain confirmation of unconditional end-buyer cash and accepted principal-and-fee disbursement instructions.
   - Resolve title exceptions, closing sequence restrictions, fee legality, and bank cutoffs before authorizing principal movement.
4. Reconcile repayment and exceptions
   - Match bank receipts to the escrow payoff ledger and post principal separately from earned compensation.
   - Escalate shortages immediately, preserve cancellation rights, and suspend new exposure until the discrepancy has a documented resolution.

## 3. Protect Earned Fees Without Confusing Referral Income, Security, or Escrow Authority  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Fee protection begins with a direct obligation from the actual borrower, not a promise that the title officer will remember to pay. State the fee's earning event, due date, repayment source, permitted deductions, extension treatment, and consequences of a failed closing. Deliver the payoff schedule early enough for inclusion in the settlement statement and obtain the settlement agent's written acknowledgment of the instructions they can lawfully accept. A borrower acknowledgment and an escrow acknowledgment serve different purposes; neither silently changes lien priority. Where appropriate, obtain a guarantee from a verified guarantor with collectible assets and properly documented authority. Security requires an actual grant, attachment, and the correct perfection method; UCC sections 9-203 and 9-310 provide useful starting points for personal-property collateral, with substantial exceptions. Search existing liens and investigate prior blanket liens before assigning collateral value. Contractual non-circumvention language can address diversion of an introduced opportunity but should not replace a receivable, security, or accepted payment instruction. Do not retain customer identity documents or threaten unrelated disclosures to collect a fee. If proceeds become disputed, preserve the ledger and communications, stop increasing exposure, and use the agreed dispute process and available judicial remedies.

## 4. Screen Counterparties, Cap Exposure, and Reconcile the Canonical Economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Counterparty screening must answer both willingness and capacity to pay, without assuming that a polished proof-of-funds letter answers either question. Verify borrower identity, entity status, beneficial owners relevant to the transaction, signer authority, completed-deal history, and references obtained through independent channels. Obtain bank information through authorized access or verifiable statements, never shared login credentials. Review litigation, bankruptcy, judgments, and UCC filings using the appropriate official court and filing systems; distinguish similarly named entities before drawing conclusions. Consumer reports require an applicable permissible purpose under 15 U.S.C. 1681b, and adverse-action obligations depend on the decision and governing credit rules. Check applicable sanctions restrictions using current official resources and resolve potential matches before proceeding. Screen the end buyer separately: cash availability, purchase agreement conditions, and the ability to complete the resale matter more than enthusiasm. Screen the settlement firm for authority, independently verified contact details, bond coverage, bank-account consistency, and disciplinary history where available. Establish exposure limits by borrower, settlement firm, bank destination, geography, and unresolved exception. Ten small transactions with one compromised settlement agent are one concentrated failure, not ten independent risks. Reduce limits when evidence ages or the closing sequence changes.
