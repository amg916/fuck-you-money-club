## 1. Classify the transaction before pricing the money  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with the actual credit transaction, not the label on the entity. A business-purpose representation supports the file but does not establish an exemption by itself. Record the borrower's identity, beneficial owners, use of proceeds, collateral address, occupancy, repayment source, and every state connected to solicitation, negotiation, execution, and performance. Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.3(a), generally exempts business, commercial, agricultural, and organizational credit from its consumer-credit requirements; the official commentary supplies purpose factors for mixed-use cases. RESPA has a related business-purpose exemption at 12 CFR 1024.5(b)(2). Neither provision supplies a nationwide commercial-lender license or erases state rate limits. An LLC borrowing against its member's residence therefore requires factual review rather than a checkbox. Regulation B reaches business credit, so standardize neutral underwriting criteria, preserve application records under the applicable business-credit rules, and use the correct adverse-action procedure for the applicant's revenue category. Do not promise identical notice mechanics for every commercial applicant. The practical file contains a dated purpose memorandum, a jurisdiction matrix, the responsible legal review, and a versioned product decision. If the facts change between intake and settlement, rerun the classification before issuing revised terms or moving capital.

Treat state authority as a product-by-state question covering lender, broker, servicer, and settlement roles separately. California Financial Code section 22100 generally requires a license for finance-lender or broker activity unless an applicable exemption exists; commercial purpose alone is not the exemption analysis. New York Banking Law section 340 has its own licensing scope, while General Obligations Law sections 5-501 and 5-521 and Penal Law section 190.40 create distinct civil and criminal usury questions. A corporate borrower's limited civil usury defenses do not authorize every pricing structure. Florida Statutes sections 687.03 and 687.071 distinguish interest restrictions and criminal consequences; Texas Finance Code Chapter 306 provides commercial-loan rules that still require transaction-specific analysis. Calculate the treatment of points, minimum fees, extensions, default charges, and lender-retained expenses before quoting. A $750 charge on $50,000 for two days equals 1.5% for the period and 273.75% under simple 365-day annualization. That screening number is not a substitute for the jurisdiction's statutory calculation, but it exposes why short duration cannot excuse careless pricing. When a lawful structure is unavailable, decline, reprice, or refer through an authorized arrangement instead of renaming interest a consulting fee.

Separate contractual control from statutory status. A 24-to-72-hour double close remains subject to its actual lending, brokerage, real estate, and settlement characteristics even when principal never reaches the borrower. Earnest-money funding requires a second analysis: a fixed repayment promise plus a purported profit split can resemble credit, while genuine shared downside introduces investment and potentially securities questions. Pooling passive investors or paying capital-raising commissions requires a separate securities analysis before solicitation. Under this playbook's release standard, unconditional end-buyer cash must already be deposited with a verified bonded title or settlement agent. Most early-stage EMD requests cannot satisfy that standard; decline them or provide a lawful referral rather than quietly relaxing the rule. Likewise, a 0%-APR corporate card is neither verified cash nor permission to manufacture purchases. Read issuer cash-advance, balance-transfer, promotional-expiration, and prohibited-use terms, then model the actual permitted proceeds and repayment date. Do not route fictitious merchant sales into escrow. Where applicable, assess state commercial-financing disclosure requirements and real-estate collateral exceptions separately from licensing. For any covered consumer settlement, avoid referral compensation prohibited by RESPA section 8; an administrative-services label does not rescue payment for a referral.

## 2. Draft contracts that survive a failed closing  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build a document stack with four coordinated components: the funding agreement, transaction schedule, escrow instructions accepted by the settlement agent, and any valid security or guaranty documents. The scope clause identifies the property, borrower, funded leg, maximum principal, authorized payees, and permitted settlement window. It also states what the operator does not control, including title underwriting and the end buyer's obligations. Define each fee by an observable event. For example, a funding fee becomes earned only after the authorized advance is disbursed and the specified closing event occurs; a separately negotiated commitment charge requires its own lawful basis and unmistakable trigger. Distinguish earned charges from refundable deposits and third-party expenses. Do not collect origination points twice through both the funding agreement and the settlement statement. Include the exact maturity time, applicable time zone, return account, cancellation procedure, and consequences of missed cutoffs. An extension requires a written amendment and fresh rate review, not a casual text promising another day. If end-buyer funds become conditional or are withdrawn before release, the instructions must prohibit disbursement and specify how any unreleased operator money returns without improvisation by the closer.

A repayment waterfall must distinguish priority by agreement from priority enforceable against third parties. State principal return, lawfully earned fees, approved expenses, and residual distributions in order, with settlement-statement lines that reconcile to the ledger. Where collateral supports the advance, attachment, perfection, and competing claims require their own work. UCC section 9-203 addresses attachment and enforceability of security interests; section 9-310 supplies the general filing rule, subject to exceptions. Use the relevant state's enacted provisions, not a generic national form. Real-property liens require the applicable mortgage or deed-of-trust process, while deposit-account perfection may require control rather than an ordinary financing statement. Do not describe a first priority position until searches, filings, and required agreements support it. For an EMD joint venture, identify who bears a forfeited deposit, who may cancel, what evidence supports release, and whether profits exist before distributions. A mandatory principal return plus fixed compensation must be reviewed for credit characterization. If the proposed recovery depends on suing an insolvent intermediary after a dry closing, the operational fallback is rejection or a materially different documented risk allocation, not stronger adjectives in the contract.

Use narrow anti-circumvention language tied to a named introduction, specified transaction, reasonable duration, and provable lost compensation; do not prohibit all future business with an entire title company. Have the remedy reviewed for enforceability and interaction with state brokerage restrictions. A liability cap should state its calculation and any negotiated exclusions for fraud, intentional misconduct, mishandling entrusted funds, or data incidents. Avoid suggesting a cap can erase statutory duties or protect every form of negligence. Choose governing law, forum, service mechanics, and either an intelligible arbitration procedure or court process, while recognizing mandatory local rules may still control. The data schedule separates ownership of borrower records from the operator's underwriting templates and vendor software. Specify permitted uses, access roles, retention periods, secure deletion subject to recordkeeping duties, incident notification, and vendor export rights. DocuSign completion certificates establish an execution trail, not the signer's authority or factual truth. Verify organizational resolutions and callback contacts independently. Require signed change control for payment instructions and a renewed independent callback; an amended PDF arriving from the familiar email thread must never replace verification. Close the file with one consistent executed version and a documented precedence rule.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Make the document stack do operational work**
> 
> Experienced funding operators remove ambiguity before the closing rush, when counterparties still have time to resolve it.
> - Ask the escrow officer to mark the exact ledger lines returning principal and paying each fee before circulating signatures.
> - Place the cutoff time and time zone beside the disbursement condition, not only in a definitions appendix.
> - Attach a named-party introduction schedule to anti-circumvention terms so ordinary future business stays outside the restriction.
> - Keep a clean fallback instruction that returns unreleased principal to the verified originating account when the second closing fails.

## 3. Bind release controls to the ledger and scorecard  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Make the settlement agent the verified release point, not merely a recognizable logo on an email. Confirm the entity's applicable license, physical office, responsible escrow officer, bond, and relevant insurance through independent sources. Bonding is an internal eligibility requirement here even where local rules permit another arrangement; a bond does not insure every loss. Verify the account beneficiary and wiring instructions using a telephone number independently obtained from an established source, then document the person reached and the confirmation time. CertifID or another verification service can supplement this process, but vendor output does not replace independent account and authority checks. Obtain direct confirmation that the end buyer's funds are received, available for the specified closing, and free of unresolved financing or release conditions. A screenshot, lender commitment, pending ACH entry, or proof-of-funds letter does not satisfy that condition. Separately verify accepted instructions that control the sequence and returns if the second conveyance fails. The title underwriter and closer must permit the actual double-close structure. If required cash or authorized instructions are missing at cutoff, preserve the capital and reschedule; do not convert a controlled transaction into unsecured overnight exposure by habit.

Keep unit economics separate from wire volume. An illustrative $50,000 advance earning a lawful $750 funding fee produces $750 of fee revenue, not $50,750. The returned $50,000 reduces the principal receivable. If an EMD arrangement distributes $2,000 to the operator from a $5,000 total venture fee pool, record only the operator's earned contractual share under the applicable accounting analysis, not the entire pool. Evaluate ASC 606 principal-versus-agent presentation where it applies; origination fees associated with loans may instead require loan-specific accounting and timing. Maintain a bridge from the management scorecard to the financial statements. The canonical Formula v2.0 revenue cases are $120,000, $535,000, and $950,000, all excluding principal. Their respective 30%, 42.5%, and 55% margins produce $36,000, $227,375, and $522,500 of annual owner net profit. Implied total annual costs are $84,000, $307,625, and $427,500. These totals include more than the stated $2,000-to-$12,000 monthly operating-expense range; bridge direct capital costs, contractors, losses, acquisition costs, and other modeled expenses explicitly. The Conservative and Base teams remain 1 FT plus 2 C; Scaled remains 2 FT plus 3 C. Never improve the margin by silently excluding a cost category.

For capacity planning, one illustrative reconciliation uses 60 earned fees at $2,000 for Conservative revenue of $120,000. Base combines 100 transactional fees at $3,500, 60 operator EMD splits at $2,000, and 65 origination-point receipts at $1,000, totaling $535,000. Scaled combines 140 transactional fees at $4,000, 90 operator EMD splits at $3,000, and 120 origination-point receipts at $1,000, totaling $950,000. These are illustrative revenue decompositions, not additional canonical assumptions or assertions that every receipt represents a separate transaction. Count an origination receipt only when it is distinct, lawfully earned, and not already inside the transactional fee. EMD receipts remain subject to the same release standard; if eligible transactions cannot support the mix, report the shortfall rather than funding weaker files. The $25,000-to-$100,000 startup capital range also constrains simultaneous advances independently of annual fee totals. Track available cash, outstanding principal, settlement cutoffs, and concentration by escrow agent. At $50,000 per advance, $100,000 supports at most two simultaneous fully funded advances before reserves and costs. Require fresh confirmation after any closing delay. A next-day release is a changed exposure, not yesterday's verified deal automatically carried forward.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Create the jurisdiction and purpose record
   - Classify the parties, proceeds, collateral, states, and actual operator roles.
   - Attach the applicable authority analysis and prevent quoting from an unreviewed product-state combination.
2. Execute a consistent transaction document stack
   - Match the funding schedule, fee triggers, security documents, and accepted escrow instructions.
   - Resolve conflicting payment terms and verify signer authority before treating the package as executable.
3. Verify cash and independently authorize release
   - Confirm bonded-agent eligibility, verified bank details, unconditional buyer cash, and closing sequence.
   - Record two-person review and repeat affected checks whenever the account, timing, or material transaction facts change.
4. Reconcile principal, earned fees, and exceptions
   - Match bank movements to settlement statements and close the payoff ledger.
   - Separate principal recovery from operator compensation, explain variances, and retain the complete execution and callback record.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four confirmations before a dollar leaves**
> 
> Every condition is necessary. A strong borrower or familiar closer cannot compensate for missing release evidence.
> - Confirm the transaction's purpose, state authority, fee treatment, and executed document version match the current facts.
> - Verify the bonded settlement agent, authorized escrow officer, account beneficiary, and wire instructions through independent channels.
> - Obtain direct confirmation of unconditional end-buyer cash on deposit and accepted instructions controlling both closing legs.
> - Have a second reviewer match available capital, cutoff time, principal-return instructions, and the final fee ledger before release.

## 4. Use public triggers without impersonation or unsupported promises  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The mapped play public_filing_trigger_mail_arbitrage uses daily Secretary of State new-LLC filings and County Recorder deed or mortgage recordings as timing signals, not evidence of creditworthiness or purchasing intent. Acquire records through permitted downloads, APIs, or licensed providers; preserve source, retrieval date, permitted use, and deduplication keys. A registered-agent address may represent hundreds of unrelated entities, so exclude those clusters rather than paying postage repeatedly. Use Lob or PostGrid to send plainly private commercial mail with accurate sender identity, optional-offer language, and a functioning response path. 39 U.S.C. 3001 restricts deceptive solicitations, including specified invoice-like mailings, and California Business and Professions Code section 17537.9 imposes particular requirements on covered recorded-document solicitations. Avoid the imitation format entirely instead of depending on tiny corrective text. Never mimic government seals, citation numbers, mandatory payment demands, or claims that a purchased deed copy is required. The fallback product must deliver real value: annual workplace-compliance updates, appropriately qualified property-tax appeal services, or a useful B2B introduction. State authority to represent taxpayers, fee restrictions, recurring billing rules, and actual service scope need review before the associated offer is launched. Public availability of an address does not eliminate data-use duties or authorize resale under every upstream agreement.

Measure the mail play separately from lending revenue. An illustrative batch of 1,000 pieces at $0.90 each costs $900; add $100 for records and deduplication for $1,000 total acquisition spend. A 2% response rate yields 20 responses; a 25% response-to-sale rate produces five customers and a $200 acquisition cost. At $480 annual subscription revenue and $180 direct annual delivery cost per customer, first-year contribution before acquisition is $300 each, or $1,500 total. Subtract the $1,000 campaign cost for $500 before fixed overhead, refunds, and taxes. These assumptions are sensitivity inputs, not observed results. With only three customers, $900 of contribution fails to cover that campaign cost. Keep this subscription activity outside the canonical funding-fee scenarios unless a separately approved scorecard explicitly includes it. Property-tax appeals should quote a clearly defined fee basis and never promise a reduction from the recording event alone. Exclusive lead monetization requires a truthful exclusivity definition, permitted data transfer, and buyer qualification. For example, two buyers cannot each receive the same supposedly exclusive lead. Stop a weak campaign, improve targeting or the actual service, and retain private commercial branding; do not rescue conversion by making the piece resemble an official collection notice.

Use scripts that invite factual verification instead of manufacturing urgency. For transactional funding: 'We provide short-duration business-purpose funding for eligible double closings. We fund only through a verified bonded settlement agent after unconditional end-buyer cash is confirmed. May we review your closing dates, purchase contracts, and settlement contact before discussing a written fee?' For discovery: 'Who is the borrower, how will proceeds be used, which states are involved, and what conditions remain on the buyer's funds?' For a failed cash check: 'We cannot release against pending financing. We can revisit the file after the agent confirms received cash and accepted closing instructions.' Commercial email must satisfy CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. 7704, including accurate headers, nondeceptive subjects, required advertising identification, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out honored within ten business days. Screen telephone and text campaigns under 47 U.S.C. 227, 47 CFR 64.1200, and relevant state rules before selecting automation or prerecorded content. A public filing does not supply required calling permission. Use manual, screened outreach where appropriate and maintain channel-specific suppression records. Never claim approval, available credit, verified cash, or a government relationship before the corresponding facts exist.
