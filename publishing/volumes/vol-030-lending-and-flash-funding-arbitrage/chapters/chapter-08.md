## 1. Build the public-record spine before buying enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Implement public_record_api_intelligence as a provenance system, not a scraped lead list. Start with the jurisdictions where the desk can actually fund through an independently verified, bonded settlement agent. For each county, record its recorder portal, assessor parcel key, civil and probate docket access, document prices, export options, and published automation restrictions. Use county plus parcel identifier as the property key; use jurisdiction plus entity filing number as the company key. Preserve original names alongside normalized names because punctuation removal can merge unrelated businesses. Every observation needs source URL, retrieval time, source effective date, document identifier, content hash, and the operator who resolved ambiguity. A recorded deed establishes a historical instrument, not a current title opinion. An assessor owner field can lag a transfer, and a probate docket can exist without authority to sell. Route these differences to an exception queue rather than overwriting the older record. The useful output is a compact evidence packet showing what changed, which business relationship might need liquidity, and which missing document prevents further action. Keep lead discovery separate from underwriting so a promising public signal cannot silently become an approved advance.

The first six directory entries cover fragmented records that national feeds often flatten. A newly filed probate matter may identify a property whose authorized representative will eventually need a coordinated sale, but the filing alone supplies neither transferable rights nor a closing timetable. Require letters of administration, the relevant court orders, and settlement counsel's interpretation before treating an estate transaction as fundable. Unclaimed property is similarly a research trigger: an official ledger may show a possible claim, but a name match does not establish entitlement or an uncontested recovery. Do not buy supposed claim ownership from an intermediary without verified authority and applicable state requirements. For a business borrower, compare active entity status, actual signing authority, and the correct UCC search jurisdiction; UCC section 9-307 often places a registered organization in its organizing state. A clean property address search cannot substitute for a debtor-name search. Store negative findings with the exact search terms and timestamp, because absence from one portal is not evidence of absence everywhere. These records prioritize verified outreach to businesses and authorized representatives; they do not authorize intrusive contact with relatives or extraction of restricted case material.

Use the named public sources below as jurisdiction examples, then reproduce the same source contract wherever the desk operates. Access charges and portal limits change; record the current published schedule during onboarding instead of embedding a guessed nationwide price. Distinguish polling cadence from source latency: checking every hour cannot accelerate a recorder's indexing process. Default to a daily retrieval queue, cache unchanged responses, and stop automated collection when authentication, CAPTCHA, or terms prohibit it. The explicit fallback for public_record_api_intelligence is scheduled bulk FOIA/public-record exports, official data feeds, and low-frequency cached queues. Federal FOIA does not govern county offices, so use the relevant state public-record process for local exports. In an illustrative monthly batch, $600 of records and enrichment expense across 300 candidate events costs $2 per event; if only thirty survive identity and relevance checks, acquisition cost is $20 per eligible review. Neither number represents a funded-deal cost until actual conversions are known. Track that denominator explicitly. If a source becomes stale, preserve its last retrieval date, suspend claims of freshness, and substitute a document order or a dated manual search.

## 2. Turn public APIs into a bounded enrichment waterfall  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run enrichment in an inexpensive-to-expensive order with a documented reason for every paid call. First reconcile the parcel and entity against official identifiers. Next obtain public company filings, federal registration evidence where relevant, sanctions screening inputs, flood context, address coordinates, and bank identity. Only then purchase missing property or court attributes. The next six directory entries are not interchangeable credit scores: SEC filings concern reporting entities, SAM registration does not establish creditworthiness, and FDIC bank data does not prove that an account belongs to a closing agent. Each source answers a narrow question and leaves an explicit remainder. For example, match a bank's name and certificate number before reviewing wire instructions, but obtain beneficiary-account confirmation through independent controls. Match sanctions candidates using identifiers and context, not a normalized name alone. Human review resolves possible matches before funds move. Keep consumer-report products outside this discovery waterfall unless the desk has a documented permissible purpose under 15 U.S.C. 1681b and a workflow for the resulting obligations. Public-record availability does not erase restrictions attached to a commercial report or permit repurposing sensitive information without reviewing its contractual use limits.

Give every connector its own credentials, request budget, timeout, retry policy, and parser version. SEC automated access requires an identifying User-Agent and adherence to its fair-access ceiling of ten requests per second; operate materially below that ceiling rather than treating it as a throughput target. Other services expose limits through documentation, contracts, or response headers, so do not copy the SEC number into a global setting. Honor HTTP 429 and Retry-After, use exponential backoff with jitter, and quarantine repeated authorization failures instead of retrying them indefinitely. Conditional requests using ETag or Last-Modified reduce unnecessary transfers where supported. Store retrieval status separately from the last successful payload so an outage never appears as a clean search. A schema change should fail closed for mandatory fields such as entity identifier, parcel number, or document date while allowing noncritical description fields to enter a review queue. Hash the original payload and retain its source reference under an appropriate retention schedule. This supports reproducible underwriting without keeping unnecessary personal data forever. Alerts should identify the affected deals and required manual substitutes, not merely announce that a background job stopped.

Measure the waterfall using reviewed files rather than raw API activity. Suppose 300 monthly events produce thirty relevant files, twelve files need a paid $5 property lookup, and six require a $15 document package. Those illustrative variable charges total $150, or $5 per relevant file, before subscriptions, labor, and official copies. If an existing $600 monthly research budget already includes those charges, do not add them again when calculating the earlier $20 review cost. Maintain a cost ledger by provider, deal identifier, and purpose so that retries and duplicate purchases are visible. Cache immutable instruments by document identifier and version mutable records by retrieval time. A possible probate or unclaimed-property trigger stays in discovery until authority and ownership are resolved; it never becomes a projected recovery merely because several providers repeat the same record. When an API fails, degrade to official bulk files, scheduled public-record exports, and low-frequency cached queues with conspicuous freshness dates. A mandatory closing check cannot degrade to stale evidence: replace it with a current independent verification or defer the transaction. This preserves the speed advantage without converting missing data into a false positive underwriting decision.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the source contract
   - Record access rights, price basis, field definitions, freshness rules, and permitted automation before connecting a source.
   - Assign a source owner and a manual substitute; store credentials outside source files and restrict them to the connector's required permissions.
2. Resolve identity before enrichment
   - Match county parcel keys and jurisdiction-specific entity identifiers before ordering commercial attributes.
   - Keep uncertain matches in a separate queue and preserve original names, legal descriptions, and the evidence used to resolve each collision.
3. Apply bounded retrieval and review
   - Fetch under provider-specific budgets, preserve provenance, and route stale or conflicting mandatory fields to a human reviewer.
   - Honor rate-limit responses, reuse permissible cached records, and substitute current official evidence when a closing-critical connector fails.
4. Publish a dated deal packet
   - Attach resolved identities, source dates, outstanding exceptions, and cost totals to the underwriting file.
   - Separate discovery facts from funding evidence, and require current settlement verification before the packet can support a wire request.

## 3. Buy normalization, then independently verify the settlement chain  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Commercial enrichment earns its place when it reduces manual reconciliation without obscuring source limitations. ATTOM can normalize property attributes and transaction history; Regrid can supply parcel geometry and identifiers; CourtListener can surface available opinions and RECAP materials. Coverage and freshness vary by dataset, county, and document contribution, so measure each service against a sample of current official records before expanding the subscription. Do not assume CourtListener is a complete state probate docket or that a commercial ownership field reflects yesterday's deed. Require the vendor contract to define permitted underwriting use, retention, redistribution, and any restrictions on individual-level data. Obtain pricing and quotas for the actual product rather than transferring a free viewer's terms to an API subscription. At a hypothetical $400 monthly platform charge, saving eight analyst hours valued at $40 each yields only $320 of labor value; the subscription needs additional measurable benefits to justify its cost. Conversely, documented reduction in expensive duplicate searches may close that gap. Never justify software by assigning a speculative value to fraud it supposedly prevented. Track actual exceptions caught, their supporting evidence, and the action taken by the desk.

Settlement verification requires a different evidence standard from property research. Check the relevant state insurance or settlement-agent registry, confirm the agent's appointment and authority with the title underwriter, and verify bond and applicable coverage directly with the issuing party. NMLS Consumer Access helps where the relevant license category participates, but an absent listing does not establish an exemption and an active listing does not authorize every product. State commercial-purpose exemptions, usury ceilings, and escrow licensing rules require a product-and-jurisdiction matrix tied to the proposed transaction. Florida Statutes section 687.03 illustrates why short-duration fees must be analyzed with the actual amount and time outstanding; section 626.8417 addresses title insurance agency licensing. For a 24-to-72-hour double close, require the settlement agent to confirm unconditional end-buyer cash on deposit and the exact release mechanics before sending principal. An email screenshot, proof-of-funds letter, or bank availability indicator does not establish that condition. Earnest-money lending that requires earlier deployment stays outside this release rule unless its structure can actually satisfy it. Do not let a discovery play silently relax the volume's stated funding discipline.

Use CertifID or an equivalent contracted verification workflow as an additional control, not as a substitute for independent callbacks and settlement confirmation. Obtain the callback number from a previously verified directory or established relationship, never from the same email that supplies changed wire instructions. Verify the receiving account, beneficiary, transaction reference, expected amount, and permitted release conditions with authorized personnel. Require a second reviewer for new beneficiaries and instruction changes, with a durable audit record linking the evidence to the payment request. A one-person full-time desk can use an authorized contractor as that independent reviewer without sharing credentials. Treat 0%-APR corporate credit as a separately documented liquidity source: promotional purchase terms may exclude cash advances, balance transfers, or escrow-related transactions, and fees can destroy the apparent spread. Verify issuer terms, available capacity, transfer timing, repayment dates, and permitted use; never disguise transaction categories to obtain promotional treatment. Automated proof-of-funds generation must reflect verified available capacity and an expiration time. If any settlement confirmation is conditional, stale, or contradictory, hold the wire and obtain fresh evidence rather than shortening the checklist to preserve a closing.

## 4. Instrument the payoff ledger and reconcile the scorecard  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Build the desk around a deal ledger with separate accounts for principal advanced, principal returned, earned transactional fees, earned EMD JV splits, origination points, provider costs, and unresolved cash differences. Principal moving through escrow is neither sales revenue nor owner profit. Where the desk acts as an agent, evaluate ASC 606 presentation and recognize only the earned operator share of applicable arrangements; do not record a partner's share as operator revenue and then subtract it twice. The supplied Formula v2.0 cases already define the annual revenue basis as earned fees, splits, and points. Preserve those totals rather than adding settlement billings or applying an invented retained share. Conservative revenue of $120,000 at 30% yields $36,000 owner profit and $84,000 of implied total costs. Base revenue of $535,000 at 42.5% yields $227,375 and $307,625 of implied total costs. Scaled revenue of $950,000 at 55% yields $522,500 and $427,500 of implied total costs. Those residual costs include every cost represented by the scenario margin; they are not interchangeable with the separate $2,000-to-$12,000 monthly operating-expense range. Keep scenario totals and actual ledger balances visibly distinct in reporting.

Use an illustrative fee bridge to test the Base arithmetic without pretending the scorecard supplied a transaction mix. One possible composition is 120 completed funding events earning $2,000 each, 100 earned EMD JV distributions of $1,750 each, and sixty origination events earning $2,000 each: $240,000 plus $175,000 plus $120,000 equals $535,000. These are distinct fee events with documented recognition conditions, not necessarily 280 different properties. Where one property generates multiple fees, link them to one master deal and test for duplicate recognition. Count an EMD distribution only when the desk's share is earned under the arrangement, rather than when earnest money is deposited. The bridge describes accounting volume, not permission to fund an earlier-stage EMD position that fails the release conditions. Capacity also depends on concurrency: two overlapping $75,000 advances require $150,000 of deployable principal before reserves, exceeding the stated $25,000-to-$100,000 startup capital range. A proposed partner line is not available capacity until committed and usable. For a $100,000 principal exposure earning a $2,000 fee, a complete principal loss consumes fifty such fees before expenses. That asymmetry explains why verification quality outranks raw lead volume.

A small desk can implement the evidence store with SQLite, encrypted document storage, operating-system credential storage, and a scheduled Python job using standard HTTP libraries. SQLite transactions should make deal-state updates atomic; idempotency keys should prevent duplicate provider orders and repeated wire-request creation. Keep payment execution outside the discovery worker. A reconciliation job compares expected principal, actual return, earned fees, and settlement statements using decimal currency arithmetic rather than binary floating-point totals. Use structured logs with deal identifiers, response status, elapsed time, and payload hashes, but redact account numbers, credentials, and unnecessary personal details. Test backup restoration, not merely backup creation, and restrict contractor access to assigned files. Track source age, ambiguous matches, paid-call duplication, verification exceptions, closing delays, and cash differences as operational metrics. The Conservative and Base teams each contain one full-time operator and two contractors; Scaled uses two full-time operators and three contractors. Assign the extra capacity to independent review and exception resolution before increasing simultaneous principal exposure. End each business day with all open advances tied to a named settlement contact and a documented next action. A profitable dashboard cannot compensate for an unexplained principal balance.
