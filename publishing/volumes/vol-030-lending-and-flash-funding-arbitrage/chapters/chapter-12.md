## 1. Days 1–7: Define One Fundable Product and Build the Evidence File  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start Day 1 by assigning the three-person pod explicit ownership. The full-time principal owns sourcing, underwriting, pricing, and capital allocation. Contractor one handles settlement coordination and independent wire callbacks; contractor two handles reconciliation and evidence retention. Obtain scoped state counsel separately when needed rather than pretending bookkeeping expertise resolves lending classification. For execution, map the volume's fourteen plays into a working launch register: P01 same-day double-close funding; P02 overnight transactional funding; P03 seventy-two-hour transactional funding; P04 refundable EMD advances; P05 EMD joint ventures; P06 EMD extensions; P07 documented origination points; P08 committed capital-partner participation; P09 promotional purchase-credit substitution; P10 issuer-permitted promotional cash transfer liquidity; P11 documented proof-of-funds issuance; P12 automated underwriting intake; P13 settlement-agent and wire verification; and P14 escrow payoff reconciliation. This chapter's register is an execution taxonomy, not fourteen independently additive revenue streams. P11–P14 are shared controls; they do not create additional scorecard revenue. Select P01 as the first live product and keep every other funding variant inactive until its distinct contract, liquidity, and state-law requirements are documented. Assign every incoming opportunity a play identifier so rejected files expose which expansion requests would actually change the desk's risk.

Days 2–4 turn the selected product into a narrow written eligibility standard. Identify the borrower entity, beneficial owners, property state, collateral, business purpose, settlement agent, expected holding period, and repayment source. Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.3(a), excludes specified business-purpose credit from its coverage; that exclusion does not settle state licensing, rate limits, brokerage treatment, or escrow authority. For California activity, examine Financial Code sections 22100 and 22050 and Article XV, section 1 of the state constitution against actual facts. Do not label a loan a joint venture merely because the agreement shares fees. EMD funding also needs a contract-specific cancellation deadline, deposit-release instructions, and an enforceable repayment structure. Under this volume's strict release standard, an early EMD request without verified unconditional end-buyer cash remains outside the live pilot. Record that commercial limitation explicitly rather than quietly substituting speculative borrower repayment. Require a properly authorized settlement agent, verified applicable bond coverage, and relevant insurance; a title policy or closing protection letter is not interchangeable with a bond. If the proposed jurisdiction or transaction cannot satisfy these requirements, change the market or stop that product before marketing executable funding.

Days 5–7 establish the cash firewall and rehearse the desk. The startup range is $25,000–$100,000, but that amount is not automatically deployable principal. Reserve $9,000 for ninety days of launch operating burn and another $5,000 for incidents; exclude both from funding capacity. Set a $3,000 cumulative operating-spend ceiling at Day 30, $6,000 at Day 60, and $9,000 at Day 90. These are this sprint's chosen ceilings within the volume's $2,000–$12,000 monthly operating range, not a promise that every jurisdiction fits them. Obtain fixed quotes for counsel, contractors, banking, and software; if essential work exceeds the ceiling, pause or explicitly recapitalize before continuing. Keep principal disbursements, principal returns, operating expenses, and earned fees in separate ledger accounts. Initial per-transaction exposure cannot exceed the lesser of $25,000 or 20% of verified eligible funding capacity, with only one outstanding pilot. Eligible capacity includes documented, immediately callable partner commitments only where their permitted use matches the deal. A small balance may therefore support no viable closing. Complete two fictitious-file rehearsals, including a changed-wire attempt, before accepting a live funding obligation; never send a test wire to an unverified account.

## 2. Days 8–14: Build Qualified Demand Without Selling Unavailable Capital  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Week two tests whether counterparties will buy the narrowly defined service at a price that survives real operating costs. Build a forty-account prospect list: twenty repeat investors or wholesalers, ten settlement offices, and ten active deal sponsors with documented business-purpose pipelines. Complete twenty personalized contacts and eight discovery conversations by Day 14. Count a qualified submission only when it includes executed purchase contracts, the proposed closing sequence, settlement contact, requested principal, expected dates, borrower identity, and a credible end-buyer cash path. A social-media message asking for proof of funds is not a qualified submission. Target four complete submissions during this week and eight cumulatively by Day 30. These are test thresholds rather than market forecasts. Log why each file fails: missing buyer cash, insufficient capacity, unsupported jurisdiction, weak economics, settlement refusal, or timing. Use P11 proof-of-funds output only when it accurately describes verified resources and their conditions; never imply an unconditional commitment from a revocable indication. P12 intake automation should route evidence and flag omissions, not manufacture credit decisions. Keep personal identity documents out of prospecting systems when a restricted transaction repository serves the purpose. Measure qualified submissions per conversation to distinguish weak positioning from a genuinely incompatible market.

Price the first eligible files from the bottom up. An illustrative $25,000 two-day advance earning $750 has a 3% transaction fee; its simple annualized arithmetic is 547.5% using 365 days, which is a classification and rate-review warning, not an expected portfolio return. Review fees, points, and partner compensation together under the applicable state's rules. If capital cost is $150, wires cost $40, allocated contractor processing costs $110, and an internal expected-loss allowance is $75, the file produces $375 contribution before fixed overhead. That allowance is a management pricing input, not automatically a booked accounting provision. Require at least $300 contribution and a 40% contribution margin on each proposed pilot using the same cost definition. Do not describe this transaction margin as the canonical annual owner margin. P07 origination points belong in revenue only when earned under the actual arrangement and applicable accounting policy. For P05 joint ventures and P08 partner participation, document whether the desk acts as principal or agent; recognize only its earned entitlement where agent presentation applies. Merchant volume, property price, loan principal, and capital-partner proceeds never become additional operator revenue merely because they cross an escrow ledger.

Days 11–14 validate execution with the settlement office and capital source before promising dates. Ask the settlement officer to explain the complete A-to-B and B-to-C disbursement sequence, funding deadlines, recording dependencies, permitted use of buyer proceeds, and payoff priority. End-buyer cash must be unconditional and available, not a screenshot of a pending transfer or a lender commitment subject to additional conditions. Obtain written confirmation that the structure fits the agent's authority and instructions; do not circumvent restrictions by describing the same funds differently. For P09, promotional purchase credit may preserve cash only through issuer-permitted, genuine business purchases; it is not itself an escrow funding rail. For P10, verify whether an issuer actually permits a cash transfer, its fee, applicable promotional rate, credit limit, expiration, and repayment terms. A purchase promotion does not establish promotional treatment for cash advances. Keep both plays inactive during the first pilot unless independent repayment liquidity exists before the promotion expires. Never cycle fictitious purchases or conceal the transaction purpose. Finish the week with two independently verified settlement relationships, a fully priced sample file, and a documented capital availability window. If any component fails, continue discovery without representing that funding is immediately executable.

## 3. Days 15–21: Execute One Controlled Pilot or Produce a Defensible No-Fund Decision  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Week three converts a promising file into a controlled decision. The volume's one-to-four-month first-deal window means a thirty-day sprint can succeed operationally without releasing principal. On Day 15 select the strongest eligible file, not the highest quoted fee. Day 16 confirms signed authority, transaction economics, settlement instructions, and partner availability. Day 17 refreshes buyer-cash evidence and checks all amounts against the approved closing statement. Verify wiring instructions through an independently sourced telephone number and retain the callback record. UCC Article 4A, as enacted by the relevant state, governs important commercial funds-transfer questions; California Commercial Code sections 11202 and 11207 address security procedures and beneficiary identification issues. Account-name familiarity does not make an incorrect account number safe. Use bank-supported dual control where available, with separate credentials and no shared approval tokens. Contractor one verifies beneficiary details; the principal authorizes release only after contractor two confirms ledger capacity and reserve integrity. If the bank cannot support the desired controls, document compensating controls and change banks before expanding transaction volume. A deadline missed because verification remained incomplete is a measured operational result, not permission to bypass the process or transfer money through the borrower.

Release on Day 18 only if every funding condition is satisfied and the actual transaction is ready; the calendar never creates an obligation to fund. Send principal strictly to the verified bonded title or settlement agent, with the bank reference and approved destination captured in P13. P14 then tracks principal outstanding, fee receivable, expected payoff time, actual receipts, and exceptions as separate fields. Reconcile the settlement statement to the bank transaction, not merely to an emailed receipt. For a seventy-two-hour maximum product, set the contractual deadline explicitly and schedule same-day monitoring around it; overnight or weekend exposure still consumes capital and carries risk. A missed contractual payoff immediately freezes new releases and starts documented collection and settlement follow-up. Do not extend the facility automatically, capitalize unpaid fees without review, or relabel a troubled advance as a new product. For EMD variants, a refundable deposit is not cash until its release conditions are actually met; disputes can immobilize funds despite favorable contract language. Record any requested extension under P06 and keep it inactive pending separate authorization and analysis. Dry-closing fallout is precisely why unconditional buyer cash must precede the initial release.

Days 19–21 close the evidence loop whether the pilot funded or was declined. Contractor two matches principal and fees to bank receipts, checks settlement deductions, and records actual capital days. The principal compares quoted contribution with realized contribution using identical categories; contractor one documents missing instructions, callbacks, and elapsed handling time. Track the principal's touch time separately from total pod time. The initial target is no more than three principal hours and five combined pod hours per clean funded file, excluding one-time system construction. These are capacity assumptions to test, not employee productivity claims. Require complete reconciliation by the next business day after payoff and evidence completeness of 100% before counting a file as clean. Maintain a separate cancellation cohort so declined files do not disappear from acquisition cost. If the week ends without funding, produce a signed no-fund memo naming the exact blocker, a responsible person, and a dated corrective action. Rehearsal files may demonstrate control readiness but cannot count as completed transactions, earned revenue, or measured capital turns. Close the week with a factual dashboard that separates readiness, pipeline, deployed principal, collected fees, and unresolved exceptions instead of compressing them into a misleading single success percentage.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Establish product and repayment eligibility
   - Match the file to the approved state, product, capital source, and signed repayment structure.
   - Record the play identifier, eligible capacity, fee calculation, deadline, and documentary basis for every material assumption.
2. Independently authenticate the settlement release
   - Verify agent authority, applicable bond, unconditional buyer cash, and beneficiary details outside the incoming email thread.
   - Capture verification sources and timestamps; changed instructions invalidate prior beneficiary verification.
3. Release within documented exposure limits
   - Have the principal authorize the bank transfer only after separate settlement and ledger checks.
   - Record the bank reference, amount, destination, payoff deadline, and outstanding exposure without treating principal as revenue.
4. Reconcile payoff and release the next file
   - Match bank receipts to principal, earned fees, partner obligations, and approved costs.
   - Complete reconciliation by the next business day after payoff; unresolved differences or overdue principal block new releases.

## 4. Days 22–30 and Reviews at 60/90: Apply Kill, Fix, or Scale Without Distorting the Scorecard  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use the final nine days to establish decision quality rather than manufacture revenue. Days 22–24 repair the largest measured failure, retest controls, and finish the eight-submission target. Days 25–27 build a thirteen-week cash forecast, review source concentration, and calculate capacity from observed handling time. Days 28–30 reconcile every account, score the decision matrix, and authorize only the next bounded experiment. Kill means stop new funding for the affected product and manage existing obligations to resolution; it never means abandon receivables. Any suspected wire diversion, unlawful operating structure, unexplained ledger difference, missed payoff, or breached reserve floor triggers an immediate hold. Confirmed diversion, an unremedied legal prohibition, or aggregate realized principal losses exceeding $2,500 kills the pilot. A breached burn ceiling also stops discretionary spending until the owner explicitly resets the funded plan. Fix means retain current exposure limits and assign a fourteen-day corrective sprint. Scale means a measured increase in qualified outreach or capital deployment, not automatic activation of all fourteen plays. Day 30 readiness may support doubling outreach without increasing principal. No first deal at Day 30 is therefore a Fix outcome when qualified demand exists, not evidence that the entire opportunity has failed.

Apply all Scale conditions together, using cumulative funded counts and the most recent ten funded files for operating quality, or all funded files when fewer exist. Day 30 deployment expansion requires at least two clean completed deals, eight qualified submissions, 100% on-time principal repayment, zero principal loss, and at least 40% realized aggregate contribution margin. Day 60 requires five completed deals; Day 90 requires ten, with at least five completed during Days 61–90. Both later reviews require the same repayment, loss, and contribution standards, full evidence, and no unresolved reconciliation exceptions. Day 90 also requires positive trailing-thirty-day operating profit after all desk expenses and accrued capital costs. An insufficient sample remains Fix, even when its single transaction was profitable. Kill the current acquisition channel at Day 30 if forty targeted contacts and twelve conversations produce zero qualified submissions; change the channel before rejecting the product. At Day 60, zero executable files from at least twelve qualified submissions kills that product-market configuration. At Day 90, zero executable files from twenty qualified submissions does likewise. Otherwise insufficient activity calls for a bounded Fix sprint, with the approaching four-month first-deal horizon explicitly reviewed rather than hidden.

Reconcile ambition to Formula v2.0 without turning a pilot into an annual earnings claim. Conservative is $120,000 recognized operator revenue minus $84,000 total costs, producing $36,000 owner profit at 30%. Base is $535,000 minus $307,625, producing $227,375 at 42.5%. Scaled is $950,000 minus $427,500, producing $522,500 at 55%. The first two scenarios use 1 FT + 2 C; Scaled requires 2 FT + 3 C. Total scenario costs include more than the launch operating ceiling: capital expense, transaction costs, labor, and losses must fit the appropriate annual envelope. Illustrative throughput equivalents are sixty deals at $2,000 earned revenue, 214 at $2,500, and 380 at $2,500 respectively; these are arithmetic decompositions, not supplied deal forecasts. Apply principal-versus-agent assessment before assigning those fees to recognized revenue; the scorecard supplies no separate retained-share multiplier. Never haircut it arbitrarily or add partner billings on top. After a successful review, increase verified outstanding capacity by at most 25%, subject to reserves, committed funds, and measured workload. Keep the one-pilot limit until five clean deals establish repeatability. Do not claim Scaled economics while retaining three-person staffing or excluding the additional team's costs from the profit calculation.
