## 1. Build an evidence pipeline before automating capital commitments  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Give the one full-time principal ownership of credit decisions, treasury authorization, and exception closure; assign one contractor to document operations and the other to relationship qualification and scheduling. Separate preparation from authorization even when the same people cover multiple shifts. Build the intake path with a signed webhook, Python validation worker, durable queue, evidence store, and PostgreSQL ledger. The standard-library implementation can use hmac, hashlib, json, decimal, and urllib.request; hosting and database adapters remain deployment choices. Authenticate the raw request body before parsing, enforce a timestamp window, and persist a provider event identifier under a uniqueness constraint. Return success only after durable acceptance, then process asynchronously. Every application receives a deal identifier connecting borrower entity, property, purchase contract, resale contract, settlement agent, funding source, expected payoff, and supporting evidence. Use allowlisted file types, malware scanning, size limits, and isolated document conversion before extraction. Treat every uploaded instruction as untrusted content, never as authority to change a beneficiary or call another service. The 14 mapped plays below are chapter-local workflow mappings; only the supplied outbound play identifier is presented as a catalog identifier. This distinction prevents invented catalog labels from contaminating downstream reporting and implementation.

Automated POF and underwriting intake should produce an evidence packet, not an autonomous promise to lend. Extract names, dates, contract amounts, deposit deadlines, assignment restrictions, financing contingencies, and escrow contacts into a versioned schema. Require each extracted field to carry its document identifier, page reference, and source text. Validate dates with datetime, amounts with Decimal, and required fields with explicit type checks; reject arithmetic that depends on a model interpreting punctuation. Compare entity names across contracts and verified formation records, then route mismatches to the document contractor. A language model may summarize conflicting provisions but cannot resolve them by choosing the more convenient document. Issue a proof-of-funds letter only after the principal verifies available capacity, authorized issuer identity, permitted recipient, expiration, and transaction-specific conditions. A POF letter describes verified capacity within its stated scope; it does not establish that end-buyer cash is already deposited. Bind the generated PDF to a content hash, record the approving person, and retain the exact issued version. If capacity changes, suspend further issuance and contact affected recipients through authenticated channels. Never create a reusable letter that implies unlimited funding across simultaneous transactions.

Measure the automation claim with a fixed reference basket covering research, qualification, fulfillment, and acquisition administration. Suppose one basket previously required 600 staff minutes: 180 for research, 150 for qualification, 180 for fulfillment preparation, and 90 for acquisition administration. Target residual effort of 40, 45, 55, and 20 minutes respectively. Removing 440 of 600 minutes produces 73.3% time reduction while preserving 160 minutes of human review. These are pilot assumptions, not observed throughput. Compare matched baskets with similar document counts and exception rates, and include callbacks, corrections, queue supervision, and failed attempts in the residual measure. Keep principal-release decisions outside the automatable task denominator and report their workload separately. Score extraction accuracy by field criticality: a wrong middle initial and a wrong beneficiary account must never share equal severity. Run the first twenty complete applications in shadow mode, comparing generated packets against independently prepared packets. Missing source evidence, stale balances, or contradictory payoff instructions force manual handling. A schema change starts a fresh regression run using retained, access-controlled fixtures. When the model service fails, the same form and checklist remain usable manually, with queued work resuming only after reconciliation.

## 2. Lock wire verification and payoff reconciliation to explicit states  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Represent the deal as explicit states: received, evidence_complete, cash_verified, human_authorized, funded, payoff_pending, and reconciled. Each transition requires named evidence and records actor, timestamp, previous state, new state, and transaction version. Only a principal with treasury authority can enter human_authorized, and that state must expire when material facts change. Release funds strictly to bonded title or settlement agents after independently verifying unconditional end-buyer cash on deposit and the documented closing sequence. Verify the agent's identity, licensing where required, applicable bond, and account ownership through independent sources; a certificate emailed by the applicant is only a starting document. Call a previously validated number, not a number supplied inside new wire instructions. Document who confirmed the cash, when it became available, and whether any hold or condition remains. A screenshot marked pending fails this test. Treat beneficiary changes as a new verification event requiring a fresh callback and authorization. New York UCC § 4-A-202 illustrates why agreed bank security procedures matter, but following a workflow does not establish that every fraudulent order will be recoverable. The bank's authenticated treasury interface remains the execution channel; the language model never receives wire-release credentials.

Maintain separate ledgers for principal movements, earned fees, unearned amounts, funding costs, and operating expenses. A $200,000 principal advance with a $2,500 earned funding fee produces $2,500 of operator revenue, not $202,500. When $202,500 returns, match $200,000 to the principal receivable and $2,500 to the contractual fee receivable, subject to the documented earning event. Store amounts as integer cents or Decimal values with explicit currency, never binary floating-point values. Each posting carries deal identifier, bank reference, settlement statement version, value date, and allocation reason. Import bank and escrow evidence independently, then reconcile both against the contract schedule. A $500 shortfall creates an open exception; it never disappears inside a favorable aggregate balance. Use reversals rather than editing posted entries, and require review for write-offs or fee concessions. When a JV agreement allocates only a retained split to the operator, recognize that earned share rather than the whole pool. Do not apply an invented retained-share percentage to the canonical scorecard. Determine principal-versus-agent presentation from the actual arrangement and applicable ASC 606 analysis, while keeping client property value and escrow throughput outside operating revenue in every dashboard.

Separate 24-to-72-hour double-close liquidity from earnest-money exposure and promotional corporate-credit funding. A $100,000 advance outstanding for two days consumes $200,000 principal-days; the same amount outstanding for thirty days consumes $3,000,000 principal-days. That fifteenfold difference matters more than a shared fee label. Earnest-money arrangements proposed before an end buyer has unconditional cash deposited do not qualify for this automated funding lane. Route them to structural review and do not release capital under this chapter's release rule. For a 0%-APR corporate facility, inspect permitted uses, cash advance pricing, transfer fees, maturity, and promotional expiry before treating availability as usable liquidity. A $50,000 draw with a 3% transfer fee costs $1,500 even when promotional interest is zero. Never disguise transaction purpose to bypass issuer restrictions. At a missed payoff deadline, stop recycling the exposed amount, notify the principal, obtain a verified settlement status, and update the liquidity forecast. California Financial Code § 22100 requires licensing for covered finance lending unless an exemption applies; Florida § 687.03 imposes interest limits subject to its scope and exceptions. Evaluate actual terms and jurisdiction before pricing, rather than assuming a commercial label removes these constraints.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Assemble the immutable decision packet
   - Collect current contracts, agent verification, deposit confirmation, and beneficiary instructions.
   - Bind every document hash and reviewer note to one deal version; reject missing or superseded evidence.
2. Verify independently and authorize explicitly
   - Complete the callback and have the principal review cash availability and release conditions.
   - Record the source telephone number, confirming person, verification time, authorization expiry, and exact authorized amount.
3. Execute through authenticated treasury controls
   - Use the bank's authorized interface with preparer and approver separation where supported.
   - Capture the bank reference without storing treasury credentials in document systems, prompts, or messaging logs.
4. Reconcile before recycling capacity
   - Match returned principal and earned fees against bank and escrow evidence.
   - Release the capacity reservation only after verified principal recovery; route shortfalls and late returns into named exceptions.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before capital leaves the account**
> 
> A green dashboard is insufficient. The principal must inspect current evidence for all four checks and record the decision.
> - Confirm the settlement agent's verified identity, required license, applicable bond, approved account ownership, and independently sourced callback record.
> - Confirm unconditional end-buyer cash on deposit, current availability, approved closing sequence, and absence of unresolved release conditions.
> - Confirm current beneficiary fingerprint, matched contract versions, available funding capacity, and independently reviewed amount and timing.
> - Confirm transaction-specific state lending, usury, escrow, and funding-source requirements are resolved; expired or conflicting evidence stops release.

## 3. Turn warm signals into qualified deals without scaling spam  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Implement high_velocity_dm_outbound_systems as a user-triggered conversation router, not a cold-message cannon. Publish a useful closing-readiness checklist, invite a relevant comment or reaction, and use an approved platform integration to acknowledge that action. Require a user-initiated comment, reaction, or inbound message before automated DM dispatch, and send external links only after the prospect replies. A trigger permits a contextual response within platform rules, not unlimited follow-up. Store trigger identifier, channel, timestamp, topic, and suppression status; deduplicate by person and conversation. Route replies among authorized inboxes by relationship owner and workload, never to evade platform restrictions. PeerFloodError, permission errors, or delivery anomalies pause the affected channel and create a review task. Do not rotate accounts to outrun enforcement. Silent Message Request graveyards are a measurement problem: report delivered messages, actual replies, qualified packets, and funded deals separately. The relationship contractor asks about property state, closing date, settlement agent, requested amount, and end-buyer cash readiness before offering intake. A language model can draft the question sequence, but it cannot promise funding, quote an unapproved fee, invent a relationship, or imply that a comment has secured a credit commitment.

Model a monthly warm funnel with 400 explicit triggers, 160 replies, 48 qualified conversations, 24 complete packets, and eight funded transactions. Those assumptions imply 40% trigger-to-reply, 30% reply-to-qualified, 50% qualified-to-packet, and 33.3% packet-to-funded conversion. At a $2,500 earned fee, the cohort generates $20,000 revenue before its allocated costs. A $400 content and distribution budget, $80 of automated runs, and eight contractor hours at $30 produce $720 acquisition cost, or $90 per funded transaction. This is a test cohort, not the Base annual scenario: eight monthly fundings would produce 96 annual transactions, below the 214-deal Base bridge. If only four deals fund, acquisition cost doubles to $180 and cohort revenue halves to $10,000. Attribute outcomes by original trigger and deduplicate repeat applicants so multiple inboxes cannot claim the same closing. Review the denominator when funding is delayed into another month; a pending deal is not a conversion. Use a thirty-day cohort report with a later reconciliation window, and publish conversion ranges only after enough observations exist. Pause content themes that attract large numbers of requests without verifiable escrow cash or plausible closing timelines.

Build the distribution flywheel around useful operating artifacts: a state-specific intake worksheet, a principal-days calculator, an escrow-readiness checklist, and an anonymized closing postmortem. Programmatic pages should contain reviewed state requirements, dated assumptions, and concrete examples rather than copied city names wrapped around identical claims. Publish a page only when its sourcing and operator utility justify its existence; refresh or withdraw it when the underlying rules change. Let title and settlement relationships generate voluntary introductions after a clean close, but do not turn that relationship into automatic compensation for settlement service referrals. For covered federally related mortgage transactions, 12 U.S.C. § 2607 restricts referral fees and unearned fee splitting. Any proposed partner payment needs transaction-specific review before launch. Use commercial email only with accurate sender information, nondeceptive subjects, a valid postal address, and a functioning opt-out process under 15 U.S.C. § 7704. Synchronize suppression records before dispatch. Seek explicit permission before publishing customer stories, and remove identifying property and bank details. Feed actual rejection reasons back into content selection: explain cash availability and closing sequence more clearly instead of purchasing more poorly matched traffic. Quality increases distribution efficiency without weakening underwriting.

## 4. Price the automation, reconcile the scorecard, and rehearse recovery  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Budget each automated run as an explicit unit, not a mysterious software subscription. An illustrative packet-processing run uses $0.12 for document-model inference, $0.02 for extraction, $0.03 for queue and worker execution, $0.01 for storage and logging, and $0.02 for permitted event delivery: $0.20 total. These are planning allowances, not vendor quotations; measure actual invoices and token usage before buying volume. Three thousand runs monthly cost $600, or $7,200 annually, before human review and fixed subscriptions. A run is a defined processing attempt, not a funded deal, and retries consume additional runs. If review takes four minutes at a $30 hourly contractor rate, the reviewed packet adds $2 of labor. Count unique packets, attempts per packet, and review minutes separately so caching does not hide rework. Apply a per-deal spending ceiling and alert when repeated OCR or model calls exceed it. Route oversized or damaged documents to manual preparation rather than looping indefinitely. The $2,000–$12,000 monthly operating-expense range is a planning envelope for the operating stack, not a replacement for total scenario deductions, which also reflect labor, funding-related costs, and other modeled expenses. Reserve startup liquidity separately from software spending.

Preserve the canonical Formula v2.0 cases exactly. Conservative revenue of $120,000 less $84,000 total deductions leaves $36,000 owner net profit at 30%. Base revenue of $535,000 less $307,625 leaves $227,375 at 42.5%. Scaled revenue of $950,000 less $427,500 leaves $522,500 at 55%. An illustrative volume bridge is sixty annual fundings at $2,000 earned revenue for Conservative, 214 at $2,500 for Base, and 380 at $2,500 for Scaled. These blended fees can represent earned transactional fees, qualifying EMD JV splits, and origination points; they are not statements about principal size or permissible pricing in every state. Conservative and Base retain one full-time operator plus two contractors. Scaled uses two full-time operators plus three contractors, five people total. The scorecard does not establish automated staffing savings beyond those teams. Treat automation spending as part of the existing deduction envelope, not another subtraction after owner profit. The $25,000–$100,000 startup-capital range does not automatically support every advance size; match committed liquidity to simultaneous reservations and verified repayment timing. Growth is constrained by peak capital exposure, qualified demand, and human review capacity rather than by webhook throughput.

Design recovery around at-least-once event delivery and exactly-once economic effects. Persist an idempotency key for every state-changing request, use a transactional outbox for notifications, and make ledger posting identifiers unique. Retry transient service failures with exponential backoff and jitter, then move exhausted jobs into a dead-letter queue with a named owner. Never retry a potentially executed wire merely because a response timed out; obtain the bank's transaction status first. Store secrets outside prompts and source files, limit service accounts to their specific task, and redact account numbers from routine logs. Evaluate coverage under 16 CFR Part 314 and implement the applicable information-security program, including access controls, encryption, monitoring, and incident response requirements. Backups need restoration tests, not just successful upload messages. Rehearse a duplicate webhook, a changed beneficiary, a model outage, and an overdue payoff every quarter. For each exercise, record detection time, exposure contained, manual recovery steps, and reconciliation evidence. Add people when verified review queues exceed service targets, not when a dashboard celebrates message volume. Release new automation in shadow mode, then limited production, with a reversible switch to the last tested workflow. The scalable asset is a reproducible decision record, not unattended access to capital.
