# Chapter 04: Reselling Adobe Through VIP Marketplace: Provisioning, Transfers, and Team Licensing

*VIP to VIP Marketplace changes, Admin Console and reseller console workflows, Extended VIP agreements, changing resellers, migrating VIP contracts to distributor portals, AppXite automation, weekly invoicing changes, downsizing and auto-renewal settings, and Adobe Teams vs Enterprise plan structures*

## Executive Summary

Adobe's VIP Marketplace is the forced successor to classic VIP, and every channel operator who spoke treats it as a migrate-or-exit event: Bechtle UK flagged commercial VIP end-of-life for January 2025, AppXite says partners will soon be unable to transact Adobe any other way, and TD SYNNEX Spain opened reselling without certification. The street mechanics are consistent: four price levels keyed to per-customer seat counts, auto-renewal on the anniversary date, a 14-day return window replacing VIP's 30 days, and licenses landing in the admin console within minutes instead of roughly 5 days. Operators split on the transfer itself, with TD SYNNEX running it partner-side without customer involvement while Bechtle requires the customer admin to enter the agreement number, and on how late renewal changes can be pushed: SoftwareOne says two days out, TD SYNNEX says one.

## Section 1: The Practitioner Consensus

Every channel voice treats VIP Marketplace as a deadline, not an option. Zoe Roby of Bechtle UK laid out the calendar: Adobe stopped accepting new commercial VIP business as of June, set January 2025 as end-of-life for commercial VIP, and required every live agreement to be migrated before the end of December, while education and government were not yet forced across [v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:02:42]. Angelina at AppXite put it more bluntly to resellers: within a few years there will be no other way to transact Adobe products, because Adobe is shifting to a purely digital model [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:01:15]. Adobe's own Doris Bing, speaking on the Pugh Computers webinar, framed the shift as a modernization of a program that had run manually for over 12 years, now API-based so the partner can quote, order and provision without bouncing between the reseller console and the licensing portal [qQoPO5MwZ60 @ 00:06:58]. David Villanueva at TD SYNNEX Spain added the angle that matters most for new entrants: no partner certification is required, only enablement on the distributor marketplace, and the recurring annual certification Adobe used to demand is gone [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:06:49]. The barrier to adding Adobe resale to an existing cloud book is now close to zero.

The structure underneath is consistent across Adobe's own explainers and the distributors running it. Adobe Care describes Direct Partners, also called APC reseller partners, as typically Platinum resellers or distributors who contract with Adobe and plug their marketplaces into it through APIs, while downstream resellers transact through those Direct Partner platforms [LCbK1S1leOQ @ 00:00:35]. The Direct Partner carries the build cost: it is their responsibility to invest in the marketplace platform, either through a third-party platform provider or an in-house team [LCbK1S1leOQ @ 00:02:38]. Support flows the same way. The reseller is the first line, handling onboarding, account management, billing questions and reseller-change requests, and only the technical relationship manager designated by the platform provider can escalate to Adobe technical support, through a contact method that may not be shared with resellers or customers [-rVmsfC5Agk @ 00:03:00]. AppXite pitches its platform exactly into that gap, saying partners who ride it avoid the technical development costs and delays of building and maintaining the Adobe API integration themselves, while running other vendor ecosystems alongside Adobe on the same platform [dhzXos8LyEg @ 00:01:40]. The practical read: a small reseller does not build anything, it rents a Direct Partner's marketplace and owns the customer relationship.

On commercial mechanics, the agreement among the speakers is near-total. Auto-renewal on the customer's anniversary date replaces the old manual renewal chase; AppXite says auto-renewals are on by default and can be switched off per subscription, while increases can be pre-scheduled for the renewal date [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:09:23]. Doris Bing confirmed the renewal kicks in automatically on the anniversary so neither customer nor reseller touches anything that day, and that the partner can toggle it off at any point in the term, including for a customer running a tender [qQoPO5MwZ60 @ 00:13:32]. Pricing still runs on four levels: Mayor Goodway at Bechtle UK said the customer's existing VIP level migrates across and three of the four levels carry volume discounts, with deeper discounts on a three-year commitment [v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:13:03]. And the customer-side surface barely moves. Adobe Care stresses the customer experience stays nearly identical for anyone migrating, with the Admin Console still the place to add licenses, assign and unassign users and monitor storage [LCbK1S1leOQ @ 00:01:28]. For a reseller, that means the migration pitch is easy: same console, same products, same anniversary, with the renewal headache shifted off the reseller's calendar and onto the platform's API layer.

## Section 2: Exact Street Mechanics, Tools & Numbers

Pricing mechanics are where resellers lose orders. Villanueva walked through the level ladder on Stream One: Level 1 is the base tier up to nine units, and prices step down at 10 to 49, 50 to 99 and 100-plus, with Levels 3 and 4 currently carrying the same discount [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:09:09]. Every product shows up as four separate references, one per level, and if a reseller attaches a Level 2 reference to a one-unit order, Adobe rejects it with an error [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:14:26]. Levels are per end customer, exactly like Microsoft licensing, so stacking 50 small customers to buy at Level 3 does not work. AppXite removes that guesswork: its platform queries Adobe through the API, returns the customer's live level, Level 3 in the demo, and flips it to Level 4 the moment a large enough quantity is typed, recalculating the yearly price [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:05:42]. The same screen exposes partner price, suggested retail and margin, lets the seller knock 5 percent off a single deal or lock a permanent 5 percent discount onto a customer, and hides margins and partner price entirely when the customer self-serves [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:07:08]. Quotes go out by email and the customer approves with one button that fires the API call back.

Moving an existing book across is a transfer, not a new order, and getting that wrong spawns a second console. Villanueva warned that placing a fresh order for a customer who already holds a VIP plan creates another VIP plan; instead, the partner opens Adobe Inbound Transfer under Opportunities in Stream One, enters the VIP number, and products, quantities, IDs and the anniversary date all carry over without the customer being notified [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:19:46]. AppXite runs it at scale: it validates every customer's eligibility, sends back a report with reasons for anyone who cannot move yet, then migrates all eligible customers in one batch [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:02:47]. SoftwareOne Benelux runs it as a zero-cost migration order inside its client portal, keyed off the membership ID found in the Admin Console under account contract ID, with a hard instruction to check quantities and leave out any admin-console orders still awaiting fulfilment [6-M7SxMm3Yc @ 00:01:04]. For changing reseller on an existing VIP number, Istiak of Technical Support Online showed the code route: the gaining reseller enters the VIP number and admin email, Adobe mails a code to the customer admin, the code goes into Change Reseller on the customer portal, and the customer with its Illustrator and Photoshop seats then shows up in the new reseller portal [DKHVlefC0YE @ 00:03:18].

Once a customer is live on the marketplace, every clock gets shorter. New licenses carry a 14-day return window; Villanueva contrasted it with VIP's 30 days and the option to return up to 120 days with a penalty, and after day 15 nothing comes back [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:17:35]. Doris Bing added that seats requested from the Admin Console now give the reseller only seven days to place the real order, after which the license simply disappears rather than blocking the console, and that pricing is now prorated daily across 365 or 366 days instead of monthly [qQoPO5MwZ60 @ 00:11:19]. On reductions, SoftwareOne Platform says annual subscriptions can only be downsized in the run-up to the vendor's renewal date, roughly 30 days out, and recommends filing every downsize order two days before the anniversary because time zones shift when auto-renewal actually fires [sNCywL0bfVI @ 00:00:55]. Billing cadence moved too: from February 2026, Adobe invoices partners weekly, so SoftwareOne issued its final monthly invoice on February 1st and its first weekly run on February 2nd, invoicing only in weeks with a transaction [3h2yhifqqWI @ 00:01:25]. SoftwareOne's advice was to warn internal finance and invoice-approval stakeholders ahead of the switch to avoid surprises.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Get Adobe-enabled on a Direct Partner marketplace
   - Request Adobe authorization from your distributor before touching any customer.
   - TD SYNNEX Spain authorizes partners from their customer number in a matter of five minutes; until then the Adobe Inbound Transfer option and Adobe search results simply do not appear [TD SYNNEX Spain — 824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:26:34].
2. Audit every VIP plan against its renewal window
   - Sort the customer book by anniversary date and flag plans inside the window.
   - VIP plans inside the 30-days-before or 30-days-after renewal window cannot be migrated; renew them the usual way, then transfer once the window closes to avoid a duplicated renewal [TD SYNNEX Spain — 824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:21:57].
3. Run the transfer and verify quantities
   - Submit the migration order using the customer's Adobe membership ID.
   - Pull the membership ID from the Admin Console under account contract ID, confirm quantities against the console, exclude pending admin-console orders, and note the migration order itself costs no money [SoftwareOne Benelux — 6-M7SxMm3Yc @ 00:01:19].
4. Preset renewal quantities and opt-outs
   - Book a session with each customer admin before the anniversary to lock renewal settings.
   - Bechtle UK targets the month prior to the anniversary for a one-to-one with each Adobe admin to set renewal quantities and auto-renew status, unticking auto-renewal on anything not being recommitted [Bechtle UK — v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:06:46].

## Where Practitioners Disagree & Alternative Playbooks

The first split is over who actually pushes the migration button. David Villanueva of TD SYNNEX Spain runs the move as a pure partner-side transfer: inside StreamOne he opens Opportunities, picks Adobe Inbound Transfer, keys in the VIP plan number, and states flatly that the end customer has no involvement, will not be notified, and keeps every product, quantity, VIP ID and anniversary date [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:19:46]. Bechtle UK takes the opposite line. Zoe Roby's team generates the customer an admin account on Bechtle Clouds, then the customer must click Transfer Adobe membership and enter their own VIP agreement number, because the Bechtle and Adobe Marketplace terms can only be accepted by a business member or admin [v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:08:45]. Doris Bing of Adobe, speaking on the Pugh Computers webinar, contradicts the silent-transfer framing: on migration day the customer receives an official Adobe communication, so nobody gets moved without knowing [qQoPO5MwZ60 @ 00:15:17]. AppXite pitches a fourth playbook built for volume operators: the platform validates every account's eligibility, sends back a report listing the reason any customer cannot move yet, and after the reseller confirms, migrates the eligible book in a single batch rather than one customer at a time [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:02:47].

Practitioners also disagree on how late an operator can kill or trim an auto-renewal. Doris Bing says auto-renewal can be switched off during the whole period, even two months before renewal when a customer gets nervous or has a tender running, but Bing also warns the 30-day post-anniversary grace is gone, so the renewal order effectively lands on the anniversary date itself [qQoPO5MwZ60 @ 00:13:47]. Villanueva at TD SYNNEX goes tighter, saying the auto-renewal or the item can be cancelled up to one day before the anniversary and it simply will not renew [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:24:30]. SoftwareOne refuses to play that close: because auto-renewal parameters are set by the vendor and timing varies by time zone, they tell operators to complete every downsize order two days before the anniversary to avoid renewing at the wrong quantity [sNCywL0bfVI @ 00:01:11]. Bechtle muddies the water further by telling customers that turning off auto-renew for a product they will not recommit to must be done within the 14-day window, framing cancellation around the return clock rather than the anniversary [v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:13:03]. SoftwareOne also limits annual and multi-year downsizes to roughly the 30 days leading into renewal, while Bechtle says quantities can be raised or lowered in the run-up to the auto-renew date.

On money, the distribution side and the platform side tell different stories. AppXite sells the move with additional financial rebates for early adopters and the advantage of securing long-term customers [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:00:56]. Villanueva at TD SYNNEX deflates that: the product and Adobe's cost are the same as VIP, the price reduction is small and not significant, and TD SYNNEX simply adjusted margins to make Marketplace a little more competitive, pitching a relatively reasonable margin [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:05:13]. Even the price-level story differs. Bechtle describes four levels, three of which carry volume discounts, with deeper cuts on a three-year commitment [v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:13:20], while Villanueva notes Adobe has priced level 3 and level 4 identically for a year and a half to two years, so pushing a customer past 100 units buys no extra discount over level 3. On the end-customer side, Daniel Studios argues most companies should stay on Adobe for Teams: Adobe recommends Teams for 1 to 10 seats, but the cart actually accepts up to 250 licenses, and online information suggests Enterprise usually costs more per license, with single sign-on and negotiated purchasing the main extras [vQ-PbEC-qMw @ 00:03:18]. Unlimited Adobe Stock access on Enterprise, Daniel Studios adds, costs significantly more money.

## Firsthand Failure Modes, Traps & Economics Checks

The classic migration mistake is placing a fresh order for a customer who already holds VIP. Villanueva at TD SYNNEX warns that a new order generates another console and another VIP plan, which is exactly what you do not want; existing customers must go through the transfer flow instead [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:18:14]. The transfer itself is a one-way door: once a plan moves to StreamOne it cannot go back to the reseller console and the old VIP model, though it can still be moved to another distributor or marketplace later [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:21:06]. Timing kills deals too. Plans sitting inside the renewal window, 30 days before or after anniversary, cannot be migrated, because Adobe wants to avoid a renewal duplicated across VIP and Marketplace; the partner renews the old way and migrates after the window closes. SoftwareOne Benelux flags a quieter trap: before submitting the migration order, check quantities against the Adobe admin console, and do not add any order placed through the admin console that is still waiting to be fulfilled, because the migration order is for fully paid licenses only [6-M7SxMm3Yc @ 00:01:04]. Bechtle adds a global wrinkle: Global SKUs were still unavailable under Marketplace then, so customers using them were scheduled to migrate only after Adobe released them [v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:13:51].

The ordering traps cluster around levels and quantities. StreamOne always shows four references for the same product by volume level, and Villanueva warns that attaching a level-two SKU to a single-unit order throws an error because Adobe refuses that price for an incorrect volume; levels also cannot be pooled across end customers [824bcq4wI-Q @ 00:14:26]. AppXite sidesteps this by validating the customer's level against Adobe through the API and recalculating automatically as quantity changes, but warns that the renewal figure is only an estimate since Adobe may change the price list by the renewal date [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:10:01]. AppXite also reminds sellers that quantities cannot be decreased during the commitment period, only increased. On the downsizing side, SoftwareOne says annual and multi-year subscriptions can only be reduced in the period leading up to renewal, roughly 30 days out, and that vendor-set auto-renewal timing varies by time zone [sNCywL0bfVI @ 00:00:55]. Bechtle reports the return window has shrunk to 14 days, which removes the old trick of effectively getting a month of use for free, while TD SYNNEX contrasts it with VIP's 30-day return and up to 120 days with a penalty [v3ZCe1YINus @ 00:04:01]. After day 15, Villanueva says, nothing comes back.

The operational bottlenecks sit outside the order screen. Adobe Care spells out that escalations to Adobe technical support may only originate from the technical relationship manager designated by the marketplace platform provider, and that contact method may not be shared with resellers or customers, so a reseller with a technical problem has to climb the chain through the direct partner [-rVmsfC5Agk @ 00:03:00]. AppXite hits an onboarding snag on the admin email: there can be only one owner email per VIP Marketplace account, and Gmail, Outlook and other private addresses sit on an Adobe domain blacklist, and customers often come back asking to change it [GDHnAQ3r53A @ 00:05:08]. Finance is the next pressure point. SoftwareOne says that starting February 2026 Adobe invoices partners weekly, so customers receive an invoice in every week a new order, renewal or credit memo lands, and finance approvers should be warned ahead of time to avoid surprises [3h2yhifqqWI @ 00:02:04]. On the legacy reseller console, Luís Guzmán shows that setting a custom anniversary end date on an extended VIP agreement to today's date leaves only today to place the order, so the date has to be pushed out, for example five days, to keep room to transact, with 50 months as the maximum term [OvtYYoCkO-w @ 00:03:08].
