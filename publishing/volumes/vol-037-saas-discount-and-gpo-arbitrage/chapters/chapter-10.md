## 1. Build an evidence graph before automating qualification  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a tenant-scoped evidence graph, not a spreadsheet of guessed renewals. Represent each organization, domain, customer authorization, agreement, subscription, invoice, and partner relationship as a separate record with a stable identifier. A scheduled Python worker can query an approved DNS-over-HTTPS endpoint using urllib.request, parse JSON, and record MX and TXT responses with observation time and resolver identity. Microsoft-associated MX records and SPF includes suggest mail infrastructure; they do not establish license quantity, purchasing channel, contract value, or renewal date. Multiple SPF records are an operational anomaly, not proof of a migration opportunity. Use those signals to prioritize a permission-based spend assessment, then collect invoices and renewal schedules through a customer-controlled upload link. Store original documents separately from extracted fields, hash each artifact with hashlib, and preserve the source page supporting every commercial claim. CRM records should distinguish observed facts, customer assertions, model extractions, and reviewer confirmations. A renewal-cliff trigger becomes actionable only when a verified agreement establishes its notice deadline. Calculate outreach dates backward from that deadline, including review time and vendor processing buffers. Missing contractual evidence routes the account to document collection rather than an invented savings estimate.

Map the chapter's twelve plays into explicit workflow lanes: DNS qualification, renewal-cliff detection, authorized CSP margin capture, CPOR association tracking, Adobe VIP Marketplace reconciliation, Telarus residual reconciliation, Avant residual reconciliation, GPO bracket monitoring, benchmark price-floor testing, contingent shared-savings audits, referral distribution, and agency advertising rewards. These are operating lanes, not twelve independent revenue streams; CPOR tracking and benchmarking often support the same earned fee. Microsoft CPOR is a relationship and attribution mechanism for eligible workloads, not a universal license-transfer instruction or entitlement to a reseller spread. Maintain separate records for CSP purchasing authority, eligible CPOR associations, and customer administrative permissions. For the discovery lane, an illustrative 200 reviewed domains at $0.02 each costs $4 before labor; if 20 customers submit authorized invoices, the machine discovery cost is $0.20 per evidence-ready account. This ratio does not include outreach labor or prove conversion. If DNS evidence conflicts with an invoice, preserve both observations and let the invoice control the commercial assessment after verification. Contractors receive only their assigned customer records. The owner controls partner credentials, pricing exceptions, and contract acceptance, keeping a four-person organization from becoming four unrestricted administrators.

Use language models as bounded document processors with no purchasing privileges. Send the minimum invoice excerpt needed to extract currency, SKU, service period, quantity, unit price, term, and cancellation language. Remove bank details and unnecessary personal identifiers before transmission, and use an approved service whose retention terms fit the customer agreement. Require a JSON object containing field values, source quotations, page references, and explicit missing-field flags. Validate types and required keys in Python; perform money calculations with decimal.Decimal rather than accepting model arithmetic. Reject unsupported SKUs, impossible date ranges, conflicting currencies, or totals that fail invoice reconciliation. Treat instructions embedded in uploaded contracts as document content, never as commands to the automation. A reviewer must confirm ambiguous renewal language, customer authority, and any proposed external savings claim. Build a regression set from redacted, authorized examples covering credits, annual commitments billed monthly, midterm seat additions, and overlapping subscriptions. Measure field accuracy separately from extraction completion: a populated renewal date is harmful if it describes an invoice cycle instead of the contractual commitment. When a model service fails, retain the document in a manual extraction queue and continue deterministic reconciliation for previously verified accounts.

## 2. Turn partner events into recoverable billing operations  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Implement a small event pipeline with an HTTPS receiver, durable queue, Python worker, and append-only audit journal. Pax8, Ingram, Telarus, and Adobe-related distributor integrations require agreement-specific access; do not assume every portal exposes a webhook or the same subscription operations. Use documented APIs where available, approved scheduled exports otherwise, and manual portal verification when neither interface supports the required field. Verify webhook signatures according to the provider's specification, reject stale messages when timestamps are supported, and persist accepted events before acknowledging receipt. Build idempotency keys from provider identity, event identifier, and operation type. Store raw event hashes alongside normalized records, then reconcile the latest authoritative state because webhook ordering is unreliable. Python's sqlite3 supports a modest single-writer deployment; transactions and unique constraints prevent duplicate event insertion. Separate ingestion from any external write so a replay cannot create a second order. The job state machine should distinguish received, validated, review-required, authorized, submitted, confirmed, and exception states. Confirmation requires a provider receipt or refreshed authoritative record, not merely an HTTP success response from an intermediary. Keep a customer-visible change log describing what actually changed and who authorized it.

Automate reconciliation aggressively while keeping commercial commitments behind human review. Match each distributor invoice line to the customer agreement, tenant, subscription identifier, SKU, quantity, service dates, and effective price. Illustratively, 100 eligible subscriptions producing $100 monthly retained spread yield $120,000 annual operator revenue, not the face value of customer software purchases. A missing customer payment does not extinguish an annual NCE distributor obligation. Require prefunded terms or an approved ACH or card collection arrangement with advance collection and documented credit limits; a debit authorization alone is not cash. Verify settlement, consider return exposure, and stop new commitments when available coverage falls below the approved threshold. Do not cancel or suspend existing customer service automatically because a collection event failed. Route that decision through the contract owner and applicable provider procedures. For Adobe VIP Marketplace, preserve membership, anniversary, transaction, and distributor identifiers rather than repurposing Microsoft fields. For Telarus or Avant, reconcile supplier payments, agent splits, eligibility, and clawback periods. An illustrative 150 accounts at $100 monthly retained residual produce $180,000 annually only if the executed agreements and collected statements support those amounts. Forecast residuals separately from earned revenue.

Design recovery before increasing throughput. Retry timeouts and temporary server errors with exponential backoff, jitter, and the provider's rate-limit guidance; stop automatic retries on authorization failures or rejected commercial instructions. After an ambiguous order timeout, query authoritative order status before resubmitting anything. Put exhausted jobs in a dead-letter queue with customer impact, evidence links, retry history, and a named owner. A daily reconciliation compares provider exports against local subscriptions and commissions, catching events that never arrived. Maintain a manual fallback worksheet containing verified identifiers, requested operation, reviewer identity, and confirmation receipt; import the resulting evidence afterward instead of bypassing the ledger. Test restoration from backups and replay events into a nonproduction environment without external write credentials. Give each integration a narrow credential, rotate it according to provider capabilities, and keep secrets outside prompts, application logs, and shared worksheets. Contractor permissions should expire with the engagement. The owner reviews every license transfer, new annual commitment, rebate dispute settlement, and bank-detail change. Routine matched invoice lines may clear automatically, but a tenfold quantity jump, changed beneficiary, or new tenant identifier must stop that path. This architecture scales reconciliation without outsourcing commercial judgment to a language model.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Normalize the authorized event
   - Validate the provider message and resolve its customer-scoped identifiers.
   - Persist the source hash, provider event identifier, receipt time, and applicable agreement version before processing.
2. Reconcile the proposed economics
   - Compare subscription state, customer authority, collected coverage, and retained contribution.
   - Use Decimal calculations and effective dated schedules; route mismatched quantities or unsupported program assumptions to review.
3. Authorize the consequential operation
   - Have the designated reviewer approve the specific transaction packet.
   - Record reviewer identity and approved payload hash; changed payloads require a fresh review rather than inherited permission.
4. Confirm and reconcile completion
   - Retrieve authoritative confirmation and update the customer evidence ledger.
   - Investigate ambiguous results before retrying; compare the next provider statement with the confirmed transaction.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four checks before consequential execution**
> 
> Automation may prepare the transaction packet. An identified reviewer must establish authority and economics before execution.
> - Verify customer authority, destination tenant, eligible program route, and the exact operation permitted by the signed agreement.
> - Verify current tier pricing, retained spread, rebate conditions, and every customer pass-through obligation against dated evidence.
> - Verify collected coverage for annual obligations, payment-return exposure, and documented credit limits before creating new commitments.
> - Verify subscription quantities, effective dates, cancellation constraints, and a recovery procedure that does not depend on reversing an irreversible order.

## 3. Price automation against retained contribution, not customer spend  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Each comparison must match SKU, edition, seat quantity, geography, currency, commitment length, billing cadence, support scope, and eligibility date. Store quote expiry and evidence provenance. Separate seat removal, product substitution, negotiated discount, and rebate effects so the same reduction cannot be claimed twice. An illustrative customer's eligible annual baseline of $120,000 compared with $100,000 of equivalent delivered service creates $20,000 of verified savings. At a contracted 30% fee, operator revenue is $6,000 and customer savings after that fee are $14,000. Add migration charges or lost benefits before assessing savings if the agreement requires them. The 25% to 35% fee range is a commercial parameter, not permission to choose the highest percentage retroactively. A price floor should cover distributor cost, delivery effort, expected credit loss, and required contribution after client rebates. If a partner reduces a rebate by $3 per seat across 1,000 eligible seats monthly, annual contribution falls $36,000 unless another contractually valid adjustment offsets it. Freeze affected quotes and reprice prospectively; never hide the compression inside fabricated benchmark savings.

## 4. Compound verified distribution without automating promises  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Create three distribution loops from verified operations. The programmatic loop publishes narrowly scoped renewal guides and calculators using approved pricing assumptions, dated evidence, and explicit inputs; it never exposes private partner schedules or customer invoices. The referral loop gives satisfied customers a shareable assessment link after a verified outcome, with permission to use any named testimonial and clear disclosure of referral compensation where relevant. The outbound loop routes evidence-supported accounts into human-approved email sequences and lawful calling workflows. A public MX record does not establish permission for automated marketing texts or prerecorded calls. Apply 47 U.S.C. 227 and 47 CFR 64.1200 to the actual calling technology, number type, and consent context. Commercial email must satisfy 15 U.S.C. 7704, including accurate headers, a valid postal address, and timely opt-out processing. Maintain suppression records across the CRM and every sending provider. The FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule does not give business outreach a blanket exemption from deception restrictions; review 16 CFR 310.3 and 310.6 against the campaign. Have a person approve initial claims and every material template change, then pause sequences automatically when consent records, source evidence, or suppression synchronization fail.

Treat agency_ad_spend_card_multiplier_rebate_cycling as an adjacent, separately tracked play, not a mechanism for inflating SaaS revenue. Route only genuine, client-authorized advertising purchases through an eligible agency payment arrangement whose issuer, advertising platform, and customer contracts permit the activity. An illustrative $100,000 monthly advertising budget earning a realizable 2% reward produces $2,000 of reward value. Deduct $800 processing expense, $300 funding cost, $600 contractual client pass-through, and $100 expected loss allowance; only $200 remains before overhead and tax treatment. Exclude points that cannot be redeemed under the operating plan, and do not stack overlapping reward assumptions without contractual support. That $100,000 is client advertising spend, not brokerage revenue, and the $200 is not added to the canonical cases presented here. A Python reconciliation job can match authorized campaign invoices, settled card transactions, reward statements, and client credits while flagging caps and excluded merchant categories. People approve funding exposure and arrangement changes. Never create artificial purchases, circular payments, or extra advertising merely to earn rewards. If issuer eligibility changes or contribution turns negative, move to client-direct billing or prefunded approved agency terms and stop rewards-dependent spending immediately.

Run distribution as a cohort ledger with explicit economic stopping rules. Illustratively, 1,000 qualified guide visits producing 50 assessment requests, 20 evidence-complete audits, and four signed customers imply a 5% request rate, 40% document-completion rate, and 20% audit-to-customer rate. At $1,200 campaign cost, acquisition cost is $300 per signed customer before assessment labor. If each customer produces $1,000 first-year retained revenue, the cohort contributes $4,000 before acquisition, fulfillment, and overhead, not $4,000 owner profit. Track refunds, payment delays, churn, and contractor time by cohort before increasing spend. The full-time owner controls partner relationships, exception economics, and consequential approvals; three contractors cover evidence operations, reconciliation, and distribution quality under documented scopes. Review weekly samples by risk stratum, not just at random: include every new vendor adapter, changed model prompt, disputed savings calculation, and unusually large commitment. Quarantine an adapter when discrepancies exceed its predeclared tolerance, revert to approved exports, and replay corrected events after testing. Publish only refreshed calculators and substantiated outcomes. The flywheel compounds because each completed engagement improves authorized evidence, benchmark coverage, and referral credibility, while the human team retains control over obligations that software cannot safely unwind.
