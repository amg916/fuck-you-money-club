## 1. Sell authorized purchasing leverage, not software ownership  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

SaaS discount brokerage converts purchasing access, renewal timing, and contract execution into retained fees. An operator finds a business paying an avoidable retail rate, verifies its subscriptions and commitment dates, and routes eligible purchases through an authorized reseller, distributor, technology services distributor, or purchasing group. The customer receives a documented price improvement or a better purchasing process; the operator earns an agreed spread, residual, administrative rebate, or savings fee. Nothing requires buying the customer's company, owning its software intellectual property, or treating its entire subscription budget as operator sales. A $120,000 customer software budget can support a $12,000 annual retained spread, but the scorecard records $12,000, not $120,000. The distinction determines valuation, staffing capacity, and whether acquisition spending actually pays back. Five practical archetypes appear below: authorized reseller intermediary, UCaaS referral agent, GPO administrator, renewal savings auditor, and comparison-led broker. Each needs a different agreement and evidence package. Combining them is useful only when contracts permit the combination and the same economic benefit is not counted twice. Start with one purchasing motion, one buyer profile, and one renewal window before adding another commission source. This keeps delivery exceptions observable and makes the first customer reference genuinely reusable.

The channel vocabulary matters because similar labels do different jobs. Microsoft CSP is a commercial distribution framework; CPOR is a partner association used for eligible activities and incentives, not a universal command that transfers every subscription or billing relationship. Adobe VIP Marketplace has its own reseller and distributor procedures. Zoom Up, RingCentral channel programs, and Telarus or Avant arrangements depend on product eligibility, geography, supplier participation, and written compensation schedules. Pax8, Ingram Micro Cloud, and TD SYNNEX should be evaluated against the actual SKU, country, term, and support obligation, rather than treated as interchangeable discount warehouses. Obtain the customer's authority and the applicable transfer instructions before promising an invoice change. Target zero downtime, but distinguish a billing relationship change from tenant migration, number porting, identity changes, or data movement. A distributor portal showing an attractive percentage does not establish eligibility or customer savings. Reconcile renewal dates, existing annual commitments, promotional expirations, and required support coverage first. When the current contract cannot move, sell an authorized renewal review and schedule the later transition. Do not bridge the gap by sharing accounts, pooling unrelated tenants, or inventing a cancellation right the existing agreement does not provide. The service is disciplined purchasing execution, not circumvention of license boundaries.

This chapter's 12 mapped plays comprise two supplied play identifiers and ten descriptive operating mappings, rather than ten invented catalog identifiers. The supplied plays are saas_cpor_vip_distributor_margin_split and competitor_intent_comparison_hijacking. The additional mappings are Microsoft renewal alignment, Adobe seat-rightsizing transfers, Google Workspace reseller renewals, Zoom master-agent referrals, RingCentral residual management, DocuSign usage-tier negotiations, GPO bracket aggregation, contingent renewal audits, technographic renewal sourcing, and distributor settlement reconciliation. The comparison matrix provides unit economics for all twelve and four execution variants. These are commercial work patterns, not claims that every named supplier currently offers the illustrated compensation. For example, a $20,000 verified annual saving produces a $6,000 fee at 30%; an eligible $100,000 purchasing bracket produces a $2,000 administrative rebate at an agreed 2%. The auditor must specify which savings count, and the administrator must establish which purchases qualify. Neither can count speculative future expansion as earned revenue. A durable small team builds reusable authorization packets and settlement checks around these narrow motions. Beginner hype starts with a headline discount and works backward toward a customer promise. The correct sequence starts with enforceable rights, measurable customer value, and cash collection, then selects the acquisition channel that can reliably reach the next eligible buyer.

## 2. Reconcile retained revenue, customer savings, and owner profit  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Formula v2.0 uses retained operator economics, even where its scenario input is labeled gross revenue. For this candidate, that input means net retained Partner-of-Record wholesale margin spreads, master-agent evergreen residuals, GPO administrative rebates, and contingent shared-savings fees; it excludes pass-through customer software subscription spend. Apply a retained_share conversion only when the starting amount is an underlying commissionable or purchasing base. A $100,000 purchasing base times an illustrative 12% retained share yields $12,000 of operator revenue. Once the ledger already contains that $12,000 fee, do not multiply it by 12% again. ASC 606 principal-versus-agent analysis examines whether the entity controls the specified service before transfer; collecting cash or holding a reseller label alone does not settle the question. If the actual contract makes the operator principal, statutory books may require gross presentation and associated service costs. Maintain a bridge to the scorecard's retained basis rather than misstating those books. Record supplier pass-through amounts, operator entitlement, customer credits, and tax collections separately. Recognition follows satisfied performance obligations and the applicable variable-consideration assessment, not merely the date a distributor dashboard displays an estimated rebate. Keep cash received, revenue earned, and collectible receivables as three separate reporting views.

The headline distributor play targets 15%–28% wholesale channel tiers, 8%–14% customer invoice reductions, and 8%–15% recurring spreads, but those ranges cannot be combined indiscriminately. Use one compatible price basis. With an illustrative $100 retail reference, a 22% wholesale reduction produces $78 cost; a 10% customer reduction produces $90 billing. The retained difference is $12, equal to 12% of reference spend or 13.33% of customer billing. On 1,000 eligible monthly units, that becomes $90,000 billed, $78,000 supplier cost, and $12,000 retained monthly before operating expenses. In contrast, a 15% wholesale reduction combined with a 14% customer reduction leaves only $1 per $100 reference, before fees or support. Quote a dollar waterfall, not overlapping percentage slogans. For contingent audits, a normalized $120,000 annual baseline reduced to $100,000 creates $20,000 gross savings; a 30% fee is $6,000, leaving $14,000 customer benefit before other implementation costs. Across the stated 25%–35% fee band, the same verified saving supports $5,000–$7,000 of fees. If a distributor rebate already funds the negotiated reduction, specify whether the audit agreement permits another fee. Disclose the complete compensation stack and never describe the customer's gross saving as its net benefit after fees.

The canonical Conservative case is $100,000 retained annual revenue at 32% net margin, producing $32,000 owner net profit with 1 FT + 3 C. Base is $500,000 at 45.0%, producing $225,000 with the same team. Scaled is $900,000 at 58%, producing $522,000 with 2 FT + 4 C. The implied annual expense envelopes are $68,000, $275,000, and $378,000 respectively; these reconcile the supplied cases without introducing a replacement forecast. The stated $1,500–$9,500 monthly operating range is a planning input, not a complete scenario expense bridge: identify which staffing, acquisition, delivery, and other costs sit outside that range rather than forcing unlike measures to match. Startup capital of $5,000–$30,000 and a one-to-three-month first-deal interval likewise describe planning bounds, not supplier commitments. Cash exposure deserves a separate schedule. An annual NCE commitment can leave supplier obligations outstanding after a customer stops paying monthly. Obtain agreed prefunding and authorized ACH or card auto-debit, but recognize that revocations, returns, and chargebacks remain possible. Pausing new commitments limits additional exposure; it does not extinguish existing liabilities. Model owner distributions only after taxes, reserves, and cash timing are separately understood, without silently changing the scorecard's stated owner-profit figures.

## 4. Build the evidence and collection system that makes the spread durable  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The defensible asset is a compact operating system linking customer authority, supplier rights, renewal timing, and settlement evidence. Store each subscription's customer entity, tenant identifier, SKU, quantity, commitment end date, transfer status, supplier cost, customer price, and compensation basis in a controlled register. HubSpot or a similarly configured CRM can hold commercial milestones; distributor exports and an accounting ledger provide transaction evidence. Automate CPOR-related association checks where supported, Adobe VIP billing reconciliation, and supplier statement matching through authorized APIs or exports. Do not assume that Pax8, Ingram, and Telarus share one interface or identical permission scopes. Use read access where sufficient, limit service-account rights, and log changes to billing instructions. A benchmark engine should compare the same edition, region, term, support bundle, and quantity before flagging a savings opportunity. Preserve price-floor approvals from the actual supplier and recalculate spread when incentives expire. A well-maintained register lets a contractor process ordinary renewals while the full-time operator handles exceptions. Without it, every deal becomes a bespoke investigation and the claimed small-team advantage disappears. Concentrate automation on repeatable evidence collection and arithmetic, leaving disputed eligibility and contract interpretation with the responsible decision-maker. Make every automated change reversible where the supplier supports reversal.

Protect the moat by separating four risks that beginners combine into one margin percentage. Commercial risk is the supplier changing a tier, incentive, or commission schedule. Credit risk is the customer failing to fund a commitment. Execution risk is a rejected transfer, broken number port, or misquoted SKU. Recognition risk is booking contingent compensation before the entitlement is sufficiently established. Assign an owner and an evidence requirement to each. For a $10,000 monthly residual book, a 20% commission reduction removes $2,000 monthly before any change in team costs; this sensitivity illustrates exposure rather than replacing the canonical scenarios. Maintain reserves for contractually specified chargebacks and review supplier concentration before accepting another large account. Call an evergreen residual recurring only while the underlying agreement, eligible service, attribution, and customer payments remain in force. Negotiate account ownership, post-termination commissions, assignment rights, and dispute deadlines in writing. A spreadsheet of expected renewals cannot substitute for these rights. When a condition fails, stop the affected new order, preserve existing service where contractually possible, and communicate the corrected economics to the customer. Never repair a weak spread by silently removing required support or license rights. That turns a purchasing service into an avoidable customer loss.

Launch by choosing one sub-vertical and proving a complete closed loop before hiring for volume. Obtain the distributor or master-agent agreement, prepare the customer authorization packet, and build one normalized savings worksheet. Run a small qualified outreach batch with documented suppression handling and accurate brand references. The first pilot should produce an accepted order, an intact service check, a reconciled supplier invoice, and collected operator compensation. Track elapsed days between those events because a nominally profitable deal can still consume scarce startup cash. Review the first renewal or commission adjustment before describing the process as fully repeatable. In the Conservative and Base staffing models, contractor roles can cover sourcing research, billing reconciliation, and authorized implementation, while the full-time operator owns commercial decisions. The Scaled model adds a second full-time role and fourth contractor without requiring every function to become a department. Use explicit handoffs rather than shared administrator credentials. Report retained revenue, verified customer benefit, uncovered commitment exposure, unresolved statement differences, and owner profit separately each month. Those five measures show whether growth strengthens the business or merely increases money moving through its accounts. The operating objective is a repeatable, collectible entitlement with low exception volume, not the largest software budget displayed in a sales presentation.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Establish an auditable purchasing baseline
   - Collect customer-authorized invoices, SKU quantities, amendments, and renewal dates.
   - Normalize editions, usage, support, taxes, and commitment terms; record which changes count toward a shared-savings fee.
2. Confirm executable channel economics
   - Obtain written supplier eligibility, transfer procedures, and compensation terms.
   - Calculate customer billing, supplier cost, retained entitlement, customer net savings, and outstanding credit exposure on one basis.
3. Execute the authorized customer transition
   - Secure funding and customer authority before submitting the eligible order.
   - Follow the actual distributor or supplier process; test service continuity and preserve incumbent coverage when the transition requires it.
4. Close the settlement and renewal loop
   - Reconcile invoices, collected compensation, credits, and the next commitment date.
   - Apply the documented recognition policy, isolate disputed amounts, and assign every billing or service exception to a named responsible role.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Verify four facts before placing the first commitment**
> 
> Proceed only when the specific transaction has evidence for authority, economics, collection, and service continuity.
> - Verify the customer's legal entity, authorized signer, tenant ownership, and applicable CSP, CPOR association, VIP, or supplier transfer process.
> - Verify the written wholesale or commission schedule, customer billing basis, retained-share calculation, and expiry of every incentive used in the quote.
> - Verify agreed prefunding, payment authorization, remaining annual commitment exposure, and the response to ACH returns or card disputes.
> - Verify the implementation plan, incumbent service preservation, porting or tenant dependencies, and post-change tests appropriate to the actual product.
