## 1. Identify the payer, the economic buyer, and the person who can bind the budget  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The check writer changes with the contract, even when the same CFO approves the purchase. In an authorized CSP resale, the customer pays the reseller and the reseller owes its distributor; in a master-agent arrangement, the supplier or technology services distributor pays the contracted residual. A GPO supplier pays an administrative rebate on eligible member purchases, while the member pays a separately agreed shared-savings fee. Procurement can sponsor every one of these transactions without possessing signing or bank authority. Build an authority map naming the economic buyer, IT license administrator, authorized signer, accounts-payable owner, and treasury approver. Obtain the purchase-order requirement before negotiating the renewal, because an enthusiastic department head cannot repair a rejected invoice after the subscription starts. The acute pain is usually a dated renewal increase, unused capacity, duplicate tooling, or an unsupported license assignment, not a statute forcing somebody to hire a broker. Microsoft NCE commitments intensify that pain by separating payment frequency from contractual duration. Sell a documented reduction in total cost with uninterrupted entitlement, not a magical transfer that erases an existing commitment. Ask treasury when funds can actually leave the account, not merely when the budget becomes available.

The mandatory play private_b2b_gpo_umbrella_rebate_arbitrage converts fragmented purchases into authorized purchasing brackets, not a shared tenant or a pool of transferable licenses. Across hundreds of member companies, a verified 10,000-seat book at $120 annual eligible spend per seat represents $1,200,000 of purchasing volume; a contracted 3% supplier administrative rebate yields $36,000 before operating expenses. At 75,000 seats the same unit spend represents $9,000,000, and a hypothetical 12% bracket would yield $1,080,000. That upper illustration is neither a published Una or OMNIA entitlement nor the canonical Scaled forecast. Validate program access, eligible products, returns, taxes, supplier exclusions, and the exact rebate base. Pair quarter-end price-floor testing with a member's own invoice evidence, using Vendr or Tropic materials only through permitted access. A $100,000 like-for-like baseline reduced to $90,000 creates $10,000 of savings and a $3,000 fee at 30%. A separate $3,600 supplier rebate retained by the operator is disclosed compensation, not another $3,600 of member savings. If eligibility or the baseline is disputed, suspend contested fees, reconcile with both parties, refund unearned collections, and place affected members on eligible purchasing agreements rather than defending an unsupported bracket.

Treat the twelve mapped plays as payer-specific operating routes rather than twelve promises of unrestricted vendor access. The first seven are private GPO umbrella rebates, Microsoft CSP wholesale spreads, Adobe VIP Marketplace renewal spreads, Zoom master-agent residuals, UCaaS expansion residuals, SaaS renewal shared savings, and parcel or telecom GPO rebates. The remaining five are quarterly benchmark true-ups, license right-sizing audits, authorized partner-attribution recovery, renewal-cliff sourcing, and the specified UCC refinance-trigger route. Only the two supplied machine identifiers designate named source plays; the other labels describe concrete implementations of this volume's authorized channels. Show each buyer who funds the economics. A procurement director values an auditable comparison that survives a CFO challenge; an IT director values retained access and a reversible transition; a supplier values incremental, attributable purchasing. Never let the broker's commission quietly determine the recommendation. Disclose supplier compensation before selection, preserve an alternative quote, and separate service acceptance from compensation eligibility. A board resolution, delegated-authority schedule, or countersigned purchasing agreement can evidence authority; a sales meeting cannot. Where a customer requires escrow, verify the escrow agent's authority and release instructions separately from the buyer's ability to approve the underlying subscription budget.

## 2. Match eight payment structures to commitment risk and settlement evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Separate the transport mechanism from the commercial condition attached to it. Wire and ACH move money; a retainer defines advance compensation; escrow and milestone holds allocate release authority; factoring sells or finances a receivable. Platform checkout bundles authorization, processing, and merchant rules. Statutory disbursement applies only when an actual legal process controls distribution, not because a broker labels an invoice protected. For an annual NCE purchase, the cleanest small-team posture is a customer-funded commitment before activation, with an authorized ACH or card instruction for subsequent eligible charges. Monthly auto-debit alone does not fund an annual liability. If the customer pays $10,000 monthly while the distributor commitment totals $110,400 annually, the operator may still face most of that obligation after a second-month default. Obtain full-term funding, documented third-party credit support, or a supplier-direct structure that leaves the commitment with the supplier. A card authorization is not cash, and a bank credit is not proof against every return, dispute, or fraud recovery. Record transaction identifiers, account ownership, funding status, and the operational release decision. Never tell fulfillment that a screenshot, remittance email, or pending checkout authorizes an irreversible subscription order.

For business ACH, collect an agreement identifying the legal entity, account, debit amount or calculation method, timing, and revocation procedure; obtain the bank's required authorization evidence and processing configuration. Validate account ownership through the processor's supported verification method and avoid storing raw banking credentials in the CRM. Corporate entries and consumer entries have different return frameworks, so do not quote one universal finality window. Regulation E primarily concerns consumer accounts; 12 CFR 1005.10 is relevant when a preauthorized consumer transfer actually falls within that coverage. Corporate ACH rights also depend on network rules and banking agreements. Wire instructions deserve a known-number callback because compromised supplier email can redirect an otherwise valid payment. UCC Article 4A, including section 4A-202 as enacted by the relevant state, makes agreed security procedures material to certain commercial payment orders. Use dual approval for beneficiary changes and compare the beneficiary entity with the executed contract. Stripe or another supported checkout provider can simplify invoices and receipts, but its dispute and reserve terms still govern. Reconcile processor fees separately from supplier costs; neither should disappear inside a casually described net deposit.

Escrow works only when the provider accepts this transaction type, identifies the parties, and receives executable release conditions. For a $6,000 savings engagement, a documented $2,000 advance and $4,000 acceptance-linked balance can protect working capital without pretending that subscription entitlements are physical goods with universal inspection rules. State exactly whether the advance earns against delivered work or is refundable until a specified event. Keep any required segregated funds outside the operating account and do not market an ordinary bank balance as escrow. Factoring a $20,000 accepted invoice at an illustrative 90% advance supplies $18,000 initially; a $600 fee and the remaining reserve must be reconciled under the factor's actual recourse terms. Customer acceptance, assignment restrictions, and dilution matter more than the headline advance. A milestone hold protects a buyer but must leave enough funded cash to satisfy any upstream commitment. Statutory distributions, such as bankruptcy priority payments under 11 U.S.C. 507, are recovery contexts rather than routine SaaS collection rails. Do not extend credit expecting priority treatment. Select the structure that matches the obligation, and price its collection delay, reserve requirement, and dispute burden into the deal before signing.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Map authority and contractual liability
   - Identify the signer, bank approver, supplier creditor, and party responsible for the full subscription term.
   - Attach delegated authority, purchasing requirements, eligible program terms, and the exact commitment amount to the deal record.
2. Verify funding and release instructions
   - Confirm account ownership, independently verify changed beneficiaries, and establish the permitted funding method.
   - Record processor or bank evidence and any escrow release conditions; a pending payment must not trigger irreversible fulfillment.
3. Activate only the authorized transaction
   - Match funded orders to customer tenant identifiers, eligible SKUs, supplier terms, and documented transfer permissions.
   - Use the program's actual transfer process; partner attribution records alone do not establish license or billing transfer authority.
4. Reconcile cash, compensation, and exceptions
   - Match customer receipts, supplier obligations, earned fees, reserves, and refunds to a single deal identifier.
   - Route discrepancies to a named owner and deadline; suspend contested compensation while preserving undisputed service obligations.

## 3. Make the fee enforceable through attribution, savings evidence, and narrow protection  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Fee protection begins with a defined service and evidence of performance, not an expansive restriction on the customer's purchasing freedom. The agreement should name covered entities, suppliers, products, baseline dates, measurement period, accepted exclusions, compensation rate, invoice trigger, and records needed for verification. Define a narrow attribution period for introduced opportunities and a bounded post-termination tail for documented work, subject to applicable law and the supplier agreement. Do not claim ownership of every future software purchase. For savings, normalize quantities, service levels, currency, contract duration, credits, implementation costs, and existing discounts. A $120,000 baseline reduced to $108,000 but requiring $3,000 migration work produces $9,000 of net first-year savings under a total-cost definition; a 35% contingent fee is $3,150, not $4,200. Document whether avoidable seat reductions qualify and whether savings must be realized before billing. If supplier compensation also exists, show its recipient and treatment in the same waterfall. Provide a review period, a specific objection process, and partial payment of undisputed amounts. Keep access to supporting invoices proportionate to the measurement task rather than demanding unrestricted tenant access as a substitute for a clear fee definition.

Authorized partner attribution protects supplier-paid economics but does not replace a customer contract. Microsoft CPOR, CSP reseller relationships, and administrative access mechanisms serve different purposes; CPOR alone does not transfer a license, invoice, or annual obligation. Adobe VIP Marketplace changes must follow that program's actual reseller and renewal procedures. For Telarus or Avant opportunities, obtain the applicable supplier schedule and confirm customer attribution, commissionable charges, payment trigger, clawbacks, and treatment after termination. A $600 monthly residual can look evergreen while remaining contingent on the customer paying and maintaining eligible service. Maintain a compensation ledger with source order, customer identifier, eligible recurring charge, rate, effective date, collected amount, and dispute deadline. Compare supplier statements against the ledger monthly. If three eligible months at $600 are omitted, the recovery claim is $1,800 supported by orders and statements, not an estimate based on the customer's entire technology budget. Submit disputes through the contracted channel, stop accruing unsupported amounts, and reconcile credits before distributing commissions. An integration from Pax8 or Ingram can transport records, but automation cannot create missing authority, repair an ineligible product, or guarantee a payout that the signed supplier schedule excludes.

The second mandatory source play, ucc1_expiration_refi_trigger_arbitrage, is a sourcing adjacency rather than permission to treat financing principal as SaaS revenue. State Secretary of State UCC filings can identify secured-credit relationships, but a financing statement normally does not establish loan maturity, current balance, or whether an MCA exists. UCC section 9-515 generally gives a financing statement five years of effectiveness, with exceptions; continuation timing is not a payoff date. Use a filing to prioritize verification, then obtain borrower-authorized documents before describing a business as four to nine months into expensive financing. Illustratively, 40 verified prospects, 10 qualified conversations, and two accepted referrals at a contractually permitted $750 each produce $1,500 of separate referral revenue, not the $200,000 of financing arranged. Exclude that adjacent revenue from this volume's canonical retained SaaS cases. Verify licensing, referral compensation, and applicable commercial-financing disclosures before introducing a provider. California Financial Code section 22800 and following addresses covered commercial-financing offers. If automated outreach or referral eligibility fails, use direct mail, compliant B2B work-email sequences, and manual human-dialed outreach with applicable suppression checks; partner with an eligible provider instead of improvising a financing offer.

## 4. Screen counterparties, reserve exposure, and reconcile the canonical profit cases  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Counterparty screening should answer whether the named entity can fund the commitment and whether the person signing can bind it. Verify registration, operating address, beneficial ownership where the payment provider requires it, signer authority, and consistency between invoice entity and bank account. Obtain proportionate financial evidence for credit requests: recent statements supplied with permission, payment references, aging reports, and business credit information from services such as Dun & Bradstreet or Experian Business. Public liens and UCC filings are leads for questions, not automatic proof of insolvency. Avoid unsupported personal credit pulls; consumer reports require a permissible purpose under 15 U.S.C. 1681b. Set exposure limits against unfunded contractual cost, not expected commission. On a $110,400 annual distributor commitment, $9,200 of initial customer funding leaves $101,200 of upstream cost unfunded before other protections. A high-margin sales presentation does not cure that balance-sheet risk. Require additional funding or a credit-supported alternative before ordering. Monitor ACH returns, late supplier remittances, rapid seat additions, entity changes, and repeated requests to switch payment accounts. Escalate new commitments immediately while handling existing service rights under the actual contract, rather than assuming that suspension cancels annual liabilities.

Reconcile the Formula v2.0 cases using the supplied retained-revenue basis, even where the scorecard labels its input gross revenue. Conservative is $100,000 revenue, $68,000 total modeled costs, and $32,000 owner net profit at 32%, supported by one full-time operator and three contractors. Base is $500,000 revenue, $275,000 costs, and $225,000 profit at 45.0% with the same staffing count. Scaled is $900,000 revenue, $378,000 costs, and $522,000 profit at 58% with two full-time people and four contractors. These cost totals are arithmetic reconciliations, not newly supplied expense allocations. The $1,500–$9,500 monthly opex range is a planning input, not evidence that every modeled cost fits within that range. The $5,000–$30,000 startup capital range likewise cannot collateralize unlimited annual commitments. Separate client subscription collections from retained compensation. For $120,000 customer billings against $110,400 distributor cost, the modeled retained spread is $9,600. Under ASC 606, evaluate principal-versus-agent status for each specified service; contractual control determines accounting presentation, not a broker label. Where agency treatment applies, recognize the earned net fee rather than customer billings. If a transaction requires principal presentation, preserve a documented bridge back to this scorecard's retained contribution measure without changing its three profit cases.

Build a monthly close around earned compensation, not dashboard pipeline value. For each deal, reconcile supplier statements, customer acceptance, processor settlements, refunds, chargebacks, and any unearned advances. Estimate constrained variable compensation and reserves using the applicable accounting policy; do not book a maximum rebate tier before its conditions are supported. A $36,000 annual rebate forecast with only $9,000 substantiated for the quarter does not authorize distributing the full annual amount. Track days to collection and expected clawback exposure by payer, because one distributor can concentrate cash risk across many otherwise unrelated customers. Outreach controls also protect collections by preventing disputed acquisition practices. The FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule contains B2B exemptions and exceptions, including specified prohibitions that reach B2B calls; do not treat business status as blanket immunity. TCPA restrictions depend on call technology, destination, purpose, and other facts, while CAN-SPAM governs covered commercial email. Manual dialing is a channel choice, not a universal exemption from calling restrictions. Finish each close with an exception owner, a resolution date, and a decision on new exposure. Profit becomes distributable cash only after upstream obligations, earned refunds, and prudent reserves remain funded.
