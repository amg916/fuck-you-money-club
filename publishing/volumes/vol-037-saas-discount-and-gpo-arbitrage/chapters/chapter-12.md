## 1. Days 1–7: Establish Twelve Plays Without Building Twelve Businesses  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a narrow buyer: organizations with 50–250 employees, a named subscription owner, and a documented renewal inside 120 days. Candidate #37 combines brokerage and aggregation, but the launch pod cannot operate twelve independent sales motions. Use this chapter's working map of twelve plays: P01 DNS/MX/SPF account qualification; P02 renewal-cliff discovery; P03 Microsoft CSP authorized procurement; P04 Microsoft CPOR attribution where the applicable program supports it; P05 Adobe VIP Marketplace procurement; P06 Zoom Up partner sourcing; P07 Telarus master-agent residuals; P08 Avant master-agent residuals; P09 private GPO membership aggregation; P10 GPO volume-bracket administrative rebates; P11 benchmark price-floor audits; and P12 contingent renewal shared-savings collection. These are an operating decomposition of the volume's stated mechanisms, rather than twelve asserted vendor entitlements. P01 and P02 feed every route; P11 tests whether an offer creates measurable value. Select one authorized fulfillment route for the first customer and leave alternatives documented but inactive. DNS records can suggest a service provider, not license counts, contract ownership, renewal dates, or purchasing authority. Require customer evidence before quoting. By Day 2, every target record must distinguish observed technographics from customer-confirmed facts, with source dates attached so stale signals never become promises.

Give the full-time founder ownership of qualification, proposals, partner escalations, and cash decisions. Contractor C1 handles discovery and renewal research; C2 handles authorized channel operations and entitlement checks; C3 handles savings verification, bookkeeping preparation, and collections reconciliation. Reserve 40 founder hours weekly and cap each contractor at eight paid hours across the first month. At illustrative rates of $35, $60, and $55, those contractor commitments total $1,200. This is a four-person pod, not four full-time salaries. Buy additional hours only from collected contribution after the founder documents the bottleneck. Establish a $5,000 startup allocation comprising $1,500 of first-month operating cash, $1,000 of setup work, and $2,500 of untouched contingency. The operating allowance includes contractors, $200 of CRM and document tooling, and $100 of banking and communications costs. The setup allowance covers entity, contract, and accounting configuration within a written scope. Geography-dependent costs that exceed this envelope require a revised allocation before commitments. Keep founder living expenses outside this business budget and disclose that the cash ceiling excludes imputed founder compensation. Record all founder hours nevertheless; otherwise apparently attractive customer economics merely hide unpaid labor and cannot support a later hiring decision.

Use Days 3–7 to prove that the intended route exists for the actual customer. Obtain written partner economics, applicable program terms, payout timing, reversal provisions, and customer eligibility directly from the chosen distributor or master agent. Pax8 and Ingram offerings depend on territory, product, and enrollment; Telarus and Avant agreements likewise determine which suppliers and residuals the brokerage can access. CPOR attribution does not itself transfer subscription billing or establish reseller rights. Keep attribution evidence separate from any CSP transfer workflow and require the current distributor's procedure for the customer's subscription. For annual Microsoft NCE commitments, default to arrangements where an authorized supplier bills the customer directly and bears the subscription credit exposure. If the brokerage becomes the obligated reseller, require settled funding covering the full noncancelable obligation before ordering; monthly auto-debit alone does not cover an annual liability. Account for payment reversals and reserve requirements before treating funding as usable. For Adobe, verify VIP Marketplace eligibility and permitted migration timing. For GPO activity, execute member authority and rebate disclosure before submitting aggregate volume. Never combine unrelated tenants into an unauthorized pooled license inventory to manufacture a lower price.

## 2. Days 8–14: Turn Renewal Evidence Into Auditable Customer Offers  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build a fifty-account research cohort by Day 8, but contact only prospects whose source, outreach channel, and suppression status are documented. Assign the founder manual, individualized email and live-call work; do not spend the launch budget on automated calling infrastructure. Commercial email must satisfy 15 U.S.C. § 7704, including truthful routing, accurate subject lines, a valid postal address, and a functioning opt-out process. Honor opt-outs within the statutory period and suppress them immediately operationally. Business context does not erase TCPA restrictions on certain calls or texts to wireless numbers under 47 U.S.C. § 227 and 47 CFR § 64.1200. The FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule also reaches specified deceptive B2B practices despite its broader business-call exemption; review the actual calling method and applicable state requirements before expanding. Ask for a renewal conversation, not administrator credentials. Request a redacted invoice, SKU inventory, commitment end date, current quote, and written authority to seek alternatives. A qualified audit requires all five evidence elements plus a decision-maker meeting. Neither a friendly reply nor a visible Microsoft MX record counts as qualification. Track fifty lawful initial contacts, ten substantive replies, and six evidence appointments as experimental funnel targets rather than promised outcomes.

Price the first audits with an explicit counterfactual. The baseline is the customer's documented renewal quote for equivalent entitlements, quantities, term, service level, payment timing, and currency. Keep avoidable unused seats separate from negotiated unit-price reductions so the customer can approve each savings mechanism. P11 records the best evidenced executable price; it does not invent a universal vendor price floor from an old spreadsheet. P12 charges an agreed 25%–35% share only on the contractually defined, verified savings base. For example, an approved $120,000 annual baseline reduced to $100,000 creates $20,000 of savings; a 30% fee produces $6,000 of operator revenue and leaves $14,000 of customer benefit before any separately identified implementation costs. Neither the $120,000 baseline nor the $100,000 subscription purchase becomes brokerage revenue. Exclude taxes, unrelated credits, scope reductions, and switching costs unless the engagement expressly specifies their treatment. Define the measurement period, evidence deadline, dispute window, and fee collection event before presenting vendor alternatives. If the brokerage also receives a supplier residual or GPO rebate, disclose the arrangement and specify whether it offsets the shared-savings fee. This prevents hidden double charging and makes the retained economics reproducible from customer records.

Make the proposal packet small enough for the four-person pod to maintain. The founder writes the commercial recommendation; C1 supplies the renewal evidence; C2 confirms the authorized route and actual entitlement match; C3 rebuilds the savings calculation independently. Use a protected Google Sheets or LibreOffice workbook with separate inputs, calculations, and evidence links, then export a versioned PDF for customer acceptance. Configure HubSpot or a lightweight CRM around dated stages: contacted, evidence requested, audit qualified, proposal issued, accepted, fulfilled, and cash collected. Do not mark accepted proposals as paid customers. Record both expected recurring retained revenue and one-time fees, without annualizing contingent amounts before entitlement is established. A $2,000 monthly supplier invoice with a documented 10% agency commission yields $200 monthly retained revenue, not $2,000. Apply the ASC 606 principal-versus-agent assessment to each contract: control before transfer determines presentation, not the label reseller. This chapter's canonical model uses the supplied net retained basis. If a contract requires gross principal presentation, keep a separate bridge to that basis rather than silently changing the scorecard denominator. End Day 14 with four qualified audits and two customer-ready proposals, or identify the specific missing evidence.

## 3. Days 15–21: Fulfill One Clean Pilot and Reconcile Every Dollar  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The third week tests execution, not breadth. Select the strongest documented opportunity and prepare a pilot with one customer, one authorized route, and one accountable supplier contact. Acceptance must identify the exact legal customer, purchasing authority, tenant or account identifier, SKUs, quantities, commitment dates, and payment obligations. A contingent audit engagement can begin while vendor onboarding continues, but it cannot promise an unavailable procurement route. If the customer cannot fund an annual commitment safely, offer direct supplier billing where available or a permitted shorter commitment with its actual higher price. Do not conceal credit risk inside a headline discount. C2 verifies service continuity and the permitted transfer sequence; the founder confirms customer communications; C3 records the obligation before any order. Schedule the change outside critical customer operating windows and retain the supplier's escalation path. Obtain customer confirmation of entitlements after fulfillment, not merely a successful portal screen. If no customer is ready, run a clearly labeled internal tabletop using synthetic records and continue evidence collection. The published time-to-first-deal range is one to three months, so a Day 21 simulation is useful process proof but never a substitute for a real paid pilot.

Measure customer acquisition cost using actual cash spent on sourcing and selling, including allocated contractor effort and outreach tools. Track founder time separately at an illustrative $75 hourly shadow rate for capacity decisions; do not insert that imputed charge into reported cash spend or silently replace the canonical owner-profit scenarios. Before a first paying customer exists, report acquisition spend and conversion counts rather than dividing by zero. For recurring business, calculate cash payback as acquisition cost divided by monthly retained revenue less incremental servicing, processing, and expected reversals. A $600 cash acquisition cost against $200 monthly retained revenue and $50 monthly incremental costs yields $150 monthly contribution and four-month payback. The corresponding $24,000 annual customer subscription purchase is irrelevant to the revenue numerator. For one-time audits, compare collected fees against acquisition and delivery cash costs directly; do not manufacture monthly recurring revenue by spreading a speculative renewal fee over twelve months. A collected $6,000 audit fee less $600 acquisition and $900 delivery costs produces $4,500 deal contribution before fixed overhead and taxes. Neither example establishes the business's annual net margin. Both expose the specific economics that a repeatable route must preserve.

Reconciliation should begin with a ledger design, not a premature API integration. Match customer acceptance, supplier order, entitlement confirmation, supplier statement, operator invoice, and bank receipt through a single transaction identifier. Stripe or bank ACH receipts prove cash movement, while partner statements establish the actual commission or rebate basis; neither document alone establishes that all revenue conditions are satisfied. C3 records earned retained amounts, deferred collections, reversals, and supplier liabilities separately. If the brokerage receives $10,000 and must remit $9,000 under a genuine agency arrangement, the retained revenue is $1,000 when the applicable recognition conditions are met. The $9,000 belongs in the payable or clearing workflow, not the sales dashboard. Validate the arrangement's substance rather than assuming every margin spread qualifies for agency treatment. Keep GPO administrative rebates separate from customer savings until the agreement determines their allocation. During the pilot, reconcile manually within two business days of receiving each required source document and track missing statements as open items. Automate Pax8, Ingram, or master-agent imports only after two clean statement cycles and confirmed access rights. This avoids spending the founder's scarce launch hours debugging integrations for a route that may never produce repeatable collections.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Lock the evidence baseline
   - Version the customer quote, entitlement inventory, and accepted savings formula.
   - C1 supplies artifacts; C3 checks scope parity and records exclusions before any proposal is finalized.
2. Verify authority and funding
   - Confirm customer authority, supplier permission, and zero uncovered commitment exposure.
   - The founder signs the transaction record only after C2 verifies the route and C3 verifies usable funds or direct supplier billing.
3. Fulfill and obtain customer confirmation
   - Use the authorized supplier procedure and verify the purchased entitlements.
   - C2 retains order evidence and customer confirmation; unresolved service defects prevent the transaction from being counted as cleanly fulfilled.
4. Reconcile retained revenue and collections
   - Match contractual earnings, partner statements, invoices, and actual bank receipts.
   - C3 separates pass-through liabilities, earned retained revenue, and cash collections; the founder reviews every open exception.

## 4. Days 22–30 and Reviews at Days 60/90: Allocate Capital Only to Proven Routes  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use Days 22–30 to close evidence gaps and make an explicit allocation decision. The targets below are proposed management thresholds for this launch, not outputs attributed to the scorecard engine. Count each customer once and preserve the original fifty-contact cohort so additional outreach cannot disguise weak conversion. By Day 30, seek six qualified audits, three written proposals, and one accepted engagement. An accepted audit can remain unpaid until its agreed success event; record that distinction. Authorize a bounded next cohort only with one paid customer, at least $1,000 of retained fee cash collected, positive pilot contribution, complete authorization evidence, and zero uncovered annual commitments. Otherwise choose Fix when at least three qualified audits and two dated decision meetings support another thirty days of work. Choose Kill for the tested route when fifty permitted contacts plus ten completed discovery conversations produce zero qualified audits, or no authorized commercial route exists after documented partner follow-up. Insufficient sample size triggers a bounded Fix, not a claim that the market failed. Any unauthorized fulfillment or uncovered commitment stops new orders immediately, regardless of sales momentum. Kill means stop new acquisition for that route while honoring existing customer and supplier obligations.

Keep Day 1–30 gross operating and setup disbursements at or below $2,500, leaving the original $2,500 reserve untouched. Customer subscription pass-throughs sit outside that operating measure but require their own fully funded ledger; excluding them never permits hidden borrowing. Without collections, cap cumulative business outlay at $4,000 through Day 60 and $5,000 through Day 90. The last interval therefore requires a deliberate slowdown below the normal $1,500 monthly operating floor, with founder-led follow-up and reduced contractor usage. Approve that reduction explicitly rather than budgeting nonexistent cash. Additional growth spending may use collected contribution only after delivery costs, supplier liabilities, and reversals are reserved. At Day 60, Scale requires three cumulative paid customers, $5,000 cumulative retained fee collections, and positive aggregate deal contribution. Recurring routes also need cash payback within six months; audit-only routes instead need at least 50% collected-fee contribution after acquisition and delivery. At Day 90, require five paid customers, $10,000 cumulative retained collections, two clean reconciliation cycles, and no more than 20% of earned retained receivables overdue beyond agreed terms. With no earned receivables, record a zero overdue balance rather than computing a meaningless ratio. Customer concentration and supplier payout delays remain explicit review items.

Apply decision precedence consistently: authorization or exposure failures override everything; a fully satisfied Scale test permits controlled expansion; evidenced but incomplete progress permits one bounded Fix; repeated failure ends the route. At Day 60, Kill if ten qualified audits have produced no accepted engagements, or three completed deals show nonpositive aggregate contribution with no executable repricing remedy. At Day 90, Kill if two documented Fix cycles still leave zero paid customers or negative aggregate deal contribution without an agreed corrective offer. A viable but slow pipeline can receive one final fourteen-day Fix funded entirely from remaining cash; never order additional commitments to rescue a sunk acquisition cost. Scale first means at most 25% more weekly outreach within current capacity, not immediate hiring. Keep founder workload below 40 scheduled hours weekly and customer servicing below eight hours weekly before increasing the cohort. The canonical annual cases remain $100,000 retained revenue less $68,000 costs for $32,000 profit; $500,000 less $275,000 for $225,000; and $900,000 less $378,000 for $522,000. Their margins are 32%, 45.0%, and 58%. Conservative and Base retain 1 FT + 3 C; Scaled requires 2 FT + 4 C. The launch evidence does not itself establish any annual run rate.
