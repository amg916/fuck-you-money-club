## 1. Find Renewal Pressure Before Buying Outreach Volume  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with one buyer profile: organizations with 50–500 employees, a named operating or procurement owner, and a software decision expected within 120 days. Assemble 200 accounts from company websites, state business registries, public procurement calendars, SEC EDGAR filings, and referrals from bookkeepers or managed service providers. Record each source URL, observation date, business reason for contact, and confidence level in HubSpot or a controlled spreadsheet. A hiring advertisement mentioning Microsoft 365 suggests a product footprint; it does not establish seat count, renewal date, or purchasing authority. MX and SPF records can suggest messaging infrastructure, but gateways and outsourced mail services obscure the actual subscription stack. Treat DNS as a hypothesis generator, never as permission to access a tenant. Public acquisitions, office consolidations, procurement notices, and departures of procurement leaders are stronger conversation starters because they explain why ownership or demand may change. Verify the actual renewal window directly with the customer. A public bid deadline belongs to a published procurement process; it is not permission to bypass that process. Keep source collection limited to business-relevant information and avoid collecting personal identifiers that the deal does not require.

Map opportunities to the twelve plays in the matrix, then choose the smallest authorized intervention. Microsoft CSP renewal placement needs an eligible distributor relationship and customer agreement; CPOR attribution is not a universal billing-transfer mechanism. Adobe VIP Marketplace opportunities depend on the applicable reseller, anniversary, and transfer rules. Zoom and UCaaS residuals require an executed supplier or master-agent schedule through an authorized route such as Telarus or Avant. GPO rebates require genuine membership and eligible purchases, not pooled seats across unrelated tenants. Shared-savings mandates need an agreed baseline before negotiation starts. For example, a documented $120,000 renewal reduced to $96,000 produces $24,000 annual savings; a 30% contingent fee produces $7,200 operator revenue. Exclude taxes, currency changes, and scope reductions unless the mandate explicitly defines their treatment. Where supplier compensation and savings fees coexist, disclose both and document any offset. Never present illustrative channel percentages as published vendor entitlements. Obtain the current written schedule and calculate after splits, eligibility exclusions, and reversals. If an authorized route fails, retain the audit engagement or refer the implementation to a qualified partner instead of inventing a transfer pathway.

The mapped play multi_inbox_free_workspace_outbound_clusters requires a hard arithmetic and entitlement correction before use. At 300 mailboxes, 15–25 messages daily, and 22 sending days, theoretical volume is 99,000–165,000 messages monthly. Exceeding 150,000 therefore requires the upper end of that range; it says nothing about delivery or placement. At $6–$12 per seat, 300 retail seats would cost $1,800–$3,600 monthly before domains and administration. Microsoft developer subscriptions are for qualifying development activity, not an assumed subsidy for commercial prospecting. Partner benefits also require their actual permitted-use terms. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards inbound messages; it does not itself supply an outbound SMTP service. Use only expressly permitted sending accounts and relays, with accurate identity and authenticated domains. A two-to-three-domain tenant cap is an internal design choice, not proof of vendor permission. Catch-all SMTP behavior cannot verify every recipient. Remove tracking pixels, validate addresses through permitted methods, and suppress uncertain contacts. Keep standby infrastructure only for legitimate continuity, never to escape provider restrictions. The practical fallback is one branded domain, a small permission-aware account cohort, and a licensed service whose rules permit the intended outreach.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Capture Contractual Timing, Not Merely Product Footprints**
> 
> The highest-value sourcing record combines a plausible demand signal with the contractual date that makes action possible.
> - Ask for the cancellation-notice deadline separately from the renewal date; the earlier date usually controls the usable sales window.
> - Maintain separate fields for CPOR attribution, reseller relationship, delegated access, and billing ownership; one does not establish the others.
> - Request the distributor's dated compensation schedule before quoting savings; store the eligible-charge definition alongside the percentage.
> - Use a customer-supplied redacted invoice for initial qualification, then request narrower contract extracts rather than an unrestricted tenant export.

## 2. Score Executability Before Requesting a Signed Mandate  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a 100-point scorecard to separate approachable accounts from executable opportunities. Allocate 20 points to renewal timing, 20 to buyer authority, 15 to baseline evidence, 20 to the authorized commercial route, 15 to retained economics, and 10 to payment exposure. Score only documented facts; an attractive logo earns nothing. Advance accounts at 75 points only when authority, entitlement, and payment requirements are independently satisfied. A prospect with a renewal in 45 days but no contract access belongs in discovery, not in the proposal forecast. Conversely, an excellent customer renewing in eight months belongs in a dated nurture queue, not a forced immediate transfer. Use task reminders at 120, 90, and 60 days before the notice deadline, adjusting for the actual supplier process. Ask five opening questions: who signs, what renews, when notice is due, what scope must remain, and who receives supplier compensation today. These questions expose incumbent relationships without accusing anyone of overcharging. Record evidence references rather than pasting full contracts into general CRM notes. Restrict document access to the owner and the specialist actually responsible for that account's authorized assessment.

Build the baseline from a dated invoice, order form, renewal quote, seat inventory, and any price-protection language. Reconcile annual commitments with monthly billing because payment frequency does not necessarily change the customer's obligation. Microsoft NCE opportunities are especially sensitive: verify the current cancellation and reduction rules for the exact subscription and transaction, then model exposure through the full enforceable term. Require prefunding or a documented payment arrangement appropriate to the exposure, with ACH or card authorization where used. Auto-debit can fail or be disputed; it is not equivalent to cash already collected. If customer credit cannot support the commitment, use an eligible direct-billing referral or an audit-only engagement. Do not fund a customer's annual obligation merely to win a thin spread. Separate list-price comparisons from actual savings: a promotional catalog discount does not prove improvement over the customer's executable renewal quote. Normalize currency, term, support, quantities, and payment dates. Retain the original baseline and a versioned adjustment log. Obtain written agreement on seat reductions, product substitutions, and implementation costs before including those items in the savings calculation.

Turn qualified demand into a narrowly scoped mandate that specifies the covered products, authorized contacts, baseline, savings percentage, measurement period, and payment trigger. A 30% fee against $24,000 verified annual savings yields $7,200, but collection timing must follow the contract rather than a sales representative's forecast. Define whether the fee becomes payable on executed renewal, realized invoice reductions, or another measurable event. Include a dispute reconciliation procedure and a deadline for delivering supporting invoices. Document channel compensation and whether it reduces the contingent charge. Obtain separate authority for distributor submissions; permission to review an invoice does not authorize a license transfer or tenant administration. Request read-only exports wherever they suffice. Never ask the buyer to email administrator credentials. An incumbent reseller objection deserves a concrete response: the audit can proceed without moving billing, and a transfer occurs only when documented economics justify disruption. When the customer refuses to share prices, offer a redacted baseline worksheet or a limited fixed-fee review instead of inventing a benchmark. Close only the service that the available evidence supports; attach later implementation work through a separately priced statement of work.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four Checks Before a Quote Becomes a Commitment**
> 
> A high numerical score cannot compensate for missing authority, unsupported entitlement, or unfunded obligations.
> - Verify the signer, covered customer entity, permitted data access, and separate authorization for each distributor or supplier submission.
> - Obtain the current CSP, VIP Marketplace, master-agent, or GPO terms supporting the precise transaction and compensation being proposed.
> - Reconcile the same-scope baseline, contingent fee, supplier compensation, referral split, and customer-facing disclosure in one worksheet.
> - Confirm available funding, enforceable collection terms, cancellation exposure, and a viable audit-only fallback before accepting annual obligations.

## 3. Run a Bounded Multi-Channel Cadence That Earns Documents  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run outreach as account research followed by a specific invitation, not an inbox-count competition. The initial message should identify one observable business event, one relevant purchasing question, and one small next step. For example: 'Your procurement calendar lists a collaboration-software renewal this quarter. We compare the executable renewal quote with authorized channel options while keeping support requirements fixed. Would the owner of that renewal want a fifteen-minute scope check?' Do not claim access to private pricing or assert that DNS proves the company is overspending. Send from an identifiable business address with accurate headers, a truthful subject, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out. CAN-SPAM applies to commercial business email; follow 15 U.S.C. § 7704, including the ten-business-day opt-out deadline and applicable advertising identification. Suppress immediately operationally rather than waiting for the statutory maximum. Authenticate mail using SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, and follow the provider's acceptable-use rules. Strip tracking pixels and measure replies, meetings, and signed mandates instead of treating unreliable open rates as buyer intent. Never treat an email opt-out as an invitation to pursue the same person through another channel.

Use six touches over eighteen business days only where each channel and contact are appropriate. Begin with a researched email, follow with a manual business call, offer a useful comparison worksheet, and finish with a close-the-loop message. Stop immediately when asked, when the address is invalid, or when an applicable restriction prevents further contact. Calls and texts require separate analysis under 47 U.S.C. § 227 and 47 CFR § 64.1200; a business title does not remove protections associated with a wireless number or prohibited calling method. Do not add automated texts or prerecorded calls to this cadence without establishing the applicable permission and requirements. The FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule contains important business-call distinctions and prohibits covered misrepresentations; avoid a blanket business-exemption assumption. Maintain channel-specific suppression and a broader do-not-contact preference when requested. Use HubSpot tasks and a shared suppression register instead of uncontrolled contractor lists. Check state calling restrictions before geographic expansion. For a small first-deal campaign, careful manual research and limited direct contact provide better learning than adding an expensive automation stack before the offer has demonstrated relevance.

Build inbound alongside outbound with a renewal-clinic page, a baseline worksheet, and one referral conversation each working day. Ask MSPs, fractional technology leaders, and bookkeeping firms for introductions to customers approaching a genuine decision, not exports of their customer databases. Publish a worked example using explicitly illustrative numbers and a defined same-scope comparison. The form should request business contact, product family, and renewal month; move contracts into a controlled upload channel after qualification. Handle objections with bounded alternatives. 'Our reseller already handles this' becomes an audit that preserves the reseller unless a better authorized outcome exists. 'We cannot change vendors' becomes a like-for-like renewal review. 'Your percentage is too high' becomes a transparent fee cap or fixed-scope diagnostic, not a hidden supplier payment. 'Send your cheapest price' becomes a request for quantities, term, support requirements, and the current executable quote. 'No time' becomes a short checklist and a scheduled date before notice expires. Record the objection category and next agreed action. A polite rejection without a future buying event belongs in closed records, not an endless automated follow-up sequence.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Build the evidence-backed account cohort
   - Research 200 accounts in one vertical and one executable product family.
   - Capture public source, event date, plausible renewal question, business contact provenance, and exclusion reason where the account does not fit.
2. Apply channel and account eligibility
   - Validate relevance, contact permissions where required, suppression status, and provider rules.
   - Use an identifiable sending domain, permitted business contact methods, and immediate suppression. Treat catch-all addresses as uncertain rather than verified.
3. Convert the reply into a bounded assessment
   - Collect redacted evidence and complete the 100-point qualification worksheet.
   - Require separate confirmation of signer authority, authorized route, comparable baseline, and payment exposure before presenting executable economics.
4. Execute the mandate and verify the first result
   - Sign the scoped agreement, obtain transaction-specific authority, and reconcile the first payable event.
   - Record signed mandate, supplier acceptance, customer benefit, fee recognition, cash receipt, and possible clawback exposure as separate milestones.

## 4. Convert the First Cohort and Reconcile Every Revenue Dollar  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Treat the initial funnel as a testable planning model rather than an industry benchmark. From 200 researched accounts, suppose 120 meet the signal definition, 60 meet timing and contact requirements, 24 produce substantive two-way conversations, 12 supply sufficient evidence and qualify, and six sign mandates. That is 60%, 50%, 40%, 50%, and 50% conversion between successive stages, or 3% from raw account to signed mandate. The 40% conversation assumption is ambitious for purely cold outreach and requires testing; warm introductions must be reported separately. Three wins from 200 accounts would be 1.5%; ten would be 5%. Define a commercial win explicitly as a signed mandate, then track accepted implementation and collected revenue as different outcomes. During weeks one and two, build evidence and obtain partner schedules. During weeks three through six, run discovery and baseline reviews. During weeks seven through twelve, close executable mandates and reconcile supplier acceptance. Some renewal calendars will exceed this window. Diagnose the stage that fails before increasing volume: poor conversations suggest relevance problems; poor document return suggests trust or effort problems; poor signatures suggest weak economics or unclear decision authority.

Keep cohort economics separate from the canonical annual scenarios. If six mandates each produce a $7,200 earned fee, the cohort yields $43,200 retained fee revenue before delivery and acquisition costs. If each customer instead produces only half the modeled savings, revenue falls to $21,600; signatures alone do not preserve the forecast. At $150 acquisition cost per signed mandate and $1,200 direct delivery cost per mandate, six completed engagements consume $8,100 and leave $35,100 before shared overhead and other costs in the full-savings illustration. These are sensitivity assumptions, not the volume scorecard. Canonical Conservative economics are $100,000 revenue less $68,000 total costs, yielding $32,000 owner profit at 32%. Base is $500,000 less $275,000, yielding $225,000 at 45.0%. Scaled is $900,000 less $378,000, yielding $522,000 at 58%. Preserve staffing of 1 FT plus 3 contractors for Conservative and Base, and 2 FT plus 4 contractors for Scaled. The stated $1,500–$9,500 monthly opex range is not a substitute for each scenario's full annual cost base. Do not backsolve it into contradictory owner-profit figures.

The scorecard revenue basis is retained operator compensation, despite its gross-revenue label; customer software billings are excluded. For an agency transaction with $100,000 underlying subscription spend and an 8% retained share, use $8,000 operator revenue, not $100,000. Apply the retained share once, not again to already-net revenue. Assess principal versus agent presentation under ASC 606 from the actual control and contractual facts; net presentation is not an elective cosmetic choice. Reconcile Pax8, Ingram, or master-agent statements against accepted customer orders, eligible charges, compensation splits, reversals, and cash receipts. Do not assume a single CPOR or VIP sync covers every distributor workflow. Use supported exports or documented interfaces and test the first transaction manually. Track rebate recognition separately from eventual payment, especially where volume brackets or customer persistence affect entitlement. Maintain a clawback register with contractual triggers and expiry dates. Expand sourcing only after one complete cycle demonstrates documented demand, authorized execution, correct fee calculation, and collection. The first ten wins should produce a reusable evidence trail and a narrower target profile, not merely a larger mailing list.
