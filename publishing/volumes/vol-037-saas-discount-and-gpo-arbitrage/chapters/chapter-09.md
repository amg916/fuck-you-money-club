## 1. Establish transaction authority before promising a transfer  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat SaaS brokerage as twelve contract-defined operating lanes, not a license to trade spare seats. The first seven are Microsoft CSP resale, Microsoft CPOR attribution, Microsoft NCE renewal management, Adobe VIP Marketplace migration, Zoom Up partner referral, Telarus or Avant UCaaS residuals, and private GPO membership aggregation. The remaining five are GPO administrative rebates, contingent renewal shared-savings audits, benchmark price-floor reviews, authorized license right-sizing, and the commercial virtual-card settlement adjacency. These are the chapter's twelve mapped plays; separate contracts determine whether each produces a fee. CPOR is a claiming and attribution mechanism for eligible Microsoft activities, not a universal license-transfer permission or substitute for CSP reseller authority. Obtain the customer's written mandate, identify the actual distributor and reseller relationship, and inspect current program eligibility before describing any move as transferable. Pax8 and Ingram workflows can synchronize eligible billing records, but automation cannot expand the underlying agreement. Record tenant identifiers, subscription identifiers, renewal dates, commitment terms, and the person authorized to accept changes. If the vendor cannot confirm an eligible transfer route, retain the incumbent subscription and offer a renewal audit instead of a migration.

Keep each customer's entitlements inside that customer's authorized tenant or membership structure. Adobe VIP Marketplace transfers require the applicable reseller and distributor process; Zoom referrals and TSD commissions require the correct partner agreement and accepted attribution. A pooled purchasing agreement can aggregate negotiating leverage without pooling user credentials, seats, or prohibited sublicenses. Obtain vendor confirmation for any proposed multi-entity deployment, including affiliates, contractors, and acquired businesses. A price sheet showing a volume bracket does not establish entitlement eligibility. Apply least-privilege delegated administration and separate commercial authority from technical access: permission to inspect billing is not permission to provision licenses or read mailboxes. For a hypothetical $120,000 annual subscription with an authorized 8% retained spread, operator revenue is $9,600 before operating expenses, not $120,000. If the vendor compresses the spread to 5%, revenue becomes $6,000 and the broker must reprice service work or leave the renewal with the incumbent. Never repair that shortfall by selling unauthorized seats. Retain the accepted quote, program version, tenant authorization, order confirmation, and commission statement together so an audit can trace the entire commercial chain without reconstructing it from sales messages.

There is no single federal SaaS-broker license that authorizes all these activities. Ordinary entity registration, assumed-name rules, foreign qualification, tax registration, and local business licensing depend on where the brokerage operates and what it actually sells. SaaS taxability also varies: New York generally treats access to prewritten software as taxable, while Texas taxes qualifying data processing services with a statutory twenty-percent exemption. Classify the actual deliverable and separately stated services before configuring tax software; do not apply one statewide label to every bundle. Private GPO agreements must identify eligible members, fee recipients, purchasing commitments, and termination rights. Joint purchasing remains subject to Sherman Act scrutiny under 15 U.S.C. 1; members should not exchange downstream prices, wage plans, customer allocations, or competitor-specific purchasing strategies. Use aggregated benchmarks with controls against reverse identification. Healthcare purchasing adds a separate issue: arrangements implicating federal healthcare program business require analysis under 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b), including the GPO safe harbor where relevant. Merely calling a software club a GPO does not establish protection. If membership eligibility or a sector rule remains unresolved, perform a separately scoped advisory review without placing orders or accepting supplier-funded compensation.

## 2. Contract for retained revenue, funded commitments, and measurable savings  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use a master services agreement plus transaction schedules that separate advisory work, resale, referral compensation, and supplier rebates. Scope must identify products, tenant boundaries, excluded implementation work, and who can issue purchase instructions. Define the fee trigger precisely: accepted referral, collected supplier commission, completed audit, or realized savings verified against paid invoices. For shared savings, fix the baseline date, eligible SKU population, quantities, currency, taxes, credits, implementation costs, and measurement period. A comparable renewal falling from $100,000 to $80,000 creates $20,000 eligible savings; a 30% contingent fee is $6,000, leaving $14,000 with the client before any separately agreed costs. Rates of 25%–35% therefore yield $5,000–$7,000 on that same savings base. Exclude reductions caused solely by unrelated headcount cuts unless the schedule expressly covers right-sizing. Disclose supplier payments and specify whether they offset the client fee. A narrow anti-circumvention clause should cover documented introductions for a stated period, not every vendor the client already knows. Give disputes a records exchange and correction process before collection escalation, and prevent sales staff from changing the baseline orally after the customer has accepted the proposal.

For annual NCE commitments, distinguish the customer's payment frequency from the upstream commitment duration. Monthly billing does not convert an annual obligation into a cancellable monthly license. Require prefunded ACH or card collection covering the broker's noncancelable exposure before placing the order, plus authorized automatic replenishment for later charges where applicable. A debit authorization alone does not eliminate insufficient funds, ACH returns, card disputes, or customer insolvency. Define funding deadlines, permitted suspension actions, credit reserves, and who bears upstream charges after default; do not promise cancellation outside the current vendor rules. Liability caps should specify the measurement period and treatment of confidentiality breaches, misuse of data, unpaid charges, and intentional misconduct, subject to enforceability review. Select governing law, venue or arbitration procedure, notice methods, and interim-relief rights. The client retains its data and entitlements; the broker retains preexisting methods while granting the agreed deliverable rights. ASC 606 requires a principal-versus-agent assessment for each promise. The canonical scenarios already use retained operator revenue: $100,000 less $68,000 costs equals $32,000 profit; $500,000 less $275,000 equals $225,000; $900,000 less $378,000 equals $522,000. Do not apply retained_share again to these figures.

The mapped b2b_commercial_vcc_interchange_rebate_arbitrage play is an adjacent AP service, not software subscription revenue. Offer 'Free Automated Accounts Payable / Instant Vendor Settlement' only when the written scope supports both price and settlement timing. Freight brokerages, construction contractors, medical suppliers, property managers, and media agencies can route eligible invoices through issuer-generated single-use Visa or Mastercard commercial cards. Model 140–195 basis points as an agreement-specific rebate range, not an automatic share of all interchange. At $100 million eligible annual settled volume, that range produces $1.40–$1.95 million before contractual splits, reversals, and costs. For a smaller illustration, $1 million monthly volume at 160 basis points creates a $16,000 rebate pool; a 25% retained_share leaves $4,000 operator revenue. Invoice principal never becomes brokerage revenue. Inject accurate Level-3 line-item metadata at card creation where supported, verify vendor acceptance, and prohibit fabricated tax or commodity fields. For card-averse suppliers, offer a separately accepted 1.5%–2% discount for payment within ten days, or ordinary ACH when discount economics fail. Prioritize fragmented long-tail suppliers. Keep funds with the bank or authorized payment provider; possession or control of customer funds requires a separate federal and state money-transmission analysis.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Classify each commercial promise
   - Separate resale, referral, audit, rebate, and payment-service schedules.
   - Identify the obligated supplier, the customer beneficiary, the compensation payer, and the evidence supporting principal-versus-agent treatment.
2. Lock authority and measurement
   - Obtain the signed mandate, comparable savings baseline, and program eligibility evidence.
   - Record tenant identifiers, SKU quantities, renewal dates, permitted transfer mechanics, and the exact supplier-compensation offset rule.
3. Fund the noncancelable exposure
   - Confirm prefunding and authorized collection before submitting an annual commitment.
   - Reconcile cleared funds against upstream exposure, review return and dispute risk, and retain the vendor's current cancellation terms.
4. Reconcile earned fees separately from spend
   - Match paid invoices and supplier statements to the signed fee trigger.
   - Record retained revenue once, track reversals and reserves, and exclude customer subscription spend and payment principal from operator revenue.

## 3. Apply federal, state, and sector rules to the actual activity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the outreach rulebook around channel, recipient, technology, and location rather than the label B2B. Under 16 CFR 310.6(b)(7), many business-to-business calls remain exempt from much of the Telemarketing Sales Rule, but specified material misrepresentations and false or misleading statements are outside that exemption. The office and cleaning supplies exception also requires separate treatment. Do not claim that business recipients erase federal restrictions. The TCPA, 47 U.S.C. 227, and 47 CFR 64.1200 impose distinct requirements depending on dialing technology, prerecorded or artificial voice, destination, and purpose. A business user's mobile number can still trigger protections applicable to wireless numbers. Use manually dialed live calls as the default and separately assess any automated calling or texting workflow. Screen applicable national and state do-not-call lists, maintain internal suppression, and honor revocation without requiring a sales conversation. Treat AI-generated voice as artificial voice, not a workaround. Preserve consent evidence where required, including the disclosure presented and the number covered. Determine whether TSR recordkeeping provisions apply to the specific campaign instead of blindly assigning every B2B call the same federal retention period. Keep operational evidence under a documented retention schedule even when an exemption applies.

Commercial email follows CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. 7704, including business-to-business messages. Use accurate routing information, nondeceptive subject lines, required advertising identification where applicable, a valid physical postal address, and a functioning opt-out. Honor opt-outs within ten business days and keep the mechanism available for at least thirty days after transmission. A purchased contact list does not supply permission for prerecorded calls or texts. Map state outreach requirements independently: Florida Statutes 501.059 addresses covered telephonic sales calls, and Florida's telemarketing licensing provisions in Part IV of Chapter 501 require an applicability and exemption review. Washington RCW 80.36.390 supplies another state-specific telephone solicitation framework. Do not assume those consumer-oriented provisions cover every enterprise call, or that an enterprise label defeats them when the recipient and purpose fit. Recording also differs by jurisdiction; California Penal Code 632 protects confidential communications through an all-party consent rule. Use affirmative recording permission as the cross-state operating default, and continue unrecorded if permission is refused. Track recipient local time, number type, suppression status, recording choice, script version, and campaign owner. If jurisdiction cannot be established reliably, use a carefully addressed email rather than escalating to automated telephone outreach.

Minimize the data collected for benchmarking: invoices, quantities, contract dates, and billing contacts usually suffice without mailbox contents or employee-level usage histories. California's CCPA, as amended, can cover business contact information when the business meets statutory applicability thresholds; the former general B2B exemption has expired. Use the appropriate service provider or contractor terms when the relationship qualifies, and do not sell identified client benchmarks as anonymous market intelligence. For healthcare customers, determine whether the brokerage actually creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected health information on behalf of a covered entity. If it does, address business-associate obligations under 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e); buying ordinary SaaS for a clinic does not by itself establish that role. The healthcare GPO safe harbor at 42 CFR 1001.952(j) requires specified written member agreements and disclosures, including its administrative-fee conditions. Financial-sector customers may demand GLBA service-provider safeguards. For AP settlement, 31 CFR 1010.100(ff)(5) and state money-transmitter laws turn on the actual funds flow and applicable exceptions, not a platform's marketing name. Have the issuer or payment provider settle directly, document control boundaries, and suspend any proposed flow that would place client funds under an unassessed brokerage-controlled account.

## 4. Use scripts that disclose economics and produce usable evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use this first-touch email only with a real brokerage identity and its actual physical postal address in the footer: 'Subject: Review your next Microsoft or Adobe renewal before commitment. Hello procurement team. We review renewal dates, licensed quantities, and authorized partner pricing for business software. We cannot determine your savings without your contract and comparable invoices. With your permission, we can prepare a scoped review that leaves existing subscriptions unchanged until you authorize a permitted transaction. We may receive supplier commissions or GPO administrative fees; the proposal will disclose those payments and any client fee. Would a fifteen-minute discussion with the renewal owner be useful? This is a commercial solicitation. Reply stop to end marketing emails from us.' The signature must identify the actual sender rather than implying Microsoft, Adobe, or the incumbent reseller sent the message. Keep the opt-out free and uncomplicated, and route replies to a monitored mailbox. DNS, MX, and SPF observations can suggest a discovery question, but they do not prove license quantities, renewal dates, or the customer's purchasing authority. Do not turn a technographic signal into a claim that the prospect is overpaying or has an expiring contract.

For a screened, manually dialed live call, say: 'Hello, I am calling from an independent software procurement brokerage. This is a sales call about renewal review services. Are you responsible for software purchasing, and is now a reasonable time for two questions?' If recording is proposed, ask separately: 'May we record this discussion for our transaction notes? We can continue without recording.' Discovery then follows a bounded sequence: 'Which agreement establishes your renewal and commitment dates? Who can authorize access to billing records? Are there affiliate, healthcare, or public-sector restrictions we should account for? Would you prefer a fixed review fee or a proposal tied to verified savings?' Do not request global administrator credentials, patient records, or payment-card details over the call. For a shared-savings proposal, say: 'If comparable annual cost falls from $100,000 to $80,000, a 30% fee would be $6,000 and your retained savings would be $14,000 before separately agreed costs. The signed baseline will define comparability and supplier-compensation offsets.' If the prospect declines, answer: 'Understood. I will record your request and stop marketing contact through the channels you identify.' Record the suppression request before scheduling any further sales task.

For the VCC adjacency, use a vendor-specific acceptance conversation: 'We support an issuer-provided single-use commercial card option for approved invoices. Do you accept this payment method, and what processing charges or contractual restrictions apply? Participation is optional. If card payment does not work for you, we can discuss an agreed early-payment ACH discount or retain the existing payment method.' Never promise instant funds before confirming issuer settlement timing, and never imply that Level-3 metadata removes merchant acceptance costs. The buyer-side script is: 'Our compensation depends on eligible settled card volume and the issuer agreement. We will disclose the retained share, reversals, and any platform fees before activation. Your invoice principal remains payable to your vendors, not earned by our brokerage.' A $10,000 invoice paid with a 2% early-payment discount becomes a $9,800 payment and $200 buyer savings; it creates no broker fee unless a separate agreement expressly provides one. Close every discovery session with a written recap identifying authority, documents requested, compensation, permitted next action, and opt-out preference. Reconcile that recap with the final schedule before ordering. A polished conversation is useful only when the resulting evidence supports the transaction that operations actually executes.
