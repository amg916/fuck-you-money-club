## 1. Establish the legal buyer and evidence hierarchy before purchasing enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The first three mapped plays are Legal-Entity Qualification, Public-Procurement Triggering, and Renewal-Evidence Acquisition. Start with the contracting entity, not an attractive domain. Delaware and Wyoming registries establish filed names and status; neither proves beneficial ownership, operational substance, or eligibility for a vendor incentive. SEC EDGAR adds dated acquisition disclosures, subsidiary exhibits, and material agreements for reporting companies. SAM.gov and USAspending.gov expose registration and award context, but an award amount is neither software budget nor available brokerage revenue. Store each observation with source URL, retrieval timestamp, effective date, entity identifier, and permitted use. A domain-to-company match becomes usable only after a second independent identifier agrees, such as a customer-provided registration number or a signed order naming the same entity. For a batch of 200 businesses, suppose 120 identity matches survive review and 30 supply actual subscription documents. The yield is 15% document-qualified accounts, not 200 opportunities. If public records conflict with an invoice, suspend the proposed transfer and request the current contracting-party document. This prevents commissions being attributed to a similarly named affiliate and stops unsupported assumptions about group purchasing eligibility from entering the proposal.

Use public purchasing evidence to select questions, not manufacture precision. A GSA eLibrary contractor listing can identify an authorized schedule holder and relevant contract categories; its existence does not establish that a private buyer receives federal pricing. A USAspending award modification may suggest implementation activity, while the actual order, scope, and option period determine timing. Attach an evidence grade to each renewal field: executed contract, authenticated billing export, buyer confirmation, or inferred signal. Only the first two should drive an irreversible purchase commitment. Consider 40 leads researched at six minutes each: four hours at a loaded $40 hourly cost equals $160 before enrichment charges. If eight buyers provide contracts, research costs $20 per document-qualified account. Compare that figure with expected retained contribution, not the customer's annual software spend. A $24,000 subscription yielding an illustrative 8% retained spread produces $1,920 before servicing costs and reversals. Losing the customer after three paid months reduces that simple annualized expectation to $480. Maintain the original document beside every extracted renewal date so a reviewer can distinguish an anniversary, cancellation deadline, billing date, and price-protection expiry without reconstructing the research trail.

Build the first six directory entries into a narrow acquisition pipeline. Use PostgreSQL for durable records, object storage for permissioned source documents, and a scheduled worker for refreshes; SQLite is sufficient for an initial single-operator desk. Separate source_changed_at from retrieved_at because a freshly downloaded record may still describe an old event. Hash document bytes, retain extraction version numbers, and preserve corrected values as new observations rather than silently overwriting history. Source costs and limits below are procurement categories and operator refresh targets, not live vendor quotations or promises of publication speed. Registry interfaces change, and some states charge separately for certificates or detailed reports. Before enabling a connector, capture its current terms, authentication method, documented limits, and assigned internal owner. Under 18 U.S.C. 1030, do not treat a visible login screen or guessed endpoint as permission to retrieve protected records. When automation is unavailable, use a permitted manual lookup or obtain the document directly from the buyer. Weekly refreshes usually suffice for entity status; contract deadlines require explicit calendar monitoring. Delete unnecessary personal information rather than turning a lean brokerage ledger into an indiscriminate contact archive.

## 2. Turn technographic hints into authorized, measurable renewal evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The next three mapped plays are DNS/MX/SPF Technographic Qualification, Licensed Enrichment Waterfalls, and Authorized Tenant Seat Diagnostics. Query public DNS with dig or a documented DNS-over-HTTPS service to identify mail-routing and sender-policy clues. Microsoft-oriented MX records suggest a mail environment, not the customer's license edition, seat count, reseller, or renewal date. SPF includes may represent dormant tooling or a third-party sender rather than an active paid subscription. RDAP supplies domain registration context, with substantial redaction and no reliable purchasing authority. BuiltWith can add website technology observations; Hunter can help locate business contact information under its permitted-use terms. Run the waterfall in that order only when each additional field improves a defined decision. If 500 domains receive free DNS checks, 150 need a hypothetical $0.10 technology lookup, and 50 need a hypothetical $0.20 contact lookup, variable data cost is $25. Those prices are planning assumptions, not vendor quotes. Stop enrichment when the buyer is already reachable. A missing field should route to direct buyer confirmation, not another opaque data broker. Preserve observation age so abandoned technologies do not masquerade as current negotiation leverage during renewal outreach.

Tenant diagnostics begin only after the customer authorizes the required access. Microsoft Graph GET /v1.0/subscribedSkus can expose subscribed products and capacity under documented permissions; it does not by itself establish cancellation rights or commercial renewal terms. Obtain the least privileged current permission that supports the endpoint, and keep each customer's tokens and exports isolated. Google Admin SDK Directory API users.list can support account inventory with administrator authorization; user existence is not proof of a paid Workspace license or recent usage. Combine authorized inventory, appropriate usage reports, and billing exports instead of equating inactive identities with removable seats. Suppose a customer has 80 seats at an illustrative $30 monthly unit price, with 12 candidates for removal. The ceiling on annual list-price reduction is 12 × $30 × 12 = $4,320, but an annual commitment may defer every dollar until renewal. At a contracted 30% shared-savings fee, fully realized eligible savings would generate $1,296. If the customer must retain four seats, the comparable ceiling falls to $2,880 and the fee to $864. Obtain service-owner confirmation before recommending deletion, reassignment, or a lower product tier.

Represent enrichment as evidence events rather than one mutable company profile. Each event should contain tenant scope, source system, observed value, observation time, confidence basis, retention deadline, and extraction version. For network diagnostics, constrain activity to ordinary public queries; do not enumerate protected tenant resources, test passwords, or probe customer applications without separate authorization. Build retries around documented response codes, exponential backoff, jitter, and a per-provider concurrency ceiling. Honor Retry-After when supplied, and never rotate accounts to defeat quotas. Cache DNS responses within their TTL, deduplicate enrichment purchases, and attach a budget ceiling to each research batch. Test connectors using synthetic customer identifiers and redacted fixtures; retain enough provenance to explain why a particular contact was approached. Outreach requires its own controls: 47 U.S.C. 227 and 47 CFR 64.1200 govern covered calling and texting activity, while 16 CFR Part 310 includes relevant restrictions on deceptive B2B telemarketing. Do not assume a business label removes every restriction. For commercial email, implement accurate sender information and working opt-out processing under 15 U.S.C. 7704. Suppress opted-out contacts across every enrichment provider before the next campaign export.

1. Create the evidence and authorization manifest
   - Record legal entity, tenant scope, source permissions, signed agreements, and approved connector access.

   - Check public records and DNS first, purchase justified enrichment second, and request authorized billing evidence third.
