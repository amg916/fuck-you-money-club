## 1. Turn warm renewal signals into authorized, underwritten opportunities  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an opportunity record, not a discount promise. Capture the customer entity, tenant identifier, billing owner, incumbent channel, renewal date, cancellation window, seat quantities, currency, and signed authority to inspect commercial documents. Use a customer-provided invoice and authorized administration export as primary evidence; DNS, MX, and SPF observations only suggest which questions to ask. A Microsoft mail record does not establish licensing rights or buying authority. Record the quoted SKU, commitment term, billing frequency, promotional expiry, and proposed effective date before comparing prices. Score renewal proximity at 40 points, a genuine prospect reply at 30, and observed buying authority at 30; the score prioritizes review without authorizing a purchase. Preserve an immutable source reference so repeated CRM deliveries update the work queue rather than create duplicate deals. A quote packet should identify exactly which subscriptions can move, which remain with the incumbent, and which require a new commercial agreement. If evidence conflicts, retain the existing service and ask for corrected documents. Losing a speculative spread costs less than creating overlapping annual commitments or interrupting a customer's identity, collaboration, and telephone services during an avoidable migration.

Implement high_velocity_dm_outbound_systems as a warm-trigger queue, not a mechanism for multiplying unsolicited messages. A prospect comments on a renewal checklist, reacts to an explicit offer, or initiates an inbound message; route that signal to one assigned inbox under the platform's current messaging permissions. Require the prospect's reply before sending an external booking or upload link. In Month 3, 240 eligible signals produce 120 replies, 48 discovery conversations, 24 qualified quote packets, and 20 activations after four scrubs. Those illustrative conversion rates are 50%, 40%, 50%, and 83.3% respectively, not promised platform performance. Deduplicate at the person-and-company level, honor opt-outs across inboxes, and record trigger evidence. PeerFloodError or disappearing Message Requests means stop and inspect permission and delivery conditions, not rotate identities to defeat limits. A reaction alone does not authorize every channel or message type. Telephone and text follow-up must be evaluated separately under 47 U.S.C. § 227 and 47 CFR § 64.1200. The FTC's business-to-business telemarketing treatment does not permit deceptive savings claims. Keep suppression, channel eligibility, and commercial underwriting separate so a promising renewal never overrides an outreach restriction.

Map twelve operating plays without pretending every step has a supplied registry identifier. The supplied acquisition identifier maps to warm-trigger routing; the remaining descriptive mappings are renewal-cliff scoring, authorized CSP spreads, Adobe VIP transfers, Zoom residuals, UCaaS residuals, GPO bracket rebates, shared-savings audits, license reclamation, term alignment, prefunded commitment control, and settlement recovery. Only the first identifier is asserted as registry metadata here. For each quote, calculate the retained amount after distributor charges, agent splits, and contractually required customer credits. A $12,000 customer software bill yielding a $1,200 retained spread contributes $1,200 operator revenue under the volume's net-agency basis, not $12,000. A completed savings engagement with $16,666.67 of agreed eligible savings and a 30% fee produces $5,000 after cent rounding. Define eligible savings against equivalent seats, features, support, taxes, and service periods; separate reductions caused by canceled business units. A license-reclamation recommendation can improve that baseline but earns no separate fee unless contracted. When the vendor floor removes the spread, switch to a permitted audit engagement or decline the transaction rather than pool unrelated customers' seats inside an unauthorized tenant.

## 2. Move from signed authority to activation without inheriting unsecured commitments  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Execute through the actual program rather than a generic partner label. Microsoft CSP resale authority, customer relationships, delegated access, subscription transfer eligibility, and CPOR associations are distinct concepts; a CPOR association is not universal permission to resell or move subscriptions. For Adobe VIP Marketplace, follow the current distributor transfer procedure and preserve the membership and order references. For Zoom or UCaaS through Telarus or Avant, verify the specific supplier agreement, commissionable products, activation event, and residual schedule. GPO work requires documented membership and the actual rebate schedule, including exclusions and customer allocations. Never assume a distributor supports an automated endpoint merely because its portal displays an operation. Use documented APIs where available and a controlled human task otherwise. Before execution, freeze the signed quote version, authorized seat counts, destination tenant, service dates, and responsible administrator. The customer retains administration rights unless separately delegated. Keep secrets out of the CRM and ledger; store credentials in a managed secret store with narrowly scoped access. If a program requires additional evidence, the opportunity waits in verification while the existing service remains intact and the quoted economics are refreshed.

Protect annual commitments before pressing the supplier order button. For an annual NCE obligation of $12,000, a customer's $1,000 monthly autodebit instruction leaves substantial exposure; it is not equivalent to prefunding the full obligation. Prefer customer-direct supplier payment where the commercial arrangement permits it, or collect cleared prefunding matched to the remaining liability. Card authorization and a newly initiated ACH debit are not cleared, irreversible funds. Document return, chargeback, and concentration exposure even after settlement. The reference workflow names its protected state funded; an escrow arrangement, when used, must be administered through an appropriately authorized provider under documented release instructions. The software state itself establishes neither custody authority nor an escrow account. Client-controlled direct payments remain evidence rather than operator cash entries; the reference client-funds postings apply only where the operator actually holds matched funds under the applicable arrangement. Maintain restricted balances separately from operating cash and never spend them on acquisition or payroll. If adequate protection is unavailable, quote an eligible monthly commitment, require a different payment structure, or decline. Do not use another customer's prepayment to bridge the shortfall.

Fulfillment ends with evidence, not an optimistic status change. Store the supplier acceptance reference, effective date, SKU quantities, customer confirmation, and first billing record. Compare the activated configuration with the frozen quote before recording an earned spread. For residuals, match each statement line to the active customer and applicable agent split. For rebates, document satisfied purchase conditions and estimate reversals only when the recognition policy supports doing so. For savings fees, the worked model assumes a distinct completed audit obligation and an enforceable fee accepted by the customer; a contingent future reduction alone is insufficient. Apply ASC 606 principal-versus-agent analysis to each arrangement. The scorecard uses net retained operator revenue, but that convention does not determine every real contract's accounting conclusion. A reviewer should reconcile the supplier statement, customer invoice, bank receipt, and ledger event before closing a settlement period. Keep recurring relationships active across periods rather than closing them after the first commission. Correct disputed statements through documented supplier adjustments and a controlled accounting procedure; the minimal reference deliberately blocks journal edits and lacks a general credit-note subsystem. Route those exceptions outside its straight-through path until that extension is implemented and tested.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the commercial packet
   - Attach signed authority, equivalent-service baseline, quote version, and current program terms.
   - Record the customer tenant and renewal window separately from inferred technographic signals.
2. Protect the commitment
   - Verify cleared funding or the permitted customer-direct payment structure before ordering.
   - Measure remaining enforceable exposure rather than treating monthly billing as monthly commitment.
3. Submit once and confirm delivery
   - Claim the durable outbox item, submit through the authorized channel, and retain the provider receipt.
   - An ambiguous response requires provider lookup or human investigation; never blindly resubmit.
4. Recognize, collect, and reconcile
   - Post earned retained fees, match bank receipts, and review the receivable and client-funds balances.
   - Close only fully resolved one-time engagements; keep recurring relationships available for later settlement periods.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before creating supplier exposure**
> 
> All four checks require attached evidence; a high lead score cannot substitute for any of them.
> - Verify the signer, customer entity, tenant, and permitted actions against signed authority and the exact supplier program.
> - Recalculate the retained spread using current price floors, agent splits, customer credits, and any rebate eligibility restrictions.
> - Match cleared prefunding or permitted direct-payment protection to the complete remaining supplier obligation, including annual commitments billed monthly.
> - Confirm the immutable order key, duplicate check, rollback limitations, and named person responsible for investigating ambiguous supplier responses.

## 3. Install the six-module ledger and serialize every consequential transition  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use the six reference modules below as a bounded transaction core, not an entire channel integration product. Save each artifact under its indicated filename in one directory. Initialize a fresh SQLite database with schema.sql and reconcile.sql; run python qa.py from that directory to exercise the included temporary-database checks. The reference code has not been executed here. Python 3.9 and the standard library are sufficient. Every mutation starts BEGIN IMMEDIATE, so SQLite serializes competing writers before they inspect balances or states. Unique natural identities block duplicate intake, journal source lines, workflow commands, and order creation. Integer cents eliminate binary floating-point drift, while each journal row contains one equal debit and credit. A replay returns the earlier result only when its immutable payload agrees; conflicting reuse raises an error. Keep the database on a supported local filesystem, not a shared network mount, and use SQLite's backup API for consistent snapshots. Restrict filesystem write access to the service identity because database constraints cannot replace application authorization. Rejected lock acquisition leaves no committed partial work; the caller may retry the same command identity after the contention condition clears.

Separate local atomicity from supplier-side execution. The order transition commits the workflow event, new state, and outbox row together. A worker claims that row in another serialized transaction and marks it ambiguous before touching the network. Only one worker can obtain the initial claim. Send the stable command key as the provider idempotency key where the documented API supports it. If the worker crashes before sending, or receives a timeout after sending, the item remains ambiguous and cannot be automatically claimed again. Resolve it by looking up the provider transaction or obtaining documented human confirmation. Providers without durable idempotency and lookup require this deliberately conservative halt. No local database can promise exactly-once effects across an arbitrary external service; the reference prevents duplicate local dispatch and fails closed where remote certainty is unavailable. An authenticated adapter must validate webhook signatures, supplier account scope, and source references before invoking these functions. Network calls stay outside database transactions, preventing a slow supplier from holding the writer lock. Configure alerting for pending age and ambiguous outcomes, with a named settlement owner rather than an unbounded background retry loop.

Treat the supplied core's accounting boundaries as explicit design decisions. Recognition increases receivables and retained revenue; collection increases operating cash and decreases receivables. Client funding and supplier releases affect restricted cash and client liabilities without touching revenue. Expenses reduce operating cash and owner profit. Opening owner capital is tracked separately from the operating cash-change view. The minimal engine supports a straight-through order, matched funding, positive earned fees, collections, and expenses; recurring invoice lines use distinct period-specific evidence references on an active deal. It intentionally excludes foreign exchange, tax allocation, fee reversals, partial activation, and supplier-credit accounting. Add those capabilities with explicit event types and invariant tests before routing affected production transactions through it. Store signed documents in access-controlled object storage and put stable references in the ledger; a reference string alone does not authenticate evidence. Test simultaneous duplicate delivery, changed-payload replay, insufficient funding, receipt overcollection, and interrupted provider submission. Reconcile the views to bank and supplier exports daily, then perform a restore drill against a copied backup. The included QA checks are a starting suite, not evidence that a particular deployment or adapter has passed acceptance.

## 4. Walk the first ninety days from qualified signals to reconciled cash  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The cohort below starts with $20,000 owner capital, no opening receivables, and no preexisting recurring book. All operating expenses are paid in the month incurred; client subscription cash is excluded. Month 1 converts 120 warm signals into 60 replies, 24 discoveries, 12 qualified opportunities, and ten activations after two scrubs. Six reseller accounts contribute $500 each, two residual accounts contribute $500 each, one rebate contributes $1,000, and one completed savings audit contributes $5,000: total recognized revenue is $10,000. Month 2 adds fifteen activations from eighteen qualified opportunities. Eight reseller accounts at $750, three residual accounts at $500, two rebates at $1,750, and two audits at $5,000 generate $21,000 new revenue. Add the first month's $4,000 recurring spread-and-residual book to reach $25,000. Rebate and audit amounts do not automatically recur. Scrubs happen before ordering and recognition, so the model does not bury their lost forecast value inside realized revenue. Acquisition and contractor expenses still absorb the work spent on those rejected opportunities. The example assumes no defaults or post-activation clawbacks; replacing that assumption requires an explicit loss expense and revised cash bridge.

Month 3 adds twenty activations: ten reseller accounts at $800, four residual accounts at $750, three earned rebates at $1,388.89, and three completed audits at $5,000. New revenue equals $30,166.67. The retained recurring book contributes another $11,500 from Months 1 and 2, producing $41,666.67 recognized revenue. All six audits across the quarter use a 30% fee on $16,666.67 accepted eligible savings, rounded to $5,000 apiece. The model assumes the contracted audit obligation is completed and collectible in its stated month. Fixed monthly overhead is $6,500, within the volume's $1,500–$9,500 monthly overhead range. Delivery labor, commissions, payment costs, and other variable operating costs equal 39.4% of recognized revenue in this illustration. Therefore Month 3 variable costs are $16,416.67 and total costs are $22,916.67, leaving $18,750 owner profit. The Base team remains one full-time owner plus three contractors; contractor costs are included rather than hidden outside the margin. The $500,000 annual revenue and $275,000 annual cost targets imply $225,000 profit at 45.0%. Month 3 is a replenished operating pace, not contracted recurring revenue or a claim that the ramp quarter already earned one quarter of the annual target.

Cash conversion determines the safe distribution, not the revenue dashboard. Month 1 collects $6,000 and carries $4,000 receivables. Month 2 collects that $4,000 plus $18,000 of current fees, ending with $7,000 receivables. Month 3 collects the $7,000 opening balance plus $31,666.67 current fees, leaving $10,000 receivables. Quarter collections total $66,666.67 against $49,706.67 paid operating costs, so cumulative operating cash generation is $16,960. Add the initial $20,000 capital to obtain $36,960 closing unrestricted cash before owner distributions. Restricted customer funds, if any, sit outside that number.
