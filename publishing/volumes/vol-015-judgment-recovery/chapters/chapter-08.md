## 1. Build a source-ranked registry desk, not a bulk people-search operation  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start every commercial judgment file with the rendered judgment, complete party caption, court identifier, entry date, remaining balance, and documented authority to act. A docket search is discovery; the filed instrument and applicable court rules determine what happened. PACER Case Locator identifies federal cases, while the originating court supplies the operative docket and documents. State trial courts, county recorders, secretaries of state, and UCC filing offices answer different questions and must remain separate evidence classes. An assessor record suggests a parcel relationship, not current title or reachable equity. A registered agent provides a service address, not ownership proof. A UCC filing identifies a claimed security interest, not the current payoff balance or a complete priority opinion. Save the original response, retrieval time, source identifier, query terms, and document hash before extracting fields. Maintain the exact debtor name alongside normalized aliases; normalization must never rewrite the legal caption. For each promising asset, require a second authoritative record connecting the debtor to that asset. A similarly named company, household member, or shared mailing address is a research lead, never a substitute for that connection.

Use the directory below as an access plan, not a claim that every portal offers unrestricted automation. Public visibility does not create permission to bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs, session controls, or contractual limits. Prefer documented APIs, licensed feeds, and official bulk downloads; otherwise assign a human retrieval task. Source publication latency and your polling interval are separate fields. A nightly collector cannot make a delayed recorder index current, and a five-minute refresh does not prove that a bankruptcy petition has appeared in a nationwide locator. The listed refresh intervals are desk targets, not vendor delivery promises. Confirm current charges, coverage, and contractual limits before purchasing access. Cache stable documents by identifier, use conditional requests where supported, and honor Retry-After responses. For SEC resources, identify the organization and contact in the User-Agent and remain below the published aggregate request ceiling. For portals without a published automation policy, use manual retrieval or obtain written access terms. Never rotate identities or network addresses to defeat a restriction. Record restricted access as an operational exception and move to the named fallback rather than silently treating missing results as clean records.

Organize the chapter's 15 mapped plays as explicit work queues: judgment intake verification, debtor identity resolution, entity status monitoring, bankruptcy screening, judgment deadline tracking, lien discovery, priority review, parcel research, business activity verification, receivable lead discovery, attorney enforcement packaging, assignment chain verification, payment reconciliation, collection cost triage, and transferable_tax_credit_brokerage_syndication. The first fourteen are operational applications within this recovery desk; the fifteenth is a separately controlled origination adjacency. Each queue needs an input document, a decisive question, an authorized operator, and an exit condition. For example, ten judgments with $100,000 face amounts create $1 million of claimed principal, not $1 million of operator revenue. If one matter produces a $20,000 collected payment and an earned 25% fee, recognized service revenue is $5,000 under that illustrative contract. The remaining $15,000 belongs in client settlement accounting, subject to documented expenses and disbursement terms. Do not assign an expected recovery merely because a directory returns a property hit. First resolve identity, available equity, enforceability, exemptions, competing claims, and the cost of obtaining executable evidence. This ordering prevents a cheap search from creating an expensive and unsupported enforcement theory.

## 2. Turn registry hits into an evidence-preserving enrichment waterfall  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Implement the waterfall with PostgreSQL for structured case data, encrypted object storage for documents, and a scheduler such as systemd timers or a managed job runner. A small desk does not need a distributed crawler to handle hundreds of active judgments. Separate matters, parties, identifiers, assets, instruments, observations, deadlines, and cash events into different records. Give every observation a source URL, source record key, retrieved_at timestamp, effective date when available, parser version, content hash, and access-purpose reference. Preserve raw files separately from extracted text. Hash the original bytes with SHA-256; hashing only normalized text loses the ability to identify an altered original. OCR with Tesseract can make scanned judgments searchable, but an operator must verify debtor names, amounts, dates, and decretal language against the image. Store money as integer cents and record currencies explicitly. Retain exact court identifiers including leading zeros and punctuation. Use stable source keys for idempotent ingestion so a repeated download creates a fresh observation without duplicating the instrument. A document correction should supersede an earlier interpretation, not erase the earlier record or its review history.

Resolve identity before buying broader enrichment. Begin with the judgment caption and entity number, then compare formation jurisdiction, historical names, service records, and business addresses. Match a business using identifiers and corroborating documents rather than name similarity alone. Keep individual guarantors separate from corporate defendants; an owner's relationship to a company does not automatically make their assets available for the company's debt. Route ambiguous matches into manual review and prohibit automated enforcement exports from that queue. The Fair Credit Reporting Act treats permissible purpose as a substantive condition, including collection-related access only where the statutory conditions apply; a commercial judgment does not itself authorize every consumer report. Likewise, motor vehicle personal information requires an applicable Driver's Privacy Protection Act exception, not merely a useful investigative purpose. Restrict Accurint or TLOxp access to contracted uses, individual accounts, and retained purpose records. Never pretext a bank, employer, or relative to obtain protected information. If a licensed report produces only an address association, return to original records or attorney-directed discovery. The correct fallback for an unresolved person match is a narrower research task, not a larger automated contact campaign.

Instrument the pipeline around false positives and missed events, not just successful HTTP responses. A login page returned with status 200 is not a valid docket response. Validate content type, expected record identifiers, pagination totals, and required fields before accepting an extract. Maintain redacted fixtures for a normal result, empty result, restricted response, amended filing, duplicate debtor name, and scanned document. Python unittest, sqlite3 scratch databases, and structured logging provide enough diagnostics for the first production version. Add exponential backoff with jitter, a bounded retry count, and a manual exception queue; authentication failures should stop that connector rather than trigger repeated login attempts. A weekly review should sample ten accepted matches and every rejected critical event, measuring incorrect joins and stale records. Track cost per verified lead, not cost per returned row. For example, fifty $4 reports cost $200; if only five produce verified new asset leads, acquisition cost is $40 per verified lead before labor. If ten review hours cost an assumed $35 each, the combined cost rises to $110 per verified lead. Stop the purchase tier when that evidence does not improve expected net recovery.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Anchor the matter
   - Register the judgment, authority documents, exact parties, and court identifiers.
   - Verify the filed image and separate principal, interest, costs, prior receipts, and disputed amounts.
2. Run the low-cost source tier
   - Check originating dockets, entity records, bankruptcy indicators, and relevant recorder indexes.
   - Preserve original responses and define the exact unresolved question before buying enrichment.
3. Escalate only decisive gaps
   - Order a licensed report, certified search, title work, or attorney-directed discovery.
   - Record permissible purpose, expected decision value, spending ceiling, and the responsible reviewer.
4. Publish a bounded action packet
   - Export verified facts, source documents, freshness timestamps, and unresolved exceptions.
   - Require current bankruptcy and authority checks before enforcement instructions or client disbursements.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Buy decisive evidence, not larger piles of search results**
> 
> Experienced recovery operators spend on the document that changes the next lawful action.
> - Purchase the actual assignment, release, or continuation instrument when an index entry changes apparent priority; the index label rarely settles scope.
> - Search historical legal names and formation jurisdictions before paying for another broad enrichment report on the same debtor.
> - Retain negative-search scope, query terms, and retrieval time; an empty county search is not evidence that every jurisdiction is clear.
> - Build the attorney packet around source-linked facts and unresolved questions, so counsel does not bill to reconstruct your research trail.

## 3. Convert court events and payment evidence into controlled operator economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Deadline tracking begins with jurisdiction-specific rules, not a universal judgment expiration formula. Store the triggering event, applicable authority, counting convention, review owner, and supporting document for each renewal, response, or enforcement task. Under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 69, execution generally follows the procedure of the state where the federal court sits unless a federal statute applies. That makes state-specific templates essential even inside one federal docket connector. California Code of Civil Procedure section 683.020 provides a useful example of a ten-year enforceability framework, but exceptions, renewal rules, stays, and the particular judgment still require review. A bankruptcy petition can trigger 11 U.S.C. section 362 before a search aggregator reflects it. Screen at intake, monitor active matters, and repeat the check immediately before consequential enforcement activity. Upon credible bankruptcy notice, pause affected collection tasks and obtain counsel's assessment of the debtor, property, claim, and any applicable order. Do not automatically resume after dismissal, discharge, or a vendor status change. The system should generate a review task with the underlying court event, preserving a visible record of who authorized the next step and why.

Payment reconciliation is the second high-value automation lever because cash errors destroy both margin and client trust. Import bank transactions through an authorized bank feed or controlled statement export, match receipts to matters, and hold ambiguous matches outside the disbursement queue. Record gross receipts, earned service fees, reimbursable expenses, client payable balances, and reversals separately. Do not calculate distributable money from a dashboard total that includes uncleared deposits. The illustrative $20,000 receipt with a 25% earned fee produces $5,000 of operator revenue, not $20,000, and does not justify recognizing the entire unpaid judgment. Apply the actual contract, accounting policy, and relevant custody requirements. If counsel holds client money, reconcile your matter ledger to counsel's remittance statement rather than assuming your own operating account should receive it. A daily exception report should flag duplicate payment identifiers, receipts without matters, fees exceeding agreed terms, stale checks, and transfers without supporting settlement instructions. Require a second person to verify changed payment destinations through a previously established contact channel. Keep bank credentials outside source-control files and limit each integration to the minimum access needed for its specific reconciliation function.

Reconcile the desk to the canonical scenarios before approving more subscriptions. Conservative recognizes $60,000 of annual service fees, incurs $48,000 of all-in costs, and leaves $12,000 owner net profit at 20% with 1 FT + 3 C. Base recognizes $280,000, incurs $196,000, and leaves $84,000 at 30.0% with 1 FT + 3 C. Scaled recognizes $500,000, incurs $300,000, and leaves $200,000 at 40% with 2 FT + 4 C. Contractors are staffing capacity, not an assumption that everyone works full time. The stated $2,000–$12,000 monthly operating range is not a cap on total scenario expenses; modeled all-in costs also need to absorb applicable labor, case costs, and other delivery expenses without double counting. At an illustrative $5,000 earned fee per completed matter, the revenue cases require 12, 56, and 100 fee-producing matters respectively. These are capacity equivalents, not predicted collections. A $300 research package on a $5,000 fee opportunity consumes 6% of the fee before enforcement expense and labor. If verified equity disappears or senior claims consume likely proceeds, stop incremental spending rather than trying to rescue sunk research costs with a more aggressive collection tactic.

## 4. Separate tax-credit origination intelligence from credit ownership and recovery proceeds  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The mapped play transferable_tax_credit_brokerage_syndication uses project intelligence to find potential sellers, but its data model must remain separate from judgment enforcement. Do not describe DOE or EPA as nationwide interconnection queue operators. DOE-funded Berkeley Lab queue research aggregates project information, while regional grid operators and utilities maintain their respective queues; EPA redevelopment datasets identify sites, not transferable tax credits. Use PJM downloads, Berkeley Lab's Queued Up research, EPA RE-Powering resources, state film-office publications, and state historic-preservation records as lead sources only. Confirm whether a project exists, who owns the eligible taxpayer entity, whether construction or production milestones were satisfied, and whether the relevant credit is actually transferable. A queue application, film listing, historic designation, or brownfield address proves none of those conclusions by itself. Public records may also omit private project ownership changes and current credit allocations. Obtain seller authorization and original eligibility documents before sending a buyer any transaction-specific representation. Keep recovery-client information out of origination outreach unless its use is independently authorized; a distressed developer's appearance in a judgment file does not make confidential client intelligence available for another business line.

IRC section 6418 permits specified eligible federal credits to move under its conditions; it does not create a freely resalable bearer instrument. The transferee cannot subsequently transfer the purchased credit under that provision. Therefore, the proposed buy-low, sell-high economics must be structured as a direct eligible seller-to-buyer transfer with a documented intermediary fee or other properly analyzed arrangement, not an operator taking title and flipping the same federal credit. For an illustrative $100,000 eligible credit, an $0.85 seller realization and $0.94 buyer outlay create a $9,000 gross economic spread. If documented transaction expenses are $4,000, the remaining contribution is $5,000 before overhead. These are illustrative negotiating assumptions, not current quotes, insurance prices, or additions to the canonical recovery scenarios. When the desk acts as agent, recognize its earned fee under the applicable accounting treatment, not the $100,000 credit face value or the buyer's entire wire. Counsel and the tax specialist must allocate seller consideration, buyer costs, escrow instructions, and intermediary compensation consistently with transfer rules. Georgia film, Louisiana film, historic rehabilitation, and brownfield programs require separate state-specific transferability analysis rather than borrowed federal assumptions.

The risk fallback is strict: never warehouse unhedged credits or advance the seller money against an uncommitted buyer. Require buyer-funded simultaneous escrow, a direct authorized transfer, and a binding tax-credit insurance policy meeting the transaction's required non-cancelable coverage terms. Do not represent that such coverage is always available or that it eliminates exclusions, recapture exposure, or claims disputes. If the insurer cannot provide the required terms, or the buyer will not fund the coordinated closing, decline the transaction rather than weaken this play's conditions. Verify insured parties, coverage period, exclusions, cancellation provisions, claims control, and the specific credit facts covered. Federal pre-filing registration is part of the applicable process, not proof that the IRS has approved substantive eligibility. State program certificates likewise need program-specific review. Keep an immutable closing packet with seller authority, eligibility workpapers, buyer commitments, escrow confirmations, insurance evidence, and fee invoices. Price origination research separately from recovery cases: twenty leads at an assumed $25 research cost consume $500 before specialist diligence. Continue only when documented buyer demand and eligible supply justify that spend, never because a public dataset makes distressed projects easy to enumerate.
