# Chapter 04: Debtor Examinations and Citations to Discover Assets

## Executive Summary

Creditor-side attorneys, collectors, and skip tracers converge on one point: the debtor examination or citation to discover assets forces the debtor under oath, and a no-show escalates to contempt and a warrant that is not an arrest for the debt itself. Street mechanics are specific: Sacramento's AT-138/EJ-125 order costs $60, reaches 150 miles, and needs a good-reason affidavit if repeated within 120 days; Cook County runs SEC 1803 forms plus Attachments A and E with mandatory personal service. Skip tracers script nine asset categories with 2-, 3-, and 4-year lookbacks, while Mr. Grey subpoenas anyone holding $250 or more for the debtor. Disagreements are sharp: Mr. Grey openly intimidates, Santiago Cueto rejects threats, and HELPS Law Group calls exams wasted against debtors whose income is protected.

## The Practitioner Consensus: Under Oath, Personally Served, Backed by Contempt

Every creditor-side speaker frames the examination the same way: it drags the debtor into a room and makes them answer under oath. Sacramento County Public Law Library describes the order of examination as bringing the debtor in physically to answer under penalty of perjury, with false answers open to challenge later [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:00:22]. Fitzgerald & Campbell, APLC says the exam compels both appearance and documents covering where the debtor banks, works, lives, and what they own [rSqWVX1naKw @ 00:00:02]. Courtroom 1401 at the Daley Center runs the Illinois version as a citation to discover assets, which identifies assets and income, can place a hold on bank accounts, wages, and other property, and lets the creditor demand financial books and records while the debtor is questioned under oath, and individual debtors must also complete the Income and Property Statement [RHS2h5dUkHk @ 00:01:03]. Debt Collectors International calls the same device a deposition in aid of execution, deployed when other remedies fail, and stresses that the plaintiff lists the items the debtor must bring, with any omission exposing the debtor to contempt [ZSpyirZPi9E @ 00:00:16]. Whatever the local label, the operator's leverage comes from the oath, the document demand, and the court standing behind both.

The enforcement teeth are just as uniform. Sacramento County Public Law Library warns that a no-show can produce an arrest warrant, which the speaker says tends to get people's attention [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:00:39]. Fitzgerald & Campbell, APLC puts a number on it: the contempt warrant typically carries bail set at the amount of the debt, and while the firm insists there is no debtor's prison, failing to appear can still mean jail, and the pickup is passive: police do not go looking, the debtor gets scooped up at a traffic stop or accident, and the court then calls opposing counsel [rSqWVX1naKw @ 00:01:23]. Debt Collectors International lays out the escalation ladder: serve the summons and order, file and serve a motion to compel after a missed appearance, file a motion for contempt on the second failure, and obtain a writ of bodily attachment, with the firm emphasizing that the arrest is for contempt and not for the debt [ZSpyirZPi9E @ 00:01:42]. Even the debtor-side HELPS Law Group concedes the point, describing a judge-signed order that must be served and a possible warrant after a second no-show [m3ELQTLjg20 @ 00:02:17]. Every camp, creditor or debtor side, treats nonappearance as the debtor's single worst move.

Consensus also holds that the exam is won before anyone is sworn in, and that the net reaches far beyond the debtor. People Locator Skip Tracing opens with the line that a debtor examination is only as productive as the preparation behind it, prescribing a pre-exam skip trace and asset investigation covering real estate owned or recently transferred, vehicles, entities in all 50 states, UCC liens, licenses, and court records, which hands the examiner a basis for cross-examination [nwM1K55PtXM @ 00:00:16]. Santiago Cueto calls the post-judgment debtor's examination one of the most underused tools, pairing it with asset searches across banks, brokerages, real property, vehicles, business filings, and UCC liens that have surfaced out-of-state rentals and six-figure brokerage accounts [W9UHSaFUIn8 @ 00:03:28]. Third parties are fair game. Robert S. Bernstein of the Five Minute Legal Master Series says you can depose any person, not just parties: a banker, a spouse, a business partner, or a neighbor [_O0Yt0qGF-k @ 00:00:58]. Sacramento County Public Law Library flags the third person option on the order form for anyone holding the debtor's property or owing the debtor money, setting up an assignment order [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:01:19]. The shared rule: investigate first, script every category, and widen the circle to whoever touches the money.

## Exact Street Mechanics, Tools & Numbers

Sacramento County Public Law Library gives the most granular filing playbook. The order runs on Judicial Council form AT-138, the same form as EJ-125: check enforcement of judgment, fill line 1 with who the order is directed to, and on line 2 usually check box a for why [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:01:37]. You pick your own hearing date, leaving room for service; Sacramento hears these Tuesday through Thursday in Department 44 at 9:00 a.m., and the saccourt.ca.gov Civil link should be rechecked each time because procedure changes. If the last exam was under 120 days ago, attach an affidavit showing good reason, such as the debtor lying. The fee is $60, a writ of execution can be issued at the same time for free, the backlog runs up to a month or more, and reach extends 150 miles from the courthouse [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:06:37]. The signature street move is the wallet tactic: bring a pre-drafted turnover order with blanks, trace the cash in the debtor's pocket to a non-exempt source, and ask the judge to sign, leaving only parking or bus fare, which pushes debtors toward installments [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:04:34]. Rules of Tomorrow with Tom Howard runs the same endgame: once disclosure shows what is exempt, move for turnover [6mmBEFEMc8g @ 00:04:26].

The question script is where skip tracers get mechanical. People Locator Skip Tracing runs nine categories in order: identity, including all addresses in the past 3 years; employment and income, from salary and commissions to rental, retirement, Social Security, alimony, and trust distributions; business interests with ownership percentage and distributions; every bank, credit union, or brokerage account in the past 2 years plus safe deposit boxes; real estate anywhere with transfers in the past 4 years; vehicles, boats, aircraft, jewelry, art, and firearms; receivables and lawsuits; transfers to family or associates within 2 to 4 years and creditor payments in the 90 days before bankruptcy; and windfalls like inheritance [6mK0uU9RBO8 @ 00:01:36]. The payoff is either the asset or a record of denial that sets up a perjury or fraudulent transfer case. Debt Collectors International adds the indirect angle: instead of asking where the debtor banks, ask what card or account paid the last utility bill [ZSpyirZPi9E @ 00:01:59]. For Illinois filers, Courtroom 1401 maps the paperwork path: Cook County Clerk website, Court Services, Circuit Court forms, search SEC 1803, choose post judgement collection for an individual, complete every field, and e-file with Attachments A and E from the standing orders [RHS2h5dUkHk @ 00:02:22].

Mr. Grey Judgment Enforcement runs pressure as a numbers game. The speaker will subpoena anyone believed to owe the debtor or to hold $250 or more of assets on the debtor's behalf, claims 17 years honing the skill, and refused a 50/50 split on a weak Tempe, Arizona case, countering with just give me the judgment [09e6Gzw3S0U @ 00:01:21]. On a Burbank ORAP, managing associate Dave Doyle ran a four bank levy strategy that, per the debtor's wife, got everything including her safe deposit box; the next moves were a third-party ORAP of the landlord to learn where the debtor banks, a turnover order for the debtor's corporate stock, an assignment order on the shop's revenue with monthly accounting to the judge, and a non-debtor spouse wage garnishment motion against the wife's Cal State University paycheck [499-NK9hWnc @ 00:02:17]. Robert S. Bernstein of the Five Minute Legal Master Series supplies the classic squeeze: the speaker's father subpoenaed a defendant's wife and girlfriend for the same time and place, and the defendant made a deal. On economics, the client pays discovery costs in advance or agrees to reimburse, and many states let those costs and fees be added to the judgment [_O0Yt0qGF-k @ 00:04:11].

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Investigate Before Filing
   - Commission a pre-exam skip trace and asset investigation, then pull prior litigation.
   - Cover real estate owned or recently transferred, vehicles, entities in all 50 states, UCC liens, licenses, and court records, then review divorce, bankruptcy, and earlier collections filings for undisclosed leads [People Locator Skip Tracing — nwM1K55PtXM @ 00:00:50].
2. File the Order and Serve It Personally
   - File the examination order or citation and arrange sheriff or private process server service.
   - Personal service is required; mail or email service is not allowed without a special order, Attachments A and E must ride with the packet, and incorrect service means re-service [Courtroom 1401, Daley Center — RHS2h5dUkHk @ 00:03:32].
3. Script Every Asset Category
   - Write out the full question script and review produced documents before the hearing.
   - Cover real property, vehicles, bank accounts, business interests, income, receivables, pending claims where the debtor is plaintiff, and transfers; review tax returns and bank statements in advance and do not rely on improvisational questioning [People Locator Skip Tracing — nwM1K55PtXM @ 00:01:23].
4. Convert Answers Into Orders
   - Bring a pre-drafted turnover order and act on disclosed accounts immediately.
   - If questioning traces the debtor's cash to a non-exempt source, the judge may sign the blank turnover order on the spot, leaving the debtor parking or bus fare and pushing them toward installment payments [Sacramento County Public Law Library — HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:04:34].

## Where Practitioners Disagree & Alternative Playbooks

Mr. Grey Judgment Enforcement runs the exam as open pressure theater. At a Burbank ORAP on a drunk-driving judgment, the speaker walked in with four or five managing associates. The debtor told the judge they would 'gang-slay us,' the wife complained 'they're trying to intimidate us,' and the speaker answered 'of course i'm trying to intimidate... because that's how i get paid' before the judge cut the entourage down to a single associate [499-NK9hWnc @ 00:00:21]. On a second file, the same operator plans to video-record the exam and 'make him cry,' citing 17 years spent honing the skill and a reputation with judges for making 'grown men cry too' [09e6Gzw3S0U @ 00:00:59]. Santiago Cueto takes the opposite temperament. In Cueto's view, creditors should be feared, but 'not... in a threatening way,' with leverage coming from well-crafted settlement offers and persistence that shows the debtor a 'competent and relentless' opponent [W9UHSaFUIn8 @ 00:08:51]. The Sacramento County Public Law Library sits between the two. It pairs a pre-drafted turnover order aimed at the cash in the debtor's wallet with a deliberate courtesy: leave the debtor parking or bus fare. The speaker says this combination pushes debtors toward installment payments rather than open warfare [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:05:26].

The prep layer splits several ways. The Sacramento County Public Law Library favors covert intelligence because ordering the debtor in tips them off, while trash pulled from the curb never alerts anyone. According to the speaker, people shred statements but not the envelopes and inserts, which reveal the bank, the property, and the county, and trash has produced more useful information than most other techniques [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:13:16]. People Locator Skip Tracing sells the commissioned route: a pre-exam skip trace and asset investigation covering owned or recently transferred real estate, vehicles, entities in all 50 states, UCC liens, licenses, and court records. That work hands the examiner undisclosed facts and a basis for cross-examination [nwM1K55PtXM @ 00:00:16]. The same channel pitches pre-examination asset reports so attorneys walk in 'already knowing many of the answers' [6mK0uU9RBO8 @ 00:02:35]. Santiago Cueto runs asset searches across banks, brokerages, real property, vehicles, business filings, and UCC liens, and reports turning up out-of-state rentals and six-figure brokerage accounts that way [W9UHSaFUIn8 @ 00:03:28]. The real choice is covert curbside work that never alerts the debtor versus documented reports built to support sworn cross-examination. Operators pick based on how badly they need surprise versus how badly they need a paper trail.

Practitioners also split on whether the exam is worth calendaring at all. Santiago Cueto calls the post-judgment debtor's examination 'one of the most underused tools,' backed by perjury exposure for lies and an arrest warrant for a no-show [W9UHSaFUIn8 @ 00:02:34]. From the debtor bench, HELPS Law Group's Eric Olsson sends plaintiffs a letter stating that the senior client's income is Social Security or pension, then mails proof that the income is protected. Olsson reports the creditor usually drops the exam or takes it by phone. Only creditors who insist 'once or twice a year' get a pro bono attorney sent in to sit for it [m3ELQTLjg20 @ 00:03:55]. Jurisdiction changes the teeth. Law Accent describes a judgment summons under the Sheriff and Civil Process Act where a debtor who has the means but refuses to pay faces committal to prison, alongside attachment and sale of property or a monthly installment order [vhXnhEvki3E @ 00:04:44]. Debt Collectors International warns that some judges resist issuing contempt and bodily attachment orders while others sign them without a hearing, so the same escalation ladder lands differently from courtroom to courtroom [ZSpyirZPi9E @ 00:01:25]. Against protected-income debtors, the defense side frames the exam as a waste of everyone's time. Against concealers, the oath is the entire point.

## Firsthand Failure Modes, Traps & Economics Checks

The first trap is the exam itself. The Sacramento County Public Law Library warns that ordering a debtor in tips them off. Money can migrate to a different bank before the creditor moves, so levies have to follow quickly once questioning exposes where the cash sits [HrhPbWDRBVU @ 00:05:46]. Service is the second choke point. Fitzgerald & Campbell stress that the order must be personally served, not mailed, because the exam compels both appearance and documents showing where the debtor banks, works, lives, and what they own [rSqWVX1naKw @ 00:00:25]. People Locator Skip Tracing treats current address information from a professional skip trace as a prerequisite to landing that service at all [leX2BIpiyNQ @ 00:00:33]. Courtroom 1401 at the Daley Center spells out what a sloppy packet costs. Incorrect service means re-service, and the citation stays inactive until it is served. A failed personal service requires an alias citation, and an unserved citation cannot be continued, so a continuance order is wasted paper [RHS2h5dUkHk @ 00:04:15]. The same courtroom denies relief to anyone ignoring its standing orders and requires Attachments A and E inside the served packet. Every week lost to a bad address or a defective packet hands the debtor another week to reposition money.

The second trap is a question worded too narrowly. On AceOfCoins, an advisor coaches a caller who opened an LLC solely in the caller's own name ahead of Colorado interrogatories. The advisor says the entity can stay off the answers unless a question names it, then states flatly, 'I'll lie if they can never discover it' [3qP0K9KK5G8 @ 00:03:37]. The same advisor recommends adding a family member to turn the entity into a 'nominee LLC,' so a creditor's writ reaches only the caller's distributions [3qP0K9KK5G8 @ 00:04:56]. Santiago Cueto sees the same moves from the creditor side: debtors lie, drive leased Mercedes, and park a million-dollar home in a spouse's name. In one example, a debtor deeded a home to a spouse the day after judgment. Cueto attacks those moves with Uniform Voidable Transactions Act claims and with reverse veil piercing built on commingling, ignored formalities, or personal use of company funds [W9UHSaFUIn8 @ 00:05:54]. People Locator Skip Tracing counters with systematic coverage of every asset category. The exam then produces either the asset or 'a record of denial' that sets up a perjury or fraudulent transfer case [6mK0uU9RBO8 @ 00:00:00]. Examiners who ask explicitly about every LLC, every entity held by family, and every transfer close that gap.

The third trap is spending hard on a thin file or losing the bench. Mr. Grey Judgment Enforcement ran a four bank levy strategy on a drunk-driving judgment. The speaker then summed up the proceeds as 'maybe we'll be able to buy a couple of burgers,' with no dollar figure given [499-NK9hWnc @ 00:01:40]. The same operator dealt with a Tempe creditor holding a Craigslist rental-scam judgment against a landlord who 'doesn't own the property.' The speaker told the creditor 'we're never gonna collect,' refused the requested 50/50 split because the case sat low on the totem pole, and countered with 'just give me the judgment' [09e6Gzw3S0U @ 00:00:20]. Robert S. Bernstein on Five Minute Legal Master Series has clients pay discovery costs in advance or agree to reimburse them, noting that many states let those costs and fees be added to the judgment [_O0Yt0qGF-k @ 00:04:11]. Courtroom conduct carries its own bill. In a Kitchen Court Cases hearing, Judge Sylvia Holmes fined plaintiff Mark Alpert $100 for direct contempt for talking over the bench. The judge ruled that the plaintiff takes nothing over a failure to itemize the deposit, calling that duty 'the absolute clear line in this case' [nRWberpO6tg @ 02:27:03]. Treble damages died because both sides 'acted in bad faith,' leaving the defendant $2,350.
