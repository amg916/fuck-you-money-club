## 1. Build an evidence-first docket pipeline before automating decisions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Design the pipeline around a case event, not an AI conversation. The full-time operator owns intake, queue economics, and client communication; three contractors cover authorized research, bookkeeping, and licensed attorney work under separate engagements. Contractor status does not create permission to practice law or collect in a jurisdiction requiring a license. Each accepted file receives a case identifier, court identifier, creditor identity, debtor entity identifier, judgment date, entered amount, assignment evidence, and source-document digest. Use PACER for federal records and the relevant state court portal for state proceedings, through permitted access methods and approved accounts. A Python worker can ingest permitted downloads with urllib.request, normalize timestamps with datetime, and deduplicate documents with hashlib. Store transactional state in PostgreSQL through a separately reviewed deployment integration, or SQLite for a genuinely single-worker installation. Keep original files immutable and attach extracted claims to page references. Treat the 15 mapped plays as consumers of this shared evidence layer, not as permission to reuse assumptions across jurisdictions. A commercial guaranty, consumer obligation, HOA assessment, and purchased judgment can require different authority checks even when the same person appears in the docket.

Send only minimized, authorized document excerpts to an approved LLM endpoint, with retention settings consistent with the engagement. Ask for a fixed JSON schema containing extracted values, quoted source spans, page numbers, and explicit review reasons. Python validates field types, checks dates against the source, and rejects unsupported assertions; the model never supplies a deadline from memory. Treat docket text as untrusted input, including instructions embedded in exhibits. A rule registry stores jurisdiction, rule citation, effective dates, triggering event, calendar convention, and attorney reviewer. California Code of Civil Procedure section 683.020 supplies a general ten-year enforcement framework subject to exceptions and renewal rules, not a universal countdown for every obligation. Build proposed dates from reviewed rules, then require the responsible professional to confirm consequential deadlines against the operative docket. Bankruptcy alerts invoke a stay-review workflow under 11 U.S.C. section 362. Section 108(c) requires separate analysis; software must not assume every state limitation period simply stops running. Changes to a trigger date invalidate downstream calculations and generate a visible correction task. Every proposed filing remains a draft until the authorized person reviews the actual source packet.

Measure automation against a stable workload rather than counting colorful dashboard actions. For an illustrative monthly cohort of 1,000 routine research, qualification, fulfillment-preparation, and acquisition-administration work items, the manual baseline is ten minutes each, or 10,000 minutes. Suppose 750 items complete extraction and routing automatically but retain one minute of human verification each; 250 exceptions require eight minutes each. Human handling becomes 2,750 minutes, a 72.5% reduction, while straight-through machine processing covers 75% of items. That result does not mean 75% of enforcement decisions are automated. Consequential approvals within this routine cohort must fit the measured review time; contested litigation and complex priority opinions remain separately budgeted work. Track corrections, missed triggers, stale sources, and reopened exceptions alongside minutes saved. Sample completed low-risk extractions weekly and inspect every material deadline change. Require four consecutive measured weeks before treating the reduction as an operating assumption. If exception volume rises, narrow the supported courts or document types instead of suppressing review. The useful deliverable is a reproducible evidence packet with an accountable reviewer, not an impressive percentage detached from case consequences.

## 2. Reconcile every payment and price every automated run  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Build payment processing as an append-only ledger of observed events, not a spreadsheet that overwrites yesterday's balance. A signed bank or payment-provider webhook reaches a narrow receiver that verifies the signature over the raw request body, checks timestamp freshness, and records the provider event identifier before returning success. Python hmac and hashlib can implement provider-specified verification without inventing a signature format. Unique constraints prevent duplicate posting, while a durable queue separates receipt from reconciliation. Use decimal.Decimal for money and retain currency, value date, payer reference, case identifier, and source transaction identifier. Distinguish pending funds, settled funds, returned payments, creditor liabilities, earned service fees, and recoverable expenses. Allocate interest and principal only through a reviewed jurisdiction-specific ledger rule. A receipt is not automatically earned revenue, and an earned fee is not automatically available cash. Where funds belong in an attorney trust account or another required custodial arrangement, the recovery operator does not route them through an ordinary operating account. Bookkeeping prepares the allocation; the authorized signatory controls disbursement. Reversal events create linked compensating entries rather than deleting the original receipt, preserving the complete creditor statement history.

Reconcile the scorecard using recognized service fees exclusively. An illustrative 25% fee on settled client recoveries produces $60,000 of Conservative revenue from $240,000 collected, $280,000 of Base revenue from $1,120,000 collected, and $500,000 of Scaled revenue from $2,000,000 collected. These collection amounts are explanatory assumptions, not additional scorecard revenue or promised recoveries. Total annual operating costs must equal $48,000, $196,000, and $300,000 respectively, leaving exactly $12,000 at 20%, $84,000 at 30.0%, and $200,000 at 40%. Conservative and Base retain 1 FT + 3 C; Scaled uses 2 FT + 4 C. Contractor headcount does not imply four full-time salaries. The stated $2,000–$12,000 monthly operating range should be tracked as a planning range for recurring overhead, while the scenario reconciliation must also include variable contractor work, acquisition, research, and operator-borne enforcement expenses. The Base cost envelope averages about $16,333 monthly when all annual costs are included. Assign each expense to the correct bucket and prevent recoverable client advances from being mistaken for earned fees. A larger judgment face amount improves neither revenue nor margin until actual economics support recognition.

Price automation at the work-item level before buying a larger stack. Assume each routine run consumes $0.18 of parsing, $0.08 of LLM inference, $0.04 of orchestration, and $0.12 of storage and monitoring allocation: $0.42 total. At 1,000 monthly runs, that is $420 of machine cost. The earlier 2,750 human minutes equal 45.833 hours; at an illustrative loaded $45 hourly rate, review costs $2,062.50. These assumptions exclude separately priced court retrieval, specialist opinions, contested proceedings, acquisition spend, and disbursement work outside the routine cohort. Vendor quotes and measured time must replace assumptions before committing capacity. Retry transient failures with bounded exponential backoff, then move exhausted jobs to an exception queue. During a provider outage, accept no blind ledger updates: reconcile bank statements manually, preserve event identifiers, and replay only after duplicate detection passes. A cheap run that misallocates client money is an expensive operational defect.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Authenticate and persist the incoming payment event
   - Verify the provider signature and timestamp, then durably store the event under a unique provider identifier.
   - Acknowledge delivery only after durable receipt; repeated deliveries return the existing result without reposting money.
2. Match the receipt to its authorized case ledger
   - Use transaction references and verified payer details to propose a case match and settlement classification.
   - Ambiguous references enter a suspense queue; no model may resolve competing case matches by probability alone.
3. Review allocation and prepare the creditor statement
   - Apply approved fee and allocation rules, reconcile totals, and obtain the required human authorization.
   - Keep earned fees, client liabilities, expenses, and returned funds separate; source every amount to a ledger event.
4. Reconcile settlement and close the exception loop
   - Match the ledger against the bank statement, investigate differences, and record the final reconciliation evidence.
   - Replay missed events through the same duplicate controls; preserve reversals and reviewer decisions for later reconstruction.

## 3. Turn reviewed knowledge into creditor acquisition loops  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build distribution for creditors and referral partners, not for public exposure of debtors. Programmatic pages should explain specific commercial recovery workflows: locating an entered judgment, preparing assignment records, assessing enforcement costs, or organizing a lien-priority review. Publish a page only when its jurisdictional differences justify distinct content and a named reviewer has checked the sources. An LLM can draft from the approved rule registry, but it cannot invent recoverability statistics, attorney relationships, or local office locations. Use Google Search Console to monitor search queries and a first-party intake form to capture creditor authorization, court, judgment date, and available documents. Do not collect a full sensitive case file through an unrestricted marketing form. A Python webhook validates the submission, creates a deduplicated lead, and sends a secure upload invitation after basic screening. Record source attribution without leaking debtor names into analytics URLs. Separate marketing contacts from case subjects so a debtor never enters a creditor prospecting sequence because their name appeared in a docket. If a page's governing authority changes, remove its automated recommendations until a reviewer refreshes the content and confirms any linked intake logic.

Make the referral loop useful before making it viral. Give commercial litigators, credit managers, and receivables teams a reusable judgment-transfer checklist, a sample evidence index, and a plain explanation of case acceptance criteria. The artifact should save partner labor even when no referral occurs. Allow partners to forward the public checklist while keeping client documents inside controlled workspaces. After a completed engagement, request permission to publish a genuinely de-identified process case study; removing a name alone may not prevent identification through a distinctive judgment amount or court history. Avoid referral payments that conflict with applicable attorney professional-conduct rules or collection restrictions. For outbound email, target verified business creditor roles using lawfully obtained contact data, truthful sender information, accurate subjects, a valid postal address, and an effective opt-out process under 15 U.S.C. section 7704. Maintain suppression across every sending system and honor statutory timing. Do not expand automatically into texts or autodialed calls, which require separate analysis under 47 U.S.C. section 227 and implementing rules. A referral engine fails when convenience turns case records into unauthorized marketing data or when an automated follow-up ignores a recipient's objection.

Model acquisition as cohorts with delayed cash, because the scorecard allows six to twenty-four months to the first deal. At the illustrative 25% fee, each paying matter corresponds to $56,000 of client collections, and twenty produce $1,120,000 of underlying recoveries. A $24,000 acquisition allocation implies $100 per qualified inquiry, $400 per accepted engagement, and $1,200 per paying matter. Include that allocation inside the $196,000 Base cost envelope alongside the $29,790 routine handling bundle, leaving $142,210 for all remaining modeled costs. Track intake qualification, acceptance, collectible-asset evidence, realized fees, and elapsed months separately. Stop scaling a channel that supplies large nominal judgments but repeated insolvency findings. When organic traffic falls, prioritize partner checklists and existing creditor renewals rather than publishing hundreds of unreviewed jurisdiction pages. Acquisition quality is measured by collectible, authorized work, not database size.
