## 2. Install a transactional production ledger before automating handoffs  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use one authoritative operational subledger for contract state, accepted service, invoices, cash receipts, and operating costs. The six modules below are sequential Python source fragments: concatenate them in order into one reference script, initialize a local database once, and give each worker its own connection. They use only the standard library. Store money as integer cents and durations as integer minutes; never round a floating-point subtotal into the ledger. A source-system key identifies the business object, while a separate request key identifies the attempted operation. Both matter: retry protection alone cannot stop two different requests from billing the same monthly service. UNIQUE constraints therefore protect source leads, contract periods, service invoices, bank references, and cost references independently. Every mutation begins with BEGIN IMMEDIATE, checks existing state under the same write lock, inserts all dependent records, and commits the idempotency result atomically. Concurrent retries either observe the committed result or wait; changed contents under the same key fail. A lock timeout returns failure for a later same-key retry. There is no read-then-write race outside the transaction in these write paths, and a failed operation leaves neither partial business records nor a successful request marker.

The verification state machine is scoped, priced, signed, verified, active, paused, and closed. Its hold is an internal restriction on mobilization, not a customer-money escrow arrangement. Contract funds remain ordinary cleaning receipts unless a separately designed banking arrangement actually exists. Attach document digests for signed scope, wage planning, safety screening, insurance, and access testing; the digest links to a controlled document repository and does not prove that the document is accurate or current. The supervisor must review the contents, monitor expiry dates outside this compact reference, and pause service when a required condition lapses. Module four implements one completed recurring service period per contract and atomically creates its invoice at the stored monthly price. Do not feed partial-month work, deposits, adjustments, or variable project charges into that function. Those require explicit schedules, credit documents, and additional posting types. Cleaning revenue represents the operator's earned service fees; a medical customer's collections, a property owner's rent roll, and customer purchase-order ceilings are not operator revenue. Completion evidence must support the service period, while the financial close separately tests whether recognition matches the actual contract and accounting policy.

Keep this SQLite file on local persistent storage behind one authorized ledger service, not on a shared network filesystem. Restrict direct database writes so scheduling connectors cannot bypass the transaction functions. Connecteam or Swept can supply assignments and inspection exports; QuickBooks Online can receive reviewed invoice and expense exports. They are peripheral systems, not competing sources of invoice identity. Never make a remote billing call inside a database transaction and assume it shares the commit. This reference deliberately performs no external side effects; an added integration needs a transactional outbox and destination-side idempotency, with delivery receipts reconciled independently. Module five matches bank cash without allowing receipts above the invoice balance, and its snapshot uses one read transaction for consistent monthly and receivable views. Costs carry separate expense and payment periods so accrual profit does not masquerade as available cash. The compact model supports fully paid cost documents, not partial accounts-payable settlements. Module six supplies concurrent replay, rejected-transition, overpayment, rollback, and integrity checks; these checks are provided for execution, not reported as already run. Back up through SQLite's backup interface and test restoration before relying on the ledger for a payroll-week close.

The supervisor owns assignment changes, substitute training, and client communication; cleaners should not discover a medical scope change from an informal message left at reception. Each shift packet includes site identity, access instructions, task frequencies, product names, dilution method, required protective equipment, and escalation contacts. Under 29 CFR 1910.1200, maintain the applicable written hazard communication program, labels, safety data sheets, and employee information and training. Select disinfectants registered for the intended use and follow label instructions, including contact time and surface restrictions; 7 U.S.C. 136j(a)(2)(G) generally prohibits pesticide use inconsistent with labeling, subject to statutory exceptions. A misted surface that dries before its required wet contact time is a production defect, even when an inspection photograph looks clean. Do not mix chemicals or substitute a product based only on fragrance or price. Separate clean and dirty equipment, record dilution-system faults, and stop tasks when the specified protective or ventilation conditions cannot be met. Schedule corrective work as paid capacity rather than asking employees to absorb it off the clock.

Medical janitorial work begins with an exposure determination, not a blanket assumption that every clinic requires identical procedures. Where occupational exposure to blood or other potentially infectious materials exists, 29 CFR 1910.1030 establishes requirements including an exposure control plan, appropriate controls, training, hepatitis B vaccination provisions, and post-exposure evaluation. Confirm which party handles regulated waste, sharps containers, isolation areas, and spill response. A cleaner encountering an uncapped needle should isolate the area and invoke the agreed response, not improvise pickup to preserve a route's completion percentage. The supervisor inspects a rotating sample of high-risk surfaces and a separate sample of ordinary appearance tasks; otherwise a polished lobby can conceal failed restroom execution. Record severity, affected room, failed task, corrective owner, and closure time. Avoid patient information in photographs and free-text notes. Link rework to the original service identifier so the site-level cost report includes the second visit. A complaint closed by telephone without confirming the correction remains an unresolved quality event. Contractor rework obligations belong in the purchased scope, but employee rework still creates compensable time and must enter payroll.

At month-end, reconcile planned visits, completed visits, exceptions, accepted service, invoices, and bank receipts in that order. A missing inspection record is an evidence problem; a missed visit may also be a price or service-credit problem. Resolve the contractual treatment before using the fixed-fee close function. The reference records one monthly invoice per completed contract period and does not silently reduce invoice amounts when the bank pays short. For a disputed deduction, preserve the original invoice, investigate the reason, and issue an authorized adjustment through a separately implemented credit workflow. Reconcile payroll to approved time, subcontract invoices to accepted scopes, and supply spending to delivered stock. The supervisor prepares the evidence packet; an existing owner or bookkeeper reviews the bank reconciliation within the stated overhead budget, rather than adding an invisible tenth full-time person. Separate preparation from review whenever practical and retain the review record. Every Friday, inspect aging receivables and the next two payroll dates together. A profitable contract paid late can exhaust the pod's working cash before its annual margin changes. Invoice delivery confirmation and a named accounts-payable contact are therefore production outputs, not optional office cleanup.
