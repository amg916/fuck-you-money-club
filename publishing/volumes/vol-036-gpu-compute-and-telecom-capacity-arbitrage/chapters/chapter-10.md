## 1. Turn Fifteen Plays into a Bounded Event System  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build one event spine rather than a collection of autonomous agents. A Python HTTPS handler receives supplier updates, buyer forms, payment notifications, and provisioning callbacks; durable storage records each event before acknowledging acceptance. Use an envelope containing event_id, tenant_id, event_type, occurred_at, schema_version, source_reference, and payload_digest. A uniqueness constraint on source plus event_id prevents duplicate work, while an outbox publishes tasks only after the corresponding transaction commits. Workers claim tasks with leases and bounded retries; expired leases return work to the queue without repeating an external purchase. Keep credentials in a managed secret store, separate tenant authorization from event routing, and reject unrecognized event versions. The full-time operator owns commercial exceptions and weekly economics. One contractor owns infrastructure reliability; the other owns carrier operations and evidence review. The fifteen-play mapping here comprises seven intake plays in the first matrix, seven fulfillment plays in the second, and the supplied citation-distribution play in section three. The descriptive local labels organize implementation; they do not assert additional canonical catalog identifiers. Every play produces an evidence record, a bounded proposed action, and an explicit recovery state. A stalled worker must never silently become a successful customer order.

Automate research through permitted APIs, supplier exports, and monitored rate sheets, not unrestricted browser agents with purchasing authority. Normalize H100, H200, and B200 offers by memory capacity, interconnect, region, tenancy, minimum term, cancellation rights, and actual available inventory. Store quoted timestamps because an attractive hourly price without a valid delivery window is not executable capacity. For SIP, retain destination prefixes, billing increments, setup charges, route restrictions, and effective dates; for IPv4, verify delegated control, permitted subleasing, routing support, and reputation history. An LLM may extract candidate fields into a fixed JSON schema, but deterministic validators enforce currencies, units, and required evidence links. Treat supplier documents as untrusted data: embedded instructions cannot modify system rules or invoke tools. An illustrative quote-analysis run costs $0.006 for extraction, $0.002 for validation infrastructure, and $0.002 for storage and delivery, totaling $0.010 before human review. At 3,000 runs monthly, direct automation costs $30. Compare that with the measured exception burden rather than calling the entire research function free. If a supplier changes its format, quarantine the affected feed and preserve the last verified offer with its original expiry. Never extend that expiry merely to keep a comparison page populated.

Define the automation denominator before celebrating productivity. In an illustrative weekly cohort of 1,000 eligible repetitive tasks, 740 finish without human execution and 260 require review, producing a 74% straight-through rate. Tasks count only after their output passes validation and reaches its intended system; failed retries, empty summaries, and duplicate callbacks do not count again. At six manual minutes per task, the counterfactual workload is 100 hours. If the 260 exceptions consume four minutes each and a 5% sample of the 740 automated completions consumes two minutes each, human handling takes 18.57 hours before maintenance and incident work. This is an illustrative capacity calculation, not a measured staffing result. Track exception rates separately for research, qualification, fulfillment, and acquisition so easy enrichment cannot conceal broken provisioning. Qualification rules should reject absent prepaid funding, prohibited resale terms, incompatible retention requirements, and unsupported calling uses before producing a quote. Human review remains mandatory for new minimum commitments, unusual identity evidence, privileged access, and consequential public claims. Suspend the affected automation when sampled errors exceed its agreed tolerance, even if overall throughput looks healthy. The purpose is fewer repeated decisions, not fewer people noticing expensive mistakes.

## 2. Connect Prepaid Money, Metered Service, and Safe Fulfillment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat Stripe as the payment processor, not as the authoritative usage ledger. Verify webhook signatures against the raw request body, apply replay protections, and record each processor event once. Payment states differ by payment method, so credit service balances only under a documented funding policy that accounts for delayed settlement and disputes. Maintain an append-only ledger separating funded credits, reserved credits, consumed credits, reversals, and adjustments. Before dispatching a request, atomically reserve its maximum permitted charge using the pricing version attached to the quote. Finalization replaces the reservation with measured consumption and releases the unused remainder; duplicate completion events must have no additional effect. For an illustrative $100 funded account, a $3 reservation leaves $97 available. If the completed job costs $2.40, consumption becomes $2.40 and available funds become $97.60. A worker timeout does not prove that upstream execution stopped, so do not release its hold until cancellation or usage reconciliation establishes the outcome. Place unresolved reservations in an age-based exception queue. Humans authorize refunds and disputed balance adjustments, while automated service suspension follows the agreed account policy. Keep all prices in integer minor currency units or explicit fixed-point decimal arithmetic.

Place LiteLLM behind authenticated tenant endpoints and route only among providers permitted by the customer's region, model, retention, and quality requirements. vLLM serves self-hosted inference pools; NVIDIA DCGM supplies hardware telemetry, including utilization and device health, rather than a complete customer billing system. Attribute usage through scheduler allocation records and request identifiers, then reconcile those records with inference token counts and infrastructure invoices. Hardware partitioning is generation- and configuration-dependent: validate supported isolation rather than treating every GPU slice as an equivalent security boundary. An illustrative million-token bundle sells for $10 and consumes $3.50 in upstream service, leaving $6.50 before other costs, consistent with the scorecard's aggregate 65% retained share. This example is a normalized bundle, not a vendor price quotation. A $0.70 fallback retry reduces that bundle's retention to $5.80; aggregate economics therefore need measured retry budgets. Use circuit breakers for timeouts, token inflation, and provider errors. Never fail over to a provider that violates a zero-data-retention commitment merely because it is available. When no eligible route remains, return an explicit capacity failure and reconcile the reservation against actual billable work. Separate control-plane metadata from customer prompts throughout recovery.

Automated least-cost routing must optimize acceptable completion cost, not the cheapest advertised SIP minute. A route selling 1,000 minutes for $20 with $7 upstream termination leaves $13 before other costs; setup fees, duration rounding, and failed-call charges can erase that illustrative spread. Use destination-specific cost ceilings alongside answer-seizure ratio, average call duration, post-dial delay, and fraud signals. These metrics are diagnostic, not proof that traffic is legitimate. Authenticate customers, restrict destinations, apply account velocity limits, and suspend suspected abuse for review. STIR/SHAKEN attestation reflects provider knowledge and authorization; it is not a legitimacy certificate. Determine actual provider roles under 47 CFR 64.6301 and 64.6305 before assigning authentication and Robocall Mitigation Database duties. For 10DLC, submit accurate brand and campaign evidence through supported provider interfaces; The Campaign Registry registration does not replace recipient permission or TCPA requirements. Poll asynchronous provisioning states and expose rejection reasons to a reviewer instead of repeatedly resubmitting altered identities. Review FCC Form 499-A obligations against the actual telecommunications offering and applicable exemptions. A failed messaging registration should leave the account inactive for that use case, not divert its traffic onto unrelated numbers.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Authenticate and persist the event
   - Verify the source, validate the schema, and insert the event with a uniqueness constraint.
   - Acknowledge only after durable persistence; reject invalid signatures without issuing downstream service requests.
2. Reserve funds and select eligible service
   - Atomically reserve the quoted maximum charge and attach the allowed provider set.
   - A single transaction records the reservation and dispatch outbox entry, preventing paid-but-untracked execution.
3. Execute with bounded retries
   - Submit using a stable request identifier and enforce deadline, retry, and cost limits.
   - Check upstream status before repeating ambiguous requests; incompatible providers remain excluded during failures.
4. Reconcile consumption and close the task
   - Match usage evidence, post consumption once, release verified surplus, and record completion.
   - Send unmatched executions to a human exception queue while preserving the original ledger and evidence trail.
