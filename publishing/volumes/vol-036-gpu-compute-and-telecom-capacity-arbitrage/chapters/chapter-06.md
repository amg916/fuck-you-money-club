## 1. Mine GPU supply dislocations and attach each one to a buyer trigger  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a narrow executable inventory: one geography, one GPU family, one workload, and one service promise. H100, H200, and B200 listings are prospecting signals, not interchangeable products. Record accelerator memory, interconnect, host RAM, storage, network egress, available dates, minimum commitment, and contractual resale permission. Compare public offers from CoreWeave, Lambda, RunPod, and specialist bare-metal hosts, then request dated written quotes rather than copying headline hourly prices. Mine provider status histories, announced capacity additions, public procurement awards, and supplier newsletters for timing mismatches. A new cluster can create seller urgency; it does not prove spare inventory or favorable terms. On the buyer side, public engineering posts about inference latency, hiring notices for GPU infrastructure engineers, and product launches with image or speech features identify testable problems. Store the source URL, observation date, proposed workload, business contact, and next verification question in a CRM. Never infer a prospect's private consumption from a job advertisement. The first outreach should offer a reproducible benchmark or dated capacity window, not assert knowledge of their cloud invoice. Separate buyer evidence from supplier evidence so an attractive discount cannot masquerade as demonstrated demand.

Translate every supply signal into a minimum sell-through calculation before contacting buyers. An illustrative 720-hour H100 reservation at $1.60 per hour costs $1,152; selling 480 hours at $2.80 produces $1,344 and only $192 before support, egress, payment fees, and credits. Bare reservation break-even is 412 sold hours after rounding upward, approximately 57.2% utilization; the operating break-even is higher. A seller calling that a 43% markup has not established owner profit. Prefer a short paid workload test on flexible capacity, followed by a buyer reservation covering the supplier's noncancelable exposure. For inference, benchmark input and output tokens separately, including context length, cache treatment, concurrency, time to first token, and accepted-output quality. LiteLLM can route requests and vLLM can serve compatible models, but neither makes upstream processing terms equivalent. Pin the approved provider set for zero-retention customers and disable payload logging throughout the serving path. Use NVIDIA DCGM for operational telemetry and a separate billing ledger for chargeable units. Stop offering the route when its measured contribution turns negative; do not silently switch to a cheaper model, weaker region, or different retention policy to preserve spread.

Package the first eight mapped plays below as distinct buying conversations, not eight simultaneous infrastructure launches. A founder with one employee-equivalent role and two contractors should choose one compute wedge and retain alternatives as referral opportunities. Public GitHub issues, Hugging Face discussions, and engineering conference recordings can reveal batch deadlines or serving constraints; read them manually or through permitted interfaces, and use published business contact channels rather than harvesting personal addresses. Create a one-page benchmark card showing hardware, model revision, quantization, test date, workload shape, delivered units, and exclusions. Ask prospects to provide synthetic or approved evaluation data, never production secrets in a discovery form. Match commercial terms to the signal: a launch deadline supports a bounded burst reservation, while stable nightly queues support scheduled capacity. A public request for proposals can justify a response only after checking submission requirements and delivery obligations. Do not imply that a procurement notice is an award. Maintain two qualified suppliers for each promised service, but validate migration time and buyer permission before advertising redundancy. The fallback is usually a smaller, reversible pilot rather than a larger reservation purchased in anticipation of demand.

> 
> Experienced brokers constrain both delivery exposure and measurement ambiguity before discussing discounts.

## 2. Mine telecom intent without mistaking subsidies or traffic volume for your revenue  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Telecom demand mining begins with service changes that buyers already disclose: contact-center migrations, new branch openings, public communications procurements, application launches requiring transactional messaging, and network expansions. Use SAM.gov and municipal purchasing portals for documented needs, FCC carrier records for entity checks, and published network contacts for IPv4 discussions. A Robocall Mitigation Database entry helps verify a provider's filing status; it does not certify every offered route. Ask SIP prospects for destination mix, authorized traffic purpose, existing completion metrics, and representative call-detail records stripped of unnecessary personal information. A million minutes billed at $0.008 against $0.005 termination creates $3,000 before platform charges, fraud, support, and taxes or fees allocated to the operator. One expensive destination burst can consume that spread, so use prepaid balances, destination restrictions, velocity limits, and tested disconnect behavior. Least-cost routing should rank only routes meeting the buyer's quality and authorization requirements. For 10DLC, obtain sender identity, use case, and opt-in evidence before registration; TCR registration is an ecosystem requirement, not permission to send unsolicited traffic. For IPv4, verify holder authority, routing authorization, and reputation before advertising address availability.

The mandatory play mvno_lifeline_acp_telecom_subsidy_aggregation requires a narrower operating interpretation than its historical label suggests. ACP ended on June 1, 2024; exclude ACP receipts from a new-deal model unless a later enacted program and current implementing authority establish an available benefit. Lifeline support generally reaches $9.25 monthly for qualifying broadband service or qualifying bundles, with up to $25 additional support for eligible Tribal subscribers; voice-only support has separate rules. Neither a white-label MVNO agreement nor aggregator status independently creates reimbursement eligibility. The ETC receives support subject to designation, subscriber eligibility, qualifying service, usage, and claims requirements. SNAP, Medicaid, and SSI participation can establish an eligibility pathway, but household limits and verification still apply. California LifeLine has its own current tariffs, reimbursement rules, and program administration; never add a generic state amount to federal support and market the result as a universal entitlement. Likewise, a $2–$4 wholesale quote may cover a restricted allowance rather than unlimited high-speed data. Verify actual network entitlement, throttling, device costs, activation fees, and geographic availability. Reject an unlimited-service headline whenever the underlying plan cannot substantiate that claim.

For this TRACK_2 play, source the first deal through a documented ETC partnership and a small authorized enrollment channel, not a projection of hundreds of thousands of households. Request the ETC agreement, approved territories, approved scripts, benefit verification workflow, commission schedule, and reversal rules. For 100 ordinary eligible lines, $9.25 support produces $925 of ETC support; an illustrative $3 per-line network cost leaves $625 before every other ETC expense. That is not the aggregator's revenue. If the contract instead pays the aggregator $20 per validated activation, 100 activations generate $2,000 in commissions; a modeled 10% reversal reserve leaves $1,800 before acquisition and service costs. Acquire customers through authorized community organizations using voluntary referrals; never buy benefit-recipient lists or collect unnecessary eligibility documents. Schedule a Day-20 SMS or IVR reminder only through authorized contact channels and explain how subscribers can make qualifying use. Under the applicable nonusage rules, 30 days without qualifying usage triggers a required 15-day notice-and-cure process, not automatic immediate termination. A reply counts only when it satisfies the actual qualifying-use criteria. Apply agent clawbacks only under signed commission terms, preserving subscriber rights and auditable usage evidence.

## 3. Qualify contribution, authority, and delivery before quoting a paid pilot  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Score each opportunity out of 100 using six evidence-backed dimensions: measured demand earns 25 points, contribution earns 25, buyer authority earns 15, delivery fit earns 15, permitted use earns 10, and payment readiness earns 10. Give full demand points only for buyer-provided usage evidence or a bounded workload with a real deadline. Give full contribution points only after counting unavoidable reservation cost, variable supply, egress, processing fees, expected credits, support, and reversals. The working threshold is 75 points for a paid pilot and 85 for considering a longer commitment, with no unresolved identity, permitted-use, or resale-rights failure. These are operating choices, not measured industry benchmarks. A high score cannot compensate for prohibited traffic or missing authority to process customer data. Capture the budget owner, technical evaluator, contract signer, and upstream account owner separately; one enthusiastic engineer rarely fills all four roles. For subsidy aggregation, replace compute consumption evidence with valid ETC authorization and the specific commission-paying event. Keep sensitive eligibility evidence inside the approved verification system, not the general CRM. Missing evidence lowers the score and creates an assigned next action rather than an optimistic forecast entry.

Reconcile the portfolio to Formula v2.0 before presenting earnings. Conservative gross billings of $150,000 less 35% upstream cost of $52,500 leave $97,500 retained contribution; subtracting $70,500 of other modeled costs produces $27,000 owner profit. Base billings of $675,000 less $236,250 leave $438,750; subtracting $249,750 produces $189,000. Scaled billings of $1,200,000 less $420,000 leave $780,000; subtracting $324,000 produces $456,000. The resulting gross-basis margins are 18%, 28.0%, and 38%; retained-basis margins are 27.69%, 43.08%, and 58.46%. These are fixed canonical portfolio cases, not margins promised for each illustrative deal. The 65% retained-share presentation also does not settle ASC 606 principal-versus-agent classification: assess control and contractual obligations for each service before choosing gross or net presentation. An enrollment agent records its earned commission, not the ETC's subsidy receipts or the household's retail benefit. Staffing remains 1 FT + 2 C for Conservative and Base, and 2 FT + 3 C for Scaled. Do not present the $2,000–$14,000 monthly operating envelope as covering all residual modeled costs; the canonical arithmetic requires a broader cost bridge.

Build a pilot order that can be accepted without negotiating an entire platform relationship. Specify delivered units, service window, benchmark conditions, support hours, data location, cancellation rules, refund mechanics, and the precise event that starts billing. A $500 prepaid compute pilot with a $175 upstream budget illustrates the canonical 65% retained share, leaving $325 before other costs; it does not prove the supplier can actually deliver at that budget. Obtain the supplier quote first. Use Stripe to collect payment and your own append-only usage ledger to manage service credits; payment processor receipts alone cannot establish remaining consumption entitlement. Define low-balance alerts and hard-stop behavior before any customer traffic arrives. For SIP, stop new chargeable sessions under the agreed balance policy while accounting for ongoing calls. For inference, reserve estimated maximum request cost and settle actual usage afterward. Keep $10,000–$60,000 startup capital allocated between setup, refundable customer obligations, and supplier exposure rather than treating every collected dollar as available profit. Limit initial noncancelable commitments to the amount supported by usable cash and contracted demand after refund reserves. An unsigned expansion promise does not belong in that calculation.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four checks before activating the first paid workload**
> 
> A passing commercial score does not replace these pre-flight verification requirements.
> - Verify supplier resale permission, service availability, customer identity, and workload purpose against signed terms.
> - Demonstrate usage metering, low-balance alerts, spend limits, and stop behavior with a deliberately exhausted test wallet.
> - Confirm tenant isolation, approved processors, retention settings, and a tested customer-approved recovery path.
> - For telecom, determine the actual provider role and resulting STIR/SHAKEN, database, Form 499-A, messaging, or ETC obligations before activation.

## 4. Run a permission-aware cadence and convert measured pilots into the first 3–10 wins  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build a weekly acquisition loop around 25 newly researched business accounts, not a purchased bulk list. In week one, choose one compute or telecom offer, validate two suppliers, and publish a benchmark page with dated assumptions. In weeks two through four, research 100 accounts and prioritize the 40 with a clear trigger and plausible buyer. Send a short business email identifying yourself, the observed public trigger, the bounded offer, and a simple opt-out. Follow applicable CAN-SPAM requirements, including accurate routing information, a postal address, and timely suppression. Use a manual professional-network interaction on day four, a relevant benchmark follow-up on day eight, and a final close-the-loop email on day fourteen. Stop on objection or opt-out; do not add SMS, automated calls, or personal-channel outreach merely because email went unanswered. For inbound, publish a GPU cost worksheet, a destination-specific SIP test guide, or an ETC partner checklist with explicit scope. Ask referral partners for an introduction rather than an exported client list. Track every touch, source, reply category, and suppression event so contractor activity remains visible and duplicate approaches do not damage the account.

Use a planning funnel with explicit denominators: 100 researched accounts produce 40 qualified targets, 12 substantive replies, eight discovery calls, five paid pilots, and three first signed service orders. That means 40% research-to-qualified, 30% qualified-to-reply, 66.7% reply-to-discovery, 62.5% discovery-to-pilot, and 60% pilot-to-order conversion, or 3% overall. These are test assumptions, not externally validated market averages. A win requires an executed order and collected payment; a trial signup or unsigned mandate does not count. For an agency mandate, also specify whether compensation is a retainer, activation fee, or earned brokerage commission. Review the funnel every Friday using account-level records, not message counts. If replies are weak, inspect targeting and offer relevance before increasing volume. If pilots fail to convert, separate performance failures from buyer-budget failures and procurement delays. Three comparable 100-account cohorts would imply nine wins at the assumed rate; add partner-originated opportunities rather than promising a tenth. Months two through four should expand only the channel with positive measured acquisition contribution, acceptable support load, and supplier headroom. Keep the 1–4 month first-deal window as a planning range, never a customer promise.

Handle objections with evidence and smaller commitments. When a buyer says hyperscaler credits are cheaper, compare the actual incremental workload cost after credits and concede workloads you cannot beat; sell an uncovered region, deadline, or service gap instead. When they fear broker disappearance, show the service boundary, escalation path, export procedure, and funded recovery option without exposing supplier credentials. When they request postpaid terms, offer a smaller prepaid pilot or a separately underwritten deposit arrangement rather than silently financing usage. When a telecom prospect asks for cheaper routes without answering traffic questions, decline routing until the use case is verified. For Lifeline partners claiming guaranteed subsidy income, show the actual payable event, current program rules, and reversal schedule. After each pilot, deliver a reconciliation containing purchased units, consumed units, supplier cost, customer credits, incident history, and the proposed renewal scope. Request a testimonial only with written permission and customer-approved metrics. Retain failed-pilot reasons as structured CRM fields so acquisition improves rather than repeatedly selling the same mismatch. The first ten customers should establish a repeatable unit, not ten bespoke services that require ten different support runbooks and consume the entire small team's capacity.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Build the two-sided evidence ledger
   - Record 100 researched accounts and two qualified suppliers for the selected service.
   - Attach public-source dates, buyer triggers, written quotes, resale terms, workload assumptions, and named evidence owners.
2. Qualify and deliver the bounded offer
   - Score prospects, select the strongest 40, and run the fourteen-day outreach sequence.
   - Require documented authority, permitted use, contribution, and payment readiness; maintain one shared suppression list.
3. Collect payment and execute measured pilots
   - Target five prepaid pilots with written acceptance criteria and tested spending controls.
   - Record delivery performance, actual supplier costs, credits, support time, and customer acceptance against the original scope.
4. Convert, reconcile, and repeat selectively
   - Seek three paid service orders from the first cohort and review results before expansion.
   - Repeat only profitable channels; use additional cohorts and authorized referrals to pursue ten wins without expanding commitments prematurely.
