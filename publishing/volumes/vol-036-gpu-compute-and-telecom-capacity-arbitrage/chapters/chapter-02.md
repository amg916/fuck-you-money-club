## 1. Identify the buyer, the budget owner, and the measurable loss  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The check writer buys relief from a measurable operating loss, not an abstract capacity spread. For an AI application founder, the acute pain is a product launch whose inference cost exceeds subscription receipts. For an infrastructure director, it is a reserved cluster sitting idle while another team queues urgent jobs. For a contact-center operator, it is rejected calls, unstable termination quality, or a carrier deposit that absorbs working capital. Quote an accepted unit before quoting savings: GPU device-hour with a named configuration, model-specific input and output tokens, answered SIP minutes by destination, or registered messaging traffic with carrier fees itemized. An illustrative H100 purchase at $1.40 per device-hour and sale at $2.00 leaves $0.60 before orchestration, idle inventory, support, and settlement expense. At 1,000 sold hours, that is $600, not $2,000 of owner earnings. This transaction has different unit economics from the portfolio scorecard and must not replace its 65% retained assumption. Ask the buyer which existing invoice or missed revenue event the purchase replaces. If they cannot identify either, sell a small paid benchmark rather than committing a month of bare metal. A benchmark must reproduce their workload, latency target, isolation requirement, and real invoice dimensions.

Separate the technical champion, contractual signer, budget owner, and person authorized to release cash. A developer may possess an API key without authority to accept a twelve-month minimum. Procurement may issue a purchase order while accounts payable still lacks the vendor record needed to remit funds. Obtain the legal entity name, billing address, purchase-order reference, signatory authority, tax documentation, remittance contact, and dispute address before reserving capacity. For escrow, identify both the depositor and the person empowered to accept deliverables; their release authority belongs in the escrow instructions. A founder paying by card should authorize recurring replenishment and its maximum amount explicitly. An enterprise paying by wire should confirm beneficiary details through a previously verified callback channel, never solely through an emailed bank-change notice. Counterparty screening combines business-registration records, domain ownership signals, expected workloads, sanctions screening where applicable, and upstream acceptable-use restrictions. These controls support commercial risk management; they do not imply every compute reseller is a regulated bank. Reject requests to conceal the end customer from a supplier when supplier terms require disclosure. A genuine buyer can explain who uses the service, who approves charges, and which entity bears payment responsibility when an affiliate or subcontractor consumes the capacity.

Use a fifteen-play operating map without pretending every play has identical buyers or margins. The first thirteen are H100 reservation slicing, H200 reservation slicing, B200 reservation slicing, batch GPU backfill, managed fine-tuning windows, dedicated private inference, multi-provider token routing, regional inference overflow, wholesale SIP termination, inbound DID bundles, registered 10DLC messaging, authorized IPv4 leasing, and managed capacity procurement. The remaining two are cloud_ai_startup_credit_aggregation_arbitrage and agency_ad_spend_card_multiplier_rebate_cycling. This is the chapter's operational mapping, not a claim that thirteen additional canonical identifiers were supplied. For each play, record a paid unit, the person owning its budget, a measurable acceptance condition, and a fallback route. A 100,000-minute SIP order at $0.012 produces $1,200 in billings; termination at $0.008 consumes $800 before number rental, surcharges, fraud, and support. If a route deteriorates, move only permitted traffic to a qualified alternative and apply a contractual spend ceiling. A 1,000-hour GPU reservation sold for only 600 hours can lose money despite a positive quoted hourly spread. Release uncommitted inventory, shorten reservation duration, or price the reservation itself. Capacity access is the product only when the operator has contractual resale rights; otherwise, sell authorized management services against the customer's own supplier account.

## 2. Select settlement rails that match reversibility and delivery  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Prefunding changes the exposure clock, but a dashboard balance is not proof of final cash. Stripe checkout can confirm a successful card payment while chargeback exposure and processor reserves remain. ACH debit can produce a return after service has already consumed upstream capacity. A wire credited to the correct account generally offers stronger settlement certainty, although fraud investigations, sanctions issues, and recall requests still require handling. Track three states separately: payment initiated, funds available, and risk reserve released. Create customer spendable credit only under an explicit policy for the particular rail and buyer. For example, a $5,000 card-funded account with a $1,000 internal risk reserve permits $4,000 of initial service, not an immediate $5,000 noncancelable supplier purchase. At $200 hourly burn, that spendable balance covers twenty hours. Stop admitting new jobs before it reaches zero, while reserving enough credit for jobs already running. Disclose this balance policy before payment. Treat prepaid service balances as customer contract liabilities until earned, with documented refund and expiration rules where permitted. Keep them closed-loop service credits rather than transferable cash equivalents; transferability, cash redemption, and holding money for third parties require a separate payments-law analysis. Never describe a processor's ordinary merchant balance as an independent escrow account.

Compare settlement rails against the same delivery obligation instead of choosing the cheapest headline fee. Wire suits larger verified reservations; ACH can suit established business customers; platform checkout accelerates small prepaid purchases. Retainers and milestone holds are contract structures layered over a payment rail, while factoring finances an eligible receivable after it exists. Escrow is useful when a buyer needs independent custody before a defined release event, but hourly compute cannot tolerate an acceptance test left open for weeks. Specify the benchmark, evidence source, objection period, partial-release method, refund allocation, and dispute procedure before either party deposits funds. Statutory disbursement is not an ordinary GPU payment option: a telecom support program pays only eligible participants and services under its governing rules. Do not sell a speculative program reimbursement as committed cash. For an illustrative $20,000 reservation, $5,000 released after provisioning and $15,000 after a specified burn-in test reduces ambiguity but still leaves supplier exposure before the second release. Match upstream commitment dates to released funds, or finance the gap explicitly. A customer-funded escrow balance cannot pay the supplier until its instructions permit release. The operator must price that timing difference rather than quietly borrowing against customer money that remains restricted.

Credit screening should translate evidence into a dollar limit, not a decorative risk score. Verify the buyer's entity and business purpose, request trade references for material postpaid exposure, inspect payment history, and investigate mismatches between the contracting party and the payer. A bank letter alone does not establish willingness to pay disputed usage. Define unsecured exposure as delivered unpaid service plus unavoidable supplier commitments, less eligible cash collateral actually available to cover those obligations. If delivered usage is $8,000, remaining unavoidable commitments are $4,000, and usable security is $5,000, exposure is $7,000. A $6,000 limit therefore requires additional funding or reduced new consumption. Avoid double counting deposits already applied to invoices. For new counterparties, begin with prefunded service and enlarge limits only after observed settlement performance. Obtain consent for any credit inquiry that requires it and collect only necessary verification data. Bind account ownership changes to renewed verification, especially before raising throughput. The contract should permit suspension of new work for exhausted credit, suspected account compromise, or prohibited traffic, with notice and emergency handling appropriate to the service. Retain enough operational capacity to export customer artifacts safely rather than using their data as collection leverage.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 3. Protect the fee and reconcile gross billings to owner profit  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Reconcile every proposal to the supplied Formula v2.0 rather than borrowing a favorable margin from one transaction. Conservative gross billings are $150,000; upstream capacity costs at 35% are $52,500, leaving $97,500 retained contribution. Subtract $70,500 of remaining modeled costs to obtain $27,000 owner profit: 18% of gross billings and 27.69% of retained contribution. Base gross billings of $675,000 less $236,250 upstream costs leave $438,750; remaining costs of $249,750 produce $189,000 profit, or 28.0% gross-basis and 43.08% retained-basis margins. Scaled billings of $1,200,000 less $420,000 upstream costs leave $780,000; remaining costs of $324,000 produce $456,000 profit, or 38% and 58.46%, respectively. These remaining-cost figures are algebraic residuals, not an invented expense allocation. The supplied $2,000–$14,000 monthly operating-expense range cannot describe every residual at Base and Scaled; obtain its scope before using it as a total-cost ceiling. Conservative and Base use 1 FT + 2 C; Scaled uses 2 FT + 3 C. Under ASC 606, principal-versus-agent presentation depends on control of the specified service, not merely collecting the invoice. Use the supplied 65% retained basis for scorecard comparison without automatically treating gross collections as recognized operator revenue.

Fee protection starts with delivering a service the customer continues to need. A buyer can purchase hardware access elsewhere; they cannot instantly reproduce tested routing, normalized metering, reserved inventory management, and documented carrier operations. Contract for those deliverables explicitly. Separate capacity charges, orchestration fees, managed-service retainers, and permitted procurement commissions so a payment dispute does not erase the entire invoice. A narrow introduction-protection clause should identify the introduced supplier or customer, covered transaction, duration, fee trigger, reporting obligation, and existing-relationship exceptions. Its enforceability depends on the governing law and actual facts; avoid punitive formulas disconnected from the earned fee. Obtain supplier acknowledgment when their settlement process must remit your commission. A signed introduction email is useful evidence but does not create payment authority by itself. Attach an order schedule defining device configuration, token tokenizer, SIP billing increments, destination rates, taxes, carrier surcharges, and service-credit calculations. Record tariff or rate-deck versions and their effective timestamps before usage begins. Preserve signed acceptance, metering exports, and invoice delivery records in an access-controlled archive. If the customer objects to one rated destination, isolate that amount while pursuing undisputed charges under the contract. Do not threaten network abuse, data withholding, or deceptive routing to collect an invoice.

The agency_ad_spend_card_multiplier_rebate_cycling play concerns genuine client-authorized advertising spend, not synthetic purchases or a way to manufacture compute revenue. Assume an eligible agency arrangement processes $100,000 of approved monthly advertising and earns 2% cash-equivalent rewards plus a contract-permitted 0.5% volume rebate. That is $2,500 before deductions. If incremental processing costs are $700, financing costs are $600, and the client receives $800 under the agency agreement, the residual is $400 before agency labor and taxes. If financing doubles, the residual becomes negative $200. Check reward-category exclusions, merchant acceptance terms, issuer limits, rebate clawbacks, client ownership of benefits, and payment timing before approving the arrangement. Value points at a defensible realized redemption amount, not a promotional travel valuation. The client's $100,000 media purchase is underlying client spend, not automatically agency revenue or GPU billings. Keep this example outside the canonical annual scenarios unless the scorecard explicitly incorporates its net economics. Require client prefunding or approved agency credit terms, and never increase advertising solely to chase rewards. The fallback is client-direct billing or prefunded approved agency terms; stop rewards-dependent spending immediately when permitted economics deteriorate. Preserve campaign authorization, invoices, card statements, and the agreed allocation of every rebate.

## 4. Connect entitlement, metering, and suspension to the cash ledger  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

For cloud_ai_startup_credit_aggregation_arbitrage, form Delaware or Wyoming entities only for genuine standalone vertical SaaS businesses, not duplicate identities designed to evade program limits. AWS Activate, Microsoft for Startups and Azure founder programs, Google for Startups Cloud, Cloudflare, and startup offers from OpenAI or Anthropic have distinct eligibility, referral, expiration, and permitted-use terms. The supplied $25,000–$350,000 grant range and $50,000–$350,000 play label are planning envelopes, not available balances or promises of stackable awards. Verify each current offer and approved account separately. An illustrative $50,000 approved credit used against eligible $5,000 monthly charges lasts ten months only if expiration and service restrictions allow it. A genuine extraction SaaS earning $10,000 monthly with $200–$1,000 remaining cash delivery costs can show 90–98% cash delivery margin while eligible compute is covered; that excludes labor, taxes, acquisition, and replacement compute costs. Never label it owner net margin. Sell the application outcome, never raw API keys or unapproved credit transfers. Use authorized data sources and permitted collection methods. Track grant depletion beside paid demand, and price renewals against unsubsidized costs. Maintain tested local open-weight Llama or DeepSeek deployments through vLLM where licensing and hardware fit; warm capacity and regression tests, rather than a model name alone, determine migration continuity.

Make the consumption ledger independently reconstructable. LiteLLM can route inference across approved providers, but record request identifier, customer, model revision, tokenizer basis, input tokens, output tokens, retry disposition, and billed price. vLLM serving metrics and NVIDIA DCGM telemetry support operations; GPU utilization percentage alone is not a customer invoice. For device-hour products, bill the contracted allocation interval, excluding only the downtime or credits actually promised. Reserve maximum expected job cost before admission and reconcile actual consumption afterward. If a job can consume $120 and the wallet holds $100, reject it, shorten it with customer authorization, or obtain more funds before starting. For SIP, rate call-detail records with destination normalization, rounding increments, connected duration, and concurrency caps. For messaging, reconcile provider-accepted segments, delivery events, campaign fees, and carrier surcharges under the agreed charging rules. Stripe webhooks require signature verification, idempotent processing, and reconciliation against the processor's records so a replay cannot mint credit twice. Keep immutable accounting events with correcting entries rather than editing historical usage silently. Enterprise zero-data-retention commitments require provider eligibility checks and payload logging disabled throughout the path; retain only contractually permitted billing metadata. Separate tenants through appropriate GPU, container, network, storage, and credential boundaries, then test those boundaries.

Telecom delivery requires role-specific obligations before automation can safely provision service. Determine whether the business is a voice service provider, intermediate provider, interconnected VoIP provider, reseller, or messaging software vendor under the applicable rule. STIR/SHAKEN implementation and Robocall Mitigation Database duties follow the covered role and current FCC requirements; buying an upstream carrier connection does not automatically discharge them. Register applicable 10DLC brands and campaigns through the carrier ecosystem, while distinguishing those contractual requirements from the TCPA and FCC calling rules. Evaluate FCC Form 499-A registration and reporting under the service classification and filing instructions; a contribution exemption does not automatically eliminate reporting. Screen workloads and traffic against supplier acceptable-use terms, including prohibited mining, unlawful calls, and identity misrepresentation. An automated least-cost router must optimize only among authorized, quality-qualified routes; never bypass blocking through caller-ID manipulation or campaign identity rotation. Run a daily close joining customer payments, earned usage, unused credit, supplier accruals, refunds, disputes, and restricted reserves. Investigate breaks before increasing throughput. The minimum operating package is a signed order, verified payment authority, funded exposure limit, reproducible meter, qualified fallback, and tested suspension path. These controls turn capacity trading into a service business whose cash conversion survives both customer disputes and supplier repricing.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Bind the commercial parties
   - Collect entity, signer, payer, end-user, purchase-order, and remittance details.
   - Attach supplier resale permissions, acceptable-use terms, service classification, and the named person authorized to accept delivery.
2. Fund the exposure limit
   - Select the settlement rail and calculate spendable funds after restrictions and reserves.
   - Exclude unreleased escrow, anticipated grants, speculative reimbursements, and unearned card rewards from usable supplier funding.
3. Test admission and metering
   - Run a paid benchmark with signed prices, reproducible usage records, and a wallet-exhaustion test.
   - Verify idempotent payment posting, maximum job-cost reservation, telecom destination caps, and the agreed treatment of retries or failed delivery.
4. Close cash and earned service daily
   - Reconcile payments, consumption, supplier accruals, refunds, disputes, and customer liabilities.
   - Investigate unmatched events before expanding limits; execute the tested fallback or suspend new consumption when funding or service conditions fail.
