## 1. Build an identity ledger from registries, not scraped assumptions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an evidence ledger whose primary keys distinguish a legal entity, domain, carrier identifier, autonomous system, and customer account. A domain string is not a company identifier, and a company registration is not permission to resell its carrier services. Store the source URL, retrieval timestamp, effective date, response hash, permitted reuse conditions, and the exact matching rule for every relationship. For the domain acquisition play, obtain approved ICANN Centralized Zone Data Service access and compare successive zone snapshots locally. A zone addition means newly observed delegation, not necessarily a newly registered domain: transfers, restored delegations, and prior snapshot gaps create false triggers. Preserve the previous snapshot and classify additions before enrichment. The .us registry sits outside any assumption that CZDS supplies every top-level domain. Discover its authoritative RDAP service through the IANA bootstrap file, inspect actual response fields, and respect redaction and access policy. There is no dependable universal unredacted .us bulk registrant feed to assume. Join domain tokens to Secretary of State records only when another public business identifier corroborates the relationship. Keep registered-agent addresses separate from operating addresses; a convenient mailing destination does not establish ownership, occupancy, or buyer intent.

Implement newly_registered_domain_trigger_arbitrage as an evidence-based technical diagnostic, not a claim to privileged registry access. After the first observation, schedule bounded checks at observation time, twenty-four hours, and seventy-two hours. Query authoritative DNS for MX, SPF-bearing TXT records, and the DMARC record at the correct organizational domain; check HTTPS status and the conventional sitemap.xml location without crawling unrelated paths. An absent sitemap at that path does not prove that the site has no sitemap, because robots.txt or application configuration may publish another location. Likewise, zero Google indexed pages cannot be established reliably by scraping search results. Use Google Search Console only after the property owner grants access; otherwise describe indexing as unverified and exclude it from scored defects. Match public business mailing addresses through corroborated company filings without trying to reconstruct redacted personal contacts. For an illustrative batch of 100 private diagnostic mailers at $1.40 each plus two review hours at $35, acquisition expense is $210. Three completed $150 diagnostic engagements yield $450 gross receipts and $240 before delivery expense. If matching confidence fails, suppress the mailer rather than purchasing speculative identity enrichment. Explain actual DNS fixes and identify the sender as a private service business.

Carrier records answer a different question: whether the proposed counterparty has a verifiable identity and the filings relevant to its actual role. Search the FCC Robocall Mitigation Database before activating voice routes, preserve the record identifier and filing date, and inspect the stated mitigation or authentication posture. A listing does not certify route quality, lawful traffic, or creditworthiness. Review FCC Form 499 records alongside contracts to establish the reported entity and services, then determine applicable filing and contribution duties under 47 CFR 54.706 and 54.711. Exemptions and reseller treatment depend on facts; buying wholesale minutes does not settle those questions. ULS license records help evaluate wireless counterparties but do not prove that a particular SIP route is authorized. Broadband Data Collection coverage helps prioritize facilities and connectivity discussions, not promise deliverable transit at a specific rack. Apply daily change checks to active voice counterparties, weekly checks to prospects, and event-driven review after legal-entity or route changes. If a portal becomes unavailable, use a dated saved record for research only and obtain fresh counterparty evidence before activation. Escalate discrepancies to a named owner instead of letting an enrichment service silently merge competing company identities.

## 2. Connect commercial APIs to capacity, quality, and prepaid balances  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Separate identity evidence from live supply evidence. Provider catalog APIs describe offered SKUs and regions, while reservation responses establish whether capacity can actually be purchased. Normalize H100, H200, and B200 offers by memory, interconnect, minimum term, egress treatment, interruption conditions, and customer-isolation controls before comparing hourly prices. A cheap advertised hour with an unavailable allocation is not inventory. Use NVIDIA DCGM metrics for device utilization, memory pressure, errors, and temperature, then join them to scheduler allocations and signed customer usage events. DCGM utilization alone cannot determine billable tokens or attribute shared GPU time reliably. Capture vLLM token counters and request timings, and let LiteLLM route only among providers approved for that customer's model, geography, and data-handling requirements. Track queue delay separately from generation speed; a fast decoder behind a long queue can miss the customer's response target. Synthetic probes should use operator-owned prompts without confidential content. For enterprise zero-data-retention commitments, disable prompt and response capture throughout proxies, tracing, exception reporting, and backups. Retain only the minimum contractually permitted billing metadata, with an explicit expiry schedule. If the only fallback provider has incompatible retention terms, reject the request rather than silently breaking the contract.

On telecom, join quoted rates to destination prefixes, billing increments, minimum charges, surcharges, and effective dates before evaluating least-cost routing. Telnyx or another contracted carrier supplies account-specific prices and call records; generic public marketing rates cannot settle an invoice. Twilio's messaging and Trust Hub interfaces expose supported onboarding workflows, but provider registration interfaces do not imply direct unrestricted access to The Campaign Registry. Track brand and campaign states, use-case restrictions, number assignments, and carrier feedback as separate objects. Registration does not establish recipient permission or make a prohibited message acceptable. STIR/SHAKEN authentication and mitigation duties follow the provider's role under 47 CFR 64.6301 and 64.6305; avoid presenting an attestation level as proof of customer identity beyond what the originating provider actually verified. Measure answer-seizure ratio, average call duration, post-dial delay, and verified customer complaints by route and traffic class. A cheaper route with false answer supervision can raise billed minutes while reducing useful completions. Run bounded test traffic to numbers the operator controls, then increase allocation gradually. When quality or required filing evidence fails, shift only to a prequalified route and stop traffic if none exists. Never repair margin by relaxing destination restrictions or customer screening.

Make the prepaid wallet an internal double-entry ledger reconciled to Stripe, not a mutable balance stored only in a checkout session. A successful payment event can fund service credits under the agreed policy, but disputes, refunds, settlement delays, and payment-method behavior still require reserves. Verify webhook signatures, deduplicate event identifiers, and handle events arriving out of order. Reserve estimated spend before dispatch, settle actual metered usage afterward, and release unused reservations exactly once. Apply separate ceilings to customer credit, provider exposure, concurrent jobs, call destinations, and campaign throughput. The internal ledger is authoritative for spend permission; the processor remains authoritative for payment events. Cache static registry evidence aggressively, refresh rate decks on effective-date changes, and refresh capacity just before committing a quote. Each connector needs explicit timeouts, bounded retries, exponential backoff with jitter, and a circuit breaker. Respect Retry-After and provider-specific quotas instead of copying a universal requests-per-second setting across services. Store a last-success timestamp and fail closed only for the decision that needs fresh evidence. A stale prospecting record can wait; an uncertain prepaid balance must stop additional consumption. These controls protect the spread when upstream services remain billable during a downstream collection failure.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture source evidence without merging identities
   - Download approved bulk records, query shortlisted identifiers, and preserve source timestamps and response hashes.
   - Keep domains, companies, networks, and carrier accounts as separate records connected by explicit evidence-backed relationships.
2. Run the bounded seventy-two-hour diagnostic
   - Check DNS and limited public HTTP endpoints at observation, twenty-four hours, and seventy-two hours.
   - Throttle per host, honor access restrictions, omit intrusive testing, and describe absent sitemap paths or indexing evidence precisely.
3. Price a reproducible delivered unit
   - Compare compatible GPU-hours, consistently defined tokens, or tariff-normalized telecom minutes using versioned buy rates.
   - Include utilization, retries, egress, increments, surcharges, and customer restrictions; label illustrative assumptions separately.
4. Reserve, dispatch, and reconcile exactly once
   - Reserve wallet credit, place an idempotent capacity order, meter delivered usage, and reconcile provider settlement.
   - Release unused reservations once, quarantine conflicting usage evidence, and preserve a tested stop path for new consumption.

Customer application sales, merchant transaction volume, and unrelated underlying business activity never enter this desk's revenue. The unit examples above explain selection mechanics; their individual spreads are not substitutes for the canonical annual scenario mix or retained-share assumption.

Keep the team assumptions unchanged at one full-time operator plus two contractors for Conservative and Base, and two full-time operators plus three contractors for Scaled. Startup capital remains $10,000–$60,000 and time to first deal remains one to four months. Track cash exposure independently of reported margin: a prepaid customer wallet can reduce collection risk while a noncancelable bare-metal commitment still drains cash during idle weeks. Build a daily utilization forecast from purchased device-hours, qualified booked demand, and contract expiry dates. Stress GPU generation price compression and simultaneous customer departures before renewing capacity. For telecom, stress destination mix shifts and newly effective surcharges. When the resulting cash requirement exceeds the funded reserve, reduce new commitments rather than disguising idle capacity as an enrichment or marketing expense.

Run the production pipeline as a small set of accountable services: registry collector, evidence store, pricing engine, prepaid ledger, dispatch controller, metering reconciler, and exception queue. Python's sqlite3 supports an initial single-writer evidence store, while hashlib, urllib.request, json, and scheduled jobs support straightforward collection without an oversized integration stack. Move operational concurrency to a suitable transactional database when needed; the chapter's module itself requires no external packages. Keep credentials outside exported evidence, scope each connector narrowly, and log identifiers rather than customer payloads. Test restored backups, replayed webhooks, stale rate decks, missing GPU counters, and duplicated call records before increasing volume. Screen prohibited mining, abusive calling, and unsupported messaging patterns against documented acceptable-use rules, with bounded review and a working suspension path. Apply 47 U.S.C. 227 and implementing calling rules to the actual traffic, not merely to the customer's campaign registration. Track false-positive suspensions alongside confirmed abuse so controls remain measurable. Finish each day with three reconciliations: customer usage to customer charges, upstream usage to supplier charges, and processor movements to wallet liabilities. Any unexplained difference receives an owner, amount, age, and next action. Information becomes an edge only when it changes a reproducible purchase, price, or stop decision.
