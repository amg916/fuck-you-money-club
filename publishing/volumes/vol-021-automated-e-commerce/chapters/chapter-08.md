## 1. Build an evidence directory that can reject inventory  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a product identity spine, not a purchased prospect list. For every proposed SKU, record the manufacturer part number, GTIN where assigned, brand, variant, country of origin, importer, supplier legal entity, and the supplier's written authorization covering your sales channels. Query CPSC recalls and SaferProducts.gov against manufacturer names, model aliases, and product descriptions; exact barcode matching alone misses renamed bundles and older recall notices. A consumer incident report is a risk signal, not an agency finding, while a matched recall requires an immediate inventory hold and review of the applicable remedy. Keep the source URL, retrieved timestamp, query terms, and reviewed evidence with the SKU. Under 15 U.S.C. 2068, prohibited sales include certain recalled or nonconforming consumer products; under 15 U.S.C. 2064(b), covered businesses have reporting duties when qualifying information reasonably supports a substantial product hazard or specified serious risk. Escalate safety information immediately rather than waiting for the next batch refresh. A clean search does not establish safety, authenticity, or permission to resell. Require applicable certificates, test evidence, traceable invoices, and lot identifiers before accepting stock into the sellable pool.

Separate identity verification from commercial permission and import classification. A state business registry can corroborate an entity's existence, but cannot establish that its salesperson controls a brand's authorized-dealer program. Verify that claim through a manufacturer contact obtained independently of the supplier's email. USPTO trademark records help resolve brand ownership questions; they do not grant distribution rights. Verified by GS1 can corroborate the company associated with an identifier, but does not authenticate the physical goods in a carton. For imports, use the current USITC Harmonized Tariff Schedule, CBP CROSS rulings, and CBP Cargo Systems Messaging Service notices together. A superficially similar CROSS ruling is research evidence, not a binding classification for a different product or importer. Have the broker validate material composition, principal use, origin, valuation, and additional duty exposure before issuing the purchase order. If a $20 landed-cost assumption omits $3 of applicable duty and handling, 2,000 units consume another $6,000 of cash. When classification remains disputed, price the higher supported cost or pause replenishment; do not force the optimistic tariff code into the margin model merely to make a supplier quote appear viable.

Treat the directory below as an access plan, not a promise of permanent pricing or universal quotas. Public portals often publish without a latency commitment; an event date, publication date, and retrieval date answer different questions. Commercial quotas depend on application review, account tier, endpoint, and current contracts. At onboarding, record the actual permitted access method, measured response time, pagination behavior, and retry instructions in a source register. Do not invent an API for a marketplace that exposes only public listing pages. Use manual research or expressly permitted collection, then request seller-authorized exports during diligence. The target cadence is daily safety monitoring, transaction-level commerce events, hourly stock reconciliation where supported, and a fresh tariff review before each materially changed import order. These are desk operating targets, not provider service commitments. Budget each connector against the $4,000–$25,000 monthly operating range and the $30,000–$250,000 startup capital envelope without assuming every subscription is incremental to the scorecard. Preserve a manual fallback that can stop sales or release verified orders during an outage. A dashboard that becomes blind when one vendor fails is decoration rather than an operating control.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Research shortcuts that preserve evidence quality**
> 
> Spend diligence time where a false match can create inventory losses, channel suspension, or an unsafe sale.
> - Search recalled products by manufacturer aliases and model fragments; preserve the matching passage and affected production dates.
> - Verify dealer authority through an independently located brand contact, then attach the response to the supplier record.
> - Compare a plugin's changelog with unresolved support threads; a recent version number can conceal abandoned customer support.
> - Ask the 3PL to demonstrate one missing-carton and one partial-shipment case before trusting its normal-order dashboard.

## 2. Turn heterogeneous records into a margin-aware enrichment waterfall  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use an enrichment waterfall that stops when evidence becomes sufficient, not when every paid endpoint has been called. First resolve product identity from the supplier's structured catalog and manufacturer documentation. Next corroborate identifiers through GS1 and official brand records. Then attach recall, import, and applicable tax evidence. Only after those joins should commercial services enrich demand, shipment estimates, or customer retention attributes. Keep product, supplier, order, shipment, and customer entities separate; matching two orders to the same shipping address does not prove that two people are the same customer. Every enrichment result needs a source, retrieval timestamp, permitted purpose, confidence rationale, and expiration rule. A hypothetical paid lookup costing $0.04 across 5,000 SKUs costs $200; resolving 80% through authoritative free records reduces that paid step to 1,000 lookups and $40. The $160 difference is a sensitivity example, not additional scorecard profit. If identity remains ambiguous, quarantine the record for review rather than attaching the closest brand or tax category automatically. Minimize customer fields in this process: product diligence rarely needs a buyer's name, email address, or complete street address.

Implement ingestion as an append-only evidence layer followed by normalized operational tables and explicit decision views. A small desk can begin with encrypted object storage for source payloads, PostgreSQL for transactional joins, and DuckDB for analyst-side checks; these are deployment choices, not dependencies of this chapter module. Store UTC timestamps, source time zones where supplied, currency codes, and integer minor units for money. Deduplicate delivery events using provider event identifiers, while handling updates through resource versions or retrieval timestamps. An order can have several fulfillments, refunds, and settlement adjustments, so flattening everything into one mutable spreadsheet row destroys the audit trail. Shopify webhooks should trigger work, while scheduled API reconciliation establishes completeness. Persist a cursor only after the corresponding page commits successfully. Use overlap windows to catch late updates, then deduplicate deterministically. Keep personally identifying fields out of ordinary job logs and use narrowly scoped service accounts. If a source withdraws access, stop collection and follow retention obligations rather than replacing authorized access with an undocumented endpoint. A reproducible pipeline must explain both what it knows and which source has stopped contributing fresh evidence.

Build the contribution view from retail economics rather than advertising-platform conversion values. An illustrative $80 merchandise order with a $4 refund allowance produces $76 of expected net retail sales. Subtract $28 of goods, $10 of fulfillment and shipping, $2.40 of processing, $18 of acquisition spend, and $3 of expected returns handling to obtain $14.60 contribution before fixed overhead. The refund allowance reduces revenue; returns handling covers a different cost and must not duplicate the refunded merchandise amount. Sales taxes collected for remittance do not become retail revenue. Reconcile actual refunds and handling costs against those estimates as cohorts mature. The canonical annual cases stay $250,000 multiplied by 5% equals $12,500; $1,125,000 multiplied by 11.5% equals $129,375; and $2,000,000 multiplied by 18% equals $360,000. These margins already include goods, fulfillment, advertising, and operating costs. Do not subtract them again or add hypothetical connector savings without rebuilding the scenario. Keep the Conservative and Base teams at two full-time staff plus three contractors, and the Scaled team at three full-time staff plus four contractors. Automation protects those staffing assumptions only when exception volume remains manageable.

**Six-stage waterfall from product evidence to contribution-margin decisions**

| Stage and authoritative starting evidence | Normalization and join rule to implement | Acceptance test with concrete operator detail | Fallback when the evidence remains insufficient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resolve product identity from manufacturer catalogs and supplier invoices. | Join exact GTIN and manufacturer part number; retain variant attributes. | Require agreement on pack size, material, model, and intended use. | Hold the SKU for manufacturer confirmation before buying or publishing. |
| Verify supplier authority using registry records and brand confirmation. | Keep legal entity identity separate from channel-specific authorization documents. | Match invoice issuer, remittance beneficiary, and independently confirmed supplier contact. | Use an already authorized distributor while disputed credentials receive review. |

| Join commerce events with processor and warehouse operational records. | Preserve one-to-many shipments, refunds, disputes, and balance transactions explicitly. | Explain every order-total difference using dated source-backed reconciliation entries. | Use authorized exports and a manual exception queue during connector outages. |

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the source and permission boundary
   - Record owner, endpoint, scopes, contract, refresh target, and manual fallback.
   - Test read-only access first and save the current documentation date beside the connector configuration.
2. Capture replayable evidence
   - Store source payloads with timestamps, checksums, currency fields, and stable identifiers.
   - Redact secrets from logs, restrict customer data, and commit pagination cursors only after successful writes.
3. Reconcile representative exceptions
   - Replay a partial shipment, duplicate event, delayed refund, and changed inventory count.
   - Confirm that each replay changes the intended state once and leaves a traceable explanation.
4. Release narrow automation with recovery
   - Enable alerts before write actions, then authorize specific routing and inventory operations.
   - Assign an exception owner, a stale-data cutoff, and a tested manual procedure for each automated action.

## 3. Underwrite the two adjacent plays without contaminating retail revenue  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

For expired_chrome_extension_acquisitions, treat dormant as a maintenance condition, not an expired domain that anyone can claim. Screen Chrome Web Store, WordPress Plugin Directory, and Shopify App Store utilities against the mapped target of 10,000–250,000 active users and ratings of at least 4.5 stars. Public metrics are not comparable across those venues: WordPress uses install buckets, Chrome displays user indicators, and Shopify listings do not establish an app's active-user count. Require owner-authorized exports to prove the actual cohort. Investigate the last substantive release, open support load, permission footprint, dependency vulnerabilities, repository ownership, billing rights, and platform transfer conditions. Acquisition requires the owner's agreement and a supported transfer process. As a sensitivity case, 20,000 verified active users multiplied by 2% paying conversion and $5 monthly pricing yields $2,000 monthly subscription revenue, or $24,000 annually before churn and refunds. If explicit annual operating costs are $12,000, potential annual cashflow is $12,000, making the mapped 1–2 times range $12,000–$24,000. That valuation is a hypothesis requiring cohort tests, not an observed market price or a reason to overlook transfer restrictions and support debt.

Monetize acquired utilities through additive Pro workflows that solve a measurable task, such as bulk catalog validation, authorized inventory exports, or merchant-controlled exception notifications. Grandfather every legacy free feature and preserve the existing narrow permission boundary. Do not insert hidden tracking or background advertising scripts; any contextual partner offer must be genuinely opt-in, clearly explained, and consistent with the marketplace's current rules. Validate paid demand with a limited voluntary cohort before converting the acquisition model into a budget. If only 1% of the same 20,000 active users buys at $5 monthly, annual revenue falls to $12,000 and the assumed $12,000 operating cost consumes all projected cashflow. That downside supports a lower fixed price, a carefully specified earnout, or walking away. Keep subscription receipts and acquisition expenses in a separate business ledger; they are not retail sales inside the volume's canonical scenarios. Before closing, verify intellectual-property assignments, dependency licenses, account transfer steps, customer-data handling, and release-signing access. The operational fallback is to maintain the existing free utility while testing optional additions, or decline the acquisition if safe maintenance cannot be funded without invasive monetization.

For agency_ad_spend_card_multiplier_rebate_cycling, the information edge is contract reconciliation, not artificial transaction volume. Obtain written client authorization, platform billing eligibility, issuer reward terms, rebate schedules, credit limits, payment dates, and client pass-through obligations before routing genuine advertising spend. Illustratively, $100,000 of monthly client media spend at an eligible 2% reward produces $2,000 of gross reward value. Deduct $500 of funding cost, $300 of incremental processing charges, and $800 contractually owed to clients; only $400 remains before desk overhead and taxes. The $100,000 is client media spend, not operator retail revenue, and the $400 is a separate illustrative contribution rather than an addition to canonical owner profit. Value points at an evidenced usable redemption rate, apply eligibility exclusions, and recognize reversals when spend is refunded or rewards are clawed back. If the reward rate falls to 1%, the same cost structure loses $600 monthly. Stop rewards-dependent spending immediately and move to client-direct billing or prefunded approved agency terms. Never manufacture purchases, rotate identities to evade limits, or increase client budgets merely to chase a multiplier. Reconcile platform invoices, issuer statements, and client balances before recognizing the residual economics.

## 4. Diagnose stale data before automation spends money  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Instrument the pipeline around freshness, completeness, duplication, and business reconciliation rather than HTTP success alone. A connector returning status 200 with yesterday's inventory can create expensive overselling. Track source-event time, ingestion time, last successful reconciliation, records processed, and the oldest unresolved exception. Use curl or HTTPie against authorized test endpoints, jq to inspect payload shape, and a restricted API client such as Postman for repeatable requests. Strip customer information and bearer tokens from saved examples. SQL assertions should test duplicate order identifiers, negative sellable quantities, missing currency, and refunds exceeding eligible captured amounts after documented adjustments. SQLite or DuckDB can reproduce a suspicious calculation from exported evidence without granting another analyst production write access. Simulate duplicate webhooks, out-of-order refunds, truncated pagination, and a warehouse outage in a sandbox. A routing worker must not create a second shipment when the first response times out. Use supported idempotency keys or an internal shipment-intent ledger plus provider lookup before retrying. Set thresholds from observed operating latency and warehouse cutoffs, then document the exact manual action triggered when those thresholds are exceeded.

Connect failures to customer promises and statutory duties. Under the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule, 16 CFR Part 435, sellers need a reasonable basis for advertised shipping times; when no time is stated, the rule generally uses thirty days. Delays require the prescribed notice, consent, or refund handling for the circumstances, so an inventory alert must reach the customer-service queue before the promise expires. A warehouse stockout should suppress new sales where fulfillment cannot be supported, not merely turn a dashboard cell red. Safety matches should isolate affected inventory and preserve lot traceability. Privacy controls should follow actual statutory applicability: California Civil Code section 1798.100 includes notice and collection, use, retention, and sharing requirements for covered businesses. Keep customer enrichment limited to specified operational purposes and implement applicable deletion and retention rules across replicas, exports, and downstream processors. Retention campaigns should use documented contact preferences and suppression records rather than reconstructing audiences from deleted identifiers. During an outage, protect existing customers first: verify outstanding orders, communicate supported shipping dates, and stop discretionary acquisition spend when the order desk cannot fulfill the demand it is buying.

Run a weekly operator review that fits the small-team model. Assign one owner to inventory and supplier exceptions, one to contribution and cash reconciliation, and contracted specialists to tightly scoped broker, security, and integration work. The two full-time staff and three contractors in the Conservative and Base cases cannot manually inspect every routine event, but they can clear a ranked queue of material exceptions. Rank by safety impact first, customer-promise exposure second, and dollars at risk third. An illustrative queue of 200 exceptions per week at six minutes each consumes twenty hours; reducing avoidable duplicates to fifty saves fifteen hours of workload, not automatically fifteen hours of cash expense. Track whether recovered capacity actually prevents hiring or improves service. Review acquisition cost by matured cohort so ad-cost inflation cannot hide behind platform attribution changes. Test one source outage and one recovery replay monthly, rotate exposed credentials immediately, and verify backup restoration before expanding write permissions. The two-to-six-month first-deal window favors a narrow initial catalog with reliable evidence over a large catalog whose inventory, returns, and authorization records cannot be reconciled.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before expanding automation**
> 
> Authorize each narrow write capability only after its evidence, economics, and recovery path have been demonstrated.
> - Verify supplier authority, product identity, applicable safety documents, and reviewed recall evidence for every launched SKU.
> - Reconcile representative orders through refund, warehouse shipment, processor fee, and settlement without counting client principal as sales.
> - Confirm connector scopes, actual account quotas, stale-data thresholds, signed webhook checks, and a named manual fallback owner.
> - Demonstrate duplicate-safe replay and outage recovery; separately validate app-transfer rights or agency reward terms before funding either adjacent play.
