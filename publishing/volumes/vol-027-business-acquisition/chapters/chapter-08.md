## 1. Build a jurisdiction-aware directory before building a target list  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Organize the acquisition desk around seven explicitly mapped plays: P1 target origination, P2 entity verification, P3 liability discovery, P4 revenue validation, P5 QoE normalization, P6 financing structure, and P7 the 100-day turnaround. These are this chapter's operating labels, not seven additional businesses or revenue streams. Assign every collected field to a play and a decision: a former legal name improves lien searches; a permit expiration changes the transition calendar; bank settlements challenge recorded sales. For the seven-full-time, three-contractor model, a practical allocation is an owner-operator, an operating manager, an administrative controller, and four delivery employees, supported by contracted transaction counsel, a QoE accountant, and an integration specialist. These are capacity assignments within the four-person envelope, not hires layered onto the scorecard. The delivery employees protect customer service while the owner and controller maintain the evidence queue. During the search period, the buyer and retained specialists cover those functions before the acquired operating team transfers. Keep only enough active diligence files to inspect each exception weekly; software cannot make ten people simultaneously operate the business, rebuild its books, and close several transactions.

Start each target with a jurisdiction map containing formation state, operating locations, property counties, historical names, and license issuers. Secretary of State records establish filed identity and status, not the actual ownership of every entity. An organizer or registered agent is not automatically an owner. Apply enacted state versions of UCC sections 9-301, 9-307, 9-503, and 9-507 when choosing filing offices and debtor-name searches; fixture filings and certain collateral require additional treatment. A web search result is a lead, while a dated official search report supports a transaction workpaper. Search federal litigation through PACER and state litigation through the relevant judiciary because neither substitutes for the other. County land records can expose mortgages, recorded liens, and property transfers, but tax-lien filing locations vary with applicable rules and property type. Treat every negative result as scoped evidence: record the name, office, date range, and indexing limitations. A clean screen never establishes that all liabilities are absent. Have counsel define closing search coverage, payoff evidence, and termination deliverables rather than letting an entity matching script decide whether a lien has disappeared.

Use the directory below as an acquisition-specific retrieval plan, not a claim that every portal offers bulk access. Cost descriptions distinguish public viewing, transaction charges, and negotiated commercial products; verify current prices before procurement. Refresh descriptions describe source behavior rather than promised delivery times. Capture both source publication time and retrieval time so a fresh download does not disguise old underlying records. For an API, read the documented quota, respect Retry-After when provided, and use bounded exponential backoff with jitter. For a human-only portal, perform a documented manual search or purchase its authorized export instead of bypassing access controls. Preserve search receipts and source files because public interfaces change during a six-to-eighteen-month deal cycle. Do not infer loan balances from SBA origination datasets, transferable permits from active license displays, or customer retention from federal award totals. Each source earns its place only when it changes outreach, price, structure, or a post-close operating action. The controller owns provenance; counsel interprets legal findings; the owner decides whether the evidence justifies spending the next diligence dollar or extending exclusivity.

## 2. Run an evidence-first enrichment waterfall with explicit stop rules  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run enrichment in a fixed order: official entity identity, company-owned website, licensed business directory, commercial contact discovery, and human verification. OpenCorporates can help connect legal names and jurisdictions, but confirm decisive facts against the filing office and license API access under its current terms. Apollo can suggest business contacts; Hunter can test domain-based email hypotheses; neither establishes ownership, authority to sell, or permission to retrieve private records. Store each provider's claim separately rather than overwriting an official fact with a higher-confidence marketing score. A target record needs a stable internal identifier, legal-name history, formation identifier, website domain, operating address, source timestamp, and match rationale. Require at least two independent matching attributes before merging records, and send shared addresses, common names, and franchise locations to human review. Stop buying credits when the legal entity is unresolved or the business falls outside the owner's acquisition criteria. A cheap contact attached to the wrong debtor can create expensive diligence work, mistaken outreach, and an incorrect concentration analysis later. Preserve rejected matches so the next provider cannot silently recreate the same mistake.

Set a deliberately small enrichment allowance inside the existing operating budget rather than describing subscriptions as incremental profit. As an illustrative purchasing rule, authorize $300 monthly for screening 100 named targets: $3 per screened target before labor and specialist work. If only 20 targets meet the mandate, that becomes $15 per qualified target, not $3. Three seller conversations would imply $100 of subscription expense per conversation, without proving that enrichment caused any conversation. Use these denominators to cancel tools that produce decorative coverage. Do not extrapolate this screening budget into deal economics or replace the canonical $6,000–$25,000 monthly operating-expense range with it. The owner approves target criteria, the controller manages provider credits, and the integration contractor maintains connectors within a capped assignment. Counsel reviews permitted use where a product includes personal information. Keep commercial outreach accurate and apply the concrete requirements of 15 U.S.C. 7704 when sending commercial email, including truthful headers, required sender details, and functioning opt-out handling. A verified address measures deliverability; it does not erase messaging obligations or justify repeated contact after suppression.

Implement the first pipeline with Python's csv, json, sqlite3, hashlib, and urllib modules before buying an orchestration platform. Store immutable source artifacts separately from normalized SQLite tables, then attach artifact hashes, retrieval times, request parameters, and transformation versions to each derived finding. Keep API credentials outside exports and redact tokens from request logs. Use least-privilege database roles where the deployed database supports them; protect local SQLite files through operating-system permissions and encrypted storage. Idempotent imports should key records by source and native identifier, with versioned snapshots for changing fields. Test entity collisions, empty pages, repeated cursors, deleted transactions, changed accounting periods, and malformed currency strings. Quarantine incomplete responses rather than converting missing amounts to zero. Maintain a queue with severity, owner, due date, evidence link, and decision impact. Only the owner and controller need routine access to the acquisition dashboard; delivery employees receive the operational tasks relevant to their work. This keeps P1 through P7 connected without exposing payroll, customer contracts, or seller bank details across the entire four-person pod. Review access again when a bidder withdraws or a contractor finishes.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Four ways experienced buyers prevent expensive false certainty**
> 
> Spend enrichment credits on resolved entities and reserve specialist time for findings capable of changing the transaction.
> - Build the historical debtor-name list before ordering UCC searches; include documented mergers and name changes so counsel can determine the correct search coverage.
> - Compare operating addresses across permits, payroll locations, and customer invoices; a mailing address alone can conceal an unreviewed operating site.
> - Use accounting document numbers and bank settlement references as reconciliation anchors; matching only dates and amounts breaks when deposits are batched.
> - Retain rejected entity matches with reasons; otherwise a refreshed commercial dataset can reintroduce a previously disproved owner or subsidiary relationship.

## 3. Turn permissioned records into a reproducible QoE and financing bridge  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Move from public research to seller-authorized access only after defining the entity, period, purpose, and recipients of each dataset. Request monthly trial balances, general ledgers, bank statements, accounts-receivable aging, payroll registers, tax returns, and customer-level sales for a consistent diligence period. QuickBooks OAuth authorization must come from an authorized account administrator, and permissions must be limited to what the platform actually supports; do not advertise a read-only restriction that the granted scope does not enforce. Where appropriate, use controlled accountant exports instead. Plaid access similarly requires authorized connection of the relevant accounts, supported institutions, and an agreed retention policy. Public registries do not expose confidential federal tax returns: 26 U.S.C. 6103 governs disclosure, and seller-provided returns or properly authorized transcript procedures serve a different evidentiary role. Do not substitute personal consumer reports for ordinary business diligence. If a proposed report is covered by the Fair Credit Reporting Act, establish an actual permissible purpose under 15 U.S.C. 1681b and applicable procedural duties before ordering it. A signed acquisition interest letter alone does not create unrestricted access to personal records.

Build the revenue bridge from invoices to ledger postings, settlement batches, bank deposits, and reported tax figures, preserving timing differences explicitly. Separate sales taxes collected, customer deposits, loan proceeds, owner contributions, refunds, chargebacks, and transfers between accounts. A deposit is not automatically revenue; an invoice is not automatically cash. For marketplace or payment businesses, distinguish merchant throughput from the target's recognized fees. For this candidate, the scorecard revenue basis is acquired-business sales, not acquisition purchase price, borrowed principal, or a broker's transaction fee. Use Decimal arithmetic for money and reconcile every import to its source control total. Construct customer cohorts using stable customer identifiers so renamed accounts do not imitate retention or attrition. Analyze concentration by both revenue and gross contribution where reliable cost attribution exists. Trace unusual month-end journal entries and post-period credits before accepting apparent growth. The contracted QoE accountant evaluates evidence-supported adjustments; the owner retains responsibility for the bid. Every proposed add-back needs a ledger reference, business rationale, recurrence assessment, and replacement-cost treatment. Removing the seller's pay while ignoring the replacement manager's cost overstates transferable earnings.

Keep financing analysis separate from the earnings bridge. The canonical Conservative case is $750,000 of sales multiplied by 8%, producing $60,000; Base is $1,875,000 multiplied by 13.0%, producing $243,750; Scaled is $3,000,000 multiplied by 18%, producing $540,000. These owner-profit figures are after interest and before principal repayments. Do not subtract interest twice, label these amounts EBITDA, or present them as cash available after all debt service. Build a separate liquidity bridge deducting scheduled principal, required capital expenditure, and working-capital increases, with other cash adjustments shown explicitly. For SBA-backed financing, obtain current lender requirements and the applicable SBA procedures; eligibility, seller-note treatment, equity injection, and standby provisions depend on the transaction and current program rules. Historical SBA datasets cannot establish approval or current indebtedness. For conventional and seller debt, model contractual maturities, amortization, security interests, and covenant definitions from actual documents. Never substitute a generic debt-service ratio for the lender's defined calculation. Counsel and the lender resolve intercreditor terms; the controller maintains the payment calendar and flags the earliest projected cash shortfall.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before using the diligence model in a bid**
> 
> Advance the model only when each check has an accountable owner and evidence that another reviewer can reproduce.
> - Entity check: confirm the seller, operating subsidiaries, asset owners, debtor names, and relevant jurisdictions against official records and transaction documents.
> - Access check: document seller authority, granted accounting and bank scopes, permitted recipients, retention period, and revocation procedure before ingestion.
> - Reconciliation check: tie every imported period to source totals, explain material exceptions, and obtain QoE accountant review of proposed earnings adjustments.
> - Cash check: preserve the canonical profit basis, model principal separately, and test actual lender covenants and working-capital needs before fixing purchase terms.

## 4. Convert diligence evidence into a bounded 100-day operating system  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

At signing, convert unresolved diligence findings into a transition register rather than leaving them inside a report. Every item needs an operating consequence, accountable person, due date, supporting artifact, and escalation threshold. A customer concentration finding becomes a retention conversation owned by the operating manager; a covenant becomes a scheduled calculation owned by the controller; a permit condition becomes an agency-confirmed task reviewed by counsel. During days 1–30, stabilize access, cash visibility, payroll, customer communication, and essential vendor payments. During days 31–60, close the first repeatable monthly reporting cycle and resolve inherited account mapping errors. During days 61–100, automate only workflows that already reconcile manually. Do not migrate accounting systems simply because the integration contractor prefers another platform. Require a documented operating benefit and a reversible migration plan. Keep the four delivery employees focused on service continuity, collecting only the operational evidence needed for exception resolution. P7 succeeds when the acquired business keeps customers and produces dependable records, not when the acquisition desk displays the largest collection of dashboards. Preserve the seller's original records through the agreed retention period.

Refresh evidence according to decision exposure. Bank liquidity may need daily review, accounting exceptions weekly review, and formal ledger reconciliations monthly review. Use lender reporting deadlines and license renewal dates rather than an arbitrary universal schedule. Repeat lien and litigation searches at counsel-directed transaction milestones; repeatedly downloading annual Census releases adds no information. Record stale-source warnings separately from actual adverse findings. An unavailable portal is a retrieval exception, not evidence that a license expired. Maintain replayable imports and test recovery from a revoked token, corrupted file, changed column name, and interrupted download. Track exception age, unreconciled dollars, connector failures, and customer-retention actions completed, rather than celebrating API-call volume. The controller should be able to reproduce the monthly cash bridge without the contractor being online. Store current runbooks alongside access inventories, backup locations, and credential-rotation responsibilities. Remove departed seller and contractor access promptly while preserving required records. This turns back-office automation into durable operating capacity instead of a fragile dependency on one technically skilled person or a forgotten personal account.

Measure the information edge against the same scorecard throughout ownership. Conservative and Base retain seven full-time employees plus three contractors; Scaled permits eight full-time employees plus four contractors, totaling twelve people. The larger case is a separate operating scenario, not permission to quietly expand the Base pod. Put subscription fees, connector maintenance, specialist retainers, and reporting labor inside the expense model supporting each margin. If those costs were omitted, show the resulting variance rather than claiming that automation preserves the stated profit automatically. Keep the $30,000–$120,000 startup-capital range distinct from annual sales, owner profit, and borrowed acquisition principal. Evaluate tools by fewer unresolved liabilities, faster reconciled closes, earlier attrition detection, and better-supported financing decisions. None of those benefits creates revenue until the operating business actually earns recognized sales. Review the seven plays quarterly and retire datasets that no longer change a decision. The desk's advantage is a short, defensible path from source evidence to action, maintained within the team's real capacity and the debt structure's actual cash demands.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Scope the target and evidence rights
   - Create the jurisdiction map, seven-play assignment, and seller-authorized data request.
   - Controller records legal identifiers and recipients; counsel specifies search coverage; owner limits active files to available review capacity.
2. Capture and normalize without destroying provenance
   - Retrieve permitted source artifacts, hash originals, and import versioned records into the evidence ledger.
   - Integration contractor implements bounded retries and duplicate controls; controller quarantines incomplete batches and verifies source control totals.
3. Reconcile earnings, liabilities, and debt cash flows
   - Trace recognized sales, challenge add-backs, and build a separate principal and working-capital bridge.
   - QoE accountant supports adjustments, counsel resolves legal findings, and lender-specific covenant definitions govern financing calculations.
4. Transfer findings into the first 100 days
   - Assign each surviving exception to a named operating owner with a deadline and supporting evidence.
   - Operating manager protects customer continuity while controller runs reporting; automate only reconciled processes and remove obsolete access.
