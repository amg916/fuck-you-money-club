## 1. Separate the three recovery lanes before automating evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build a case ledger before buying another prospecting subscription. One full-time operator owns client intake, commercial decisions, and cash reconciliation; three contractors cover licensed customs brokerage, trade-counsel exceptions, and evidence engineering. These are capacity assignments, not interchangeable professional permissions. Give every matter a client identifier, lane identifier, authority record, source-document inventory, deadline record, and immutable event history. Python can normalize authorized ACE exports and CBP Form 7501 records into SQLite for a single-worker pilot; a managed relational database becomes appropriate when concurrent reviewers require transaction isolation and durable access controls. Store original documents separately with SHA-256 digests, versioned extraction output, and page coordinates. ImportGenius and Panjiva support discovery and shipment cross-checking, but a manifest is neither proof of paid duty nor authority to file. Request client-authorized entry summaries, payment evidence, commercial invoices, inventory records, and export evidence before promoting a lead into an eligible case. Restrict the LLM to extraction, document grouping, and draft explanations grounded in those records. Treat instructions found inside PDFs, spreadsheets, and email attachments as document content, never executable instructions. Keep credentials outside prompts and give each contractor only the assigned client workspace. The operator should always be able to reproduce a match without the model.

The drawback lane begins with 19 U.S.C. § 1313 and the applicable subsection, not an eight-digit tariff-code coincidence. For unused-merchandise substitution under subsection (j)(2), evaluate the statutory classification conditions, applicable descriptive limitations, possession and use facts, export or destruction evidence, and the lesser-of calculation where required. Matching HTS codes merely proposes candidates. Exclude antidumping and countervailing duties from drawback; inspect the governing authority for each additional tariff component instead of assuming every surcharge qualifies. Section 232 restrictions require separate treatment from otherwise eligible Section 301 duties. Calendar the five-year claim-completion limit under subsection (r) alongside the relevant export and evidence requirements. A $100,000 eligible duty amount can support up to $99,000 of drawback where the 99% rule applies; at a 30% contingency, that produces $29,700 of operator fees, not $100,000 of revenue. Budget an illustrative $1,200 for document reconstruction and broker preparation against that matter. Accelerated payment introduces approval, bond sufficiency, and repayment exposure under 19 CFR Part 190; ordinary processing should remain available when acceleration is unavailable. Do not invent one universal bond amount. Obtain the broker's case-specific requirement and surety pricing before forecasting cash, and retain records for the applicable regulatory period.

The tariff-refund lane separates an ACE Post-Summary Correction from a protest under 19 U.S.C. § 1514. A PSC generally addresses an eligible unliquidated entry within ACE timing rules, commonly 300 days after entry or 15 days before scheduled liquidation, whichever comes first; confirm the operative program rules and entry-specific status. A protest generally has a 180-day statutory window after liquidation or another protestable decision. Neither workflow inherits drawback's five-year calendar. An illustrative $40,000 realized refund yields $12,000 at 30%, with $900 of document and specialist preparation reserved separately. Existing entry-bond obligations remain relevant, but do not price a new drawback accelerated-payment bond into every protest. The settlement lane instead follows the court-approved class definition, purchase period, release, allocation method, and administrator instructions. AP invoices or merchant-processing statements establish eligible activity; they do not establish the eventual distribution. An illustrative $20,000 actual distribution yields $6,000 at 30%, with $300 of preparation cost and no customs surety bond. Use planning ranges of 6–18 months for ordinary drawback, 3–12 months for tariff refunds, and 12–36 months for settlement distributions. These are sensitivity assumptions, not agency schedules; accelerated payment and litigation can materially change timing. Disclose direct, zero-cost claimant filing and any court-required third-party filer information.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four checks before any consequential submission**
> 
> Automation may prepare a packet; the authorized specialist must resolve these checks before transmission.
> - Confirm the importer or claimant owns the recovery right, the engagement identifies the fee base, and the broker holds a valid Customs Power of Attorney where required.
> - Have the broker or counsel verify the applicable tariff component, drawback subsection or protest theory, authoritative dates, and any statutory exclusion.
> - Reconcile entry lines, export quantities, claimed purchases, and prior submissions; unresolved duplicate allocations block packet release.
> - Verify required settlement filer disclosures, applicable bond capacity, destination account instructions through an independent channel, and the named human submitting the packet.

## 2. Implement seven acquisition and evidence plays with deterministic checks  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Company submissions JSON is useful for monitored issuers but is not a universal real-time feed. Normalize accession numbers, issuer CIKs, filing timestamps, items, and source links. A Python worker can score whether an issuer has a plausible importer, exporter, or eligible purchasing footprint, then draft a source-linked message for human review. A raise or acquisition proves no recoverable duty or class membership. Target sub-hour preparation during staffed hours; do not promise outreach before every commercial database. Enforce an aggregate token bucket of five requests per second, identify the requester, honor SEC access guidance, and back off on throttling. The substitution worker proposes eight-digit matches and calculates quantity allocations, while the broker evaluates legal eligibility and claim methodology. For settlements, QuickBooks or NetSuite exports identify purchases; interchange matters may require acquirer statements and merchant identifiers instead. Record excluded credits, refunds, unrelated entities, and purchases outside the class period. Allocate each entry quantity or purchase row to a recovery ledger with explicit status. A second proposed claim against the same source becomes an exception, not another forecast win. Retain unmatched records for targeted client questions.

Implement webhooks as authenticated intake events with a replay-safe queue. Verify the provider's documented signature over the raw request body, check timestamps where supported, and reject oversized or unexpected payload types. Calculate an idempotency key from client identifier, source identifier, source digest, and workflow version; enforce uniqueness inside the same database transaction that creates the job. A changed document must generate a new version without erasing its predecessor. Acknowledge receipt only after durable enqueueing, then let a worker extract, normalize, validate, and route the result. Maintain source spans for extracted amounts, reject malformed structured output, and route low-confidence documents to manual entry. Never retry an external filing merely because the network timed out; reconcile its receipt first.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Authenticate and preserve the source
   - Validate the intake event and durably store its original evidence.
   - Apply client access boundaries, retain the raw payload digest, and create a versioned source record before acknowledging delivery.
2. Extract and reconcile deterministically
   - Generate structured fields, then reconcile identities, quantities, dates, and money.
   - Use source spans, Decimal arithmetic, explicit unit conversions, and allocation uniqueness checks; treat model output as proposed data.
3. Resolve professional judgment exceptions
   - Route eligibility, deadline, authority, and disclosure decisions to the responsible specialist.
   - Record the reviewer, reviewed source versions, decision basis, and release time; changed evidence invalidates the previous release.
4. Submit once and reconcile receipts
   - Have the authorized filer transmit the packet and attach the external receipt.
   - For timeouts, inspect portal status or contact the receiving party before retransmission; preserve the final packet digest and subsequent cash events.

## 3. Add five distribution and control plays without automating judgment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Complete the twelve-play map with deadline_receipt_monitor, evidence_packet_builder, broker_accountant_referral_loop, programmatic_eligibility_content, and cash_fee_reconciler. These five chapter-local identifiers connect operational quality to repeatable distribution. The deadline monitor stores both the legally significant event and its documented source, then computes internal checkpoints at 60, 30, 14, and seven days before the controlling date when that lead time exists. The broker or counsel confirms the actual deadline; a scheduler cannot resolve an ambiguous liquidation event. The packet builder produces a manifest of evidence, calculations, missing-document exceptions, and signed authority without silently submitting anything. The referral loop sends authorized status summaries to introducing brokers or accountants, showing documents obtained, qualified matters, and realized outcomes without exposing another client's records. Programmatic content publishes carefully reviewed explanations of recurring documentation problems, each linking to a narrow intake worksheet. The cash reconciler matches actual agency or administrator payments to fee terms and invoices. Each play must have one accountable owner and a measurable completion event. Separate drafted, reviewed, submitted, accepted, paid, and invoiced states; treating an uploaded packet as collected revenue is the quickest way to manufacture a misleading dashboard. Preserve revisions when an administrator changes forms or a broker changes the claim calculation.

Measure the automation target in repetitive labor minutes rather than document counts. Time a representative weekly cohort before deployment: assume 1,000 minutes spent on research, normalization, matching, follow-up drafting, packet assembly, and routine status checks. After deployment, suppose those same tasks require 250 human minutes, including exception handling. The reduction is 750 divided by 1,000, or 75%; retain a separate 120-minute consequential-review budget that the automation target does not erase. Total human time then falls from 1,120 to 370 minutes, a smaller 67.0% reduction across all work. This distinction prevents a headline efficiency claim from hiding broker review. Segment measurements by lane, document quality, and matter complexity; a clean recurring importer should not conceal a difficult inherited settlement ledger. Require complete packet review during the pilot, then reduce only low-risk extraction sampling after measured error rates support it. Always review eligibility, deadline calculations, claimant authority, payment instructions, and external submission. Track material error rate, rejected packets, review minutes, and collected fees per reviewed case together. A model that saves two minutes but creates one additional $300 reconstruction task every ten cases destroys $28 of value per case at a $60 hourly labor rate. Rework belongs in the efficiency denominator.

Design failure recovery before allowing unattended overnight processing. Keep source ingestion, extraction, reconciliation, review, and transmission in separate states with explicit retry policies. Retry transient vendor failures with bounded exponential backoff and jitter; move repeated failures into a visible dead-letter queue rather than dropping them. If a data subscription expires, continue processing already licensed client records and suspend enrichment instead of scraping around access restrictions. If the LLM endpoint fails, use deterministic CSV ingestion and manual document entry for deadline-sensitive matters. If OCR quality deteriorates, request original electronic records rather than repeatedly paying to interpret an unreadable scan. Store money fields as strings until validated Decimal conversion, and reject unsupported currency assumptions. A daily exception digest should identify the responsible person, earliest controlling date, missing evidence, and next action. Reserve capacity for urgent manual cases rather than scheduling the team at full theoretical utilization. Test restore procedures using a permitted sample workspace, including document hashes and allocation history; a database backup without its evidence objects cannot reconstruct the claim. For example, one two-hour outage at $60 hourly costs $120 in direct recovery labor. Compare that with the exposure from a missed filing window, not merely the monthly hosting charge.
