## 1. Separate eligibility, authority, and cash mechanics before quoting a fee  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat drawback as a statutory matching business, not a generic tariff rebate. Under 19 U.S.C. § 1313 and 19 CFR Part 190, identify the applicable manufacturing, unused-merchandise, rejected-merchandise, or other authorized provision before building the population. Unused-merchandise substitution under § 1313(j)(2) requires more than a convenient eight-digit HTS match; apply the statutory description restrictions, possession requirements, export or destruction conditions, and valuation limits. An ImportGenius or Panjiva manifest establishes a lead, not paid duty, qualifying use, or export entitlement. Obtain ACE entry summaries, Form 7501 line detail, payment evidence, commercial invoices, export bills of lading, and the relevant transfer certificates. Exclude antidumping and countervailing duties and any other barred duty category; assess Section 301 and Section 232 treatment separately rather than applying one tariff rule. Illustratively, $400,000 of fully eligible duties at a 99% refund rate produces $396,000 of claimant recovery and a 30% fee of $118,800. A $12,000 documentation and broker budget leaves $106,800 before acquisition, overhead, and other case costs. Track the five-year statutory claim-completion limit against each import. Budget six to eighteen months for ordinary payment as a planning assumption, not an agency service commitment; accelerated payment requires separate privilege approval, adequate bond coverage, and recapture reserves.

Keep tariff correction and protest work in a separate deadline register. A Post-Summary Correction is an ACE correction mechanism for eligible unliquidated entries, not a protest and not a way to revive expired rights. Confirm current CBP eligibility and timing instructions; the ordinary PSC window is within 300 days of entry or fifteen days before scheduled liquidation, whichever comes first, subject to applicable exceptions. Once a protestable decision occurs, 19 U.S.C. § 1514 generally requires a protest within 180 days of liquidation or the relevant decision. Classification, valuation, origin, exclusion coverage, and preference claims require different evidence; some post-importation claims instead follow § 1520(d) and its own time limit. Route formal customs submissions through a Part 111 licensed customs broker or trade counsel with authority for the specific act and a valid Customs Power of Attorney where required. Do not assume an attorney may perform every category of customs business without a broker license. Illustratively, a documented $100,000 refund yields a $30,000 fee at 30%; a $4,500 evidence and professional-services budget leaves $25,500 before shared expenses. Model three to twelve months to receipt, then stress longer disputes. No separate drawback accelerated-payment bond attaches merely because this lane seeks a tariff refund.

For settled B2B antitrust claims, eligibility comes from the settlement agreement, class definition, court orders, claim form, and distribution protocol, not from the size of the announced fund. Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 23 governs class settlement procedure; the Clayton Act, including 15 U.S.C. § 15, supplies a substantive antitrust damages framework but does not make every business an eligible claimant. Match purchasing entities, covered products, transaction dates, exclusions, releases, and any assignment restrictions. Reconcile AP ledgers, processor statements, acquisitions, and duplicate submissions before the authorized claimant signs. Use the court-approved administrator's current instructions, disclose third-party assistance when required, and state plainly that the claimant may file directly at zero cost. A $60,000 actual distribution creates an $18,000 fee at 30%; a $2,400 ledger-packaging budget leaves $15,600 before shared expenses. Purchase volume is evidence, never operator revenue. Budget six to twenty-four months after a timely claim for planning, with longer delays possible during appeals or allocation disputes. Ordinary claim submission has no customs surety requirement. Counsel must handle legal objections, disputed ownership, or release interpretation. A closed deadline means checking an express late-claim process, not promising that an administrator will reopen the class.

## 2. Contract for collected fees without purchasing authority you do not possess  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a master services agreement with separate lane schedules, not one broad promise to recover everything. The scope clause should authorize administrative data collection, reconciliation, project coordination, and expressly identified claim preparation support. Reserve customs business to properly authorized professionals and legal representation to counsel; a private contract cannot create a license. Identify the claimant, importer of record, exporter, purchasing entity, and any predecessor separately. Have the broker obtain the Customs Power of Attorney directly from the appropriate principal under 19 CFR § 111.36 rather than treating the recovery operator as a substitute principal. The client should separately retain counsel when legal work is needed. State whether professional invoices are absorbed into the operator's contingency or require specifically authorized reimbursement; zero-upfront marketing cannot conceal a mandatory intake charge. Define a 25%–35% fee range with a signed case-specific percentage and an objectively identified recovery schedule. Sample wording: 'The service fee equals 30% of covered recovery cash actually received by Client, excluding unrelated refunds, unpaid awards, and amounts returned to the payor.' Specify treatment of interest, offsets, installments, withholding, and later reversals rather than leaving those issues to invoice-time negotiation. Preserve claimant ownership of the underlying recovery.

Build payment verification around claimant-controlled receipts. Prefer direct CBP or administrator payment to the claimant, followed by an invoice supported by remittance advice and a redacted bank credit. Do not change administrator bank instructions merely to simplify fee collection. If a professional holds funds, their applicable trust-account and professional rules control. Accelerated drawback payments require special drafting because cash receipt can precede final allowance: define a reserve, fee adjustment, and repayment mechanism for recapture rather than calling the initial payment permanently earned. An anti-circumvention clause should cover only scheduled opportunities materially developed during the engagement and a reasonable, stated tail period, such as twelve months subject to state-law review. Exclude independently documented preexisting claims and prohibit double billing with an incumbent provider. Termination must preserve the claimant's ability to meet deadlines, obtain their records, and change representatives. Allocate intellectual property precisely: the client owns source records and claim-specific deliverables; the operator retains preexisting matching code and generalized methods, with a usable license for any embedded material needed to maintain the claim. Prohibit resale of identifiable ledgers and prohibit model training on client records unless separately and expressly authorized. Document the subcontractor access list and deletion obligations.

Make liability and dispute terms operationally usable. A fee-based liability cap needs a negotiated floor and jurisdiction-specific enforceability review; carve out intentional misconduct and address confidentiality breaches, mishandled funds, and unauthorized filings explicitly. Do not let a narrow cap excuse a promised deadline-control service without explaining the allocation. Use a short notice and executive-resolution period, then a defined court venue or a clearly drafted arbitration process with costs, emergency relief, and evidence preservation addressed. Require clients to certify record accuracy without shifting responsibility for the operator's own transformations back onto them. The commercial schedule must reconcile to Formula v2.0: Conservative revenue of $110,000 less $74,800 total operating costs produces $35,200 at 32%; Base revenue of $515,000 less $283,250 produces $231,750 at 45.0%; Scaled revenue of $920,000 less $386,400 produces $533,600 at 58%. Staffing remains 1 FT + 3 C, 1 FT + 3 C, and 2 FT + 4 C respectively. These are annual earned-fee scenarios, not sums of the illustrative lane cases. Evaluate principal-versus-agent presentation under ASC 606 for pass-through professional services; do not book claimant recoveries or gross settlement pools as operator revenue, and do not apply a second retained-share haircut to the supplied fee totals.

## 3. Build a jurisdiction matrix and an evidence-preserving intelligence queue  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Federal customs authority does not erase state boundaries around legal services, collections, privacy, or finder contracts. California Business and Professions Code § 6125 and New York Judiciary Law § 478 illustrate restrictions on unlicensed legal practice; local rules also govern lawyer referral arrangements and fee sharing. The operator should earn a separate administrative-services fee, not a share of counsel's legal fee. If a public-record lead becomes a state unclaimed-property recovery, stop applying the customs contingency template. California Code of Civil Procedure § 1582 imposes restrictions on investigator agreements, including a 10% compensation ceiling and timing conditions; New York Abandoned Property Law § 1416 has its own agreement requirements and 15% ceiling. These are adjacent-lane rules, not caps on every customs or antitrust engagement. A probate docket may identify an estate-owned business claim, but it does not authorize the operator to represent the estate or decide succession. Confirm fiduciary authority through appropriate counsel. Assess collection-agency licensing if the actual service expands into collecting debts for others, rather than presuming every refund service falls within or outside those statutes. Record the claimant state, operating state, service location, property situs where relevant, and professional reviewer before selecting contractual terms.

Implement public_record_api_intelligence as a bounded prospecting and evidence pipeline. Ingest official court notices, public dockets, county business-related filings, probate registries, unclaimed-property ledgers, and permitted public APIs only for a defined lead hypothesis. A docket can reveal purchasing relationships or an authorized successor; an unclaimed-property listing can suggest a separate research task. Neither proves customs eligibility. Join those signals to permissioned ImportGenius or Panjiva records and client-supplied ACE exports only after obtaining appropriate access. Store source URL, retrieval time, license terms, entity-resolution confidence, and the human review decision. Illustrative weekly math is 500 records at $0.04 acquisition cost, or $20, plus three analyst hours at $35, or $105. If twenty qualified contacts produce four discovery calls, direct research cost is $31.25 per call before outreach software and selling time. At one signed engagement from those calls, research-only acquisition cost is $125; no recovery or revenue exists yet. If an API closes or access terms change, switch to scheduled bulk FOIA/public-record exports, official data feeds, and low-frequency cached queues. Under 5 U.S.C. § 552, request existing agency records; disclosure exemptions and processing delays mean FOIA is neither unrestricted access nor an instant substitute for client records.

Apply sector-specific evidence and communication rules before expanding outreach volume. Manufacturing drawback needs production traceability; resellers need possession and export evidence; related-party imports can require specialized valuation analysis. Interchange settlements require the particular administrator's merchant and processor definitions, not a generic claim that every card acceptor qualifies. For commercial email, 15 U.S.C. § 7704 requires accurate routing information, nondeceptive subjects, required advertisement identification unless an exception applies, a valid physical postal address, and a workable opt-out honored within ten business days. B2B targeting does not remove these duties. Calling and texting require channel-specific review under 47 U.S.C. § 227, 47 CFR § 64.1200, applicable state telephone-solicitation rules, and recording laws; do not infer permission from a published number. Default to manually reviewed business email rather than automated calls or texts. If California privacy-law thresholds and scope apply, assess Civil Code § 1798.100 requirements and applicable service-provider contract terms before sharing personal information. Retain drawback records under the applicable Part 190 period, generally three years after liquidation of the drawback claim under § 190.15, while separately honoring longer Part 163, court, contract, or preservation duties. A five-year claim window is not a universal record-deletion schedule.

## 4. Use exact outreach language and a documented four-step intake  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use the following email body verbatim only after the sender's system appends the real business identity, valid physical postal address, and working unsubscribe mechanism. Subject: 'Commercial inquiry about your import refund records.' Body: 'This is a commercial solicitation for recovery-support services. We help businesses organize records for potential customs duty drawback and tariff refunds. Public shipment information may indicate relevant activity, but it does not establish your eligibility or any refund amount. Would your import manager consider a short records-screening call? Formal customs submissions are handled by an appropriately authorized licensed customs broker or trade counsel. Our engagement has no upfront service fee; any contingency percentage and permitted expenses are agreed in writing before work begins. We do not need passwords or unrestricted ACE access for an initial conversation. If this is irrelevant, reply stop or use the unsubscribe link and we will suppress further marketing.' Do not insert a projected refund from manifest value, and do not imply CBP affiliation. Route replies to a human who can distinguish an exporter, importer, broker, and domestic buyer. Preserve the exact sent version, source signal, delivery timestamp, and suppression event so a contractor cannot restart outreach after a prospect has opted out.

For a settled antitrust opportunity, use this discovery opening: 'We are an independent paid recovery-support provider, not the court, settlement administrator, or class counsel. The settlement's official materials determine eligibility, deadlines, releases, and distributions. You may file directly with the official administrator at zero cost. If you choose our help, our fee and scope will be stated in a separate agreement. May we first confirm your purchasing entity, covered transaction dates, and whether anyone has already filed for those purchases?' Send the verified official administrator link with the written follow-up and reproduce any court-required third-party filer disclosure exactly; the general script does not replace prescribed language. Then ask: 'Can your team export the relevant AP or processor records through your own authorized account? Please exclude unrelated employee and customer details. Who can certify the submission, and are there predecessor entities, assignments, releases, or overlapping providers we should flag?' Never call an estimate an allocated award. If asked whether hiring the operator is necessary, answer: 'No. Direct filing is available at zero cost; our paid service is optional administrative support.' If the administrator bars the proposed third-party arrangement, stop that arrangement instead of changing the claimant identity or hiding the provider.

Use discovery to kill weak cases early while preserving strong ones. Ask: 'Which entity paid the duty or made the covered purchases? What evidence links each entry or transaction to that entity? What is the earliest possible filing deadline? Has another provider, broker, or affiliate already claimed this recovery? Are any receipts provisional or subject to repayment?' Record answers as unverified statements until source documents support them. Do not promise that a broker can repair missing export evidence or that a court will accept reconstructed purchases. The four-step procedure below turns those questions into an auditable intake. Use encrypted client uploads, least-privilege workspaces, deterministic reconciliation scripts, and a separate exception register rather than collecting credentials by email. Every handoff should carry the claim identifier, responsible professional, evidence index, deadline basis, and unresolved exceptions. If the project fails eligibility screening, explain the specific missing condition and return usable records; do not replace the original lane with a different service without agreement. At receipt, compare approved claim, remittance, deposit, fee calculation, and any reserve before closing the file. Review monthly conversion by qualified lead, signed engagement, submitted claim, realized recovery, and collected fee so apparent pipeline growth cannot conceal stalled cash conversion.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Classify the lane and identify the rights holder
   - Open a separate claim record for drawback, tariff refund, or settlement recovery.
   - Record legal entities, authorized signatory, controlling source, earliest deadline, incumbent providers, and the specific basis for entitlement. Quarantine adjacent unclaimed-property or estate matters for separate review.
2. Secure the engagement and professional authority
   - Sign the lane schedule and verify the required professional engagement.
   - Confirm fee percentage, covered receipts, reversals, data rights, termination handoff, and required disclosures. The broker obtains the proper direct power of attorney; counsel confirms authority for legal work.
3. Reconcile evidence and independently verify submission readiness
   - Build the indexed packet and resolve exceptions before professional submission.
   - Recompute line eligibility, document exclusions, check duplicates, validate deadline calculations, and confirm any bond-dependent payment assumptions. Retain professional review and the actual filing acknowledgment.
4. Verify cash, invoice the earned fee, and preserve the file
   - Match the claimant's receipt to the covered claim before calculating compensation.
   - Reconcile remittance and deposit evidence, apply reserves and reversal terms, invoice only the agreed fee base, and retain records under the applicable lane-specific schedule and any preservation hold.
