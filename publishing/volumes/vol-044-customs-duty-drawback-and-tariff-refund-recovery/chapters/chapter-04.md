## 1. Protect Collection Runway Before Funding Production  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Start with three capital envelopes, not a shopping list: $8,000 for a narrow founder-led launch, $20,000 for a documented repeatable service, and $35,000 for concurrent broker and counsel capacity. Ring-fence $4,400, $11,000, or $19,250 respectively as working capital, exactly 55% of each envelope. These balances support operating cash needs; they are not importer refunds, settlement proceeds, or collateral already pledged to a surety. Spend the remaining 45% against written deliverables: entity setup, coverage binding, scoped professional review, evidence normalization, basic software, and qualified outreach. At $1,500 monthly overhead, the smallest reserve covers only 2.93 months before variable case costs. At $9,500 monthly overhead, even the largest reserve covers only 2.03 months. The advertised one-to-three-month first-deal interval means an engagement can arrive then, not that CBP or a settlement administrator will distribute cash then. Build a rolling thirteen-week forecast with separate engagement, filing, acceptance, disbursement, and fee-collection dates. Freeze discretionary purchasing whenever the downside forecast breaches eight weeks of committed expenses. If recoveries require longer runway, reduce intake or arrange additional owner capital before accepting obligations; do not bridge the gap by spending money belonging to claimants.

Keep startup spending, recurring overhead, and full annual delivery expense in different schedules. The Formula v2.0 scorecard fixes Conservative revenue at $110,000 and owner profit at $35,200, leaving $74,800 of total annual expense. Base revenue of $515,000 less $231,750 profit leaves $283,250; Scaled revenue of $920,000 less $533,600 profit leaves $386,400. Those totals include the modeled cost burden, not merely subscriptions and rent. An illustrative recurring-overhead allocation of $18,000, $54,000, and $114,000 annually corresponds to $1,500, $4,500, and $9,500 monthly. The remaining expense envelopes are $56,800, $229,250, and $272,400 for delivery labor and other operating costs without double counting. Treat these splits as budgeting assumptions, not additional scorecard outputs. Contractor headcount means available specialists, not four guaranteed full-time salaries: the first two cases use 1 FT + 3 C, while Scaled uses 2 FT + 4 C. Stage contractor work by evidence milestones and prohibit open-ended review engagements. At an illustrative 30% collectible contingency rate, the revenue cases require approximately $366,667, $1,716,667, and $3,066,667 of realized client recovery. That recovery base is never operator revenue; neither projected awards nor gross class settlement funds enter the revenue ledger.

Establish the entity and money movement before collecting sensitive records. Obtain the EIN directly from the IRS without a filing-service surcharge, register where the business actually operates, and ask the tax preparer which state registrations follow from the chosen footprint. Open separate operating and reserve accounts with documented beneficial ownership and signer authority. Have CBP and settlement administrators pay claimants directly whenever the applicable process permits, then invoice the earned fee under the engagement agreement. If a particular matter requires another payment route, have counsel document custody, reconciliation, and disbursement duties before accepting funds. Obtain professional-liability and cyber quotations that expressly describe customs-data processing, recovery administration, and subcontractor access; inspect exclusions rather than assuming a generic consulting policy responds. The budget allowances below are procurement ceilings, not insurer or attorney quotes. A bound premium above the allowance requires reducing scope or increasing capital, not silently consuming the reserve. Require written service scopes, confidentiality terms, record-return obligations, and incident contacts from each broker, lawyer, and analyst. A customs power of attorney goes to the authorized filing professional; a software vendor receives only the access necessary for its specific processing task.

## 2. Assemble Twelve Layers Without Buying a Data Warehouse  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the stack around claimant-owned evidence rather than a subscription-driven prospecting habit. The twelve layers below give every function an owner, a named implementation, and a lean fallback. Listed dollar amounts are exact planning inputs for the named tiers, not independently verified current offers; confirm regional pricing, tax, billing commitment, and product limits before purchase. Free software still consumes deployment and maintenance time. One Google Workspace Business Starter seat at $7 monthly on an annual commitment, one Bitwarden Teams seat at $4 monthly on annual billing, and 100 GB of Amazon S3 Standard storage at $0.023 per GB-month produce a $13.30 monthly licensing-and-storage subtotal. Requests, retrieval, transfer, backups, and implementation labor sit outside that subtotal. Carry a $75 monthly stack allowance for a minimal deployment, then expand only against measured usage. Use encrypted local processing for the first pilot instead of publishing an unmaintained web service. ImportGenius and S&P Global Panjiva can support prospect qualification and shipment leads, but subscription quotes belong in a later acquisition experiment. Manifest data does not prove duties paid, establish claim ownership, or replace an authorized ACE export and supporting CBP Form 7501 records.

Create an evidence manifest before writing a matcher. Each source file receives a claimant identifier, source system, export date, content hash, record count, currency, and access classification. Preserve original files unchanged; write transformations and analyst decisions to separate versioned outputs. A practical relational model links entry number and line, import date, importer of record, HTS classification, Chapter 99 treatment, duties paid, purchase order, SKU, quantities, export date, bill of lading, and the claimed recovery lane. Use Python decimal arithmetic for money and explicit unit conversion for quantities. Reconcile totals to the source before introducing fuzzy matching. An eight-digit HTS match is a candidate substitution link, not a legal eligibility conclusion: commercial interchangeability requirements, statutory restrictions, value limits, and the applicable drawback provision still control. Route unresolved quantities, inconsistent classifications, duplicate exports, and competing prior claims to a licensed reviewer. For antitrust work, keep purchaser identity, covered products, dates, payment records, and administrator identifiers in a separate schema. Never let one shared spreadsheet silently equate imported merchandise value with eligible duties or class purchases with an expected distribution. The database should preserve those distinctions even when the same client uses all three services and one analyst prepares the supporting evidence.

Provision access by client and role, not by convenience. Use multifactor authentication, individual accounts, short-lived sharing links where available, and a password manager that can remove contractor access without changing every shared credential. Keep production evidence out of public repositories and task-card attachments. GitHub stores code and synthetic fixtures; the evidence store holds private documents under separate permissions. S3 versioning protects against some accidental changes but does not independently create an immutable archive; evaluate Object Lock retention and lifecycle settings before enabling them because incorrect retention can make deletion impossible until expiry. Follow 19 CFR 190.15 for applicable drawback retention and preserve longer when a hold, dispute, or another controlling obligation requires it. Run an actual restore test using a sanitized claim package and verify hashes, permissions, and links after restoration. A self-hosted signature product requires patching, access controls, backups, and an audit record; if nobody owns those duties, use the broker's approved signature process instead. Separate the confidential first-sale document collection from the importer-facing reporting area. The final release package should expose precisely what each recipient needs, while maintaining a complete examination-ready record accessible to authorized professionals and CBP through the agreed lawful process.

## 3. Price Three Recovery Lanes Against Their Own Cash Clocks  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Duty drawback begins with recoverable duties, not the importer's merchandise spend. Under 19 U.S.C. § 1313, many qualifying claims recover 99% of eligible duties, taxes, and fees, subject to the specific provision and its restrictions. A screened $100,000 eligible-duty base therefore supports a $99,000 recovery example and a $29,700 operator fee at 30%. Exclude antidumping and countervailing duties under 19 U.S.C. § 1677h and test other tariff components against their controlling instruments; never assume every Chapter 99 charge qualifies. Verify import, export or destruction, transfer, and prior-claim evidence before assigning value. Apply the relevant five-year filing and event rules rather than treating every old import as recoverable. For illustration, budget 25 analyst hours at $40 plus a $1,500 broker package, totaling $2,500 in documentation expense before surety costs. Accelerated payment requires applicable approval and bonding under 19 CFR 190.92; standard payment does not automatically carry that same accelerated-payment bond requirement. Stress the cash forecast at six, twelve, and eighteen months without promising any agency processing interval. If collateral would consume protected operating cash, obtain a different surety structure, use the ordinary payment path, or decline the accelerated-payment commitment.

Tariff correction is a separate process with different deadlines and a different collectible base. A Post-Summary Correction addresses eligible unliquidated entries within ACE program limits; a protest under 19 U.S.C. § 1514 challenges a protestable decision and generally must arrive within 180 days of liquidation or the relevant decision. Do not market PSC as a protest or assume either route can revive a missed deadline. Check current PSC rules with the broker, including the generally applicable 300-day entry limit and fifteen-day pre-liquidation cutoff, as well as exceptions and entry exclusions. For a documented $60,000 overpayment, a 30% contingency yields $18,000 only after the agreed realization condition occurs. An illustrative review budget is fifteen analyst hours at $40 plus $1,200 professional review, totaling $1,800. A routine refund request has no universal additional recovery bond; existing entry bonds and case-specific requirements still matter. Model three-, nine-, and eighteen-month collection stresses rather than financing payroll from the shortest case. Route all formal CBP filings through the retained licensed broker or trade counsel, with authority appropriate to that role. An unresolved classification, valuation, origin, or liquidation issue goes back to the professional before a fee forecast becomes a sales commitment.

Settled B2B antitrust and interchange claims derive their eligibility from the particular settlement agreement, court orders, claim form, and distribution plan, not from customs statutes. Establish the purchasing entity, covered product or service, class period, exclusions, assignment restrictions, and filing deadline using the official administrator's materials. A $2 million accounts-payable ledger is evidence to screen, not a $2 million recovery base. If an administrator ultimately distributes $40,000 and the signed contract provides a permitted 30% contingency, earned operator revenue is $12,000. Budget eight analyst hours at $40 plus $400 review expense, or $720, for an illustrative straightforward package. There is no general CBP surety requirement in this lane; follow any matter-specific security or payment conditions instead. Explain that claimants can file directly at zero cost and supply every third-party filer disclosure required by the court or administrator. Stress collection at six, eighteen, and thirty-six months because appeals, deficiencies, allocation work, and distribution scheduling can dominate the clock. Reject duplicate submissions and keep prior representative appointments visible. Across all three lanes, a 25%–35% headline contingency must survive authorized cost commitments and collectibility testing; gross recovery, potential savings, and submitted claim value remain outside the earned-fee revenue ledger.

## 4. Test First-Sale Evidence and Release Only a Ready Production Stack  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The mapped play customs_first_sale_rule_valuation_arbitrage belongs in the startup design because confidentiality can determine whether a prospect releases usable evidence. Under 19 U.S.C. § 1401a, 19 CFR § 152.103, and the Nissho Iwai decision, a qualifying first sale requires defensible transaction value, a bona fide sale, merchandise clearly destined for the United States, and satisfaction of applicable relationship and valuation conditions. An $8 factory-to-middleman price versus an $18 middleman-to-importer price creates a $10 valuation difference before statutory additions. At a hypothetical 10% ad valorem duty, duty falls from $1.80 to $0.80 per unit, saving $1.00; 100,000 units would save $100,000. The resulting 55.56% arithmetic reduction does not establish portfolio savings. The play's 35%–55% screening range across $10 million–$250 million-plus merchandise programs requires transaction-specific testing of assists, royalties, proceeds, freight treatment, and tariff components. At 30%, a separately contracted verified-savings fee could equal $30,000 in this example, but prospective savings fees are outside the canonical realized-recovery revenue basis used here. Do not add them to the three scorecard cases or describe forward valuation savings as an already collectible customs refund.

Implement the first-sale fallback before requesting sensitive factory invoices. Establish an encrypted, customs-broker-controlled escrow vault with counsel-approved access terms, a complete document index, and a tested retrieval procedure. The importer-facing workspace can exclude the middleman's margin details, but the arrangement cannot obstruct CBP examination or deprive the importer and authorized representatives of records needed to meet their obligations. Obtain a formal customs-counsel opinion addressing the actual transaction chain, related parties, destination evidence, valuation additions, and documentary gaps. An opinion supports professional judgment; it does not replace missing evidence or bind CBP. If the initial legal allowance cannot fund that review, defer this play or obtain a separately approved project budget instead of diluting the reserve. Fall back to the otherwise supportable valuation basis when the first-sale package fails. For historical entries, ask the professional whether a timely correction or protest route actually exists; a prospective valuation theory does not reopen closed entries. Log a matter-specific exclusion whenever a potential saving lacks a recovery path. This keeps the full twelve-play opportunity shelf available without letting one attractive valuation narrative consume the working capital needed for documented, collectible recovery work.

Run the pre-launch exercise as a rehearsal of an actual matter, not a presentation. The founder supplies a sanitized entry-and-export sample, a correction sample with a known liquidation date, and a settled-class purchase sample. The analyst produces reconciled evidence packages; the broker or counsel checks lane eligibility and authority; a second reviewer attempts to find duplicate quantities, missing documents, and deadline mistakes. Demonstrate that an expired contractor account cannot retrieve records, restore a deleted test package, and reconstruct every transformed amount from its source. Obtain written agreement on submission ownership, claimant communications, deficiency handling, escalation contacts, and fee invoicing. The statutory calendar needs an owner and an independent checker; a task-board reminder alone is insufficient. Release production spending only after the remaining reserve still covers the downside cash schedule and each lane has a bounded delivery budget. Start with one accepted matter per lane rather than hiring ahead of collections. Review weekly hours per package, exception rates, professional rework, and days from distribution to fee receipt. Expand subscriptions or contractor capacity only when those measurements show a repeatable bottleneck whose removal improves collected contribution after its full incremental cost.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Classify and bound the matter
   - Assign drawback, tariff correction, or settled antitrust as the recovery lane before calculating any fee.
   - Record claimant identity, controlling authority, deadline, eligible collectible base, reviewer, and maximum authorized preparation expense in the matter register.
2. Construct the reproducible evidence package
   - Hash original files, reconcile source totals, and link each proposed recovery line to its supporting records.
   - Use exact monetary arithmetic, retain transformation versions, and isolate unresolved quantities or classifications instead of forcing approximate matches.
3. Obtain professional and operational verification
   - Have the filing professional and independent checker confirm authority, eligibility, dates, disclosures, and access.
   - Document surety terms when applicable, test the restore procedure, and record the forecast impact of a delayed payment before releasing submission work.
4. Submit through the authorized channel and reconcile
   - The authorized professional submits customs matters; the approved claimant representative follows settlement-specific instructions.
   - Retain receipts, monitor deficiencies, verify actual client disbursement, invoice the earned fee, and reconcile collected operator revenue without recording recovered principal.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four Checks Before the First Live Submission**
> 
> Each check requires an identifiable reviewer, dated evidence, and a recorded decision rather than a verbal assurance.
> - Capital check: preserve the 55% opening reserve allocation and demonstrate that committed spending fits the thirteen-week downside cash schedule.
> - Authority check: confirm the claimant entity, authorized signer, appropriate Customs Power of Attorney, professional filing role, and settlement-specific representative authority.
> - Evidence check: reconcile source totals, remove prohibited duty components, resolve duplicate claims, and independently verify every statutory or administrator deadline.
> - Security and collection check: complete a restore test, validate confidential-vault access, document any surety collateral, and confirm claimant-directed payment and fee terms.
