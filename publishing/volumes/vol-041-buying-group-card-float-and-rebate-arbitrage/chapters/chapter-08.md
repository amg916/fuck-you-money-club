## 1. Build an evidence waterfall before assigning buying-group credit  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a counterparty record, not a promotional spreadsheet. BuyForMeRetail, Pointsmaker, and MaxOutDeals are research targets, not interchangeable credits or endorsements. Obtain the contracting entity, warehouse operator, remittance entity, written payment terms, and accepted product specifications directly from each proposed counterparty. Resolve the entity against its formation registry, then search UCC filings in the legally appropriate jurisdiction using the exact debtor name and documented variants. A warehouse address in Delaware does not establish the counterparty's state of organization. Preserve search date, search criteria, filing number, and source document; a clean search is evidence about indexed filings, not proof of solvency. The first six directory entries below establish identity, secured-credit context, and tax treatment. Apply the waterfall in order: first-party contract, official entity record, relevant UCC record, state tax authority, then a small paid pilot. Do not purchase consumer enrichment profiles when the business decision requires corporate authority and payment performance. For a $10,000 purchase, cap initial exposure at the amount the desk can repay without any buying-group receipt. Expand only after matching accepted inventory to cleared funds and resolving every short-pay explanation in writing.

Treat DE, NH, and OR warehouse routing as a sourcing fact requiring documentation, rather than a universal exemption switch. These states lack a general statewide sales tax, but destination, delivery evidence, retailer policy, local exceptions, and resale rules still matter. Delaware's gross receipts regime is distinct from a retail sales tax; Oregon has targeted taxes, including vehicle-related provisions, rather than a general sales tax. New Hampshire's tax structure also contains transaction-specific taxes outside ordinary merchandise sales. A resale certificate must be valid for the actual seller and transaction when required; a warehouse ZIP code does not replace it. Store certificate jurisdiction, taxpayer identity, scope, effective date, and seller acceptance alongside the invoice. The purchasing operator must verify that commercial resale quantities are permitted before creating an order. For example, a $10,000 electronics invoice reimbursed at $10,200 produces $200 merchandise spread; $45 shipping and $35 expected loss reduce that spread to $120. Record a separately earned $300 card reward in the rebate subledger, not as another wholesale reimbursement. If the seller rejects the proposed tax treatment, recalculate landed cost with the actual tax or abandon the commitment before purchase.

Measure counterparty quality using settlement cohorts that public records cannot supply. For each accepted shipment, capture delivery timestamp, warehouse acceptance timestamp, contractual payment trigger, promised payment date, ACH initiation, and bank-posted availability. Net-3 and Net-5 describe negotiated terms only when the agreement defines the starting event and business-day calendar. A carrier delivery scan can precede warehouse inspection by several days. Report median and ninety-fifth-percentile acceptance-to-cash intervals separately from delivery-to-acceptance intervals. With five $2,000 batches outstanding, a counterparty interruption creates $10,000 of repayment exposure even when every parcel shows delivered. Use a concentration limit based on independently available cash, not advertised card limits. Require a second operator to verify any beneficiary change through an established contact channel before updating payment instructions. Public filings may lag real events; review entities monthly, UCC evidence quarterly, and payment exceptions every business day. Increase frequency after returned ACH entries, unexplained entity changes, or repeated acceptance disputes. The useful fallback is a smaller purchase commitment or another already vetted buyer, not a last-minute transfer to an unverified warehouse offering a higher headline spread. Keep rejected counterparties in the audit history with their documented reasons.

## 2. Capture authorized commerce evidence and price rebate eligibility  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The commercial evidence layer begins with authorized exports, not assumed public APIs. Obtain buying-group commitments and settlement reports from the authenticated account interface, email confirmations, or a feed the counterparty explicitly provides. Rakuten and TopCashback consumer accounts do not automatically confer access to affiliate reporting APIs or permission to automate account interactions. Use downloadable reports where offered, saved confirmations, and permissioned integrations whose contracts match the operator's role. Snapshot merchant exclusions, commercial-resale restrictions, eligible categories, coupon rules, maximum rewards, and posting deadlines before purchase. A portal click is evidence of referral, not an earned receivable. Maintain separate states for observed, pending, confirmed, payable, paid, and reversed rewards. On $10,000 of eligible spend, a 3% card reward and 1% portal reward total $400 before fees and losses; if resale purchases are excluded, the portal component is zero, leaving $300. Do not silently substitute the advertised percentage when eligibility fails. Freeze the source terms associated with each purchase so later website changes do not overwrite the evidence used to underwrite that transaction. Attribute reversals to the original purchase cohort rather than hiding them in the current month's acquisition performance.

Price invoice-payment rails independently from merchandise routing. Plastiq and Melio product availability, card acceptance, permitted payees, fees, and delivery times depend on the account and transaction; capture the actual checkout quote before approval. An illustrative $10,000 eligible invoice with a 2.9% service fee costs $290. A 3% reward calculated on the invoice amount earns $300, leaving only $10 before financing, exceptions, or reward disqualification. At 2%, the same transaction loses $90. If the fee itself earns rewards, record that increment only after confirming issuer treatment; do not assume it in underwriting. Portal rewards generally should not be attached to invoice-payment services without express eligibility evidence. Reject self-payments, fabricated invoices, and any flow that violates issuer or payment-service terms. A legitimate supplier invoice does not establish card-reward eligibility by itself. The fallback for a negative spread is ordinary ACH or another approved low-cost method, not a larger payment intended to manufacture bonus volume. Keep commercial-card activity within approved purchasing capacity and documented repayment resources; exclude consumer card-cycling. Reconcile provider settlement confirmations to the supplier's receipt because a successful card authorization does not prove final delivery of the supplier payment.

Normalize parcel evidence without granting it more authority than it deserves. UPS Developer Portal tracking access requires an application and the applicable credentials and account permissions; permitted volumes and available services depend on the developer agreement. Use a shipment-level polling schedule, slow polling after stable transit events, and stop routine polling once a terminal status is reconciled. A delivered scan does not establish serial-number acceptance, damage condition, or reimbursement liability. Join tracking number, purchase order, carton identifier, insured value, and warehouse acceptance evidence. If $10,000 of inventory is split across five parcels, a missing $2,000 parcel remains an unresolved asset exposure until an accepted claim or buyer payment resolves it. Insurance coverage must match merchandise class, value, packaging, and claim conditions; declared value alone may not provide the coverage the operator expects. Store shipping receipts, packing images, serial records, and claim deadlines outside the carrier's short-lived tracking interface. When an API fails, use the carrier's authorized web tracking and warehouse evidence, tagging the observation as manually collected. Do not mark a shipment accepted merely to make the settlement dashboard look current or to release another purchase into an already concentrated counterparty.

## 3. Join treasury feeds to a liability-first, auditable pipeline  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the treasury waterfall from bank-posted balances, card statements, and instrument settlement facts before adding public yield observations. Treasury Fiscal Data supplies official auction records through a documented API; it does not supply a guaranteed executable secondary-market price. TreasuryDirect explains auction and holding mechanics, while a brokerage confirmation supplies the actual purchased security, settlement date, maturity date, and cash proceeds. FDIC BankFind Suite verifies bank identity and insurance status, but it does not determine whether every fintech balance arrangement satisfies pass-through coverage requirements. Review account ownership and deposit placement separately. For an illustrative $10,000 reserve earning 4.5% simple annualized yield over 25 days, expected gross interest is $30.82 using a 365-day denominator. That calculation is a planning approximation, not Treasury bill pricing. A $40 transfer or liquidation cost makes the proposed incremental sweep unattractive. Prefer maturities and available settlement cash that precede card obligations by a documented buffer. Never count the same $10,000 as both unrestricted purchasing capacity and reserved repayment cash. Bank bonuses belong in a separate conditional receivable schedule with qualifying activities, holding periods, payment windows, account fees, and clawback conditions linked to the issuer's dated offer.

Represent a 55-day statement float as a possible timing interval, not a standing entitlement. Actual purchase date, statement close, grace-period conditions, due date, and issuer terms determine the obligation. Suppose a $10,000 purchase occurs on day zero and its full card payment is due on day 55. A buying group expected to pay on day 12 may instead pay on day 60; the desk must fund five days without that reimbursement. At an illustrative 24% annualized simple borrowing cost, five days costs $32.88 before fees, although the card agreement may impose a different interest calculation or remove the grace period. Stress a longer delay and an issuer limit reduction together. Keep bank API observations distinct from final statements: pending card charges, reversed transactions, and changing bank transaction identifiers can distort apparent liquidity. Plaid Transactions access, where supported and authorized, provides useful observations but does not replace the issuer statement for the amount due. The fallback is an issuer-exported statement and direct bank balance verification. Stop new purchases when the repayment coverage test fails; do not extend the model by assuming another card will remain available during the same issuer or counterparty disruption.

A small desk can implement the evidence pipeline with Python's sqlite3, decimal, csv, json, hashlib, urllib.request, and zoneinfo modules. Use integer cents or Decimal for money, explicit currency fields, timezone-aware timestamps, and an append-only observation table. Preserve source identifiers and hash original statements; a hash detects later alteration but does not authenticate the original issuer. Model commitments, orders, shipments, acceptances, reimbursements, rewards, obligations, and treasury positions as separate records joined by stable internal keys. Deduplicate using provider identifiers plus account context, not amount and date alone. Poll with bounded concurrency, pagination checkpoints, timeouts, exponential backoff, and Retry-After handling; never invent a universal requests-per-minute allowance. Quarantine malformed or stale observations rather than silently replacing missing values with zero. Keep credentials outside source files and encrypt stored account exports using the operating system's supported storage controls. Each morning, run sqlite3 integrity_check, balance roll-forwards, unmatched-event counts, and due-date coverage tests before refreshing the operator dashboard. Restore a backup weekly into an isolated database and reconcile a sample statement end to end. If an integration fails, continue from signed-off manual imports carrying source dates and reviewer identity, while preventing duplicate ingestion when automated collection resumes.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Acquire and fingerprint original evidence
   - Collect authorized commitments, account exports, statements, and official reference observations.
   - Assign source, observation time, effective time, account scope, and SHA-256 digest; retain the original outside the working tables.
2. Normalize without merging distinct economic events
   - Create separate merchandise, rebate, liability, and treasury records using stable internal identifiers.
   - Use integer cents or Decimal, reject unsupported currency mixtures, and quarantine missing identifiers instead of guessing purchase matches.
3. Reconcile cash and stress the next due date
   - Match warehouse acceptances to reimbursements and bank-posted cash to issuer-stated obligations.
   - Run delayed-ACH, reward-reversal, and issuer-capacity scenarios; reserve independent repayment cash before releasing another purchase.
4. Publish exceptions with accountable resolution
   - Issue a daily dashboard showing stale sources, unmatched amounts, concentrated counterparties, and repayment coverage.
   - Assign an owner and deadline to every exception, preserve the correction trail, and verify a backup restoration each week.

## 4. Map eleven workflows without corrupting the three-model scorecard  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Keep three economic models separate: wholesale fulfillment earns merchandise spread, commercial cards and portals earn eligible rebates net of fees, and treasury management earns interest or satisfied bonus awards. The canonical Formula v2.0 planning basis combines gross wholesale reimbursements, rebates, and treasury yield; it does not include card advances, invested principal, or underlying customer spending as additional income. Conservative gross basis of $250,000 multiplied by 35% produces $87,500 retained contribution; subtracting $67,500 of remaining costs yields $20,000 owner profit. Base gross basis of $1,025,000 produces $358,750 retained contribution; subtracting $215,250 yields $143,500. Scaled gross basis of $1,800,000 produces $630,000 retained contribution; subtracting $270,000 yields $360,000. Gross-basis margins are 8%, 14.0%, and 20%; retained margins are 22.86%, 40.0%, and 57.14%. These residual costs are reconciliation amounts, not invented monthly overhead budgets. The supplied $1,500–$9,000 monthly opex range cannot automatically be equated with every annual residual cost category. Maintain the 35% retained-share bridge while assessing ASC 606 principal-versus-agent presentation from actual contracts; a planning multiplier alone does not establish recognized accounting revenue. The illustrative transaction examples above explain controls and are not forecasts that mechanically reproduce the annual scorecard.

The eleven mappings below are ten chapter-defined operating workflows and one supplied cross-shelf identifier. The ten workflows are entity verification, tax routing, commitment matching, parcel claims, settlement forecasting, card eligibility, portal attribution, invoice-fee routing, treasury maturity matching, and bank-bonus qualification. They are not claimed to be ten additional canonical play identifiers. Each workflow needs a measurable decision and a fallback that reduces exposure when evidence fails. For instance, a $300 expected card reward cannot justify $400 of incremental shipping and handling, and a $200 bank bonus less $75 in account fees contributes only $125 before taxes and capital opportunity cost. Preserve the supplied staffing cases of 1 FT + 2 C for Conservative and Base, and 2 FT + 3 C for Scaled. Automation should remove duplicate collection and matching work rather than manufacture additional purchasing authority. Route beneficiary changes, unexplained short-pays, and missing statement obligations to a named reviewer. Test the system with a duplicate shipment event, a reversed reward, a stale bank connection, and a delayed warehouse acceptance. Correct handling means the ledger remains balanced and available purchasing capacity falls when uncertainty rises.

Its claimed guaranteed conversion should not be adopted: eligibility, promotional rules, market movement, settlement differences, and counterparty restrictions prevent a universal risk-free result. That calculation does not establish $3,000–$8,000 per resident or validate access across multiple platforms. The supplied fallback is participant-only execution on their own device and cellular connection, with high-liquidity mainlines; those conditions do not remove eligibility, settlement, or licensing questions. No account access, participant funds, or promotional balances enter this desk's pipeline. For the three permitted models, retain purchase invoices, price-adjustment evidence, interest statements, and payment-network reports under the applicable recordkeeping rules. IRC Section 6050W reporting does not by itself determine taxable profit, and purchase-linked rebates require treatment distinct from interest or standalone promotional income. Reconcile information returns to the ledger rather than forcing ledger classifications to match an unexplained gross reporting number.
