## 1. Build an evidence directory before buying enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a source contract, not a scraper. For every registry, record the business question, stable identifier, permitted purpose, retrieval method, publication timestamp, retention period, and fallback. A productized agency needs evidence that a workflow exists and that a buyer can authorize access; it does not need an indiscriminate database of personal details. SEC filings can expose acquisition integration work, USAspending can identify contract-heavy operating models, and Census aggregates can size a service niche without identifying individual households. None proves that a company wants an AI implementation. Convert each signal into a testable discovery question and retain the exact supporting document. Use CIK for SEC entities, award identifiers for spending records, trademark serial numbers for USPTO events, and jurisdiction plus filing number for state entities. Company names are display fields, not primary keys. A Delaware registration and a California operating address can describe one business; collapsing them incorrectly creates duplicate outreach and inaccurate research. Store source-specific identities separately, then create a reviewed relationship between them. Make that relationship reversible so corrected evidence does not require rebuilding every downstream client workflow or silently rewriting the original audit trail.

The directory below is a procurement and integration map, not a claim of live account entitlements. Prices, quotas, and service eligibility change; capture the current published terms and account limits before estimating a sprint. Free public access does not mean unlimited automated retrieval, and a commercial subscription does not automatically permit redistribution. Treat source refresh and your polling interval as separate measurements. A nightly pipeline cannot make a quarterly business dataset current, while a rapidly updated API still requires a completeness watermark. Persist published_at, observed_at, and fetched_at, recording absent timestamps as explicit machine states rather than invented dates. Use conditional requests when supported, exponential backoff with jitter, and a single quota controller for all jobs sharing credentials. SEC automation should identify the requester and stay below its published ten-requests-per-second ceiling across the organization, with additional headroom for retries. For services without a universal public quota, obey account headers and documented limits instead of guessing. Prefer official bulk files for historical backfills and narrow API requests for recent changes. When a source fails, expose stale evidence visibly; silently substituting a similarly named company is a worse failure than delaying delivery.

Sell the resulting research layer as a bounded implementation artifact: one agreed population, a documented source manifest, a repeatable import, and a reviewed exception queue. An illustrative pilot might ingest 1,000 business entities, resolve 850 automatically, send 100 ambiguous matches to review, and exclude 50 records with insufficient evidence. Those are planning assumptions, not measured vendor accuracy. If review averages three minutes per ambiguous record, the queue consumes five hours; price those hours before calling the pipeline automated. Public availability does not remove privacy obligations or source restrictions. FOIA, 5 U.S.C. § 552, provides an agency-record access framework with exemptions; it is not permission to obtain every personal field or bypass an API restriction. Where applicable, California Civil Code § 1798.100 requires purpose and retention discipline, while service-provider relationships need appropriate contractual restrictions under § 1798.140. Minimize contact details, document deletion propagation, and avoid collecting sensitive attributes merely because a document contains them. The agency's deliverable is an evidence-backed workflow that a named client owner can operate, inspect, and stop, not a speculative list whose provenance disappears after export.

## 2. Turn trademark events into verified attorney-directory workflows  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The mapped play patent_trademark_office_action_arbitrage belongs to TRACK_1, but its useful implementation is a transparent event-to-referral workflow, not an official-looking payment demand. Poll USPTO Trademark Daily XML for candidate changes, then verify each candidate against TSDR documents and current representation information. A Non-Final Office Action can contain substantive refusals, procedural requirements, or both; classify the actual document rather than treating every status change as a rejected business. Determine whether counsel is currently listed, while recognizing that public records can lag a recent appointment. Preserve serial number, document identifier, issue date, observation date, and the evidence supporting apparent self-representation. Trademark abandonment is a separate event class requiring separate verification; never infer it merely because an estimated response window elapsed. For applications subject to the ordinary rule in 37 CFR § 2.62, nonfinal responses generally have a three-month period with an available paid three-month extension; certain application categories follow different timing. The implementing software should surface the notice and its applicable rule for attorney review, not calculate a universal deadline and print it as authoritative. An attorney-approved classification is the release condition for any individualized statement about prosecution status.

Keep the agency's commercial role explicit. The client can buy a fixed-scope event monitor, a directory subscription, or a managed document-routing service; the agency should not present itself as the USPTO or sell attorney judgment through an unlicensed operator. A private referral mailer should identify its actual sender, explain why the record was selected, distinguish optional private services from government fees, and direct recipients to verify their status through official USPTO channels. Avoid government seals, simulated invoices, compulsory-payment language, and unsupported claims about registration outcomes. Before launching attorney referrals, have participating counsel assess applicable advertising, solicitation, referral-payment, and fee-sharing rules in their jurisdictions. Use objective directory criteria and disclose paid placement. Foreign-domiciled trademark applicants face representation requirements under 37 CFR § 2.11, which makes domicile and representation classification consequential rather than a casual segmentation field. If the referral arrangement cannot support those requirements, use the stated fallback: operate strictly as a transparent legal-marketing directory or software platform connecting applicants with licensed USPTO trademark attorneys. Limit software output to sourced facts and routing recommendations; a human attorney owns substantive response strategy and any advice delivered to the applicant.

Price the workflow on verified work units. In an illustrative monthly batch, 800 source events might produce 120 apparently unrepresented candidates, of which document review clears 90 for the agreed directory program. At four minutes per candidate, review requires eight hours; at an assumed loaded reviewer cost of $45 per hour, that is $360. Ninety physical mailers at an assumed all-in $1.40 cost add $126, and an assumed $64 ingestion and storage allocation brings direct batch cost to $550. A $1,500 monthly support retainer therefore leaves $950 before other delivery labor, overhead, and taxes; it does not establish the agency's net margin. Treat all figures as planning inputs to replace with supplier quotes and actual time logs. Charge initial implementation separately against defined acceptance tests, including duplicate-event suppression and corrected-status handling. If TSDR is unavailable, hold the mail queue rather than sending from yesterday's classification. If representation changes, suppress unsent outreach and correct the directory record. Applicant engagement, attorney engagements, and client matter value are downstream client outcomes, never recognized agency revenue. That boundary prevents an attractive referral funnel from inflating the canonical implementation-fee business.

## 3. Build enrichment and messaging around authorized capacity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The TRACK_2 play multi_inbox_free_workspace_outbound_clusters proposes subsidized developer or partner tenants, hundreds of secondary domains, and low-volume inbox sending. Treat that premise as an economic hypothesis requiring entitlement verification, not an infrastructure recipe to execute unquestioningly. Microsoft 365 developer subscriptions are subject to eligibility and development use restrictions; they are not a dependable source of free production cold-outreach seats. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards inbound messages and does not turn aliases into licensed outbound mailboxes. Likewise, a low-cost SMTP relay has its own acceptable-use rules and sending limits. The proposed arithmetic is straightforward: 300 inboxes sending 15 messages over 22 business days yield 99,000 messages, while 25 per day yield 165,000. Reaching 150,000 therefore requires roughly 23 daily messages per inbox under those assumptions, before suppression, failures, and pauses. Neither calculation predicts primary-inbox placement. At an illustrative paid-seat benchmark of $6–$12 monthly, 300 seats cost $1,800–$3,600 before domains, verification, and labor. Present those costs honestly in the client design. Use client-owned, properly licensed tenants and a provider that expressly supports the intended messaging; reject capacity built on misrepresented eligibility or bypassed service limits.

An enrichment waterfall should reduce unnecessary disclosure and spending before it increases contact volume. Start with the client's authorized CRM and suppression records, resolve the business domain through a published company source, then use one contracted discovery provider only for missing fields. Send permitted addresses to a verification provider when needed, but do not equate a deliverability signal with permission or inbox placement. SMTP catch-all behavior cannot establish that every guessed mailbox exists. A catch-all result should remain ambiguous and normally be excluded from automated cold outreach, not labeled as fully verified. Illustratively, resolving 600 of 1,000 domains from reusable client records leaves 400 paid lookups. At an assumed $0.08 each, those lookups cost $32; if 320 return usable, permitted records, effective cost is $0.10 per usable result. Record rejected and duplicate results so apparent coverage cannot conceal waste. Keep person-level data out of model prompts unless the workflow requires it and the processing arrangement permits it. When a provider fails or its contract disallows the intended use, fall back to existing customer relationships, published business contact channels, or permission-based acquisition rather than adding another opaque reseller to the chain.

Retain the useful parts of the proposed fallback while changing its unsafe assumptions. A maximum of two or three domains per tenant can simplify ownership and incident response, but it does not override licensing or provider rules. Removing open and click tracking pixels reduces unnecessary collection; it does not make unsolicited mail welcome. Replace the promised 100% SMTP verification with documented verification outcomes, catch-all exclusion, and a shared suppression service. Maintain a legitimate service-continuity plan, but do not swap warmed domains to evade complaints, blocks, or reputation consequences. Pause the affected stream, inspect the cause, and obtain provider clearance where required before resuming. CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. § 7704, applies to commercial B2B email and requires accurate transmission information, nondeceptive subjects, a valid postal address, and a functioning opt-out mechanism, with opt-outs honored within ten business days. Other jurisdictions can impose stricter prerequisites. Authenticate authorized domains with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, and implement unsubscribe headers when provider rules require them. Measure complaints, hard bounces, suppression latency, and accepted-versus-delivered events. Promise a configured, observable messaging workflow, never universal primary-inbox delivery or revenue inferred from messages merely accepted by an SMTP server.

> 
> Release requires documented checks against the client's actual accounts and intended audience, not assumptions copied from a play.
> - Confirm production licensing, domain ownership, provider messaging policies, and written client authorization for every connected tenant.

> - Exercise tenant isolation and emergency stopping; verify that a paused or revoked client cannot continue sending through queued jobs.

## 4. Instrument delivery and reconcile every sprint to agency economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Implement a small, inspectable data plane before adding orchestration complexity. PostgreSQL can hold source manifests, identity mappings, job states, and suppression records; encrypted object storage can hold permitted source documents with explicit expiration dates. Use separate client namespaces and scoped credentials, with tenant identity enforced in authorization checks rather than trusted from a request payload. An idempotency key should combine client, source, object identifier, and source version so retries cannot duplicate notifications or billable actions. Put external side effects behind an outbox whose worker rechecks current authorization and suppression immediately before execution. Secrets belong in a managed secret store, not spreadsheet cells or trace attributes. OpenTelemetry should expose fetch latency, parse failures, queue age, retry counts, model usage, and human-review minutes without copying customer payloads into third-party dashboards. Treat retrieved documents as untrusted data: instructions embedded in a filing or email must not change tool permissions or disclose other client records. Test that boundary with seeded hostile documents and cross-client access attempts in authorized staging. When extraction confidence falls below the agreed threshold, route work to review and retain evidence rather than allowing a model to manufacture missing facts.

Reconcile economics to the scorecard before presenting automation savings. One illustrative Conservative fee mix is ten $6,000 implementations plus four $10,000 annual support packages: $100,000 recognized annual revenue. Its canonical 20% net margin means $80,000 total costs and $20,000 annual owner net profit with 2 FT + 3 C. Base can be illustrated by twenty-five $14,000 implementations plus ten $20,000 annual support packages: $550,000 revenue, $385,000 costs, and $165,000 owner net profit at 30.0%, again with 2 FT + 3 C. Scaled can use forty $17,500 implementations plus fifteen $20,000 annual packages: $1,000,000 revenue, $600,000 costs, and $400,000 owner net profit at 40% with 3 FT + 4 C. These are reconciled fee mixes, not proven sales forecasts or alternative scorecard assumptions. Support amounts assume a full delivered year; partial-year contracts require appropriate period allocation. Client GMV, attorney matter value, attributed sales, and customer principal remain outside agency revenue. The $5,000–$60,000 startup range and $2,500–$18,000 monthly operating range are planning context, not permission to omit labor or double-count costs already inside each annual total.

Instrument the capacity model as carefully as the API calls. For the illustrative Base mix, twenty-five sprints at sixty delivery hours consume 1,500 hours. Ten support clients using eight hours monthly add 960, producing 2,460 annual delivery hours before sales, administration, incident response, and leave. Compare that demand with contracted hours and actual staffing; the scorecard's contractor count does not imply full-time availability. Establish acceptance tests for source completeness, identity precision, unauthorized-action prevention, deletion, and outage recovery, then price changes outside those boundaries. Measure effective review minutes per accepted record and gross contribution per support client rather than celebrating model token reductions alone. A cheap extraction that doubles review time can destroy the retainer. Diagnose failures with correlated request identifiers, controlled replay fixtures, and a dead-letter queue that cannot trigger external actions during inspection. Substantiate performance claims with dated baseline samples, comparable workloads, and observed results; FTC Act § 5, 15 U.S.C. § 45, makes deceptive claims an operating risk. At renewal, show fees earned, hours consumed, exceptions resolved, and measured outcomes separately. A client should understand exactly what improved without mistaking a projection for evidence.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Authorize and inventory the workflow
   - Document client-owned accounts, purposes, data classes, and named operational owners.
   - Record system-access authorization, provider entitlements, processing terms, IP ownership, deletion obligations, and the exact deliverables included in the implementation fee.
2. Collect and verify a bounded sample
   - Run a source-manifest-driven import into an isolated staging namespace.
   - Check stable identifiers, timestamp semantics, representation or contact evidence, duplicate handling, and review minutes against a manually labeled sample before estimating production throughput.
3. Exercise failure paths before external actions
   - Test revoked access, stale sources, hostile documents, throttling, and suppression propagation.
   - Confirm that retries are idempotent, paused jobs cannot send, cross-client requests are rejected, and replay tooling cannot accidentally publish messages or duplicate paid actions.
4. Release, measure, and reconcile the retainer
   - Start within agreed quotas and review operational evidence with the client owner.
   - Report accepted work units, human-review hours, source freshness, exceptions, provider costs, and earned agency fees; route scope changes into explicit estimates instead of absorbing unlimited work.
