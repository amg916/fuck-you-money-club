## 1. Assign one accountable pod and purchase only evidence-backed inventory  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run Candidate #14 through three explicitly mapped plays: diagnostic replacement lots, surgical/veterinary procedure bundles, and pre-sold aesthetic refurbishment. These are merchandising lanes, not three independent businesses. Diagnostic lots require probe condition, software entitlement, and exportable test results; procedure bundles require a verified accessory manifest and compatible power and gas connections; aesthetic units require treatment-counter evidence, handpiece condition, and lawful software access. Keep human-use and veterinary claims separate because intended use changes the applicable requirements. In every lane, the modeled operator takes title and acts as principal; an agency commission arrangement would require a different revenue model. Assign the first full-time operator to sourcing, pricing, buyer qualification, and collections. Assign the second to inventory custody, evidence completeness, dispatch, and ledger review. Four fractional contractors cover biomed testing, specialist repair, decontamination/media handling, and crating/freight. Purchase these services against defined tickets rather than assuming four continuously available technicians. The same pod serves all three lanes through a shared serial register. A unit without a named buyer profile, accessible service procedure, and bounded refurbishment budget stays outside purchased inventory even when the auction spread looks attractive.

At intake, create a device identity from manufacturer, model, and physical serial number, then photograph the nameplate and every included accessory. Record the seller's authority to transfer the unit, acquisition document, known liens, service history, software restrictions, recall findings, and pickup conditions. A hospital surplus list is a sourcing lead, not proof that every accessory or embedded license transfers. Obtain a lien release when the seller's equipment is encumbered; possession alone does not resolve a secured creditor's interest. Keep source documents in restricted object storage and place their hashes and references in the inventory record. Patient records, screenshots containing patient identifiers, and drive images do not belong in the sales database. Route the device into dirty quarantine before powering it or connecting it to any network. Require the seller's decontamination evidence, then apply the receiving procedure appropriate to the device and contamination risk. Price inbound freight, test labor, replacement parts, cleaning, and expected outbound support before bidding. A low purchase price cannot compensate for unavailable passwords, unsupported firmware, damaged probes, or consumables that the buyer cannot obtain through an authorized supply channel.

Use a purchasing score to prioritize investigation, never to certify clinical safety. The reference score weights title evidence, documented demand, serviceability, and price headroom, but release still requires device-specific technical evidence. Separate servicing that restores original performance from work that significantly changes performance, safety specifications, or intended use. Remanufacturing can change the operator's FDA role and associated registration, quality-system, labeling, and reporting duties; classify the actual activity before accepting the work. Ordinary resale does not automatically make every reseller a manufacturer or covered HIPAA entity. Conversely, contractual responsibility for protected data cannot be dismissed because the chassis is secondhand. Determine whether a business associate relationship exists and document the required handling arrangements before receiving accessible patient data. The release packet should contain the applicable service procedure revision, calibrated test-equipment identity, measured results, repair history, accessory reconciliation, and an independent reviewer decision. Preset failure thresholds and rework limits before testing begins. Otherwise, a technician under shipment pressure can turn an unresolved fault into a cosmetic description, leaving the sales operator to discover the defect during buyer acceptance.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Buy service access and evidence before buying apparent margin**
> 
> Experienced equipment traders price the missing dependency, not just the visible chassis. Use these tactics before committing scarce pod hours.
> - Ask for probe or handpiece serials before bidding; a chassis photograph can conceal the component that carries most replacement cost.
> - Have the specialist confirm password, calibration fixture, and parts access before scheduling pickup; discovering a locked service mode at receiving consumes storage and capital.
> - Photograph accessory layouts twice, at receiving and sealed packing, so a missing pedal or cable can be assigned to the correct custody interval.
> - Negotiate evidence-backed acceptance criteria before cosmetic refurbishment; otherwise the buyer can introduce new clinical performance expectations after freight has been paid.

## 2. Implement six transactional modules with evidence-linked journals  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The six reference modules below concatenate into one Python 3.9 script using SQLite and the standard library. They are an accounting and workflow kernel, not a replacement for device-specific service procedures or a payment provider. Run one connection per worker against a local disk database, enable foreign keys on every connection, and keep the database off shared network filesystems. BEGIN IMMEDIATE serializes competing writers before either can read mutable state. Every operation records a canonical payload under a stable event key; an identical retry returns without side effects, while changed content under the same key fails. Device transitions also compare an expected version, so two operators cannot both advance the same version. A busy timeout produces a retryable failure rather than a partial transaction. Store integer cents and explicit account names, never floating currency. The journal accepts only balanced batches and makes posted rows immutable. This reference intentionally excludes payables, deposits, taxes, and foreign currencies; extend its account model and reconciliation equation before introducing those transactions. Restrict writes to these functions because arbitrary SQL access bypasses application-level authorization and batch validation.

Verification is an evidence decision, not a Boolean supplied by a marketplace webhook. The authenticated application must check reviewer identity, document hashes, signer authority, and provider receipts before calling advance. The compact kernel requires nonempty evidence references and preserves them inside the event payload; it does not validate a laboratory certificate or authenticate an escrow message itself. Its state path moves from intake through verified, funded, released, accepted, and settled. Funded means the agreed escrow provider has confirmed the required funds, not that operator cash or revenue has been recognized. Released requires a packing record and shipment authority; accepted requires the contractual acceptance record. Post revenue when the governing sale terms establish the recognition event, using an accountant-reviewed policy rather than the state label alone. Settled requires a matched bank reference and the relevant receipt journal. Keep returns and disputes in a separate controlled exception process with reversing entries; never overwrite the original sale. The deliberately linear reference rejects unsupported transitions instead of pretending that every equipment dispute can be resolved through one universal automated state diagram.

Posting creates its notification in the same database transaction as the journal, eliminating the lost-message interval between committing money and queuing work. Dispatch is at least once: a crash after remote receipt but before the local acknowledgement causes a retry with the identical key and payload. The receiver must atomically deduplicate that key and reject payload changes; without that contract, duplicate external effects remain possible. Notifications contain no payment or shipment command, and consumers must not derive ordered physical actions from their delivery order. This confines race-free mutation to the documented transaction boundary rather than making an impossible promise about arbitrary external systems. Use the provider's immutable transaction identifier as the posting reference and reject identifiers invented for retries. Reconcile the trial balance, inventory account, receivables, and bank statement daily. The reference identity assumes zero opening balances and separately posted capital, which suits the worked cohort. Production operation also requires encrypted backups, restricted evidence access, tested restores, and independent review of source totals. A balanced journal proves arithmetic consistency; it does not prove that the equipment exists or that a customer actually accepted it.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Establish identities and evidence ownership
   - Create the device record and retain serial photographs, seller authority, and source-document hashes.
   - Normalize maker and model using controlled vocabulary; preserve the original serial transcription and prohibit speculative identity merges.
2. Verify before recording funded status
   - Complete decontamination, media handling, biomed review, and buyer-specific acceptance terms.
   - The authenticated application validates evidence and escrow receipts; the workflow kernel only preserves references and enforces transitions.
3. Post recognition and cash as distinct events
   - Record the approved sale recognition, release inventory cost, and post collections against the matching receivable.
   - Use immutable source references for every posting; buyer escrow funding alone creates neither recognized sales nor settled operator cash.
4. Close custody and reconcile independent sources
   - Match bank receipts, signed acceptance, serialized inventory, and journal balances before closing the deal.
   - Resolve discrepancies through documented reversing entries and exception tickets; retain original evidence and never rewrite posted history.

## 4. Stress the handoffs, preserve exceptions, and close the operating quarter  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The first thirty days of implementation should establish evidence quality before expanding inventory exposure. Configure the serial register, assign restricted evidence folders, verify each contractor's scope, and rehearse receiving with one representative unit from the chosen lane. Obtain device-specific cleaning and test procedures and confirm that necessary fixtures are available. Build buyer acceptance language around measurable configuration and performance, not vague promises of excellent condition. During days 31 through 60, exercise the complete ledger fixture in an isolated database and then process a limited live batch only after technical and commercial readiness. Deliberately submit identical intake events, altered retries, and competing state transitions. The identical retry must preserve counts; altered content must fail; only one competing version may advance.
