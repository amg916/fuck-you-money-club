## 1. Identify the payer, the signing authority, and the measurable pain  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The merchant owner signs because interrupted acceptance threatens payroll, inventory replenishment, and customer retention; the controller signs payment instructions because unexplained deductions threaten cash forecasting. Those are different buying motives, and a proposal must answer both. A restaurant processing $100,000 monthly might accept a separately priced $50 retained service contribution when documented reconciliation and terminal support save four staff hours. That illustrative contribution is not its total processing bill. Interchange, network assessments, processor charges, and contractual splits still require their own statement lines. Obtain the merchant agreement signer, settlement-account authority, and person empowered to dispute invoices before boarding. An accounts-payable contact cannot authorize changes to a sponsor-bank settlement arrangement merely because they receive statements. For larger merchants, the treasury team controls bank details while procurement negotiates the gateway contract. Sell an itemized improvement against three actual statements, not a promise to make processing free. Statutes constrain pricing and disclosure; they do not force a merchant to buy an ISO's services. The acute pain is operational: legitimate sales cannot settle dependably, reserve deductions are poorly understood, or the existing provider cannot support an accurately disclosed business model.

An ATM has three economically distinct counterparties: the cardholder paying a disclosed surcharge, the venue providing placement rights, and the vault-cash owner supplying withdrawal inventory. The venue manager may welcome installation but lack authority to grant exclusivity; verify the lease, ownership entity, and any existing machine contract. With a $3.50 surcharge, a hypothetical $1.00 venue allocation and $0.50 processor allocation leave $2.00 of retained surcharge revenue per completed withdrawal before operator expenses. At 300 monthly withdrawals, that is $600 monthly retained revenue, not the $30,000 of cash dispensed if average withdrawals are $100. Reg E requires the applicable screen or paper notice before commitment and the consumer's election to proceed. It does not require the obsolete federal exterior fee placard. A venue payment should follow settled transaction counts, not screen impressions or attempted withdrawals. Separately document who funds replenishment, bears shortages, carries cash coverage, and receives network reimbursements. A $15,000 cash load remains cash inventory or another party's funded asset according to ownership, never surcharge income. Venue demand for predictable rent should be met with a conservative minimum only after measured traffic supports it.

This chapter's 12 mapped plays comprise the two specified strategies plus ten operating applications: Schedule A residual capture, gateway markup, terminal support subscriptions, approved dual pricing, dispute deflection, reserve reporting, exclusive ATM placement, venue revenue sharing, vault-cash coordination, and residual recovery audits. These applications describe work, not additional canonical scorecard revenue. The specified strategies are high_risk_payfac_iso_mid_cascading_arbitrage and ucc1_expiration_refi_trigger_arbitrage. The first sells approved acceptance and orchestration; the second identifies potential refinancing conversations from public filings and verified obligations. A business four to nine months into an expensive advance may value a cash-flow review, but a UCC filing alone proves neither that age nor a refinance need. A contracted funder might pay a disclosed 2% referral commission on $100,000 actually funded, producing $2,000 before specified reversals and expenses. Principal remains the borrower's financing, not the referrer's sales. Require a signed compensation schedule, documented referral attribution, and a funded-event definition before transmitting the lead. If a financing partner will not identify its payment entity or reversal period, reject the arrangement. Keep illustrative referral and subscription economics outside the three canonical cases unless a separately approved model explicitly incorporates them.

## 2. Price approved risk without disguising merchants, reserves, or declines  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

For high_risk_payfac_iso_mid_cascading_arbitrage, treat a proposed network of 25–60 domestic and offshore acquiring banks as a diligence universe, not an entitlement to route transactions anywhere. An ISO relationship is not automatically a payment-facilitator appointment. Each acquirer must approve the actual merchant entity, products, customer locations, descriptors, beneficial owners, and settlement terms. A business rejected by Stripe or PayPal is not thereby acceptable to another provider. Telehealth GLP-1 services, peptide products, nutra subscriptions, sweepstakes gaming, high-ticket coaching, and AI companions have materially different licensing, product, advertising, and network restrictions. Accept only activities lawful in the relevant jurisdictions and expressly supported by the sponsor and card brands. Route among approved MIDs belonging to the proper merchant under documented rules; never substitute another business's identity or disguise the transaction category. A hard decline or prohibited retry response ends that attempt. Permitted retries need processor-specific response handling, duplicate prevention, and frequency limits. Offshore acquiring introduces additional country, currency, and cross-border requirements rather than an escape from domestic restrictions. If no approved acquirer supports the disclosed activity, the fallback is to decline boarding, not conceal it behind orchestration software.

The advertised buy rate of roughly 2.10% and merchant pricing of 5.50%–8.90% require a scope comparison before anyone calls the difference profit. On $100,000 of monthly processed sales, those prices produce $5,500–$8,900 in merchant processing charges and a nominal $2,100 wholesale charge only if that quote applies to the same transaction population. The apparent $3,400–$6,800 spread is preliminary: determine whether interchange, assessments, cross-border costs, gateway events, refunds, and dispute fees are included. Under an explicitly illustrative contract, $5,500 less $2,100 and $1,400 of additional charges leaves $2,000 distributable residual; a 50% retained share yields $1,000 operator revenue. A 10% rolling reserve withholds another $10,000 of merchant settlement for that month; it is collateral, not additional processing revenue. Make the 10% reserve the minimum proposal for this high-risk design, subject to stricter underwriting and a written release schedule. Deploy Verifi RDR or Ethoca only where supported, reconcile resulting refunds, and prevent duplicate credits. Use 3DS2 step-up on approved risk signals, including relevant BIN patterns, while recognizing that authentication does not eliminate all disputes or replace accurate descriptors and fulfillment evidence.

For ucc1_expiration_refi_trigger_arbitrage, separate lien-record intelligence from debt underwriting. Under UCC 9-515, an ordinary financing statement generally lasts five years, and continuation generally occurs during the six months before expiration; statutory exceptions exist. Filing expiration is not the underlying note's maturity, and a continuation does not establish a new advance. Use state Secretary of State records to identify the debtor, secured party, collateral description, and filing dates, then obtain actual agreements and payoff information with the business's authorization. To locate borrowers four to nine months into an MCA or equipment note, verify funding dates rather than subtracting months from an assumed lien expiration. Compare total payoff, payment frequency, liens, and proposed proceeds before presenting consolidation. A $120,000 refinance paying $108,000 of verified obligations and $4,000 of disclosed costs leaves $8,000 usable proceeds; it does not create $120,000 operating revenue. Avoid additional stacking that breaches existing covenants or conceals indebtedness. Use direct mail, compliant B2B work-email sequences, and manually human-dialed outreach as practical fallbacks, while checking applicable calling, email, broker, and state commercial financing disclosure requirements. Manual dialing is a channel choice, not a universal exemption.

## 3. Choose settlement rails that preserve custody boundaries and collection evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Card acceptance follows authorization, capture, clearing, and settlement; none is interchangeable with a final guarantee of merchant proceeds. The issuer approves an authorization through the network and acquirer, the merchant submits capture, and the acquirer calculates funding under its merchant agreement. The sponsor or processor funds the verified merchant account after applicable fees, reserves, refunds, and other adjustments. The ISO ordinarily receives its separately calculated residual on the processor's contractual payment cycle, often after month end. The ISO should not collect merchant sales into its operating account to simplify reconciliation. An ATM withdrawal instead debits the cardholder through the supported network, dispenses physical cash, and produces settlement entries that reimburse the appropriate cash-funding party and allocate charges. Match terminal identifier, transaction reference, journal, dispense status, and settlement report before declaring a shortage or paying venue commissions. A reversed or partially dispensed transaction requires the processor's adjustment procedure. Daily proof should reconcile cash loaded, cash dispensed, remaining inventory, and settlement receivables. Neither merchant settlement balances nor ATM cash reimbursements should enter the ISO's retained-revenue ledger. Keep operational access to reports separate from authority to redirect funds or change beneficiary accounts.

The eight-rail matrix distinguishes actual transfer mechanisms from commercial payment arrangements. Wire and ACH move money; escrow, retainers, statutory disbursement, platform checkout, factoring, and milestone holds describe custody, contracting, or collection structures that may themselves use those mechanisms. Select the arrangement according to the payable obligation, not the sales team's preference. A $2,000 installation invoice can use a $500 retainer and $1,500 documented completion payment, while recurring processor residuals should follow the processor's contracted ledger and payout route. Escrow is appropriate only when an authorized independent provider accepts the transaction and release conditions; the ISO's ordinary bank account is not escrow. Statutory disbursement applies only where a specific legal process controls distribution, not as a label for ordinary merchant settlement. Factoring an earned receivable requires assignment rights and clarity about recourse, reserves, and obligor notice. A platform checkout receipt proves that a payment was initiated, not that chargebacks or refunds are impossible. For wires, independently verify instructions using a known contact and segregate beneficiary changes from payment release. For ACH, preserve authorization, account validation where required, and return handling; consumer and business protections differ by transaction and governing rules.

Protect fees with precise earning events rather than ownership claims over the merchant's entire cash flow. The ISO agreement should define the residual calculation, payment deadline, statement delivery, permitted setoffs, audit access, termination effects, portfolio transfer rules, and survival of earned payment rights. A narrowly drafted non-circumvention provision can address introduced accounts, but processor acceptance, attribution evidence, and commercial enforceability matter more than aggressive wording. A merchant service order should distinguish gateway subscription charges from pass-through processing deductions and state what happens after account closure. Never attempt to collect an unrelated service invoice by redirecting card settlement without contractual and sponsor authority. Credit screening begins with verified entity records, beneficial ownership required by the sponsor, operating history, bank evidence obtained with authorization, refund practices, and financial capacity. Ask the sponsor to perform authorized MATCH and network screening; do not treat a clean search as credit approval. For a $10,000 monthly residual payer, a two-month delay creates $20,000 of exposure. Set an escalation threshold, stop adding unsecured exposure when statements become unreliable, and use contractual collection remedies. Reserve availability belongs in the counterparty assessment but cannot be assumed available to satisfy the ISO's own overdue invoices.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Map the obligation and authorized payer
   - Identify the legal payer, beneficiary, earning event, and person empowered to approve payment.
   - Separate merchant proceeds, reserve collateral, vault cash, venue allocations, and the operator's own earned fees.
2. Approve the contractual movement of funds
   - Document the appropriate rail, custody arrangement, deduction rights, and release evidence.
   - Use independent beneficiary verification and keep bank-detail maintenance separate from payment release authority.
3. Reconcile transaction evidence to the payout
   - Match processor batches or ATM journals to settlement reports, residual statements, and bank entries.
   - Investigate missing references, duplicate credits, reversals, venue allocations, and reserve changes before closing the period.
4. Escalate exceptions without diverting customer funds
   - Open a dated collection or reconciliation case and apply the agreement's documented remedies.
   - Pause additional unsecured exposure where permitted; never seize merchant settlement or vault cash to cover unrelated invoices.

## 4. Reconcile retained economics and build a defensible collection cadence  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Formula v2.0 labels the three scorecard totals gross revenue, but this candidate's specified basis is net retained ISO residuals, gateway markups, and ATM surcharges. Use that basis consistently and document principal-versus-agent conclusions under ASC 606 for the actual contracts. Do not mechanically net every receipt: determine which amounts the operator controls as principal and which it collects or reports for others. For this scorecard, the retained_share adjustment belongs before the displayed revenue total, and a share already removed must not be deducted again. An illustrative Conservative bridge is 150 merchant accounts at $30 monthly retained residual, or $54,000 annually; 150 gateway accounts at $10 monthly retained markup, or $18,000; and 18,000 annual ATM withdrawals at $1 retained surcharge, or $18,000. Total retained revenue is exactly $90,000. Deducting $63,000 of annual operating costs leaves $27,000 owner net profit and a 30% margin with 1 FT + 3 C. These account and transaction counts are illustrative reconciliations, not additional canonical inputs or measured portfolio performance. Merchant card volume and dispensed currency are intentionally absent from revenue. A separate cash forecast must address residual payment delays, expense timing, and any operator-owned vault-cash requirements.

The Base bridge uses 500 merchants at $50 monthly retained residual, producing $300,000 annually, plus 250 gateway accounts at $15 monthly retained markup, producing $45,000. Another 50,000 annual ATM withdrawals at $2 retained surcharge contribute $100,000. That totals $445,000; $255,875 of annual costs leaves exactly $189,125 owner net profit, a 42.5% margin, with 1 FT + 3 C. The Scaled bridge uses 800 merchants at $60 monthly retained residual for $576,000, 400 gateways at $20 monthly retained markup for $96,000, and 64,000 annual ATM withdrawals at $2 retained surcharge for $128,000. Revenue is $800,000; costs of $360,000 leave $440,000 owner net profit, a 55% margin, with 2 FT + 4 C. Account overlap is allowed because gateway markup pays for a distinct contracted service; duplicate residual entries are not. Staffing labels describe headcount categories, not assumed contractor hours or compensation. The supplied monthly opex range of $1,500–$10,000 is an operating planning range, not a substitute for these full annual cost totals. At Base, losing 50 accounts contributing $50 monthly removes $30,000 annual residual revenue before replacement costs or any reduction in expenses.

Close monthly using IRIS CRM exports, processor statements, gateway invoices, ATM telemetry, and bank records rather than treating a dashboard balance as collected profit. Record opening receivables, earned retained fees, reversals, collections, and closing receivables by contractual payer. Age exceptions against actual due dates, and separate merchant attrition from processor underpayment. Audit rate changes and portfolio transfers because the same merchant can survive while its residual economics deteriorate. For pricing, apply current Visa and Mastercard rules alongside state requirements; credit surcharging, cash discounts, and dual pricing are not interchangeable labels. Do not surcharge debit transactions merely because the terminal processes them without a PIN. Durbin provisions govern debit interchange and network issues but do not independently authorize every merchant pricing program. For ATMs, preserve Reg E fee-election evidence and distinguish an ordinary withdrawal-only machine from added services that could change FinCEN analysis. Remote telemetry should generate cash and connectivity exceptions, not autonomously bypass settlement controls. Finally, define who reviews disputed residuals, who authorizes refunds, and who contacts a late payer. A small team scales when each exception has an owner, source evidence, and a resolution deadline, not when customer money temporarily masks missing fees.
