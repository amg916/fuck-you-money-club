## 1. Turn sixteen named sources into evidence-backed merchant and venue queues  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a source register, not a purchased contact dump. The sixteen directory entries across this section and the next form a deliberately small collection surface: eight discovery and verification sources followed by eight commercial and operational sources. Store source URL, retrieval time, source publication time when available, jurisdiction, permitted use, and evidence identifier beside every extracted field. A municipal license establishes an administrative event; it does not establish that the storefront opened, accepts cards, or wants an ATM. A Secretary of State record establishes an entity filing, not beneficial ownership or authority to sign a merchant agreement. Never treat registered agents as sales contacts merely because their addresses are public. The twelve chapter mappings are new-opening detection, entity resolution, venue-density routing, venue exclusivity verification, Schedule A normalization, residual leakage recovery, gateway attachment, dispute intervention, authorized MID orchestration, ATM cash forecasting, ATM uptime recovery, and high_velocity_dm_outbound_systems. These are workflow mappings, not twelve independently additive profit forecasts. Assign each a measurable input, one accountable owner, and an explicit stop condition before connecting it to outreach or underwriting. Preserve source corrections rather than silently replacing the original evidence.

For new-opening detection, pull a bounded municipal license increment and compare issue dates, expiration dates, and status changes with the previous snapshot. An illustrative batch of 240 changed records might yield 60 plausible openings, 30 confirmed operating locations, and six requested pricing reviews; that is a 20% review rate on confirmed locations, not on the original feed. Entity resolution then links legal entity, trade name, premises address, and operator contact without collapsing separate franchisees. If 100 candidates produce 20 ambiguous joins and each review takes four minutes, reserve 80 minutes instead of allowing fuzzy matches to auto-board merchants. Venue-density routing uses Census coordinates and verified opening hours: twelve visits grouped into three four-stop routes can replace twelve separate dispatches, but measure actual drive time before claiming savings. For venue exclusivity verification, inspect the venue's existing ATM agreement and landlord authority with permission. A $3 surcharge at 600 monthly withdrawals produces $1,800 in collected surcharges; a 50% retained contractual share yields $900 before desk expenses. Public maps cannot establish that share or terminate another operator's contract. When records conflict, move the location to manual verification and keep the sales queue moving elsewhere.

Treat refresh cadence as an operator choice constrained by source publication, not a promise of instantaneous government data. The directory distinguishes retrieval schedules from actual freshness; no portal's unverified rate ceiling is invented. Use conditional requests where supported, bounded date windows, saved cursors, and single-worker access for portals without published automation allowances. Honor Retry-After, add exponential backoff with jitter, and stop on repeated authorization failures rather than cycling identities. Check source terms before retaining Google Places content; persistent identifiers and licensed merchant evidence serve different purposes from a copied commercial directory. OFAC screening is an escalation input for sponsor procedures, not an autonomous verdict based on a similar name. FDIC BankFind verifies insured institution identity, not whether a processor has a current sponsorship agreement. State UCC results can reveal financing statements but do not prove lien priority, current debt, or ownership of an ATM. Obtain certified searches or underlying agreements when the decision requires them. FinCEN beneficial ownership information is not an open prospecting feed; public corporate filings must never be represented as a substitute for sponsor-required ownership verification. Record these limits directly in the evidence schema.

## 2. Join authorized APIs to retained economics, not merchant settlement volume  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Commercial connectors become useful only after public records resolve to an authorized merchant relationship. Use IRIS CRM for boarding state, relationship ownership, and residual audit workflow, but let executed agreements and processor statements govern the economics. Obtain tenant-specific API documentation and credentials through the contract administrator; a product name does not imply universal endpoint availability. Schedule A normalization translates basis points, per-item fees, monthly minimums, gateway costs, and exception charges into comparable fields. For illustration, $200,000 monthly card volume multiplied by a 15-basis-point eligible spread equals $300. If the contract assigns the desk 60% of that spread, retained monthly processing revenue is $180 before desk expenses, not $200,000 and not $300. Gateway attachment adds a separate example: a $20 monthly merchant charge less an $8 contractual upstream allocation leaves $12 retained contribution. Across 100 active eligible accounts, that is $1,200 monthly or $14,400 annually before desk expenses. These examples isolate mechanics; interchange, assessments, transaction mix, and minimums must be modeled from actual statements before quoting. If the statement lacks a required fee line, request the missing schedule rather than imputing an attractive spread.

Residual leakage recovery compares the effective-dated contract with paid line items at merchant, month, and fee-code granularity. A recurring $8 shortage across 120 eligible merchants represents $960 monthly, or $11,520 annualized if the same conditions persist; an annualized exception is not booked revenue until recognition and collectability requirements are satisfied. Save the disputed statement, contract version, calculation, and processor case reference as one evidence packet. Dispute intervention uses Verifi RDR and Ethoca only through the processor or enrolled provider's authorized interfaces. If 40 eligible events cost an assumed $15 each and avoid an otherwise applicable $35 fee each, the narrow fee difference is $800, calculated as $1,400 less $600. Refund principal, alert overlap, lost product, and merchant policy can reverse that result; do not count refunded sales as savings. Authorized MID orchestration means allocating activity only among sponsor-approved merchant accounts for the actual business, products, and channels. Two approved MIDs at $25 monthly platform cost each require $50 of eligible retained contribution just to cover those charges. A declined or restricted activity stays stopped; another MID is never a route around underwriting or monitoring.

Keep the scorecard bridge explicit in every dashboard. Formula v2.0 uses $90,000 recognized annual revenue at 30% margin for $27,000 owner profit, $445,000 at 42.5% for $189,125, and $800,000 at 55% for $440,000. Corresponding annual expense envelopes are $63,000, $255,875, and $360,000. Teams remain one full-time operator plus three contractors in Conservative and Base, and two full-time operators plus four contractors in Scaled. The scorecard's gross revenue label refers here to the desk's retained revenue basis, not gross card billings or gross ATM surcharge collections before contractual allocations. Apply retained_share once when the underlying input is gross eligible fees; do not multiply an already net residual statement by the share again. Preserve an ASC 606 principal-versus-agent assessment for each arrangement because a payment flow alone does not determine presentation. The stated $1,500–$10,000 monthly operating range describes operating setup needs, not a replacement for the full scenario expense envelopes including labor and other modeled costs. Reconcile monthly recognized revenue to statements, receivables, and cash receipts with documented timing differences. Pipeline estimates, disputed recoveries, merchant GMV, and vault cash occupy separate ledgers.

## 3. Build a replayable pipeline that routes permissioned signals instead of spraying messages  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A small desk can begin with Python's urllib.request for documented HTTP interfaces, csv for processor exports, sqlite3 for local reconciliation, and hashlib for file integrity checks. Keep production credentials in an approved secret store rather than spreadsheets, command history, or exported notebooks. Separate raw ingestion, normalized entities, contract versions, economic events, and work queues. Each event needs source_system, source_event_id, merchant_key, observed_at, effective_at, and ingestion_version; a unique constraint on the source identifier makes replays idempotent. Do not use a postal address as the merchant primary key because one premises can contain several businesses or successive operators. Normalize currency with integer minor units or decimal.Decimal, preserve original signs, and distinguish statement reversals from deleted records. A daily control compares source row count, accepted row count, quarantined row count, and amount totals. If 1,000 source rows produce 995 accepted and five quarantined rows, the ingestion balances only when those five records remain visible with reasons. Reject silent coercion of blank amounts to zero. Encrypt sensitive business records, restrict contractor access by task, and keep cardholder data out of the prospecting database entirely. Test restoration before relying on the backup.

Run an enrichment waterfall from cheap, authoritative facts toward paid, conditional lookups. First deduplicate the public feed; then verify jurisdiction and premises; then confirm operating status; finally enrich only records that have a permitted contact purpose. Suppose 500 raw records reduce to 300 distinct premises, 120 active target businesses, and 30 requested follow-ups. At an illustrative $0.10 paid lookup cost, enriching all 500 costs $50 while enriching the final 30 costs $3, a $47 difference before research labor. This is a sensitivity example, not a quoted vendor tariff. Maintain field-level provenance so a Google listing cannot overwrite a legal entity name taken from a formation document. Rate-limit budgets belong to the connector, not to whichever worker happens to call it. Reserve capacity for onboarding and incident checks, use dead-letter queues for exhausted retries, and require an operator to resolve persistent schema changes. If an API fails, replay the last validated snapshot with a visible age label rather than presenting old data as current. A fallback source can support a narrower conclusion; it cannot inherit the authority of the unavailable original. Track match precision from reviewed samples before increasing acquisition spend.

The mapped play high_velocity_dm_outbound_systems replaces blind mass-DM blasts with comment-to-DM triggers, hand-raiser reaction hooks, and multi-inbox warm signal routing. Use supported Meta messaging interfaces or an authorized messaging provider, and verify the exact platform's current event eligibility, messaging window, and app permissions. A reaction is only usable where the platform permits the resulting automation. Require a user-initiated comment, eligible reaction, or inbound message before automated dispatch; send external links only after the prospect replies. Route the event to the merchant's assigned inbox, not a rotating account pool. For example, 200 eligible hand-raisers with a 30% reply rate produce 60 conversations; a 25% statement-review acceptance rate produces 15 reviews; a 20% close rate produces three merchants. At an assumed $150 retained monthly contribution each, that cohort contributes $450 monthly before desk expenses and attrition. These conditional conversions do not increase the canonical scorecard automatically. PeerFloodError, platform throttling, or silent Message Request delivery failures require a stop and channel review, not proxy rotation or extra accounts. Fall back to the public thread or an inbound contact route. Store trigger evidence, reply time, suppression status, and the authorized next action with every conversation.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register and authorize the source
   - Document the access route, permitted purpose, cost owner, and retrieval schedule.
   - Obtain contractual credentials where required; test one permitted request before creating any scheduled connector.
2. Stage and reconcile the batch
   - Save raw evidence, hash the file, normalize identifiers, and compare control totals.
   - Quarantine malformed records with reasons; preserve source timestamps and never silently discard economic events.
3. Resolve and route the evidence
   - Join merchant entities conservatively and create a named owner's next-action queue.
   - Require documented trigger eligibility for automated messages and contracted account authority for operational API actions.
4. Close the measurement loop
   - Reconcile completed actions to retained revenue, service outcomes, and documented exceptions.
   - Report actual cohorts separately from forecasts; replay corrections without duplicating messages, refunds, or recognized amounts.

## 4. Instrument ATM cash, dispute exposure, and operator workload before scaling  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

ATM cash forecasting joins authorized electronic journals, dispense totals, cash loads, reversals, and telemetry timestamps without equating cassette cash with revenue. At 80 withdrawals daily and a $100 average withdrawal, expected daily dispense demand is $8,000. Three days between service visits plus one day of buffer imply $32,000 of usable cash capacity under that simplified assumption; check denomination mix, cassette limits, events, and actual demand dispersion before scheduling loads. If capacity is lower, shorten the replenishment interval rather than assuming the machine can hold the modeled amount. ATM uptime recovery is equally concrete: twelve hours offline at an average five withdrawals per hour means 60 potentially missed transactions. At a $3 surcharge and 50% retained share, the indicated retained opportunity is $90, not the $6,000 of hypothetical customer withdrawals. Demand may shift to another machine, so mark this as an estimate. Monitor heartbeat age, dispenser faults, communications status, receipt capability, and cash alerts through supported provider controls. When telemetry goes stale, compare processor transaction activity and request an authorized site check; never infer that silence means zero demand. Keep physical access, vault funding, and replenishment instructions limited to assigned personnel.

The diagnostic stack should expose decisions rather than accumulate dashboards. Use IRIS residual exceptions for pricing drift, provider dispute portals for case deadlines, contracted ATM monitoring for service faults, and a simple cohort report for merchant attrition. Twelve lost merchants at $150 retained monthly contribution each remove $1,800 monthly run-rate revenue; a replacement cohort does not erase the loss unless acquisition cost and activation timing also reconcile. Card surcharge controls require separate treatment from ATM surcharges. Credit-card surcharge eligibility, network caps, notices, and state requirements must be checked against the actual merchant configuration; debit and prepaid exclusions under applicable network rules are not cured by selecting a credit button. Do not label an added checkout fee as dual pricing merely because the terminal offers a cash option. Regulation II, 12 CFR Part 235, addresses debit interchange and routing restrictions; it is not a universal authorization for merchant surcharge practices. For ATMs charging covered consumer fees, 12 CFR 1005.16 requires disclosure through the screen or paper before the consumer is committed, with the consumer electing to continue. Test the actual transaction flow, not only a photograph of cabinet signage.

Scale only after the evidence queue fits the stated team. If the Base desk handles 300 monthly exceptions at six minutes each, triage consumes 30 hours before sales, servicing, and processor follow-up. Reducing the queue to 120 well-deduplicated exceptions saves 18 hours at the same handling time; measure that reduction before assigning more merchants to one operator. Four contractors in the Scaled case are distinct people, not unlimited pooled capacity. Define coverage windows for dispute deadlines, cash alerts, and failed onboarding jobs. The $8,000–$45,000 startup range funds a lean launch, but a vault-cash requirement must be separately identified and funded according to the actual ownership and servicing arrangement. FinCEN's ATM guidance distinguishes limited cash-access operations from additional services that can change the regulatory analysis; do not assume every ATM business has identical obligations. Complete the four pre-flight checks below before adding automated execution. A desk that cannot prove an event's origin, authority, retained economics, and fallback should leave that event in review. The objective is a compact operating system with recoverable failures, traceable decisions, and reconciled owner profit, not a larger collection of loosely connected vendor accounts.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before enabling automated execution**
> 
> Each check requires retained evidence and a named operator. A failed check leaves the affected workflow in review while unaffected, authorized work continues.
> - Source authority: verify permitted access, account scope, credential ownership, current terms, and the documented fallback for every production connector.
> - Money integrity: reconcile signed Schedule A, retained-share treatment, statement totals, reversals, and recognition timing without importing settlement volume as revenue.
> - Execution authority: verify sponsor-approved MIDs, dispute-action permissions, eligible user-initiated messaging triggers, and a tested suppression mechanism.
> - Service readiness: verify ATM fee-disclosure flow, supported monitoring, cash-service coverage, incident ownership, and recovery from a simulated stale-data event.
