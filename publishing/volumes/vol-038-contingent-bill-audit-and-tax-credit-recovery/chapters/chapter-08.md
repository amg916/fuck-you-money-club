## 1. Build an eighteen-source directory that separates signals from evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a source register, not a purchased prospect list. The eighteen directory entries across this section and the next distinguish public discovery from account-authorized evidence. Each connector records its owner, permitted purpose, access route, fee arrangement, observed publication delay, retrieval timestamp, and fallback. Refresh schedules below are desk polling policies, not promises that agencies publish on those schedules. Attach the source URL, document date, effective period, content hash, and retrieval identity to every download. A county assessment roll can identify a large warehouse, but it cannot establish its parcel-carrier account, contracted service guarantee, utility rider, or eligible employees. Resolve the parcel owner through the county assessor and the relevant Secretary of State business search; then ask the actual operating company for account documents under signed authority. Do not equate a registered agent with the purchasing decision maker. Preserve both public entity identifiers and customer-confirmed account identifiers, because landlords, tenants, management companies, and payroll entities often differ. That identity discipline prevents a plausible prospect from becoming an unauthorized claim. Before disclosing account-specific tariff or refund findings, secure the Letter of Authorization and a dated invoice baseline covering the relevant service period.

Use twelve explicit operating lanes: parcel service-guarantee recovery, parcel duplicate-charge recovery, parcel accessorial correction, utility tariff reclassification, utility meter-error recovery, cooling-tower sewer deductions, waste-service right-sizing, telecom inventory recovery, property-assessment appeals, WOTC processing, historic and brownfield credit screening, and transferable_tax_credit_brokerage_syndication. These are twelve mapped plays, not twelve separate revenue assumptions. Give each lane its own evidence requirements, filing clock, and fee trigger. The first six can share invoice normalization, while WOTC needs restricted employee records and credit transactions need project-specific tax documentation. Public utility tariff libraries reveal rider changes; municipal sewer ordinances establish whether a qualifying deduction exists; county records provide assessment history rather than an automatic appeal valuation. For an illustrative utility screen, 400,000 billed kWh multiplied by a suspected $0.012 rate difference produces a $4,800 investigation lead. A verified, issued $4,800 refund at a 35% contracted share produces $1,680 of operator revenue. If demand charges or rider eligibility eliminate the difference, close the lead without booking revenue. Store that rejected hypothesis so the next analyst does not rediscover it. Treat vacancy, ownership changes, and service addresses as routing clues, never substitutes for measured usage or the governing account contract.

Energy-project origination requires a correction to a common sourcing shortcut: DOE and EPA are not universal interconnection-queue operators. Berkeley Lab's Queued Up compilation supplies periodically published market context, while the relevant ISO, RTO, or utility maintains the underlying queue. EPA ECHO supplies facility and environmental records, not proof of transferable credit eligibility. FERC eLibrary provides relevant filings, but a filing does not establish placed-in-service status or a usable tax basis. Join projects using queue identifiers, legal entities, locations, and documented ownership chains rather than project names alone. State film offices and historic-preservation records support different statutes and different transfer mechanics; never put them through an IRC § 6418 calculator merely because both involve credits. An illustrative 100-project batch with 20 documented operating projects and five sellers willing to share records yields five diligence files, not five saleable credits. If the originating registry stops publishing or prohibits automated extraction, use permitted downloads and direct document requests. Cache unchanged files, honor published access conditions, and record manual collection dates. This makes public-record research reproducible without pretending that a stale directory constitutes transaction-ready inventory. Preserve rejected entity matches alongside accepted ones to expose mistaken joins during review.

## 2. Connect authorized billing rails without inventing refund APIs  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Carrier developer accounts are not blanket access to billing adjustments. FedEx and UPS expose documented services, but available refund, invoice, and claim functions depend on product, account, geography, entitlement, and current contractual terms. Read the current documentation with the customer's authorized account administrator; record supported endpoints and explicitly mark unsupported workflows as manual tasks. Do not assume that a loss-and-damage claims API also processes service-guarantee requests. For account-authorized EDI, request the carrier's implementation guide and validate its supported transaction format; X12 210 is relevant where offered, not a universal parcel billing feed. A desk examining 10,000 shipments might flag 300 late events, then retain only 120 after service commitments, exclusions, and account waivers are applied. At $18 average eligible charges, the resulting $2,160 recovery opportunity yields $864 at a 40% share only when the contractual earning condition is satisfied. Preserve shipment identifiers, promised and actual delivery timestamps, time zones, service levels, and waiver evidence. If a service endpoint fails, switch to authorized portal exports and queue submission tasks against the actual filing deadline. Tracking events support investigation; they do not independently prove a refundable billed amount. Never resubmit a request merely because the first response timed out.

Enrichment should follow a cheap-to-expensive waterfall. Begin with the customer's account roster, then public parcel and entity records, then verified billing contacts, and only then a licensed commercial contact provider when its terms permit the use. Retain the provider, retrieval date, matching basis, and deletion requirements; do not enrich WOTC applicant records with unrelated marketing attributes. For utilities, Green Button interval data can reveal load patterns, while ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager provides benchmarking context. Neither replaces the billed tariff, meter multiplier, demand ratchet, taxes, or approved sewer deduction. An illustrative 2,000,000 gallons of documented cooling-tower evaporation at a qualifying $7 per thousand gallons suggests $14,000 of avoided sewer charges. Meter approval dates and local rules may reduce that amount to zero for earlier periods. At a 30% earned share, $14,000 of accepted benefit supports $4,200 of operator revenue. When interval feeds lag, reconcile monthly bills and postpone interval-based conclusions rather than filling gaps with invented consumption. Keep provider processing lag separate from meter-read dates so analysts can distinguish delayed delivery from genuinely missing measurements. Use customer-approved engineering records when portal data cannot resolve the physical system boundary.

WOTC connectors need a different trust boundary from expense auditing. Under IRC § 51(d)(13), the certification workflow includes a pre-screening notice completed on or before the employment offer and submission to the designated local agency within 28 days after work begins. Form 8850 and the applicable ETA documentation must follow current instructions; an ATS timestamp alone does not establish eligibility. The last established federal authorization covered qualifying hires through December 31, 2025, so verify any subsequent extension before offering credits for later hires. An illustrative standard eligible employee with $6,000 of qualifying wages and at least 400 hours produces a $2,400 credit at 40%, subject to category-specific rules and other restrictions. A contracted $120 processing charge is desk revenue; the $2,400 employer credit is not. Missing timely pre-screening or certification submission cannot be repaired by altering timestamps. Retain the documented exception and have the responsible specialist determine available treatment. Scope ATS permissions to required fields, restrict sensitive responses, and use state portal acknowledgments as submission evidence. Review fee structure under Treasury Circular 230 § 10.27 wherever covered tax services are involved rather than copying a utility contingency agreement.

## 3. Turn raw records into a reproducible claim ledger  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the pipeline around immutable evidence and explicit transformations. Store original PDFs, CSV files, EDI payloads, and API responses separately from normalized tables. A practical small-desk implementation uses an encrypted object store, PostgreSQL for multiuser records, and SQLite for isolated analyst workbooks; none is a substitute for access control or tested backups. Python's csv, decimal, hashlib, json, sqlite3, and zoneinfo modules cover essential parsing, money calculations, hashing, and timestamps. Use Decimal values with explicit currency and rounding rules rather than binary floating-point amounts. For image-only invoices, Tesseract and OCRmyPDF are optional external tools, while pdftotext extracts existing text layers without reinterpreting images. Record tool versions and page coordinates for every extracted amount. Require invoice totals to reconcile to line items, taxes, adjustments, and credits before generating claim candidates. An illustrative batch of 1,000 invoices with 40 extraction exceptions needs a forty-item review queue, not silent deletion of those records. When OCR confuses a meter identifier or decimal separator, retain the original image and request human confirmation. Only confirmed fields should enter a recoverable-amount calculation; confidence scores prioritize review but do not authorize claims. Preserve each correction as a versioned event with its reviewer and rationale.

Create four linked ledgers: source evidence, claim candidates, submissions, and cash realization. The evidence ledger holds immutable file hashes and provenance; candidates hold the rule version and proposed adjustment; submissions hold the actual external acknowledgment; cash realization holds remittance references and the fee allocation. Use a business key such as account, invoice, charge line, reason code, and service period to prevent duplicate recovery attempts. A network timeout changes a submission to reconciliation required, not automatically to failed. Check the carrier or agency record before retrying. Model status transitions explicitly so an analyst cannot jump from suspected overcharge to earned fee. For shared savings, compare the accepted counterfactual with actual normalized consumption, approved rates, and contract exclusions. A $500 monthly verified reduction over 36 months creates $18,000 of client benefit and $5,400 at a 30% share, but billing and recognition follow the contract and applicable accounting treatment rather than booking the entire benefit immediately. If operations change, pause the affected savings calculation and rebuild the baseline. Keep client acknowledgments, rate-change dates, and measurement assumptions alongside the calculation, because those records resolve most fee disputes more effectively than a polished dashboard.

Diagnostics should expose economic failure before throughput rises. Track source freshness, rejected joins, invoice reconciliation errors, deadline coverage, submission duplicates, denial reasons, cash aging, and collected fees per reviewer hour. Split each metric by play and counterparty; a utility refund backlog should not hide behind fast parcel credits. An illustrative week with $12,000 of proposed refunds, $7,000 accepted, and $4,000 issued produces distinct proposed, accepted, and cash figures. At a 35% fee payable on issued refunds, the immediately billable amount is $1,400, subject to the agreement. Report the remaining amounts as pipeline states, not revenue. Restrict payroll and WOTC documents to their own role group, encrypt credentials outside workbooks, redact sensitive identifiers from logs, and test restoration from backup. Retention rules should distinguish statutory substantiation, contractual evidence, and short-lived contact enrichment. A deletion request must not silently erase records subject to an applicable retention obligation; route the conflict to the responsible records owner. If a connector loses authority, stop collection and preserve existing evidence according to the governing terms. Favor a manually verified ten-claim batch over an unattended thousand-claim batch with unresolved identity errors.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Authorize and freeze the baseline
   - Collect signed authority, account identifiers, governing agreements, and source-period invoices.
   - Hash the originals, record the contractual fee trigger, and capture applicable filing clocks before analysis begins.
2. Normalize and test the evidence
   - Extract charge lines and join only verified account, service, meter, project, or employee identifiers.
   - Reconcile totals, preserve effective dates, review extraction exceptions, and attach the rule version to every candidate.
3. Submit through the supported route
   - Use the entitled API, approved portal, or documented manual submission process.
   - Check duplicate keys and deadlines first; save the external acknowledgment and reconcile ambiguous responses before retrying.
4. Reconcile benefit and earned fees
   - Match issued refunds or verified savings to the agreement and invoice only the earned operator amount.
   - Separate pipeline value, client benefit, recognized fees, and collected cash; feed denial reasons back into the rule library.

## 4. Structure credit origination correctly and preserve scorecard economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The mapped play transferable_tax_credit_brokerage_syndication belongs to TRACK_1 and needs separate transaction logic. For eligible federal credits under IRC § 6418, a transferee cannot subsequently transfer the purchased credit; a broker therefore cannot buy title at $0.85 and resell that same credit at $0.94. Structure a direct eligible-taxpayer-to-buyer transfer with separately documented intermediary compensation, or decline the proposed structure. State film, historic rehabilitation, and brownfield programs require their own statutory eligibility and transfer analysis. At $1,000,000 face value, the proposed seller pricing range of $0.82–$0.88 implies $820,000–$880,000, while buyer pricing of $0.92–$0.96 implies $920,000–$960,000. The arithmetic difference ranges from $40,000 to $140,000 before transaction costs, but it is not automatically an available or lawful brokerage fee. A $0.85 seller reference and $0.94 buyer reference imply $90,000 of gross economic room; illustrative $20,000 insurance, $15,000 diligence, and $10,000 escrow and other costs leave $45,000 before remaining expenses. Have specialists establish which payments constitute transfer consideration, service compensation, or buyer costs, and allocate every dollar explicitly. Never describe a spread calculation as a completed transaction or recognized revenue before contracts and tax treatment support it.

Source-credit verification starts with the eligible taxpayer, project ownership, credit type, tax year, calculation workpapers, placed-in-service evidence, and applicable labor or bonus-credit substantiation. Federal pre-filing registration through IRS Energy Credits Online supplies registration numbers; it does not certify the substantive credit amount. Verify the buyer's capacity to use the credit, including limitations relevant to individuals; a high-net-worth prospect is not automatically an eligible economic buyer for every project. The stipulated fallback is no unhedged balance-sheet inventory and simultaneous back-to-back escrow funded by the buyer's wire, with bound non-cancelable tax-credit insurance. For federal transfers, that describes coordinated funding and documents, not successive credit ownership. Require the insurer's actual cancellation terms, covered risks, exclusions, limits, retention, and claims process; the label alone establishes nothing. If the required coverage or simultaneous structure cannot be secured, do not close. Insurance does not cure an ineligible credit or erase statutory recapture and excessive-transfer exposure. Model seller indemnity collectibility separately from insured recovery. Keep escrow release instructions conditional on executed documents, verified funding, and the agreed evidence package, while leaving return elections and filing requirements with the responsible taxpayers and their authorized advisers.
