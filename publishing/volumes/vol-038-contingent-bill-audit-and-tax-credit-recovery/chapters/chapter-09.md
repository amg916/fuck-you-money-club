## 1. Assign Each Recovery Lane Its Own Authority and Fee Rule  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build twelve operating lanes before quoting one blended contingency: FedEx parcel refunds, UPS parcel refunds, utility tariff corrections, utility sales-tax refunds, cooling-tower sewer credits, waste invoice recoveries, telecom billing disputes, property assessment appeals, WOTC processing, specialty federal credit substantiation, 36-month shared-savings administration, and state transferable-credit placement. These are the chapter's twelve mapped plays, not twelve interchangeable permissions. Commercial invoice analysis does not authorize tax representation, a property appeal, or credential sharing. Create a jurisdiction register recording the client entity, service address, taxing authority, carrier account, claim period, limitation date, permitted representative, and applicable fee restriction. For FedEx and UPS, obtain signed Letters of Authorization and baseline invoice snapshots before disclosing shipment-level findings. Inspect the customer's negotiated agreement for service-guarantee waivers, claim exclusions, and duplicate-recovery provisions. A documented late shipment is an investigative lead, not automatically an eligible refund. For utilities, distinguish tariff misapplication from an engineering retrofit and separate historical refunds from prospective rate changes. Waste and telecom claims turn on service orders, renewal terms, usage records, taxes, and dispute deadlines. Freeze the original source documents with collection dates and hashes so another reviewer can reproduce the conclusion after the supplier changes its portal or invoice format.

Treasury Circular 230, 31 CFR §10.27, restricts practitioner contingent fees for covered matters before the IRS, subject to defined exceptions and relevant judicial limits. Do not translate a carrier's thirty-percent recovery arrangement into a percentage of a federal credit merely because both engagements produce cash. Section 10.27 distinguishes ordinary return work from specified examination, interest-or-penalty refund, and judicial proceeding circumstances; court decisions also limit its application in certain settings. Have the responsible tax practitioner classify the actual service and document the permissible pricing basis rather than asserting a universal percentage-fee exemption. Use a separate fixed-fee or hourly engagement when the proposed contingency is restricted. Under 31 CFR §10.22, covered practitioners must exercise due diligence; attaching software output does not discharge that responsibility. WOTC introduces a different clock: 26 U.S.C. §51(d)(13) requires timely prescreening and certification procedures, including submission within the statutory twenty-eight-day period when using that route. The provision in §51(c)(4) sets a commencement-of-work cutoff of December 31, 2025 unless subsequently amended. For later starts, verify enacted extension language before representing eligibility, while continuing properly supported work on earlier qualifying starts. Never backdate prescreening records to repair a missed deadline.

Property-tax engagements need a state-by-state credential map, not a national consultant label. Texas Occupations Code Chapter 1152 regulates property-tax consultants and provides specific exemptions; determine whether the actual person performing the work must register and whether supervision requirements apply. California Revenue and Taxation Code §1607 addresses written authorization for agents appearing in assessment proceedings. Neither example establishes permission for every state, tribunal, or court appeal. Record who may inspect records, sign the application, negotiate, appear, and litigate; retain local counsel where the next procedural step requires an attorney. For utility and sewer claims, retrieve the applicable commission tariff or municipal ordinance, including meter certification, retroactivity, and refund-interest rules. A cooling-tower deduction may require an approved deduct meter rather than an estimated evaporation spreadsheet. Utility sales-tax exemptions belong in a separate state tax workstream because certificate requirements and refund claimants differ. Carrier API access must follow the account owner's authorization and the provider's developer agreement; an EDI invoice feed does not establish claim-submission rights. When authority is incomplete, keep the engagement in read-only analysis, identify the missing credential or document, and pause submission without allowing a filing deadline to disappear from the task register.

> - Reconcile the proposed claim to immutable invoice snapshots, remove duplicate submissions, and confirm the client approved the factual calculation.

## 2. Draft Separate Schedules for Scope, Earnings, Data, and Exit  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a master services agreement with lane-specific schedules rather than a single promise to recover every overpayment. The scope schedule names covered entities, accounts, periods, taxes, suppliers, claim types, and exclusions; its attachment freezes the baseline invoice inventory before findings are disclosed. The authorization schedule permits only specified acts, such as retrieving invoices and submitting supported billing disputes, and separately addresses settlement authority. It does not give the operator ownership of client refunds or power to change banking instructions. Define a recovery as cleared cash or an actually applied account credit, net of reversals and agreed offsets, rather than an issued claim number. At a thirty-percent fee, a $20,000 accepted claim that yields $18,000 cash and a $2,000 rejection earns $5,400, not $6,000. Specify invoicing dates, payment terms, credit verification, reversal adjustments, and collection responsibility. Separate any fixed WOTC processing charge from commercial refund shares and from restricted federal tax services. Have the client pay each supplier normally while disputes proceed unless the supplier expressly authorizes another arrangement. An auditor-created payment shortfall can trigger service interruption, late charges, or an avoidable dispute over the operator's own fee.

For 36-month shared savings, attach a measurement formula that neutralizes usage, weather, occupancy, service level, and tariff movements where relevant. A $5,000 monthly baseline reduced to $4,000 at equivalent activity supports $1,000 verified monthly savings; a thirty-percent split produces $300 per month and $10,800 over thirty-six fully realized months. Those future installments are not automatically current revenue. Define the commencement date, comparison period, verification source, treatment of facility closures, and whether negotiated extensions require a fresh signature. Prevent double billing when a historical refund and a future rate correction address the same charge. Anti-circumvention language should protect identified findings and documented introductions for a defined period, with exclusions for preexisting projects and independently documented discoveries. It should not claim every future improvement across the client's business. Require a dated findings register and client acknowledgment rather than relying on a broad confidentiality sentence. A termination schedule distinguishes earned fees, pending authorized claims, prospective work, and a narrowly defined collection tail. State who completes deadline-sensitive filings after termination, whether the client may revoke portal access immediately, and how both parties exchange the evidence needed to avoid duplicate claims or abandoned appeals.

Negotiate liability, dispute resolution, and information rights as operating mechanics, not decorative boilerplate. A liability cap can reference a defined amount or specified fee period, with negotiated treatment of fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality breaches, and other appropriate exceptions; enforceability depends on governing law and the actual claim. Specify required insurance, notice procedures, mitigation duties, and responsibility for penalties caused by inaccurate client records versus operator errors. The dispute clause identifies governing law, venue or arbitration rules, escalation contacts, and treatment of undisputed invoices. It cannot extend a statutory appeal deadline or prevent a regulator from exercising authority. Give the client ownership of its invoices, payroll records, claim packets, and engagement-specific deliverables, while reserving the operator's preexisting methods and reusable software. Permit use of confidential records only for contracted purposes and identify approved subprocessors. Establish access controls, incident notification timing, export formats, retention periods, and deletion obligations subject to applicable preservation duties. IRS Form 2848 and Form 8821 serve different purposes; information access is not equivalent to representation authority. Tax-return information may implicate 26 U.S.C. §7216 and implementing regulations when handled by covered preparers, so data reuse and disclosures require a separate, fact-specific review.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Protect Attribution Before the Client Can Implement the Finding**
> 
> Experienced recovery operators preserve the commercial chain from source record to accepted finding to realized benefit.
> - Capture negotiated FedEx and UPS guarantee provisions before counting eligible shipments; a clean lateness report cannot overcome a contractual waiver.
> - Record each disclosed tariff correction in a dated findings register linked to the signed scope, so fee attribution survives a supplier's direct adjustment.
> - Request utility credit-ledger exports as well as refund checks; an applied credit can satisfy the agreed fee trigger without a bank deposit.
> - Give credit purchasers a certificate-level diligence index and settlement checklist, but withhold unnecessary taxpayer identifiers from exploratory buyer outreach.

## 3. Settle Transferable Credits Without Misstating Value or Revenue  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The play state_film_historic_brownfield_tax_credit_brokerage, TRACK_1, matches certified state film, historic rehabilitation, and brownfield credits with eligible taxpayers at negotiated discounts while retaining disclosed placement fees. Transferability belongs to the particular state program and credit vintage; a label resembling a transferable program elsewhere proves nothing. Federal historic rehabilitation credits under 26 U.S.C. §47 cannot simply be sold outright because a state historic credit permits assignment. Establish statutory transfer permission, seller ownership, certification status, permitted transfer count, transfer registration, buyer eligibility, tax type, carryforward, expiration, and recapture exposure before circulating a priced opportunity. Georgia's film-credit framework in O.C.G.A. §48-7-40.26 illustrates why the actual state provision matters, but its mechanics must not be copied onto another state's historic or brownfield program. Distinguish an application from an allocation, an allocation from a final certificate, and a certificate from a completed transfer. Build the buyer list around verified tax usability, not merely corporate size. Obtain qualified state tax review and examine whether the proposed structure introduces additional licensing obligations, especially when someone proposes selling an entity interest instead of assigning a credit. A placement operator earns for documented intermediation, not for issuing a tax opinion or promising the purchaser's ultimate result.

Use transparent settlement arithmetic. In an illustrative placement, $100,000 of certified state credit face value sells for $90,000, and the seller pays a disclosed three-percent fee on purchase price. The operator earns $2,700; the seller receives $87,300 before other costs; the purchaser pays $90,000 before its own costs. The $100,000 face amount and $90,000 settlement proceeds are not operator revenue. A nominal $10,000 purchaser discount is not established realized tax savings until eligibility, usability, timing, and costs are resolved. State clearly whether the fee is seller-paid, buyer-paid, or split, and disclose any compensation from both sides before engagement. Use an independent settlement provider where appropriate, confirm bank instructions through a known contact, and release funds only against the agreed transfer evidence. The purchase agreement allocates title warranties, recapture responsibility, indemnity limits, survival periods, and dispute procedures without treating contractual promises as substitutes for statutory eligibility. If certification is defective, buyer usability fails, or transfer registration stalls, suspend settlement under the diligence contingencies. Rematch only verified eligible credits without guaranteeing tax savings. Keep a failed placement out of earned revenue and return funds according to the documented settlement instructions rather than improvising custody arrangements.

Maintain separate operating ledgers for refund shares, shared-savings installments, processing fees, and placement fees, then reconcile their earned totals to the scorecard. Formula v2.0 fixes Conservative revenue at $110,000 with $77,000 expenses and $33,000 owner net profit; Base revenue at $505,000 with $287,850 expenses and $217,150 profit; and Scaled revenue at $900,000 with $396,000 expenses and $504,000 profit. The corresponding margins are thirty percent, 43.0 percent, and fifty-six percent. Staffing remains one full-time operator plus three contractors for Conservative and Base, then two full-time operators plus four contractors for Scaled. These are canonical annual cases, not promises that a particular contract mix will achieve them. The placement example explains a fee unit without adding revenue to those totals. Under an ASC 606 principal-versus-agent analysis, evaluate the promised service and control before recognizing gross amounts; customer refunds and credit purchase proceeds never become operator sales merely by passing through an account. If settlement documents show gross billings, bridge them explicitly to the operator's retained share and assess variable-consideration constraints. Age municipal refund receivables separately from earned fees, because slow issuance affects collection timing and working capital even when the underlying claim is valid.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Classify the credit
   - Identify the state statute, program, vintage, seller, and final certification status.
   - Record assignment permission, transfer limits, registration requirements, recapture rules, and the responsible state tax reviewer.
2. Verify the purchaser
   - Confirm the purchaser can use the particular credit against the relevant state tax.
   - Document tax type, utilization limits, expiration, carryforward, and any restrictions tied to purchaser identity or transaction structure.
3. Document settlement
   - Execute price, fee, transfer, diligence, warranty, and funds-release terms.
   - Show the operator's disclosed fee separately from face value and purchase price; independently verify payment instructions.
4. Close or suspend
   - Release settlement only after the agreed transfer evidence is complete.
   - If a condition fails, suspend under the contract and rematch only verified eligible credits; recognize fees only when earned.

## 4. Use Verbatim Outreach That Sells Evidence Before Outcomes  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Commercial outreach must identify the sender, describe the actual service, and avoid invented findings. Under CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. §7704, business email is not categorically exempt: use accurate routing information and subjects, required commercial identification, a valid physical postal address, and an effective opt-out honored within ten business days. Keep the suppression list operative across contractors. Use this opening verbatim after supplying the sender's normal identifying signature: 'Subject: Review of parcel and utility billing terms. We audit corporate parcel and utility invoices for documented billing errors and eligible contractual adjustments. We have not reviewed your accounts and cannot state that money is owed. For eligible commercial recovery work, our fee is an agreed share of realized recoveries. Would you consider a fifteen-minute scope call before sharing any records? This is a commercial solicitation. Reply stop to end these emails.' Append the firm's real postal address before sending. For calls and texts, classify the recipient number, technology, message purpose, consent evidence, and applicable state restrictions under the TCPA and FCC rules. Prefer individually initiated business calls with accurate identification, but do not assume that method removes every restriction. Honor a recipient's stop request immediately across the outreach sequence.

Use discovery to establish authority and constraints before discussing a recovery estimate: 'Which legal entity owns these accounts, who may authorize records access, and are another auditor or existing refund service already working on them? Can we review the carrier amendments and baseline invoices after signing the scope and authorization? We will identify exclusions before calculating any fee opportunity.' For utilities, add: 'Which meters serve production, irrigation, and cooling equipment, and has the municipality approved any sewer deduction? We need the operative tariff and meter evidence before asserting an overcharge.' For tax work, say: 'Our commercial refund percentage does not automatically apply to federal tax services. The responsible practitioner will define the service, fee basis, and filing authority before work begins.' For WOTC, ask: 'What are the employment start dates, when was prescreening completed, and which state workforce agency received each request? We verify the applicable statutory employment window before offering processing.' Do not solicit full Social Security numbers through email or a sales form. Move sensitive documentation into the approved restricted workspace after authorization. Record the prospect's answers as unverified statements until documents substantiate them; a salesperson's transcript must not silently become an eligibility determination.

For state credit placement, use: 'We introduce verified eligible state credits to purchasers whose tax position supports their use. Availability, transferability, price, and usability depend on the program and diligence. Our disclosed placement fee is separate from credit face value. We can exchange a redacted certificate summary before either party commits to settlement.' If asked for certainty, answer: 'We can commit to the documented review and settlement process, not to a tax outcome. If a diligence condition fails, settlement pauses and we consider only verified eligible alternatives.' For an account-access objection, say: 'We do not need your personal password. We can begin with authorized exports and request provider-supported access only within the signed scope.' Close discovery with a specific next step: 'Please designate the account owner, confirm existing auditor commitments, and review the proposed authorization and fee schedule. We will return a scoped evidence request before disclosing account-level findings.' Track outreach source, suppression status, authorization, qualification, document completeness, and submission readiness separately. Review a sample of sent messages weekly for unsupported savings claims, misleading government affiliation, and missing opt-outs. Measure signed qualified engagements and collected earned fees rather than inflating performance with client bill totals, credit face values, or unsubmitted recovery estimates.
