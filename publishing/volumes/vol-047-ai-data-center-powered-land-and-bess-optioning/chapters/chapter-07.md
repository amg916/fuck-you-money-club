## 1. Originate an evidence-backed interest before pricing a megawatt  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start every parcel with a county parcel identifier, ownership extract, geometry checksum, seller contact authority, and separate load or generation intent. QGIS can intersect county parcel polygons with FEMA flood layers, USFWS wetland inventories, utility territory boundaries, and published 69kV–345kV infrastructure. Those intersections rank research effort; they do not establish available capacity. Query the serving utility for the proposed service configuration, requested demand, study pathway, upgrade allocation, required deposits, and energization schedule. Record the document date and its limitations alongside every MW field. A generation queue position does not reserve large-load service, and a battery charging requirement cannot be assumed satisfied by an export study. FERC interconnection requirements implemented through 18 CFR § 35.28 operate through applicable tariffs and procedures; identify the actual provider and jurisdiction before adopting a deadline. An attractive parcel remains research inventory until the utility supplies a sufficiently specific written basis for valuation. Underwrite twenty documented MW differently from twenty requested MW, even when both appear in the same developer presentation. If the utility will not bound the exposure, offer a diligence extension rather than increasing the land-option payment. Keep the unsupported capacity claim out of every buyer-facing summary.

Split the product at intake. A standalone BESS parcel commonly occupies 5–25 acres, but usable area must survive setbacks, access requirements, drainage, equipment spacing, emergency response access, and the actual interconnection design. Track charge capacity, discharge capacity, duration, and operating restrictions separately. A campus requires a different record: demand ramp, fiber route diversity, water strategy, cooling assumptions, building envelope, backup generation, and local use classification. Two fiber providers sharing one conduit do not create physical diversity. Secure carrier route evidence and utility service assumptions rather than inferring connectivity from a road crossing. For a twenty-MW campus, an illustrative $60,000 assignment equals $3,000 per documented MW; a $4 million upgrade estimate implies another $200,000 per MW of project exposure, not operator income. Test whether the buyer still accepts the site after the upgrade and schedule sensitivity. A twenty-five-MW battery assignment at $40,000 equals $1,600 per documented MW and belongs in its own comparison set. Neither multiple values land ownership, installed equipment, or speculative future power. If water or zoning fails for the campus, evaluate a separate battery concept only after confirming that its utility pathway and permitted use remain independently viable.

Control the interest with a 12–24 month diligence-contingent option whose assignment language, extension price, access rights, termination mechanics, and recording treatment fit the state and transaction. Have local counsel resolve mineral reservations, severed surface rights, lender restrictions, title exceptions, and any memorandum recording requirements before paying substantial consideration. Trade an actual principal interest or use an appropriately licensed brokerage relationship; describing a fee as an assignment does not settle the state licensing analysis. Require dated municipal confirmation of use classification and current moratorium status. A planning discussion is not an issued permit. Wetland screening also remains preliminary: potential discharge of dredged or fill material requires analysis under 33 U.S.C. § 1344, while flood development requirements depend on the applicable local program and site work. Budget failed options explicitly. Six expired interests costing $2,000 each consume $12,000 even if the two successful interests close profitably. The operating owner should authorize each irreversible payment against a dated evidence packet, a remaining research budget, and a termination calendar. Assign title, civil, and utility work to three scoped contractors instead of turning every preliminary lead into an open-ended engineering engagement.

## 2. Install six transactional modules and make replay a harmless event  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The six reference modules below form one deliberately bounded SQLite implementation. Save the named files together in an operator-controlled project, run ledger_core.py once, and import the remaining functions from that directory. They are reference artifacts embedded in this chapter, not imports required by this volume module. Use Python 3.9 or later and a local filesystem; do not put the database on a shared network drive. Every writer opens its own connection, enables foreign keys, and enters BEGIN IMMEDIATE before reading data that controls a write. SQLite therefore serializes competing write decisions. Busy timeouts can fail an operation without partially committing it; retry the original key and identical payload after investigating persistent contention. UNIQUE constraints protect parcel identities, event keys, journal references, and statement matches. A replay with changed content raises an error instead of silently substituting new instructions. The schema stores integer cents and balanced two-account entries, avoiding binary floating-point currency. Preserve the database, document archive, and application version together in encrypted backups, and exercise restoration before admitting real closings. Filesystem access remains restricted to the service account because database constraints do not replace control over who can open or replace the file.

Scoring is intentionally a qualification test rather than an automated valuation. The intake script requires bounded upgrade exposure, a utility reference, a positive documented MW figure, and a usable schedule before labeling a parcel screened. It checks battery acreage independently from campus fiber and water evidence. The verification module then requires current human attestations linked to document hashes; no software routine pretends that a hash proves engineering correctness. Its state progression separates screening, verification, escrow readiness, and executed closing. Terminal scrubs and expirations remain visible. Every advancing event carries an expected version, so a stale browser tab cannot overwrite a later decision. An event and its notification outbox row commit together. The dispatcher holds the write lock while submitting one notification to a receiver that must persistently honor the event key. If the receiver accepts the request and the local process crashes, the same key can be submitted again without duplicate external effect. This is a receiver contract, not a claim that SQLite alone creates distributed exactly-once delivery. Never connect that notification endpoint directly to a wire-transfer action. Escrow professionals authenticate payment instructions outside this reference, including independent callbacks for changed beneficiary details.

Journal events distinguish earning a fee from collecting it. An accepted $46,250 performance event debits receivable and credits fee revenue; a $36,250 receipt debits cash and credits receivable, leaving $10,000 outstanding. An option payment debits prepaid option and credits cash until the adopted accounting policy requires expense recognition or another treatment. The cohort illustrates expense upon expiration for the failed interests. Record owner funding against capital rather than revenue, and never post a buyer's land purchase or battery construction budget as an origination fee. The reconciliation view reports earned revenue, expense, operating profit, and cash movement separately. Statement imports match one actual cash transaction to one cash journal event; bundled net settlements require a separate clearing design before use, not invented gross bank lines. QA runs within one consistent locked snapshot and requires every recorded cash movement to have a matching statement line at period close. External statement completeness still requires opening balance, closing balance, and source-file checks. The reference prevents competing writers from winning the same transition; it does not remove utility uncertainty, document fraud, contract interpretation, or the need to review an unexpected accounting balance.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Initialize and exercise replay behavior
   - Save the six reference files together and initialize a new local database.
   - Submit identical intake twice, then alter the payload under the same identifier; require one site and an explicit changed-content error.
2. Prove competing transitions cannot both succeed
   - Open separate connections and submit two different transitions against the same site version.
   - Require one committed version advance and one stale-version rejection; a retry of the winning event must return its original result.
3. Exercise the external crash boundary
   - Use a test receiver with durable key storage and interrupt dispatch after acceptance.
   - Restart dispatch with the same event key and verify one receiver-side notification despite the repeated request.
4. Close the period against complete statements
   - Post earning, receipt, option expiration, expense, and capital events before matching statement lines.

## 3. Walk ninety days from thirty first-month leads to reconciled Base cash  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The Worked 90-Day Cohort Walk uses three monthly intake cohorts and two completed assignments from the first cohort. It is an illustrative operating schedule, not a prediction that every new business closes within ninety days. The stated two-to-six-month first-deal range still applies. Begin with $50,000 owner capital, the upper end of the $10,000–$50,000 startup range. Month 1 admits thirty research leads: eighteen fail the desktop screen, twelve receive deeper diligence, and seven of those twelve eventually fail utility or zoning work. Four failures are utility-related and three are zoning-related. Three surviving prospects are parked without further paid commitments; two advance to closing. Six failed interests each consume $2,000 of option consideration, while one additional failed prospect is rejected before payment. Month 2 admits twenty-four new leads, rejects sixteen, and leaves eight in evidence collection. Month 3 admits twenty-four, rejects eighteen, and leaves six awaiting deeper qualification. These later prospects produce no revenue or additional paid option exposure inside the window. Across seventy-eight leads, fifty-nine are scrubbed, three are parked, two close, and fourteen remain active. The count reconciliation prevents a presentation from silently recycling failed parcels as fresh qualified inventory.

Site A is a ten-acre BESS interest with twenty-five MW supported by the relevant utility documentation and its stated conditions. Month 2 earns a $40,000 assignment and realizes $6,250 of contracted development carry. The latter is settled consideration, not an appraisal of future upside. Of the $46,250 earned, $36,250 arrives during Month 2 and $10,000 remains receivable. Site B is an eighty-acre campus interest supporting a documented twenty-MW service plan with separately accepted upgrade and schedule exposure. Month 3 earns its $60,000 assignment plus four distinct $5,000 origination milestones.
