## 1. Establish the parcel, power, and ownership evidence hierarchy  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a parcel evidence register, not a statewide heat map. Give each candidate a county identifier, assessor parcel number, normalized geometry, recorded owner, instrument reference, source date, retrieval date, and permitted-use note. Keep assessor ownership separate from recorder evidence because tax rolls can lag conveyances and frequently omit mineral reservations, access limitations, or option-related instruments. Query the county assessor's GIS REST service where published; otherwise download its released parcel file or request the current extract. Retrieve deeds, easements, plats, and relevant memoranda through the recorder's index, then order title work before paying meaningful option consideration. Regrid accelerates cross-county discovery but does not replace the county record or a title commitment. Deduplicate by county plus parcel identifier, preserving split and merger history rather than joining on an address alone. An illustrative screen of 1,000 parcels might yield 100 accessible sites and 20 title-review candidates; those are workflow counts, not conversion promises. Track each rejection reason so the next acquisition batch excludes the same access defect. A cheap parcel beside expensive infrastructure is still a poor option when the seller cannot convey the required surface rights or authorize a transferable diligence package.

Build the power layer from three distinct evidence classes: physical infrastructure, published interconnection activity, and site-specific utility correspondence. HIFLD transmission geometry can help identify 69kV–345kV corridors, while ISO queue exports show proposed generation or storage projects and their published status. Neither establishes spare import capacity for an AI campus. A distribution hosting-capacity map also cannot substitute for a transmission-connected large-load study. Record load import, BESS charging import, and BESS export separately; a project may require different studies, tariffs, and operating restrictions for each direction. Request a dated utility response identifying the contemplated point of connection, deliverable MW assumptions, required studies, upgrade responsibility, and earliest credible service sequence. Store whether the response is preliminary, conditional, or backed by an executed agreement. FERC eLibrary helps trace tariff proceedings and relevant filings, but critical energy infrastructure information is restricted under 18 CFR 388.113. Use public material and authorized customer channels rather than attempting to reconstruct restricted network detail. If the utility cannot substantiate capacity, retain the parcel as an unvalidated lead with zero power-backed valuation uplift. Physical proximity earns a question for an engineer, never a marketable capacity claim.

Use the directory's first six entries as the authoritative backbone and record source-specific limitations alongside every download. Public access commonly costs nothing, but county image fees, certified copies, commercial licenses, and specialist review still consume the acquisition budget. Distinguish publisher refresh latency from desk polling cadence: a nightly download cannot make an annually maintained parcel layer current. Configure conditional HTTP requests when supported, identify the client where requested, honor Retry-After, and cap concurrency to the published service terms. Where no numerical request limit is published, start with serial requests and a conservative delay rather than inventing an allowance. Save the source URL, response status, content hash, publication timestamp, and transformation version. For JavaScript portals without a documented export, prefer their download function or a records request over brittle automated browsing. A failed refresh must mark the dataset stale without deleting the last verified snapshot. Escalate changes in ownership, connection status, and queue withdrawal to a human because those events can invalidate a signed option package. The desk's advantage is an auditable chain from a sales statement to its underlying record, including the date and scope of the evidence.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Four ways experienced originators prevent expensive false positives**
> 
> Spend automation on finding contradictions before spending option money on defending a favorite parcel.
> - Store both the substation's public label and the utility-confirmed connection reference; similarly named facilities can belong to different service territories.
> - Ask separately about firm charging import and export restrictions for BESS; an attractive export case can conceal an unusable charging arrangement.

> - Compare queue snapshots by stable project identifier, not spreadsheet row position; sorting changes otherwise create fictitious withdrawals and additions.

## 2. Screen buildable land before purchasing commercial enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run the environmental waterfall before buying contact enrichment or commissioning a developer-facing valuation. Intersect the parcel with FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service National Wetlands Inventory, NRCS soils, USGS elevation, and local zoning. Preserve each intersection as an observation, not a permitting conclusion. FEMA layers have geographic and publication gaps; absence from the downloadable layer does not establish negligible flood exposure. Wetlands Inventory polygons are screening evidence rather than a jurisdictional determination under Clean Water Act section 404, 33 U.S.C. 1344. A qualified field delineation and applicable agency process resolve the development question. Estimate contiguous buildable acreage after setbacks, access, drainage, slopes, and known constraints, avoiding double subtraction where exclusion polygons overlap. For an illustrative 20-acre BESS parcel with six acres of overlapping exclusions and two additional acres reserved for access and stormwater, begin layout work on 12 acres, not 20. Do not convert that acreage directly into MW without equipment spacing, fire access, noise, and utility design inputs. Escalate ambiguous boundaries to a surveyor or environmental specialist before representing the parcel as construction-ready to any potential assignee.

Separate standalone BESS screening from AI-campus screening at the schema level. A 5–25 acre BESS option may depend primarily on electrical configuration, fire access, setbacks, drainage, and dispatch economics; a campus also needs a credible cooling strategy, water or alternative cooling resources, fiber routes, large-load service, and a zoning pathway that accommodates its specific use. The FCC National Broadband Map describes reported availability and supporting datasets, not verified dark-fiber strands or physically diverse enterprise routes. Use it only to identify providers for follow-up. Obtain written carrier responses identifying proposed lateral construction, capacity, route diversity, splice location assumptions, and installation lead times. Two carriers occupying the same conduit do not create resilient connectivity. For water-dependent designs, request written service and allocation assumptions from the water utility; a nearby main is not an entitlement. Read municipal agendas, adopted ordinances, and pending amendments because data-center and battery moratoriums can appear between GIS updates. A planning employee's informal email is useful context but does not override an adopted ordinance. Record the proposed use description, hearing sequence, and written interpretation needed for the actual parcel rather than importing a neighboring jurisdiction's development assumptions.

The second directory segment covers six environmental, zoning, and connectivity sources. Use free bulk datasets first, then pay for targeted verification only when a parcel survives the initial screen. An illustrative batch budget could allocate $300 for document retrieval, $1,200 for preliminary environmental review, and $1,500 for power-engineering triage across ten shortlisted sites: $3,000 total, or $300 per site before option consideration. Those allocations are planning assumptions, not vendor quotations. If only two sites survive, the effective screening cost is $1,500 per survivor, which belongs in the acquisition economics rather than disappearing into software overhead. Obtain scopes and quotes for fieldwork because wetlands delineation, geotechnical work, and full utility studies are separate commitments. Keep public-data processing local where practical and upload confidential option documents to external services only with authorization and appropriate contractual controls. Map tools should display evidence age and confidence instead of a single misleading suitability score. The useful output is a ranked work queue identifying the next decisive question, the responsible specialist, the cost to answer it, and the date beyond which the answer no longer protects the option holder.

## 3. Turn docket changes and enrichment into reproducible operator decisions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Implement the waterfall as append-only raw acquisition, normalized records, spatial screening, specialist verification, and publication. Python's urllib.request, csv, json, hashlib, sqlite3, and datetime support downloads, hashes, tabular transformations, storage, and UTC timestamps without importing project-specific modules. Use QGIS for spatial inspection and processing; do not pretend the standard library supplies a complete geospatial intersection engine. Keep original coordinates and declared reference systems, then calculate acreage and distance in an appropriate projected system rather than geographic degrees. Diagnose malformed geometry, duplicate parcel keys, missing source dates, changed column names, and unexpectedly empty extracts before updating the operator dashboard. Reconcile row counts and bounding boxes against the previous snapshot; quarantine suspicious changes for review. Record source URL, license, retrieval time, payload hash, parser version, and reviewer disposition in SQLite. An identical hash can skip redundant downstream work, but a new hash should not automatically invalidate every parcel. Compare relevant attributes and geometries to generate specific alerts. Keep seller contact details in an access-controlled table and avoid distributing personal records inside general developer exports. A reproducible pipeline tells the reviewer exactly which changed fact moved a parcel from viable to suspended.

The mandatory mapped play forced_pooling_mineral_lease_curve_jumping uses public pooling applications and drilling schedules to surface unleased mineral interests before applicable election deadlines. For an Oklahoma example, review Corporation Commission oil-and-gas case records, hearing materials, signed orders, and Oklahoma Statutes title 52, section 87.1; do not transplant its procedures to another state. The controlling order and applicable service rules determine the actual election sequence, not a generic calendar scraped from a hearing notice. Link legal descriptions to recorder instruments, confirm the interest through mineral title work, and ask counsel to explain permitted elections and non-consent cost exposure before acquisition. Illustratively, five net mineral acres within a 640-acre unit at a 20% royalty yield a decimal of 0.0015625. Applied to an assumed $8,000,000 royalty valuation base, that equals $12,500 before applicable deductions, taxes, title adjustments, and timing effects. It is neither a production forecast nor powered-land desk revenue. Acreage allocation can differ for multiunit development. If title, notice, or election economics remain unresolved, stop acquisitions pending counsel review and choose only timely, fully understood permitted elections. Surface-option value and speculative mineral upside stay in separate ledgers.

Enrichment should resolve a specific uncertainty at the cheapest reliable level. Begin with county instruments and Secretary of State entity records, use a licensed aggregator only to accelerate discovery, and confirm signing authority through seller-provided organizational documents. A registered agent is a service contact, not proof of beneficial ownership or authority to assign an option. For mineral screening, use public commission orders before a commercial well-history subscription; for fiber, use carrier engineering after provider discovery rather than purchasing broad contact lists. The eighteen named directory entries deliberately combine registries, downloadable feeds, authenticated APIs, and verification tools because no single endpoint resolves the investment decision. Maintain an alert queue for pooling-order issuance, material queue changes, zoning amendments, expiring seller authorizations, and option notice deadlines. Polling frequency should follow event importance and source rules: review a live election matter each business day, while unchanged elevation tiles need no daily retrieval. If a portal is unavailable, record the outage and use the official clerk or agency contact; never infer that silence means no new order. Preserve the fallback response in the same evidence register so manual diligence remains auditable.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Acquire and fingerprint source evidence
   - Download authorized exports and register their source dates, retrieval times, licenses, and hashes.
   - Keep original files unchanged; quarantine failed responses and HTML error pages instead of treating them as datasets.
2. Normalize and test parcel relationships
   - Join county-qualified parcel identifiers, recorded instruments, entity names, and documented connection references.
   - Review splits, mergers, duplicate keys, coordinate systems, and mineral legal descriptions before spatial scoring.
3. Escalate the decisive uncertainty
   - Assign power, title, environmental, zoning, fiber, or mineral-election questions to the appropriate specialist.
   - Record the scope, quote, response deadline, unresolved assumptions, and option termination notice date together.
4. Publish a versioned decision package
   - Release only reviewed claims with linked evidence, scope limitations, and an explicit as-of date.
   - Withdraw superseded packages and notify active counterparties when a material capacity or title assumption changes.

## 4. Price evidence-backed options and reconcile the desk's economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

A preliminary utility letter supports only its stated assumptions; it does not create an unconditional delivery obligation. Consider an illustrative BESS option with ten utility-documented MW subject to identified conditions and a proposed $15,000-per-MW assignment fee: the conditional fee basis is $150,000. If the buyer also faces $2,000,000 of upgrades and a 36-month service sequence, those burdens affect willingness to pay and closing conditions even though they are not operator revenue. If restudy reduces usable capacity to six MW, the same fee convention yields $90,000 before any further commercial renegotiation. Model a 12–24 month diligence-contingent land option against that longer utility timeline, with express extension rights or a termination plan. Assignment language must address seller permission where required, study-transfer restrictions, diligence access, deposits, and milestone evidence. An option that expires before the decisive study arrives should not be priced as though its holder controls the eventual project. Route state-specific principal-versus-broker questions through qualified counsel.
