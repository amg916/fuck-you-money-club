## 1. Control a transferable development position, not a power narrative  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Powered-land origination is a principal-led business that buys time and organizes evidence before a developer commits to land acquisition and construction. The operator secures an expressly assignable, diligence-contingent land option, usually lasting 12–24 months, and packages access, title, zoning, environmental constraints, utility correspondence, and a credible project schedule. The asset being sold is the documented contractual position, not an electricity entitlement created by drawing a circle around a substation. A standalone BESS screen might begin with 5–25 acres, but acreage remains subordinate to equipment layout, fire access, setbacks, drainage, and the chosen storage duration. An AI campus requires a separate model for phased IT load, cooling water, fiber diversity, backup systems, and local land-use restrictions. An option covering either parcel must state purchase price, extension consideration, inspection access, assignment conditions, seller cooperation, and termination mechanics. Counsel should determine whether recording a memorandum is appropriate and permitted; recording does not cure defective terms. The small-team advantage is acquiring inexpensive, reversible control while expensive questions remain open, then converting answered questions into a buyer-ready position. Paying a premium before those answers arrive reverses the arbitrage and makes the operator the developer's unpaid risk warehouse.

The principal origination play is bess_and_modular_ai_substation_land_option_assignment, the TRACK_1 Utility-Validated BESS and Modular AI Land-Option Origination Desk. Its prospecting stack overlays 69–345 kV corridors, utility hosting-capacity information where available, dark-fiber routes, and environmental constraints in QGIS or ArcGIS Pro. Distribution hosting maps cannot establish transmission-level headroom, and generation queue entries cannot establish retail load service. For a hypothetical 20 MW BESS assignment priced at $2,500 per documented deliverable MW, the contractual fee would be $50,000; the example is arithmetic, not a market quotation. If an initial 10 MW phase is the only supported operating case, do not invoice the remaining 10 MW merely because equipment could fit. Capture charging import, discharging export, operating restrictions, upgrade allocations, and the utility's current schedule separately. Land rights, interconnection rights, and utility transfer permissions are different deliverables with different counterparties. If a restudy defeats the budget, exercise the diligence exit or renegotiate price and term before another nonrefundable payment. An alternative dispatch or load profile requires fresh utility validation. Remove unsupported capacity statements from every buyer document immediately, including older presentations still circulating through intermediaries. This discipline protects both assignment value and the next developer relationship.

Organize the 11 mapped plays as one operating system rather than eleven disconnected products. The two expressly identified tracks are option origination and permission-based outbound; nine supporting workstreams cover utility evidence, title and mineral control, zoning, environmental screening, fiber verification, BESS operating profiles, campus assembly, transfer execution, and carry administration. These workstream labels describe execution responsibilities rather than additional official play identifiers. Five practical operator archetypes appear below. Each must sell a distinct deliverable and retain proof that the relevant obligation was completed. A title specialist can remove a surface-use conflict without claiming to solve interconnection, while a campus assembler can consolidate parcels without claiming that water service is secured. The principal-versus-broker distinction also matters before outreach begins. Holding an option does not automatically exempt every transaction or advertising practice from state real-estate rules. Texas Occupations Code Section 1101.002 defines brokerage activities, and Section 1101.004 addresses equitable-interest transactions and related disclosures. Use state-specific counsel and licensed brokers where the actual activity requires them. The moat is an auditable chain of rights, evidence, and introductions that a developer can reuse in diligence, not an unsupported claim of exclusive access to utility capacity.

## 2. Segment buyers by operating profile and sell evidence through permission-based channels  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Substation headroom is not a single reusable number. A battery's maximum export, its charging demand, and a compute campus's near-continuous import stress different constraints and may follow different utility processes. Record voltage, point of connection, requested service, ramp assumptions, operating hours, protection requirements, and contingency limitations before comparing sites. PJM's New Services Queue, MISO's interconnection resources, CAISO's queue records, and ERCOT's generation interconnection reports support regional screening; none substitutes for a project-specific service determination. FERC Order No. 2023 concerns generator interconnection reforms, not a universal promise of large-load availability, and jurisdiction varies by grid and transaction. A utility letter itself needs interpretation: distinguish preliminary feedback, completed study findings, conditional service offers, and executed agreements. Price against the document's actual conditions and validity period. Build an upgrade exposure sheet showing estimated cost, deposit deadline, refundability, allocation method, and restudy risk. Energization dates must include equipment lead times, rights-of-way, permitting, and network construction. A nearby transformer with an attractive nameplate rating tells the operator where to ask questions; it does not tell a buyer how many usable megawatts can be purchased or when those megawatts will arrive.

The comparison matrix separates sixteen buyer situations because their fatal flaws and payable deliverables differ. Every fee example is a proposed contract structure, not observed pricing or an extra contribution to the scorecard. For BESS, evaluate a 5–25 acre parcel against the actual layout and both import and export requirements; an export-only study cannot prove feasible charging. For AI, compare utility-supported facility demand with IT demand after cooling and other overhead, and never silently substitute one measure for the other. FiberLocator and TeleGeography help locate potential carrier infrastructure, but a route on a map does not establish available strands, diverse entrances, installation cost, or a service commitment. Obtain carrier confirmation and identify shared physical choke points. FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, the USFWS Wetlands Mapper, and NRCS Web Soil Survey accelerate screening without replacing field diligence. National Wetlands Inventory polygons are not jurisdictional determinations. Check current municipal restrictions and meeting records directly, especially pending data-center or BESS moratoriums. Under Clean Water Act Section 404, regulated discharges of dredged or fill material can require authorization; a developer's preferred pad location does not eliminate that constraint. Separate screen results from determinations in the buyer package.

If a developer pays a seller $4 million for land and pays the operator $65,000 for an assigned option position, the operator does not report $4.065 million merely because both amounts appear in closing documents. For customer contracts within ASC 606, identify the promised deliverable, transaction price, performance obligations, satisfaction point, and any variable consideration constraint. A signed agreement or refundable deposit alone does not establish earned revenue. A per-MW fee needs a contract definition of MW and objective milestone acceptance evidence. Classify a transfer of an option interest under the accounting guidance applicable to its actual substance rather than assuming every transfer is a service contract. Development carry may be investment income or fall under other guidance, including ASC 323 where applicable; do not force it into ASC 606 because the scorecard groups realized proceeds together. Keep the scorecard's operating measure reconciled to the accounting ledger. A projected exit valuation is neither a cash distribution nor an earned assignment fee, and it cannot rescue a weak annual profit case.

## 4. Build a repeatable evidence desk and stop weak sites before extension  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the operating desk around one parcel identifier and one evidence register. Store geometry in QGIS or PostGIS, maintain counterparties in a CRM, and link documents through an access-controlled repository with version history. Automation should collect public queue changes, flag option expirations, compare parcel constraints, and assemble source-linked buyer briefs. It should not manufacture a utility conclusion from distance-to-line calculations. For each material assertion, record source, date, author, geographic scope, project configuration, and superseding documents. Require human review of title exceptions, utility conditions, and zoning interpretations before those assertions reach a buyer. Assign the principal commercial decisions, an electrical consultant the power-service analysis, counsel the option and transfer structure, and a land-use or environmental specialist the site-specific constraint work. The canonical contractor counts describe staffing capacity, not permission for one specialist to practice outside their expertise. Track diligence spend per rejected site, qualified buyer conversations per accepted brief, option days remaining, and receipts per completed assignment. A claimed two-to-six-month time to first deal concerns origination and closing activity, not construction completion or energization. Those later schedules must remain grounded in the actual utility and development record.

Make failure handling contractual before it becomes emotional. The option should specify how notices are delivered, when consideration becomes nonrefundable, whether extensions are unilateral, and which obligations survive termination. Title work should address access, easements, restrictive covenants, severed minerals, and seller authority; a mineral waiver is useful only if the relevant rights holder signs an effective instrument. Separate property assignment permission from project-transfer requirements imposed by utilities, interconnection agreements, or agencies. Land transfer cannot carry rights the seller never controlled. If a municipality imposes a BESS or data-center moratorium, stop presenting the previous entitlement path as current. If network-upgrade exposure rises, update the buyer model, check termination deadlines, and decline further nonrefundable spending unless the revised case stands independently. If wetlands or flood constraints remove the buildable pad, obtain engineering support for a revised footprint before renegotiating price. Maintain a written rejected-site record stating the actual reason and the evidence that could justify reconsideration. This record prevents automated sourcing from repeatedly resurfacing the same failed parcel and helps the team distinguish a temporary timing problem from a permanent physical or contractual defect.

The durable moat is a reliable reduction in developer uncertainty per dollar of option exposure. Measure it through clean assignments, repeat buyers, documented utility interactions, and low avoidable write-offs rather than the number of map pins collected. Before marketing, confirm the seller's authority, your transferable interest, the utility evidence level, and the buyer's acquisition brief. Before invoicing, confirm acceptance of the exact promised deliverable and whether refunds, credits, or contingent terms affect the amount earned. Before booking carry, reconcile the governing waterfall, actual distribution, and ledger classification. A $65,000 assignment that requires $80,000 of unrecoverable upstream spending is a loss regardless of the campus's eventual construction budget. Conversely, a small, clean assignment can establish a repeatable buyer relationship without exposing the desk to construction risk. Start with one utility territory, one operating profile, and a short list of qualified developers. Expand only when the evidence workflow survives another operator's review and the option reserve survives a failed cohort. This is how a one-person principal with specialist contractors becomes a disciplined origination business instead of a speculative inventory of expiring land promises.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Define one buyer-backed operating profile
   - Obtain a written acquisition brief from qualified developers in one utility territory.
   - Record facility load or storage import and export, acreage, timing, upgrade budget, fiber needs, and evidence required before an assignment can be accepted.
2. Screen cheaply and secure reversible control
   - Overlay parcel, corridor, flood, wetland, and zoning information before negotiating an option.
   - Use an expressly assignable 12–24 month diligence structure where commercially available; calendar notices, extensions, seller obligations, and the maximum nonrefundable exposure.
3. Validate utility and physical constraints
   - Obtain project-specific utility feedback and specialist review of the critical site constraints.
   - Keep load service, generation interconnection, land rights, and transfer permissions distinct; exit or renegotiate when the supported operating case no longer clears the budget.
4. Transfer, collect, and reconcile
   - Deliver the controlled evidence package and close only the contracted transferable position.
   - Secure required consents, document acceptance, reconcile credits and retained fees, record actual option losses, and distinguish realized carry from projected project value.
