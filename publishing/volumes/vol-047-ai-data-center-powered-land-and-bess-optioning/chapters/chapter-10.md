## 1. Build an evidence pipeline before increasing parcel throughput  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a parcel-centered evidence ledger, not a chatbot searching for cheap land. A Python scheduler retrieves permitted downloads from PJM, MISO, ERCOT, utility hosting-capacity portals, county GIS services, and recorder indexes. Use urllib.request for transport, csv and json for structured records, sqlite3 for the local ledger, and hashlib for immutable source fingerprints. Each observation carries the source URL, retrieval time, effective date, coordinate reference, parser version, and parcel identifier. Queue identifiers belong in a separate relationship table because one project can span several parcels and one parcel can attract several proposals. Keep raw snapshots alongside normalized fields so a changed column name cannot silently alter a capacity claim. Public availability does not establish permission for unlimited automated access; honor access terms, request limits, and licensed reuse boundaries. Schedule county ownership refreshes separately from utility engineering updates. A daily deed event may justify owner outreach, while a monthly utility map cannot support a same-day capacity assertion. The eleven-play map below uses one supplied canonical identifier and ten descriptive implementation labels. Its purpose is to assign an economic test and a recovery path to every repetitive activity before volume increases.

Measure the seventy-percent objective against observed human minutes, not the percentage of screens that contain an AI feature. An illustrative benchmark processes 100 routine parcel packets with a 1,000-minute manual baseline: 420 research minutes, 240 qualification minutes, 180 fulfillment minutes, and 160 acquisition-administration minutes. After including review and exception handling, target net reductions of 330, 180, 120, and 100 minutes respectively. That removes 730 minutes, or 73%, leaving 270 human minutes. Deep utility studies, negotiations, field visits, and title resolution remain outside this routine-task benchmark and retain separate time budgets. The principal owns utility representations, option commitments, and buyer negotiations. Three contractors cover GIS and research, electrical feasibility, and title/document operations; any licensed legal work uses appropriately qualified counsel. Record reviewer identity and elapsed time at each consequential transition. If source deterioration increases review time, report the lower achieved automation rate instead of hiding those minutes in another category. Run a weekly matched sample against manual processing, checking missed restrictions and incorrect parcel joins. A faster pipeline that distributes the wrong owner or transformer evidence has increased liability and rework rather than productive capacity.

Treat language models as constrained extraction workers. Submit a bounded document excerpt and request fields such as study date, stated project type, MW quantity, limiting condition, and supporting quotation. Validate the returned JSON against explicit keys, numeric ranges, and required evidence spans before storing it. Absence of a supported value produces a pending-evidence state, never a fabricated engineering answer. Models must not obey instructions embedded in filings, contact landowners, edit option terms, or publish packets. An illustrative 100-parcel routine batch budgets $6 for licensed source access, $4 for compute, $8 for model usage, and $2 for evidence storage: $20 total, or $0.20 per parcel. The model allowance assumes two million input tokens at $2 per million and half a million output tokens at $8 per million; these are budgeting assumptions rather than vendor quotes. Adding 270 review minutes at $60 per hour produces $290, or $2.90 per routine packet. This excludes mail, surveys, engineering, legal work, and option deposits. Cap spending per batch, cache unchanged documents by fingerprint, and reroute malformed responses to deterministic parsing or manual entry rather than repeatedly paying for an unsupported answer.

## 2. Separate map-based screening from utility-backed site qualification  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build two independent qualification schemas. Standalone BESS starts with a 5–25 acre parcel envelope, usable area, access geometry, fire access, setbacks, topography, charging import, discharge export, and interconnection position. An AI data-center campus adds materially different requirements: firm load service, energization phases, redundant service architecture, fiber diversity, cooling strategy, water availability where required, noise limits, and expansion area. A 69kV–345kV line near either property is only a screening feature. A hosting-capacity layer can also describe a particular generation assumption rather than firm load availability. Never translate its displayed number directly into sellable campus MW. Store service type, study scope, issuing utility, conditions, expected in-service date, and estimated network-upgrade exposure together. FERC interconnection requirements under 18 CFR 35.28 provide relevant context for covered generation interconnections; they do not replace the utility's retail load-service process or establish jurisdiction over every project. When a developer requests a capacity headline, respond with the actual documented service position and its limitations. Until the utility resolves deliverability, price the parcel as a conditional development lead rather than a powered asset. This distinction belongs in database fields, marketing text, and the assignment agreement.

Use environmental and title automation to prioritize specialist work, not to issue definitive clearance. FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service National Wetlands Inventory can expose obvious conflicts quickly, but map age, scale, and unmapped features limit conclusions. A wetland inventory is not a jurisdictional determination under Clean Water Act Section 404, 33 U.S.C. 1344. Record source dates and route potential aquatic-resource impacts to an environmental specialist before committing to grading assumptions. County zoning portals require similar discipline: store adopted text, district boundaries, use definitions, effective dates, and temporary moratorium actions separately. A staff email may explain process without establishing an entitlement. Title extraction must flag severed mineral interests, surface-use rights, leases, easements, and missing referenced instruments. Do not describe a mineral waiver as secured until the correct rights holder has executed an effective instrument. Python can reconcile document references and flag exceptions; it cannot resolve competing property rights. For campus candidates, obtain carrier evidence covering actual serviceability and route separation. Two providers sharing the same duct bank do not create physical diversity, and a nearby backbone does not establish a usable local connection.

Value option interests against documented service outcomes and explicit downside branches. Consider an illustrative BESS candidate whose utility study supports a conditional 20 MW export position and identifies $1.2 million of upgrade exposure. At an assumed $6,000 per documented MW, a negotiated origination milestone would be $120,000; the developer's $60,000 per MW upgrade burden is separate project expenditure, not operator revenue. If a restudy doubles upgrades to $2.4 million, recalculate buyer demand and milestone probability before paying an extension. An illustrative branch with a 40% chance of collecting $120,000, $22,000 of remaining diligence and option spending, and $8,000 of closing expense only on success produces $22,800 of expected incremental contribution: $48,000 less $22,000 less $3,200. That estimate supports a decision, not booked revenue. For a campus, build a separate load-service schedule; a BESS export study cannot substitute for campus import rights. If energization slips beyond the 12–24 month option term, obtain a priced extension or terminate before the next deposit. Carry expired option fees as real portfolio expenses. A failed utility validation should reduce the budget immediately rather than survive as an optimistic pipeline asset.

Prohibit government-lookalike poster or deed invoices, official-looking seals, invented citation numbers, and language suggesting mandatory payment. 39 U.S.C. 3001 restricts specified deceptive mailed solicitations; California Business and Professions Code Section 17537.9 imposes specific requirements on covered recorded-document solicitations. Review the actual offer and target state before mailing. A direct-mail API may render and dispatch an approved creative, but a human must authorize the recipient segment and final proof. If a utility revises its study, invalidate every downstream packet that references the old fingerprint and notify recipients who received affected claims. A weekly dashboard should show accepted introductions, utility validation rate, deposit burn, review minutes, packet corrections, and collected operator fees. Measure source-to-qualified-option conversion by cohort rather than celebrating raw traffic. Increase distribution only when current evidence, reviewer capacity, and cash runway support the added workload.
