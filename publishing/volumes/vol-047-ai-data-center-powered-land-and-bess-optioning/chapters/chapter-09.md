## 1. Establish the principal interest before selling the opportunity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start every parcel file with a role memorandum identifying the acquiring entity, the interest it actually holds, the property jurisdiction, and the services it will perform for anyone else. An executed option can create enforceable contract rights and, depending on state law and drafting, an equitable interest; a GIS pin, unsigned letter, or introduction does not. Recording a permitted memorandum may protect notice priority, but recording alone neither creates valid ownership nor supplies a brokerage exemption. Texas Occupations Code Section 1101.0045 expressly addresses selling an option or assigning a purchase-contract interest without brokerage activity, subject to written disclosure of the interest's nature. California Business and Professions Code Sections 10130, 10131, and 10133 require a separate analysis of compensated acts for another and applicable exemptions. Do not generalize either state's treatment nationwide. If the actual service is locating buyers, negotiating another owner's land sale, or earning compensation for procuring a counterparty, engage a properly licensed state broker under a written allocation of duties. A principal label on an invoice does not repair brokerage conduct. Keep acquisition compensation, assignment proceeds, brokerage commissions, and technical consulting fees in separate contract schedules so the facts remain auditable.

Build a jurisdiction sheet before spending the first option dollar. Record state licensing statutes, commercial versus residential scope, transaction frequency restrictions where relevant, required assignment disclosures, local land-use authority, and the professionals authorized to perform engineering or legal work. New York Real Property Law Sections 440 and 440-a define brokerage activity and impose licensing requirements; determine how the actual entity and services fit rather than importing a Texas conclusion. State wholesaling reforms sometimes target residential property specifically, so verify covered property types before applying registration or disclosure rules to a utility-scale commercial tract. Local zoning can independently prohibit a BESS use or impose a data-center moratorium even when the assignment structure is sound. Confirm signatory authority for LLC owners, trustees, estate representatives, and co-owners through documents acceptable to the closing title company. A probate docket is a diligence signal, not proof that a beneficiary can grant an option. Have state counsel resolve ambiguous authority or exemption questions before buyer solicitation. The licensed surveyor establishes boundaries, the engineer evaluates technical constraints, and the title company reports insurability exceptions; none of those functions should be implied by an automated parcel score. Preserve the dated conclusion and its supporting documents.

Use public_record_api_intelligence to detect commercial triggers, not to manufacture ownership certainty. County recorder indexes, assessor exports, probate registries, planning dockets, utility commission filings, and official APIs can expose fragmented ownership, expiring permits, industrial reuse, or new infrastructure proceedings. Unclaimed-property ledgers may help resolve entity identity where lawful access permits, but they do not establish parcel title or authorize recovery services. Federal FOIA, 5 U.S.C. Section 552, governs federal agency records; county records generally require the applicable state public-records process instead. Store source URL, retrieval date, document identifier, reuse terms, and confidence level beside each claim. An illustrative monthly batch of 300 parcels at $0.40 retrieval cost plus 12 analyst hours at $50 costs $720. If 24 survive document screening, the intelligence cost is $30 per surviving parcel, before title, engineering, or option payments. Do not count those survivors as contracted sites. When an endpoint closes or access terms prohibit automation, switch to scheduled bulk FOIA/public-record exports, official data feeds, and low-frequency cached queues. Respect rate limits and access restrictions; collect only necessary contact details. Route disputed ownership, protected personal records, and conflicting parcel identifiers to manual review rather than auto-generating seller demands.

## 2. Tie every power claim to a dated utility and permitting record  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat a 69kV–345kV substation layer as a screening instrument, not a deliverability certificate. Maintain separate fields for requested MW, studied MW, conditionally available MW, contracted service, and actual energization. Each entry needs the utility or transmission provider, document date, service configuration, dependencies, expiration, and authorized disclosure scope. A hosting-capacity map may address distributed generation rather than a large load, and an ISO generation queue does not reserve data-center load service. FERC Order No. 2023 and implementing tariff changes concern generator interconnection processes within their jurisdiction; they do not replace utility retail service rules, state commission requirements, or applicable large-load procedures. For BESS, separately establish charging load, export rights, operating limitations, and whether the project follows distribution or transmission interconnection rules. For a campus, determine redundancy, ramp schedule, curtailment exposure, power quality, and construction lead times. Request written treatment of network upgrades, cost allocation, security postings, restudy events, and transfer permissions. A study number is evidence of process status only. Marketing must reproduce material conditions instead of turning a preliminary estimate into a firm delivery date. If the utility will not support the proposed MW, price the parcel as speculative land rights rather than powered inventory.

Separate a standalone BESS parcel of 5–25 acres from a data-center campus at the first screening step. The BESS file needs usable acreage after setbacks, emergency access, equipment separation, drainage, noise controls, fire-water arrangements where required, and charging/export feasibility. The campus file additionally needs dark-fiber route evidence, carrier availability, cooling design, water supply and discharge permissions, backup-generation permitting, and expansion geometry. A carrier map does not establish a service commitment or diverse physical entrances. Local adoption of NFPA 855 or fire-code provisions determines enforceable installation requirements; UL 9540 and UL 9540A documentation serve different equipment and test functions and do not themselves grant a land-use permit. Check the enacted ordinance and current moratorium language with the planning authority. Clean Water Act Section 404, 33 U.S.C. Section 1344, can require authorization for discharges of dredged or fill material into covered waters, including jurisdictional wetlands. A GIS wetland layer is not a jurisdictional determination. CERCLA liability under 42 U.S.C. Section 9607 makes environmental history material to eventual acquisition; timely all-appropriate-inquiries work and other statutory conditions matter to available protections. Require qualified investigation rather than assuming a clean-looking agricultural parcel has no environmental exposure.

Value the option against utility-documented deliverable MW, upgrade exposure, and a credible energization schedule, then discount for remaining conditions. Illustratively, a milestone priced at $2,500 per qualifying MW produces $50,000 only when 20 MW satisfy the contract's evidence definition. If an authorized utility update supports just 12 qualifying MW, the same formula produces $30,000; if the evidence threshold is unmet, that milestone produces zero. These are underwriting mechanics, not additional scorecard revenue. A $4 million upgrade estimate on 20 MW equals $200,000 per MW of developer capital exposure, not originator revenue. Set a buyer-specific upgrade ceiling and latest acceptable energization date, and give the option holder a termination or repricing mechanism when either fails. Budget failed sites explicitly: four expired options at $3,000 each consume $12,000 before recovered deposits or transferable reports. Keep that burn inside the scenario expense envelope rather than subtracting it again from canonical profit. Underwrite BESS revenue and operating assumptions separately from campus economics; an attractive battery location may fail data-center fiber or water requirements. Do not capitalize hoped-for development carry as current fees. A dated utility correction should immediately update valuation, buyer materials, and the decision on the next extension payment.

## 3. Draft transferable rights and reconcile every fee to the scorecard  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a 12–24 month diligence-contingent option only when its extension economics match the likely utility and entitlement calendar. Identify the legal description, purchase-price mechanism, option consideration, payment recipient, effective date, exercise procedure, notice addresses, and exact expiration time. Separate the initial term from paid extensions and state which payments are refundable, credited at closing, or earned immediately. Do not bury a utility-dependent business plan inside an unconditional payment schedule. Give the holder defined access for surveys, environmental investigation, utility meetings, and carrier inquiries, subject to insurance, restoration, and restrictions on invasive work. Address easements, mineral and surface-use conflicts, access rights, title objections, and seller cooperation with applications. Assignment language must distinguish land-option rights from utility applications, study agreements, permits, and consultant reports, each of which may require separate consent or reapplication. Specify notice-only transfers, permitted affiliates, third-party consent standards, and whether the original holder remains liable. Attach any recordable memorandum and the release obligation after termination. An option that cannot be transferred on the buyer's schedule is not assignment inventory merely because its cover page says assignable. Calendar exercise notices with redundant reminders and delivery evidence; missing an exact contractual deadline can destroy an otherwise valuable position without any deterioration in the site.

Define fee triggers so an accountant and a hostile counterparty reach the same answer. An assignment fee should identify the transferred interest, closing conditions, escrow instructions, and payment timing. A per-MW milestone needs the qualifying utility evidence, measurement unit, partial-performance treatment, dispute procedure, and exclusion of previously paid quantities. Development carry needs a distribution waterfall, reporting rights, dilution treatment, and realization conditions; unsigned projections are not earned fees. The canonical Conservative case is $110,000 revenue less $77,000 expenses, producing $33,000 at 30%. Base is $505,000 less $292,900, producing $212,100 at 42.0%. Scaled is $900,000 less $414,000, producing $486,000 at 54%. Illustrative fee compositions are two $55,000 assignments; five $85,000 assignments plus four separate $20,000 milestones; and six $100,000 assignments plus ten separate $30,000 milestones, respectively. Do not count a milestone twice if it is credited against an assignment fee. These compositions reconcile the scorecard rather than establish closing forecasts. Underlying land prices, loan principal, developer capex, and reimbursed third-party amounts do not become operator fee revenue merely by crossing an account. Have the accountant determine applicable recognition, asset-sale, variable-consideration, and principal-versus-agent treatment; do not invent a retained-share adjustment absent from these canonical scenarios.

Make scope, confidentiality, anti-circumvention, liability, dispute resolution, and data rights operational rather than decorative. Scope should list deliverables and exclusions, including whether utility coordination means transmitting documents or performing licensed engineering. An anti-circumvention clause should identify introduced counterparties and parcels, preserve documented pre-existing relationships, specify a reasonable survival period, and tie any remedy to enforceable terms instead of claiming ownership of every regional developer. Use a jurisdiction-appropriate liability cap with negotiated exclusions for matters such as fraud, intentional misconduct, confidentiality breaches, or unpaid amounts; enforceability and insurability require review. Allocate indemnities to actual control over access work, third-party claims, and inaccurate representations. Choose governing law, forum or arbitration procedure, notice method, interim-relief treatment, and responsibility for fees. Separate public source material from proprietary scoring logic, licensed GIS layers, seller confidential material, and commissioned reports. A paid consultant report may require an assignment or reliance letter before a buyer can use it. Grant only the data rights actually held and prohibit redistribution where source terms require it. Finally, define correction duties: a capacity reduction, title defect, or enacted moratorium must reach active counterparties promptly. Confidentiality cannot become an excuse to preserve a materially outdated sales statement.

## 4. Run truthful outreach, structured discovery, and documented handoff  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Segment outreach by channel and recipient before drafting the first message. CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. Section 7704, applies to commercial email including business-to-business messages; use accurate routing information, a nondeceptive subject, required advertising identification where applicable, a valid physical postal address, and a working opt-out mechanism. Honor opt-outs within ten business days and maintain suppression across contractors rather than buying the same address back through another list. For calls and texts, evaluate 47 U.S.C. Section 227, 47 CFR Section 64.1200, federal do-not-call requirements where applicable, and relevant state calling and recording laws. Manual dialing is not a universal exemption from solicitation restrictions, and a business contact's published mobile number is not blanket permission for automated marketing. Determine whether the technology, destination, message purpose, and consent satisfy applicable rules before launch. Start with individually researched business email and permitted manual calls, not a high-volume text campaign. FTC Act Section 5 and state deceptive-practices laws make misleading capacity or ownership claims dangerous regardless of the channel. Maintain a campaign record containing the source, permitted purpose, message version, send date, opt-out status, and any promised follow-up. Restrict access to contact exports and remove unnecessary personal details.

Use scripts that match the actual stage of ownership, and read them verbatim only when their factual statements are true. Before acquisition, say: 'I acquire commercial site options as a principal. I am evaluating whether your parcel could support a battery facility or a data-center use. I do not represent the utility, and I have not established power availability. Would you consider a paid, time-limited option with defined diligence and assignment terms?' After execution, a buyer opening can say: 'Our entity holds an option on this parcel. We are offering our contractual interest, not representing that we own the land outright. The diligence package separates utility-confirmed facts from unresolved conditions. May I send the rights summary and evidence index?' For utility intake, say: 'We hold written owner authorization to investigate this parcel. Please identify the applicable process for the proposed load or storage configuration, required deposits, study scope, and any restrictions on sharing or transferring the resulting documents.' If authorization is absent, obtain it before using that script. These openings avoid artificial scarcity and do not turn a nearby substation into a capacity promise. Follow each conversation with a written summary identifying assumptions, requested documents, and the next decision date.

Discovery should expose disqualifiers before an extension payment comes due. Ask the developer: 'What initial and ultimate load, export capability, redundancy, curtailment tolerance, maximum upgrade contribution, and latest energization date would make this site usable?' Then ask: 'Which utility documents and transfer permissions must be present before your team pays an assignment fee or a per-MW milestone?' For a campus, add: 'What fiber diversity, cooling-water strategy, backup-generation envelope, and expansion acreage are essential?' For BESS, ask: 'What charge and discharge limits, fire-access geometry, and operating restrictions would defeat your design?' Record answers as acceptance criteria, not loose sales notes. When evidence changes, use a correction script: 'The utility's latest written position changes the capacity assumption in our earlier package. Please stop using that version. We will provide the replacement evidence and revised commercial terms before requesting any commitment.' A decline closes with: 'Understood. We will record your preference and stop further marketing contact through this channel.' The handoff package should contain the signed rights chain, evidence index, unresolved issues, cost responsibility schedule, approved message history, and milestone ledger. Require a named person to own each unresolved item. Automation can prepare reminders and comparison reports, but it cannot convert missing authority or unsupported MW into a sellable fact.
