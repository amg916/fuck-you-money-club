## 1. Define Seven Service Plays Before Choosing Any Compensation Formula  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Government contract brokering is a commercial label, not a federal license category or permission to sell influence. For Candidate #4, use seven explicit operating plays: P1 solicitation monitoring, P2 opportunity qualification, P3 bid-calendar control, P4 proposal coordination, P5 prime/sub teaming, P6 supplier readiness, and P7 post-award administration. P1 produces a sourced alert; P2 produces a documented bid decision; P3 maintains amendment deadlines; P4 assembles client-approved submissions; P5 documents actual work allocation; P6 verifies supplier capacity; and P7 tracks contractual deliverables and invoice evidence. Each play needs its own acceptance test and accountable employee. The two full-time roles are a managing procurement lead and a bid-operations lead. Three contractors provide proposal production, sector-specific supplier analysis, and specialist contract review under defined work orders. Contractors do not gain authority to certify client representations, promise awards, or contact restricted officials merely by joining the pod. Configure SAM.gov saved searches and a shared calendar before buying expensive databases. Keep the solicitation identifier, amendment version, source URL, retrieval date, and assigned reviewer with every opportunity. These records turn a vague introduction business into auditable service delivery, while exposing work that would exceed the pod's available capacity before a client signs.

Compensation must survive the actual solicitation and the governing rules, not merely carry a consulting label. FAR Subpart 3.4 and FAR 52.203-5 address contingent fees for soliciting or obtaining government contracts, subject to their coverage and bona fide employee or agency exceptions. FAR 3.404 prescribes the covenant for contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, except commercial products or commercial services; other restrictions can still apply outside that prescription. A payment triggered only by award can remain contingent even when described as a completion bonus. Conversely, an ordinary fixed retainer does not excuse improper influence, unlawful lobbying, or prohibited access. Default to monthly fees for enumerated monitoring work and fixed milestones for completed proposal packages, payable independently of award. Have the specialist review any exception claim against FAR 3.401 and the documented relationship before offering it. Under 31 U.S.C. 1352, covered federal funds cannot pay for specified lobbying activities; disclosure requirements can also attach to payments from other sources. Separate procurement administration from efforts to influence covered officials, and examine lobbying registration obligations independently. The contract must describe real services, real records, and the exact event creating an earned fee.

Treat protected information and supplier compensation as separate risk controls. The procurement-integrity provisions in 41 U.S.C. 2101-2107 and FAR 3.104 restrict knowingly obtaining or disclosing covered contractor bid, proposal, and source-selection information before award. A former official's familiarity with a program does not authorize delivery of a competitor's pricing or an evaluator's private notes. Public amendments, published questions, and authorized market research belong in the workroom; suspicious nonpublic evaluation material belongs in a restricted incident process without further circulation. The Anti-Kickback Act, 41 U.S.C. 8701-8707, reaches things of value provided to improperly obtain or reward favorable treatment involving covered prime contracts or subcontracts. Do not pay a prime's purchasing employee personally for selecting a supplier, and do not route that payment through a marketing invoice. Require written disclosure of referral arrangements, supplier-side compensation, familial interests, and ownership ties before P5 begins. The procurement lead decides whether the engagement can proceed after specialist review; the analyst cannot resolve a conflict by adding a spreadsheet note. Keep separate client workspaces and access logs so competing bidders never share draft prices, strategies, or proposal personnel without a specifically assessed, authorized arrangement.

## 2. Recheck State, Sector, and Small-Business Boundaries for Every Opportunity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build a jurisdiction sheet for each buyer instead of treating SAM.gov familiarity as nationwide authorization. Record whether the purchaser is a federal agency, state entity, municipality, authority, or recipient spending federal assistance. Federal grant procurement can implicate 2 CFR 200.318-200.327 without turning a municipal purchase into a FAR procurement. Add the solicitation's communication restrictions, lobbying rules, vendor registration steps, professional licensing requirements, and public-records procedures. New York State Finance Law sections 139-j and 139-k establish restricted-period contact and disclosure requirements for covered governmental procurements; route communications through designated contacts and document permitted exceptions. Florida Statutes section 287.055 governs covered professional services and includes a contingent-fee prohibition with stated exceptions. Texas Government Code Chapter 2254 uses qualifications-based selection for specified professional services, making a generic lowest-price pitch the wrong operating model. California Public Contract Code section 4104 requires listed subcontractor information in covered public works bids. The point is not to memorize fifty summaries. It is to attach the governing text and buyer instructions to P2 before committing P4 hours or P5 introductions, then assign one reviewer to resolve every identified mismatch.

There is no single federal procurement-broker credential that authorizes engineering, construction, insurance placement, freight brokerage, or other separately regulated services. A bid desk can coordinate a licensed engineer's submission without representing that it offers engineering itself. If the desk begins selling the regulated service, controlling its delivery, or acting as the contracting provider, recheck the applicable firm and individual licensing requirements before accepting that scope. For interstate motor-carrier brokerage, examine 49 U.S.C. 13904 and 13906 alongside the proposed transportation role; ordinary supplier research does not itself establish that role. For public works, inspect contractor licensing, bonding, prevailing-wage provisions, and subcontractor listing requirements. Federal construction may implicate the Davis-Bacon provisions at 40 U.S.C. 3141-3148; covered federal service contracts may implicate 41 U.S.C. Chapter 67. P6 should collect current licenses, insurance evidence, capacity commitments, and applicable wage classifications without pretending those documents prove complete eligibility. Where controlled information or defense clauses appear, identify the actual security requirements before collecting files. Do not put controlled client material into an ordinary shared drive or drafting service merely because the vendor advertises encryption.

Teaming must allocate performance, not manufacture the appearance of an eligible prime. Under 13 CFR 125.6, covered service set-asides generally limit payments to subcontractors that are not similarly situated to fifty percent of the amount paid by the government for contract performance, subject to applicable exclusions and details. Supply and construction categories use different calculations; never reuse the service worksheet without classifying the requirement. Similarly situated status depends on the relevant program status and size under the assigned subcontract NAICS code, not a friendly supplier's assertion that it is small. Also examine the ostensible-subcontractor rule in 13 CFR 121.103 where applicable: an eligible-looking prime can create affiliation risk through unusual reliance on another firm or its performance of primary and vital requirements. FAR 9.6 recognizes contractor team arrangements but does not excuse these restrictions or promise that a teaming memorandum will become an enforceable subcontract. Specify tasks, personnel, supervision, payment responsibility, and replacement rights before pricing. Candidate #4 should normally sell coordination services rather than become a hollow prime. If a client requests a different structure, pause that structure while the pod continues permissible monitoring and document work.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four Checks Before Any Submission or Teaming Commitment**
> 
> The procurement lead records the decision and supporting documents. An unresolved item blocks the affected commitment, not unrelated authorized research.
> - Verify buyer jurisdiction, funding source, current solicitation amendments, designated contacts, and the exact service requiring any license.
> - Verify compensation triggers against applicable contingent-fee terms, lobbying restrictions, supplier payments, and disclosed conflicts.
> - Verify prime eligibility, similarly situated subcontractor status, actual performance allocation, and any applicable nonmanufacturer requirements.
> - Verify client authorization, current representations, permitted data environment, supplier capacity, and named ownership of every submission certification.

## 3. Contract for Deliverables and Reconcile Fees to the Canonical Economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use a master services agreement with opportunity-specific work orders rather than a broad promise to obtain government business. The scope schedule should identify which of P1 through P7 are purchased, which client inputs are required, and what constitutes a completed output. For P4, completion might require a populated compliance matrix, assembled narrative, attachment inventory, and client review copy; it should not silently include authority to sign certifications or submit through the client's account. Give the client a defined review period and objective correction procedure. Tie the fee to accepted services or clearly described availability, with deposits treated according to the applicable accounting policy rather than automatically as earned revenue. State how amendments, cancellations, protests, and shortened deadlines affect work already performed. Anti-circumvention language should protect identified introductions and legitimate unpaid services for a bounded period, not create an indefinite ownership claim over public opportunities. It must not resurrect a prohibited award commission through liquidated damages or a termination charge. The specialist should assess enforceability under the chosen state law and replace punitive economics with a defensible measure of actual contracted value and documented loss.

Allocate risk to the party controlling the underlying fact. The client owns its pricing decisions, eligibility representations, performance promises, and certification accuracy; the desk owns its agreed process and deliverable obligations. A liability cap might reference fees paid during a defined period, with expressly negotiated treatment of confidentiality breaches, intellectual property claims, intentional misconduct, and other exceptions. Private limitations do not extinguish statutory exposure, and states differ on enforceability. Define escalation, governing law, venue or arbitration, interim relief, collection expenses, and whether work pauses during a payment dispute. Do not confuse this private service dispute process with a bid protest, an agency claim, or the prime's Contract Disputes Act procedures. For intellectual property, distinguish preexisting templates from client-owned bid content and licensed third-party material. For data, identify authorized users, approved storage, incident notice timing, return or deletion procedures, and legally required retention. Government data-rights and security clauses require separate flowdown analysis when applicable. Do not promise that marking every document confidential defeats public-records laws. Finally, require written change orders when P7 expands from tracking invoices into managing performance, accepting deliverables, or directing subcontractors; those activities create materially different responsibility.

Keep the scorecard anchored to recognized service fees. The Conservative case is $120,000 revenue less $102,000 total costs, leaving $18,000 owner net profit at fifteen percent. Base is $560,000 less $434,000, leaving $126,000 at 22.5 percent. Scaled is $1,000,000 less $700,000, leaving $300,000 at thirty percent. An illustrative Base mix is eight clients paying $4,000 monthly for twelve months, producing $384,000, plus twenty-two completed proposal packages at $8,000, producing $176,000. Those packages must be incremental services, not retainer work billed twice. The client's award value and subcontract purchasing spend are excluded from this desk's service-fee revenue. Conservative and Base use two full-time employees plus three contractors; Scaled uses three full-time employees plus four contractors. These are canonical staffing assumptions, not proof that local compensation fits the cost envelope. The Conservative cost allowance is especially tight for that staffing pattern and needs a priced labor plan. The stated $5,000-$25,000 monthly operating range must be reconciled with variable delivery costs rather than substituted for total annual expenses. Budget $25,000-$150,000 startup capital against the six-to-eighteen-month first-deal window, milestone collections, and payment-delay exposure.

## 4. Use Authorized Outreach Scripts and a Four-Step Evidence Workflow  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start contractor outreach with a service proposition that can be delivered without privileged access. Use this script verbatim: 'Hello, I run a procurement support desk that monitors public solicitations, controls bid calendars, and assembles client-approved proposal packages. We charge for defined services, not introductions to officials or promised awards. Are you the person responsible for deciding which government opportunities your company pursues? If so, may we schedule a short discussion about your target buyers, delivery capacity, and current bidding process? If this is irrelevant, reply stop and we will suppress further marketing to this address.' Add a truthful sender identity, descriptive subject line, commercial identification where required, and the business's valid physical postal address. CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. 7704, applies to covered commercial email, including business recipients; honor opt-outs within ten business days and supervise any outreach vendor. Do not scrape restricted bidder portals, impersonate a purchasing office, or imply government endorsement. For automated calls or texts, assess 47 U.S.C. 227, implementing FCC rules, and relevant state requirements before using the channel. A public business listing does not by itself authorize every dialing method or destination.

Discovery should establish operational fit before anyone quotes a percentage. Use this script: 'Which legal entity would bid, and who can authorize its representations? Which agencies, municipalities, NAICS codes, and service territories match work you already perform? What licenses, certifications, past-performance records, staffing, insurance, and working capital can you document? Which tasks will your employees perform, and which suppliers would perform the rest? Are there referral payments, competing bidder relationships, former-official relationships, or restricted information we should identify before accepting the engagement? Our next step is a written qualification worksheet and priced service scope, not an award prediction.' Capture answers as client statements until verified, and assign missing evidence to a named owner with a date. For supplier discussions, use: 'We are coordinating a possible bid for a client. Please confirm your proposed work, delivery capacity, license status, price validity, and any conflicts. No award or subcontract is committed by this conversation; any commitment requires authorized written terms.' This separates P2 qualification from P5 promises and gives P6 a usable checklist. Reject requests to backdate commitments, lend an eligibility label, or fabricate project references even when the submission deadline is hours away.

Government-facing outreach must follow the solicitation's channel and timing. Use this script only when that channel permits the contact: 'We support a prospective offeror reviewing the published solicitation. Please confirm the designated process for submitting clarification questions and receiving public amendments. We are not requesting competitor information or evaluation deliberations. Our technical question concerns the stated deliverable and will be submitted through the published channel by the stated deadline.' For pre-solicitation market research, identify the client and purpose accurately, then ask whether capability materials are being accepted. FAR 15.201 encourages appropriate exchanges, but it does not authorize private access to protected information or override procurement-specific instructions. Log the recipient, client authorization, exact message, response, and any required follow-up. The bid lead owns the shared deadline calendar; the procurement lead owns conflicts and exceptions; the three contractors receive task-specific access rather than the entire client portfolio. Automate alerts, calendar reminders, and checklist completeness checks, while retaining human review of eligibility and certifications. At Scaled, add the third employee and fourth contractor to support review capacity, not indiscriminate outreach. Measure overdue questions, amendment acknowledgment, delivery acceptance, and receivable age before increasing opportunity volume.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Open a jurisdiction and authority record
   - Assign the procurement lead to identify buyer, funding source, clauses, and authorized contacts.
   - Attach current solicitation versions, state purchasing requirements, licensing checks, and the client's written authority before contractors receive substantive work.
2. Review compensation and performance structure
   - Route fees, conflicts, set-aside calculations, and supplier arrangements to the specialist reviewer.
   - Record the governing rule and resulting decision for each affected play; revise prohibited structures without stopping unrelated authorized research.
3. Execute scoped services and controlled communications
   - Have the bid lead issue work orders, approved scripts, calendar assignments, and access permissions.
   - The client retains certification authority. Every submission package includes an amendment check, deliverable manifest, and documented client review.
4. Close delivery evidence and reconcile service revenue
   - Record acceptance, earned fees, receivables, supplier exceptions, and retention obligations.
   - Exclude client award value from desk revenue, reconcile costs to the canonical scenario, and revoke access when each contractor's assignment ends.
