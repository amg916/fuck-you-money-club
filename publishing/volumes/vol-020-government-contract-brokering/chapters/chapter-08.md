## 1. Build an authoritative source directory around seven billable plays  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Give Candidate #4 seven explicit operating lanes before buying subscriptions: P1 solicitation discovery, P2 award intelligence, P3 prime qualification, P4 supplier discovery, P5 lawful teaming, P6 bid control, and P7 post-award monitoring. These are this chapter's operational mapping, not seven separate revenue forecasts. P1 finds actionable notices; P2 identifies buying patterns; P3 checks whether a proposed prime can actually perform; P4 develops supplier alternatives; P5 documents a permitted relationship; P6 controls submissions; P7 tracks delivery and payment dependencies. Assign the first full-time employee to capture and client decisions and the second to data operations and bid calendars. Use three scoped contractors for procurement review, supplier diligence, and proposal or delivery support. Contractor count does not imply five full-time salaries. Record hours, turnaround commitments, and coverage explicitly. Every source must serve a lane, produce a named artifact, and have an escalation owner. A notice without a responsible reviewer is merely inventory. An award record without transaction interpretation is potentially misleading. A vendor profile without entity verification is a lead, not a qualified partner. Start with two agency families and one municipality so the five-person pod can inspect evidence rather than spend its week maintaining connectors.

Use the directory below as a source register, not as a promise of universal API access. Cost entries distinguish free public records from separately priced delivery, analytics, and account features. Refresh entries describe desk retrieval targets or publication dependence, not provider service commitments. Check current documentation and account entitlements when configuring each connector; numeric quotas and commercial prices can change. SAM.gov opportunity records support discovery, while the issuing office's solicitation documents and amendments establish the working requirements. USAspending supports spending analysis, but obligations, outlays, ceilings, and modifications answer different questions. Municipal notices often point into another portal for attachments, registration, or submission. Preserve that chain instead of treating the first search result as the complete procurement. A published notice may arrive after a portal update, and a vendor email may arrive after both. Store publication time, retrieval time, and the source's own update field separately. Never manufacture a source timestamp when only retrieval time exists. For an active pursuit, subscribe to official notifications and inspect the issuing portal on the bid calendar's schedule. The directory's fallback is an alternate retrieval route, not authority to override newer instructions or extend a deadline.

Federal and municipal evidence require different identifiers. Carry the federal notice identifier, solicitation number, agency hierarchy, office, NAICS, product or service code, and place of performance as distinct fields. Link awards using the award identifier plus awarding agency and relevant parent award identifiers; a short award number alone can collide. For entities, preserve UEI, CAGE when applicable, legal name, address, and registration status without assuming every municipal supplier has federal identifiers. Keep municipal vendor and solicitation identifiers in their own namespaces. Use Census NAICS definitions for classification interpretation, but do not replace the solicitation's assigned code with a classifier's preferred label. P2 research can combine USAspending transactions with source award notices to identify repeat buyers and incumbent concentration. It cannot establish an incumbent's actual profit or the government's next buying decision. P3 and P4 can combine SAM registration, exclusions, SBA profiles, and supplier documents, but each proves a different proposition. A registry entry does not establish available capacity. A certification claim needs program-specific verification. Record the question each source answers so analysts stop turning one credible fact into several unsupported conclusions during qualification meetings and client-facing opportunity reviews.

## 2. Run an evidence-first enrichment waterfall with reversible joins  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Implement the enrichment waterfall as a sequence of questions, not a sequence of vendors. First retrieve the authoritative notice and its attachments. Second resolve the issuing office, deadline, submission channel, set-aside designation, and required qualifications. Third identify likely suppliers from existing client records, official registrations, and program directories. Fourth enrich unresolved corporate relationships through a licensed commercial source. Fifth ask the supplier for evidence that public records cannot establish: current production capacity, insurance, delivery commitments, pricing validity, and available personnel. Stop enrichment when the next field cannot change a pursuit decision. The data operator owns collection, the capture lead owns interpretation, and the relevant contractor reviews exceptions within a defined service window. Keep the original value, normalized value, source locator, retrieval timestamp, and matching rationale for every material field. Label deterministic identifier matches separately from name-and-address candidates. Never merge two entities solely because a commercial database assigns a shared domain. Parent companies, operating subsidiaries, and joint ventures can carry different registrations and obligations. Make merges reversible with an explicit linkage record rather than overwriting source rows. This lets P3 qualification and P4 discovery share data without turning uncertain research into an asserted legal identity.

A small stdlib-based pipeline can begin with urllib.request for permitted HTTP requests, json for payloads, sqlite3 for structured records, hashlib for attachment fingerprints, and logging for execution events. Keep downloaded evidence outside the database and store its content hash, byte length, media type, and authorized storage reference. Save retrieval parameters without API secrets. Use an append-only raw layer, a normalized layer, and a decision layer containing human assessments. Fetch overlapping update windows because late publication and corrected records can defeat a simple last-seen timestamp. Deduplicate using source-specific keys and content versions, not titles. Advance a retrieval checkpoint only after every page in that window has been stored and validated. Retry network failures and appropriate server errors with bounded exponential backoff and jitter; honor Retry-After when supplied. Authentication errors require credential or permission review, not repeated retries. Reject unexpected HTML login pages masquerading as successful downloads. Store provider quotas as connector configuration instead of assuming one universal request budget. Keep the scheduler modest enough that the second full-time employee can understand every failed job and restore essential bid-calendar coverage manually during an outage without depending on an unavailable specialist.

Treat dates, money, and amendments as separate normalization problems. Preserve the deadline exactly as published, its stated timezone, and an explicitly verified UTC conversion. Python 3.9 zoneinfo can handle named zones when timezone data is available; never silently substitute the workstation's local zone. Escalate ambiguous abbreviations to the issuing office through the permitted question channel. Store monetary values using decimal.Decimal during transformation and exact decimal text or integer minor units in SQLite. Keep award ceilings, obligations, deobligations, and desk service fees in different fields. Version solicitation attachments independently from notice metadata because either can change alone. For P6, an amendment event must create a review task, identify affected requirements, and record whether acknowledgment is required. For P7, supplier milestones and invoice events should come from the authorized client's records, with public award data serving as context rather than a receivables ledger. Restrict client files by project and avoid copying sensitive proposal content into broad research indexes. Public access does not eliminate purpose limitations or contractual restrictions. Retain the smallest useful contact dataset, prefer role-based procurement contacts, and document deletion rules for expired research copies while preserving required bid evidence under the client engagement's retention schedule.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture a bounded evidence window
   - Retrieve configured sources and preserve raw responses before transformation.
   - Record source, query, retrieval time, pagination position, response status, and content hashes; exclude credentials from logs and exported evidence.
2. Normalize without erasing source meaning
   - Resolve identifiers and extract deadlines, classifications, amounts, and attachment versions.
   - Keep original values beside normalized values; quarantine ambiguous identity matches, deadline conversions, and unrecognized payload shapes for review.
3. Review changed decision fields
   - Route material changes to the capture lead and the appropriate scoped contractor.
   - Prioritize deadline, scope, eligibility, workshare, quotation-expiry, and acknowledgment changes; record reviewer, evidence reference, and disposition.
4. Publish and reconcile the desk queue
   - Update authorized client calendars and confirm the retrieval window completed.
   - Advance checkpoints only after complete storage and validation; reconcile unresolved tasks against upcoming deadlines and retain a manual source-check fallback.

## 3. Diagnose silent failures before they become missed bids or bad structures  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Measure source health separately from business activity. A job that returns HTTP 200 can still retrieve a login page, an empty result caused by a changed filter, or only the first page of a large response. Maintain connector tests for expected media type, required keys, date parsing, page coverage, and attachment accessibility. Compare counts with recent comparable windows, but investigate deviations rather than declaring every quiet holiday an outage. Use a small fixture set containing known amendments, multiple pages, duplicate notices, negative award adjustments, and missing optional fields. Python unittest can exercise these cases without making live requests. urllib.error distinguishes HTTP and network failures; json identifies malformed payloads; sqlite3 queries expose duplicate identifiers and orphaned attachments. Optional workstation tools such as curl and jq accelerate diagnosis but are not dependencies of the stdlib pipeline. Never paste API keys, client proposal material, or restricted attachments into public debugging services. Save redacted request metadata so another authorized operator can reproduce a failure. The useful incident report states which sources and pursuit windows were affected, which manual checks replaced automation, and whether any client deadline or requirement decision remained unverified after recovery.

Apply procurement rules to the proposed service and relationship, not merely to the data source. FAR Subpart 3.4 and FAR 52.203-5 address contingent-fee arrangements, with defined exceptions that require factual review; calling a payment a consulting fee does not settle its treatment. 41 U.S.C. 2102 restricts disclosure and obtaining of covered bid, proposal, and source-selection information before award. A person offering an internal evaluation spreadsheet is not providing ordinary public-record enrichment. Under 41 U.S.C. 8702, kickbacks connected to covered prime contracts and subcontracts are prohibited. Structure P5 introductions and supplier payments accordingly, with a documented service scope and no hidden compensation for favorable treatment. FAR 52.215-23, where included, addresses excessive pass-through charges; 13 CFR 125.6 establishes limitations on subcontracting for covered set-aside arrangements. Evaluate applicable exceptions, contract type, similarly situated entities, performance period, and solicitation clauses rather than applying a universal percentage to every procurement. Municipal transactions require their own local rules and solicitation terms. The procurement-review contractor documents these determinations, while the capture lead ensures that the supplier list, workshare model, and fee agreement reflect the same facts before a recommendation reaches the client.

Use adversarial operational tests against your own workflow without probing agency systems beyond authorized access. Change a stored deadline in a test fixture and confirm that the calendar receives a review task rather than an unreviewed overwrite. Replace an attachment with different bytes under the same filename and confirm the hash comparison raises an amendment investigation. Introduce two suppliers with similar names and different UEIs, then confirm that enrichment keeps them separate. Simulate throttling, expired credentials, an incomplete final page, and a timezone conversion failure. In each case, the pipeline should retain the last verified record, mark freshness accurately, and identify the person responsible for manual coverage. Do not label stale records current because the scheduler ran successfully. Give P6 deadlines higher operational priority than P2 historical research when capacity is constrained. For P7, simulate a supplier milestone slipping while the public award record stays unchanged; the client-facing exception must still appear. Review incidents weekly with both full-time employees and only the contractor needed for the issue. Close an incident after a reproducible test passes, affected pursuits are reconciled, and the revised procedure has a clear owner rather than after an error message disappears.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight verifications before publishing a pursuit recommendation**
> 
> A passed technical job is insufficient. The capture lead records these four checks against the specific pursuit.
> - Verify the current solicitation packet, every located amendment, the operative deadline and timezone, and the designated submission channel directly against the issuing source.
> - Verify the proposed prime's identity, relevant registration and certification evidence, and exclusions findings; investigate potential matches instead of inferring eligibility from a name search.
> - Verify fee terms, supplier compensation, required workshare, and subcontracting structure against applicable clauses and program rules with the procurement-review contractor.
> - Verify complete retrieval coverage, acknowledged material changes, supplier quotation validity, and a named backup operator before the recommendation enters the client queue.

## 4. Fund the tooling from service economics and protect five-person capacity  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Keep Candidate #4's scorecard intact when justifying the stack. Conservative recognized service revenue is $120,000, with a 15% net margin and $18,000 annual owner net profit; implied total annual costs are $102,000. Base revenue is $560,000, with a 22.5% net margin and $126,000 owner profit; implied costs are $434,000. Scaled revenue is $1,000,000, with a 30% net margin and $300,000 owner profit; implied costs are $700,000. Conservative and Base use two full-time employees plus three contractors. Scaled uses three full-time employees plus four contractors, seven people total. Those are canonical cases, not outputs of this chapter's connector design. Client contract ceilings, obligations, subcontract values, and supplier invoices never become desk revenue merely because the pipeline tracks them. Recognize only the desk's earned service fees under its actual engagement terms. The supplied monthly operating-expense range of $5,000 to $25,000 is a planning range, not a substitute for each case's complete annual cost bridge. Separate recurring overhead, direct delivery labor, variable contractor costs, and other expenses so the scorecard's margins reconcile without double counting or pretending that every cost belongs in one monthly overhead bucket.

Evaluate paid intelligence through a bounded test before adding another recurring obligation. An illustrative $400 monthly subscription plus $100 for incremental storage and monitoring totals $6,000 annually; these are desk planning assumptions, not vendor quotations. If verified automation saves ten contractor hours each month at an avoidable $60 hourly cost, the annual benefit is $7,200 and the direct contribution is $1,200 before implementation and review costs. Saved salaried time creates capacity, not automatic cash savings. Count it separately through faster qualified briefs or fewer overdue reviews. Keep this test inside the existing scenario cost envelope; otherwise the published margin must change, so it cannot be presented as the canonical case. Use the $25,000 to $150,000 startup capital range to plan setup and runway against the six-to-eighteen-month time to first deal. Neither range proves that the smallest capital budget covers the longest delay. At $5,000 monthly operating expense, six months already consumes $30,000 before setup costs or additional delivery spending. Favor engagement structures with earned, documented service milestones where appropriate, and review contingent-fee restrictions before linking compensation to awards. A discovery platform cannot cure payment delays or a fee arrangement that the procurement structure does not permit.

Manage the pod through exception volume, not the number of scraped records. The data operator publishes a morning source-health report and a deduplicated change queue. The capture lead reviews deadline and eligibility changes first, then assigns supplier and proposal exceptions to the relevant contractors. Batch lower priority incumbent research into a weekly P2 review. Measure collection freshness, missed amendments, unresolved identity matches, qualified briefs per reviewer hour, supplier quotation expiry, and milestone exceptions. Set internal service targets from actual staffing coverage, distinguishing those targets from agency publication timing and portal availability. Track the fraction of recommendations with complete evidence lineage; a high alert count can hide poor decision quality. Review paid subscriptions monthly against verified decision use, not logins. Retire connectors that produce no actionable evidence, while preserving the authoritative manual fallback. Expand into another municipality only after existing active pursuits have dependable coverage and a tested backup operator. At the Scaled case, assign added capacity to documented bottlenecks such as supplier verification or bid review rather than assuming more sources produce proportionally more fees. The durable advantage is a shorter, auditable path from public evidence to an executable client decision, with supplier failure and payment uncertainty still visible in the operating queue.
