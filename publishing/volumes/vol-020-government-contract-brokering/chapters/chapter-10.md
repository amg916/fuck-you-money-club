## 1. Turn seven operating plays into one evidence-linked work queue  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Design the desk around seven mapped operating plays rather than seven disconnected software subscriptions. The chapter's operational mapping is solicitation intelligence, qualification, lawful teaming, bid production, fulfillment evidence, service-fee collections, and partner distribution; these labels organize delivery without adding new scorecard assumptions. Assign the first full-time person as capture lead and the second as operations lead. Allocate the three contractors to procurement review, proposal production, and supplier verification, with purchased hours and backup coverage recorded explicitly. Contractor headcount does not mean unlimited availability. Every opportunity receives a stable identifier, buyer, jurisdiction, notice type, solicitation number, source URL, retrieval timestamp, document digest, deadline, assigned reviewer, and next action. Keep opportunity records separate from client engagements and supplier records: one procurement can produce several client discussions but should never become several supposed awards. Use SQLite for a single-writer pilot and a managed relational store when concurrent workers justify migration. A shared queue, not a chat transcript, determines ownership. The capture lead owns pursuit decisions; the operations lead owns queue health and evidence completeness. This division keeps the five-person pod from silently delegating binding commitments to whichever contractor happens to receive an alert.

Build the ingestion worker with Python urllib.request, json, hashlib, sqlite3, and zoneinfo. Query the SAM.gov Opportunities API using documented filters, pagination, and account-specific limits; obtain credentials through the operator's authorized account and keep them outside prompts and logs. Treat municipal Bonfire, OpenGov Procurement, and BidNet Direct feeds as separate connectors because access methods and attachment permissions vary. Where no authorized machine interface exists, route portal email into a review inbox and let a person retrieve restricted material. Never bypass authentication to improve coverage. Store each original document before extracting fields, then deduplicate on jurisdiction, solicitation identifier, and document digest rather than title alone. A changed digest creates an amendment event even when the title remains identical. Polling workers emit versioned events into an outbox; a dispatcher posts them to the calendar and task service using authenticated webhooks. Validate provider signatures, reject stale delivery timestamps, and enforce unique event identifiers. Persist the event before acknowledging delivery so a process restart cannot erase an amendment. Limit document size and isolate parsing because public procurement attachments remain untrusted input.

Measure the 70%+ target against repetitive labor minutes, not total headcount, award dollars, or the percentage of records touched by software. Establish a matched baseline across research, qualification preparation, fulfillment evidence indexing, and acquisition-list preparation. In an illustrative sample, those tasks previously consumed 1,000 active minutes; automation removes 780 minutes but introduces 60 minutes of exception handling, leaving 280 minutes and a 72% net reduction. Count the exception minutes even when contractors perform them. Keep final bid approval, supplier judgment, and client commitments outside the automation claim unless their actual repetitive components were separately timed. Instrument each run with task category, source version, outcome, reviewer minutes, and correction reason. Compare similar document lengths and notice types so an easy week does not masquerade as a productivity gain. Require stable results across four weekly samples before changing staffing assumptions. A sample that saves 650 minutes after rework supports 65%, and the dashboard must say so. Use the recovered capacity for additional qualified pursuits and faster amendment reviews before promising more clients. The seven-play design scales only when each additional pursuit has an accountable human owner.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture and fingerprint the authoritative source
   - Persist the notice, attachments, retrieval time, and SHA-256 digests before extraction.
   - Assign the jurisdiction and solicitation identifier; retain separate versions whenever content changes.
2. Extract into a constrained opportunity record
   - Request schema-constrained fields with supporting page references and source excerpts.
   - Reject unsupported dates, missing evidence, oversized responses, and fields outside the permitted schema.
3. Route consequential differences to the responsible person
   - Create review tasks for deadlines, eligibility, pricing, certifications, and supplier commitments.
   - Keep dependent drafts blocked until the assigned reviewer records a disposition against the current source digest.
4. Publish internal tasks and reconcile delivery
   - Dispatch calendar and checklist events through the durable outbox and verify acknowledgments.
   - Reconcile open events daily; preserve a manual operating sheet for service interruptions and urgent amendments.

## 2. Use LLMs for evidence extraction while people control procurement representations  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Place the LLM behind a narrow extraction contract rather than giving it authority over the bid desk. Supply public solicitation text, an explicit field schema, and instructions to return source excerpts for every consequential assertion. Procurement attachments can contain malicious instructions or merely confusing boilerplate; treat both as document content, never as executable directions. The model receives no portal password, signing authority, or ability to email a contracting officer. Validate JSON types, allowable enumerations, deadline syntax, and excerpt presence before displaying results. Preserve the distinction between a requirement, an evaluation factor, an optional attachment, and background narrative. A missing performance location should create a reviewer task, not a plausible location invented from the agency address. Use deterministic checks for attachment counts and required forms, then reserve model work for clause classification and cross-document comparison. Benchmark the extractor on a frozen set of reviewed solicitations containing amendments, scanned schedules, and conflicting dates. Track false clearances separately from harmless extra review tasks: declaring a bidder eligible when evidence is absent is more costly than asking a reviewer to inspect an additional page.

Qualification must test the proposed transaction, not simply find the word subcontractor. FAR 52.203-5 restricts contingent-fee arrangements where the clause applies, subject to its defined exceptions; labeling a success payment a consulting retainer does not change its substance. Prefer documented fees for actual research, proposal, and administration services, and have the procurement reviewer examine compensation structure before engagement. Procurement integrity under 41 U.S.C. 2102 and FAR 3.104 requires rejecting protected bid, proposal, or source-selection information received outside authorized channels. Anti-kickback controls under 41 U.S.C. 8702 require scrutiny of supplier referral payments, gifts, and credits intended to obtain or reward favorable treatment. For covered set-asides, evaluate FAR 52.219-14 by contract category and the treatment of similarly situated entities; do not apply one universal subcontracting percentage. Where applicable, FAR 52.215-23 addresses excessive pass-through charges, making an empty intermediary structure especially fragile. Municipal work requires its own ordinance and solicitation review rather than an assumption that federal clauses govern. Automation assembles these questions with citations; the reviewer resolves applicability, and the authorized prime signs its own representations.

Make the bid calendar a dependency graph with explicit evidence locks. An amendment affecting delivery dates should reopen supplier availability, pricing validity, staffing assumptions, and the draft schedule instead of merely changing a calendar entry. Generate internal reminders at seven days, three days, and one day when the procurement timeline permits, with an earlier cutoff for uploads requiring large attachments. Capture the solicitation's actual submission mechanism: portal upload, sealed package, and authorized email are different workflows. The proposal contractor can assemble a checklist from reviewed clauses, but the capture lead verifies every required attachment, signature, acknowledgment, and price schedule against the latest source version. Require supplier quotations to identify scope, exclusions, validity period, lead time, and the person authorized to commit resources. No model-generated capability statement becomes a factual claim without supporting evidence. For restricted client material, use only processing environments approved for that data category and contractual obligations; otherwise keep extraction local or manual. The final submission remains a deliberate human action, followed by receipt capture and an independent check that the submitted files match the approved package.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before any consequential release**
> 
> Extraction can run unattended; binding representations and external commitments require named review against current evidence.
> - Verify the latest solicitation and every amendment by digest; reconcile closing time, timezone, submission channel, and required acknowledgments.
> - Have the procurement reviewer examine contingent compensation, information provenance, referral benefits, and jurisdiction-specific restrictions before engagement.
> - Confirm the prime's eligibility, applicable subcontracting limits, supplier capacity, and actual intermediary services before presenting the structure.
> - Require the authorized signer to approve factual claims and the final file manifest; capture and independently check the submission receipt.

## 3. Price automated runs and recover failures without breaking the scorecard  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Price an automated run as a bounded document-processing attempt, not an entire successful procurement. For an illustrative planning rate, 6,000 input tokens at $2 per million cost $0.012, and 1,500 output tokens at $12 per million cost $0.018. Add $0.020 for allocated compute, storage, and event delivery plus a $0.010 retry reserve: total direct cost is $0.060 per run. These are budgeting assumptions rather than a vendor quote; substitute the contracted model tariff and measured token usage before purchasing capacity. At 3,000 runs monthly, direct processing costs $180. If 90 runs require eight minutes of exception review at a loaded $60 hourly rate, that review costs $720, producing $900 combined expense or $0.30 per run. Final bid reviews, software subscriptions, connector maintenance, ordinary fulfillment labor, and sales work remain additional expenses. Log those costs separately to prevent a cheap extraction metric from concealing an expensive desk. Route long documents through section selection and digest-based caching, but invalidate cached decisions when relevant source content changes. Cost controls must never suppress a material amendment merely because the monthly token budget is nearly spent.

Reconcile the operating model to recognized service fees, not the face value of client awards. Conservative revenue is $120,000 at a 15% net margin, leaving $18,000 annual owner net profit and $102,000 implied annual expenses. Base revenue is $560,000 at 22.5%, leaving $126,000 profit and $434,000 expenses. Scaled revenue is $1,000,000 at 30%, leaving $300,000 profit and $700,000 expenses. Illustrative fee arithmetic is twelve $10,000 completed service packages, twenty-eight $20,000 packages, and forty $25,000 packages respectively; actual recognition follows the engagement's earned-service terms, not invoice issuance alone. These are alternative annual scenarios, not additive revenue streams. Conservative and Base retain 2 FT + 3 C; Scaled requires 3 FT + 4 C. The Conservative expense ceiling is tight for that staffing label, so explicitly budget compensation, contractor hours, and owner labor rather than pretending headcount establishes affordability. The stated $5,000–$25,000 monthly operating range is a planning envelope, not permission to exclude delivery labor from scenario expenses. Startup capital of $20,000–$90,000 and a 6–18 month first-deal window demand a separate cash runway schedule before hiring or committing to annual software contracts.

Fulfillment automation begins after scope and responsibility are settled. Create an evidence register for supplier milestones, inspection records, delivery confirmations, client acceptance, and the service desk's own completed work. Keep client goods and subcontractor obligations separate from the operator's service-fee ledger. The collections play drafts invoices from reviewed milestones, reconciles receipts, and alerts the operations lead to disputes; it does not charge clients for a model's unsupported completion inference. Federal prompt-payment rules do not automatically determine when a private client owes this desk, so monitor the actual engagement terms and cash aging. Supplier failure requires a named escalation path, documented alternatives, and buyer or prime authorization where changes require it. For technical failures, set connector timeouts, bounded retries, and a circuit breaker that stops repeated processing of a corrupt attachment. Preserve a daily export of active pursuits, deadlines, and evidence links so the pod can operate manually during an outage. Restore from tested backups, replay idempotent events, and reconcile every external action before restarting automatic dispatch. A recovered database is insufficient if the calendar still contains superseded deadlines or invoices were transmitted twice.

## 4. Build distribution loops from reviewed procurement intelligence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build programmatic distribution from the same reviewed opportunity records used by the desk, but publish a deliberately smaller public dataset. A useful page serves a specific buyer problem: municipal facilities maintenance bids in one region, upcoming federal laboratory supply opportunities, or a supplier-readiness checklist for one contract category. Include authoritative links, last-checked timestamps, closing dates, and a clear explanation of what the service desk actually provides. Do not republish client pricing, restricted attachments, or another bidder's capability evidence. Generate drafts automatically, then have the capture lead approve substantive pages before first publication and material revisions. Expire closed notices visibly and distinguish opportunity summaries from official government instructions. Thin location pages containing identical text will not create a durable acquisition advantage. Instead, combine verified local portal coverage with original comparisons of submission mechanics and supplier preparation requirements. Track indexed pages, qualified inquiries, accepted engagements, and earned fees by source. Impressions are diagnostic, not revenue. One accurate niche page that generates a service-ready client can outperform hundreds of automatically published pages that attract suppliers unable to meet the underlying requirements.

Create a permissioned sharing loop around supplier usefulness rather than promised influence. After a reviewed shortlist is delivered, offer the recipient a shareable public digest and a separate invitation link for colleagues who want future notices. The recipient chooses whether to share; the system never uploads their address book. Attribute referrals with campaign identifiers that reveal no private client relationship. Partner with relevant APEX Accelerators, trade associations, and complementary proposal specialists through educational resources and transparent service descriptions; never imply government endorsement. For commercial outbound email, use accurate headers and subjects, identify the sender, provide a valid postal address and working opt-out, and honor suppression requirements under 15 U.S.C. 7704. Screen business contacts against the desk's suppression list before any send. Keep outreach specific: identify the public notice, explain a documented capability fit, and invite a conversation rather than asserting award likelihood. The capture lead approves recipient criteria and message templates before dispatch. Referral compensation requires the same contingent-fee and anti-kickback analysis as other compensation; a marketing label does not remove procurement risk.

Close the flywheel with evidence from delivery, not vanity engagement. A completed qualification checklist improves the next supplier intake; an amendment postmortem improves the extraction test set; an accepted service milestone supports a client-approved case study; a helpful public digest attracts another properly matched supplier. These connections join all seven plays without granting software independent authority to pursue, certify, submit, or commit resources. Run a weekly forty-five-minute pod review covering feed freshness, disputed deadlines, false clearances, supplier exceptions, unpaid service fees, and acquisition quality. Give each defect one owner and one measurable repair. Track qualified-lead conversion and service contribution by channel, then throttle distribution when the human review queue exceeds capacity. For example, stop new campaign sends when unresolved material amendments threaten active submissions; keep essential client notifications running. Add the Scaled case's third full-time person and fourth contractor only after earned service demand and cash coverage support that capacity. The durable advantage is a growing library of verified requirements, capable suppliers, and trustworthy operating evidence, not a larger pile of unreviewed model output.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Make procurement intelligence useful enough to circulate**
> 
> The strongest distribution asset saves a supplier real preparation time while preserving source accuracy and client confidentiality.
> - Maintain a municipal portal coverage ledger showing last successful retrieval; a quiet connector can mean broken access rather than absent opportunities.
> - Publish amendment deltas with source links instead of repeatedly sending entire notices; suppliers notice the specific change affecting price or delivery.
> - Ask suppliers to refresh quotation validity and lead times before a pursuit becomes urgent; stale supplier evidence destroys otherwise efficient bid assembly.
> - Turn accepted service milestones into client-approved case studies that describe the desk's work and earned fees without claiming credit for an entire award.
