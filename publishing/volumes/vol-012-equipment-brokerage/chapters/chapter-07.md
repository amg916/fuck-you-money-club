## 1. Build the evidence packet before publishing an executable offer  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start each mandate with an asset identity, an authorized seller, and an explicit commission contract. Capture manufacturer, model, serial photograph, engine or spindle hours, location, accessory inventory, ownership entity, and the person empowered to sign. A machine advertised on MachineryTrader or Surplus Record is a sourcing lead, not proof that the advertiser can convey it. Normalize manufacturer and serial into one asset key; retain original photographs and documents under versioned object references. The broker's intake score measures commercial readiness only: asset fit, reachable authority contact, buyer fit, and service records each contribute twenty-five points. A perfect score does not establish ownership, condition, export eligibility, or clear title. Record fee percentage, fee payer, earning event, payment deadline, and treatment of inspection failures before introducing buyers. At a $250,000 sale and five percent commission, the broker earns $12,500 only when the contracted earning event occurs. The $250,000 belongs to the equipment transaction, not broker revenue. Assign one employee as deal owner and use three contractors for inspection, transport coordination, and specialist document work. Contractors receive scoped tasks, deadlines, and evidence requirements rather than broad authority to change price, release funds, or promise equipment performance.

Clear ownership and liens through the seller's actual legal identity, not merely the machine's yard address. Under UCC sections 9-301 and 9-307, the debtor's location generally determines the governing perfection jurisdiction, subject to important exceptions. Search the appropriate filing office using the debtor's exact name, inspect relevant amendments, and investigate certificate-of-title rules for titled equipment or vehicles. A clean serial search alone misses blanket security interests. Obtain a lender payoff letter identifying the covered assets, good-through date, per-diem accrual, wire instructions, and release mechanics. UCC section 9-315 explains why an unauthorized sale can leave a security interest attached; section 9-513 addresses termination statements but does not make every termination an asset-specific release. Independently call a previously established lender number to verify settlement instructions. Commission an equipment-class inspection: cold start and undercarriage measurements for excavators, spindle tests and controller alarms for CNC machines, and diagnostic reports for fleet vehicles. Preserve raw findings, not just a pass label. If the seller refuses inspection or the lender cannot document release, suspend the listing or terminate the mandate before spending more on freight reservations and buyer acquisition.

Translate the evidence packet into a controlled offer and settlement instruction set. The seller's purchase agreement should identify the exact machine, included tooling, inspection rights, acceptance procedure, risk-of-loss allocation, and loading responsibilities. Express descriptions can matter under UCC section 2-313; warranty exclusions require the specificity and presentation addressed by section 2-316. Do not transform an inspector's limited observations into a broker promise of future uptime. For a fleet transaction, determine whether the activity triggers a state vehicle-dealer license; California Vehicle Code section 11700 illustrates why truck brokerage and stationary CNC brokerage need different classification work. For exports, record equipment classification, destination, end user, end use, and the applicable EAR analysis before arranging shipment. Use a suitable independent escrow provider with verified instructions, rather than routing purchase principal through the broker's operating account. Release conditions should connect cleared buyer funds, lender payoff, asset release, agreed acceptance, and commission disbursement. Document exceptions in signed amendments. When an inspection changes the price, update the commission calculation and settlement statement together; an informal discount in email must not leave an obsolete fee in the accounting system or an inconsistent payoff amount with escrow.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Identify and contract
   - Create one normalized asset record and execute the commission mandate.
   - Record seller authority, price, fee payer, earning trigger, and document references before buyer introductions.
2. Verify and price
   - Complete independent condition review and jurisdiction-specific lien research.
   - Resolve exceptions with written repricing, asset-specific release instructions, or a documented withdrawal.
3. Settle through controlled instructions
   - Match escrow conditions to acceptance, payoff, release, and commission disbursement.
   - Authenticate beneficiary changes outside the requesting email channel and retain the signed final statement.
4. Recognize and reconcile
   - Post the earned commission once and match actual bank receipts.
   - Separate equipment principal from broker cash, resolve receivable differences, and close the evidence packet.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Four habits that protect equipment commissions**
> 
> Prevent expensive disputes at intake rather than negotiating around missing evidence at settlement.
> - Request the controller's alarm-history export before scheduling a CNC demonstration; a warm machine and cleared screen can conceal intermittent faults.
> - Photograph serial plates together with surrounding machine features, then compare those photographs with inspection and pickup evidence.
> - Obtain lender payoff per-diem figures before choosing a closing date; a delayed wire can otherwise leave a small unpaid balance blocking release.
> - Ask the rigger for written loading assumptions, floor restrictions, disconnect scope, and insurance evidence before promising a pickup window.

## 2. Implement six serialized modules for evidence, events, and the commission ledger  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The reference below uses Python's standard sqlite3 module and one local SQLite database. Copy the six code cells into one script in their displayed order, or load them sequentially into the same namespace. Initialize the schema before starting workers; each worker opens its own connection with identical pragmas and never shares a connection between threads. SQLite must live on storage with reliable local locking, not a casually shared network filesystem. Every business mutation enters BEGIN IMMEDIATE before reading decision state. That serializes competing writers and closes the read-check-write race around state advancement, duplicate commission creation, and receipt allocation. A busy worker may receive a lock timeout; its caller retries the same event identifier, never a newly minted identifier. The event receipt stores the complete canonical request and committed response. Replaying the same request returns that response; changing the payload while retaining its identifier raises an error. Database uniqueness provides a second defense for intake keys, active assets, invoices, earned-fee postings, and bank references. Restrict database file access to the application account and backups. These guarantees govern this single-database write path, not arbitrary direct edits, external escrow systems, or uncoordinated application copies operating separate databases.

The state machine separates commercial readiness from verified evidence. An intake record can advance to VERIFIED only after authority, asset identity, condition, lien, and export checks exist. ESCROW_READY additionally requires verified escrow instructions. Issuing the commission invoice requires delivery or acceptance evidence satisfying the mandate's earning condition, and all required checks must be current at that transaction. The reference uses a seven-day freshness window as an explicit operating assumption; a live payoff may require a much shorter deadline. Replace that window with document-specific expiry rules before handling such transactions. Evidence records are append-only in this minimal model, and reviewers submit their authenticated identity through the application. The script records that identity but does not authenticate it. Corrections, refunds, reversals, partial asset releases, and changed mandates require additional versioned workflows; do not bypass constraints to improvise them. A withdrawn deal may be scrubbed before invoicing, while an invoiced deal requires a proper credit or reversal extension. Purchase principal never enters the ledger. Pair each amount with debit and credit accounts in one row, ensuring structural balance without relying on a later process to discover an orphaned journal leg.

The accounting sequence is deliberately narrow: earning creates a receivable and commission revenue; collecting debits operating cash and credits that receivable; paying an operating expense debits expense and credits cash. Amounts are positive integer cents, and the program rejects percentages that produce fractional cents instead of silently choosing a rounding policy. The recognition event requires the full contracted fee to be earned; milestone commissions need a separate schedule extension. Receipts may be partial but cannot exceed the open invoice. A unique bank reference prevents two workers from allocating the same imported bank line to different deals. Export bank transactions from the broker's own account, normalize them outside the write transaction, and post only authenticated matches. Never keep a database transaction open while calling escrow, sending email, or fetching an inspection. External notifications should follow committed receipts; add a transactional outbox and provider-supported idempotency before automating outward actions. The supplied QA module checks a consistent snapshot, but these snippets have not been executed here. Before deployment, run crash injection, simultaneous duplicate deliveries, changed-payload retries, stale-evidence attempts, and concurrent overcollection tests. Prove backups restore successfully and reconcile the restored ledger to bank statements before accepting live work.

## 3. Walk a seasoned 90-day cohort from lead scrubs to collected commission cash  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The worked cohort represents a seasoned Base operation, not a promise that a new brokerage closes nine deals in its first ninety days. Time to first deal remains two to nine months. At day zero, two earlier cohorts each contain five signed mandates undergoing diligence; neither has earned a commission or created a receivable. Three deals from the earlier cohort close in Month 1, three from the later cohort close in Month 2, and three from Month 1 intake close in Month 3. Each sold machine has a $250,000 price and a five percent contracted fee, producing $12,500 commission. Nine closings therefore represent $2,250,000 equipment transaction value but only $112,500 recognized broker revenue. Each month also brings thirty fresh leads. Eight duplicate listings, seven poor-fit machines, and five unsupported seller-authority claims are scrubbed before expensive diligence, leaving ten qualified opportunities. Five fail to secure an acceptable mandate, leaving five signed candidates. Eventually three close, one fails inspection, and one fails lien release. Track early lead scrubs separately from post-mandate losses so the team can distinguish sourcing noise from costly execution failures. Month 2 and Month 3 intake remain future pipeline at the observation cutoff.

Commission recognition and cash collection intentionally diverge during the quarter. Month 1 earns $37,500 but collects only $25,000, leaving $12,500 receivable. Month 2 earns another $37,500 and collects $37,500: $12,500 from the previous month's receivable plus $25,000 from current closings. Its ending receivable remains $12,500. Month 3 earns $37,500 and collects $50,000, comprising the prior balance plus all current fees, so ending receivables return to zero. Assume all operating costs are paid in their month, with no opening or ending expense payables. Monthly fixed overhead is $10,000, within the stated $2,500 to $14,000 monthly operating-overhead range. Another $14,375 covers variable contractor work, paid acquisition, inspection coordination, travel, and failed-deal handling, making total monthly costs $24,375. This explicitly treats the quoted overhead range as fixed operating overhead rather than the entire cost base. ## 4. Route seven plays through one revenue boundary and explicit loss fallbacks  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Seven mapped plays feed the same controlled brokerage pipeline; they are not seven independent revenue forecasts to add together. Because only one external play identifier is supplied, the first six names below are chapter-defined operating applications rather than invented catalog identifiers. Lender dispositions source motivated sellers but demand asset-specific release evidence. CNC matching uses controller type, travel envelope, spindle specifications, tooling, and power requirements to reduce irrelevant buyer introductions. Fleet breakups trade one large buyer dependency for several smaller settlements while increasing title and transport coordination. Plant relocations exploit a dated shutdown or lease deadline, but removal costs can erase apparent price advantage. Inspection-led recovery preserves a viable transaction through disclosed repricing rather than concealing defects. Buyer alerts accelerate matching only when recipients have current purchasing authority, budgets, technical requirements, and geographic constraints. Use HubSpot saved views or a small SQL matching job to select buyers, then review each introduction before sending detailed asset information. In every channel, count a lead once, an active mandate once, and a commission once. Attribute a primary sourcing channel and retain secondary touches separately so multiple contractors cannot each claim the same closed deal as incremental production or inflate the operating forecast.

The required cross-track play, ferc_grid_interconnection_queue_land_speculation, belongs to FERC / ISO Substation Interconnection Queue & Powered-Land Option Flipping, TRACK_1. Its equipment-brokerage use is adjacent demand intelligence, not a claim that the brokerage controls deliverable power. Overlay public PJM, MISO, or CAISO queue records with county parcel data, zoning, substation locations, and independently checked access constraints. Queue records frequently describe generation interconnection rather than a site's entitlement to new industrial load. Queue position, project status, and nearby infrastructure are signals requiring utility studies, service discussions, land-use review, and actual contractual rights. In a separately funded option exercise, an illustrative $3,000 nonrefundable option premium plus $2,000 diligence budget creates $5,000 direct exposure, assuming the contract permits lapse without further purchase obligation. Neither that spending nor a possible option assignment belongs in the equipment commission scorecard. If independently verified site development later produces two $250,000 equipment transactions at five percent, only their $25,000 earned commissions enter this brokerage ledger. When power or zoning fails, let contingent options lapse or reposition the land for independently viable uses without advertising secured capacity or promising an energization date that the utility has not committed to deliver.

Control scale through throughput and exception economics rather than listing volume. At the Base run rate, five signed mandates per monthly intake cohort produce three eventual closings, a sixty percent mandate-to-close rate. Losing one additional closing removes $12,500 gross commission, while much of the inspection, acquisition, and coordination cost may already be spent. Measure days from mandate to verified packet, verified packet to accepted offer, accepted offer to earned commission, and earned commission to collected cash. Keep separate reason codes for defective equipment, unsupported authority, unreleased liens, buyer withdrawal, and uneconomic transport. A weekly owner review should examine stale mandates, approaching payoff expiries, unmatched receipts, and contractors carrying excessive work in progress. Automate inventory matching and buyer alerts first because they improve search throughput without delegating title judgments or custody of funds. Expand to the six-person Scaled team only after observed pipeline and contribution economics support it; more people do not cure fraudulent inventory or lumpy commissions. Maintain a liquidity reserve for delayed closings and fund growth from collected broker fees, not anticipated equipment principal. The operating objective is a repeatable evidence-to-cash cycle with bounded loss exposure, not a dashboard full of impressive machine values and unsupported expected commissions.
