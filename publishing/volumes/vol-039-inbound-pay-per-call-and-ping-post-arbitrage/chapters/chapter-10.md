## 1. Separate the sub-second money path from the asynchronous evidence path  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Design two independent execution paths before buying additional traffic. The synchronous path accepts a Ringba or Retreaver event, validates the publisher and campaign, reads cached buyer eligibility, obtains permitted bids, and routes the call within a measured deadline. The asynchronous path retrieves evidence, processes recordings, drafts dispositions, reconciles settlement, and prepares acquisition feedback. Never put an LLM, a medical-record request, or a slow certificate fetch inside the auction deadline. An illustrative 750 millisecond budget allocates 50 milliseconds to ingress, 150 to eligibility, 450 to buyer responses, and 100 to selection and dispatch; actual vendor deadlines govern implementation. Use Python json for strict payload decoding, decimal.Decimal for money, and time.monotonic for elapsed deadlines. Verify the vendor's supported authentication mechanism; use hmac and compare_digest only where signed webhooks are actually supported. Reject stale signed requests and duplicate event identifiers. Keep a small, versioned routing policy with buyer hours, geography, vertical permissions, buffer definitions, and capacity limits. If the auction exceeds its deadline, use a previously authorized fixed-price destination or stop routing; do not silently auction the same live caller twice. The full-time owner owns destination economics, while the technical contractor maintains adapters and the quality contractor reviews exceptions.

Represent each call as an append-only sequence rather than one mutable spreadsheet row. Use distinct identifiers for publisher submission, platform call, buyer leg, evidence certificate, transcript revision, and settlement line. An ingress handler durably records the event before acknowledging it, then creates an outbox task in the same transaction. For a small pilot, sqlite3 with a single writer can implement this pattern; higher concurrency requires a database architecture tested against the actual write workload. A worker reads pending tasks, makes bounded urllib.request calls, and records results with an idempotency key. Transport retries must not create second buyer transfers or duplicate conversion uploads. Route failed tasks into a quarantine queue with error class, retry count, and next action. Retain original payloads under a restricted retention schedule, but exclude recordings, medical facts, and raw telephone numbers from routine diagnostic logs. Buyer responses should carry currency, payable event, bid expiry, and the exact definition of a qualified second. A 120-second connected call can still fail a contract requiring 90 seconds after an agent answers. Preserve the timing evidence needed to distinguish ringing, IVR, queueing, and buyer conversation instead of arguing from total recording length.

Measure automation against work removed, not webhook volume. Establish a two-week baseline of repetitive research, qualification, fulfillment, and acquisition tasks, including retries and downstream corrections. For an illustrative 1,000-task week, 780 straight-through tasks yield 78% task automation; the other 220 remain visible exceptions. If baseline work averages four minutes per task, 4,000 minutes were previously required. At three minutes per exception plus 300 minutes of sampling and maintenance, recurring effort becomes 960 minutes, a 76% time reduction. These are acceptance targets, not measured results for this module. Track both measures because a thousand easy classifications can conceal ten expensive payment disputes. Give the owner a daily exception digest ranked by dollars exposed, evidence gaps, and time remaining before dispute deadlines. The quality contractor samples clean calls as well as flagged calls, preventing the model from defining its own audit population. The technical contractor maintains replay fixtures for malformed events, expired bids, and duplicate callbacks. The first seven local play mappings below cover deterministic infrastructure; the next seven cover judgment support and distribution. These fourteen chapter implementations are not fourteen separately verified revenue lines, and their savings must never be added twice.

## 2. Use AI for evidence extraction while humans control consequential decisions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat TrustedForm and Jornaya as evidence services, not machines that manufacture permission. Capture the landing-page version, disclosure text, timestamp, consumer action, certificate identifier, intended seller, and permitted communication channel where those fields are available. Retrieve and preserve evidence according to the product's actual access and retention terms. The FCC's attempted federal one-to-one consent amendment was vacated in Insurance Marketing Coalition Ltd. v. FCC in January 2025; do not present that vacated amendment as an operative nationwide requirement. Existing TCPA rules still govern covered autodialed or artificial or prerecorded calls, and state restrictions can be broader. A consumer-initiated live inbound call does not automatically authorize later automated marketing. Use seller-specific permission as a contractual operating control where appropriate, while applying Medicare's separate TPMO data-sharing requirements when that vertical is involved. Before outbound callbacks, check applicable National and entity-specific do-not-call restrictions, revocations, calling windows, and state requirements. A commercial litigation-risk list can flag review, but neither proves consent nor replaces suppression. Fail closed on missing permission for a restricted action without unnecessarily preventing an otherwise lawful inbound service interaction. Counsel-approved rule versions belong in the eligibility cache, alongside effective dates and affected campaigns.

Build transcript disposition as constrained extraction with evidence spans. A worker downloads an authorized recording, produces a transcript through an approved service, and submits only the minimum necessary content for classification. Require a JSON object containing call identifier, proposed vertical, intent, agent-connect evidence, possible duplicate indicators, and quoted supporting spans. Validate types, enumerations, and span locations in Python before accepting the result. The model must never invent a recording timestamp, diagnose an injury, determine legal eligibility, or declare consent merely because the caller sounded cooperative. Treat transcript instructions as untrusted caller speech, not commands to the classifier. Run new prompts against a human-labeled holdout set and compare false acceptance rates by publisher, language, and vertical. Model self-confidence is not a calibrated probability; promotion requires observed error rates and stable review burden. Auto-tag routine operational categories only after validation. Route disputed qualification, suspected misrepresentation, vulnerable-consumer concerns, and payment-affecting recommendations to a reviewer. Redact sensitive facts from general model telemetry, restrict recording access, and establish deletion schedules consistent with applicable retention duties. If a provider processes protected health information for a covered entity or business associate, assess the applicable HIPAA relationship and agreement requirements before transmission.

Medicare and legal intake require vertical-specific workflows rather than a universal ninety-second qualification script. Under 42 CFR 422.2264, applicable personal marketing appointments generally require a documented Scope of Appointment at least 48 hours beforehand, subject to the regulation's exceptions. This is not a blanket rule that every unsolicited inbound service call must wait two days. Applicable TPMOs must record marketing, sales, and enrollment calls in their entirety under 42 CFR 422.2274, and individual data sharing with another TPMO requires the rule's recipient-specific written consent. Apply corresponding Part D provisions where relevant. Independently assess state recording rules and required disclosures. The quality contractor checks recordings and evidence before expanding a Medicare source; the owner reviews any appointment flow that changes timing or recipients. For legal calls, enforce jurisdiction, advertising, solicitation, runner, and fee-sharing restrictions before destination selection. An AI label cannot turn a purchased lead into a retained client. Maintain a release checklist for new verticals and rollback instructions for each policy revision. When recording fails, pause the affected regulated workflow and investigate; do not reconstruct the conversation from an LLM summary and call that a recording. Humans resolve consequential exceptions using original evidence, not merely another model response.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Ingest and preserve
   - Authenticate the callback, validate required fields, and durably record its unique identifier.
   - Commit the event and outbox task together; acknowledge only after persistence and preserve a replayable payload reference.
2. Route within policy
   - Apply cached eligibility and bounded RTB deadlines before transferring the live call.
   - Exclude expired bids, exhausted caps, missing required evidence, and destinations lacking applicable permissions; use only an authorized fallback.
3. Extract and review
   - Process authorized recordings asynchronously and validate model output against the schema.
   - Sample routine results and send consequential decisions to a named reviewer with original recordings, timing evidence, and contract versions.
4. Settle and learn
   - Match buyer statements, approve adjustments, and transmit supported acquisition feedback.
   - Reconcile collections before increasing publisher exposure; replay failed postbacks with stable identifiers and reverse values through supported mechanisms.
