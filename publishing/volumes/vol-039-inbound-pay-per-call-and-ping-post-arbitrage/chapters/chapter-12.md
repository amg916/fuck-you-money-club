## 1. Days 1-7 — Contract the Spread Before Buying Traffic  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with one emergency-trade service in one metropolitan area, rather than simultaneously entering Medicare, legal referrals, and insurance. The first-week deliverable is a runnable commercial specification, not an advertising account with unrestricted spend. Assign the full-time operator buyer negotiation, treasury, and the daily decision log; assign contractor C1 tracking and routing engineering; assign contractor C2 evidence review and settlement quality assurance. Budget approximately forty operator hours and eight hours per contractor weekly. To make the fourteen-play requirement executable, this chapter uses an explicit operational mapping rather than asserting unavailable source-play titles: P01 vertical selection, P02 buyer underwriting, P03 qualification contracts, P04 acquisition attribution, P05 permission evidence, P06 suppression controls, P07 tracking infrastructure, P08 RTB routing, P09 duration qualification, P10 fraud detection, P11 transcript disposition, P12 server-to-server feedback, P13 settlement reconciliation, and P14 cash-constrained optimization. Maintain one evidence folder and one accountable owner for each play. Two buyers provide routing resilience, but neither receives traffic until its service area, staffed hours, credit exposure, duplicate definition, and rejection timetable are written down. A quoted payout without those terms is only a sales conversation. The operator signs off on the complete specification before C1 enables any production destination.

Use an illustrative $18,000 capitalization within the supplied $8,000-$45,000 startup range. Ring-fence $8,000 as untouched reserve, $4,000 as maximum settlement working capital, and $6,000 as the maximum first-month loss allowance. Inside that allowance, cap traffic acquisition at $4,500 and setup, software, telephony, and contractor commitments at $1,500; reprice the sprint if written quotes exceed either envelope. These are pilot assumptions, not the scorecard's steady-state monthly expense projection. Track gross committed outlays before giving credit for uncollected invoices, and count publisher liabilities when incurred rather than when paid. The $4,000 float funds timing differences, not extra acquisition experiments; never count the same publisher payment in both spending categories. Stop new commitments when either the $6,000 spending cap, $4,000 receivable exposure cap, or reserve floor would be breached. Require buyer net-7 terms in writing and publisher terms that the operator can honor without collecting early. A buyer refusing a short pilot settlement cycle can remain a prospect, but cannot justify uncapped publisher intake. Expected first-deal timing remains one to three months, so Day 30 may legitimately produce a controlled Fix decision instead of revenue.

Translate permissions into specific call paths before selecting TrustedForm or Jornaya. A certificate supports evidence retention; it does not independently establish lawful outreach, authorized recording, or permission to share information with every bidder. Separate consumer-initiated inbound calls from callbacks, automated outreach, texts, transfers, and recorded conversations. Apply 47 U.S.C. § 227 and 47 CFR § 64.1200 to the actual technology and activity. The FCC's 2023 one-to-one consent amendment was vacated in Insurance Marketing Coalition Ltd. v. FCC in January 2025; do not describe it as an operative nationwide FCC requirement. Adopt named-recipient permission as a contractual control where useful, while checking existing written-consent duties independently. For Medicare work, apply the distinct TPMO personal-data sharing requirements in 42 CFR §§ 422.2274 and 423.2274, and the scope-of-appointment timing provisions in §§ 422.2264 and 423.2264, including their exceptions. A 48-hour appointment rule is not a universal inbound-call waiting period. For California legal referrals, evaluate Business and Professions Code §§ 6151-6155 before accepting per-call compensation. Keep Medicare and legal traffic disabled through this launch unless the pod can implement their separate operating requirements with documented specialist review.

## 2. Days 8-14 — Prove Routing, Qualification, and Event Integrity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build one Ringba or Retreaver routing tree, not two competing production systems. Use the second product only if a documented requirement makes migration worthwhile. C1 configures territory, opening hours, concurrency, buyer caps, and a deterministic fallback destination before enabling auctions. Treat sub-second RTB as a measured engineering objective: target ping-response p95 below 800 milliseconds in authorized testing, subject to the buyer's supported endpoint contract. Timeout behavior must release the call to a suitable available route without duplicate posting or unsupported transfers. Bind each bid to an expiry, destination, qualification specification, and call identifier. Minimize information in the ping; bid solicitation does not authorize distributing the consumer's identity or health details to every potential buyer. Test stale bids, empty auctions, duplicate webhook delivery, rejected posts, closed destinations, and downstream disconnects. Preserve response codes and timestamps so an apparently low acceptance rate can be separated into routing failure and commercial rejection. The operator approves the destination roster; C1 cannot add a higher-paying buyer without updating its contract and eligibility rules. Keep a manual shutdown control available to the operator throughout staffed hours, and automatically stop traffic when no contracted destination can safely handle the selected service.

Implement the 90-120-second qualification buffer as a buyer-specific commercial timer, never as a reason to keep an unsuitable caller talking. Record arrival, answer, transfer, connected conversation, disconnect, and disposition timestamps separately. If a buyer pays after ninety seconds of connected conversation, IVR time and ringing cannot inflate that measure. C2 reviews the first twenty genuine calls in full and compares buyer disposition with the contract, using recordings only where collection and access are permitted. AI transcript labels can triage wrong geography, duplicate demand, silence, solicitation, or service mismatch, but disputed payment decisions require human review of the underlying evidence. Configure fraud controls around repeated device or source patterns, implausible call bursts, recycled callers, and repeated short connections without making caller identity alone a definitive fraud finding. For outbound callbacks, implement applicable National Do Not Call and entity-specific suppression controls, honoring relevant exceptions and revocations. A private litigation-risk list is merely supplementary risk screening, not permission evidence or a replacement for required suppression. Document recording disclosures and applicable state requirements separately, including California Penal Code § 632 when confidential communications fall within its scope. Limit recording access to the assigned reviewers and preserve an auditable retention schedule.

Release one acquisition cell on Day 11 after synthetic testing passes: one source, one service, one geography, and one declared creative promise. Set a $150 daily acquisition ceiling initially, with cumulative commitments still constrained by the monthly budget. If the vendor requires a prepaid minimum beyond that ceiling, defer that source rather than disguising the commitment as unused credit. Use Google Ads call assets or an individually contracted publisher, but do not combine untraceable publisher traffic with search traffic in one reporting bucket. For P12, distinguish an early connected-call signal from a finalized payable-call conversion. Send idempotent server-to-server events using a stable event identifier and retain correction or retraction support where the receiving platform provides it. Never train bidding solely on duration when the buyer also rejects geography or duplicates. The three-person pod can support a fifteen-minute morning review and one bounded afternoon exception queue; it cannot manually investigate hundreds of unclassified calls while negotiating new verticals. Pause a cell when attribution completeness falls below 99%, required permission evidence is missing, or destination failures exceed 2% of attempted transfers. Restart only after replaying the failed condition and recording a successful test against the corrected configuration.

> 
> These tactics improve diagnosis without multiplying vendors, traffic cells, or contractor hours.

## 3. Days 15-21 — Reconcile Mature Calls and Collect Actual Cash  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use three separate measures: gross billable settlements, retained contribution after publisher payouts and click-to-call media, and owner profit after all remaining expenses. The canonical retained share is 55%, leaving 45% for those acquisition payouts and media costs. Conservative therefore reconciles $200,000 gross to $110,000 retained, $80,000 of remaining annual expenses, and $30,000 owner profit. Base reconciles $850,000 gross to $467,500 retained, $267,750 of remaining expenses, and $199,750 owner profit. Scaled reconciles $1,500,000 gross to $825,000 retained, $345,000 of remaining expenses, and $480,000 owner profit. The corresponding gross-basis margins are 15%, 23.5%, and 32%; retained-basis margins are 27.27%, 42.73%, and 58.18%. These residual annual expenses are mathematical requirements of the supplied cases, not independently quoted software budgets. The supplied $2,500-$14,000 monthly operating range cannot alone reconcile every residual; build an itemized expense bridge before adopting a forecast. Use the supplied ASC 606 retained-share convention for comparison while documenting principal-versus-agent presentation from actual contracts. Neither the homeowner's repair invoice, an insurance premium, nor a legal client's recovery belongs in operator call-exchange revenue. Day 30 performance should test this accounting structure, not extrapolate one unusually profitable afternoon into annual earnings.

Model the acquisition cell with explicit denominators. Suppose one hundred connected calls generate eighty final payable calls at $75 each: gross settlements equal $6,000. A 55% retained contribution allows $2,700 of total acquisition costs, or $27 per connected call and $33.75 per payable call, leaving $3,300 before remaining expenses. If search clicks convert to connected calls at 10%, the maximum CPC compatible with that contribution target is $2.70, assuming no additional publisher payouts; adding publisher payments reduces the available media budget. This is unit arithmetic, not a forecast that the launch will achieve those prices or volumes. With the same $2,700 acquisition spend but only sixty payable calls, gross falls to $4,500 and retained contribution falls to 40%. A high quoted bid cannot rescue poor payability. Report effective cost per payable call only after including rejected-call acquisition spending, telephony classified consistently, and any publisher minimums. Keep fixed overhead outside retained contribution and inside owner profit, avoiding double counting. Trigger a same-day Fix review when the three-day median CPC exceeds the launch baseline by 20% without an offsetting increase in collected value per click.

Define a mature call as one whose contractual rejection window has expired and whose disposition has been reconciled, not merely a call that occurred yesterday. Track connected calls, final payable calls, gross finalized settlements, acquisition liabilities, and collected cash by source, buyer, and service date. Acceptance equals final payable calls divided by mature connected calls; dispute rate equals formally disputed initially billed dollars divided by initially billed dollars for the same mature cohort. Due-dollar collection equals cash applied to invoices due by the checkpoint divided by those invoices' collectible balances after contractually valid finalized adjustments. Show the adjustments separately so write-offs cannot manufacture a perfect collection score. C2 matches the buyer ledger to event identifiers; the operator sends the invoice and resolves commercial exceptions. Request the first net-7 remittance by Day 21 if the invoice date supports it. Until a buyer completes one full pilot settlement cycle, cap its unpaid exposure at $500 within the overall $4,000 limit. Freeze that buyer when an undisputed balance is more than seven days overdue. Publishers still receive payment on their agreed terms; buyer delinquency does not retroactively authorize withholding valid publisher earnings.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the evidence cohort
   - Export calls whose contractual rejection windows have expired.
   - Preserve source, buyer, specification version, connected duration, permission references, acquisition liabilities, and original billed amounts.
2. Resolve commercial differences
   - Match each buyer disposition and adjustment to its contractual rule.
   - Keep unresolved disputes visible; distinguish wrong geography, duplicate demand, duration differences, and unsupported retroactive scrubs.
3. Bridge contribution to cash
   - Calculate gross finalized settlements, retained contribution, remaining expenses, and invoice collections separately.
   - Apply bank receipts to invoice balances and retain a separate aging schedule for overdue amounts and valid adjustments.
4. Authorize only bounded commitments
   - Record the operator's next-day source caps and buyer exposure limits.
   - Check aggregate spending, available working capital, reserve floor, routing reliability, and evidence completeness before reopening traffic.

## 4. Days 22-30 — Decide with Evidence, Then Recheck at Days 60 and 90  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use Days 22-24 to close the first invoice cycle, repair the largest rejection cause, and rerun the routing regression suite. Use Days 25-27 to measure one unchanged cell, test a reversible 20% budget increase only if provisional scale conditions hold, and verify the second buyer's collection evidence. Days 28-30 are for expense accruals, a written decision, and next-month caps, not a last-minute traffic surge. All thresholds here are proposed operating rules rather than measured performance of Candidate #39. Evaluate economic thresholds per acquisition cell, with treasury and evidence failures overriding the entire affected operation. At Day 30 require at least one hundred mature connected calls, 75% acceptance, 55% retained contribution, disputed dollars no higher than 5%, and at least 95% collection of due dollars before scaling. Both active buyers must have completed one invoice cycle. Zero due invoices means collectability is unproven, not 100% collected. Require complete mandatory evidence, at least 99% attribution completeness, and transfer failures no higher than 2%. A sample shortfall produces Fix with frozen expansion; it never justifies more spending beyond the cash limits merely to reach statistical convenience.

Kill means stop paid acquisition for the failing cell and settle existing obligations; it does not mean erase records or abandon valid debts. Immediately pause affected activity for missing required permission, unlawful routing, unauthorized disclosure, or breached treasury limits. Terminate a source relationship when material evidence falsification is substantiated. For economic failures, kill a cell when retained contribution is zero or negative across two consecutive non-overlapping mature cohorts of at least fifty connected calls each, despite one documented repair attempt. Otherwise classify below-threshold results as Fix and assign one cause, one owner, one test, and a seven-day deadline. At Day 60 require three hundred mature connected calls in the trailing thirty-day service cohort; at Day 90 require six hundred, with the same maturity rule. An immature portion remains outside the denominator and is reported explicitly. Raise acceptance to 80% and 85%, reduce dispute limits to 3% and 2%, and increase due-dollar collection to 98% and 99%, respectively. Maintain 55% retained contribution throughout. These are cumulative requirements within each checkpoint, not interchangeable achievements. Recheck whenever a publisher, buyer contract, geography, or routing configuration changes materially, because prior evidence does not automatically transfer to the new cell.

Scale only by 20% in daily acquisition budget after seven additional stable calendar days and another mature-cohort review; reserve buyer capacity before lifting source caps. At Day 60 also require nonnegative trailing-thirty-day owner profit after accrued expenses; at Day 90 require at least 15% owner margin on gross finalized settlements. The Day 90 threshold matches the Conservative margin, not the Base or Scaled promise. Track collection lag and publisher payment dates before each increase because profitable receivables can still exhaust cash. Hold expansion if the operator exceeds forty-five hours weekly for two weeks, either contractor exceeds ten hours weekly, or unresolved exceptions remain older than two business days. First automate repetitive identifier matching, rejection categorization, and invoice preparation; do not automate disputed commercial judgments merely to hide a staffing deficit. The Conservative and Base cases use one full-time person plus two contractors. The $480,000 Scaled owner-profit case explicitly requires two full-time people plus three contractors, so crossing into that operating design needs a separately funded staffing plan. End Day 30 with a signed internal decision record listing live cells, paused cells, unpaid exposure, remaining cash, repair owners, and the next checkpoint date.
