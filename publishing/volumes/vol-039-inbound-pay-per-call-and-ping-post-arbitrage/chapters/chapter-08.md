## 1. Build the buyer eligibility graph from authoritative records  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the information edge around buyer eligibility, not around harvesting consumers. The minimum buyer record joins a legal entity, billing counterparty, authorized signer, licensed service territory, accepted vertical, transfer endpoint, and settlement terms. Use the state insurance department for producer authority, the state bar for attorney status, and the relevant contractor board for emergency trades. A Secretary of State registration establishes an entity record; it does not establish professional authority or the ability to pay. NIPR can accelerate insurance verification under its access terms, while the issuing department remains the escalation source for ambiguous status. Store the retrieved status, jurisdiction, source address, retrieval time, and human review outcome rather than flattening everything into a permanent approved flag. One suspended license or mismatched trading name should remove only the affected buyer-territory route, preserving healthy routes elsewhere. For a desk with twenty buyers across five states, model one hundred eligibility edges rather than twenty company profiles. Refresh active edges on a documented schedule and immediately after a buyer change, complaint, returned payment, or official status alert. Public record availability is useful evidence, but neither a marketing permission nor permission to evade a portal's access restrictions.

Separate three evidence questions before a call reaches an auction. First, can this buyer serve this location and requested service? Second, does this interaction permit the proposed marketing, transfer, recording, and subsequent contact? Third, does the contract define a collectible billable event? The federal one-to-one seller consent amendment adopted by the FCC was vacated in Insurance Marketing Coalition v. FCC on January 24, 2025; do not present that vacated amendment as a universal current TCPA requirement. Existing TCPA restrictions still require analysis of the technology, purpose, recipient, and applicable consent standard. Buyer-specific evidence remains a strong contractual control, and CMS separately imposes specific TPMO data-sharing requirements. Likewise, the Medicare forty-eight-hour scope-of-appointment rule concerns covered personal marketing appointments, with stated exceptions; it is not a mandatory delay on every inbound call. Capture the appointment category and applicable exception instead of coding an indiscriminate timer. Apply relevant recording obligations and state recording rules before collecting audio. For legal calls, verify state advertising, referral-fee, and runner restrictions before selecting a per-call compensation model. No routing vendor or consent certificate resolves these separate questions automatically; record the controlling rule and responsible reviewer against each vertical configuration.

Use public records as slow-changing configuration inputs, never as synchronous dependencies inside a sub-second ping. A nightly job can compare previously verified buyer edges with newly retrieved records, creating a review queue for status changes. A failed retrieval must produce a source-outage event, not silently replace a valid record with an empty value. Assign freshness budgets by risk: a recently changed buyer may require manual confirmation before receiving another transfer, whereas an unchanged entity address can tolerate a longer refresh interval. Keep an explicit expiry on routing eligibility so stale evidence eventually closes the affected route. Government portals frequently provide search forms rather than supported APIs; use documented exports or permitted manual checks instead of reverse-engineering private endpoints. Budget lookup work as an operating expense. At one hundred eligibility edges and four minutes per monthly review, the workload is roughly 6.7 hours before exceptions; at an illustrative $35 hourly contractor rate, that is approximately $233. Those checks protect settlement quality, not the scorecard's gross revenue ceiling. Link each finding to the contract version and destination number so a buyer cannot substitute an unverified affiliate behind an otherwise familiar brand.

## 2. Design a bounded enrichment waterfall outside the auction clock  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The enrichment waterfall should answer a short sequence of routing questions with increasing cost and sensitivity. Normalize the submitted telephone number, validate the declared service location, check first-party suppression, evaluate applicable external restrictions, and retrieve required consent evidence. Only then request commercial line intelligence when it changes a legitimate decision. An area code does not prove current residence or service location, and a line-type result does not establish consent or identify the present caller. Ask for ZIP code or service address directly where needed. Twilio Lookup can provide documented number intelligence packages; TrustedForm and Jornaya provide evidence workflows with different capture, retrieval, and retention semantics. A certificate identifier without accessible evidence is insufficient for a dispute packet. Keep the landing-page disclosure version, capture timestamp, publisher identifier, permitted recipients, and evidence retrieval outcome in the same event chain. Do not send the whole consumer record to every bidder. Define a minimal pre-transfer ping schema using service category, permitted geographic granularity, requested timing, and an opaque request identifier. Release additional fields only to an authorized recipient when the documented interaction and contract permit that disclosure.

Distinguish a genuine consumer-initiated inbound inquiry from subsequent outbound marketing; the latter can trigger additional TCPA, Telemarketing Sales Rule, and state requirements. National Do Not Call access belongs to authorized entities and permitted purposes, with seller-specific access obligations where applicable. The federal registry's thirty-one-day refresh requirement for covered outbound telemarketing is not a claim that government suppression runs in real time. Maintain a locally refreshed suppression index and check it immediately before a covered contact. Apply internal opt-outs faster than the external refresh cycle. Commercial litigator indicators, if used, are imperfect risk signals rather than authoritative determinations about a person or substitutes for consent. Minimize their use and restrict access. Build a lookup budget with an explicit stop rule: an invalid service territory should end enrichment before paid certificate work. For an illustrative thousand inquiries, a $0.02 lookup on every inquiry and a $0.10 evidence operation on four hundred eligible inquiries cost $60, or $0.06 per initial inquiry. Those are planning inputs, not vendor quotations. Replace them with contracted prices, and stop any optional lookup whose measured recovery or qualification improvement fails to cover its actual cost.

Ringba and Retreaver belong in the live decision path only through supported interfaces and account-enabled capabilities. Document whether an endpoint performs configuration, reporting, bid collection, or transfer reservation; those operations have different timeout and retry consequences. Set a measured end-to-end auction budget, then allocate time for network transit, buyer responses, decision logic, and call connection. A local target such as 700 milliseconds is an engineering budget, not a vendor service commitment. Cache stable eligibility configuration and avoid calling licensing portals during that interval. Attach request IDs and expiration times to bids so a late response cannot win an expired auction. Respect documented quotas, use capped exponential backoff with jitter for safe background requests, and honor Retry-After responses. Do not blindly retry a transfer or reservation operation after a timeout; reconcile its state first to avoid duplicate calls or charges. If required evidence is unavailable, use only a separately documented permissible path or stop the transfer. If optional enrichment fails, route using verified first-party inputs and a conservative buyer configuration. Maintain a circuit breaker per dependency so one provider outage does not exhaust the entire desk's worker pool.

## 3. Make call diagnostics and settlement evidence share one event ledger  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A settlement-grade pipeline begins with identifiers that survive every handoff. Create an internal interaction ID before the first ping and map it to publisher click IDs, routing-platform call IDs, buyer transaction IDs, consent evidence references, and eventual invoice lines. Record event occurrence time separately from receipt time, both in UTC, because delayed webhooks otherwise distort duration and response-latency measurements. Append events rather than overwriting history; materialized views can calculate the latest disposition. Verify webhook authentication using the vendor's documented mechanism, restrict payload size, and enforce replay protection where supported. A duplicate callback should update neither payable totals nor conversion counts twice. For a small desk, PostgreSQL with unique event keys, scheduled reconciliation jobs, and access-controlled object storage can outperform an unnecessarily elaborate streaming stack. Store recording references separately from ordinary analytics and restrict playback to authorized staff. Use synthetic identities in staging and redacted payloads in support tickets. Establish separate retention schedules for recordings, evidence, billing records, and diagnostic traces, with legal holds handled explicitly. Deleting a raw recording should not silently delete the invoice facts or the documented reason that a specific call was considered billable.

The critical diagnostic distinction is between network duration, agent-connected duration, and the buyer's contractual qualification duration. A 120-second call including thirty seconds of queue music may fail a contract requiring 120 seconds with an agent. Instrument ringing, answer, bridge, agent connection when available, disconnect, and disposition as separate events. Where agent connection is not observable, disclose the proxy and reconcile against buyer evidence rather than inventing precision. Twilio Voice Insights, authorized SIP traces, and platform reports can distinguish early hangups, codec issues, transfer failures, and busy destinations. Capture only traffic from systems you operate or are expressly authorized to inspect. A packet capture can contain caller identifiers or audio; narrow its scope and delete it according to the incident schedule. Prometheus and Grafana should display connect rate, bid expiry, evidence failure, duplicate events, webhook lag, and disputed settlements by buyer. Alert on changes relative to each route's baseline rather than declaring every short call fraudulent. If one destination degrades, reduce its traffic allocation and test a verified alternate while preserving the incident's timestamps and affected call identifiers for recovery.

AI transcript disposition can accelerate classification, but it must not become the sole authority for invoice deductions or sensitive service decisions. Evaluate it against a labeled sample containing interruptions, accents, background noise, wrong numbers, and unsuccessful transfers. Record model version, confidence, and reviewer corrections; send uncertain cases to a small exception queue. If an external transcription service receives audio, verify authorized processing, retention, and permitted use before transmission. For a weekly batch of two hundred disputed calls, reviewing every call for three minutes takes ten hours. If automation correctly resolves routine categories and leaves forty calls for review, the manual queue takes two hours, plus quality sampling and system maintenance. That is a workload illustration, not eight guaranteed hours of savings. Feed ad platforms only contractually permitted conversion fields through supported server-to-server interfaces. Distinguish provisional qualification from final settlement, and use supported adjustment mechanisms when scrubs reverse a conversion. Google Ads API quotas, upload windows, identifiers, and attribution behavior vary by integration. Keep a local delivery ledger so a failed upload can be reconciled without creating a second conversion or exposing transcript text to an advertising endpoint.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the interaction before enrichment
   - Create one internal interaction ID and record the publisher, vertical, and disclosure version.
   - Reject malformed inputs, attach source timestamps, and keep personal fields outside ordinary application logs.
2. Evaluate cached eligibility and required evidence
   - Apply territory, buyer status, suppression, and interaction-specific evidence rules before requesting bids.
   - Record the rule version and failure reason; stop evidence-dependent routes instead of treating outages as successful checks.
3. Execute and observe one bounded auction
   - Issue minimal pings, discard expired bids, and connect through the selected verified destination.
   - Persist bid identifiers and connection events; reconcile ambiguous transfer results before attempting another transfer.
4. Reconcile qualification through collection
   - Join buyer dispositions, payable calculations, invoice lines, and received cash against the original interaction.
   - Deduplicate callbacks, open timed dispute cases, and send supported advertising adjustments after final settlement changes.

## 4. Price comparison intent against collected contribution, not dashboard revenue  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The mapped TRACK_2 play competitor_intent_comparison_hijacking uses programmatic comparison pages, live fee calculators, and migration guides to capture specific purchase questions. Build useful pages around '[Competitor] pricing', '[Competitor] cancel', and '[Competitor] vs [Offer]' only where the desk can substantiate the comparison and fulfill the resulting service request. The volume contains fourteen mapped plays; this chapter details this named play without inventing identifiers for the other thirteen. A comparison record should contain the public source URL, observation date, geography, service tier, introductory conditions, recurring charges, and cancellation mechanics. Fetch only permitted public pages, honor access terms, and use manual verification when a price depends on an individualized quote. A calculator must expose its assumptions rather than quietly turning a promotional fee into a universal saving. Use text-only brand references, clearly identify the desk, and avoid logos or layout that suggests affiliation. Nominative fair use is fact-specific, not an automatic exemption. If reliable comparison evidence disappears, remove the unsupported claim or replace the page with a sourced category guide. Do not masquerade as a competitor's cancellation department or divert someone seeking account support into an unrelated sales transfer.

Treat the play's 10–18% conversion range as a test hypothesis with a defined denominator, not a promise or a scorecard assumption. At one thousand paid comparison-page visits and $2 CPC, media costs $2,000. A 10% visit-to-call rate yields one hundred calls; at 18%, it yields one hundred eighty. If 60% become accepted billable calls at $50 each, gross settlements are $3,000 and $5,400 respectively. With no separate publisher payment in this direct-media example, contribution after media is $1,000 or $3,400, before tooling, labor, refunds, and other costs. To fit the canonical 55% retained-share planning envelope, total publisher and media acquisition cost must equal 45% of gross: $1,350 at the lower outcome and $2,430 at the higher outcome. Consequently, the lower case fails that envelope at $2 CPC; its matching CPC is $1.35. The higher case has $430 of acquisition headroom. Keep this funnel sensitivity separate from the annual scenarios. If scrub rates rise, substitute collected eligible calls for provisional acceptances and reduce bids before expanding page coverage. A prettier comparison table cannot rescue an uncollectible buyer or negative acquisition spread.

Reconcile every dashboard to Formula v2.0 using the same revenue labels. Conservative gross settlements of $200,000 less $90,000 acquisition payouts produce $110,000 retained revenue; subtract $80,000 remaining costs for $30,000 owner profit. Base gross settlements of $850,000 less $382,500 produce $467,500 retained revenue; subtract $267,750 for $199,750 profit. Scaled gross settlements of $1,500,000 less $675,000 produce $825,000; subtract $345,000 for $480,000 profit. Gross-basis margins are 15%, 23.5%, and 32%; retained-basis margins are 27.27%, 42.73%, and 58.18%. The model uses a 55% retained share; actual ASC 606 principal-versus-agent presentation depends on the contracts and control assessment, not a switch inside the routing software. Never report insurance premiums, legal case values, or emergency repair invoices as desk revenue. The supplied monthly opex range of $2,500–$14,000 is not the complete residual-cost bridge in every scenario; classify labor, contractors, usage, and other costs explicitly without counting acquisition twice. Track receivables aging separately from profit, retain startup liquidity within the $8,000–$45,000 planning range, and expand publisher limits only after actual net-7 collections validate the buyer's reported acceptance quality.
