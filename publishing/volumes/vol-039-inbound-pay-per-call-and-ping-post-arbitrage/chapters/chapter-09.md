## 1. Encode Permission, Recording, and Sector Rules Before Auctioning Calls  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Separate a consumer-initiated inbound conversation from any later outbound marketing call, automated text, prerecorded message, or artificial-voice contact. Under 47 U.S.C. § 227 and 47 CFR § 64.1200, obligations depend on the technology, destination, purpose, and permission involved; a genuine inbound request does not authorize every subsequent channel. The Eleventh Circuit vacated the FCC's 2023 one-to-one consent restriction in Insurance Marketing Coalition Ltd. v. FCC on January 24, 2025. Consequently, do not describe that restriction as an effective nationwide FCC mandate. Instead, require seller-specific written permission contractually whenever your outbound marketing workflow requires prior express written consent, and verify subsequent authority before launch. TrustedForm and Jornaya can preserve evidence associated with a disclosure, but neither product supplies missing permission or conclusively establishes validity. Capture the actual rendered language, named recipient, affirmative action, timestamp, source address, and certificate reference. A listing of hundreds of buyers behind a link fails the operator's narrower permission standard. Route an unsupported inbound request only to a recipient the caller actually selects; do not manufacture a callback authorization from a completed transfer. Store each permission event separately from auction and billing events so later changes cannot rewrite the historical record.

Build state controls around both caller location and recipient operations, rather than the telephone number's area code alone. California Penal Code § 632 regulates recording confidential communications, and Florida Statutes § 934.03 generally requires all-party consent for covered interceptions. Obtain an appropriate affirmative recording response before retaining conversation audio where required; have counsel map interstate conflicts and statutory exceptions. If consent is declined, use an approved unrecorded route where the sector permits one, or end the recorded workflow. For outbound telemarketing, apply national and applicable state do-not-call requirements, internal suppression, permitted hours, identification, and revocation handling. Refresh National Do Not Call Registry information at least every 31 days where the FTC rule applies, then evaluate the current suppression state at each contact attempt. Florida § 501.059 and Oklahoma's Telephone Solicitation Act require separate analysis of covered technology and exceptions. Litigation-risk lists can prompt source-quality review, but must never become a method for evading consumer rights or replacing statutory checks. Propagate applicable revocations within the governing deadline, generally no more than ten business days under the FCC rules, and maintain dated rule versions. An inbound insurance inquiry is not permission for an unrelated home-services text campaign tomorrow.

Medicare routing needs its own product configuration because CMS rules do not simply mirror ordinary inbound insurance calls. Under 42 CFR §§ 422.2274 and 423.2274, covered third-party marketing organizations must record marketing, sales, and enrollment calls in their entirety, including covered web-based audio, and retain those recordings for ten years. Reconcile that duty with state recording consent before the substantive conversation starts. The 48-hour requirement in §§ 422.2264 and 423.2264 concerns documenting a Scope of Appointment before a scheduled personal marketing appointment; it is not a universal waiting period imposed on every inbound inquiry. Apply only documented exceptions, including qualifying beneficiary-initiated walk-ins and appointments during the final four days of a valid election period. CMS also requires prior express written consent identifying each receiving TPMO before personal beneficiary information collected for marketing or enrollment is shared with another TPMO. A broad insurance permission record does not automatically satisfy that requirement. Keep beneficiary identifiers out of speculative pings, confirm the selected recipient, and transfer permitted information only after the applicable evidence is available. If recording, recipient permission, or appointment documentation fails, suppress the affected route instead of silently reclassifying the call as generic insurance.

## 2. Keep Licensed Services Separate From Marketing and Arizona ABS Ownership  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Define your role before quoting a payout. A marketing exchange can describe service categories, geography, availability, and connection mechanics without selling insurance, advising on coverage, recommending a legal strategy, or representing that a contractor holds a license it has not checked. Insurance producer licensing is state-specific; California Insurance Code § 1631 requires appropriate licensing for covered solicitation, negotiation, and effecting insurance contracts. Appointment requirements and restrictions on compensation to unlicensed referrers also vary. A per-call fee does not cure conduct that constitutes licensed solicitation, and compensation tied to a policy sale can create additional restrictions. For legal traffic, distinguish permissible advertising payments from prohibited recommendations, solicitation, runner activity, and fee sharing. California Business and Professions Code §§ 6151–6152 addresses runners and cappers, while applicable professional conduct rules govern advertising and referral arrangements. Route emergency-trade requests to verified providers without diagnosing electrical, plumbing, or medical hazards. Ask a licensed local reviewer to approve the specific script, compensation trigger, and landing-page representations for each vertical and state. A single national insertion order is insufficient when the same intake question can be neutral scheduling in one setting and regulated advice in another. Store these decisions beside the actual routing configuration.

The mapped play arizona_abs_mass_tort_cocounsel changes the ownership model, not merely the advertisement. Arizona Supreme Court Rule 31.1(c) and ACJA § 7-209 establish an authorized path for licensed alternative business structures with nonlawyer economic interests. Arizona removed its own professional-rule prohibition embodied in former ER 5.4; it did not abolish the ABA model rule or other states' restrictions. Obtain the ABS license, qualified legal leadership, required ownership disclosures, and ongoing oversight before operating the law practice. Licensed attorneys must control legal judgment and perform substantive intake, investigation, and case work. Institutional sexual-assault survivor matters, juvenile detention claims, clergy or boarding-school cases, medical-abuse claims, rideshare litigation, and catastrophic mass torts each require jurisdiction-specific liability and limitation analysis. A lookback window or multidistrict proceeding does not itself establish a valid individual claim. Verify identity, relevant dates, defendants, jurisdiction, prior representation, and available primary records before filing, using trauma-informed evidence requests rather than demanding unnecessary sensitive details from an auction operator. Any co-counsel division must satisfy the applicable professional rules, client agreement requirements, responsibility allocation, and multistate practice restrictions. Arizona ownership permission does not travel automatically with an advertising campaign into every destination state.

Use the play's $50,000–$400,000-plus settled-case fee range only as an illustrative firm-level opportunity, never as earned exchange revenue or a forecast for every lead. For example, a hypothetical $600,000 client recovery with a one-third contractual fee produces a $200,000 legal fee before costs and any governing limitations. If an otherwise lawful, documented co-counsel allocation assigns 40% of that fee to the ABS, its fee allocation is $80,000; deducting $20,000 of allocated acquisition and case costs leaves $60,000 before overhead and ownership distributions. Client recovery, trust funds, earned legal fees, and owner distributions are four separate ledgers. The example does not modify this volume's exchange scorecard or imply immediate cash collection. In restrictive states, evaluate a management services organization providing genuine administrative services at permitted, supportable compensation, without controlling lawyers or taking an unlawful percentage of fees. An MSO label does not rescue prohibited conduct. When claim verification fails, stop acquisition for that source and let the responsible attorney decide the matter's disposition. Blend suitable single-event injury work with long institutional dockets only after assessing litigation budgets and client needs. Maintain separate working capital because even strong claims can remain unresolved for years and produce no collectible fee.

## 3. Contract the Billable Event, Evidence Window, and Collectible Spread  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Write the insertion order as an event specification rather than a promise of good leads. Define permitted sources, approved creatives, geographies, recipient identities, recording responsibilities, transfer behavior, duplicate lookback, operating hours, and capacity limits. A 90–120-second qualification buffer must state when its clock begins, whether ringing and queue time count, what constitutes an answered connection, and which platform timestamp controls a disagreement. Consider this illustrative schedule: the buyer owes $100 after 120 seconds of connected conversation with an eligible caller; a documented wrong service category remains rejectable, but agent silence cannot be used to manufacture a failure. Require bid identifiers, call identifiers, answer events, disconnect reasons, and versioned dispositions. State that price acceptance occurs at the specified binding event, not at an earlier nonbinding ping. Define timeouts and fallback routing explicitly so an expired bid never becomes a retroactive buyer obligation. Prohibit publishers from impersonating providers, manufacturing permission, coaching callers to exceed duration, or resubmitting duplicates. Avoid open-ended suitability clauses allowing buyers to reject objectively qualified calls weeks later. The signed schedule should let a neutral reviewer reconstruct the fee using stored events without guessing what either party meant by qualified.

Reconcile economics before negotiating credits. Formula v2.0 uses $200,000, $850,000, and $1,500,000 in annual gross billable settlements, with 45% consumed by publisher payouts and click-to-call media. The respective retained amounts are $110,000, $467,500, and $825,000. Deducting other modeled costs of $80,000, $267,750, and $345,000 yields $30,000, $199,750, and $480,000 in annual owner profit. These equal gross-basis margins of 15%, 23.5%, and 32%, or retained-basis margins of 27.27%, 42.73%, and 58.18%. The scorecard models the exchange on a 55% retained-share basis; the actual ASC 606 principal-versus-agent conclusion requires analysis of control and contractual facts rather than a chosen label. At the illustrative $100 billable-call price, 8,500 annual accepted calls generate the Base gross settlements. Allocate $45 per accepted call to acquisition and $55 to retained contribution before other modeled costs; actual campaign costs still include unsuccessful traffic. If a paid call later receives a full $100 credit without acquisition recovery, that credit consumes the retained contribution from approximately 1.82 otherwise accepted calls. Record credits and recoveries explicitly instead of concealing them inside an unexplained change to the 55% assumption.

Make net-7 terms an exposure limit, not a belief about payment. For an initial buyer test, cap unpaid accepted settlements at $2,000 and stop routing at that amount even if the seventh day has not arrived. At the modeled 45% acquisition ratio, $900 is already exposed before other costs; buyer default can eliminate the entire expected receipt. Increase the cap only after reconciled cash reaches the bank and disputes are resolved. Specify a short evidence-based dispute window, payment of undisputed amounts, a defined reserve, and a final reconciliation date, while preserving appropriate treatment of proven fraud and statutory claims. Anti-circumvention should identify protected introductions, duration, and reasonable remedies without claiming ownership of callers or preventing their free choice. Allocate intellectual property rights in creatives and software separately from limited rights to process personal information. Include deletion, retention, security, breach notification, and subprocessor obligations. Negotiate a commercially coherent liability cap with expressly identified exceptions and avoid indemnities that purport to authorize unlawful conduct. Choose governing law, venue, and a dispute escalation sequence. Finally, make publisher obligations supportable and observable; downstream buyer misconduct does not become the publisher's responsibility merely because a template says all liability flows upstream.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Build the signed event map
   - Align the buyer schedule, publisher schedule, and platform configuration.
   - Specify source, destination, price, binding event, duration clock, rejection reasons, and dispute deadlines before the first paid call.
2. Replay qualification and rejection examples
   - Test a qualified call, a short call, a duplicate, and a wrong-category connection.
   - Require matching outcomes from the exchange ledger and buyer ledger; document disagreements before enabling live settlement.
3. Enforce the unpaid settlement cap
   - Set the initial buyer exposure limit to the illustrative $2,000.
   - Count accepted unpaid settlements continuously, pause when the cap is reached, and release capacity only under the signed credit policy.
4. Reconcile cash and authorize measured expansion
   - Match bank receipts to accepted calls, credits, and publisher liabilities.
   - Increase traffic only after collected net-7 receipts support the new exposure and no unresolved evidence defect threatens the cohort.

## 4. Use Exact Outreach Scripts and Minimize Every Ping Payload  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use this buyer-discovery opening in a permitted business contact: 'We operate an inbound call exchange connecting people who request a named service. Which legal entity purchases calls, which states and service categories can it serve, and who owns licensing and recording decisions? We propose a documented 120-second connected-call trigger, a $100 illustrative price, and an initial $2,000 unpaid-settlement cap. What evidence supports each rejection, and can your settlement team pay undisputed amounts within seven days?' Then ask: 'Do your agents make subsequent outbound contacts, use prerecorded or artificial-voice messaging, or share caller information with another recipient? Please describe the required permission record and suppression process for each activity.' These questions expose an incompatible buyer before traffic acquisition begins. For publisher discovery, say: 'Show us the actual advertisement and complete consumer journey, including the rendered permission language, redirects, source identifiers, and recording notice. We prohibit fabricated requests, caller coaching, misleading provider identities, and undisclosed downstream recipients. Which acquisition costs remain payable when a buyer issues a supported rejection?' Keep the response with the contract version. Commercial email outreach must independently satisfy applicable CAN-SPAM requirements, including accurate identity, a valid postal address, an effective opt-out, and timely suppression; business recipients do not create a blanket exemption.

For ordinary consumer routing, use a narrow connection script: 'You reached Harbor Connection, a marketing connection service, not the service provider. You requested help with a plumbing repair in Phoenix. Would you like us to connect you to Desert Plumbing so you can discuss availability and pricing directly?' Those business names are illustrative and must be replaced with the actual entities. Where recording permission is required, add before recording substantive conversation: 'We would like to record this conversation for routing and quality review. Do you agree?' A spoken yes supports only the permissions that the law allows it to support; it does not replace required written marketing authorization. If a callback is requested, say: 'You asked Desert Plumbing to return this service inquiry at the number you provided. We will record that request separately from any marketing subscription.' For Medicare, use: 'We connect callers with the identified licensed insurance representative. Before we share personal information with another marketing organization, we need the required written permission identifying that organization. The representative will explain appointment scope and timing requirements.' Add any applicable CMS TPMO disclaimer using the currently required wording and placement; this connection script does not replace the full sector-specific script.

For survivor matters, use: 'You reached the intake team for the identified law firm. You may share only what you are comfortable sharing now. An authorized legal professional will assess whether the firm can assist; this call does not establish that a claim qualifies or that representation has begun. May we collect a safe contact method and arrange that conversation?' Follow the firm's approved engagement and prospective-client confidentiality procedures rather than promising that an exchange conversation is privileged. Never auction an abuse narrative. Initial pings should contain the minimum permitted fields, such as coarse service geography, neutral category, capacity request, and a nonreversible event identifier. A hashed phone number can remain personal information and is not automatically anonymous. Restrict AI transcript disposition to approved purposes, redact sensitive details where feasible, and prohibit vendor training reuse unless separately authorized and lawful. California Civil Code §§ 1798.100 and 1798.120 require covered businesses to address notice, purpose restrictions, and sale or sharing opt-outs as applicable; service-provider contract labels alone do not determine the relationship. Use server-to-server advertising postbacks with minimal conversion signals, not call recordings, allegation text, beneficiary identifiers, or unrestricted certificate links. When permission is withdrawn, stop affected processing and preserve only records supported by remaining duties or lawful purposes.
