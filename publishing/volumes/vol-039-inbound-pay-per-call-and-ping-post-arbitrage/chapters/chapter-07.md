## 2. Build an atomic ledger and an explicitly bounded delivery workflow  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use SQLite for a single-host control plane with modest write volume, WAL journaling, foreign keys, and short explicit write transactions. Store money as integer cents and hash canonical payloads before accepting idempotency keys. The six reference modules below concatenate into one executable Python file; their database belongs to the operator, not a platform administrator. Intake receives evidence already validated by an authenticated adapter. The reference deliberately does not pretend that accepting a Boolean performs TrustedForm verification, DNC screening, or telephony authentication. Quote retrieval and evidence checks happen before the critical transaction; capacity reservation and durable dispatch insertion happen together inside it. Each call has a fixed final-price ceiling and a supplier obligation. Calls needing repricing must obtain a new qualified quote before dispatch, rather than mutating an accepted request. Parameterized statements prevent payloads from becoming executable SQL. Begin with database permissions restricted to the service account, encrypted storage, separate recording storage, and tested backups. Keep certificates and recordings outside the hot ledger; retain their hashes and access-controlled object references in the evidence system. Restore a backup into an isolated environment before describing recovery as operational.

The state machine distinguishes QUALIFIED, RESERVED, CONNECTED, VERIFIED, REJECTED, SETTLED, and PAID. RESERVED means internal credit capacity is committed; it is not a bank escrow account or client-money custody service. VERIFIED means the contractual evidence and billability decision are complete. SETTLED creates an invoice entry, while PAID requires authenticated cash evidence. Every transition records a unique event and its payload hash in the same transaction as the state change. A replay with identical content is harmless; a reused key with different content fails loudly. Conditional updates and the SQLite writer lock prevent two workers from spending the same remaining buyer capacity. The durable dispatch queue closes the local crash gap between reservation and routing intent. External delivery has a separate boundary: the adapter must enforce a stable idempotency key or reconcile an ambiguous response against an authoritative provider receipt before retrying. Do not assume every Ringba or Retreaver endpoint supplies that guarantee. The included dispatcher requires an idempotent send contract and tolerates duplicate workers only under that explicit contract. Without it, stop automatic delivery rather than promise impossible exactly-once behavior across an uncontrolled network.

The reference models final adjudication before invoicing, one buyer and one supplier obligation per call, and full cash collection rather than installments. It is a bounded production foundation, not an undocumented substitute for a complete accounting system. Invoice posting debits buyer receivables and credits supplier payables plus retained revenue; it does not credit gross settlement volume to operator revenue. This implements the supplied 55% retained presentation when aggregate supplier costs equal 45%. Actual principal-versus-agent conclusions require the underlying ASC 606 assessment, not merely a percentage setting. Collection clears the receivable and releases exposure; supplier payment clears the payable, including when payment precedes buyer collection. The cohort assumes acquisition obligations are paid within their production month. Prepaid advertising requires a separate prepaid-asset subledger before consumption enters these obligations. Partial payments, taxes, post-invoice credits, and chargebacks require balanced, uniquely keyed extension events before those features are enabled. Never repair a posted event by editing history. The reconciliation view exposes receivables, payables, retained revenue, and cash independently. Run the concurrency and replay checks against a temporary database, then add provider-specific failure tests before connecting real routing or payment adapters.

## 3. Operate the daily evidence, adjudication, and collections loop  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Begin each shift by reconciling buyer availability, exposure, suppression updates, recording health, and yesterday's unresolved exceptions. The routing service should receive a signed configuration version, not an analyst's mutable spreadsheet. Verify webhook signatures against the provider's documented scheme, check timestamps where supported, and persist the original delivery identifier before acknowledging receipt. A callback may arrive before a slower transfer receipt; park out-of-order events for replay rather than forcing an illegal state transition. Separate the caller's phone number from public campaign identifiers, and restrict recording access to people handling the actual dispute. Monitor qualification by publisher, buyer, geography, hour, and creative experiment. A rising connection count with falling billability usually points to changed targeting, unavailable agents, duplicate traffic, or a different qualification-clock interpretation. Inspect representative audio and carrier events before changing acquisition bids. Use AI transcript disposition to prioritize human review of ambiguous intent, fraud, and disputed transfers; do not let a probabilistic transcript silently authorize payment or fabricate permission. Keep the model version, transcript reference, rule decision, and reviewer resolution with the final disposition. Send S2S advertising postbacks only after the selected conversion event becomes authoritative and deduplicate them by event identity.

Construct each daily settlement packet from call-level facts rather than a buyer's aggregate spreadsheet alone. Include the internal call identifier, provider leg identifiers, buyer acceptance timestamp, qualifying conversation duration, contract version, evidence receipt, final price, and scrub reason. Compare carrier-connected duration with the buyer's agent-connected duration; these are often different legitimate measurements. A 118-second call does not satisfy a 120-second agent-conversation requirement merely because two seconds of ringing appear elsewhere. Challenge a scrub only with evidence matching the actual contract. Before the daily cutoff, classify calls into payable, rejected, or disputed inventory, and exclude unresolved inventory from finalized revenue. Preserve both the buyer's reason and the operator's conclusion. For 1,250 Month 3 routed calls, 250 rejections leave 1,000 billable calls, a 20% scrub rate. An additional 50 rejected calls at the modeled average price removes about $3,541.67 gross settlements. If their acquisition costs cannot be recovered, the profit loss is larger than simply applying the normal retained percentage to that revenue reduction. Maintain source-specific loss attribution so a publisher's fraud does not become an unexplained buyer-margin deterioration. Freeze the offending slice while preserving healthy routes.

Run collections as a credit process, not a calendar reminder. Net-7 describes the contractual due date; collectability comes from bank-matched performance and a solvent counterparty. Review unpaid exposure against both the contractual ceiling and the operator's available working capital. A buyer that pays its first small invoice has demonstrated one payment, not unlimited creditworthiness. Increase exposure in measured increments after repeated clean settlement cycles. Keep publisher contracts explicit about scrub allocation, payment timing, documentation deadlines, and responsibility for invalid traffic. Do not make an unconditional publisher promise depend on an unstated pay-when-paid assumption. Where media is prepaid, reserve cash before campaign activation and include consumed spend in the same 45% acquisition budget used for publisher charges. Match bank receipts to invoices using remittance references, then investigate short payments instead of marking the invoice fully collected. The reference ledger rejects partial collection because that feature needs an additional allocation table and amount-level event keys. Finish the shift with a balanced journal, reconciled exposure, a dated exception queue, and named owners for every disputed invoice. Export an immutable daily close and test that it reconstructs the dashboard independently of the telephony platform.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Validate and reserve
   - Authenticate intake, validate evidence, and reserve buyer exposure atomically.
   - Persist the immutable request, contract reference, evidence digest, and durable dispatch intent before routing begins.
2. Deliver and adjudicate
   - Transfer through the controlled adapter and reconcile the actual qualifying conversation.
   - Retain transfer receipts, both duration clocks, final scrub reasons, and a human resolution for disputed classifications.
3. Invoice and collect
   - Post balanced final invoices and match authenticated buyer receipts.
   - Keep gross receivables separate from retained revenue; do not release exposure for an unallocated or short payment.
4. Pay and close
   - Match supplier payments, reconcile exposure, and archive the daily close.
   - Reconstruct cash, receivables, payables, and revenue from the journal before authorizing higher traffic limits.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Protect the spread where dashboards usually conceal leakage**
> 
> Experienced operators optimize adjudicated contribution and cash conversion, not auction win rate.
> - Replay one buyer's disputed calls against agent-connected timestamps; a clock mismatch can explain losses that look like low-quality traffic.
> - Maintain separate preliminary and adjudicated conversion feeds so ad bidding does not scale calls later rejected for duplicates.
> - Give every creative experiment a durable source ID and compare collected contribution after scrubs, rather than quoted payout.
> - Reserve an explicit dollar exposure limit for each new buyer; a high bid from a slow payer can be worse than a lower collectible bid.
