## 1. Classify the contact, channel, jurisdiction, and compensation before launch  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build a channel register before loading accounts into Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, or Salesloft. Record the recipient's state, business role, number type, address source, campaign purpose, dialing method, and seller identity. A business title does not convert a personal mobile number into an unrestricted calling endpoint. The TCPA, 47 U.S.C. § 227, and 47 CFR § 64.1200 distinguish equipment, artificial or prerecorded voice, called number, and message purpose; evaluate each combination rather than treating B2B as a universal exemption. AI-generated voices fall within artificial-voice restrictions. For covered telemarketing, obtain the required prior express written consent before using restricted technologies. Human dialing avoids some automation-specific issues, but it does not erase applicable do-not-call rules, state solicitation restrictions, calling hours, or deception prohibitions. Scrub covered campaigns against the National Do Not Call Registry using data no older than 31 days, and maintain seller-specific suppression records. Apply recipient-local scheduling and document reasonable revocations across the relevant campaign systems. Export a prelaunch evidence packet containing script version, list provenance, suppression timestamp, seller authorization, and equipment configuration. A vendor statement that its dialer is compliant cannot substitute for these campaign-level facts.

Commercial work-email sequences need their own operating rules. CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. § 7704, applies to B2B commercial email: use accurate routing information, nondeceptive subjects, required advertising identification, a valid physical postal address, and a clear opt-out mechanism. Keep that mechanism functional for at least 30 days after sending and honor requests within 10 business days; immediate suppression is the stronger operating standard. Both the promoted business and the sender can face responsibility, so allocate execution without pretending the contract eliminates statutory exposure. Configure suppression centrally rather than separately in every sequence. California's CCPA can cover business-contact personal information when the business meets statutory applicability conditions; its former broad B2B exemption has expired. Document whether the agency acts as a business, service provider, contractor, or third party for each use. California Civil Code § 1798.100 requires relevant contractual controls when personal information is disclosed to covered recipients. Public UCC records and privately enriched contact profiles require separate analysis; a public filing does not automatically make every appended mobile number unrestricted. Map applicable access, deletion, correction, and sale or sharing opt-out workflows to actual database fields and downstream recipients.

Maintain a jurisdiction matrix that follows recipients and activities, not just the agency's incorporation address. Florida Statutes § 501.059 and Oklahoma's Telephone Solicitation Act contain state-specific definitions and restrictions; determine their consumer, transaction, and technology scope before applying them to enterprise outreach. Do not assume every state provision covers every B2B call, and do not assume a federal exemption answers the state question. California Penal Code § 632 creates recording exposure for confidential communications; interstate discovery calls should use an announced recording request and an unrecorded alternative. For covered consumer telemarketing, evaluate the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, including applicable recordkeeping and calling restrictions. Although many B2B calls retain exemptions from much of that rule, its expanded misrepresentation prohibitions reach B2B telemarketing. Check state seller registration, bonding, and salesperson rules where applicable. Route international campaigns into a separate jurisdiction review rather than exporting the domestic playbook unchanged. The operating deliverable is a dated decision record: covered activity, relevant rule, implemented control, responsible owner, and evidence location. Revisit that record when a campaign adds SMS, synthetic voice, consumer contacts, a new state, or a compensation structure tied to regulated transactions.

## 2. Constrain the three mapped plays to lawful signals and genuine economic demand  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

For ucc1_expiration_refi_trigger_arbitrage, treat Secretary of State filings as research signals, never proof of financing terms. UCC § 9-515 generally gives ordinary financing statements five-year effectiveness and permits continuation during the final six months, subject to exceptions. Filing lapse is not loan maturity, and neither an initial filing nor a continuation establishes an MCA balance, stacking, interest cost, or refinancing eligibility. Identify businesses four to nine months after a relevant filing, then ask an authorized finance contact to verify the instrument and timing. Illustrative screening math is 1,000 filings, 120 verified operating-company matches, 24 substantive conversations, and six accepted opportunities at $1,500, producing $9,000 in agency fees before costs. That fee requires a permissible compensation structure; client financing principal never becomes agency revenue. California Financial Code § 22100 can require licensing for covered commercial loan brokering, while California and New York commercial financing disclosure regimes require separate product and role analysis. An MCA label does not settle transaction classification. Use direct mail, lawful B2B work-email sequences, and manual human-dialed outreach as channel fallbacks, paired with required state financing disclosures at the applicable offer stage. If compensation or conduct crosses a licensing boundary, narrow the service or use an appropriately licensed partner.

For telecom_voice_traffic_pumping_8yy_sms_arbitrage, reject manufactured demand and begin with carrier economics. Free conference bridges, radio call-in services, AI voice utilities, and verification endpoints create different traffic and compensation profiles; the play's name does not establish a lawful tariff entitlement. A CLEC revenue share requires review of access-stimulation definitions under 47 CFR § 61.3, applicable transport obligations under 47 CFR § 51.914, current 8YY compensation treatment, and the executed carrier agreement. International Tier-2 or Tier-3 routing does not remove those obligations where they apply. Require genuine user initiation for all monetized sessions, auditable fraud controls, and appropriate STIR/SHAKEN treatment for covered voice traffic. Attestation authenticates calling-number information; it does not prove consent or validate the business model. SMS instead needs documented user authorization and applicable messaging-program controls; voice attestation is not an SMS safeguard. Illustratively, 10 million genuine minutes at an assumed contractual $0.001 net contribution produce $10,000 before fixed costs, disputes, and reserves, not demand-generation revenue. The fallback is a non-access-stimulation SIP service with genuine customer demand. Distribute operations across jurisdictions only for legitimate service needs with destination-specific review, never to escape compensation restrictions. Pause settlement assumptions until carrier statements substantiate billable traffic and collectability.

For agency_ad_spend_card_multiplier_rebate_cycling, preserve the distinction between media throughput and agency earnings. Obtain written client authorization identifying the advertising accounts, eligible payment arrangement, budget, reward ownership, rebate treatment, and funding schedule. Card issuer terms, agency program eligibility, Google Ads or Meta billing permissions, and client procurement terms must support the arrangement; ordinary card access is insufficient. Illustrative monthly economics are $100,000 genuine media spend, $2,000 contract-permitted rewards, less $600 processing, $400 financing, and $700 client pass-through, leaving $300 before labor and other costs. The $100,000 is not agency revenue, and that $300 is not an addition to this chapter's canonical retainer-and-opportunity scorecard. Assess gross-versus-net accounting from the actual principal-agent relationship. Never purchase unneeded advertising, cycle artificial transactions, misstate merchant categories, or extend unauthorized client credit to chase points. Fall back to client-direct billing or prefunded approved agency terms and stop rewards-dependent spending. Across all three plays, screen sector restrictions before promising success-based compensation: securities introductions can implicate 15 U.S.C. § 78o, federally reimbursed healthcare referrals implicate 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b(b), and federal procurement contingent fees implicate FAR Subpart 3.4. A fixed-fee label cannot cure conduct that remains regulated.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Protect margin by challenging the signal before pricing it**
> 
> The expensive mistakes occur when an attractive event is treated as proof of debt, traffic eligibility, or reward ownership.
> - Store UCC filing, continuation, amendment, and termination events separately; ask the finance contact to validate facility timing before proposing refinancing.
> - Require sample carrier settlements and contractual dispute deductions before valuing a telecom introduction; a quoted tariff is not collected cash.
> - Run the advertising arrangement at zero rewards in the cash forecast; reject budgets that become unaffordable when issuer benefits change.
> - Create a Salesforce related record containing the opportunity evidence bundle, so invoice disputes reference immutable facts rather than overwritten lead fields.

## 3. Write the agreement around accepted evidence, bounded rights, and exact fee math  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use an MSA plus a campaign-specific statement of work. Define target accounts, excluded sectors, permitted channels, seller identity, subcontractor access, and the exact qualification evidence that earns a fee. A useful trigger is an attended discovery meeting with an identified buying-role participant, a documented business problem within scope, and an agreed next step, provided the account survives the contractual duplicate check. Do not sell calendar bookings as qualified pipeline without saying so. Specify a five-business-day review window, enumerated rejection reasons, and a named escalation contact. Any deemed acceptance mechanism needs conspicuous agreement and accessible evidence; unresolved objections should follow the dispute process rather than disappear through CRM status changes. Define cancellation, no-show replacement, refunds or credits, taxes, invoice timing, and termination assistance. Provide a sample clause: 'An Opportunity Fee becomes payable only when the meeting occurs, the Qualification Record contains every required field, and acceptance is established under the review procedure in this statement of work.' Add an attribution exhibit covering existing pipeline, account hierarchies, contacts moving employers, and competing sources. Store the agreed baseline account export before outreach begins, with a timestamp and restricted modification rights.

Reconcile the commercial schedule to operator revenue rather than client deal size. One illustrative Conservative construction is three clients paying $3,000 monthly for twelve months, plus 28 accepted opportunities at $1,500: $108,000 plus $42,000 equals $150,000. Costs of $120,000 leave $30,000 owner net profit, a 20% margin, with 2 FT plus 3 C. Base can comprise five clients at $7,500 monthly, plus 90 opportunities at $2,500: $450,000 plus $225,000 equals $675,000. Costs of $472,500 leave $202,500, a 30.0% margin, with the same team count. Scaled can comprise eight clients at $10,000 monthly, plus 80 opportunities at $3,000: $960,000 plus $240,000 equals $1,200,000. Costs of $720,000 leave $480,000, a 40% margin, with 3 FT plus 4 C. These are alternative annual constructions, not additive revenue streams or forecasts of identical client behavior. Contractor counts do not imply full-time utilization. Track delivery hours, collected cash, replacement meetings, and account concentration separately. The stated monthly operating range is not a substitute for total annual scenario costs; payroll, variable delivery, and other expenses must reconcile to the complete profit bridge.

Keep anti-circumvention narrow enough to identify what was actually introduced. Use named accounts, documented introductions, a negotiated protection period, and exclusions for proven preexisting relationships. Avoid claiming ownership of an entire market or all future client revenue. Allocate liability with a negotiated aggregate cap and explicit treatment of unpaid fees, confidentiality breaches, data incidents, intentional misconduct, and indemnities; state law can constrain enforceability. Assign responsibility for inaccurate client claims separately from unauthorized agency outreach, with defense control and prompt notice procedures. Select governing law, forum, escalation deadlines, and either litigation or a specified arbitration process. Define ownership of client account lists, commissioned deliverables, agency templates, campaign telemetry, and derived scoring models. A database ownership clause cannot authorize processing that privacy law or the source license forbids. Require subprocessor disclosure, minimum access, security incident notification timing, return or deletion at termination, and narrowly justified retention of billing or suppression evidence. Preserve only what serves a documented purpose, and prevent retained suppression records from becoming another prospecting list. Tie audits to specific records and reasonable access procedures so verification does not expose unrelated clients or grant unrestricted access to production systems.

## 4. Use exact scripts, evidence-first discovery, and a repeatable release procedure  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use scripts that identify the commercial purpose without pretending to know private account facts. In this illustrative campaign, Signal Ridge is the agency and Harbor Systems is its authorized client. Email subject: 'Commercial outreach: vendor discovery for your operations team.' Body: 'I work with Signal Ridge, an agency introducing enterprise teams to Harbor Systems. This is a commercial message about a potential vendor discussion. Are you the right person to evaluate workflow automation for your operations team? If this is relevant, I can arrange a twenty-minute discovery conversation. If you prefer no further marketing email from this campaign, reply unsubscribe or use the unsubscribe link below.' Send only after replacing illustrative identities with actual authorized identities and appending the sender's valid physical postal address and functioning opt-out link. Do not disguise advertising as an invoice, internal escalation, security alert, or prior conversation. For UCC-triggered outreach, add: 'We reviewed a public financing filing; that record does not tell us your current balance or maturity date. Would a review of financing options be relevant?' Never claim the prospect is distressed, overpaying, or approaching default based only on filing history.

For a permitted manually dialed call, use: 'Hello, I am calling from Signal Ridge on behalf of Harbor Systems about an enterprise vendor discussion. Is this a suitable time for a brief explanation?' After permission: 'We help arrange discovery meetings about operations automation. Do you evaluate that area, or should we stop contacting this number?' If declined: 'Understood. I will add this number to our do-not-call list for this campaign.' Apply the actual suppression scope required by the request and applicable rules, not a narrower interpretation chosen by the script. Before recording, say: 'May we record this discussion for accurate qualification notes? We can continue without recording.' Obtain agreement before recording confidential substance. For discovery, ask: 'What operating problem is receiving budget attention? Who evaluates a solution, who controls procurement, and what event would justify a next meeting?' Close with: 'May we share these business requirements and your work contact details with Harbor Systems to arrange that next meeting?' Record the answer accurately. Permission for that introduction does not establish permission for automated marketing calls, unrelated vendors, or indefinite resale of the contact profile.

For financing discovery, use: 'Are you authorized to discuss the business's financing options, and would you like an introduction to the identified provider? We do not determine approval or pricing. Any offer will come from the provider with the applicable disclosures.' Keep bank statements, account credentials, and sensitive underwriting documents out of the lead-generation CRM; use the provider's authorized intake process. For advertising arrangements, ask: 'Does your procurement policy permit agency payment of media, and who owns any rewards or rebates under the signed arrangement?' For telecom buyers, ask: 'Which genuine user action initiates each session, which carrier contract permits settlement, and who bears disputed traffic charges?' These questions expose commercial feasibility without soliciting manipulation. Train contractors on the approved scripts, then audit actual message samples and authorized call notes against them. Reconcile accepted opportunities to invoices weekly, investigate repeated rejection reasons, and measure concentration by collected client revenue. Stop a defective channel while preserving lawful alternatives. The service should survive a lost data vendor, withdrawn card benefit, disputed carrier settlement, or unavailable automation feature because its real asset is verified buyer intent supported by retrievable evidence.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Classify the campaign and compensation
   - Create the jurisdiction, channel, sector, and payment-role record.
   - Identify recipient states, contact types, equipment, actual seller, relevant registrations, and any financing, securities, healthcare, telecom, or procurement restrictions.
2. Bind scope to evidence and data rights
   - Execute the MSA, statement of work, and applicable data terms.
   - Attach the account baseline, qualification fields, fee schedule, acceptance procedure, attribution window, source licenses, ownership rules, and termination handling.
3. Test the actual outreach path
   - Verify suppression, sender identity, permissions, scripts, and routing.
   - Use internal test records to confirm opt-out propagation, correct postal identification, lawful scheduling, recording choices, and the absence of unauthorized automated outreach.
4. Release, reconcile, and investigate exceptions
   - Launch the authorized campaign and review evidence-linked invoices weekly.
   - Compare accepted opportunities with qualification records and collected fees; suspend defective channels, investigate disputes, and preserve lawful alternative delivery methods.
