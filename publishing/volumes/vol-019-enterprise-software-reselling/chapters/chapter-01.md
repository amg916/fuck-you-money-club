## 1. Define the authorized transaction before selling the software  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Enterprise software reselling is the business of acquiring authorized commercial rights to supply software subscriptions, licenses, or related services to an organization, then earning the contractual resale spread and separately priced delivery fees. The operator connects an independent software vendor, a distributor or marketplace where applicable, and a buyer whose purchasing process may require security review, negotiated terms, a purchase order, and documented acceptance. A value-added reseller adds selection, deployment, migration, integration, support, or renewal administration rather than merely forwarding a checkout link. Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider arrangements, AWS Channel Partner Private Offers, and direct vendor partner programs are different contracting rails, not interchangeable labels for the same entitlement. Authorization must cover the actual product, territory, customer class, and transaction route. A badge on a website does not establish permission to discount every SKU. Before quoting, identify who contracts with the customer, who supplies support, who bears payment exposure, and who can cancel or change the subscription. Those answers determine the operating model more reliably than headline deal size. A $100,000 subscription sold for $112,000 creates a $12,000 preliminary spread, not $112,000 of spendable earnings; sales effort, support, collection losses, and delivery costs still consume that spread.

Five useful operator archetypes separate this market into manageable businesses: the vertical application specialist, the Microsoft estate operator, the marketplace transaction specialist, the implementation-led integrator, and the renewal portfolio manager. A two-person core team should choose one primary archetype and one adjacent service instead of attempting universal coverage. For example, a HubSpot specialist serving industrial distributors can reuse permission models, lifecycle definitions, and reporting templates; a generalist covering every CRM loses that repetition. A Microsoft operator instead builds tenant administration, subscription reconciliation, and escalation discipline. A marketplace specialist earns their place through offer accuracy and purchasing coordination, but must not claim that marketplace access alone creates exclusivity. Implementation-led operators can earn attractive service contribution while assuming real delivery risk, including failed imports and unpriced customization. Renewal managers depend on accurate customer authority, contract dates, and service history, not possession of an email list. The table below defines each archetype by the operational asset that compounds. Across all five, the scarce resource is accountable execution inside a narrow customer workflow. Vendor certifications help open conversations, but measurable deployment outcomes, clean reconciliations, and references from comparable buyers create the repeat purchase behavior that a small team can actually defend.

The revenue boundary is an accounting conclusion, not a marketing preference. The canonical scorecard uses gross license resale and service billings, with vendor costs included in margin. Apply that presentation only where the operator is principal for the specified good or service under ASC 606, including its principal-versus-agent guidance. Assess whether the operator controls that specified good or service before transfer; invoice routing, payment collection, and the presence of a distributor do not decide the question alone. If the operator merely arranges another party's supply, recognized revenue is generally the earned fee or commission, not the full customer purchase. A mixed contract can require separate conclusions for software and implementation. Consider a $120,000 software order and a $15,000 deployment engagement: principal treatment may support $135,000 gross revenue with software acquisition costs recorded separately, while an agent arrangement paying $9,000 plus the same principal service engagement produces $24,000 operator revenue. Customer sales processed through that software are neither amount. Recognition timing also follows performance obligations: signatures, cash receipts, and annual prepayments are not automatically earned revenue. Document the conclusion and allocation before building dashboards, so pipeline value never silently substitutes for recognized revenue or collected contribution.

## 2. Reconcile owner profit, supplier commitments, and collection exposure  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Keep the three scorecard outcomes fixed: Conservative revenue of $300,000 at 8% produces $24,000 annual owner net profit; Base revenue of $1,400,000 at 14.0% produces $196,000; Scaled revenue of $2,500,000 at 20% produces $500,000. One illustrative bridge allocates Conservative costs to $210,000 of vendor and direct delivery costs plus $66,000 of remaining operating costs. The Base bridge uses $980,000 plus $224,000; the Scaled bridge uses $1,720,000 plus $280,000. Each bridge reconciles exactly, but these cost splits are chapter assumptions rather than additional scorecard facts. They imply contribution before remaining overhead of $90,000, $420,000, and $780,000 respectively. Better mix, reusable implementations, and renewal efficiency must explain the improvement; purchasing more software at the same spread does not automatically raise net margin. The remaining operating budgets average $5,500, approximately $18,667, and approximately $23,333 monthly, within the stated $5,000 to $25,000 monthly operating range. Actual classification depends on the ledger, and labor cannot appear in both direct delivery and overhead. Do not treat distributor rebates as earned merely because a target appears reachable; establish the applicable conditions and record reversals when eligibility fails.

Working capital can break an apparently profitable reseller before the renewal book matures. Suppose a principal transaction bills the customer $120,000 and requires a $108,000 supplier payment. If the supplier requires payment on day 30 while the customer pays on day 75, the operator must bridge $108,000 for 45 days, excluding other business obligations. That exposure already exceeds the low end of the $15,000 to $75,000 startup capital range. A customer's monthly payment preference does not shorten an annual supplier commitment. Require a deposit, matching payment dates, a suitably approved credit facility, or a different transaction structure before accepting the obligation. Marketplace billing may change who collects cash and when settlements arrive, but its actual terms decide the residual exposure. Build a weekly schedule showing supplier due dates, expected settlement dates, customer disputes, and contractually available cancellation remedies. A $12,000 spread is not protection against a $108,000 default. Similarly, tax collected for remittance is not operating revenue, and customer deposits may remain liabilities before performance. Measure collected contribution alongside booked orders so a salesperson cannot improve the dashboard by selling longer commitments to buyers whose ability or willingness to pay has not been established.

The canonical staffing cases are 2 FT + 3 C for both Conservative and Base, and 3 FT + 4 C for Scaled, totaling seven people in the final case. Preserve those counts rather than quietly replacing the Conservative operation with a solo founder. The scorecard does not establish salaries, contractor utilization, geography, or owner compensation treatment, so the cost bridges above do not validate a full market-rate payroll. Price those inputs explicitly before committing to hires. At $24,000 annual owner profit, a single $12,000 unrecoverable account consumes half the modeled result; even modest support leakage matters. Use the first 3 to 9 months, the stated first-deal window, to secure authorization, build references, and prove one delivery pattern rather than assume immediate recurring cash. Assign one accountable commercial owner and one delivery owner, with contractors handling bounded specialist work. Renewal alerts, quote generation, and license reconciliation are the core automation levers, but each needs a named reviewer and exception queue. Track support hours per customer, quote correction frequency, and collections aging alongside revenue per person. A higher selling price does not create operating leverage if every additional customer requires bespoke administration that the staffing plan cannot absorb.

## 3. Choose a sub-vertical where procurement knowledge becomes reusable  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The mapped signal play saas_technographic_churn_pixel_scraping, or DNS/Script Technographic Drop-Off & Renewal-Window Interception, is a prospect prioritization method, not proof of contract expiration. Crawl permitted public commercial pages and inspect public DNS TXT and CNAME records weekly for evidence associated with Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Segment, or NetSuite. Weekly observations establish an interval between sightings, not the exact installation or removal day. Browser-side tags can disappear because of redesigns, regional configuration, server-side tracking, or scanner errors; an old DNS verification record can persist after a vendor relationship ends. A tag does not expose contract term length. Treat months 10 and 11 after a reliable adoption observation as a testable outreach hypothesis, then ask the buyer to confirm renewal timing. For illustration, 200 monitored domains producing 20 corroborated changes, five qualified conversations, and one $60,000 principal resale at 10% preliminary spread yield $6,000 before acquisition and support costs. If confirmation fails, combine passive DNS evidence with public job-posting mentions and route only corroborated vendor-switch signals. Respect published access restrictions, conservative request rates, and applicable privacy duties. Do not evade access controls or collect customer credentials to improve signal coverage. Discovery accuracy, not scrape volume, determines whether this play earns its operating cost.

Select the transaction rail after understanding the customer's buying constraints. Microsoft CSP involves program roles and distributor relationships that determine support and commercial obligations. AWS CPPO lets participating channel partners construct eligible private offers using a seller's authorized commercial terms; it does not create a universal discount schedule. Azure multiparty private offers have their own participation, geographic, offer, and customer eligibility conditions. Never assume one marketplace's structure applies to another. For each quote, obtain current supplier economics, applicable platform charges, settlement terms, refund treatment, and any account-specific conditions. Ask the buyer whether the proposed purchase is eligible for their particular cloud commitment before suggesting that it consumes committed spend. A $200,000 offer with $180,000 supplier cost and an illustrative $2,000 operator-borne transaction charge leaves $18,000 before support, not $20,000. The charge is a modeling input, not a published platform rate. Direct registered resale can be simpler when a marketplace route is ineligible, but the vendor must authorize that alternative and the buyer must accept its procurement implications. The comparison matrix below therefore describes fee mechanisms rather than claiming fixed discounts. Durability comes from navigating the correct route consistently while preserving economics through order changes, cancellations, and settlement.

Commercial authorization and statutory duties belong in the same transaction file, although they answer different questions. Copyright rights under 17 U.S.C. 106 explain why a reseller cannot assume permission to reproduce or distribute software outside the license chain. Federal civilian procurement under FAR 12.212 addresses acquisition of commercial computer software and documentation under customary licenses, subject to federal law and agency needs; it does not make every vendor term enforceable against an agency. For California personal information, California Civil Code 1798.100(d) requires covered businesses to use specified contractual protections when disclosing information to service providers, contractors, or third parties. Determine the operator's actual role and whether the law applies instead of attaching a generic processing schedule to every resale. Installation work may introduce administrator access and personal information handling that a license-only transaction never requires. Use customer-authorized accounts, least-privilege roles, logged changes, and agreed deletion or return procedures. Separately, review purchase-order conflicts and applicable state enactments of UCC 2-207 where relevant; software and mixed-service classification can affect the analysis. Resolve inconsistent support, indemnity, payment, and termination terms before accepting orders. A clean technical deployment does not repair a transaction whose signed documents allocate obligations the operator cannot perform.

## 4. Operate nine mapped plays through one controlled customer lifecycle  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The nine-play map combines the supplied technographic play with eight chapter-defined operating motions, rather than presenting additional invented catalog identifiers. Registered SaaS resale, Microsoft CSP administration, AWS CPPO transactions, Azure private offers, implementation and migration, license reconciliation, renewal recovery, and procurement consolidation complete the map. Each earns money differently and needs its own fallback. For implementation, $30,000 of service fees less 150 delivery hours at $120 per hour leaves $12,000 before shared overhead; 50 unpriced additional hours reduce that contribution to $6,000. License reconciliation can bill $6,000 while consuming 30 hours at $100, leaving $3,000 before overhead, but customer subscription savings are not reseller revenue. A procurement retainer of $2,000 monthly yields $24,000 annual service revenue; it is not permission to count the buyer's entire software budget. The matrix provides illustrative transaction economics, not components to add onto the canonical scenarios. Some plays overlap on the same customer order, so aggregate only distinct recognized consideration. A renewal recovered through technographic outreach remains one sale, not two. If a vendor refuses authorization, retain only the independently authorized service work and remove the anticipated resale spread from the forecast. That discipline prevents an attractive play map from becoming double-counted pipeline.

Run the customer lifecycle through a single opportunity record containing the buyer's entity, supplier authorization, transaction route, product configuration, recognition conclusion, payment schedule, and renewal evidence. HubSpot or Salesforce can own commercial activity; Jira Service Management can own delivery issues; an accounting ledger remains authoritative for recognized revenue and receivables. A lightweight script can compare distributor exports with customer entitlements using subscription identifiers, SKU, quantity, term, and currency. Do not reconcile on product names alone because editions and commitment periods often look deceptively similar. Automate renewal reminders at customer-appropriate intervals, such as 120, 90, 60, and 30 days before the confirmed decision deadline, while preserving earlier supplier notice requirements. Generated quotes should carry source-price timestamps, validity dates, and explicit service assumptions. Route changed quantities, expired discounts, and unusual payment terms to a named human reviewer. After activation, verify the correct customer tenant and purchasing account, collect acceptance evidence, and reconcile the first invoice. Restrict automation credentials to necessary actions and retain change logs. The objective is not autonomous purchasing: it is faster preparation with fewer unexplained exceptions, so the same small team can manage more renewals without increasing avoidable support work.

Durable small-team moats are cumulative and observable: a narrow reference base, accurate entitlements, trusted procurement coordination, reusable implementation assets, and a renewal history that predicts actual work. Fragile beginner hype substitutes partner logos, gross transaction volume, supposed secret discounts, or unverified buying signals for those assets. Score the business against collected contribution per account, support hours per renewal, supplier concentration, overdue receivables, and the proportion of renewals with buyer-confirmed dates. These measures expose a portfolio that looks large but cannot support its obligations. Review vendor policy changes monthly and before material commitments, because discount revisions, program exits, or changed cancellation rights can invalidate yesterday's unit economics. Maintain a second authorized route where practical, but do not promise seamless portability when licenses or marketplace commitments prevent it. If channel competition compresses license spreads, defend a measurable service outcome rather than conceal fees inside confusing quotes. If collections slow, reduce new unsecured exposure rather than chasing volume to cover the shortfall. The $500,000 Scaled owner-profit case is a disciplined operating result on $2,500,000 gross revenue, not a commission on customer transaction volume. Build toward it by retaining the canonical headcount and testing every proposed efficiency against actual delivery capacity and cash.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Qualify the buyer and transaction route
   - Confirm the customer entity, product need, purchasing authority, and authorized supply path.
   - Treat technographic observations as leads; obtain buyer-confirmed timing and verify current vendor or distributor permissions.
2. Price contribution and cash exposure
   - Build a quote from current supplier terms, delivery estimates, charges, and payment dates.
   - Record the principal-agent conclusion, test cancellation asymmetry, and identify the maximum cash shortfall before accepting liability.
3. Deliver and reconcile the actual entitlement
   - Activate the correct customer account, execute the scoped work, and retain acceptance evidence.
   - Match purchased SKU, quantity, term, and currency against activation records, customer invoices, and supplier or marketplace statements.
4. Collect, review contribution, and prepare renewal
   - Track settlement, close exceptions, and schedule reminders against confirmed contractual deadlines.
   - Compare actual support and delivery costs with the quote, update renewal quantities, and revalidate supplier authorization before requoting.
