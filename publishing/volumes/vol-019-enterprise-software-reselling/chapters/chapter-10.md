## 1. Map nine operator plays into one evidence-controlled event system  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the automation boundary around a commercial record, not around an autonomous salesperson. Candidate #23's nine mapped operator plays are account research, qualification, deal registration, quote preparation, marketplace offer execution, implementation attachment, license reconciliation, renewal orchestration, and distribution referrals. Treat this chapter's mapping as the operating taxonomy, with one accountable owner and one measurable output per play. The first full-time operator owns vendor relationships, pricing decisions, procurement conversations, and customer commitments. The second owns delivery coordination, renewal operations, collections visibility, and exception triage. Three contractors supply bounded integration engineering, implementation capacity, and channel content production; contractor seats do not imply unlimited weekly hours. Record each person's contracted availability before allocating review work. HubSpot or Salesforce holds the opportunity record; a PostgreSQL event ledger holds processing history; vendor portals remain authoritative for program rights and transaction status. Assign stable customer, tenant, opportunity, and subscription identifiers before adding AI. Similar company names, subsidiaries, and shared email domains otherwise produce attractive but incorrect account matches. Every event must carry its source, retrieval time, permission scope, and evidence link so a reviewer can reconstruct the decision without searching chat transcripts. Separate customer-specific records from reusable channel templates at ingestion, rather than trusting a later prompt to separate them.

Use Python 3.9 workers for normalization, deterministic arithmetic, and workflow routing; use an LLM only where extraction or drafting beats a fixed parser. A webhook receiver verifies the provider's supported signature or authentication mechanism, checks freshness where supported, persists the event durably, and acknowledges receipt before expensive processing. AWS API Gateway, Lambda, and SQS provide one implementation; an authenticated service and managed queue provide another. The ledger stores event_id, source, account_id, object_version, payload_hash, received_at, state, and reviewer_id. Enforce a unique source-event key to neutralize retries. Workers fetch current authoritative records before preparing consequential changes because delivery can be duplicated or out of order. Put credentials in AWS Secrets Manager or Azure Key Vault, grant separate read and write identities, and redact tokens from traces. Documents, email bodies, and web pages are untrusted evidence: instructions inside them cannot authorize tools, change pricing rules, or select another customer's records. Send the model only the fields required for the current task. Persist structured output separately from source evidence, validate its schema, and route unsupported claims to a reviewer rather than allowing persuasive prose to fill missing facts. Tenant isolation must be enforced by application queries and storage permissions, not by the model's willingness to obey a prompt.

Measure the 70% target as avoided repetitive labor on a fixed workload, not as the percentage of messages touched by software. Establish a two-week baseline across research, qualification, fulfillment preparation, and acquisition operations, recording active minutes and completed outcomes. In an illustrative matched cohort of 100 events, manual handling consumes 1,000 minutes. After automation, scheduled human review consumes 220 minutes and exception recovery consumes 60 minutes, leaving 280 minutes and removing 72% of repetitive effort. This is a measurement design, not a forecast for every reseller. Include rework, sampling, queue maintenance, and failed runs in the automated cohort; otherwise the dashboard conceals labor in contractors' inboxes. Break results out by play because an easy research classifier can mask a fragile provisioning workflow. Track unsupported-claim rate, quote correction rate, entitlement mismatch rate, and time to resolve exceptions alongside minutes saved. Reserve named review windows for the two full-time operators and cap releases at their actual capacity. When the queue exceeds that cap, slow intake instead of relaxing evidence requirements. The target concerns repetitive handling; negotiation, vendor disputes, architecture choices, and customer commitments remain human work. Publish a weekly denominator showing eligible events, excluded events, and the reason for each exclusion.

## 2. Automate renewals, quote preparation, and license reconciliation safely  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start renewal orchestration with signed order terms and current subscription records, not with a universal ninety-day countdown. Microsoft CSP new commerce subscriptions can have term-specific change and cancellation rules; AWS Marketplace agreements and private offers carry their own commercial conditions. Store term end, renewal behavior, notice deadline, quantity, billing cadence, and the source supporting each field. A daily Python scheduler computes configurable reminders at 120, 90, 60, and 30 days where those dates are useful, then prioritizes the earliest binding action deadline. A model may extract candidate dates from an order document, but deterministic date checks and a reviewer must establish the operative record. Compare purchased seats with assigned and meaningfully used seats only where customer authorization covers those datasets. Generate a renewal packet containing the current entitlement snapshot, unresolved support issues, proposed scope, and quote assumptions. Suppress duplicate messages after a customer response and keep an audit trail of delivery failures. Never let an inferred usage decline trigger automatic cancellation, employee deprovisioning, or seat reduction. Those actions can interrupt operations and may not reduce the current contractual obligation. Escalate approaching deadlines to the relationship owner with a specific decision request and a documented last-safe action time.

Quote generation should resemble a compiler: approved inputs enter, reproducible commercial outputs leave, and invalid combinations fail visibly. Represent currency with Python Decimal, store the currency code explicitly, and distinguish monthly billing from the full committed term. Validate SKU, region, customer segment, license metric, support tier, quantity, term dates, tax treatment, and vendor authorization before rendering a document. Keep vendor acquisition cost, customer license price, implementation price, marketplace charges, and expected delivery cost in separate fields. An LLM can draft the scope narrative from approved facts; it cannot invent discounts, service levels, indemnities, or eligibility. AWS Channel Partner Private Offers use applicable selling authorizations and reseller offer mechanics; Microsoft CSP ordering follows the partner's actual direct or indirect relationship and supported Partner Center capabilities. Do not assume equivalent APIs or pricing controls across those routes. Persist a quote version and its input digest, then require the commercial owner to approve the exact rendered output before customer release. If vendor pricing changes, expire the draft and regenerate it rather than silently modifying a reviewed quote. Customer acceptance must link to the accepted version, not merely to the opportunity's latest document. This prevents later reconciliation from treating an unsigned revision as contractual evidence.

License reconciliation joins three different truths: contractual entitlement, provisioning state, and observed assignment. Collect authorized exports through Microsoft Graph, Partner Center, vendor APIs, or customer-provided files according to available permissions; access to one system does not imply permission to inspect every tenant. Normalize timestamps, SKU aliases, tenant identifiers, subscription identifiers, and effective dates before comparing quantities. Shared devices, pooled licenses, service accounts, and bundle components require product-specific rules, not a universal named-user calculation. Classify differences as timing lag, mapping error, assignment exception, or commercial discrepancy, with evidence attached to each finding. A reconciliation result creates a remediation proposal, never an automatic accusation or purchase. For fulfillment, use a state machine such as prepared, reviewed, submitted, confirmed, and exception. Before retrying an ambiguous submission, query the provider's transaction record; a timeout after acceptance must not create duplicate purchases. Use provider idempotency features where supported and maintain local duplicate protection regardless. Reversible internal updates can be retried automatically, while purchases and access changes require stronger confirmation. If the API is unavailable, export a dated review packet for a designated operator to process through the authorized portal, then reconcile the portal receipt into the ledger before resuming automated writes.

**Renewal, Quote, and Entitlement Failure-Recovery Matrix**

| Failure condition and detection | Automated containment and evidence | Named human decision boundary | Recovery and completion proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Renewal date extraction conflicts with signed order language | Freeze reminders requiring that date and attach both sources | Relationship owner determines the controlling contractual notice deadline | Correct the operative record and regenerate dated reminder tasks |
| Vendor price source changes after customer quote review | Invalidate the unreleased draft and preserve its previous digest | Commercial owner approves revised economics before customer release | Link the newly reviewed quote to current price evidence |
| Customer tenant identifier differs between order and discovery | Block provisioning preparation and display both identifier sources | Delivery owner obtains direct customer confirmation of tenant identity | Resume only after matching identifiers across the reviewed packet |
| Submission times out after the provider might accept | Stop blind retries and retrieve provider transaction status | Commercial owner resolves any ambiguous purchase commitment first | Record confirmed receipt before permitting another submission attempt |
| Assignment totals exceed normalized contractual entitlement quantities | Create a discrepancy packet without changing customer access | Delivery owner checks product rules and effective dates | Close findings using verified mappings or authorized remediation evidence |
| Provider endpoint remains unavailable beyond bounded retry limits | Move the event into a recoverable exception queue | Designated operator chooses authorized portal processing or deferral | Import portal receipts before releasing queued dependent actions |

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Ingest and authenticate
   - Persist authenticated events before scheduling downstream processing.
   - Record the provider event identifier, payload digest, tenant scope, receipt time, and validation outcome; reject unauthorized sources.
2. Prepare and reconcile
   - Build a versioned packet from current authoritative inputs.
   - Run deterministic pricing and entitlement checks, attach source evidence, and isolate model-generated language from verified facts.
3. Review and release
   - Obtain recorded authority for the exact consequential output.
   - Check reviewer permissions and packet freshness, then submit once through the supported provider interface or authorized portal.
4. Confirm and recover
   - Reconcile the external receipt before closing the event.
   - Resolve ambiguous outcomes through status retrieval, route failures to an owner, and retain replayable evidence without duplicating purchases.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Verify four conditions before any consequential external action**
> 
> A generated draft is preparation. Publication, purchase, provisioning, and customer commitments require the appropriate recorded authority.
> - Verify current reseller authorization, permitted geography, eligible product, and the specific direct or indirect transaction route.
> - Verify customer tenant, purchasing entity, currency, term, quantity, and the evidence supporting every binding date.
> - Verify vendor cost, selling price, marketplace charges, delivery capacity, and the exact reviewed document version.
> - Verify a named authorized reviewer, a duplicate-submission check, and a recovery path before releasing the action.

## 3. Build distribution loops from verified deployment evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Programmatic distribution should publish useful procurement and deployment knowledge, not thousands of lightly rewritten product pages. Build a controlled catalog of vendor-approved product facts, deployment prerequisites, supported procurement routes, implementation scope patterns, and dated pricing assumptions. A Python generator can assemble segment-specific pages for a narrow workload, such as Microsoft 365 tenant consolidation or a documented AWS Marketplace purchasing path, while an LLM drafts readable explanations from that catalog. Each page needs an actual operator contribution: a migration checklist, entitlement worksheet, private offer readiness assessment, or implementation acceptance template. Do not imply vendor endorsement, reproduce restricted partner material, or publish customer-derived configurations without the required rights. Under 17 U.S.C. 106, reproduction and adaptation rights matter even when a model rewrites source material. Store publication permissions beside reusable assets, and review brand-use conditions separately. Instrument Search Console, first-party form submissions, and CRM attribution to measure qualified opportunities rather than raw page count. Version pages when vendor terms change and remove obsolete transaction instructions promptly. Publication remains a human-reviewed external action; internal drafts can be generated freely within the authorized source boundary. A smaller library of accurate, maintained assets is easier for a five-person pod to defend and convert.

Outbound automation should improve account selection and evidence quality before it increases message volume. Use authorized account lists and documented firmographic sources to identify companies with a plausible deployment need, then draft a short hypothesis tied to public facts. Do not present inferred software usage, renewal dates, or buying authority as confirmed information. The relationship owner reviews targeting rules and initial sequences; routine messages may run only within the approved campaign scope and suppression controls. For commercial email, 15 U.S.C. 7704 supplies concrete requirements covering truthful transmission information, nondeceptive subjects, required identification, a valid physical postal address, and a functioning opt-out mechanism. Honor applicable opt-outs within ten business days and suppress internally immediately when received. Keep a durable suppression record that survives list refreshes. Stop sequences on replies, complaints, and delivery failures; do not route around provider restrictions with replacement identities. Qualification automation can summarize responses and propose discovery questions, but pricing promises and vendor eligibility claims require verified evidence. Track positive replies, accepted meetings, qualified opportunities, paid invoices, and delivery contribution as separate stages. A campaign producing many meetings but expensive unqualified implementation requests is consuming scarce pod capacity rather than creating a distribution advantage. Feed that result back into targeting rules each week.

The strongest referral loop begins after a verified operational win. Following an authorized license cleanup or successful implementation, prepare a customer-facing evidence pack showing the starting condition, approved changes, measured outcome, and remaining tasks. Ask the customer whether they want a version suitable for an internal purchasing committee or a peer introduction; do not assume permission to name their organization publicly. Reusable worksheets and readiness assessments can circulate among procurement, IT, and department leaders, bringing the reseller into additional buying conversations without revealing tenant-level data. Attribute introductions with explicit CRM relationship records rather than hidden tracking. Separately, use permitted vendor co-selling and deal-registration processes to return high-quality opportunity evidence to the channel manager. Accurate scope, clean buyer identity, and dependable implementation follow-through make the next referral easier to evaluate. Route new leads back through the same qualification and authorization checks; referral status does not establish purchasing authority. Measure the loop as collected contribution from referred accounts divided by the fully loaded effort required to support it. Feed objections and delivery defects into the content catalog, then update the corresponding research and quote templates. This creates compounding operating knowledge without pretending that traffic, partner introductions, or marketplace visibility automatically become recognized revenue.

## 4. Price automation, rehearse recovery, and scale only measured capacity  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Price every automated run with explicit assumptions and include the human work it creates. For an illustrative extraction task, assume 1,500 input tokens at $2 per million and 500 output tokens at $8 per million: model cost is $0.003 plus $0.004, or $0.007. Add an assumed $0.005 for queue, compute, logging, and attributable storage, producing $0.012 of machine cost per completed run. These are planning inputs, not quoted provider prices. If 20% of runs receive three minutes of review at a loaded $60 hourly rate, expected review cost is $0.60 per run and combined cost is $0.612. At 5,000 runs monthly, that means $60 machine expense plus $3,000 review expense, with 50 review hours required. Add exception investigation, software subscriptions, and maintenance separately; the example does not conceal them inside token pricing. High-consequence quotes and purchasing actions still receive complete review, so this sampling assumption applies only to the eligible low-risk extraction population. Estimate each play separately and set budget alarms on actual retries and model usage. Cheap text generation can still be operationally expensive when weak extraction pushes hundreds of ambiguous records into the two full-time operators' queues. The earlier labor-saving cohort is a separate experiment, not this cost model's denominator.

Keep the canonical scorecard intact when evaluating these costs. Conservative gross license resale and service billings are $300,000; an 8% net margin leaves $24,000 annual owner net profit and implies $276,000 of total modeled costs. Base billings of $1,400,000 at 14.0% leave $196,000 and imply $1,204,000 of costs. Scaled billings of $2,500,000 at 20% leave $500,000 and imply $2,000,000 of costs. Vendor acquisition costs are included within those margins, not deducted again from the stated profits. Conservative and Base use 2 FT + 3 C; Scaled uses 3 FT + 4 C, seven people rather than five. Allocate automation expense within the relevant modeled cost envelope or explicitly revise an implementation budget; never add hypothetical labor savings directly to canonical owner profit. Keep customer cloud consumption and unrelated marketplace spend outside reseller billings. Where a particular arrangement makes the business an agent, determine the applicable presentation rather than assuming every routed dollar is gross revenue; this chapter preserves the supplied gross-resale scorecard basis. Track implementation scope contribution separately from license resale contribution, then reconcile both with shared operating costs. Slow customer collections can still create a cash shortfall when vendor obligations fall due before customer receipts.

Scale by proving repeatability under failure, not by removing the last reviewer. Maintain test fixtures for duplicate webhooks, expired vendor authorization, mismatched tenants, changed prices, rejected model output, ambiguous purchase responses, and delayed customer payment. Rehearse queue replay with writes disabled, then compare proposed actions against recorded outcomes before permitting production execution. Use bounded exponential backoff with jitter for transient errors, stop retries on permission failures, and send exhausted events to an owned exception queue. A nightly reconciliation should detect events that never reached a terminal state and compare external receipts with internal commitments. Restrict emergency access and record every manual portal action. Under 18 U.S.C. 1030, automation does not create authorization to access systems beyond granted scope. For covered federal contract information systems, implement FAR 52.204-21 when the clause applies, including required access controls and safeguarding practices. Assign the integration contractor a documented restoration procedure and train a full-time operator to use it. Before moving toward the seven-person Scaled configuration, verify review capacity, collected contribution, defect rates, and support coverage over several complete renewal and delivery cycles. More events are useful only when the pod can preserve evidence, fulfill commitments, and recover without turning vendor-policy changes into customer-facing errors.
