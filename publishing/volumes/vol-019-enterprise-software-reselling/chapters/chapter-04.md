## 1. Ring-fence float before buying capacity  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Allocate startup capital against the cash conversion cycle, not the apparent size of a software contract. At $25,000, place $15,000 in a separate working-capital reserve; at $150,000, reserve $90,000. Both allocations protect 60%, exceeding the 50% minimum. The remaining envelopes fund entity and banking setup, insurance and contract review, baseline systems, vendor enablement, and initial delivery or acquisition work. These are proposed spending limits, not vendor quotations or additional scorecard expenses. Release each envelope only against a named deliverable: an executed distributor agreement, a tested invoice, or a completed implementation checklist. Keep customer deposits visible as obligations until earned; a bank balance containing prepaid subscriptions is not unrestricted owner cash. Maintain separate ledger accounts for resale billings, implementation revenue, vendor purchases, marketplace deductions, and refundable deposits. Reconcile the operating account weekly and the reserve account after every committed order. The smallest capitalization cannot support twelve months of speculative hiring. At $5,000 monthly operating expenditure, the entire $25,000 lasts five months before vendor purchases; the $10,000 outside reserve lasts only two. A stated three-to-twelve-month first-deal cycle therefore demands founder-led selling, staged spending, customer prepayment, or additional committed funding before obligations accumulate.

Model payment timing by contract rather than assuming marketplace settlement matches distributor terms. Suppose a registered annual license sale bills $100,000 and creates an $85,000 supplier payable due on day thirty. If the customer pays on day sixty, the business must bridge $85,000 for thirty days before considering payroll, taxes, or disputes. The nominal $15,000 spread does not fund that bridge. A 50% customer deposit reduces the uncovered purchase obligation to $35,000, still larger than the lean reserve. The proper fallback is a smaller order, full advance payment where commercially acceptable, supplier terms that match collections, or a transaction structure that removes the reseller's purchase obligation. Do not characterize receivables as cash until settlement occurs. Maintain a thirteen-week forecast with customer receipt dates, distributor due dates, tax remittances, marketplace adjustments, and implementation payroll. Stress every material receipt by thirty additional days and model a disputed invoice separately. Credit availability counts only when documented and drawable under its actual conditions. Record borrowing interest and fees in deal contribution. Where a lender takes receivables collateral, review the security agreement, filing jurisdiction, and priority under the applicable state's UCC Article 9 before promising the same collateral to another funder.

Keep the canonical scorecard separate from the launch cash budget. Conservative gross revenue of $300,000 less $276,000 total costs produces $24,000 owner net profit at 8%; Base revenue of $1,400,000 less $1,204,000 produces $196,000 at 14.0%; Scaled revenue of $2,500,000 less $2,000,000 produces $500,000 at 20%. Those cost totals already encompass vendor purchases and operating expenses; subtracting the infrastructure budget again would double count costs. The canonical teams are two full-time people plus three contractors in Conservative and Base, then three full-time people plus four contractors in Scaled. Contractor headcount does not imply five-day weekly utilization, but hours and rates must be explicit in the staffing model. Do not make an otherwise unworkable plan appear profitable by silently excluding owner labor or deferring salaries. A $5,000–$25,000 monthly operating envelope annualizes to $60,000–$300,000 before separately modeled vendor purchases. The top of that range cannot fit the Conservative cost ceiling even before buying licenses. Treat the scenarios as annual operating cases, not a promise that startup year revenue arrives evenly. Track gross resale and service billings for scorecard comparison, while separately evaluating principal-versus-agent presentation for each actual contract with the accountant.

## 2. Configure twelve layers without twelve expensive platforms  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the stack around one customer record, one commercial approval trail, and one accounting ledger. The twelve-layer matrix gives exact planning price points and named tiers, not a claim that checkout prices were verified today. Prices are USD list-price assumptions before taxes, discounts, and optional features; annual commitments can require cash earlier than monthly accounting expense suggests. Recheck the vendor's current offer before purchase. For two users, Google Workspace Business Starter at $7 per user monthly on an annual commitment contributes $14, Bitwarden Teams at $4 contributes $8, one DocuSign Personal seat contributes $10, and one Backblaze Computer Backup subscription contributes $9. That $41 monthly-equivalent software subtotal covers only the stated quantities; it excludes hardware, additional endpoints, professional services, insurance, labor, and usage charges. The free layers do not make the whole business free to operate. Start Microsoft CSP through an authorized indirect provider rather than assuming a new company satisfies direct-bill requirements. For AWS CPPO, confirm seller eligibility, the independent software vendor's selling authorization, and the supported commercial arrangement. A zero-dollar account subscription says nothing about transaction charges or responsibility for supplier payment. Document those charges separately in each deal worksheet.

Configure identity before importing customer contacts or vendor credentials. Use individual accounts, phishing-resistant multifactor authentication where supported, two controlled recovery administrators, and an offline recovery record. A password manager stores secrets; it does not replace account-level permissions or employee offboarding. Google Workspace Business Starter is a narrow entry tier, so do not assume advanced retention, endpoint controls, or audit capabilities that belong to other editions. BitLocker requires a supporting Windows edition; FileVault is available on supported Macs. Record endpoint encryption status, patch ownership, and recovery-key custody. Backblaze Computer Backup protects selected endpoint data, not every SaaS record or cloud mailbox. Export the CRM, accounting records, signed agreements, and license ledger on a defined schedule, then restore a sample into an isolated workspace. Cloudflare DNS and a UptimeRobot HTTP check establish basic reachability signals, not customer application monitoring. GitHub Free can version quote templates and operating scripts, but credentials and customer records stay outside repositories. Where a customer requires single sign-on, longer audit retention, regional hosting, or a signed data-processing agreement, price the necessary tier into the contract before accepting the requirement. A low subscription price is useful only when its actual controls match the workload.

Connect the layers with a deliberately small data model: customer identifier, legal purchasing entity, vendor identifier, registration reference, offer identifier, subscription identifier, invoice identifier, renewal date, and collection status. Keep offer expiration separate from subscription renewal. HubSpot Free tracks opportunities; an access-controlled Google Sheet can hold the initial entitlement ledger; Wave Starter records invoices and expenses where its geography and feature set fit the business. Do not pretend a spreadsheet provides transactional locking or a full audit trail. Once simultaneous editing creates reconciliation errors, migrate the ledger to a database with permissions and change history. Trigger renewal reminders at ninety, sixty, and thirty days, then require a human review before issuing binding prices. Quote automation should populate quantities and approved price schedules without inventing discount authority. License reconciliation should compare purchased quantities, delivered entitlements, and active assignments, flagging differences rather than automatically deleting access. Avoid a paid integration platform until repeated manual work has a measurable cost. If a connector costs $50 monthly and saves two hours valued at $40 each, its illustrative benefit is $30 before maintenance; one broken mapping can reverse that result. Retain a manual export-and-reconcile procedure for every automation.

## 3. Establish entity, payment, insurance, and contract evidence  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Form the selling entity in a jurisdiction justified by actual operations, obtain its employer identification number directly through the IRS, and open business banking under the same legal name used in customer and vendor contracts. State filing charges vary, while the IRS does not charge for an EIN. Confirm foreign qualification and state tax registrations where required by activities and nexus; software and implementation tax treatment varies by jurisdiction and delivery method. Collect exemption certificates before applying an exemption rather than improvising after an audit request. Maintain separate operating, tax, and reserve ledger balances even if the bank uses subaccounts. The $250,000 standard FDIC insurance amount is per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category, not per account label. Review coverage if balances grow or a platform distributes funds among banks. Verify bank instructions through a previously established contact before changing supplier payment details; an emailed replacement invoice is insufficient. Assign one person to prepare payments and another to review new beneficiaries and unusual amounts. At a two-person company, that separation is practical and substantially cheaper than recovering a misdirected annual license payment. Store tax records and bank evidence outside the salesperson's personal mailbox.

Buy insurance against the work actually promised. Technology errors and omissions coverage should reflect failed implementation, configuration mistakes, and contractual service allegations; cyber coverage should address the company's own incident costs and relevant third-party exposures. Ask the broker to identify exclusions affecting contractual liability, funds-transfer fraud, subcontractors, and work outside declared territories. Compare deductible, aggregate limit, retroactive date, notification duties, and subcontractor requirements, not just premium. A certificate alone does not establish that a particular loss is covered. Make the master agreement, statement of work, purchase order, and vendor license terms agree on scope, payment, cancellation, support boundaries, and acceptance evidence. State who can authorize changes and which party is responsible for migrations, backups, customer identity access, and third-party outages. Federal commercial computer software procurement raises specific license questions under FAR 12.212; a commercial reseller cannot assume every standard vendor term becomes enforceable merely because a buyer signs an order. Confirm the customer's purchasing authority and procurement path before provisioning. E-SIGN supports electronic records and signatures under 15 U.S.C. 7001, but the operational requirement remains preserving the executed record and proving who accepted which version.

Treat regulated customer data as a separate architectural decision, not an incidental CRM attachment. A reseller that receives protected health information while performing covered functions may become a business associate; map the actual role before collecting patient-level records. Where applicable, 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e) govern business associate arrangements, while the Security Rule requires applicable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. The inexpensive baseline stack is not designated as a patient-data environment. Use synthetic records during demonstrations and restrict normal sales systems to commercial contacts, contract identifiers, and aggregated workload estimates. If delivery requires sensitive records, procure supported services, execute the necessary agreements, implement access and retention controls, and price that operating burden before accepting the work. Apply the same discipline to customer administrator access: grant the minimum role, time-limit delegated permissions where supported, and capture the customer authorization. A Microsoft CSP relationship does not justify unrestricted tenant administration, and an AWS marketplace purchase does not itself authorize entering the customer's account. Close the loop at offboarding by removing delegated access, transferring documentation, returning or deleting data according to the agreement, and retaining only the transaction evidence that the documented retention schedule requires.

## 4. Prove nine mapped plays before expanding commitments  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use nine mapped plays as distinct worksheets, not nine businesses launched simultaneously. Only the specialty-drug identifier is supplied as a formal play identifier; the other eight names below are chapter-local channel execution patterns. Authorized AWS CPPO spread, Microsoft CSP seat resale, and registered enterprise license resale all begin with written authority and an accurate supplier purchase price. Implementation spreads then depend on a bounded scope and measured contractor hours. Renewal recovery, license reconciliation services, prepaid onboarding, and procurement evidence services monetize specific operating work without claiming customer savings as reseller revenue. Each worksheet separates customer billings, direct costs, contribution, collection timing, and allocated overhead. For example, a $12,000 implementation using eighty contractor hours at $75 costs $6,000 and contributes $6,000 before overhead; twenty additional hours reduce that contribution to $4,500. Use explicit acceptance milestones and change orders rather than assuming automation eliminates overruns. None of the illustrative play contributions changes the canonical annual profit cases, and the nine examples are not additive forecasts. The same customer could buy several services, creating duplicate counting unless each invoice line and underlying obligation has a unique reference. Choose one resale rail and one adjacent service first, then expand only after reconciliation and collections work repeatedly.

The supplied erisa_self_funded_specialty_drug_importation_arbitrage play describes self-funded employers with 100–5,000 employees, specialty biologics and GLP-1 products priced at $6,000–$15,000 monthly in the United States, and proposed overseas sourcing at $800–$2,200. Those figures are supplied commercial hypotheses, not verified prices, evidence of identical approved products, or proof of permitted importation. At $8,000 versus $1,500 monthly, the arithmetic difference is $78,000 annually; a 30% fee would be $23,400, leaving $54,600 before dispensing, logistics, clinical, and administrative costs. The broader supplied fee range of 25–35% against $50,000–$100,000 savings implies $12,500–$35,000, but savings must use actual net employer costs rather than PBM list prices. ERISA status does not create a federal drug-importation exemption: 29 U.S.C. 1144(d) preserves other federal law. The importation framework in 21 U.S.C. 384 excludes biological products and other specified categories, and foreign pharmacy licensure alone does not establish a lawful United States supply route. Do not fund purchasing or promise zero copays on that premise. The practical fallback is domestic bridge-fill coordination and manufacturer patient assistance screening through qualified providers, working with independent TPAs and transparent pass-through PBMs while respecting program eligibility and applicable benefit rules.

Keep that healthcare adjacency outside the software reseller's startup inventory and canonical revenue forecast. A lawful opportunity for this chapter is selling workflow software, implementation, or documented administrative services to an appropriately authorized operator, not acting as a pharmacy because a spreadsheet shows an attractive spread. If a $10,000 workflow implementation requires $6,000 of direct delivery, the reseller's contribution is $4,000 before overhead; employer drug spending and patient savings remain outside its revenue. Before launch, execute a complete synthetic transaction from opportunity through signed offer, supplier order, entitlement delivery, customer invoice, payment reconciliation, and renewal reminder. Introduce one deliberate failure at each boundary: expired authorization, incorrect tenant identifier, delayed settlement, and rejected acceptance. Confirm that the process stops the commitment and assigns a responsible person rather than merely sending an ignored notification. Require current authorization, adequate stressed liquidity, demonstrated delivery controls, and complete contract evidence before taking a binding order. After the first five deals, review realized contribution and collection days against the worksheet. Increase subscription tiers or contractor capacity only when measured workload justifies the change. The $150,000 capitalization supports more resilience, not permission to accumulate unused applications or extend unpriced customer credit.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Create the deal evidence packet
   - Link the customer, supplier, registration, offer, and contract references.
   - Record selling authority, pricing validity, payment obligations, customer acceptance requirements, and the person accountable for resolving discrepancies before supplier commitment.
2. Run the stressed cash worksheet
   - Compare dated receipts and payments across thirteen weeks.
   - Delay the customer receipt by thirty days, include taxes and transaction charges, and reject the order structure if documented available liquidity cannot cover the exposure.
3. Execute a synthetic delivery rehearsal
   - Test quote acceptance, entitlement assignment, invoicing, and exception routing.
   - Use synthetic data and nonbinding test records; demonstrate that an incorrect tenant, expired offer, or rejected acceptance stops further commitment and reaches the assigned operator.
4. Close and review the first operating cycle
   - Reconcile settlement, direct costs, delivered scope, and renewal scheduling.
   - Archive the evidence packet, compare realized contribution with the original worksheet, and correct recurring exceptions before increasing credit exposure or adding subscription tiers.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four proofs required before the first binding order**
> 
> A successful demonstration is insufficient. The accountable operator must retain evidence that the commercial and delivery chain can perform.
> - Authorization: confirm reseller status, permitted territory, current registration, applicable license terms, and authority to issue the specific offer.
> - Liquidity: stress collections by thirty days, include all committed supplier payments, and preserve the documented minimum reserve after the proposed order.
> - Delivery: verify the customer tenant identifier, demonstrate entitlement reconciliation, restore a sample record, and test contractor access removal.
> - Evidence: retain the executed scope, purchasing authority, tax treatment, required data agreements, insurance review, and independently verified payment instructions.
