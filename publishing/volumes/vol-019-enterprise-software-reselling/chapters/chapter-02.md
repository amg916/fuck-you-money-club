## 1. Identify the budget owner and sell against a dated operating loss  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The enterprise writes the check, but four different people usually determine whether it happens. The CIO or application owner defines the technical outcome; the CFO or delegated budget holder authorizes expenditure; procurement negotiates the commercial instrument; accounts payable releases an approved liability. A security lead may have veto power without spending authority. Map those roles before issuing a binding quote, including the legal buying entity, budget code, purchase order threshold, authorized signatory, and receiving contact. Ask which dated loss the purchase prevents: an expiring identity subscription, an unsupported ERP release, duplicate licenses after acquisition, or a migration that otherwise requires expensive parallel systems. A $120,000 renewal protecting a system with $20,000 weekly disruption exposure has a different decision clock from optional analytics. Quantify that exposure with the buyer rather than presenting invented savings. Statutory pressure is specific, not universal: a HIPAA business associate arrangement under 45 CFR 164.504(e) can require contractual safeguards where the implementation involves protected health information. It does not prescribe a particular software brand. Sell the documented control and implementation work; never imply that a reseller invoice alone satisfies the underlying duty. Record whose acceptance permits payment and who substitutes when that person is absent.

The mapped play sec_edgar_8k_vendor_trigger_arbitrage turns public disclosures into timely, evidence-based discovery, not automatic purchasing intent. Monitor SEC EDGAR current-filings RSS and available JSON submission records for Form 8-K Item 5.02 executive changes, Item 1.01 material agreements, and Item 2.01 completed acquisitions. Track Form D filings separately for exempt offering activity; the reported amount sold is not proof of unrestricted cash available for software. Persist accession number, issuer identifier, filing time, event category, and a short commercial hypothesis. An acquisition may create duplicate identity tenants; a new CFO may question renewal sprawl. Route a relevant public filing to a human-reviewed, sub-hour outreach queue during staffed hours, avoiding sensitive personal assumptions. Illustratively, 40 researched events producing eight qualified conversations and two $25,000 projects yield $50,000 billings. At $18,000 delivery cost per project, contribution is $14,000 before outreach expense and overhead. Those are planning assumptions, not measured conversion rates. Enforce a five-request-per-second token bucket with an identifying User-Agent, caching, and slower retries when requested. For historical lookbacks, mirror available SEC nightly bulk ZIP archives rather than repeatedly retrieving individual records. Missing event data sends the lead to manual research, not fabricated personalization or duplicate automated messages.

The remaining eight mapped plays are chapter operating mappings, each tied to a payer and a measurable spread. Deal registration on $100,000 license billings against $82,000 vendor cost leaves $18,000 before other expenses; if protection expires, requote instead of assuming exclusivity. Renewal rescue at $60,000 less $54,000 acquisition cost leaves $6,000, but an unconfirmed cancellation window requires written vendor clarification. License reconciliation charges $6,000 for documenting 100 removable seats at $30 monthly, supporting $36,000 annualized buyer savings only when contract terms permit removal. AWS CPPO on $100,000 billings with $85,000 wholesale cost and an assumed $2,000 applicable marketplace charge leaves $13,000; substitute actual fee terms before quoting. Microsoft CSP billings of $36,000 against $31,680 distributor cost leave $4,320 before support. Implementation milestones produce $12,000 contribution on $30,000 fees and $18,000 delivery cost. Procurement consolidation earns a separately contracted $9,000 service fee against $4,000 administration cost. Receivables protection preserves cash through deposits, limits, or selective funding; a $1,500 funding charge reduces a $10,000 deal contribution to $8,500. None of these deal spreads equals annual owner profit. Where authority, eligibility, or acceptance fails, pause the affected commitment while preserving separately authorized discovery work.

## 2. Choose the settlement structure before accepting the vendor obligation  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Separate the payment rail from the commercial funding structure. Wire and ACH move money; escrow, retainers, factoring, and milestone holds allocate timing or counterparty risk. Marketplace checkout combines contractual routing with a platform collection process. Statutory disbursement describes a governed payment process, not an independent bank network. Start every deal with a funds-flow schedule naming customer, reseller, software publisher, distributor, marketplace, and any financing provider. Show who invoices whom, who owes tax, who advances supplier cost, when entitlement activates, and what evidence releases funds. A customer purchase order is an instruction under its procurement process, not cash or a bank undertaking. For a $120,000 annual license with $102,000 supplier cost, supplier terms of net fifteen and buyer terms of net sixty create a forty-five-day financing gap if both invoices start together. A $60,000 advance still leaves $42,000 of supplier cost temporarily unfunded, before taxes and operating costs. Reserve that liquidity or negotiate aligned terms. Confirm beneficiary changes through an independently sourced telephone number, require dual release for outbound wires, and retain the verification record. Invoice bank instructions alone are inadequate protection against compromised email threads, especially when a genuine purchase order gives fraudulent instructions an appearance of legitimacy.

For AWS channel partner private offers, confirm the selling authorization, buyer account, eligible product, offer duration, wholesale economics, and disbursement treatment in the actual marketplace records. Do not assume the buyer accepting an offer causes immediate cash settlement to the reseller. Reconcile acceptance, invoice, customer collection, adjustments, and payout as separate events. A $100,000 offer may carry channel economics very different from the cash deposited after publisher allocations, fees, taxes, and credits. Microsoft CSP is another specific channel structure, not a synonym for every Microsoft marketplace private offer. Under an indirect arrangement, the distributor relationship can leave the reseller owing subscription charges before the enterprise customer pays. New Commerce Experience cancellation windows and term commitments must be checked for the particular subscription and current program rules; monthly billing does not automatically create monthly cancellation rights. Where a buyer wants escrow, obtain written release criteria that match license provisioning and vendor payment deadlines. Otherwise money can sit safely with an escrow agent while the reseller defaults on an unconditional supplier invoice. A cash retainer for support also needs a clear drawdown ledger and replenishment rule; calling it a retainer does not explain whether it buys availability, prepays hours, or remains refundable.

Government buyers require a different collection map. Under applicable federal contract clauses, a proper invoice, satisfactory performance, and receiving documentation drive payment timing; FAR 32.905 and FAR 52.232-25 provide concrete requirements and due-date mechanics. The Prompt Payment Act, including 31 U.S.C. 3902, addresses interest on qualifying late payments, but it does not supply working capital while acceptance or invoice defects remain unresolved. Identify the contracting officer, authorized receiving official, designated billing office, and required invoice submission system. A program manager's enthusiasm cannot substitute for contractual authority. For commercial accounts, ACH debit requires appropriate authorization and creates return exposure; ACH credit shifts initiation to the buyer but still needs reliable remittance matching. Wires reduce some collection delays without eliminating fraud or recall issues. Factoring converts eligible invoices into earlier liquidity, subject to assignment rights, dilution reserves, recourse, and concentration limits. On a $100,000 invoice, an illustrative 85% advance produces $85,000 initial cash; a $3,000 fee leaves $12,000 reserve release if the customer pays in full and there are no other deductions. That $3,000 is expense, not a reduction in customer purchasing volume. Compare all structures using net cash dates, enforceable release conditions, and the maximum exposed supplier balance.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Draw the legal and cash counterparties
   - Record every invoicing entity, recipient account, supplier obligation, and contractual payer.
   - Attach the customer purchase order, reseller authorization, distributor terms, and marketplace offer identifiers to one transaction record.
2. Calculate the peak funding deficit
   - Schedule customer deposits, supplier payments, taxes, platform settlements, and expected collections by date.
   - Stress collections by an additional thirty days and require committed liquidity for the resulting maximum cumulative cash shortfall.
3. Bind acceptance to release evidence
   - Specify license provisioning records or implementation tests that make the invoice payable.
   - Name the authorized acceptor and substitute, define the review period, and separate undisputed charges from contested work.
4. Reconcile receipt before closing the transaction
   - Match bank or platform settlement to invoice lines, taxes, credits, fees, and retained balances.
   - Escalate unmatched deductions and keep supplier liabilities visible even when the customer invoice shows a partial payment.

## 3. Protect the fee with authorization, evidence, and counterparty credit limits  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Fee protection starts with a valid right to sell, not a sweeping noncircumvention paragraph. Verify reseller authorization for the product, geography, customer segment, and channel route; save the publisher or distributor evidence with its expiry date. Register the opportunity where the program permits and document named account, scope, renewal ownership, protected pricing, and any conditions that remove protection. A registration may protect a discount without preventing the publisher from selling directly. Put discovery and implementation fees in a signed statement of work so useful labor is not contingent on winning the license order. For example, a $4,000 assessment with $2,000 delivery cost contributes $2,000 even when the buyer later chooses a different license channel, provided the assessment is independently contracted and delivered. Define treatment of that fee if a later project includes a credit, preventing double counting. Narrow any noncircumvention provision to identified introductions, a reasonable period, and a defined economic remedy reviewed for enforceability. Do not use it to obstruct a customer's existing publisher relationship. Where the channel agreement prohibits particular markups, assignments, or subcontracting, redesign the service scope rather than concealing compensation in an inaccurate invoice description. Make renewal handover duties explicit before the first subscription starts.

Credit screening measures the legal customer's ability and willingness to pay, not the prestige of its logo. Confirm entity registration, purchasing address, tax documentation where required, and whether the purchase order issuer matches the contracting party. A subsidiary is not its parent; a brand name does not create a parent guarantee. Use commercial bureau reports, customer-authorized trade references, filed accounts, and public insolvency records as complementary evidence. For public issuers, compare cash, operating cash flow, debt maturities, and going-concern disclosures with the proposed obligation. For private issuers, a Form D filing is an event signal, not a substitute for current liquidity evidence. Establish a credit limit covering unpaid invoices plus committed supplier charges that cannot be canceled, less genuinely available deposits. A customer with $40,000 unpaid invoices and a new $70,000 irreversible supplier commitment creates $110,000 exposure before an applicable $30,000 deposit reduces it to $80,000. Do not subtract expected marketplace payouts that are already pledged or unresolved. Reassess limits after repeated short payments, ownership changes, invoice disputes, or material deterioration. A profitable order that exceeds available liquidity should move to advance payment, a smaller phase, or a separately approved credit facility rather than automatic fulfillment.

Disputes become expensive when the record proves activity but not acceptance. Create an evidence packet containing executed terms, approved quote, purchase order, vendor order confirmation, tenant or entitlement identifiers, delivery notice, acceptance tests, and invoice submission receipt. Do not include administrator passwords or unnecessary production data. For implementation, define a test such as fifty pilot identities authenticating through the agreed provider with documented rollback, instead of an ambiguous promise that integration will be satisfactory. Route scope changes to priced change orders before performing extra work. If $8,000 of a $30,000 milestone is disputed, the agreement should support collection of the $22,000 undisputed portion rather than freezing the entire project. Suspension rights need notice, contractual authority, and product-specific feasibility; a reseller should not disable critical access merely because an internal receivables report is overdue. For public receivables, assignment can trigger federal requirements including 31 U.S.C. 3727 and FAR Subpart 32.8, so obtain specialist review before promising collateral to a factor. State-enacted UCC Article 9 may govern security interests in commercial receivables, but perfection does not repair a defective invoice or eliminate the customer's valid contractual defenses. Preserve the operational record that makes the receivable worth financing.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before the reseller becomes the lender**
> 
> Release an irreversible supplier order only when the transaction record answers all four questions with current evidence.
> - Authorization: Confirm current reseller rights, protected pricing validity, eligible customer account, and an executed order from the actual purchasing entity.
> - Liquidity: Calculate peak committed exposure, verify available deposits and credit capacity, and stress the collection date by thirty additional days.
> - Acceptance: Name the customer's authorized acceptor, objective delivery evidence, review deadline, and treatment of undisputed invoice lines.
> - Settlement: Verify beneficiary details independently and document the actual rail, marketplace payout rules, escrow release terms, or government invoicing requirements.

## 4. Reconcile recognized revenue, supplier cost, and cash into the canonical cases  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use the scorecard exactly: Conservative is $300,000 gross revenue, 8% net margin, and $24,000 annual owner net profit with two full-time people and three contractors. Base is $1,400,000 gross revenue, 14.0% net margin, and $196,000 owner net profit with the same headcount. Scaled is $2,500,000 gross revenue, 20% net margin, and $500,000 owner net profit with three full-time people and four contractors. The implied total annual cost envelopes are $276,000, $1,204,000, and $2,000,000 respectively. Those envelopes include vendor acquisition costs and all other costs assumed by the scorecard; they are not spare overhead budgets. As one arithmetic bridge, ten $30,000 transactions, twenty $70,000 transactions, and twenty-five $100,000 transactions produce the respective revenue totals. These transaction mixes illustrate scale, not a claim that every license carries identical economics. Contractor counts do not imply full-year utilization, so price delivery hours explicitly. The stated $5,000 to $25,000 monthly operating expense range is contextual, not an extra deduction after these net profits. Likewise, $25,000 to $150,000 startup capital is funding capacity, not revenue. With three to twelve months to a first deal, cash runway and supplier-credit headroom must be planned separately from the annual profit target.

The canonical revenue basis is gross license resale and service billings, with vendor costs included in margin. Preserve that basis for these scenarios, but assess principal-versus-agent treatment for actual financial reporting under the applicable accounting framework and contract facts. If a channel transaction is only an agency commission, do not inflate revenue with the customer's full software purchase. A $100,000 underlying order generating a $12,000 agency commission contributes $12,000 revenue before related expenses, not $100,000 gross reseller revenue. Conversely, a qualifying principal resale at $100,000 with $85,000 vendor cost starts with $15,000 gross spread before marketplace fees, delivery, support, selling expense, and overhead. Taxes collected for a government, escrow principal held for another party, and customer cloud spending outside the reseller's sale are not additional operator revenue. Advance cash for an undelivered service is not automatically earned revenue. Reconcile each transaction across quote, order, invoice, entitlement, supplier bill, bank receipt, and platform settlement using stable identifiers. Where platform cash is net of supplier allocations, retain the supporting gross-to-net bridge rather than treating the bank deposit as the entire economic record. Review credits and cancellation adjustments in the same ledger so late reversals cannot silently overstate the month's margin.

Build the control cadence around the three automation levers: renewal alerts, quote generation, and license reconciliation. Renewal alerts should join notice deadlines, customer purchase order status, supplier commitment dates, and credit limits, not merely send reminders thirty days before expiration. Quote generation should pull valid distributor pricing, current authorization, currency, tax treatment, payment terms, and service scope into a versioned document. License reconciliation should compare purchased entitlements, assigned seats, active usage, and contractually available reductions while preserving administrator review before changing access. Each week, reconcile marketplace settlement exceptions, aging balances, unaccepted milestones, registration expiries, and projected supplier payments. At the Base run rate, $1,400,000 divided by 365 is approximately $3,836 daily billings; thirty additional collection days can tie up about $115,068 if sales are evenly distributed and entirely on credit. That is a working-capital sensitivity, not an automatic profit loss. A $14,000 credit loss equals one percent of Base revenue and would reduce the modeled $196,000 profit to $182,000 unless offset elsewhere. Preserve the canonical target while showing that stress case separately. Scaling means increasing collected contribution per accountable person, not adding unmanaged subscription commitments that make the revenue dashboard look larger while treasury runs out of cash.
