## 1. Mine Purchase Events Instead of Collecting Unqualified Names  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with one buyer, one software category, and one purchase event. A workable opening wedge is a regional manufacturer with 250 to 2,000 employees, an infrastructure lead, and a renewal or integration deadline inside 180 days. Build an account ledger in HubSpot or Salesforce with legal entity, parent company, signal date, evidence URL, probable product, incumbent partner, estimated buying window, and next verification question. Use SEC EDGAR acquisition filings, SAM.gov notices, municipal purchasing portals, vendor partner directories, company engineering posts, and job advertisements naming migration responsibilities. A job advertisement proves recruiting activity, not an installed license count; a contract award proves a historical purchase, not an open renewal. Preserve that distinction in every record. Review fifty accounts weekly, retain twenty supported hypotheses, and ask a human to validate timing before assigning opportunity value. Avoid collecting personal contact details unrelated to the purchase. The best signal combines a dated operational change with an identifiable budget owner and a plausible implementation burden. A generic technology fingerprint without a deadline belongs in monitoring, not the active pipeline. Track signal age so stale evidence cannot inflate coverage. Record contradictions alongside supporting evidence rather than deleting them.

Use eight direct software plays and one adjacent customs-workflow play as the chapter's nine mapped sourcing routes. Renewal rescue starts with a verified expiration and a comparison of current entitlements against actual use. License reconciliation starts with customer-authorized exports and ends with a purchased remediation scope, not an unsolicited accusation of underlicensing. Cloud commitment alignment connects an eligible software purchase to the customer's existing marketplace procurement process, while security remediation attaches implementation to a documented control gap. Acquisition integration uses public transaction announcements to identify duplicate identity, backup, and collaboration estates. Public procurement uses published solicitations and award histories, respecting the stated communications process. Implementation recovery targets stalled deployments through customer referrals rather than public shaming. Vendor referral coverage serves territories or account sizes the publisher cannot support economically. Each route needs its own unit calculation and fallback, shown below. Treat these as sourcing hypotheses, not nine guaranteed revenue streams. The opening economics should reward a narrow specialist: one credible license analysis and one repeatable implementation package outperform a catalog containing hundreds of products nobody on the team can deploy. Do not represent informal introductions as authorized reseller status.

The supplied adjacent play, section_321_de_minimis_bonded_tariff_arbitrage, describes routing inventory through Tijuana IMMEX or Canadian bonded warehouses before individual consumer fulfillment. Do not sell its claimed tariff elimination as a current entitlement. The United States suspended duty-free de minimis treatment globally effective August 29, 2025; verify subsequent changes before designing any entry workflow. Section 321, 19 U.S.C. § 1321, is not blanket permission to divide commercial shipments, and Entry Type 86 is a filing mechanism rather than independent eligibility. For sourcing, translate the disruption into demand for customs data integration, order management, landed-cost controls, and broker exception queues. A brand shipping 10,000 monthly orders at an illustrative $4 duty exposure has $40,000 of customer exposure, not reseller revenue. Applying the historical 35–50% savings-fee concept would produce $14,000–$20,000 only if lawful, documented savings actually existed; exclude that contingent amount from this software pipeline. Instead quote a $12,000 integration with $7,000 direct delivery cost. Non-301 origin alone does not restore suspended treatment. First-sale valuation requires transaction evidence and broker review; bonded storage may defer duties rather than erase them. Fall back to ordinary duty-paid workflows when eligibility cannot be established.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Use Channel Friction as Evidence, Not as a Sales Claim**
> 
> Experienced specialists make timing, authority, and delivery constraints visible before negotiating the headline discount.
> - Ask the customer for a redacted renewal notice before requesting publisher pricing; dates and product identifiers make registration defensible.
> - Request written confirmation of registration duration, protected entity, and renewal treatment; a portal submission alone does not establish protection.
> - Ask a distributor credit desk about required deposits before quoting payment terms; a profitable order can still exhaust available cash.
> - For customs-adjacent software, involve the customer's broker in data-field design and preserve ruling references instead of promising tariff savings.

## 2. Qualify Margin, Authority, and Cash Before Building the Quote  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Score opportunities on six dimensions totaling one hundred points: verified purchase timing twenty, budget-owner access twenty, economic spread twenty, reseller authorization fifteen, implementation fit fifteen, and collection quality ten. Award full timing points only when the customer supplies a dated renewal, purchase milestone, or published procurement schedule; a salesperson's optimism earns nothing. Give economic points against an actual distributor quote with expiry, currency, quantities, and cancellation terms. Set seventy-five points as the active pursuit threshold, but treat absent authorization or unacceptable payment exposure as disqualifying regardless of total. An opportunity scoring eighty-five with a nontransferable license cannot be repaired by enthusiasm. In the first call, ask who signs, who administers, who pays, and who can block deployment. These are often four different people. Confirm the buying entity before registering a parent company or subsidiary; publisher coverage may differ. Document the customer's reason to change partners without soliciting restricted competitor material. A qualified opportunity has a buyer-confirmed next step and a dated evidence request, not merely a friendly meeting. Recalculate the score whenever price, timing, scope, or payment terms change materially.

Separate reseller authorization from marketplace routing. AWS Marketplace channel partner private offers, commonly CPPO, require the applicable seller relationship and selling authorization; Microsoft CSP requires the relevant direct or indirect channel arrangement and customer relationship mechanics. Microsoft marketplace multiparty private offers are a separate route, not a synonym for CSP. Confirm the actual program, product, geography, buyer account, tax treatment, and settlement flow before drafting commercial promises. Deal registration can protect an opportunity or improve pricing under publisher rules, but it does not automatically override an incumbent agreement. Ask the publisher to confirm the registered legal entity and expiration in writing. For cloud commitments, have the customer verify which eligible purchases count under their specific agreement; never promise universal dollar-for-dollar treatment. The operator's worksheet should show customer price, vendor payable, platform charges, implementation labor, support reserve, and expected cash dates separately. If the operator acts only as an agent, commission revenue may be the appropriate recognition basis. Do not inflate that commission into gross resale billings merely to resemble the scorecard. Compare the canonical gross-billing cases only with transactions whose contractual and accounting treatment supports that basis.

Run a cash test before a margin test becomes a commitment. An illustrative $100,000 license order with $88,000 vendor cost has a $12,000 product spread before platform charges, labor, support, and overhead. If the vendor requires payment on day fifteen and the customer pays on day sixty, the reseller carries $88,000 for forty-five days. That single order can consume much of the canonical $15,000–$75,000 startup capital range, while monthly operating expense still runs $5,000–$25,000. Seek a deposit, shorter customer terms, distributor credit, or a permitted marketplace settlement structure; calculate each alternative from its actual contract rather than assuming immediate payout. For public buyers, follow solicitation instructions and any incorporated FAR 52.212-4 payment provisions instead of demanding terms the contract cannot support. For subscriptions, disclose term length, renewal mechanics, seat-reduction restrictions, and cancellation exposure before signature. Preserve written scope acceptance for implementation milestones. A ten-percent customer deposit does not fund an eighty-eight-percent supplier obligation. When no workable cash structure exists, offer a paid assessment or services-only delivery and let the publisher transact the license directly.

**Six-Part Qualification Scorecard with Evidence-Based Pursuit Rules**

| Dimension and maximum available points | Evidence required before awarding full credit | Specific verification question for the buyer | Disposition when supporting evidence is weak |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase timing carries twenty points in the pursuit score | Dated renewal notice or published purchase milestone supplied directly | Which documented deadline determines when the purchase must happen | Move the account into monitoring until timing becomes verifiable |
| Budget-owner access carries twenty points in the pursuit score | Named economic buyer attends discovery and confirms spending ownership | Who can approve this amount for this purchasing entity | Request a joint meeting before investing in custom demonstrations |
| Economic spread carries twenty points in the pursuit score | Current vendor quote supports the proposed customer billing amount | Which delivery obligations and charges reduce this transaction spread | Reprice the scope or decline economically unsupported license resale |
| Reseller authorization carries fifteen points plus a mandatory check | Publisher or distributor documents valid rights for this transaction | Does this authorization cover the product territory and entity | Stop license quoting and pursue only independently authorized services |
| Implementation fit carries fifteen points in the pursuit score | Delivery lead confirms prerequisites effort estimate and available capacity | Which dependencies must the customer complete before work starts | Sell bounded discovery before committing to uncertain implementation work |
| Collection quality carries ten points plus a cash check | Accepted payment terms and credit evidence support supplier obligations | When will cleared customer cash precede each supplier payment | Restructure deposits credit or transaction route before accepting exposure |

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four Checks Before Issuing a Binding Commercial Offer**
> 
> A strong pursuit score does not replace these transaction-specific checks. Resolve failures before accepting customer obligations.
> - Verify current reseller rights, product eligibility, territory, customer entity, and registration status against written publisher or distributor evidence.
> - Reconcile customer billing, vendor payable, platform charges, delivery effort, support reserve, and payment dates in one quote worksheet.
> - Confirm the signer's purchasing authority, required procurement process, license terms, implementation acceptance criteria, and customer data-access permissions.
> - Test supplier-payment coverage under delayed collection and confirm a workable deposit, credit facility, or alternative transaction structure.

## 3. Run a Permission-Aware Cadence That Converts Signals into Paid Discovery  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Organize outbound around the account event rather than a scraped contact list. On day one, send a short email connecting the public signal to one operational hypothesis and ask for a fifteen-minute verification call. For an acquisition, write that duplicate identity administration often complicates integration, then ask whether consolidation is on the current plan; do not assert that the buyer has a security defect. On day three, call a published business number and leave one concise message. On day six, send a relevant implementation checklist or an anonymized scope example. On day ten, request an introduction through a publisher representative only with an appropriate relationship and without sharing customer-confidential material. On day fourteen, close the sequence with a clear choice to discuss, defer, or stop. Honor suppression across channels. Commercial email must meet 15 U.S.C. § 7704 requirements, including accurate routing information, required identification, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out honored within the statutory period. Do not treat business outreach as permission for automated calls or texts; assess those methods separately under applicable rules. Keep sequences small enough that every response receives human review.

Use an explicit funnel hypothesis to plan the first wins. Across twelve weeks, research 600 distinct accounts, retain 240 with credible purchase signals, reach 120 through delivered messages or completed introductions, conduct thirty discovery meetings, qualify fifteen opportunities, issue ten proposals, and win three contracts. Those stage conversions are 40%, 50%, 25%, 50%, approximately 67%, and 30%, respectively; the overall account-to-win rate is 0.5%. They are illustrative planning benchmarks, not observed industry averages. Define reached consistently and deduplicate companies before calculating conversion. A proposal means a priced scope with an identified buying process, not a generic brochure. A win means an executed purchase instrument accepted through the correct channel, not a verbal endorsement. With a 3–12 month first-deal window, the twelve-week cohort may produce discoveries long before bookings. Track cohorts by first signal date and report pending opportunities separately from losses. At unchanged conversion, ten wins require roughly 2,000 researched accounts, which may exceed a small team's delivery capacity. Improve qualification and referral quality before multiplying outreach. Measure elapsed days and cash collected alongside conversion so rapid signatures cannot conceal slow settlement.

Build inbound assets that answer procurement questions rather than repeat product marketing. Publish a renewal evidence checklist, a license export preparation guide, a private-offer purchasing worksheet, and an implementation acceptance template. Each asset should identify required inputs, likely bottlenecks, and the scope of a paid diagnostic. Offer a fixed $3,000 discovery engagement requiring roughly twenty delivery hours at $90 loaded hourly cost: $1,800 direct labor leaves $1,200 before selling expense and shared overhead. If that fee is later credited against implementation, record the credit so revenue is not counted twice. Keep customer production data out of public forms; request sanitized counts first and obtain authorized access only after scope agreement. Co-host narrow workshops with an authorized publisher representative or distributor specialist, using a sample environment rather than customer credentials. Tag each inquiry by asset, partner, purchase event, and verified timeline. Measure qualified discoveries per asset and gross contribution per partner referral, not download totals. For regulated or public buyers, provide procurement-ready descriptions without implying certifications or contract vehicles the business does not hold. Retire assets that attract unfunded research but no actionable purchase events.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture and verify the event
   - Research fifty accounts weekly and retain only evidence-backed hypotheses.
   - Store source URL, publication date, legal entity, suspected product, and the precise fact requiring customer confirmation.
2. Run the account-specific contact sequence
   - Use the fourteen-day email, business-call, asset, introduction, and closeout cadence.
   - Assign one account owner, maintain a shared suppression list, and stop or defer immediately when the buyer requests it.
3. Convert discovery into a bounded commercial scope
   - Score the opportunity and offer paid discovery when delivery uncertainty remains.
   - Obtain authorization evidence, a current supplier quote, buyer-confirmed timing, implementation prerequisites, and a cash coverage worksheet.
4. Close the correct instrument and hand off delivery
   - Confirm purchase acceptance, provisioning ownership, implementation milestones, and collection dates.
   - Record the executed instrument, reconcile ordered quantities, schedule acceptance checks, and create renewal alerts with a named owner.

## 4. Close the First Wins Without Mistaking Product Spread for Owner Profit  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Handle objections by locating the missing decision fact. When the buyer says the incumbent is cheaper, ask whether the comparison includes the same term, quantities, support, implementation, and payment conditions; reconcile those fields before discussing discount. When procurement prefers buying direct, offer a transparent services-only alternative or an eligible marketplace route, explaining the distinct operational benefit rather than inventing channel exclusivity. When the buyer asks for guaranteed commitment drawdown, obtain agreement-specific confirmation from the relevant marketplace team. When security blocks administrator access, propose customer-operated exports, temporary least-privilege access, or a supervised working session. When the sponsor wants a free proof of concept, define a paid diagnostic with acceptance criteria and an optional, explicitly priced conversion credit. When purchasing requests extended payment, price and fund that exposure or restructure the transaction. Do not accept an unfunded obligation to save a headline win. Capture the objection category, evidence supplied, commercial change, and final result in the opportunity record. Repeated losses to one issue should change the targeting or offer design, not merely lengthen the follow-up sequence. Close the loop with a written decision summary the buyer can circulate internally.

Reconcile every annual planning case to the canonical scorecard. Conservative gross license resale and service billings are $300,000; total modeled costs of $276,000 leave $24,000 owner net profit at 8%, supported by 2 FT + 3 C. Base billings are $1,400,000; total modeled costs of $1,204,000 leave $196,000 at 14.0%, again with 2 FT + 3 C. Scaled billings are $2,500,000; total modeled costs of $2,000,000 leave $500,000 at 20%, with 3 FT + 4 C, seven people. Those residual cost totals include vendor costs and all other modeled expenses; they are not separate estimates of payroll. An illustrative volume bridge is six $50,000 contracts, fourteen $100,000 contracts, or twenty-five $100,000 contracts, respectively. These are equivalent billing packages, not promises about average order size or the first selling year. The first three to ten wins establish delivery evidence and renewal cohorts; they do not automatically produce the Base case. Contractor counts do not specify full-time utilization, so budget actual hours before hiring. Do not add product spread, assessment contribution, customer cloud spend, or avoided duties to owner profit as though they were independent gains.

Close the first transaction with a delivery packet that another operator can execute without reconstructing the sale. Include the accepted quote, publisher authorization, customer purchase instrument, license quantities, tenant or account identifiers supplied through an authorized channel, implementation scope, acceptance tests, support boundaries, and collection schedule. Confirm whether signature, marketplace acceptance, purchase order, or another event actually forms the order under the governing documents. After provisioning, reconcile ordered versus delivered entitlements and obtain customer confirmation of the configured outcome. Schedule renewal alerts at 180, 120, 90, and 60 days, adjusted to contractual notice requirements. Automate quote assembly and license reconciliation only after the underlying fields have accountable owners; automation cannot repair an incorrect product identifier or unauthorized discount. Request a reference after demonstrated value and ask permission before sharing any customer-identifying material. Hold a weekly review of stage aging, expected contribution, cash exposure, delivery capacity, and publisher policy changes. During months three through twelve, favor repeatable wins inside the selected specialty over unrelated catalog expansion. A renewal-ready customer with clean entitlements and collectible invoices is a stronger asset than a large unscoped order requiring continual founder intervention.

**First-Deal Objection Playbook: Evidence, Commercial Response, and Stop Conditions**

| Buyer objection and underlying decision issue | Evidence to request before changing the offer | Concrete response that preserves transaction quality | Stop condition or lower-risk alternative route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The incumbent is cheaper on the proposed renewal | Comparable quantities term support scope and payment conditions together | Reconcile equivalent coverage before negotiating any further price reduction | Decline unsupported discounting and offer a separately priced assessment |
| Our procurement team prefers purchasing the software directly | Written purchasing preferences and the actual required transaction process | Offer services-only delivery or a genuinely eligible marketplace purchase | Let the publisher transact when resale adds no value |
| We require this purchase to reduce our cloud commitment | Customer agreement terms and product-specific eligibility confirmation from counterparties | Document confirmed treatment without promising benefits beyond the agreement | Compare ordinary procurement when commitment treatment cannot be confirmed |
| Security will not permit your requested administrator access | Customer access policy and the exact diagnostic data requirements | Use supervised sessions or customer-operated exports with minimized privileges | Limit scope when essential evidence cannot be accessed safely |
| We want a free proof before considering implementation | A named sponsor measurable success criteria and funded decision | Sell bounded discovery with an explicit optional conversion credit | Provide public documentation instead of unpaid production engineering work |
| Our payment cycle requires substantially longer settlement terms | Accepted payment schedule credit evidence and supplier payable dates | Restructure deposit credit support or the permitted transaction route | Decline unfunded resale while preserving a viable services relationship |
