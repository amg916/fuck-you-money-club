## 1. Prove the right to sell before registering, quoting, or provisioning  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build an authority packet for each vendor, distributor, product family, territory, and selling entity. A partner badge does not establish authority to resell every SKU, issue sublicenses, access customer tenants, or appoint subagents. Save the executed channel agreement, distributor appointment, current product schedule, territory restrictions, customer eligibility rules, and dated deal-registration acknowledgment. Record expiration and termination effects in Salesforce or HubSpot, with the source document attached rather than summarized from memory. Copyright rights under 17 U.S.C. § 106 and the limited distribution rule in § 109 do not create a general right to transfer subscription credentials or duplicate software. The ownership-versus-license distinction and the actual transaction terms matter. Use vendor-approved transfers rather than selling spare tenant accounts. For a hypothetical $100,000 license order with an $82,000 vendor commitment, the $18,000 initial spread exists only if the customer is eligible and the vendor honors the quoted buy price. If authority cannot be evidenced, stop provisioning and use an authorized referral arrangement with separately documented compensation. Never represent referral status as resale authority, and never count the customer's entire purchase as referral revenue.

Treat AWS Channel Partner Private Offers and Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider transactions as different contract stacks, not interchangeable checkout buttons. For CPPO, verify the software vendor's selling authorization, permitted buyer, offer terms, wholesale economics, and marketplace settlement mechanics against the applicable agreements. For CSP, distinguish direct-bill and indirect-reseller responsibilities, the distributor relationship, customer agreement acceptance, subscription term, and the relevant cancellation window. New Commerce commitments can leave the reseller owing a distributor after customer nonpayment; a monthly customer invoice does not necessarily mean a monthly cancellable vendor obligation. Illustratively, $120,000 customer billings minus $100,000 vendor charges leave $20,000 before platform charges, delivery, financing costs, and overhead. Record who invoices, who collects, who issues credits, and who bears refunds. The scorecard uses gross resale and service billings, but statutory reporting still requires a principal-versus-agent assessment for each arrangement. If the business acts only as an agent, do not force marketplace transaction value into recognized revenue. Reconcile that difference explicitly rather than silently changing the scorecard's economic basis. Capture the accepted offer identifier, buyer account, entitlement dates, and settlement report before marking the opportunity fulfilled.

Map nine plays into distinct permission and evidence requirements: reseller authorization, deal registration, CPPO, CSP, implementation spreads, renewal management, license reconciliation, public-sector procurement, and authorized payment orchestration. Eight are ordinary channel workstreams; the ninth is an adjacent, separately controlled integration opportunity, not implied permission to acquire merchants. For deal registration, a $60,000 order bought at $48,000 yields a $12,000 initial spread; losing a $3,000 registration benefit reduces that spread to $9,000. Require written protection dates and a documented fallback quote before presenting the discount. Protect customer-specific opportunities through narrow, time-limited non-circumvention provisions that identify the introduced account and preserve existing relationships. Do not exchange future pricing with rival resellers or coordinate customer allocation; Sherman Act § 1 applies independently of partner program labels. Screen international transactions for applicable export controls and sanctions, including product classification, destination, end user, and end use. An offshore buyer is not automatically prohibited, and a domestic billing address does not settle the analysis. Preserve screening evidence and escalate unresolved restrictions before releasing keys, credentials, technical assistance, or controlled software. A distributor's willingness to invoice does not replace your own duties.

## 2. Write contracts that protect spread, acceptance, and collection  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use a contract stack with a reseller master agreement, transaction order, implementation statement of work, incorporated vendor terms, and a data-processing addendum when the facts require one. State document precedence expressly, including which document governs license restrictions versus your own service obligations. Scope must identify SKU, quantity, tenant, authorized users, subscription period, support tier, migration assumptions, and exclusions. Define the fee trigger separately for license delivery and services: an accepted private offer may create a payment commitment before an implementation milestone is accepted. A hypothetical $24,000 implementation package with $14,400 direct delivery cost leaves $9,600 contribution before overhead; it does not establish a 40% enterprise net margin. Use testable acceptance criteria such as twenty designated users completing SSO login and an agreed migration sample passing reconciliation. Give the customer a defined review period, a written defect process, and a correction path. Do not make a vendor's entire product roadmap your deliverable. Change orders must price additional connectors, historical data cleanup, and customer-caused delays before contractors perform the extra work. Where an automatic acceptance mechanism is used, make notice and review rights explicit and assess enforceability under the governing law.

Allocate liability to the actor that controls the risk. A negotiated cap might reference fees paid or payable under the affected order during twelve months, but that is a bargaining position, not a universally enforceable formula. Define treatment of confidentiality, security incidents, IP indemnities, intentional misconduct, and unpaid fees without casually accepting unlimited exposure for every breach. Check whether vendor remedies actually flow through to your buyer; a vendor's liability cap does not automatically cap your independent promises. Specify indemnity notice, defense control, settlement consent, and exclusions for customer modifications or combinations. Separate customer data, preexisting reseller tools, vendor software, and bespoke deliverables in the ownership clause. Give the customer sufficient rights to use paid deliverables while retaining reusable methods where negotiated. Choose governing law, forum, escalation contacts, and a dispute process that preserves practical collection options. Review mandatory venue, sovereign-immunity, and arbitration restrictions for public customers. UCC Article 2 applicability varies with state law and the software-service mix; draft payment and acceptance mechanics explicitly rather than assuming one classification settles every remedy. Contract terms should explain suspension and data retrieval, not permit surprise lockouts of unrelated customer systems.

Keep the canonical annual model separate from illustrative order spreads. Conservative billings of $300,000 at 8% leave $24,000 owner net profit and imply $276,000 total costs. Base billings of $1,400,000 at 14.0% leave $196,000 and imply $1,204,000 costs. Scaled billings of $2,500,000 at 20% leave $500,000 and imply $2,000,000 costs. Vendor costs are already inside these totals; subtracting them again would understate profit. Conservative and Base each use two full-time staff plus three contractors; Scaled uses three full-time staff plus four contractors. Renewal management on a hypothetical $200,000 renewal at a 12% initial spread produces $24,000 before overhead, not another annual profit scenario. License reconciliation that identifies forty unused $30 monthly seats identifies $14,400 annual customer spend, not reseller revenue. Bill a separately agreed $3,000 review fee only when its stated deliverable is accepted. For slow collections, a $100,000 vendor payment preceding customer receipt by sixty days ties up $100,000; at an illustrative 12% annual carrying rate, that interval costs about $1,973. Deposits, credit limits, and enforceable suspension rights are commercial controls, not substitutes for cash forecasting.

## 3. Apply state, sector, and procurement rules to the actual delivery model  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Data-processing duties follow what the reseller actually does. A license-only seller that never accesses customer records differs from an implementer migrating employee files or administering production tenants. Map collection, access, storage, support exports, subprocessors, retention, and deletion before assigning contract roles. California Civil Code § 1798.100 requires specified contractual treatment for covered disclosures; service-provider and contractor restrictions also depend on the applicable definitions and regulations. Do not assume every business customer or every reseller falls within the same scope. Other state privacy laws use different thresholds, exemptions, and processor duties. Attach the appropriate addendum when facts establish coverage, and prohibit using implementation records for unrelated marketing. In healthcare, receiving or maintaining protected health information on behalf of a covered entity can create business-associate duties under HIPAA. A vendor's BAA does not cover your separate consulting company merely because both touch the same tenant. Apply least privilege through Microsoft Entra or the relevant cloud IAM system, with named accounts and time-bounded access. Keep production credentials out of CRM notes. Use synthetic discovery data until the processing arrangement and access controls are established. Specify incident notice timing contractually while preserving applicable statutory deadlines.

Separate customer security questionnaires from statutory applicability and procurement authority. A bank may impose vendor-management and incident-reporting terms that exceed the reseller's direct obligations; evaluate those promises before signing. Schools can require FERPA-compatible access restrictions and state student-data terms. Public-sector orders add purchasing authority, funding, competition, and recordkeeping questions that commercial templates do not answer. FAR 12.212 addresses federal acquisition of commercial computer software and documentation; it does not authorize a salesperson to accept agency-specific deviations or prove that a contracting officer has funds. Identify the actual purchasing vehicle, authorized buyer, applicable clauses, and order precedence. Never describe a product as government-authorized, domestically sourced, or security-certified without evidence matching the claim's scope. Knowingly false material certifications can create exposure under 31 U.S.C. § 3729. For an illustrative $150,000 public-sector order with $126,000 vendor cost, $24,000 initial spread must absorb bid preparation, required reporting, and delivery costs. If the vehicle does not permit the reseller or product, use a properly authorized prime relationship rather than borrowing another company's contract number. Document subcontract compensation and responsibilities before treating the opportunity as executable revenue.

Create a state-by-state issue register around customer location, delivery location, transaction type, and contract term. Software taxability can change between downloaded software, remote access, maintenance, and separately stated implementation. Economic nexus and resale-certificate rules also vary; a marketplace's collection activity does not automatically settle every direct-sale obligation. Use the relevant state revenue department's classifications, supported by transaction facts, rather than copying a distributor's tax result. An illustrative 6% tax on a taxable $50,000 order is $3,000 collected for remittance, not additional operator revenue. Automatic-renewal requirements vary by state and by business versus consumer customer; New York General Obligations Law § 5-903 can affect certain service, maintenance, or repair contracts when its conditions apply. Check scope and required notice timing instead of assuming all enterprise subscriptions are exempt. State breach-notification statutes require a separate assessment from privacy-law applicability. Maintain a renewal calendar keyed to statutory notice, contractual cancellation, and vendor commitment deadlines. If notice was missed, determine the actual remedy before asserting that the customer owes another year. Preserve approved notice text, recipient records, and delivery evidence with the renewal order.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Classify the transaction
   - Identify the buyer, product, channel, jurisdiction, and actual service activities.
   - Record whether the company resells licenses, earns referral fees, performs implementation, or accesses regulated customer records.
2. Assemble the authority packet
   - Link vendor authorization, customer acceptance, purchasing authority, and required addenda.
   - Compare entity names, tenant identifiers, product schedules, permitted territory, and relevant commitment dates before releasing the order.
3. Reconcile obligations and cash
   - Match vendor payment commitments to customer billing triggers and expected receipt dates.
   - Calculate transaction contribution separately from annual net profit; segregate collected taxes and any third-party reserve balances.
4. Preserve evidence and monitor changes
   - Archive executed versions and schedule renewal, access, and vendor-policy reviews.
   - Assign a named owner to entitlement reconciliation, collection escalation, notice delivery, and removal of expired administrative access.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Verify four conditions before accepting an executable customer order**
> 
> The responsible account owner records evidence for each condition before vendor commitment, production access, or customer billing.
> - Authority verified: selling entity, product rights, buyer eligibility, offer validity, and any procurement vehicle all match the order.
> - Economics verified: vendor commitment, marketplace deductions, customer credit, collection timing, and renewal exposure appear in one cash schedule.
> - Delivery verified: scope, acceptance criteria, access permissions, data roles, and required sector addenda have assigned owners.
> - Claims verified: outreach, security statements, product rights, tax treatment, and routing representations are supported by transaction-specific evidence.

## 4. Use evidence-backed outreach and bound the payment-orchestration adjacency  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Commercial email to business recipients remains subject to CAN-SPAM, including accurate routing information, nondeceptive subjects, applicable advertising identification, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out honored within ten business days. Do not harvest addresses through unauthorized access or buy lists without reviewing provenance and suppression handling. Telephone and text campaigns require a separate TCPA, FCC, and state-law analysis based on technology, number type, purpose, and required permission; a business title is not universal permission to text a personal mobile. Use this email body: 'I work with enterprise buyers on license reconciliation and authorized renewal options. Would a review of your next renewal's seat count, term, and implementation requirements be useful? I would first confirm whether our vendor authorization covers your product and purchasing entity. This is a commercial inquiry. Reply stop to end outreach.' Append the actual sender identity, monitored reply address, and valid postal address before sending. In discovery say: 'Who owns the subscription, which entity signs, what cancellation deadline applies, and may we review a redacted entitlement export?' Do not request passwords, assert savings without a baseline, or imply vendor endorsement beyond documented rights.

The supplied adjacent play high_risk_payfac_iso_mid_cascading_arbitrage concerns wholesale high-risk ISO and payment-orchestration economics, not an ordinary software-reseller license. Its proposed 25–60 domestic and offshore acquiring relationships require genuine bank approval, applicable network registration, underwriting, and jurisdiction-specific review. Vertical labels such as telehealth GLP-1 products, peptides, nutraceutical subscriptions, sweepstakes gaming, coaching, and AI companions do not establish lawful merchandise or acquirer eligibility. A prohibition by Stripe or PayPal is not permission to conceal the merchant elsewhere. On $100,000 merchant processing volume, a 2.10% buy rate costs $2,100 and a 5.50%–8.90% sell rate generates $5,500–$8,900 processing charges, leaving $3,400–$6,800 before other fees, losses, and operating costs. Merchant volume is not reseller revenue, and an ISO may recognize only its contractual residual. A 10% rolling reserve holds $10,000 for the stated release schedule; it is neither earned spread nor available operating cash. Do not add these adjacent figures to the volume's canonical annual cases. Selling integration software does not authorize custody of funds, merchant aggregation, or use of another business's merchant identifier. Funds flow determines whether additional payments licensing analysis is required.

For that adjacent play, define cascading narrowly as routing permitted transactions among disclosed, approved acquiring relationships under applicable network and bank rules. Never retry to defeat an issuer restriction, conceal a merchant, misstate a descriptor, or bypass a prohibited-business decision. The risk fallback is a contractually required 10% rolling reserve for accepted merchants, automated Verifi Rapid Dispute Resolution refunds where eligible and configured, supported 3DS2 step-up authentication using approved risk signals, and strict descriptor transparency. These measures do not cure illegal sales or guarantee authorization. If a bank rejects the activity, disable the route and return the case for underwriting rather than silently moving volume offshore. Say: 'We implement orchestration only for merchant entities and activities approved by your acquiring partners. Please provide the approved entity, descriptor, permitted products, routing restrictions, and reserve schedule before technical discovery.' Price a bounded $12,000 integration against $7,200 direct implementation cost for $4,800 contribution before overhead, with payment credentials tokenized through approved providers. The safer fallback is software-only implementation or a disclosed referral with explicit compensation, no fund custody, and no promise of acquiring acceptance. Suspend work when evidence contradicts the merchant's stated activity.
