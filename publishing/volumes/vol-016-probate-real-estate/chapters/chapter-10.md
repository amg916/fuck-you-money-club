## 1. Instrument eleven plays before automating the probate evidence chain  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a work ledger, not an AI subscription. The two full-time roles are a properly licensed brokerage lead and an operations lead; three contractors supply bounded research, transaction support, and distribution production within their permitted scopes. Measure repetitive minutes by docket collection, identity matching, qualification preparation, checklist maintenance, and distribution preparation. In an illustrative 1,000-minute weekly sample, automate 740 minutes and retain 260 minutes of corrections, exceptions, and required review: 74% net repetitive-work removal. Include review within the remaining time rather than hiding it outside the denominator. This does not mean eliminating 74% of negotiation, fiduciary judgment, or relationship work. Freeze the sample definition for four weeks, record failures as labor, and compare like-for-like case complexity. The eleven mappings below are this chapter's operating decomposition, not eleven additional revenue streams. Only the supplied GIS mapping carries a canonical play identifier. Each mapping must produce either a sourced fact, a reviewed task, or a measurable distribution event. Give every event an owner and a terminal state. If the team cannot explain what happens when the automation stops, the process is not ready for unattended execution.

Build the intake worker with Python's urllib.request, json, hashlib, sqlite3, and datetime modules. Use court-provided feeds, licensed data exports, or expressly permitted portal access; do not bypass authentication or access restrictions. Many probate courts lack stable APIs, so maintain a manual retrieval path and a source-specific polling schedule. Store jurisdiction, case number, document identifier, retrieval timestamp, source URL, content digest, and parser version. An idempotency key combining jurisdiction, case number, document identifier, and digest distinguishes a revised filing from a repeated notification. Download into quarantine, validate expected content type and size, then commit the evidence record and pending job in one SQLite transaction. Treat names as matching clues, never unique keys. An estate caption can resemble another case, and a decedent's mailing address may not identify estate-owned property. Require separate links between case, authorized representative, parcel, vesting document, and proposed service. Route low-confidence matches to the researcher before creating any contact task. Maintain an explicit deletion schedule and least-privilege access because public court availability does not justify unrestricted internal copying of sensitive estate records.

Use an LLM as an evidence extractor behind a deterministic validator. Supply only necessary document excerpts, instruct the model to treat document text as data, and permit no outbound messages or transaction tools. Require a JSON object containing field value, document identifier, page reference, quoted supporting text, and extraction status. An absent fact becomes a review task rather than a guessed representative, deadline, or sale power. Validate types, allowed states, date formats, and source references in Python; reject extra keys and unsupported claims. Store the model identifier, prompt version, and input digest so reviewers can reproduce the decision path without retaining unnecessary personal details in logs. For webhook inputs, verify the provider's documented signature over the original bytes with hmac.compare_digest, enforce timestamp tolerance, and reject reused event identifiers. A valid signature proves message origin, not authority to sell property. Persist accepted events before acknowledging receipt and process them asynchronously. Limit retry counts and route exhausted jobs to a visible exception queue. The operations lead owns daily queue clearance; the brokerage lead owns any interpretation that could change who receives advice or what action proceeds.

## 2. Turn authority, court-sale timing, and parcel access into reviewed checklists  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Model a transaction as dependencies, not a universal closing-date template. Begin with representative identity, appointment evidence, scope of authority, property ownership, engagement terms, and the jurisdiction's sale procedure. California illustrates why branching matters: Probate Code section 10501 distinguishes powers available under full versus limited authority, while sections 10580 through 10592 address notice of proposed action procedures when applicable. Sales subject to court confirmation follow a different path; section 10308 addresses qualifying increased offers at confirmation. Do not generalize that path to every state or every California estate. Have estate counsel or the responsible qualified professional identify the applicable route and record the supporting instrument. Only then generate draft tasks for notices, valuation evidence, marketing, hearings, objections, and closing conditions. Never derive a controlling deadline solely from an LLM reading a docket caption. Use the actual order, applicable rule, service details, and reviewed calendar calculation. A new objection, amended order, or authority change invalidates dependent tasks and triggers re-review. This design prevents a polished automated checklist from advancing a sale that the representative cannot presently authorize.

Define consequential outputs narrowly and explicitly: legal-authority conclusions, pricing recommendations, listing publication, offer submission, vendor spending, notices, and settlement instructions require the designated person's review. AI can assemble comparable property evidence, but the licensed professional selects adjustments and approves pricing. A concierge worker can prepare a cleanout scope, but the authorized representative approves disposal and spending before anything leaves the property. Store review identity, timestamp, evidence version, and approved action scope. Approval expires when material inputs change; it does not attach forever to the estate record. Use two-person verification for changed payment instructions, with an independently sourced callback number rather than contact details inside the change request. California Business and Professions Code section 10130 anchors the licensing boundary for brokerage activity; contractor titles do not expand permitted work. Route questions about fiduciary duties, conflicts, or contested authority to estate counsel. For acquisitions, disclose the operator's buyer-side interest and separate it from brokerage representation under the applicable duties. Never let an automated qualification score decide which family member controls the engagement or whether an estate should accept an affiliated buyer's offer.

The required gis_landlocked_parcel_easement_assemblage play belongs inside this evidence system, not outside it. Overlay county parcel geometry with roads and candidate access corridors, then retrieve deeds, easement instruments, plats, and relevant title exceptions. Geometry screens opportunities; it cannot establish legal access, extinguishment, scope, maintenance obligations, or the right to transfer an easement. Illustratively, screen 100 parcels at $0.04 each for $4 of variable compute, review ten candidates for twenty minutes each at $45 per hour for $150, and purchase three record packets at $60 for $180. Initial screening costs $334 before title counsel, survey work, travel, or any option payment. If one candidate supports a voluntary strip option, a $2,000 option payment plus $3,000 of scoped diligence adds $5,000 of separate capital exposure; neither amount is brokerage revenue. Obtain title and survey review before negotiating recorded access terms, and use a documented diligence contingency with a realistic expiry. Do not obstruct existing access or manufacture pressure against neighbors. If rights or economics fail, exercise diligence contingencies and offer survey-backed voluntary access services instead. Recognize any separate service economics outside the canonical brokerage scorecard.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the evidence event
   - Persist the source digest, jurisdiction, case identifier, and document version.
   - Reject duplicate events while preserving revised filings as separate evidence versions.
2. Assemble the proposed checklist change
   - Generate source-linked draft tasks without sending messages or changing commitments.
   - Mark affected dependencies and assign a named reviewer for every consequential change.
3. Review and release the bounded action
   - Record the reviewer, supporting evidence version, approved scope, and release timestamp.
   - Recheck current evidence immediately before execution so stale reviews cannot authorize new facts.
4. Reconcile execution and recover exceptions
   - Confirm delivery or completion, then reconcile the action against the durable event ledger.
   - Route uncertain outcomes to manual verification before retrying any externally visible action.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight checks before an external action**
> 
> Stop the affected action when any check fails; continue unrelated research that does not depend on the disputed fact.
> - Verify the representative, property, current appointment evidence, and action-specific authority against source documents.
> - Verify the governing sale route, applicable notices, reviewed dates, and unresolved objections with the responsible qualified reviewer.
> - Verify the recipient, permitted channel, suppression status, and exact message version before releasing any outreach.
> - Verify recorded access evidence, conflict disclosures, capital exposure, and signed diligence terms before committing to a parcel option.

## 3. Build permissioned distribution loops around attorney usefulness and county accuracy  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The attorney referral loop starts with service reliability. Offer probate practices a concise, permissioned property-readiness report: verified authority status, occupancy questions, approved vendor milestones, marketing progress, and unresolved decisions. Share only information the client has authorized for that recipient. A Python worker can assemble the report from reviewed states, but the broker releases it after checking accuracy and tone. Measure introductions, accepted consultations, signed engagements, and closed commissions by originating relationship. An illustrative cohort of twenty attorney relationships producing two introductions each creates forty introductions; a 50% consultation rate, 50% engagement rate, and 60% closing rate yields six expected closings. At $17,000 retained commission each, that cohort contributes $102,000 toward, not on top of, the $510,000 Base case. Track actual cohorts over the four-to-eighteen-month first-deal horizon rather than declaring monthly projections earned revenue. Do not pay for referrals involving covered settlement services contrary to 12 U.S.C. section 2607 and Regulation X. Document bona fide services and any permitted arrangements independently; a newsletter label does not cure a disguised referral payment. Relationship quality comes from reducing execution uncertainty, not purchasing access to distressed families.

Programmatic acquisition should mean structured publishing, not hundreds of interchangeable county pages. Build one reviewed page per genuinely supported jurisdiction, using current court links, filing terminology, local sale-path explanations, and a practical executor property-readiness worksheet. Publish source dates and an editorial owner; remove stale procedural claims when the source changes. Google Search Console identifies questions bringing qualified visitors, while first-party form events connect requests to permissioned follow-up. In an illustrative monthly cohort, 1,200 relevant visits at a 3% request rate produce 36 requests. A 25% consultation rate produces nine consultations; a 20% engagement rate produces 1.8 expected engagements; a 60% eventual closing rate produces 1.08 expected closings. These are planning expectations, not fractional transactions booked into revenue. Deduplicate people and estates across attorney and organic sources before attributing results. A shareable worksheet can generate a second visit from a sibling or adviser without revealing a decedent's address or case history. Avoid public client maps, fabricated testimonials, and implied court endorsement. If a page cannot answer a local question better than the underlying official source, link to that source and spend the production budget on a useful service workflow.

Outbound automation should prioritize relevant professional relationships rather than converting docket exposure into indiscriminate family solicitation. Research probate attorneys, qualified property vendors, and other appropriate business contacts, then draft an individually relevant introduction with a specific operational resource. A public email address does not establish permission for every marketing channel. Commercial email must follow applicable CAN-SPAM requirements, including accurate routing information, required identification, a valid postal address, and a functioning opt-out process. Telephone and text programs require a separate assessment under 47 U.S.C. section 227, implementing rules, and applicable state requirements; do not assume manual and automated campaigns share identical obligations. Illustratively, 100 reviewed professional emails costing two minutes each at $40 per hour consume $133.33 of labor. Twelve replies, four meetings, and one productive referral relationship would create useful learning, but no commission until a resulting transaction closes. Suppress opt-outs centrally before every send, cap frequency, and stop sequences after a reply or complaint. When delivery quality deteriorates, pause the campaign and inspect targeting, permission, and message substance instead of rotating identities or increasing volume. The useful loop is better service producing better introductions, not more automation producing more intrusion.

## 4. Price every automated run and scale only after recovery works  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Cost the automation at its real unit boundary. Assume an illustrative completed evidence run uses 4,000 input tokens at $2 per million, 1,000 output tokens at $6 per million, and $0.004 of variable compute and storage: $0.018 per run. These are budgeting assumptions, not quoted vendor tariffs. At 6,000 runs monthly, variable machine cost is $108. If 8% need three minutes of exception handling, 480 exceptions consume 24 hours; at $40 per hour, review costs $960. The resulting $1,068 is $0.178 per processed run before fixed software, paid court records, acquisition labor, ordinary transaction review, and supervision. If exceptions double to 16%, handling rises to 48 hours and $1,920; total variable machine-plus-exception cost becomes $2,028. That sensitivity matters more than trimming a fraction of a cent from inference. Record tokens, retrieval charges, retries, review minutes, and accepted outputs separately. Report both cost per processed run and cost per accepted evidence packet so failed output is not disguised as productivity. Use weekly samples to compare machine-extracted facts against source documents and to identify which parser, court feed, or document type generates rework.

Reconcile improvements to the canonical scorecard without changing its revenue definition. Conservative retains $120,000 of annual brokerage commissions after splits, incurs $96,000 of total modeled costs, and leaves $24,000 owner net profit at 20% with 2 FT + 3 C. Base retains $510,000, incurs $369,750, and leaves $140,250 at 27.5% with the same team count. Scaled retains $900,000, incurs $585,000, and leaves $315,000 at 35% with 3 FT + 4 C. Illustrative closing bridges are eight closings at $15,000 retained each, thirty at $17,000, and fifty at $18,000 respectively; actual negotiated commissions and brokerage splits must replace those assumptions. Home sale prices, acquisition principal, escrow balances, and option proceeds do not belong in these brokerage revenue totals. Treat the cost envelopes as reconciliation constraints, not proof that local wages and contractor demand fit. Build an actual staffing budget with hours, payroll costs, and contractor scopes before committing spend. The $3,000 to $18,000 monthly operating range is not a substitute for all-in annual scenario costs. Allocate automation expenses within those totals; do not deduct them again after calculating canonical profit or count hypothetical labor savings as additional revenue.

Prove recovery before expanding geography or outreach. Maintain a durable event ledger, bounded exponential backoff for transient failures, and a queue for jobs requiring manual intervention. Honor source rate limits and stop retrying permanent authorization failures. Reconcile source event counts against accepted records daily; a silent connector can look healthy while missing an amended order. Test restoring the database and document index from encrypted backups, then replay a controlled event set without duplicate messages or vendor bookings. An external send can succeed even when its acknowledgment is lost, so inspect provider delivery records before replaying uncertain actions. Keep a manual board containing current case owners, reviewed milestones, and upcoming deadlines for service continuity. Expand only after measured repetitive-work removal exceeds 70%, review queues clear within documented service targets, and source-backed accuracy holds across representative samples. Housing downturns reduce closing probability, family disputes increase review hours, and court delays extend cash collection. Stress the plan with fewer closings and longer holding periods rather than merely higher software usage. Move to the seven-person Scaled configuration when sustained workload supports it, preserving named owners for authority review, client decisions, and distribution quality instead of letting automation create ownerless obligations.
