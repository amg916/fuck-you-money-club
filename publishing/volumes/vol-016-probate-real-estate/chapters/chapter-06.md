## 1. Mine estate-property intersections instead of buying bereavement lists  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start in one county where your supervising broker can support probate listings and local title counsel can explain the sale path. Build a daily work queue from the superior court probate index, assessor parcel records, recorder instruments, and municipal enforcement portals. A petition identifies a possible estate, not an authorized seller; an assessor mailing address identifies a tax destination, not necessarily the owner or personal representative. Join records through parcel number, vesting name, property address, and case number, retaining each source URL and retrieval date. Require a human check before merging common names or contacting anybody. Exclude sealed records and avoid republishing family allegations or sensitive docket attachments. Use Airtable or a protected SQLite database for normalized records, with separate tables for estates, parcels, authority documents, contacts, and activities. One estate can own several parcels, while one parcel can involve multiple proceedings; forcing everything into a single lead row produces duplicate outreach and mistaken authority assumptions. Prefer permitted docket alerts and bulk downloads over fragile scraping. Record access limits, source refresh intervals, and suppression decisions so another operator can reproduce the shortlist without reconstructing a researcher's memory.

The highest-value signal is usually a solvable coordination problem attached to a marketable asset: an appointed representative living elsewhere, an unsecured vacant home, an unresolved permit, or carrying costs consuming estate liquidity. None proves distress pricing or permission to sell. Implement municipal_code_violation_trigger_arbitrage by checking permitted municipal building portals and county recorder dockets daily for failed structural or fire inspections, expired permits, code citations, and newly filed mechanic's liens. Cross-match those events against verified estate parcels, then determine whether the estate wants contractor or construction-attorney assistance. A filed lien is a claim requiring investigation, not an adjudicated debt. Route an authorized introduction exclusively to one suitable provider for a documented response window; share only necessary details and never imply municipal endorsement. Illustratively, 40 alerts at eight review minutes consume 320 minutes; at $30 per research hour, screening costs $160 before software and supervision. If six events match estates and two produce requested introductions, research cost is $80 per introduction. Book no contractor invoice as brokerage revenue. When daily access is unavailable, use low-frequency scheduled API or bulk exports and identify every outreach as private commercial contractor or remediation assistance.

Operate the 11 mapped plays below as complementary entry points into one estate pipeline, not eleven independent businesses or eleven revenue forecasts. New appointments establish authority candidates; attorney relationships reduce documentation friction; executor education captures voluntary demand; condition signals identify specific work somebody must coordinate. Every record receives one primary acquisition source and optional supporting signals, preventing a permit notice, lawyer introduction, and webinar registration from becoming three credited opportunities. Distinguish a brokerage prospect from an acquisition prospect before discussing price. If your business might purchase the property, disclose that role and associated conflicts, obtain independent valuation support, and follow the jurisdiction's fiduciary and brokerage requirements. Do not present your own offer as independent market advice. Underwriting must separately identify purchase principal, repair capital, financing expense, and resale exposure; none belongs in this volume's retained-commission scorecard. For the initial launch, favor brokerage and executor coordination over speculative inventory. Review source quality weekly using documented authority, reachable decision-makers, and achievable sale timing rather than raw lead counts. Suspend a source that repeatedly creates mistaken identities or unwanted contact, even when its apparent cost per record looks low.

## 2. Qualify authority, economics, and readiness before spending acquisition money  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a 100-point qualification scorecard, but separate ranking from permission. Award up to 25 points for documented authority, 20 for a verified estate-property ownership connection, 15 for a stated service need, 15 for feasible net proceeds, 15 for a realistic decision timeline, and 10 for an appropriate contact route. A high total never cures missing authority or a prohibited contact method. Records scoring 75 or more can receive an appointment request only after those prerequisites are satisfied; scores from 50 through 74 receive missing-evidence tasks; lower scores remain research records or are closed with a reason. These thresholds are internal workflow choices, not externally validated predictors. Request current letters, the appointment order, relevant restrictions, and the representative's preferred counsel contact. In California, distinguish full from limited authority under the Independent Administration of Estates Act; Probate Code section 10501 addresses transactions requiring court supervision, including real-property sales under limited authority. Proposed-action notice procedures may apply under sections 10580 and following. Have counsel establish the applicable procedure before promising a signing or closing date. A beneficiary's enthusiasm is useful context, but it does not substitute for the representative's verified powers.

Build a property economics sheet before recommending repairs or promising an attractive net. Obtain a broker price opinion or comparative market analysis, request title review through the proper channel, and identify mortgage payoff requirements, recorded liens, taxes, occupancy, insurance, utilities, and probable sale expenses. Separate recorded amounts from current payoff figures and preliminary estimates from written quotes. For illustration, a $750,000 sale less $310,000 mortgage payoff, $18,000 other verified obligations, $45,000 selling expenses, and $12,000 approved property work leaves $365,000 before other estate liabilities and distributions. That figure is a property-level planning estimate, not the brokerage's revenue or the beneficiaries' assured distribution. Compare as-is and repair paths using incremental net proceeds and time, not photographic appeal. If $20,000 of work supports only $25,000 of additional proceeds before another $6,000 of carrying expense, the repair path loses $1,000 before execution risk. Ask the representative and counsel who can authorize spending, sign vendor contracts, and approve access. Do not advance substantial repair money casually; reimbursement, security, conflicts, and estate liquidity need explicit treatment. Preserve vendor estimates and version every revised net sheet.

Translate qualification into a service proposition with clear boundaries: establish the sale route, document condition, present pricing options, coordinate authorized vendors, and report progress. Avoid selling speed where the bottleneck is a hearing, objection, or contested appointment. During discovery, ask what must happen before a sale decision, who advises the representative, whether anybody occupies the property, and which expenses are already uncomfortable. Do not ask a newly contacted family to recount personal circumstances merely to improve a sales script. A useful first deliverable is a two-page readiness brief with an evidence list, unresolved questions, estimated work sequence, and three pricing alternatives supported by comparable sales. If the family disagrees, stop framing consensus as a marketing objection and return decision authority and dispute management to counsel. If the property is occupied, require a lawful access plan rather than assuming probate permits immediate possession. If title is unclear, do not solicit a binding price commitment before the appropriate professionals resolve the issue. Assign every blocking question a responsible person and next review date. This makes your pipeline operationally useful even when the correct immediate answer is to wait.

> 
> Qualification scores prioritize work; these checks determine whether the next proposed action is appropriate.
> - Match parcel vesting to estate evidence and verify current letters, appointment restrictions, and the actual signing representative.

> - Document the applicable court-supervised or independent-administration sale procedure with counsel, including notice and objection handling.
> - Check source access permissions, contact suppression, channel-specific requirements, and the representative's preferred communication route.

## 3. Run a restrained multichannel cadence that earns the first appointment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Design a 45-day initial cadence around usefulness and contact permissions, not escalating pressure. On day one, send a plain postal letter when that route is appropriate: identify your licensed brokerage, explain the property coordination service, and offer a short readiness checklist. Do not print probate details on the envelope or imply court affiliation. On day ten, make a manual call only after applicable federal and state solicitation rules, suppression lists, and calling hours have been checked. Publication of a telephone number does not itself authorize marketing calls or texts. On day twenty-one, send one substantive follow-up by a permitted route, such as an explanation of valuation versus an investor offer. On day forty-five, ask whether they want milestone-based follow-up; without a response, stop the initial sequence rather than silently enrolling them in indefinite pursuit. Use requested email for the checklist and appointment logistics, while applying CAN-SPAM requirements to commercial email. Do not launch automated calls, prerecorded messages, or marketing texts merely because a CRM supports them. Configure channel eligibility and suppression before importing records into HubSpot, Pipedrive, or another CRM.

Make professional outreach more specific than asking lawyers to send deals. Identify twelve local probate practices and offer a sample property-status brief, a description of broker coverage, and a reliable response standard for requested valuation work. Ask which property problems consume their staff's time and show how your deliverable reduces that work without trespassing into legal representation. Track meetings, requested follow-ups, introductions, and completed assignments separately. Do not exchange settlement-service referrals for things of value where RESPA section 8 applies; also assess state brokerage and professional-conduct rules. A cleanout contractor's invoice and an attorney's fee remain their revenue, not yours. For inbound demand, publish a county-specific executor property checklist, a page explaining your brokerage service, and an accessible consultation form that requests only necessary information. Google Business Profile and Search Console can identify whether local searchers find the pages and request appointments. Avoid publishing identifiable case studies without permission. Offer educational events through libraries or professional groups when invited, and distinguish general process education from a proposed engagement. Measure qualified consultations per staff hour rather than page views alone; a small audience with actual authority can outperform a large audience of curious researchers.

Handle objections by resolving the underlying uncertainty, not by defeating the speaker. When a representative says their attorney handles everything, ask whether counsel would find a concise property-status brief useful; accept a refusal immediately. When they prefer an investor, compare a documented cash offer with a supported brokerage net sheet using identical carrying-cost assumptions and clearly disclosed roles. When they fear repair costs, present an as-is route alongside a bounded vendor scope; never manufacture safety assurances about an uninspected property. When relatives disagree, explain that you can document pricing alternatives while counsel addresses authority and disputes. After a useful conversation, request one modest next action: a document review, an authorized walkthrough, or a scheduled pricing meeting. Send a written recap stating who supplies each document and what you will deliver next. The appointment should end with an explicit decision: signed brokerage engagement, a defined follow-up milestone, or a respectful close. Do not classify a conversation, free valuation, or unsigned proposal as a win. Record objections as structured reasons so the next month's messaging answers actual questions rather than repeating a script that failed the previous cohort.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Build and verify the county queue
   - Deduplicate estate-property matches and preserve source evidence.
   - Check appointment status, parcel ownership, existing representation, and source-use restrictions before any outreach task is released.
2. Assign qualification and contact eligibility
   - Score six dimensions and separately verify channel prerequisites.
   - Place missing documents with a named owner; keep suppressed contacts out of every active campaign regardless of their score.
3. Deliver the readiness consultation
   - Offer one practical next step through the permitted cadence.
   - Produce an evidence-backed brief, discuss sale routes and costs, and confirm what the representative wants done next.
4. Convert and instrument the mandate
   - Obtain the broker-approved engagement and open the transaction checklist.
   - Record signing authority, negotiated compensation, notice or court requirements, source attribution, and the expected collection milestone.

## 4. Measure closed wins, collected commissions, and the economics of patience  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use a bounded cohort model to plan the first 3-to-10 closed wins over 4–18 months; the following conversion rates are test assumptions, not published market benchmarks. Suppose 600 deduplicated estate-property matches produce 240 verified authority candidates, of which 120 receive an appropriate, deliverable outreach attempt. Thirty-six hold substantive conversations, eighteen attend consultations, ten sign mandates, and six eventually close. The conditional stage rates are 40%, 50%, 30%, 50%, 55.6%, and 60%, respectively; raw-record-to-close yield is 1%. At unchanged performance, three closings require about 300 initial matches and ten require about 1,000, but county supply and court capacity can invalidate that linear scaling. Track elapsed days between stages and retain open files in their actual stages rather than counting them as losses or successes prematurely. Separate no-contact outcomes from disqualification, rejected proposals, and delayed closings. Six closings at an illustrative $15,000 retained commission each produce $90,000 for that cohort when earned under the engagement; this is neither an annual forecast nor a replacement for the canonical scenarios. Track collection separately from recognition so cash planning reflects actual receipts.

Reconcile every annual plan to retained brokerage commissions after brokerage splits. One illustrative unit is a $750,000 completed sale with a separately negotiated 2.5% side commission and 80% brokerage retention: $750,000 multiplied by 2.5% multiplied by 80% equals $15,000. These inputs are modeling choices, not a customary or prescribed commission rate. Eight such closings produce the Conservative $120,000 revenue; subtract $96,000 of total modeled operating costs to leave $24,000 owner profit at 20%. Thirty-four produce Base revenue of $510,000; $369,750 of costs leaves $140,250 at 27.5%. Sixty produce Scaled revenue of $900,000; $585,000 of costs leaves $315,000 at 35%. The corresponding teams remain 2 FT plus 3 C, 2 FT plus 3 C, and 3 FT plus 4 C. The underlying sale values are $6,000,000, $25,500,000, and $45,000,000, respectively, and never become operator revenue. Treat the stated $3,000–$18,000 monthly opex range as a planning input whose covered categories must be identified, not automatically the complete annual cost envelope. Payroll, contractors, marketing, and transaction costs must reconcile to the canonical total without double-counting amounts already removed in brokerage splits.

Instrument the first cohort before increasing spend. If its acquisition work costs $12,000 across research, mail, content, and attributable labor, six collected closings imply $2,000 acquisition cost per closed client. If only three close, that rises to $4,000; spending the same money does not preserve the original efficiency. Include acquisition costs inside, not in addition to, the annual scenario's expense total. Review the funnel monthly and the legal or title blockers weekly. Poor authority yield suggests a record-matching problem; strong conversations but weak mandates suggest an unclear service proposition; signed mandates stalled before closing suggest transaction execution or court timing rather than insufficient marketing. Allocate the $15,000–$100,000 startup capital range through a dated cash schedule that includes licensing, insurance, systems, research, marketing, and runway. A $15,000 balance against $3,000 monthly outflow lasts five months before setup costs, making an eighteen-month first closing untenable without additional funding or lower burn. Use docket alerts and transaction checklists to reduce missed work, not to remove professional judgment. Expand only after a mature cohort demonstrates repeatable sourcing, service delivery, and collected commission economics.
