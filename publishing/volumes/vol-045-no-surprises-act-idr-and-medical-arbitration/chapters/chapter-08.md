## 1. Establish authoritative identities before enriching an opportunity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an identity ledger, not a prospect spreadsheet. Give each provider organization, rendering clinician, payer legal entity, employer plan, and disputed service a separate internal identifier. NPPES supplies an NPI and published taxonomy; it does not establish network status, licensure, ownership, or federal IDR eligibility. Join an organization NPI to the signed engagement, authorized billing records, and relevant practice locations before attaching claims. Use CMS Care Compare to corroborate publicly reported affiliations, then verify current practice relationships directly. An apparent hospital affiliation is neither proof of employment nor proof that a particular encounter qualifies under the No Surprises Act. Store the source URL, retrieval time, effective period, and original artifact hash beside every asserted attribute. Keep contradictory observations rather than overwriting them. A directory address changed yesterday cannot retroactively establish the service location six months earlier. The first three chapter-local mapped plays are Provider Identity Resolution, Practice Affiliation Verification, and Federal-versus-State Routing. At 100 prospective practices and six minutes per identity check, initial screening consumes ten hours; resolving only twenty ambiguous matches at fifteen minutes each adds five hours. That bounded queue beats letting every ambiguous record consume a full arbitration analyst review.

Jurisdiction is a document question, not a payer-logo classifier. DOL Form 5500 records can corroborate an employer plan's existence and reported arrangements, but filing exemptions, reporting lag, and sponsor subsidiaries prevent a reliable one-field determination of funding status. Obtain the applicable plan documents, coverage details, initial remittance, service location, and evidence concerning any state election that matters to the encounter. Separate ERISA status from self-funding; neither label alone resolves every federal-versus-state payment issue. Test whether a specified state law or applicable All-Payer Model Agreement determines the payment amount before routing to federal IDR under 42 U.S.C. § 300gg-111. Medicare, Medicaid, and other excluded coverage must leave this commercial workflow. Record why the service category and circumstances meet the relevant federal protections, including emergency care or covered nonemergency services at participating facilities. For thirty uncertain claims, a twelve-minute document check costs six analyst hours; compare that with thirty avoidable filing expenditures and dismissed cases. The fallback is a missing-evidence queue with a named requester and deadline, not an automated federal filing based on an insurance card. State insurance department materials supplement, but do not replace, encounter-specific plan evidence.

Use CMS IDR public-use releases to understand the market's case mix, reporting periods, and operational congestion. They are aggregate observations, not a provider prospect list, a current case-status API, or a forecast that your offers will be collected. Preserve each release with its methodology because submissions, disputes, determinations, and payment outcomes have different denominators. A reported prevailing offer share cannot supply a collection probability without separate receipt evidence. The directory in this chapter contains eighteen named resources across three matrices; the fourth matrix contains operating tests rather than additional directory entries. Cost tiers distinguish free public access, included customer access, and negotiated licenses, because commercial endpoints rarely have universal pricing. Refresh cadence likewise separates a source's publication schedule from the desk's polling schedule. Do not manufacture a latency commitment where the publisher provides none. Maintain an acquisition manifest containing retrieval timestamps, response status, content hashes, parser version, and row counts. A public download that silently changes columns should fail into quarantine instead of rewriting the identity ledger. For six public sources checked weekly, twenty minutes of review per source budgets two hours; measure whether detected changes actually affect routing before increasing that frequency.

## 2. Normalize claim evidence and build a defensible enrichment waterfall  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The next four mapped plays are ERA Underpayment Triage, Remittance Code Explanation, QPA Disclosure Reconciliation, and Licensed Benchmark Enrichment. Begin with authorized X12 835 delivery through the provider's clearinghouse or payer channel. Preserve the original interchange and parse transaction, claim, service-line, adjustment, remark, and payment reference structures without flattening them prematurely. A CARC explains an adjustment category; it does not prove the payer violated the Act or that the claim belongs in federal IDR. RARCs add context but cannot replace the benefit document and actual notice. Track payer claim identifiers, service dates, modifiers, units, rendering NPI, location, and remittance trace references as distinct fields. Corrected claims and reversals must reference their predecessors. If 2,000 service lines arrive monthly and rules flag eight percent, 160 lines need review; at seven minutes each, the queue consumes about 18.7 hours before evidence collection. That is a staffing input, not 160 payable disputes. Route missing QPA disclosures to a document request, contradictory adjustments to analyst review, and suspected duplicates to a reconciliation queue. Never promote a code match directly into a demand packet without verifying the coverage and service circumstances.

The enrichment waterfall should spend progressively, stopping whenever a cheaper authoritative source answers the question. First use the engagement records, 835, explanation of benefits, and payer's required QPA disclosures. Next consult official identity and jurisdiction records. Then request missing encounter evidence from the provider through a secure channel. Only after eligibility is supportable should the desk buy licensed benchmark observations or commission clinical analysis. FAIR Health products can inform market context under their license terms; access to a website does not establish permission to scrape or redistribute a database. CMS Medicare schedules can help detect unit errors and implausible coding assumptions, but public-payer reimbursement rates are among the prohibited considerations in federal IDR determinations. Likewise, do not use usual-and-customary charges or billed charges as award-setting evidence. Keep those diagnostics outside the submitted merits packet. Offer construction must follow the current governing statute, operative rules, and applicable judicial decisions, rather than treating a benchmark multiplier as entitlement. If twenty eligible cases require an illustrative $35 licensed lookup allocation each, the enrichment budget is $700; if ten fail routing first, purchasing only the remaining ten saves $350 without sacrificing admissible evidence.

Treat HIPAA architecture as part of the data model. Execute Business Associate Agreements where required under 45 CFR §§ 164.502(e) and 164.504(e), and confirm that the selected vendor service, storage region, support access, and subcontracting arrangement are covered. A vendor's healthcare marketing page is not the agreement. Apply minimum-necessary access to caseworkers, segregate prospecting data from claim evidence, encrypt stored and transmitted records, and record access events without copying clinical narratives into ordinary application logs. HHS OCR's breach portal is a diligence signal about reported incidents, not a certification of current security. Use it to frame vendor questions rather than automatically rejecting every listed entity. Validate parsers against authorized test fixtures or genuinely synthetic examples before processing production remittances. A payment reversal, split remittance, duplicate interchange, negative adjustment, or changed claim identifier must produce an explainable result. Five fixtures covering those patterns are worth more than a dashboard showing thousands of untested rows. For restricted data, fallback means an approved provider transfer channel and manual structured extraction, not uploading the record to a public converter or general enrichment service. Retain required security documentation separately from the retention schedule chosen for the underlying claim artifacts.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Accept a traceable source artifact
   - Receive the authorized ERA and associated notice through an approved channel.
   - Record sender, receipt time, content hash, coverage period, and immutable storage reference before parsing.
2. Normalize without losing lineage
   - Extract claim and service-line records while retaining adjustment relationships.
   - Preserve original identifiers, reversals, payer traces, code-list versions, and links back to source segments.
3. Resolve eligibility before buying enrichment
   - Verify service circumstances, applicable payment regime, and deadline evidence.
   - Request missing plan documents first; buy licensed context only for cases that remain supportable.
4. Reconcile and release a review packet
   - Compare parsed totals with the remittance and route exceptions to an analyst.
   - Require an explained difference of zero or a documented adjustment before the packet enters deadline processing.

## 3. Control deadlines, portal evidence, and restrained commercial signals  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The next three mapped plays are Open-Negotiation Deadline Control, IDR Portal Receipt Capture, and Cooling-Off Cohort Control. Model deadlines as events with evidence, not dates someone typed into a calendar. Under the federal framework, distinguish the window for initiating open negotiation after initial payment or denial from the thirty-business-day negotiation period itself. Then calculate the four-business-day initiation window following that period using the operative counting instructions and any applicable extensions. Certified entity selection is a separate procedural step; four business days is not a universal entity-selection or batching deadline. Capture the triggering notice, delivery method, receipt evidence, calculation version, holiday assumptions, and reviewer. Maintain both the statutory due date and an earlier internal target. If twelve cases approach a filing window and each needs twenty minutes of final verification, reserve four analyst hours before the internal target. Portal downtime does not automatically suspend a deadline. Preserve outage evidence and follow CMS's current documented assistance or extension process. Never treat an email to support as proof of a filed dispute. The fallback is a documented escalation using authorized channels, with the case visibly unresolved until an actual receipt exists.

Use the CMS Federal IDR portal as the submission system of record, not as an assumed general-purpose API. Do not invent a bulk endpoint or automate interactive credentials around portal restrictions. Capture confirmation identifiers, submitted versions, fee instructions, entity communications, and determinations through authorized access. Reconcile internal status to those artifacts daily during active filing windows. Cooling-off logic must attach the determination event to the relevant parties and same or similar items or services under the operative rule. Do not reduce every case to payer brand plus procedure code. Track the ninety-calendar-day restriction and the applicable subsequent initiation window separately; do not confuse this clock with the thirty-business-day negotiation clock. Batching needs its own dated eligibility test because governing rules and judicial decisions can change permissible grouping. Similar spreadsheet rows are not sufficient proof. At forty candidate lines, a proposed four-case batch may reduce duplicated preparation, but model four separate filings until grouping is confirmed. Fee savings belong in the forecast only after eligibility is documented. Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook can mirror internal reminders, but the event ledger and preserved source documents must remain authoritative when reminders are edited or invitations fail.

A removed script might reflect a tag-manager change, regional delivery, or site redesign rather than churn. Treat months ten through eleven after an observed installation as a weak outreach hypothesis, not a known annual renewal date. ## 4. Reconcile collections, test failure modes, and protect the scorecard  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The twelfth mapped play is Cash-Verified Contingency Reconciliation. Build its ledger around money received, not offers selected. Suppose the payer initially paid $1,200, an IDR determination sets total payment at $4,200, and only $2,400 of the additional $3,000 has actually arrived. Collected incremental cash is $2,400; the remaining $600 stays a receivable and earns no contingency fee under this model. For illustration, allocate $120 of non-refundable administrative expense, $80 of other unrecovered case fees, and $200 of casework to the collected increment. These are modeling amounts, not the current CMS fee schedule. The distributable recovery basis is $2,000; a 25% contractual operator share earns $500. Record certified entity fee deposits separately while disposition is pending. Return of the prevailing party's deposit must be supported by the applicable procedure and actual refund evidence; losing or ineligible-case deposits must not become assumed refunds. Treat unrecovered amounts as costs unless a documented refund right supports a receivable. Define who advances expenses, how recoveries reimburse them, and which casework charges reduce the sharing basis. A dollar allocated before the split cannot also be deducted a second time from the same operator earnings calculation.

Conservative recognized operator revenue is $110,000, allowable total operator expense is $77,000, and owner net profit is $33,000 at 30%. Base revenue is $505,000, expense is $287,850, and profit is $217,150 at 43.0%. Scaled revenue is $900,000, expense is $396,000, and profit is $504,000 at 56%. At the illustrative $500 earned share per completed collection equivalent, the revenue targets require 220, 1,010, and 1,800 equivalents annually. They are collection equivalents, not required filing counts or promised outcomes. If only 80% of otherwise comparable completed matters reach that collection level during the year, illustrative work volumes rise to 275, about 1,263, and 2,250 matters respectively. Test whether the assigned 1 FT + 2 C, 1 FT + 2 C, and 2 FT + 3 C teams can support those queues. Apply principal-versus-agent analysis under ASC 606 to actual contracts; the scorecard here counts earned operator shares, not gross provider recoveries passed through an account. Maintain separate expense buckets so case allocations and operator overhead reconcile without duplicate deductions.

Turn the scorecard into a diagnostic dashboard with four independent measures: evidence completeness, procedural timeliness, earned revenue, and cash aging. Do not combine them into a single success percentage. An eligible, timely, prevailing dispute can still have an unpaid balance. Match supplemental ERAs to bank deposits and provider posting records, then flag unmatched receipts, recoupments, reversals, and cross-claim offsets before issuing the contingency invoice. Use SQLite for a small local reconciliation prototype or PostgreSQL for a controlled multiuser ledger; neither product choice substitutes for access restrictions, backup testing, and audit history. Keep protected records out of source control, telemetry exports, and prospecting systems. Test recovery from a backup using a restricted environment and document elapsed restoration time. A nightly job should produce an exception count, oldest unresolved age, and named owner, not merely a green execution status. Review the eight controls below weekly and before every material parser or rule change. If an input feed fails, freeze affected decisions while preserving unaffected work. The useful automation boundary is precise: software assembles traceable evidence and calculations; accountable staff resolve disputed facts, confirm submissions, and authorize collection-based billing only after the underlying cash movement is matched.
