## 1. Protect liquidity before buying capacity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a segregated launch ledger, not an annual software shopping list. The $10,000 configuration below reserves $5,500, or 55%, while the $45,000 configuration reserves $25,000, or 55.6%. Both exceed the 50% working-capital requirement before any filing begins. These reserves cover collection delays, temporary entity-fee deposits, and operating interruptions; they are not permission to subsidize every provider dispute. Require the provider agreement to allocate CMS administrative fees, certified-entity deposits, unrecovered charges, and casework reimbursements explicitly. Prefer provider-funded dispute costs with a separate reconciliation ledger rather than unlimited operator advances. At $1,800 monthly recurring expense, $5,500 buys approximately 3.1 months before deposits; at $10,500, $25,000 buys only 2.4 months. Since the first deal can take one to four months and an award can take longer to become cash, the upper spending envelope requires contracted volume or additional liquidity. Release each nonreserve allocation against a deliverable: executed agreements, a restore-tested workspace, a validated remittance parser, or a qualified provider pipeline. Do not exhaust the integration allocation on custom payer connectors before a representative 835 file has demonstrated recoverable claims. Record vendor cancellation dates beside committed cash so annual subscriptions cannot silently consume the next filing cycle.

Treat the $1,800–$10,500 monthly range as recurring operating infrastructure and retained support, not the entire annual expense base. A lean $1,800 planning month allocates $120 to licenses, $180 to infrastructure, $200 to insurance, $150 to bookkeeping and administration, $850 to contractor availability, and $300 to security and quality checks. A $10,500 operating month can allocate $1,000 to tools and infrastructure, $500 to insurance and administration, $3,000 to coordination, $2,000 to coding expertise, $2,000 to specialist counsel, and $2,000 to security and quality operations. These are spending envelopes, not vendor quotations or guaranteed staffing rates. Variable production labor and other portfolio expenses must still fit the canonical annual cost ceilings. Conservative revenue of $110,000 permits $77,000 of total expenses; Base revenue of $505,000 permits $287,850; Scaled revenue of $900,000 permits $396,000. Do not add recurring expense a second time after calculating those totals. Use a thirteen-week cash forecast with separate columns for recognized fees, bank receipts, refundable deposits, provider-funded costs, and payroll commitments. If collections slip two weeks, pause discretionary acquisition and software expansion first. Preserve eligibility screening and deadline coverage, because missed filings destroy assets rather than merely delaying administrative work.

Establish the contracting entity, obtain an EIN through the IRS, and maintain separate operating, tax, and reserve accounts using an FDIC-insured bank whose actual account ownership and insurance coverage have been checked. A fintech interface is not itself evidence of deposit insurance. Document bank signers, payment limits, vendor onboarding, and dual authorization for changes to provider remittance instructions. Keep provider recoveries flowing directly to the provider whenever possible; invoice the earned contingency after bank-level reconciliation instead of pooling patient or provider money in the operating account. Engage a bookkeeper who can distinguish a refundable deposit asset from an expense and a reimbursed disbursement from earned revenue. Bind professional liability and cyber coverage suited to healthcare claims services, checking exclusions for billing disputes, subcontractors, social engineering, and contractual obligations. Obtain specialist review of representative authority, state collection-agency requirements, and any legal work that requires licensed counsel. Execute the provider services agreement and HIPAA business associate agreement before receiving protected health information. Contractors receive written confidentiality, access, incident-reporting, and deletion obligations, with subcontractor BAAs where required. None of this requires premium offices or enterprise procurement staff; it requires signed documents, accountable owners, and evidence that money and sensitive records cannot move through informal channels.

## 2. Build twelve layers without twelve enterprise contracts  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use the twelve-layer matrix as a procurement architecture, not an instruction to activate every service immediately. Its exact dollar figures are explicit planning assumptions based on named tiers and standard list-price structures, not verified current quotations. Confirm current terms, region, annual commitments, eligible services, and BAA coverage before purchase; taxes, support, egress, implementation, and hosting can change the invoice. With two Microsoft 365 Business Premium seats at $22 each, two Bitwarden Teams seats at $4 each, two GitHub Team seats at $4 each, and two Tailscale Starter users at $6 each, the modeled fixed subtotal is $72 monthly. Add 100 GB of S3 Standard storage at $0.023 per GB-month and 10,000 Textract text-detection pages at $0.0015 each: the narrow subtotal becomes $89.30 before requests and other costs. This is a component calculation, not a complete HIPAA-ready operating budget. The $120 license and $180 infrastructure envelopes leave room for operational requirements that list prices omit. Start with structured ERA ingestion rather than OCR whenever possible. Text extraction cannot establish jurisdiction, interpret every adjustment code, or prove that an award was paid. Free software also consumes maintenance time, so retire overlapping tools when a covered existing service already performs the same task.

Separate the workspace into protected case records, internal operational controls, and public commercial material. Microsoft 365 and AWS receive protected records only after the relevant agreements, service eligibility, configuration, and minimum-necessary access have been established. A vendor's healthcare marketing page does not establish that every product feature falls within its BAA. GitHub contains source code and synthetic fixtures, never production remittances. Sentry receives opaque job identifiers and sanitized errors, never patient names, diagnosis descriptions, payer member identifiers, document excerpts, or signed download links. Cloudflare serves the public website only in this design; its free tier does not become a patient-document proxy. Password vault entries identify systems rather than patients. Use phishing-resistant multifactor authentication where supported, separate administrator identities, device encryption, conditional access, and role-specific case permissions. A coordinator should not change payment instructions, and a developer should not need production charts to repair parsing logic. Export audit records to a restricted location and define retention from the records' purpose, contractual duties, and applicable requirements. Maintain required HIPAA policy documentation for its applicable retention period without assuming every clinical artifact has the same rule. Test account termination with a contractor identity before actual offboarding becomes urgent.

Build the case ledger around immutable source references and reproducible calculations. Store the original 835, EOB, negotiation notice, proof of transmission, offers, determination, deposit receipts, and subsequent bank evidence under one internal case identifier. Preserve payer and plan identifiers separately: a familiar insurer logo does not establish whether the benefit arrangement is insured or self-funded. Python can normalize ERA segments and PostgreSQL can enforce unique claim keys, but neither replaces a documented exception queue. Send ambiguous CAS adjustments, missing dates, duplicate payments, and contradictory plan evidence to a named reviewer. Generate briefs from versioned templates and clinician-approved facts; do not let a language model invent acuity, network participation, or contracting history. Use LibreOffice locally for controlled document assembly if the endpoint is managed, avoiding an additional online PDF processor. Self-hosted DocuSeal belongs on patched infrastructure for business contracting; the free software license does not include hosting, security operations, or protected-record handling. Define recovery-point and recovery-time objectives in hours, then perform a restore into an isolated environment. A second encrypted backup under a separate administrative boundary protects against both deletion and account compromise. Monthly maintenance must include dependency updates, access review, restore evidence, and a sample reconciliation from raw remittance through final invoice.

## 3. Reconcile recovery economics and isolate the 340B adjacency  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Anchor every revenue forecast to incremental cash actually received above the payer's initial QPA or allowed remittance, not the provider's billed charges or the amount offered in arbitration. For this chapter's illustrative contract, define eligible recovery as the positive incremental cash balance after nonrefundable administrative fees, unrecovered certified-entity fees, and contractually allocated casework costs. Apply the contingency percentage to that balance, with a floor of zero. The canonical scorecard does not prescribe a contingency percentage; using 20% solely for capacity planning requires $550,000, $2,525,000, and $4,500,000 of annual eligible recovery to produce Conservative, Base, and Scaled operator revenue respectively. Actual incremental provider receipts must exceed those amounts by the deducted costs. Keep baseline collections outside operator revenue, and do not label a provider's gross recovery as an ASC 606 fee. Evaluate principal-versus-agent treatment for reimbursed disbursements separately; cash passing through a bank account does not establish a performance obligation or earned revenue. The canonical margins then reconcile exactly: $110,000 less $77,000 equals $33,000; $505,000 less $287,850 equals $217,150; $900,000 less $396,000 equals $504,000. Those expense totals include operating costs once. Provider-paid deductions used to define the fee base are not automatically a second operator expense.

Stress-test one collected case before extrapolating a portfolio. Suppose the initial allowed payment was $2,000 and total cash eventually reaches $5,000. Incremental cash is $3,000. For this example only, assume a $115 administrative charge, a $600 entity deposit, and $385 of provider-paid casework; these are budgeting inputs, not assertions of current federal or entity prices. If the provider prevails and the full entity deposit is returned, eligible recovery equals $3,000 minus $115 minus $385, or $2,500. A 20% contingency earns $500, leaving $2,000 of incremental benefit after those costs and the operator fee. Until the deposit refund is received, the ledger shows $600 of tied-up cash and excludes it from available liquidity. A loss with no additional payment yields no contingency and can consume $1,100 if the administrative charge, entity fee, and casework are unrecovered. Budget ineligibility dismissals for full fee loss unless the applicable disposition rules establish a refund; never assume dismissal makes the deposit recoverable. Maintain separate counts for initiated cases, eligible cases, determinations, awards, collections, and reconciled refunds. An 80% favorable-determination rate cannot substitute for the proportion of submitted cases that produce collected, cost-adjusted cash within the modeled period.

The mapped play section_340b_contract_pharmacy_spread_arbitrage belongs in this capital chapter because pharmacy inventory can silently consume the entire IDR reserve. Across the volume's 12 mapped plays, this adjacency requires separate underwriting rather than a blended revenue claim. Partner only with an eligible, registered covered entity, such as a qualifying FQHC, Ryan White grantee, or qualifying hospital; rural location alone establishes nothing. A hypothetical specialty prescription with $10,000 acquisition reference cost, $6,000 eligible 340B acquisition cost, and $9,000 actual reimbursement produces $3,000 before dispensing, administration, reversals, and other charges. The 40% modeled acquisition reduction is not a universal statutory discount, and reimbursement is never automatically 100% of a reference price. A support operator charging a documented $150 flat administrative fee earns $150, not the $3,000 spread or the $9,000 reimbursement. Separate TPA split-billing, specialty fulfillment, and tele-referral arrangements, testing patient eligibility, diversion prevention, Medicaid duplicate discounts, and federal remuneration restrictions. If manufacturer restrictions block contract-pharmacy access, assess a genuinely entity-owned pharmacy and required 340B ESP submissions where applicable; neither guarantees access to restricted NDCs. Apply strict EHR encounter checks before replenishment. When eligibility or access remains unresolved, stop replenishment and preserve IDR liquidity rather than financing speculative drug inventory.

## 4. Prove eligibility, clocks, evidence, and cash before launch  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Pre-flight begins with jurisdiction, because paying a filing fee does not make a dispute eligible. Identify the service, coverage type, plan funding, relevant state law, provider status, and whether federal surprise-billing protections govern the item. ERISA status alone is not a complete routing rule: distinguish self-funded plans from insured coverage, evaluate specified state-law applicability and permitted elections, and recognize excluded programs and services. Obtain plan evidence rather than inferring funding from the payer's logo or an ERA adjustment. Under the federal framework, the open-negotiation initiation window generally runs for 30 business days from receipt of the initial payment or denial; the negotiation period itself is a separate 30-business-day interval. The federal IDR initiation window generally follows for four business days. That four-day window is not a universal deadline for selecting a certified entity or assembling every batch. Encode each event with its own legal trigger, service evidence, calendar definition, and applicable extension. Maintain a holiday-aware business-day calculator and require an independent human deadline check. Track the 90-calendar-day suspension for the initiating party's same-party, same-or-similar disputes and the applicable subsequent filing window. Batch only under rules operative for the filing date, preserving the authority used for every eligibility decision.

Construct the evidence packet before escalating a claim from negotiation to paid submission. The packet needs the initial remittance, QPA information supplied by the plan, a defensible offer, clinical facts, negotiation history, jurisdiction support, and proof of timely action. FAIR Health data can inform an analyst, but the federal statute excludes certain considerations, including usual and customary charges, billed charges, and public-payer rates, from the certified entity's determination. Do not repackage excluded charge information as a permissible acuity argument. Link each offered fact to an allowed consideration and actual records, and have qualified staff resolve coding or clinical disputes. Use the applicable current guidance rather than assuming that a historical template survived litigation or rule changes unchanged. After a determination, establish the payment obligation and track the generally applicable 30-calendar-day payment period from the determination date. An unpaid award remains a collection task, not earned cash-based contingency revenue. Reconcile payer remittances, bank deposits, offsets, recoupments, and deposit refunds independently. Keep escalation correspondence and supporting records ready for the appropriate enforcement channel when payment is overdue. Recalculate the fee if a reversal changes incremental collected cash. The operator invoice should let the provider reproduce the baseline, uplift, deductions, percentage, and final charge without opening a sales presentation.

Run a supervised rehearsal using synthetic records, then a small authorized pilot with real provider files. Include an insured state-law claim, an ambiguous self-funded plan, a late negotiation trigger, an invalid proposed batch, a duplicate ERA, a losing determination, an unpaid favorable determination, and a refunded deposit. The software must route uncertainty to review rather than manufacturing eligibility. Verify that reminders reach a primary owner and backup, that access revocation blocks an old session, and that restored records retain their links to the case ledger. Reconcile the pilot against the provider's bank evidence before issuing its first contingency invoice. Measure rejection rate, minutes per eligible case, administrative cost per initiation, entity-fee losses, days from determination to receipt, and actual fee realization by payer. Use those observations to decide whether a second contractor should handle intake or clinical evidence; adding staff to the wrong bottleneck merely increases fixed burn. Keep the initial provider cohort narrow enough that one person can inspect every exception while another covers deadlines. Authorize growth only when the thirteen-week forecast remains positive after a delayed-payment stress case and all four verification checks below pass. If a check fails, repair the specific defect and continue safe preparatory work without sending unsupported disputes or exposing additional records.

1. Fund and partition the launch
   - Approve the complete capital budget and segregate the reserve allocation.
   - Record all committed subscriptions, establish deposit responsibility by provider, and test the cash forecast against delayed collections before releasing discretionary spending.

   - Execute required agreements and configure identity, records, and audit controls.

   - Match actual provider receipts to the baseline and cost-adjusted contingency.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight verification checks before the first paid filing**
> 
> Each check requires retained evidence and an accountable owner. A verbal assurance does not repair a missing agreement, deadline, or cash reconciliation.
> - Authority and privacy: executed provider representation terms, HIPAA BAA, subcontractor obligations, and tested least-privilege access cover every recipient of protected records.
> - Eligibility and timing: documented plan funding, service eligibility, state-law analysis, negotiation evidence, batching rationale, and independently verified filing dates support the dispute.
> - Liquidity and fee exposure: at least 50% of launch capital remains allocated to reserve, with funded deposit responsibilities and a positive stressed thirteen-week cash forecast.
> - Economics and evidence: a sample invoice reproduces incremental collected cash after allocated costs, while excluded evidence and unpaid determinations remain outside the recovery fee calculation.
