## 1. Establish jurisdiction, licensing boundaries, and protected-data authority  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Play 1, jurisdiction routing, starts with the service and plan, not the payer logo. Obtain the service date, place of service, provider network status, plan funding evidence, initial payment or denial, and applicable state payment rules. The federal framework in 42 U.S.C. § 300gg-111 addresses specified emergency services and certain nonemergency services furnished by nonparticipating providers at participating facilities; air ambulance disputes have a separate statutory provision. Ground ambulance bills do not become federal IDR cases merely because they are out of network. A specified state law or applicable All-Payer Model Agreement can determine the payment route instead. ERISA preemption under 29 U.S.C. § 1144 is important, but an ERISA label alone does not settle every claim's jurisdiction. Funding status, state opt-in arrangements, service category, and the law's actual reach matter. Store the evidence supporting each routing decision rather than inferring self-funding from an 835 transaction. New York and Texas illustrate why one national payer can administer claims governed by different dispute systems. If evidence conflicts, obtain plan documentation and a written routing determination before incurring an entity fee. Separately evaluate balance-billing protection; federal arbitration eligibility and patient protections are related but not interchangeable determinations.

Play 2, licensing and representation, separates administrative recovery work from practicing law, collecting consumer debts, and selling clinical referrals. A provider authorization should identify the business acting as its representative, permitted portal actions, negotiation authority, and who can accept a settlement. That authorization does not create a law license or override a state's collection-agency requirements. Florida's commercial collection regime, including Fla. Stat. § 559.553, deserves a specific applicability review when the proposed activity involves collecting commercial claims. New York Judiciary Law §§ 478 and 484 illustrate separate restrictions on unauthorized legal practice. Have qualified local counsel classify activities by provider location, business location, debtor relationship, and forum; do not assume that calling the service analytics changes its substance. Escalate litigation, subpoenas, disputed legal interpretation, and contested ERISA benefit litigation to retained counsel. Analyze percentage fees under applicable professional fee-splitting rules rather than declaring every contingency arrangement permissible. Where federal healthcare program business is implicated, assess 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b(b) before compensating referral sources. Keep marketing compensation independent of patient referrals and treatment volume. The practical fallback is a narrower administrative scope, a properly licensed service partner, or declining the affected work while preserving the provider's remaining filing time.

Play 3, privacy authority, requires an executed HIPAA business associate agreement before the recovery operator receives protected health information on the provider's behalf. Pair 45 CFR § 164.504(e) with Security Rule safeguards under 45 CFR §§ 164.308 and 164.312, and apply minimum-necessary access under § 164.502(b) where applicable. The services agreement alone is insufficient. Specify permitted payment and operations uses, incident reporting, subcontractor restrictions, return or destruction, and treatment of records that must be retained. Use individual accounts, multifactor authentication, encrypted transfer, and access logs; keep prospecting tools outside the clinical-record boundary. Microsoft 365 or a cloud storage brand is not proof that a particular product, subscription, and configuration are covered by the required contractual commitments. Verify those commitments for each service receiving records. An 835 ERA can contain sensitive identifiers even when the attachment lacks a clinical narrative. Collect only claim fields necessary for screening, then obtain selected clinical evidence through the controlled workspace. Evaluate additional restrictions before accepting substance-use-disorder records governed by 42 CFR Part 2. If a prospect emails records prematurely, restrict access, notify the designated privacy contact, and follow the documented incident process instead of forwarding the attachment into a sales sequence.

## 2. Contract for collected uplift, controlled authority, and clean ownership  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Play 4, contractual scope, defines the service as screening, authorized negotiation, eligible IDR administration, evidence assembly, and cash reconciliation for listed provider entities and claim populations. Attach the tax identifiers, specialties, service periods, excluded payers, and excluded litigation activities. State who funds administrative fees, entity fees, clinical review, and external counsel; require express budget authority before expanding a matter. Give the operator limited representation authority rather than unrestricted power to bind the practice. Settlement instructions should identify minimum acceptable terms and the person authorized to change them. Play 5, the fee trigger, defines baseline collections as the payer's actual initial allowed payment allocated to the claim, not the QPA disclosure when those amounts differ. Incremental recovery excludes baseline receipts, patient cost sharing, unrelated claim payments, and uncollected awards. Deduct the agreed nonrefundable administrative charges, unrecovered entity fees, and attributable casework costs before applying the contingency percentage. Attach a worked example and a claim-level reconciliation format. Define treatment of interest, partial receipts, recoupments, refunds, and bundled remittances explicitly. A favorable determination creates a collection task; it does not by itself establish an earned cash-triggered contingency invoice or authority to debit the provider's bank account.

Use a precise waterfall: eligible incremental cash equals reconciled payer cash attributable to the recovery minus baseline payments already included in that total; subtract unrecovered filing charges and agreed casework costs; floor the resulting contingency base at zero. For example, a $4,000 initial payment followed by $10,000 additional cash creates $10,000 of incremental receipts, not $14,000 of fee basis. If actual unrecovered charges are $750 and attributable casework costs are $1,250, the net recovery base is $8,000. A 25% contingency produces $2,000 of operator revenue and $6,000 of provider residual benefit after those costs. The $750 is an illustrative combined ledger amount, not a quoted CMS fee schedule. If the operator also incurs $400 of nonreimbursed overhead allocated to that engagement, its contribution is $1,600. Track reimbursed advances through the appropriate clearing accounts and evaluate principal versus agent presentation under ASC 606; never count the provider's recovery as operator revenue. Where the operator bears a case expense rather than obtaining reimbursement, record that expense consistently and do not deduct it twice in the profit bridge. A later entity-fee refund changes unrecovered costs and requires a transparent true-up.

Play 6, asset ownership, assigns provider records, claim decisions, and matter-specific deliverables to the agreed owner while reserving reusable software and general templates to the operator. Ownership language does not itself authorize secondary use of protected information. Restrict training, resale, and cross-client benchmarking unless separately permitted under applicable contracts and privacy rules. Play 7, liability and dispute design, makes anti-circumvention a narrow collection-reporting obligation covering identified worked claims for a defined tail period. It should not capture unrelated future collections or obstruct provider access to records. Require receipt reporting, reasonable audit access, and cooperation after termination. A liability cap should specify its monetary basis and deliberate exceptions for fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality breaches, and other negotiated exposures; evaluate enforceability under the selected state's law. Align indemnities with responsibility for inaccurate source records, unauthorized submissions, and security incidents. Define notice, cure, venue, governing law, emergency relief, and mediation or arbitration for disputes between operator and provider. Keep that private contract mechanism distinct from statutory payer-provider IDR. On termination, deliver the deadline register, submission receipts, pending determinations, payment ledger, and authorized record export. Invoice disputes should never become an excuse to hide a live filing deadline or withhold essential transition information.

## 3. Operate versioned calendars, defensible submissions, and cash controls  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Play 8, deadline control, implements distinct clocks instead of one generic thirty-day task. Under the baseline federal process, a party generally initiates open negotiation within 30 business days after receiving the initial payment or denial. The open-negotiation period then lasts 30 business days; federal IDR generally must be initiated within four business days after that period ends. The four-day window is not a universal entity-selection or batching deadline. Record receipt evidence, negotiation delivery, calendar definitions, holidays, portal acknowledgments, and any authorized extension. Implement the clock in a versioned state machine with a second-person check rather than trusting a spreadsheet formula copied between jurisdictions. The 90-calendar-day cooling-off restriction concerns the initiating party bringing subsequent disputes involving the same other party and same or similar items or services after a determination; it is not a blanket ninety-day suspension of all payer work. The statute also provides a subsequent initiation opportunity for affected claims. Maintain a linked cooling-off register and calculate the applicable post-period window. Check current regulations, controlling court orders, and CMS implementation instructions before relying on operational batching criteria or portal procedures. Store the version used, because a historically correct workflow can become wrong after an implementation change.

Play 9, evidence construction, distinguishes permissible claim-specific support from prohibited arbitration inputs. The statute directs consideration of the QPA and specified additional information, while excluding usual and customary charges, billed charges, and public-program reimbursement rates as payment determinants. Do not instruct a brief builder to multiply a FAIR Health charge percentile and present the result as a permissible federal IDR valuation. Identify the licensed dataset, its actual measure, the evidentiary proposition, and whether that use is permissible before including any benchmark. Clinical acuity, patient complexity, training, facility characteristics, and documented contracting efforts need claim-specific support and careful treatment of information already reflected in the QPA. CARC and RARC values help classify remittances but cannot prove jurisdiction, clinical complexity, or an incorrect QPA independently. Use templates to assemble exhibits, then require a qualified reviewer to verify every assertion and source. For batching, evaluate the operative statutory and regulatory conditions, including relevant party, service, coding, and service-period requirements, rather than assuming one payer name makes all claims compatible. When compatibility is uncertain, split supported cases or obtain clarification before filing. A cheaper aggregate submission is expensive if dismissal destroys its expected recovery and consumes unrecoverable fees.

Play 10, cash reconciliation, tracks submitted offer, determination, payment due date, actual receipt, fee refund, and recoupment as different ledger events. The federal statute generally requires payment within 30 calendar days after the determination; an overdue award belongs in a documented collection escalation, not in cash revenue. The administrative fee is nonrefundable. In an ordinary completed determination, the prevailing party's advanced entity fee is generally returned while the nonprevailing party bears that fee. Dismissals, withdrawals, settlements, and eligibility failures require the applicable fee terms and current instructions; do not assume a refund merely because the merits were never reached. Budget ineligible dismissals as potential entity-fee forfeitures. The canonical annual bridge remains $110,000 revenue less $77,000 total operator expense equals $33,000 owner profit; $505,000 less $287,850 equals $217,150; and $900,000 less $396,000 equals $504,000. These are 30%, 43.0%, and 56% margins with teams of 1 FT plus 2 C, 1 FT plus 2 C, and 2 FT plus 3 C. At an illustrative 25% fee, those revenues require $440,000, $2,020,000, and $3,600,000 of net incremental collected fee bases. Neither favorable offer statistics nor pending awards establish those cash volumes; report both collection delays and unrecovered case costs explicitly.

## 4. Use lawful outbound infrastructure and exact provider discovery language  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Play 11 is multi_inbox_free_workspace_outbound_clusters, the mapped TRACK_2 outbound infrastructure play. Its proposed capacity math is straightforward: 300 authorized inboxes sending 15 to 25 messages across 20 working days produce 90,000 to 150,000 attempted messages monthly. At retail prices of $6 to $12 per seat, 300 seats cost $1,800 to $3,600 monthly before domains and relay charges. Subsidized Microsoft 365 developer or partner accounts may be used only when actual eligibility and product terms expressly permit the intended production sending; free access is not assumed. Cloudflare Email Routing handles forwarding, not a substitute outbound SMTP service. Use a relay that explicitly permits the campaign type and configure sender authentication. Keeping two or three domains per legitimately provisioned tenant is a management limit, not permission to evade provider restrictions. Remove open and click pixels, centralize suppression, and verify address quality through an authorized service. SMTP catch-all responses cannot certify every mailbox or guarantee delivery. Maintain standby domains only for approved continuity with stable identity and suppression, never to evade blocks. If subsidies or acceptable-use terms fail, reduce volume and purchase permitted service. No inbox-count formula establishes primary-folder placement, positive replies, or collected recovery revenue.

CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. § 7704, applies to commercial business-to-business email as well as consumer campaigns. Use accurate headers, a nondeceptive subject, the required advertising identification, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out; honor requests within ten business days and suppress them across every sending identity. Retain the suppression record rather than reimporting an opted-out address from another vendor. Outsourcing the send does not eliminate the advertiser's responsibilities. Address verification is a deliverability control, not recipient permission. Do not use harvested lists obtained through prohibited methods or buy lists whose provenance cannot be explained. For phone discovery, distinguish manual calls from automated dialing, prerecorded messages, and texts; evaluate 47 U.S.C. § 227, implementing FCC rules, applicable state restrictions, and the recipient's number type before selecting a channel. A practice manager's mobile number is not automatically an unrestricted business line. Avoid patient-facing solicitations and never put patient identifiers in outreach. Campaign economics must include verification, domains, permitted mail service, suppression operations, and staff review, not merely seat savings. If 150,000 attempted messages yield an illustrative 0.2% positive response rate, that is 300 responses, not 300 qualified practices or engagements. Scale only after measuring legitimate opportunities and staff capacity.

Play 12, discovery and handoff, converts a response into a bounded eligibility conversation, not an award prediction. Use this opening verbatim: 'We help provider groups evaluate eligible out-of-network payment disputes and administer authorized recovery work. We charge an agreed share of incremental cash actually collected after specified unrecovered fees and case costs. Could we discuss your specialty, service states, payer products, and current dispute workflow without sharing patient information?' Then ask: 'Who owns your initial-payment records, open-negotiation calendar, and settlement authority, and how do you distinguish self-funded plans from insured products?' Explain the handoff directly: 'Please do not email patient records. If the scope fits, we will execute the services agreement and business associate agreement, then provide the controlled intake method.' For outcome questions, say: 'We can model eligibility, costs, and collection timing after reviewing the records; a favorable determination and actual payment are separate events.' End with a specific next action: 'May we schedule twenty minutes with your revenue-cycle owner and review aggregate claim counts first?' Record objections and exclusions in the CRM, never diagnoses or member identifiers. If the prospect declines, acknowledge the request and suppress further promotional contact across the cluster.
