## 1. Build an evidence-first intake spine before adding language models  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a tenant-isolated intake spine, not a chatbot attached to a clearinghouse export. Receive authorized 835 ERA files, associated 837 claim records, payer correspondence, and plan documents through an authenticated transfer channel. Execute HIPAA Business Associate Agreements with covered-entity clients and applicable downstream service providers before transmitting protected health information. Python parses transaction envelopes, CLP claim records, SVC service lines, CAS adjustments, and remark-code references into a versioned claim ledger. Match CARC and RARC combinations against dated code dictionaries; never interpret every contractual adjustment as recoverable underpayment. Preserve original bytes, source hashes, parser version, receipt timestamp, and the relationship between claim and service line. A PostgreSQL record stores operational state; encrypted object storage holds evidence with access logging and tested retention controls. Signed webhooks enqueue identifiers rather than clinical narratives. Duplicate files produce the same ingestion key, while corrected remittances create linked versions instead of silently overwriting evidence. The employee owns exceptions and client authority; one contractor reconciles claims; the other prepares evidence. This division lets machines normalize thousands of lines without giving a model discretion to classify disputed jurisdiction or authorize an external filing.

Treat the ERISA classifier as an evidence router with an abstention state. An employer name, insurer logo, or 835 payer identifier cannot establish whether a plan is self-funded, fully insured, subject to a specified state law, or affected by an applicable All-Payer Model Agreement. Collect the summary plan description, funding evidence, service location, service date, provider status, and the relevant emergency or nonemergency service facts. Record state opt-in evidence when that mechanism matters. ERISA section 514, codified at 29 U.S.C. § 1144, supplies a framework rather than a shortcut that sends every employer claim to federal IDR. A rules engine proposes a route and names missing documents; a reviewer signs the route before fees are exposed. Maintain separate queues for federal candidates, state-process candidates, ordinary payment disputes, and unresolved evidence. For language-model extraction, require a structured object containing source page, quoted support, proposed field, and uncertainty reason. Reject unsupported values instead of filling gaps with plausible answers. Claims containing instructions inside attachments remain evidence, never executable workflow commands. A low-confidence result returns to document collection, not directly to the arbitration portal.

The chapter's 12-play map uses eleven local implementation labels plus the supplied distribution play identifier; the local labels are operating modules, not additional canonical catalog claims. Plays one through four are era_evidence_normalization, jurisdiction_evidence_router, deadline_event_ledger, and idempotent_work_dispatch. Each module owns a measurable input, output, exception queue, and human override record. Measure the automation objective using a frozen catalog of repetitive task instances. In an illustrative thousand-task week, 740 tasks complete without manual rework and 260 require intervention: 740 divided by 1,000 equals 74%. Do not inflate the numerator with retries or split one extraction into dozens of trivial events. If those tasks previously required four minutes each, automated completion removes 2,960 gross minutes, before quality sampling, maintenance, and exception handling. Audit 10% of automated completions initially, with heavier sampling for new payers and changed formats. Any material jurisdiction error pauses that rule version and replays affected records into review. Track minutes as a second measure because automating many cheap classifications can conceal expensive brief corrections. The target becomes credible only when rework-adjusted labor and missed-deadline rates improve together.

## 2. Turn statutory clocks into tested state transitions and controlled dispatch  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the deadline engine around distinct events rather than a single thirty-day timer. Under the applicable federal framework, the initiating party generally must start open negotiation within thirty business days after receiving the initial payment or notice of denial. The open-negotiation period itself runs thirty business days beginning on its initiation date. A federal IDR initiation generally follows within four business days after that period ends. These are different clocks, and the four-day window is not a universal deadline for choosing the certified entity or assembling every batch. Encode each requirement from the effective version of 45 CFR § 149.510 and applicable official implementation instructions. Preserve the source receipt date, counting convention, holiday calendar version, local time context, computed deadline, and reviewer confirmation. Use states such as received, evidence_pending, route_confirmed, negotiation_sent, negotiation_elapsed, initiation_ready, initiated, offer_pending, determination_received, and cash_reconciled. A clock continues while evidence is missing; moving a claim into a document queue does not suspend a statutory deadline. Escalate approaching deadlines to two people and show the exact missing action, not a generic red badge.

Plays five through eight are batch_eligibility_matcher, permitted_evidence_builder, offer_packet_validator, and award_cash_reconciler. Batch matching must evaluate the effective legal criteria, not simply combine claims sharing a payer and CPT family. Store the initiating and opposing parties, qualified items or services, service dates, and every criterion supporting aggregation. The same-party, same-or-similar-item cooling-off restriction generally spans ninety calendar days after a determination; affected claims require separate tracking, including the applicable post-suspension initiation opportunity. Do not stop negotiation preparation merely because initiation is temporarily restricted. Version rules against current agency implementation and operative court decisions before using them on live claims. A scheduled Python worker queries due actions, acquires a transactional lease, writes an outbox event, and dispatches only approved actions. Webhook acknowledgments update delivery state, while official portal confirmations establish submission status. A timeout means status unresolved, not permission to file again. If portal access fails, preserve screenshots, timestamps, and support correspondence; follow the official extension process when available. An outage does not itself extend a deadline, and a model must never invent that relief.

Budget the machine layer by completed run and separate it from reviewer labor. An illustrative intake run costs $0.003 in parsing compute, $0.004 in queue and database usage, $0.006 in OCR, and $0.015 in bounded extraction: $0.028 total under the assumed allocation. Ten thousand runs therefore cost $280 before storage, fixed subscriptions, and human review. If 5% require one full retry, 500 additional runs add $14, producing $294 or $0.0294 per original record. Vendor prices, document lengths, and OCR incidence must replace these planning assumptions during measurement. A three-minute review at $45 per hour costs $2.25, so preventing unnecessary review matters far more than shaving a fraction of a cent from tokens. Apply bounded retries only to transient technical failures. Invalid jurisdiction, missing authorization, contradictory receipts, and unsupported clinical assertions require people. A dead-letter queue carries the source hash, failed operation, next deadline, and named owner. Run nightly reconciliation between queue records and portal receipts. Test holidays, duplicate callbacks, out-of-order messages, daylight-saving transitions, corrected remittances, and worker crashes before expanding the automated population. Keep a manual deadline register ready for infrastructure outages.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the event evidence
   - Hash source records and record the actual receipt and initiation events.
   - Retain document versions and delivery artifacts so a reviewer can reconstruct every deadline calculation.
2. Calculate and independently inspect
   - Run the versioned calendar engine and compare its output with the review register.
   - Resolve jurisdiction, batching, and cooling-off questions before making any fee-bearing submission.
3. Authorize and dispatch once
   - Record reviewer authorization and dispatch through a unique transactional outbox action.
   - Treat missing acknowledgments as unresolved status and inspect the destination before attempting another submission.
4. Reconcile and recover
   - Match the official receipt to the case ledger and assign the next obligation.
   - Send failures to a named owner with the deadline, evidence bundle, and documented manual fallback.

## 3. Generate permitted evidence, then reconcile awards to actual incremental cash  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The brief builder should resemble a cited evidence compiler, not an advocate allowed to invent facts. Play six retrieves the QPA supplied for the disputed service, documented clinical acuity, provider training and experience where relevant, facility characteristics where applicable, and supported contracting history. A language model drafts only from approved excerpts and returns a source map for every factual assertion. Licensed FAIR Health material requires especially careful separation: its usual-and-customary charge benchmarks cannot become an arbitration anchor merely because the data are commercially reputable. Federal IDR excludes specified considerations, including usual and customary charges, billed charges, and public-payer reimbursement rates. Use any licensed product only for a permitted purpose consistent with its terms and the governing evidentiary rules; otherwise exclude it from the offer packet. Distinguish operational opportunity screening from evidence actually submitted. Do not assume the QPA carries a conclusive presumption or automatically determines the result. Current operative rules and court decisions govern consideration of permissible information. Human reviewers test whether each acuity assertion explains this service and avoids double counting factors already reflected in the QPA.

Play seven validates the final-offer packet because baseball arbitration selects between party offers rather than inviting the model to manufacture a compromise award. Keep the requested amount, initial payment, service identifiers, offer rationale, and supporting exhibits mutually consistent. A clinical reviewer confirms acuity and service facts; the authorized case lead approves the offer and submission. Generate a PDF with stable exhibit numbers and verify that every cited page exists. Use a bounded model for extraction and a stronger model only for complex drafting, both inside appropriately contracted environments with documented retention and training controls. An illustrative packet costs $0.18 for extraction, $0.42 for drafting, and $0.10 for rendering and validation: $0.70 in variable machine spend. Twelve minutes of review at $60 per hour adds $12, making $12.70 before allocated subscriptions and filing costs. If two of twenty packets require ten extra review minutes each, the cohort adds $20 and averages $13.70 per packet. Reject unsupported citations, contradictory amounts, and missing exhibits automatically. After repeated drafting failure, switch to the approved template and human completion rather than sending a weaker packet to preserve throughput.

Play eight reconciles determinations to bank-confirmed incremental collections, because a favorable offer statistic is not a cash conversion rate. Suppose matched claims originally paid $100,000 and eventually show $160,000 in cumulative payer collections. Incremental cash is $60,000, not $160,000 and not an unpaid award balance. Deduct $10,000 of non-refundable CMS administrative fees, unrecovered certified-entity fees, and direct casework costs to obtain a $50,000 eligible fee pool. At a contracted 25% contingency, operator revenue is $12,500. Track prevailing-party entity-fee refunds as receivables until returned; losing or ineligibility-dismissed disputes retain their forfeited fees in cost reconciliation under the applicable rules. CMS administrative fees remain separate and non-refundable. Do not deduct the same casework expense again in company profit if it was borne and recorded as a provider-level fee-base deduction; operator-borne expenses still belong in the operator ledger. Age awarded-but-unpaid balances and pursue documented payment follow-up, including the applicable thirty-calendar-day payment requirement. Never invoice an earned cash contingency merely because a determination arrived. Reverse or adjust invoices for linked recoupments according to the signed engagement terms.
