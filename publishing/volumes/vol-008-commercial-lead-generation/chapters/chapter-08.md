## 1. Build a twenty-source directory around buying events, not contact volume  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an account spine containing legal business name, jurisdiction, registry identifier, operating domain, service territory, and source timestamps. A secretary of state filing establishes an entity record; it does not establish that the business occupies a property, needs roofing, or accepts sales calls. Join municipal permit events to commercial parcels and operating businesses using address normalization, then retain the original address and match confidence. A registered agent is usually a poor prospect contact. Separate applicant, owner, contractor, and tenant roles before enrichment. A useful signal might be a recently issued commercial alteration permit, a documented facility expansion, or an expiring public service contract—not simply an incorporation date. Store source URL, retrieved time, event time, permitted use, and a content hash with every event. These fields let an operator explain why an account entered a campaign and reconstruct a disputed qualification decision. Prefer official exports and documented APIs over repeated page retrieval. Where a portal offers only interactive search, keep collection manual or obtain an authorized export. The directory below describes access paths and operating cadences; actual publication lag and contractual quotas must be measured for each selected jurisdiction and plan.

For gsa_municipal_set_aside_facility_ghost_brokering, use SAM.gov opportunities to identify requirements, USAspending.gov to understand prior awards, and SBA Small Business Search to research potential partners. Award history is context, not proof that a recompete will occur on the same schedule. Read amendments, the statement of work, NAICS classification, place of performance, and solicitation-specific eligibility before introducing a facility-service prime. Match that prime with disclosed subcontractors whose licenses, insurance, capacity, and territorial coverage fit the actual work. A $500,000 government service contract remains the prime's contract value; a separately documented $2,000 monthly coordination retainer produces only $24,000 of annual desk revenue. Do not assume a success-based introduction fee is permitted: FAR Subpart 3.4 and applicable contingent-fee clauses require specific review. Service-contract limitations under 13 CFR 125.6 generally restrict payments to nonsimilarly situated subcontractors, with defined exceptions and calculation rules. Maintain a workshare worksheet rather than disguising an unsupported pass-through. If the proposed structure fails eligibility or performance requirements, withdraw it and pursue disclosed subcontracting on unrestricted work. Never represent a directory listing as certification of eligibility for the particular solicitation.

Treat access cost as a purchasing decision with a testable acceptance condition. Free government retrieval can still consume expensive normalization labor, while a paid API can save money if it materially improves accepted opportunities. For example, a $300 monthly enrichment subscription spread across thirty incremental accepted leads costs $10 per incremental acceptance before research labor. Spread across three acceptances, it costs $100 and may consume the entire lead fee. Record subscription cost, credit consumption, manual review minutes, match precision, and source coverage in the same vendor scorecard. Do not buy overlapping databases because their advertised contact counts look different. Run a shared, representative account sample and measure unique useful evidence after deduplication. Cache responses according to license terms, honor retry guidance, and stop collection when access conditions change. Refresh latency has two components: the publisher's update delay and your polling interval. Reporting only the latter creates false precision. Keep a fallback source for each critical field, but preserve disagreements instead of silently overwriting them. An address conflict should become a review task, not an automatic campaign expansion. This discipline protects both delivery quality and the desk's limited working capital.

Diagnose delivery from authentication and recipient feedback before changing infrastructure. Publish SPF for actual sending services, enable DKIM, and configure DMARC with alignment verified on delivered test messages. Review aggregate reports for unauthorized senders and legitimate services omitted from the inventory. Investigate those signals alongside replies and acceptances. Pause affected sources when evidence shows stale contacts or recipient objections. Do not substitute manufactured engagement or domain replacement for fixing the cause.

Make permission and suppression first-class data objects rather than scattered CRM notes.
