## 1. Days 1-7: Bound the portfolio thesis, staff the pod, and authorize cash  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Launch one domain and one commercially coherent topic cluster before building a portfolio. Candidate #25 has 30 mapped operator plays; the schedule below gives that scope 30 daily execution slots, P01 through P30. Attach the authoritative play identifiers when that catalog is available, preserving all 30 slots and recording overlaps. The launch pod contains two full-time role holders: the publisher owns positioning, commercial relationships, and cash; the managing editor owns briefs, evidence, and release quality. Four contractors cover technical SEO, specialist research, writing, and design plus analytics implementation. Assign one accountable owner to every deliverable, even when another person reviews it. Reserve the publisher's mornings for decisions and the editor's afternoons for acceptance; do not create six parallel editorial authorities. During this sprint, cap contractor allocations at 24 technical hours, 32 research hours, 48 writing hours, and 16 design or measurement hours. These purchased hours are capacity limits, not declarations that contractors work full time. Use a shared issue board with evidence links, acceptance criteria, due dates, and blocked-work reasons.

Authorize a $12,000 first-month operating budget: $4,000 for publisher and editor cash compensation, $6,000 for contractor hours at an illustrative blended $50 rate, $800 for hosting and software, $700 for licensed research or original testing, and $500 for repairs. Preserve another $3,000 as an untouched liquidity reserve. The resulting $15,000 initial funding requirement meets the bottom of the stated startup range, but it funds only this limited experiment. It does not finance the stated six-to-twenty-four-month path to a first deal. Continuing three comparable months requires $36,000 of operating cash plus the $3,000 reserve; continuing twelve requires $144,000 before revenue, beyond the startup range's upper bound. Secure later funding or reduce scope before making commitments. Record unpaid founder time separately at replacement cost so the small cash draw cannot masquerade as durable profitability. Maintain incurred, committed, and paid columns; the ceiling applies to incurred costs plus remaining commitments, not merely bank withdrawals. Warn at $9,600 and freeze discretionary purchases at $12,000. Releasing reserve cash requires a written revised budget and funded runway, not optimistic search forecasts.

Choose the initial cluster through observable buyer tasks: installation comparisons, maintenance decisions, compatibility questions, or professional purchasing research. Reject topics whose answers require expertise the pod cannot substantiate. Inspect thirty representative queries manually, recording dominant intent, incumbent publishers, freshness needs, and whether an original test, dataset, or diagram would improve the answer. Ahrefs or Semrush can help prioritize queries, but estimated search volume is not measured site demand. Select twenty-four pages with distinct purposes and three plausible monetization partners; none needs to accept the site during this month. Prepare a rights ledger covering text, photographs, datasets, screenshots, and contractor deliverables. Under 17 U.S.C. 106, ownership and permission matter before reproduction and adaptation; a citation alone does not authorize copying. ## 2. Days 8-14: Publish a measurable twenty-four-page evidence cohort  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the first twenty-four pages from original research packets, not from a keyword spreadsheet passed directly into a text generator. Each brief specifies the reader's decision, source hierarchy, supporting measurements, required visuals, and a reason this page deserves to exist separately. Use three reusable layouts: an explanatory guide, a comparison with explicit evaluation criteria, and a reference page built from licensed or independently gathered data. Programmatic production is appropriate only when each record contains meaningful differentiated facts and receives verification. Do not mint location or product permutations merely because a template can render them. The research contractor supplies evidence; the writer drafts; the editor checks claims and reader usefulness; the technical contractor verifies rendering. The twenty-four-page target assumes narrow, research-efficient pages rather than twenty-four laboratory investigations. If the evidence cannot support that output inside the allocated hours, publish fewer pages and classify the evaluation as sample-limited. Do not buy low-quality volume to satisfy a denominator. Freeze a release manifest with publication dates, page types, source versions, reviewer names, and content hashes so subsequent repairs are attributable rather than remembered informally after traffic moves.

Release twelve accepted pages on Day 10, six on Day 12, and six on Day 14. Keep unfinished material out of production sitemaps. Every released URL must return a successful response, render its main content without requiring user interaction, declare the intended canonical, and remain reachable through relevant internal links. Use Screaming Frog or a small scheduled crawler to test status codes, canonical targets, titles, robots directives, and orphaned pages. Submit a clean XML sitemap in Google Search Console; submission requests discovery and does not establish indexing. Do not use Google's Indexing API as a general publishing shortcut for ordinary articles. Use the URL Inspection API or documented manual inspection within available quotas to collect index evidence. Keep the inspection timestamp and distinguish reported index status from a live accessibility test. Before removing a staging restriction, confirm that production and staging have separate settings; an inherited exclusion can erase the experiment's meaning. Record uptime and response errors daily. The target is zero operator-caused crawl blocks, zero broken canonical chains, and zero pages whose essential information disappears when a crawler renders the published template.

Instrument the commercial path without confusing an outbound action with earned income. Define a qualified affiliate click as a human-initiated outbound event from an eligible editorial placement after internal tests and identifiable automation are excluded. Reconcile aggregate event counts against partner reporting, noting that attribution windows, devices, reversals, and reporting delays prevent exact agreement. Use campaign identifiers that avoid personal information. Advertising revenue belongs in the ledger when earned under the network's reporting terms; affiliate revenue reflects commissions after the applicable recognition policy and expected reversals; sponsorship revenue follows delivered contract obligations. A referred $500 merchant order is not $500 of publisher revenue. For illustration, a 4% approved commission produces $20 before publisher expenses. Create readable disclosure language beside affected recommendations rather than relying solely on a remote policy page. FTC endorsement requirements also make fabricated product experience unacceptable. For generative search visibility, add clear source attribution, stable entity descriptions, dated measurements, and concise answer passages. Track ten fixed prompts with their model and observation dates, but treat citation capture as a diagnostic: unstable generated mentions are neither attributable visits nor booked revenue.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Accept the evidence packet
   - Match each material claim to an original observation or appropriate source.
   - Record permitted use, source date, limitations, and the editor responsible for verification before drafting.
2. Review the rendered page
   - Check reader usefulness, factual support, disclosures, and accessible presentation.
   - Verify the actual production rendering, including tables, images, mobile layout, and adjacent commercial explanations.
3. Release and inspect
   - Publish only accepted pages and validate responses, canonicals, links, and sitemap inclusion.
   - Archive the release timestamp and initial crawl result; roll back the affected release if a systemic defect appears.
4. Observe and reconcile
   - Join cohort-level search observations with events, partner statements, and costs.
   - Keep merchant sales, estimated commissions, recognized revenue, receivables, and received cash in distinct fields.

## 3. Days 15-21: Diagnose discoverability, build refresh queues, and protect attribution  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start the observation week by freezing cohort membership rather than constantly adding pages to improve reported totals. An eligible URL is an intended indexable production page, published at least fourteen days before the checkpoint, with stable ownership and a recorded release date. Count accidental exclusions as failures within that denominator; removing broken pages from the denominator would conceal operational defects. For Day 30, require at least twenty eligible pages to classify indexing results. Calculate indexed share as inspected indexed URLs divided by eligible URLs, with inspection evidence no older than seven days. A published page that has impressions can corroborate discovery, but search performance and current inspection status measure different things. Export Search Console page-level totals after allowing for reporting delay, and store the extraction date and reporting timezone. Keep query exports separate because privacy filtering can make their sums smaller than page totals. Branded and nonbranded query segments are directional views, not an exact reconciliation target. The publisher signs the denominator manifest before seeing the checkpoint result. This prevents a weak week from quietly changing which pages count.

Use a causal repair order: accessibility first, canonical selection second, internal discovery third, then usefulness and intent alignment. A page blocked by configuration does not demonstrate weak market demand. Conversely, changing titles cannot rescue a dataset that adds no meaningful information. Build a content refresh queue with one defect class, one owner, an expected observable effect, and a review date for every item. Prioritize errors shared by many pages over cosmetic edits affecting one sentence. Require critical crawl defects to receive an assigned repair within one business day and verified resolution within three business days. Keep content changes in small logged batches so attribution remains interpretable; this is an observational workflow, not a randomized ranking experiment. Run ten fixed generative-search prompts on Day 20 and compare source citations with the baseline, recording product version where visible. Do not multiply tests until a favorable answer appears. If a citation disappears, inspect source accessibility and factual clarity before assuming a platform penalty. Technical audit automation and refresh queues should remove repetitive inspection work, not replace editorial judgment or manufacture unsupported explanations for ranking volatility.

Protect the economic model while traffic remains small. Canonical Conservative revenue is $80,000 with $64,000 total costs and $16,000 owner net profit; Base revenue is $490,000 with $330,750 costs and $159,250 profit; Scaled revenue is $900,000 with $495,000 costs and $405,000 profit. Those identities produce 20%, 32.5%, and 45% margins respectively. The first two cases use 2 FT + 4 C; the third uses 3 FT + 5 C. The monthly operating-expense range describes an operating envelope, not a complete reconciliation of every annual scenario cost. Keep variable production, commercial servicing, and other scenario costs visible rather than forcing annual costs into that monthly range. For an illustrative monetization sensitivity, 10,000 sessions at $20 advertising revenue per thousand sessions produce $200. If 300 qualified outbound clicks convert at 3% with $25 approved commissions, affiliate revenue adds $225. Combined revenue is $425, not the merchants' underlying order value. These assumptions are neither canonical traffic requirements nor measured launch results. Use them to expose how much audience and commercial validation remain before the annual profit cases become credible.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Four tactics that preserve diagnostic value**
> 
> A small search portfolio wins by keeping evidence interpretable while larger publishers confuse activity with progress.
> - Save rendered HTML and a screenshot with each release hash; template regressions often disappear before someone investigates them.
> - Maintain an original-cohort report beside the whole-site dashboard so new URLs cannot hide deterioration in earlier pages.
> - Separate affiliate approval dates from click dates; delayed approvals otherwise make one week appear artificially profitable.
> - Preserve source licenses, contractor assignments, and monthly revenue reconciliations from launch; those records support later media-exit diligence.

## 4. Days 22-30 and checkpoints 60/90: Enforce Kill, Fix, or bounded Scale  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The final nine days turn observations into a signed resource decision. Audit every published page against five checks: supported claims, permitted assets, distinct reader value, accurate commercial presentation, and functioning technical delivery. Require complete rights and disclosure coverage; require at least 95% of cohort pages to pass all five checks before expansion. With twenty-four pages, that means at least twenty-three complete passes. Day 30 Scale means permission to add at most six pages during the next month inside the same $12,000 monthly ceiling, not permission to launch another site. Require at least twenty eligible URLs, at least 90% indexed, zero unresolved critical technical defects, and funded continuation. With twenty-four eligible URLs, at least twenty-two must be indexed. Sparse search traffic alone does not kill a new domain after thirty days. If the sample is smaller or quality misses the threshold, choose Fix and stop net-new commissioning until the shortfall is addressed. If rights cannot be secured or cash is unavailable, stop affected publishing or spending immediately. Keep useful existing pages available when doing so is operationally supportable.

At Days 60 and 90, evaluate the latest complete twenty-eight-day Search Console window, ending three days before review. Use the frozen original cohort for demand comparisons and report expansion pages separately. Day 60 bounded Scale requires at least 1,000 impressions, twenty-five organic clicks, and impressions on ten distinct cohort URLs, alongside the technical, quality, and cash prerequisites. Authorize no more than six additional pages. Day 90 bounded Scale requires 3,000 impressions, one hundred clicks, impressions on ten URLs, and at least 25% impression growth against the preceding nonoverlapping twenty-eight-day window. A zero comparison denominator cannot establish that growth condition. These are deliberately modest experiment thresholds, not search-industry benchmarks. Kill paid expansion at Day 60 when impressions remain below one hundred after a documented repair cycle and fourteen technically stable days. At Day 90, kill paid expansion when impressions remain below three hundred and clicks below ten under those same conditions. Otherwise choose Fix when Scale criteria fail. A first deal can still take six to twenty-four months; these checkpoints test whether continued learning deserves additional capital.

Apply decision precedence consistently: unresolved rights or funding failures stop affected activity first; measurement or technical defects produce Fix; valid low-demand evidence can then trigger Kill; Scale requires every stated prerequisite. Fix authorizes one thirty-day repair cycle with named defects and no net-new page commissioning. Never exceed $36,000 cumulative operating spend through Day 90 without a separately funded plan. If data remain inconclusive then, choose a funded maintenance experiment or stop paid expansion rather than renewing the sprint indefinitely. Larger portfolio expansion needs separate economic evidence: three closed months with positive contribution after direct production and servicing costs, reconciled revenue statements, and a documented customer acquisition or audience-growth mechanism. Before making that decision, require at least one hundred qualified commercial outbound clicks and ten approved attributed affiliate conversions if affiliate economics support the case; otherwise use delivered sponsor contracts or closed advertising statements. Flag any partner exceeding 40% of recognized revenue and stress-test its complete loss. The eight-person Scaled scenario is a distinct staffing decision, never an automatic reward for indexing. Preserve these records for future buyers assessing transferable earnings rather than screenshots.
