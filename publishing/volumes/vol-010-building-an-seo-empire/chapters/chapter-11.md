## 1. Assign thirty plays to six accountable seats before adding capacity  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Design Candidate #25 around accountable outcomes rather than a miniature agency hierarchy. The first full-time seat is the publisher and portfolio owner, responsible for capital allocation, commercial relationships, treasury, and the weekly decision meeting. The second is the managing editor, responsible for editorial acceptance, content architecture, refresh scheduling, and publication quality. Four distinct contractor seats cover writing, research and verification, technical SEO and automation, and revenue operations. A seat is an accountable person with a defined scope, not a promise of forty purchased hours. Research cannot silently become editorial approval, and the writer cannot approve their own evidence. Give the technical contractor scoped repository and staging access; keep registrar ownership, payout changes, and recovery credentials under the publisher's control. The editor can authorize routine releases within a documented change policy, while migrations require rollback evidence and explicit publisher authorization. Use GitHub issues for technical changes and Airtable or Linear for editorial work, but retain one shared asset identifier across both. Every domain, article, template, sponsor agreement, and affiliate relationship should resolve to an owner and a record. The resulting organization has six people, even when one contractor supports several sites.

Route all thirty mapped operator plays through six work baskets of five plays each. These baskets are an operating overlay, not a reconstruction of an unsupplied canonical play catalog: portfolio selection, research and authority, production and refresh, technical distribution, monetization, and reporting and transferability. Attach the actual play names to this register before deployment; record one accountable seat, one acceptance artifact, one capacity estimate, and one economic metric for every entry. Give each basket a weekly work-in-progress limit and prioritize expected incremental contribution per constrained editorial hour. Programmatic page families consume technical and verification capacity together; they are not free inventory simply because generation is automated. GEO citation capture belongs in research and distribution, with sampled citation evidence and referral measurement rather than promised ranking outcomes. At Scaled, add a full-time operations and analytics lead to own close preparation, capacity planning, and reporting automation, plus a specialist contractor for original testing, data acquisition, or vertical expertise. That produces exactly 3 FT + 5 C. Do not multiply the entire pod by the number of domains. Shared technical systems, research libraries, and commercial reporting create leverage; independent domain-level accountability prevents a profitable site from concealing another site's persistent losses.

Use the canonical economics as a constraint, not a recruiting advertisement. Conservative revenue of $80,000 less $16,000 owner profit implies $64,000 of total annual expenses. Base implies $330,750, and Scaled implies $495,000. Those totals include every expense within the scenario's profit definition; contractor budgets cannot be added afterward without reducing the stated profit. The published $3,000–$20,000 monthly operating range is a planning reference, not an all-in ceiling consistent with every annual case: Base and Scaled average $27,562.50 and $41,250 in implied monthly expenses. Resolve scope differences explicitly in the model. Conservative cannot credibly purchase two ordinary market-rate full-time salaries plus substantial contractor capacity from $64,000. Document any founder labor, limited contractor allocations, or separately funded runway; do not describe unpaid effort as transferable earnings. The $15,000–$120,000 startup capital range and six-to-twenty-four-month first-deal window make liquidity planning material. Maintain a thirteen-week cash forecast and a twelve-month capacity budget. Add the two Scaled seats only after observed acceptance throughput, collection behavior, and contribution forecasts support their full cost. A hiring decision should survive a ranking-loss sensitivity, not merely the previous month's unusually strong affiliate payout.

## 2. Buy accepted work and enforce a weekly evidence scorecard  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Compensate contractors for bounded deliverables with transparent acceptance rules. An illustrative purchase schedule might pay $300 for a researched article, $90 for a verification packet, $120 for a material refresh, and $100 per hour for approved technical work. These are negotiating examples, not extra canonical expenses or universal market rates. At twelve articles, twelve verification packets, sixteen refreshes, and twenty technical hours monthly, those orders total $8,600. A $1,200 revenue-operations retainer makes the illustrative contractor commitment $9,800 monthly, or $117,600 annually, leaving $213,150 of Base's expense envelope for all other costs. The same schedule does not fit Conservative's $64,000 annual expense allowance. State what an article includes: original synthesis, accessible source links, documented image rights, agreed metadata, and working commercial links. Distinguish corrections within the original scope from newly requested research. Promise editorial review within two business days, scoped corrections within three, and payment on the contractual schedule for accepted milestones. Purchase rush response separately. An availability retainer should specify coverage windows and escalation channels instead of implying continuous support from a part-time technical contractor. Track accepted unit cost, including editor rework, rather than celebrating a cheap initial draft.

Close the weekly scorecard at a fixed timestamp using consistent reporting windows. Search Console supplies clicks and impressions by property and page cohort; GA4 supplies measured engaged sessions and referral events; partner statements supply earned revenue. These systems have different definitions and reporting delays, so display freshness and reconcile material differences instead of forcing identical totals. Compare trailing twenty-eight days with the preceding comparable period, then annotate seasonality and releases. Measure accepted deliverables divided by submitted deliverables, median editorial turnaround, overdue refreshes, indexed eligible pages, and contribution per accepted production dollar. Set an initial 90% first-pass acceptance target and investigate repeated misses, but do not let the target encourage concealing rejected work. For programmatic templates, sample at least ten rendered pages per changed family and expand the sample after any defect. A broken canonical, missing disclosure, or unsupported consequential claim blocks that affected release regardless of the aggregate acceptance rate. Revenue per thousand measured sessions is a diagnostic ratio, not a substitute for recognized revenue. GEO monitoring should record the query set, engine, timestamp, citation URL, and repeat observations; volatile citations cannot independently justify contractor bonuses or staffing increases.

Pay variable compensation for controllable, verified improvements rather than raw word counts, indexed URLs, or rankings purchased through undisclosed links. An editor-approved quality bonus can depend on sustained acceptance and low defect recurrence; a revenue bonus should use collected incremental contribution after reversals, with a written baseline and attribution window. Never penalize a writer mechanically for an unrelated platform update. Run a thirty-minute Monday meeting: inspect anomalies, identify the binding constraint, authorize the next batch, and assign one owner and deadline to each exception. Across the thirty-play register, distinguish active, queued, and deliberately paused work so the six-person pod is not committed to thirty simultaneous initiatives. Contractor classification also depends on the actual relationship. The Fair Labor Standards Act's employee definitions in 29 U.S.C. section 203 and applicable state rules cannot be displaced by labeling a person a contractor. California Labor Code section 2775 establishes an ABC framework subject to statutory exceptions and their conditions. Assess control, independent business activity, and the relevant jurisdiction before engagement. Where the relationship requires employment, use the appropriate arrangement and recalculate the staffing budget. Quality standards and evidence requirements do not eliminate employment-classification obligations.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Protect the Editorial Bottleneck Before Buying More Drafts**
> 
> Search portfolios usually lose leverage through hidden review work, tracking defects, and repeated template mistakes rather than insufficient output.
> - Attach the managing editor's review minutes to each accepted article so cheap drafts cannot hide expensive correction cycles.
> - Keep a frozen template test set containing pagination, comparison tables, affiliate links, and mobile layouts before each programmatic release.
> - Record partner subidentifiers by domain and cohort before publishing; reconstructing attribution during a sale consumes time and weakens evidence.
> - Maintain a sampled GEO citation log separately from revenue reporting; require attributable referral evidence before assigning commercial value.

## 3. Build accrual cohorts, enforce concentration caps, and secure transferable rights  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Maintain one chart of accounts with dimensions for domain, revenue stream, counterparty, publication cohort, and originating play. The cohort can be publication month plus template or topical cluster; preserve it when an article is refreshed and record refresh spending separately. Advertising revenue follows earned delivery under the contract, affiliate revenue follows the applicable earning conditions with supportable reversal estimates, and sponsorship revenue follows satisfied delivery obligations. Cash receipt dates belong in settlement records, not the recognition policy. Reconcile provisional network reports to final statements and document true-ups. A dashboard showing $100,000 of referred merchant sales at a 5% earned commission supports $5,000 of operator revenue before applicable reversals, not $100,000. Similarly, sponsored campaigns paid in advance create deferred revenue until the promised obligations are delivered. Use QuickBooks Online or Xero for the ledger and a controlled reporting export for cohort analysis. Allocate shared technical and editorial expenses using a stable driver, such as documented hours or accepted work units, and preserve both direct and allocated contribution views. Do not change allocation methods to make a weak acquisition look attractive immediately before diligence. Maintain a written policy for expensing or capitalizing content costs under the applicable accounting framework.

Cap each customer or contractual revenue counterparty at 25% of trailing twelve-month recognized revenue, and apply the same ceiling to the next ninety-day contracted revenue forecast. Aggregate entities under common economic control; four affiliate programs paying through the same obligor do not necessarily create four independent credit exposures. At Base, 25% is $122,500 annually; Conservative permits $20,000 and Scaled $225,000. A new portfolio with one paying partner necessarily breaches this mature-state policy: label the breach, restrict incremental exposure, and set a dated diversification plan rather than pretending the ratio passes. With four counterparties, every one must sit exactly at 25% to meet the ceiling; five or more material relationships provide room for normal fluctuations. Assess ad-network contractual payment exposure separately from the advertisers appearing through that network. Track affiliate merchant dependence, network dependence, and site dependence without double-counting them as separate revenue. Search-engine traffic concentration is a different risk: twenty affiliate programs do not diversify an audience acquired almost entirely through one search platform. Report organic channel share, top-page contribution, and ranking-loss sensitivity alongside the contractual cap. Choose mitigations from the thirty-play register rather than buying unrelated traffic merely to improve a concentration chart.

Secure transferable rights when work is commissioned, not after a buyer requests the data room. Under 17 U.S.C. section 204(a), a transfer of copyright ownership generally requires a signed writing by the owner or their authorized agent. Contractor status alone does not give the publisher ownership. Work-made-for-hire treatment has narrower statutory conditions under sections 101 and 201(b); use a properly drafted assignment for commissioned content where appropriate instead of relying on that label. Link each signed agreement to deliverables, source files, permitted third-party materials, attribution requirements, and any retained rights. Capture assignments from actual contributors and address subcontracting before it occurs. Inventory stock-photo licenses, datasets, code dependencies, and AI-assisted material separately because an assignment cannot transfer rights the contributor never possessed. For monetized recommendations, 16 CFR section 255.5 addresses disclosure of material connections; retain page-level examples and change history. For personal information, map collection purposes, processor relationships, retention, and applicable transfer restrictions; California Civil Code section 1798.100 supplies concrete duties for covered businesses. Partner accounts, audience records, and analytics properties may have transfer conditions distinct from copyright ownership. A transferable content library does not automatically make every commercial account assignable.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the monthly evidence package
   - Export partner statements, analytics reports, invoices, and contractor acceptance records.
   - Store dated copies under stable domain and counterparty identifiers; log later revisions instead of replacing source evidence silently.
2. Post recognition and settlement differences
   - Reconcile earned revenue, reversals, receivables, deferred sponsorship balances, and cash receipts.
   - The publisher approves material judgment entries; the operations lead assumes preparation at Scaled while approval remains separate.
3. Refresh cohort economics and exposure limits
   - Allocate shared costs consistently and recalculate annual and forward counterparty concentration.
   - Publish direct contribution, allocated contribution, cash collections, and any cap breach with one responsible owner and a dated remedy.
4. Update the transferability register
   - Attach new assignments, license evidence, account-transfer terms, and completed operating procedures.
   - Review a sample of newly published assets against their rights records and retain the review result with the monthly close.

## 4. Price transferable earnings and preserve the cash-cow alternative  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Value the portfolio on an explicitly defined earnings basis. Small owner-operated publishing transactions may quote a multiple of monthly seller discretionary earnings, while more institutional buyers may use annual adjusted EBITDA after management costs. These measures are not interchangeable with canonical owner net profit. Build a bridge starting from the ledger: identify owner compensation, genuine nonrecurring costs, recurring software and content spending, replacement management needs, and deferred maintenance. An unpaid founder who performs essential editorial work creates a replacement-cost deduction in a manager-run case, not a magical add-back. Expense policies for content must be consistent across the historical period. For negotiation sensitivity, use an illustrative 24–42 times normalized monthly earnings, equivalent to 2.0–3.5 times annual earnings. This is a planning range, not a claim about current completed transactions or an automatic premium. Obtain recent closed comparables with matching revenue mix, concentration, traffic history, and transaction terms before setting an asking price. Place a fragile, declining, owner-dependent site below the range when evidence warrants; reserve the upper portion for stable, transferable operations with defensible economics. A larger portfolio is not automatically worth a higher multiple if its sites share the same ranking vulnerability.

If normalized annual earnings happened to equal the canonical owner profit without adjustments, the illustrative range would produce $32,000–$56,000 for Conservative, $318,500–$557,375 for Base, and $810,000–$1,417,500 for Scaled. The condition matters: these are sensitivity outputs, not appraisals. Base monthly earnings would be $13,270.83 before rounding, so calculate transaction values from annual figures to avoid rounding drift. Never apply the multiple to $490,000 of Base revenue or to referred merchant sales. Separate headline asset value from seller proceeds: broker fees, transaction costs, taxes, debt settlement, working-capital adjustments, holdbacks, and contingent consideration can materially change cash received. Define treatment of cash, debt, receivables, and deferred sponsorship obligations in the term sheet rather than assuming one universal convention. Discount an earnout for its collection risk and dependence on buyer-controlled decisions; do not treat it as cash at closing. Give buyers monthly revenue bridges, concentration history, cohort economics, signed rights evidence, and a complete access inventory. Remove unnecessary personal data from diligence copies and use controlled access. A clean financial package can reduce uncertainty and negotiation friction, but it cannot erase declining demand or guarantee an account provider will permit transfer.

Run a thirty-day owner-absence rehearsal before choosing between sale and continued operation. The publisher stays available for reserved decisions but stops performing routine production, reconciliation, and reporting tasks. Record interventions, missed deadlines, undocumented credentials, and emergency contractor purchases; convert those observations into a realistic replacement-cost schedule. Verify that backups restore, commercial tracking survives a routine release, and the weekly scorecard still reaches decision-makers without manual rescue. Preserve necessary separation between payment changes and their approval during the rehearsal. A durable cash cow uses the same evidence package as a sale-ready portfolio: disciplined refresh spending, diversified counterparties, rights ownership, and an accountable operating cadence. Compare after-cost sale proceeds with the present value of explicitly modeled future distributions, including reinvestment, traffic decline sensitivities, and the owner's desired involvement. Do not compare a gross asking price with an optimistic perpetual profit stream. Review the thirty-play register quarterly and retire activities that consume scarce editor hours without strengthening contribution or defensibility. Expansion from six to eight people earns its place only when the added seats improve accepted throughput and transferable earnings. The strongest negotiating position is the ability to keep operating profitably without requiring a sale to solve a staffing or liquidity problem.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Verify the Earnings Package Before Hiring Up or Marketing an Exit**
> 
> Use these four pre-flight checks to prevent unsupported staffing commitments and valuation claims.
> - Reconcile the selected scenario to $64,000, $330,750, or $495,000 of implied annual expenses and explicitly price essential founder replacement labor.
> - Verify the thirty-play ownership register, six-seat or eight-seat roster, contractor acceptance records, and completed owner-absence rehearsal.
> - Test every contractual counterparty against the 25% annual and forward revenue limits; document breaches and dated diversification actions without presenting a breach as a pass.
> - Confirm signed copyright assignments, material license permissions, account-transfer requirements, and a ledger-to-normalized-earnings bridge before quoting any multiple.
