*Navigating state fee ceilings, post-sale waiting periods, mobile notary packets, and anti-fraud disclosures.*

## Executive Summary

A surplus lead is a public record, not permission to promise payment or purchase an owner's rights. Build the engagement around the sale type, the controlling statute, the documented claimant, and a transparent distribution statement. This chapter separates judicial foreclosure from tax-deed claims, compares five state pathways, and turns outreach into a reproducible evidence trail. It also explains why an attorney trust account cannot rescue an unlawful fee or forged assignment. The operating objective is a valid claim with informed owner participation, defensible deductions, and independently confirmed disbursement instructions.

## Classify the sale before pricing the engagement  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with the instrument that created the fund, not the county's marketing label. Obtain the tax deed or certificate of title, final judgment when applicable, sale confirmation, surplus deposit entry, and the clerk's current claim instructions. Record the sale date separately from the deposit date and the date any notice was mailed. A judicial foreclosure surplus and a tax-deed surplus in the same Florida county do not share one interchangeable procedure. Identify which event starts each statutory clock and preserve the underlying document beside the calculated deadline. A spreadsheet populated from an auction date alone can produce a perfectly formatted contract that addresses the wrong fund and the wrong claimant.

Florida section 45.033 concerns judicial foreclosure surplus ownership, assignments, and surplus-trustee requirements; section 197.582 addresses tax-deed surplus distribution and claims. Texas section 34.04 requires special attention to both capped recovery compensation and post-sale assignment restrictions. California Civil Code sections 2924j and 2924k govern trustee-sale distribution, while Revenue and Taxation Code section 4675 supplies a different tax-sale claim and assignment framework. Georgia section 48-4-5 addresses tax-sale excess funds and competing interests. Ohio and Pennsylvania require the actual court docket, controlling distribution rules, and local motion practice. Never infer that a state lacking a percentage in this comparison permits any desired fee or allows an unlicensed operator to represent claimants in court.

Treat the comparison as a routing document rather than a reusable contract opinion. Counsel must identify the current statutory text, subsection, effective date, county procedure, applicable deadline, and whether the proposed arrangement is a service agreement, assignment, or legal representation. Keep those classifications separate in the engagement record. The assignment buyer's purchase price is not automatically a permissible recovery-service charge, and renaming compensation does not change its substance. For Texas, a covered $98,000 recovery would not support an ordinary $24,500 collection fee under the lesser-of cap: the arithmetic ceiling is $1,000. Model economics only after the legal service structure is settled, and decline files whose lawful proceeds cannot support delivery costs.

## Make the first contact transparent and traceable  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use an opening that distinguishes the public deposit from the owner's eventual entitlement: 'We are a private recovery service, not the county or a court. The county record dated June 4 lists $142,000 in gross surplus connected to this sale. Liens and other claims may reduce or exhaust that amount. You can ask the clerk about claiming directly without hiring us. We charge no upfront fee under our proposed contingent arrangement, and we will provide the fee and expense terms in writing before you decide.' Include the official office name and a public contact route the owner can independently locate. Never describe the entire deposit as an approved payment already belonging to the recipient.

Before telephone outreach, classify the channel, number source, dialing method, message content, and applicable federal and state requirements. A public property record does not establish consent for automated marketing calls, prerecorded voices, or texts. Scrub against relevant national and state do-not-call lists, maintain an internal suppression list, observe calling-hour limits, and retain the scrub date with the campaign version. Honor revocation and stop requests across vendors rather than merely deleting one phone field. Manual calls are not a blanket exemption from solicitation restrictions. Where the contact basis is uncertain, use a plainly branded letter with the clerk's direct-claim route rather than testing whether a disputed call produces a complaint.

Record the precise script, contact date, recipient identity confidence, and outcome without collecting unnecessary sensitive identifiers. If a relative answers, request a safe contact route instead of announcing a deceased owner's assets or alleged entitlement. For returned mail, investigate the address using authorized sources and preserve the uncertainty instead of escalating to repeated calls. Offer a written comparison of self-filing, independent counsel, and the proposed recovery service. Explain whether any expense is deducted before or after the contingent percentage and whether the owner owes anything if nothing is recovered. Give the recipient time to verify the county record independently; urgency should describe an actual documented filing deadline, never an invented expiration on an offer.

## Build a verifiable packet and a lawful trust-account waterfall  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The packet should identify the actual service provider, the claimant, the specific sale and fund, the agreed scope, and every compensation recipient. Include the written contingency terms, expense treatment, direct-claim explanation, narrowly scoped authorizations, and any assignment disclosures required for that transaction. Counsel must distinguish assistance gathering records from legal advice and court representation. Do not assume that adding an attorney signature cures unauthorized legal work or permits sharing legal fees with a nonlawyer. A research vendor can have a separately lawful compensation arrangement, but the invoice, engagement, and distribution statement must describe what actually happened. Do not split one prohibited recovery fee into several smaller invoices to conceal the combined charge from the owner or court.

A mobile notary verifies the appearance and identity required under the governing notarization law; the notary does not establish title, inheritance, claim priority, or contract fairness. Send a versioned packet with no blank economic terms, verify the venue and permitted identification method, and match the signer's name against the claim documents. For an estate, trust, or company, obtain the relevant letters, trust authority, resolution, or other authority evidence in addition to personal identification. Retain the completed certificate and legally appropriate audit record without copying unnecessary identity documents. If the owner denies a signature, stop using that instrument, preserve the original scan and transmission history, and have counsel determine the correction and notification path rather than asking a notary to backdate a replacement.

When permitted, counsel receives recovered funds in the appropriate attorney trust account; IOLTA is appropriate only where the jurisdiction's rules and expected holding circumstances call for it. Larger or longer-held client funds may require a separate interest-bearing account. Confirm the account holder and disbursement instructions through an independently verified office number, not an emailed replacement instruction. Maintain an individual client ledger showing cleared receipts, lien payments, authorized fees, reimbursable expenses, and the owner's distribution. Counsel should retain disputed amounts as required while releasing undisputed funds when permitted. The owner receives a settlement statement reconciling the original deposit to every deduction. The recovery company never treats the entire client receipt as its own revenue or advances money against uncleared trust funds.

## Forensic teardown: three traps and an evidence-backed fallback  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The illegal upfront-fee trap often begins with an official-looking notice promising release of a specific check after an owner pays a processing charge. Examine the envelope, payment recipient, asserted agency relationship, and cited authority. Compare the supposed fee with the clerk's published schedule and the actual engagement. Do not imply every advance professional retainer is unlawful everywhere; the defect may instead be a prohibited recovery charge, deception, or charging for a nonexistent release. In this operating model the owner pays zero upfront, the firm funds agreed investigation, and counsel confirms what compensation is lawful on success. Preserve questionable notices as evidence, remove their claims from the sales script, and route any refund or reporting decision through verified counsel rather than forwarding more money to the sender.

The forged-assignment trap attacks ownership rather than merely price. Warning signs include an execution date inconsistent with a statutory waiting period, a signature denied by the named owner, an unexplained address change, a mismatched notarial venue, or an agent unable to produce authority. Compare the assignment against the recorded ownership chain and original transmission metadata; do not certify authenticity from visual similarity alone. A fresh notarization cannot retroactively make a forged transfer genuine. The deceptive-government-mailer trap instead borrows seals, court formatting, threatening deadlines, or words such as mandatory to manufacture authority. Replace it with the firm's actual name, an accurate private-service description, documented deadlines, and the independently verifiable public claim route. Preserve the misleading original without reusing it as a conversion template.

The fallback is a verified claimant, a documented fund, independently retained counsel where required, and a transparent escrow distribution, not a cleverer assignment form. If an owner disputes a contract, pause use of that contract and let counsel establish whether a new lawful engagement or direct claim is appropriate. If a senior lien may consume the fund, update the recovery estimate before requesting a signature and avoid presenting a speculative fee as earned. Track a complaint against the exact campaign and packet versions so the same representation does not recur elsewhere. At closing, compare promised and actual deductions, record any variance explanation, and retain the final order and cleared-payment evidence. A trustworthy process is demonstrated by a file that another examiner can reconstruct without relying on the salesperson's memory.

**Trap evidence and verified fallback**

| Trap | Evidence to preserve | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Illegal upfront charge | Mailer, invoice, payment demand and claimed authority | Verify actual court fees; use a lawful zero-upfront engagement |
| Forged assignment | Original instrument, metadata, identity discrepancy and authority records | Stop reliance; counsel verifies ownership and corrective procedure |
| Deceptive government mailer | Envelope, seals, sender details and false deadline | Private branding, accurate dates and a direct clerk contact |
| Diverted escrow payment | Changed bank instructions and communication history | Independent callback and client-ledger reconciliation |

## Public-filing trigger mail and claimant objection scripts  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The public_filing_trigger_mail_arbitrage play earns attention through timely evidence rather than borrowed authority. Target dispatch of a private commercial priority-mail packet within 24-48 hours after a completed sale only when the public filing, fund evidence, claimant review, and applicable solicitation rules already support contact. This is an internal service target, not a statutory deadline or a promise that surplus will be confirmed immediately. If the county has not posted a deposit or the examiner cannot connect the former owner to the fund, hold the packet until the missing evidence arrives. Never convert a scheduled auction or winning bid into a statement of available excess proceeds. Preserve the source publication time, retrieval time, examiner release, packet version, and mailing receipt so the timing claim is auditable. Check any jurisdiction-specific solicitation restriction separately from an assignment waiting period; permission to send an informational letter does not make an early assignment valid. Have a second operator match the address and case identifier before printing. A fast packet sent to the wrong household creates expense and exposes another person's financial circumstances without advancing a valid claim.

Use the firm's real business name and return address on the envelope, with a prominent private commercial solicitation description. Postal priority service describes delivery, not governmental sponsorship. Under 39 U.S.C. 3001, evaluate subsection (h) when a solicitation conveys federal connection and subsection (d) when material resembles a bill or account statement. Those provisions do not create a universal design recipe for every county-themed mailer; state deception rules and other postal restrictions also matter. Avoid seals, agency-style typography, payment vouchers, official-benefit language, and anything suggesting the recipient must purchase assistance to receive public funds. Include a source excerpt labeled with its date, a plain explanation of gross versus recoverable surplus, the independently searchable clerk contact, the direct-claim route, and written proposed economics. Redact unrelated owners from source pages. Do not place sensitive balances or property-loss details on the exterior. Retain the complete envelope and insert artwork together, because a candid interior cannot reliably correct a misleading exterior. Tracking proves dispatch and delivery events, not that the claimant read, understood, or accepted the engagement. Returned packets enter address review rather than an automatic repeat-mail sequence.

Use the table's language verbatim after replacing bracketed fields with verified facts; it is a conversation aid, not permission to call a suppressed number. Start by identifying the private firm and asking whether the person wants the public reference. Answer skepticism with independent verification, never a testimonial presented as proof of this claimant's entitlement. When someone mentions a cheaper locator, compare the same fee base, expense allocation, scope, cancellation provisions, and lawful aggregate charge rather than attacking the competitor. A ten-percent proposal can be cheaper and entirely legitimate; the owner should see that possibility plainly. When the bank exhausted the property equity, distinguish the foreclosure accounting from the separate surplus ledger without asserting that money remains payable to the owner. For a request to receive money first, explain the chosen contingent model and the actual distribution process without promising an advance or claiming control over the clerk. Record objections as neutral questions, not vulnerability scores. Send one requested written follow-up, honor a refusal across channels, and document unresolved factual questions before offering a contract. Measure verified responses and completed lawful engagements against printing, postage, examination, and failed-file costs, not merely signed packets.
