# Chapter 06: Finding Your First Qualified Opportunities

*County excess-proceeds ledgers, auction portals, and the 4-Gate Surplus Qualification SOP.*

> THE BIG MONEY PLAYBOOKS · THE FUCK YOU MONEY CLUB

## Executive Summary

Build the prospect queue from official balances, not auction excitement. County ledgers establish custody, auction portals locate sale events, and targeted public records requests fill gaps in unposted data. The four qualification gates then test deposit size, identity and title continuity, residual value after competing claims, and a live statutory filing window. This chapter turns those checks into a repeatable sourcing procedure, including an alternative when portal automation is blocked. The output is a small queue of evidenced opportunities with reproducible calculations, rather than a large spreadsheet of unverified former owners.

## Start with the custodian's ledger  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Choose one county and one sale type for the first production batch. Find the official Clerk, Treasurer, Tax Collector, or Sheriff page responsible for that jurisdiction's surplus custody. Search for excess proceeds, surplus funds, tax deed surplus, foreclosure surplus, and unclaimed court funds, but confirm the office actually holds this category of money. Download the official PDF or CSV ledger and retain the publication date, retrieval date, source address, and original file. An old ledger is a historical lead, not a current balance confirmation. Some counties move older balances to a different custodian; record that transfer rather than calling a missing row paid. Establish who can confirm the current deposit and where the controlling case records can be inspected before contacting owners.

Create one source row per sale event with county, sale type, case number, parcel identifier, sale date, former owner label, ledger balance, and custodian reference. Preserve identifiers as text so leading zeros survive spreadsheet imports. Keep separate fields for the auction bid, total sale proceeds, and excess remaining after disbursement; they are not interchangeable. Where a PDF uses wrapped lines, reconcile each extracted record against the visible page before loading the queue. Carry a page reference so another operator can reproduce the extraction. If the ledger has totals, compare extracted counts and sums with those totals and explain exclusions. A duplicated parcel or shifted dollar column can manufacture a high value opportunity that never existed in the custodian's accounts.

Use a stable source key combining jurisdiction, sale type, case number, and sale event rather than an owner's name alone. One person may own several parcels, and one parcel may appear in multiple canceled or repeated auctions. Retain corrections as dated observations instead of overwriting the original balance without history. For the first ten records, ask the custodian to identify the deposit entry and any later distributions using existing records. Record the evidence returned, not just an employee's informal estimate over the telephone. Keep contact research separate from financial qualification: discovering a working phone number does not improve a deficient surplus claim. The batch is ready for screening when each row points to both an identifiable sale and an identifiable custody record.

## Connect sale platforms to a targeted records request  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

RealTaxDeed and RealForeclose can expose county tax deed and mortgage foreclosure auction events; Bid4Assets and Grant Street Group serve participating jurisdictions with configurations that vary by location. County sheriff sale results may provide another route to the sale report or docket. Begin from the county's official link to the service rather than assuming a similarly named website is authoritative. Capture the auction identifier, status, date, amount, and linked case documents. Distinguish sold, canceled, postponed, redemption, and pending payment statuses. A winning bid is not proof that the bidder paid, the sale became final, or a surplus reached the clerk. Reconcile the platform record against the deposit ledger and any later order vacating the sale before counting money in the pipeline.

When a county publishes no ledger, use the applicable state public records process, often informally called FOIA or Sunshine Law. Federal FOIA is not the governing statute for an ordinary county request. In Florida, section 119.07 addresses inspection and copying of public records, including applicable fees and statutory exemptions. Request existing records rather than asking staff to calculate investment opportunities or decide lien priority. A practical request identifies tax deed or foreclosure surplus account records for a specified date range and asks for available case number, parcel number, sale date, deposit, disbursement, remaining balance, and record update date. Ask for the existing electronic format if available, and permit redaction of exempt fields without requesting unnecessary personal identifiers. Narrow categories make routing and production easier.

Specify that an existing CSV, spreadsheet, or ordinary system export is preferable to printing and scanning, without asserting that the office must build a new report or custom program. Ask for a cost estimate before work that incurs special service charges, and identify a budget amount that triggers a follow-up decision. Track request date, custodian acknowledgment, clarification, estimate, production date, and the period the records actually cover. A response containing last quarter's balances cannot silently become today's inventory. If no responsive consolidated list exists, request the underlying deposit and disbursement records for a smaller batch of identified cases. Keep a reusable request template but update office names and statutory references for each state; a Florida citation does not dictate a Georgia county's production obligations.

> **OPERATOR GATE — A sale result is not a deposit**
> 
> Hold the lead until an official custody record supports the balance.
> - Record sale finality and payment status.
> - Reconcile later distributions and reversals.
> - Keep requests bounded to existing records.

## Run the four qualification gates in order  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Gate 1 requires a confirmed post-sale deposit of at least $15,000, using the custodian's actual surplus balance rather than the gross bid. A $180,000 bid against a $160,000 debt is not automatically a $20,000 surplus because costs, credits, taxes, and later adjustments may intervene. Gate 2 reconciles the vesting deed with the foreclosure plaintiff and the interest actually foreclosed. Match does not mean the owner and lender must share a name: connect the record owner, borrower where relevant, plaintiff, assignments, judgment, parcel description, and sale event in one evidence chain. For tax sales, use the corresponding tax proceeding and selling authority instead of inventing a mortgage plaintiff. Flag trusts, estates, divorces, and co-ownership for authority analysis rather than assigning the entire fund to one familiar name.

Gate 3 requires at least $12,000 in supportable net residual after subordinate claims, municipal amounts payable from proceeds, and other applicable deductions. Assume a confirmed $38,000 deposit, a $17,000 junior mortgage payoff, and a $6,000 municipal claim properly chargeable to this fund: the modeled residual is $15,000 and clears the internal threshold. A further $4,000 allowed judgment claim reduces it to $11,000 and fails. Do not deduct a surviving property lien from owner proceeds unless the applicable distribution rules justify that treatment, and do not double count amounts already paid. Unknown payoffs mean unresolved qualification, not zero liabilities. This residual is still before the owner's expenses and any permitted recovery fee; it is neither firm revenue nor a promised check amount.

Gate 4 tests the statutory waiting period and claim bar-date against the actual sale type and operative notice. Record the triggering event, rule, computed date, and source document; distinguish a distribution waiting period from a filing deadline. Classify files as actionable now, pending a future event, expired, or unresolved. Where no fixed bar applies to the specific procedure, document that conclusion and any transfer or court deadlines instead of inventing an expiration date. Georgia section 48-4-5 addresses tax-sale excess proceeds, interested-party notice, and distribution or interpleader mechanisms; it is not a universal clock for mortgage foreclosures. Bankruptcy authority and disputed priority remain separate blockers even if all numerical gates pass. Refresh the balance and docket immediately before submitting a claim because qualification describes dated evidence, not permanent entitlement.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Confirm custody
   - Verify at least $15,000 deposited surplus.
   - Attach the current ledger and disbursement history.
2. Reconcile the chain
   - Match vesting and the foreclosed interest.
   - Resolve parcel, owner, plaintiff or selling authority, and assignments.
3. Calculate residual
   - Document at least $12,000 after competing claims.
   - Use supported payoffs and reserve unresolved exposure.
4. Check timing
   - Verify waiting periods and the live claim window.
   - Identify the triggering notice, bar-date if applicable, and current docket.

## Public-record intelligence without a fragile portal dependency  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Track 01 play public_record_api_intelligence turns repeatable public records retrieval into a sourcing advantage, but the useful asset is a reliable evidence pipeline rather than aggressive scraping. Prefer an official downloadable file, documented public API, or export supplied by the records custodian. Record each source's allowed retrieval method, publication cadence, schema, and last successful acquisition. Limit requests to the necessary fields and time range; store a local snapshot so repeated analysis does not repeatedly hit the county server. API output still needs reconciliation with the official case record. A missing field or empty response may reflect a schema change or outage, not an absence of claims. Make these conditions visible so a broken importer does not quietly certify empty competition.

The Portal-Blocking Trap appears when a business model depends on a brittle browser path, undocumented endpoint, session cookie, or CAPTCHA protected search. A redesign, access limit, or blocked request can stop the feed overnight. Do not route around restrictions with disguised traffic, borrowed accounts, or automated challenge evasion. Pause that collection path and use the Scheduled Public-Records Export Fallback: arrange a recurring request or agreed export cadence with the custodian, within the office's available production process. A standing request may not be supported, so schedule your own renewed requests where necessary. The fallback is not a demand for free custom engineering. Budget for lawful production charges, staff response time, and occasional manual reconciliation while maintaining the same qualification standards.

Run each delivered export through a simple reconciliation checklist before it changes the prospect queue. Check required column names, parse dates explicitly, preserve identifiers, reject malformed currency, and compare row counts with the prior delivery. Investigate large differences rather than automatically marking missing cases as paid. Retain the original source file and attach a retrieval timestamp to each calculated balance. Separate new, changed, and unchanged records so operators review material events instead of rereading the entire county. For a first batch of twenty sale rows, report how many failed each gate and why, with unresolved rows kept outside the qualified total. The final handoff should contain evidence links, a residual worksheet, claimant authority notes, and the next actionable date. This is a reproducible sourcing system, not merely a contact list.

**Portal-Blocking Trap vs Scheduled Public-Records Export Fallback**

| Failure | Portal-Blocking Trap | Scheduled Export Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access restricted | Retry blocked automation indefinitely | Pause; request existing records from custodian |
| Schema changes | Import shifted balances silently | Validate columns and reconcile source totals |
| No standing export | Assume continuous delivery | Calendar bounded repeat requests |
| Stale balance | Sell an old auction lead | Refresh custody and docket before filing |

## Verbatim County Public Records / FOIA / Sunshine Request Template  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use the complete message below after replacing the bracketed fields with the county, office, date range, and return address. Send it to the records custodian identified on the official county website, not to an auction vendor merely because that vendor displays the sale. Tax-deed excess proceeds, foreclosure court-registry deposits, and uncashed warrants may belong to three different offices with different disclosure procedures. A finance department may hold warrant reports while the clerk holds registry balances and the treasurer administers tax-sale proceeds. If necessary, send separately scoped requests to each custodian, identifying which category that recipient maintains. State public-records law generally governs county administrative records, but judicial records may instead follow court-access rules; federal FOIA does not impose the response process for an ordinary county office. Confirm the applicable route before inserting a statutory citation. The template deliberately requests existing records rather than a new opinion about who deserves the funds. It also permits partial production, so an unavailable consolidated ledger does not prevent delivery of a useful existing report. Keep a copy of the exact outgoing text because the scope, date range, and fee authorization are evidence of what was requested.

Set the date range to a manageable initial batch, such as the prior twelve months, and distinguish transaction coverage from the report's balance date. A report produced today may contain balances last refreshed several weeks ago. Ask for available identifiers and amounts without insisting that the office create missing columns or join unrelated databases. CSV and XLSX preserve sortable values better than scanned printouts, but an existing PDF or native report remains useful when no electronic table exists. The request expressly excludes bank details and other sensitive identifiers because parcel numbers, case references, public payee labels, and transaction dates usually suffice for qualification. Uncashed warrants need especially careful reconciliation: an issued check may already have reduced the registry balance, and a stale warrant may have been canceled, reissued, paid, or transferred. Never add the warrant face amount to a reported surplus without checking how the custodian accounts for it. Match the warrant identifier to the case and transaction history, retain the status date, and investigate duplicate payments before classifying the entry as recoverable. A returned check is a lead to custody research, not an independent entitlement or proof that the named payee remains authorized.

The template authorizes up to $25 in lawful charges for this request, not $25 per installment or attachment. Choose a lower amount before sending if the batch economics require it, and maintain a running total across partial productions. An estimate above that ceiling should prompt a narrower date range, fewer record categories, inspection where available, or an explicit additional authorization; silence should not become an open-ended purchase order. Calendar an acknowledgment check after five business days and a status follow-up after ten business days, then weekly while production remains pending. Those are internal service reminders, not claims that every state imposes those response deadlines. Record any applicable statutory deadline separately and use an earlier legally required date when relevant. If the office supplies an expected production date, follow up against that commitment rather than sending repetitive daily messages. Preserve the request number, clarification history, estimates, payments, and delivery links alongside the original files. On receipt, reconcile report dates, counts, and totals before loading opportunities. If records were withheld, request the applicable authority and any releasable remainder; if no records exist, document that response and ask which existing underlying records can answer the narrow custody question. Never classify a pending request or an empty attachment as proof that no surplus exists.
