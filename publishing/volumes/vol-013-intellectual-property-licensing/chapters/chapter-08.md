## 1. Build an eighteen-source directory around four distinct rights plays  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Organize the directory around four operator plays, not four undifferentiated prospect lists. Patent licensing starts with publication numbers, issued claims, ownership instruments, prosecution history, maintenance status, and territorial family members. Brand licensing starts with the exact mark, goods and services, owner entity, live registration status, and proposed quality-control mechanism. Catalog licensing separates musical compositions, sound recordings, photographs, footage, and text because identical titles can conceal different rights holders. Dataset licensing starts with collection provenance, source permissions, personal-data exposure, permitted transformations, and redistribution scope. Assign every lead a play before buying enrichment. The directory below names eighteen working sources and describes access rather than assuming that every portal offers an unrestricted API. Cost categories distinguish free searches from paid documents, subscriptions, and negotiated contracts; they are procurement categories, not current price quotes. Latency describes the source's practical evidence boundary rather than promising a service level. Record the observed update timestamp during onboarding. The full-time operator owns source selection, while three contractors cover rights research, pipeline engineering, and royalty accounting. Specialist counsel can occupy the rights-research allocation when interpretation requires it. Contractor seats describe capacity, not three additional full-time salaries. Reserve specialist hours for disputed links, not routine identifier copying.

Public records reveal evidence, not a complete permission graph. USPTO assignment recordation is especially useful for locating conveyance documents and discovering owner-name changes, but recording an instrument does not establish its validity or legal effect under 37 CFR 3.54. Read the instrument and reconcile schedules, signatures, effective dates, exclusions, and subsequent transfers. Under 35 U.S.C. 261, patent assignments require a written instrument, and recordation timing can affect priority against later purchasers or mortgagees. A patent family hit therefore cannot substitute for a chain-of-title packet. For marks, use TSDR to inspect the prosecution file and registration history, then verify that the proposed license covers the actual goods, territory, channels, and quality controls. For copyrighted catalogs, registration and recordation searches help identify claimants and transactions, but omissions, older records, pseudonyms, and unrecorded transfers prevent a clean search from proving exclusive ownership. Section 204 of Title 17 generally requires a signed writing for transfers of copyright ownership. Treat a registry result as a dated observation linked to a document, never as a universal green light. Escalate contradictory instruments before issuing a term sheet that represents exclusivity or sublicensing authority.

Treat access policy as part of source engineering. Start each connector with the provider's current documentation, account entitlement, permitted retention rules, and published request limits. A browser search interface is not permission to scrape its internal endpoints. Prefer official downloads for bulk analysis, documented APIs for incremental refreshes, and saved manual extracts when no supported machine interface exists. The SEC requires fair-access behavior; identify the client and remain below its published maximum rather than using every available request slot. Other services may impose account-specific quotas, so store limits as configuration rather than hard-coded folklore. Honor Retry-After, back off with jitter, cache unchanged documents, and halt on repeated authorization failures. Record collection time separately from filing time, publication time, and the source's last update. An overnight pipeline cannot eliminate a registry's publication delay. When access fails, preserve the last successful snapshot, mark its age, and route a targeted manual check instead of silently presenting old evidence as fresh. This design lets the four-person pod maintain a narrow, useful evidence system without funding a universal web crawler or depending on fragile screen automation. Review connector health before each material rights decision.

## 2. Resolve owners before spending on commercial enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Create an entity-resolution waterfall that spends money only after public evidence establishes a plausible transaction. First normalize asset identifiers: publication and application numbers for patents, serial and registration numbers for marks, ISWC for compositions, ISRC for recordings, and versioned repository identifiers for datasets. Preserve raw values alongside normalized values so punctuation cleanup cannot conceal a mistaken match. Second resolve the contracting entity using jurisdiction, formation identifier where available, prior names, acquisition history, and official domain. Third attach documentary ownership evidence and identify the missing link. Fourth enrich the company, then locate a relevant licensing or business-development contact. People Data Labs can help distinguish businesses with similar names; Hunter can help route a message to a business mailbox. Neither service proves title, authority, or willingness to license. Require a confidence explanation such as matching registration number plus recorded transfer, not an opaque score alone. Merge records only when the identifier evidence supports the merge. A shared postal address, outside lawyer, distributor, or corporate parent is a relationship signal, not proof that two legal entities are interchangeable. This ordering avoids enriching hundreds of irrelevant subsidiaries before the desk knows which entity can actually sign.

Give each play a different evidence packet and commercial question. The patent packet contains the claim set, family map, assignment chain, prosecution events, and a technically supported use hypothesis; keyword overlap is insufficient. The brand packet contains registration documents, actual market use, licensed categories, territory, channel restrictions, and a workable product-review process. The catalog packet distinguishes composition from master rights, identifies writers and performers where relevant, and records ownership shares, territory, media, term, and any required third-party permissions. Songview and The MLC help reconcile composition metadata, but neither replaces a synchronization license or a master-use agreement. The dataset packet records every upstream source, collection date, source terms, version, redistribution permission, attribution requirement, and deletion dependency. Public availability does not itself authorize commercial redistribution. Under 17 U.S.C. 102(b), copyright does not extend to ideas, procedures, systems, or methods; a claimed dataset right therefore needs a precise basis rather than a sweeping assertion that all facts are owned. Contract restrictions and other applicable rights must be analyzed separately. Write the proposed grant in plain operational terms before pricing it, because enrichment cannot repair an incoherent description of the asset or the permitted use.

Operate the waterfall through a weekly exception queue. The full-time operator selects commercially credible targets and owns counterpart communications. The rights contractor resolves ownership gaps and flags questions requiring specialist interpretation. The engineering contractor maintains connectors, matching rules, and freshness reports. The royalty contractor checks whether the contemplated reporting fields can support the proposed fee structure. Batch ordinary research, but give exclusive-rights conflicts and contradictory transfers immediate attention. Store enrichment cost per qualified rights packet, not merely cost per email address. A cheap contact record becomes expensive when the desk spends two hours approaching the wrong owner. Use a stop-loss rule: after the agreed research budget is consumed, record the unresolved issue and request documents directly rather than purchasing another nearly identical profile. Maintain a suppression list for contacts who decline outreach and for invalid addresses. For commercial email, implement the sender identification, truthful routing, postal address, and opt-out requirements that apply under 15 U.S.C. 7704. Keep outreach permission and asset-licensing permission as separate records. A responsive executive may lack signing authority; a verified mailbox establishes neither authority nor ownership. Measure the queue by resolved blockers and qualified conversations, not raw enrichment volume.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Spend on missing evidence, not redundant profiles**
> 
> Experienced licensing desks use enrichment to shorten a specific decision, not to decorate an incomplete rights file.
> - Search predecessor names and assignment schedules before assuming that the current registry owner is the complete contracting story.
> - For music, retain both ISWC and ISRC where relevant; matching a song title never clears both composition and recording rights.
> - Read EDGAR license exhibits for audit windows, deduction definitions, and minimum-guarantee mechanics, while treating redacted schedules as evidence gaps.
> - Ask dataset suppliers for one versioned provenance manifest and one worked downstream-use example before buying a broad commercial data subscription.

## 3. Build a reproducible evidence pipeline with visible exceptions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a small evidence architecture with explicit lineage. SQLite is sufficient for an initial desk if one controlled writer handles imports; PostgreSQL becomes useful when concurrent work and access separation justify the operational burden. Store entities, assets, source observations, ownership assertions, contracts, reporting periods, and exceptions as separate tables. An ownership assertion must reference evidence, scope, and effective dates; it must not overwrite the raw registry observation. Keep original PDFs, CSVs, and JSON responses in versioned object storage with access controls and retention aligned to source permissions. Calculate SHA-256 digests using Python's hashlib, but remember that a digest detects later changes rather than proving that the original claim was true. Use deterministic record keys and ingestion identifiers so rerunning a job does not duplicate licenses or obligations. Separate retrieved_at, source_updated_at, effective_from, and effective_to timestamps. Preserve currency, units, timezone, and source precision rather than normalizing them away without a trace. Every dashboard assertion should resolve to the underlying observation and document. This structure makes a rights dispute or royalty challenge answerable through evidence, instead of requiring the operator to reconstruct months of decisions from email threads. Keep correction history append-only where source permissions allow.

Build connectors as short, testable adapters. Python's urllib.request, csv, json, sqlite3, decimal, and unittest can support a lean first implementation without third-party dependencies. Configure credentials outside source files, redact them from logs, and separate read access from administrative permissions. A connector should emit a source identifier, retrieval timestamp, response digest, schema version, and explicit success or failure state. Validate required fields before loading transformed tables. A provider returning an HTML login page with a successful HTTP status must fail content validation rather than enter the warehouse as usable data. Handle pagination with saved checkpoints and deduplicate on stable identifiers. Use conditional requests where supported, bounded retries for transient failures, and immediate escalation for revoked credentials. Diagnose HTTP behavior with curl or Postman against authorized endpoints, inspect certificates with OpenSSL, and use SQL EXPLAIN when queries slow down. For document comparisons, use difflib on extracted text and retain the original page reference. OCR output is a search aid, not the governing contract text. A single misread decimal or omitted exception can reverse an obligation, so verify extracted rates and deadlines against the signed document before activating calculations or alerts.

Define refresh cadence by decision risk rather than by the availability of a scheduler. Recheck title and status before an offer, signature, renewal, or material expansion of territory; monitor active assets at a cadence matched to the relevant source and contract. Refresh royalty reporting when a statement arrives, then reconcile it against the agreed delivery deadline. Store renewal, notice, cure, audit, and minimum-guarantee dates as structured obligations with named owners and separate reminder offsets. Calendar reminders alone cannot distinguish an expiring license from an auto-renewal requiring timely cancellation. The engineering contractor runs daily connector health checks, while the operator reviews only changes that alter a decision or require action. Test against frozen sample records: renamed owners, split territories, duplicate statement uploads, missing pages, refunded sales, and expired API keys. Track source age, parse failures, unmatched identifiers, and unresolved obligations on one exception board. Require each exception to carry an owner, next action, and due date. If a source disappears, the desk should know which conclusions depend on it and which manual checks preserve deal continuity. This dependency map is more valuable than another dashboard showing total records collected without any indication of decision readiness.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the source and its permitted use
   - Record official access documentation, account scope, retention terms, quotas, and fallback procedure.
   - Assign one connector owner and distinguish public observations from private counterparty documents.
2. Capture a reproducible evidence snapshot
   - Save the authorized response or document with timestamps, identifiers, version information, and a SHA-256 digest.
   - Preserve the original representation and record any extraction or normalization applied afterward.
3. Validate the decision-bearing fields
   - Test identifiers, title links, territorial scope, rates, reporting periods, and date interpretations.
   - Route contradictions to the rights or royalty contractor instead of silently choosing a convenient value.
4. Publish bounded conclusions and schedule rechecks
   - Attach evidence links, freshness limits, obligation owners, and next-action dates to the operator view.
   - Recheck material rights before signature and preserve an audit trail of later corrections.

## 4. Reconcile royalty evidence to the scorecard without counting licensee sales  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The pipeline earns its keep when it converts a signed definition into a repeatable statement calculation. Suppose a brand agreement pays five percent of defined net sales, and an accepted annual report shows $2,000,000 of eligible sales after contractually permitted deductions. The computed royalty is $100,000, not $2,000,000 of operator revenue. If that same annual period carries a $60,000 recoupable minimum guarantee already paid and credited against those royalties, the remaining royalty balance is $40,000; adding the guarantee again would overstate the economics. Actual recognition timing follows the agreement and the applicable accounting treatment, so store earned amounts, invoiced amounts, cash receipts, and guarantee credits separately. Apply equivalent discipline to patent royalties per covered unit, catalog fees per permitted exploitation, and dataset subscriptions by authorized delivery or usage tier. A perpetual license, a refundable advance, and an annual access fee do not automatically share the same recognition pattern. Use Decimal arithmetic, explicit currency codes, contract-specific rounding, and documented exchange-rate conventions. The royalty contractor owns the calculation specification; the engineer implements it; the operator approves commercial exceptions. Keep every override attributable and reversible without deleting the original statement or its calculation inputs.

Reconcile three layers: the licensee's operational ledger, the contractual royalty base, and the desk's recognized revenue. Obtain only the records authorized by reporting and audit provisions, using customer-approved exports rather than assuming access to storefront or accounting systems. Shopify order exports, Stripe balance and transaction reports, and QuickBooks Online ledger extracts can support different reconciliation tasks, but payment settlements are not automatically the royalty base. Taxes, shipping, refunds, bundles, intercompany transfers, foreign exchange, and channel timing require the actual contract definition. Match sampled transactions to SKU mappings and permitted territories, then trace totals to the submitted statement. For catalog licenses, test the reported media and exploitation period; for datasets, test delivery versions, authorized seats, and any contractually measurable usage. Investigate underreporting through documented exceptions and the agreed audit process, not covert access to licensee systems. Preserve the contractual notice period and record-retention window so a valid discrepancy does not become an unusable claim through delay. Where terms affect exclusivity, territorial allocation, tying, or competitor coordination, review the actual arrangement against applicable competition rules, including 15 U.S.C. 1. Ownership of an asset does not remove the need to evaluate the surrounding commercial restrictions.

Keep the chapter's economics anchored to Candidate #19. Conservative recognized revenue is $50,000 at a twenty-five percent net margin, producing $12,500 annual owner net profit and $37,500 of total implied costs. Base revenue is $425,000 at forty-two and one-half percent, producing $180,625 profit and $244,375 of implied costs. Scaled revenue is $800,000 at sixty percent, producing $480,000 profit and $320,000 of implied costs. These are the three canonical cases, not outputs of the illustrative brand agreement above. Conservative and Base retain one full-time operator plus three contractors; Scaled uses two full-time operators plus four contractors. Startup capital of $12,000 to $75,000 and monthly operating outlays of $2,000 to $12,000 describe planning ranges, not a complete reconciliation of every annual scenario cost. Do not force those ranges to replace the implied total expense figures. With six to twenty-four months to a first deal, buy expensive commercial coverage only when qualified opportunities justify it. Review connector maintenance hours, enrichment spend, statement rework, and rights-exception backlog monthly. Add capacity when verified obligations and deal throughput demand it, rather than treating automation as a reason to multiply unqualified targets or subscribe to every available database.
