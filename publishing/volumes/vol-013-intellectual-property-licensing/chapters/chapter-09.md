## 1. Establish transferable rights before offering any of the four plays  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build one rights register before building a prospect list. Candidate #19 has four mapped plays: patent field-of-use licensing, brand licensing, catalog rights licensing, and permissioned dataset licensing. Every offer must identify the actual owner, the instrument granting your authority, the asset schedule, and the restrictions surviving earlier transactions. For patents, collect executed inventor assignments, employment invention agreements, recorded transfers, maintenance status, and existing licenses. Under 35 U.S.C. § 261, assignments require a written instrument; timely recordation protects against specified later purchasers or mortgagees without notice. USPTO Assignment Center is evidence infrastructure, not a substitute for reading the instruments. Check joint ownership under 35 U.S.C. § 262 because, absent an agreement otherwise, a joint owner can exploit or license the patent without accounting to the others. For brands, connect registrations and common-law rights to the operating entity and the relevant goods or services. For catalogs, separate the underlying work from the recording, photograph, edition, or other embodiment. For datasets, trace collection, contributor permissions, supplier contracts, and restrictions on redistribution or model training. A seller's invoice proves a transaction, not ownership of every embedded right.

Treat each play as a different permission architecture rather than reusing one universal license. Patent schedules should identify issued claims, application families, jurisdictions, expiration assumptions, and the licensed acts of making, using, selling, offering for sale, or importing. A pending application is not an issued exclusionary right. Brand licenses require enforceable quality standards, sample review, inspection rights, and documented corrective action; uncontrolled licensing can undermine trademark rights. Catalog licenses should enumerate reproduction, distribution, adaptation, public performance, and display rights under 17 U.S.C. § 106, along with media, languages, territories, and duration. A musical composition and its sound recording usually require separate rights analysis. Exclusive copyright transfers require a signed writing under 17 U.S.C. § 204, and statutory termination under §§ 203 and 304 can defeat assumptions about perpetual grants. Dataset contracts should distinguish protectable selection or arrangement from underlying facts, personal information, confidential know-how, and third-party materials. Download access does not establish permission to resell, sublicense, or train a model. Require an asset-specific clearance memo that explains exactly which rights support the proposed commercial use and which uses remain excluded.

Keep the Base pod small by assigning accountable decisions rather than building a miniature law firm. The one full-time operator owns pricing, prospecting, negotiations, and the authoritative deal register. Contractor one is qualified IP counsel responsible for title exceptions, statutory interpretation, and negotiated drafting. Contractor two is a rights analyst who assembles instruments, verifies asset identifiers, and maintains permission evidence. Contractor three is a royalty accountant who designs reporting definitions, reconciles statements, and tests underpayments. These are roles within the 1 FT + 3 C constraint, not four additional departments. Airtable can hold the searchable rights register, while a restricted Google Drive or SharePoint repository holds signed instruments and source evidence. Give every document an asset identifier, version, effective date, owner, and review date. Store hashes of delivered catalogs or dataset manifests where versions matter. The operator may draft a commercial term sheet but must not promise exclusive rights while a prior grant remains unresolved. Maintain a short exception queue with an accountable reviewer and a proposed resolution. Stop only the affected asset or use; unrelated cleared inventory can continue through the pipeline without consuming the entire pod's capacity.

## 2. Convert scope, payment, and enforcement into executable contract clauses  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start drafting with a scope schedule that a royalty accountant can apply without interpreting a sales presentation. Identify licensed assets, permitted acts, fields of use, territory, channels, affiliates, sublicensing rights, duration, and reserved rights. Define whether exclusivity is absolute, channel-specific, or contingent on minimum performance, and specify the remedy when minimums are missed. A conversion to nonexclusive status can preserve revenue without forcing an immediate termination dispute. Put ownership of improvements, derivative works, annotations, fine-tuned models, and feedback in an express allocation rather than assuming that delivery resolves title. For data, distinguish source records, customer inputs, generated outputs, and usage telemetry. State permitted retention and deletion duties separately for production systems, backups, and legally retained records. Anti-circumvention language should target an identifiable commercial problem, such as routing licensed sales through an affiliate to evade royalties. Define covered transactions, duration, attribution, and remedies; avoid a vague ban on all contact with an entire industry. Do not confuse that negotiated covenant with technological-measure circumvention under 17 U.S.C. § 1201. Counsel should test restrictive terms against the governing state's restraint rules and applicable competition principles.

Define the fee trigger before discussing the royalty percentage. An upfront access fee, an advance recoupable against royalties, an annual minimum, and an earned sales royalty create different obligations. Specify currency, taxes, withholding documentation, invoice timing, payment dates, interest where enforceable, and permitted deductions from the royalty base. Returns, discounts, freight, bundles, affiliate transfers, and bad debts need explicit treatment. Illustratively, a 2% royalty on $1,000,000 of eligible licensee sales produces $20,000 of earned royalties, not $1,000,000 of operator revenue. If a $10,000 advance is fully creditable against those royalties, the remaining payment is $10,000, not another $20,000; recognition timing still depends on the performance obligations and applicable accounting policy. Keep this transaction example separate from the canonical annual scenarios. Conservative revenue is $50,000 with $37,500 of costs and $12,500 owner profit at 25%. Base revenue is $425,000 with $244,375 of costs and $180,625 owner profit at 42.5%. Scaled revenue is $800,000 with $320,000 of costs and $480,000 owner profit at 60%. Neither advances nor minimums justify double-counting earned royalties. The royalty accountant maintains separate cash, receivable, deferred-revenue, and earned-revenue records as applicable.

Allocate dispute risk to the party that controls it, then make the remedy usable. Specify title and authority representations, known encumbrance disclosures, infringement-claim notification, defense control, settlement restrictions, and cooperation duties. Limit any indemnity by clearly defining covered claims and exclusions, including unauthorized modifications or combinations where appropriate. A liability cap needs a measurable base and lookback period; negotiate payment obligations, confidentiality breaches, misuse, and indemnities expressly rather than leaving conflicting exceptions across exhibits. Choose governing law, forum or arbitration rules, service mechanics, interim-relief rights, and cost allocation. The Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. § 2, supports covered written arbitration agreements subject to applicable defenses; it does not cure an incoherent clause. Include cure periods, suspension rights, termination triggers, sell-off limits, post-termination reporting, and surviving audit obligations. For patent rights, separate compensation for pre-expiration activity from royalties on post-expiration patent use; hybrid know-how deals need deliberate allocation and counsel review. Track bankruptcy issues under 11 U.S.C. § 365(n), whose defined IP categories do not simply include trademarks. The operator routes unusual exceptions to counsel instead of improvising during a closing call, and the rights analyst records the final clause version.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Draft the accounting examples before negotiating the headline royalty**
> 
> A compact pod wins leverage through repeatable definitions and verifiable evidence, not through longer agreements.
> - Attach three worked royalty examples covering a standard sale, a discounted bundle, and an affiliate transfer; make the agreed definitions control any arithmetic conflict.
> - Tie brand sample approvals to a versioned specification and inspection record so quality control remains demonstrable after personnel change.
> - For patent-plus-know-how packages, have counsel separate rights, consideration, and expiration mechanics rather than copying one perpetual royalty provision.
> - Hash each delivered dataset manifest or catalog inventory and reference that version in the acceptance record, royalty schedule, and renewal notice.

## 3. Apply territorial, sector, and audit rules without expanding the pod  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build a jurisdiction matrix around actual activity, not merely the contract's governing-law sentence. Patent protection is territorial; a United States patent does not create worldwide exclusion rights. Trademark coverage varies by territory and goods, while copyright clearance may require collecting-society or other locally administered permissions for particular uses. Within the United States, dataset licensing triggers different state analyses depending on residency, coverage thresholds, processing purpose, and party roles. California Civil Code §§ 1798.100 and 1798.140 establish important notice, contracting, and definitional requirements for covered businesses and recipients. Calling a buyer a service provider cannot cure a transaction that actually permits independent commercial reuse. Virginia Code §§ 59.1-575 and 59.1-579 distinguish controllers and processors and require processor contracts where applicable. Illinois BIPA, 740 ILCS 14/15, imposes specific duties for covered biometric collection and disclosure and prohibits covered private entities from selling, leasing, trading, or otherwise profiting from biometric identifiers or biometric information. Permission to collect therefore does not automatically create a licensable resale asset. The rights analyst records affected jurisdictions and intended uses; counsel resolves coverage, exemptions, and incompatible promises before the operator prices the transaction.

Sector restrictions can invalidate an otherwise elegant dataset offer. HIPAA applies to covered entities, business associates, and protected health information within its scope, not to every health-related spreadsheet. Where applicable, use the de-identification pathways in 45 CFR § 164.514 or analyze a permitted disclosure with the required instruments; a limited data set under § 164.514(e) still requires a data use agreement and is not unrestricted public inventory. Consumer reporting uses require separate Fair Credit Reporting Act analysis, including permissible purposes under 15 U.S.C. § 1681b. Public availability does not establish suitability for employment or credit screening. For government-funded inventions or deliverables, inspect award terms, retained government rights, and applicable FAR or DFARS clauses before promising exclusive commercial control. Export-controlled technical data requires a separate classification and access analysis before foreign-person disclosure. Competition review runs across all four plays: Sherman Act §§ 1 and 2 remain relevant to market allocation, anticompetitive tying, coordinated pricing, and exclusionary arrangements. A license does not immunize restraints, but a limited field-of-use grant is not automatically unlawful. Counsel evaluates market context, the actual restraint, and plausible effects rather than treating all exclusivity as either forbidden or harmless.

Make royalty reporting reproducible before the first invoice. Require period, asset identifier, licensee entity, territory, channel, units, gross sales, allowed deductions, royalty rate, credits, and amount due in a stable CSV schema. Define how zero-sales periods are reported. The royalty accountant reconciles the statement to supporting ledgers and selected invoices, while the operator follows material exceptions instead of inspecting every row. Contract for record retention, audit frequency, notice, independent auditor access, confidentiality, underpayment remedies, and an agreed cost-shifting threshold. A negotiated 5% underpayment threshold is a contract choice, not a statutory entitlement. For brand deals, add quality-control evidence; for catalogs, reconcile usage logs; for patents, validate covered products and fields; for datasets, check authorized recipients and permitted-purpose attestations without collecting unnecessary personal records. Use Airtable automations for reporting deadlines and renewal alerts, and reconcile invoice statuses in QuickBooks Online or Xero. The Base pod remains 1 FT + 3 C. At Scaled, 2 FT + 4 C adds operational capacity without changing the $800,000 revenue basis or $480,000 owner profit target. Automation should surface missing statements and contradictions; it must never silently convert disputed usage into accepted earned revenue.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the rights and use specification
   - Record the asset version, recipient entities, territories, channels, and requested downstream uses.
   - The rights analyst links title instruments and source permissions; the operator confirms that the specification matches the priced offer.
2. Resolve statutory and contract exceptions
   - Have counsel classify ownership, privacy, sector, competition, and territorial issues material to that specification.
   - Record the disposition, required clause changes, and excluded uses; unresolved assets remain outside the signed schedule.
3. Test the reporting and payment mechanics
   - Run the accountant's standard-sale, bundle, advance-credit, and affiliate-transfer examples through the proposed royalty definitions.
   - Reconcile earned fees, payment credits, deductions, and invoice timing without treating licensee product sales as operator revenue.
4. Activate the signed obligation register
   - Load reporting dates, quality inspections, audit windows, renewal notices, and termination duties into the deal register.
   - The operator owns exceptions; the accountant owns statement reconciliation; the analyst maintains asset and permission versions.

## 4. Use precise outreach and discovery scripts that never overstate rights  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run outbound email as a documented commercial process under CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. § 7704. Business-to-business email is not categorically exempt. Use accurate routing information, a truthful subject, clear commercial identification where required, a valid physical postal address, and a working opt-out method. Keep that method operational for at least thirty days after transmission and honor qualifying opt-outs within ten business days. Do not charge for removal or require unnecessary information. Before using the following exact body, append the operator's actual postal address and functioning unsubscribe mechanism: 'Subject: Commercial licensing inquiry for your product team. This is a commercial licensing inquiry. We represent a documented portfolio available for defined commercial uses. May I send a nonconfidential summary showing the assets, territories, permitted uses, and proposed fee structure? A discussion does not authorize use or imply exclusivity. If this is outside your remit, a referral to the licensing owner would help. Reply stop to end these messages.' Use only when representation authority exists and reply-based removal is monitored. HubSpot or Pipedrive should store source, outreach basis, date, asset interest, and suppression status. Never infer permission to receive automated calls from a publicly displayed business email address.

Use a discovery sequence that produces a draftable scope, not vague enthusiasm. Open with: 'Before we discuss rates, which legal entity would license the rights, where would it operate, and what would customers receive?' For patents, ask: 'Which products and manufacturing steps need coverage, and which countries involve making, selling, or importing?' For brands, ask: 'Which marks, goods, factories, channels, and sample-review process would you accept?' For catalogs, ask: 'Which works, media, territories, languages, edits, and release dates are planned, and who clears separately controlled rights?' For datasets, ask: 'Will access support internal analytics, redistribution, model training, or individual-level decisions, and which affiliates or vendors will receive records?' Close with: 'Who can provide quarterly usage reports, approve an audit provision, and confirm the payment entity?' The operator records answers directly against the rights schedule rather than accepting a generic procurement description. Do not send confidential samples until the disclosure purpose, recipients, security controls, and applicable confidentiality terms are settled. An NDA protects specified disclosures; it does not repair missing title or grant downstream exploitation rights. Where a sample can reveal personal information or trade secrets, prefer synthetic examples or a cleared descriptive schema.

Respond to overbroad requests without manufacturing authority: 'We can quote the documented rights in the attached schedule. Worldwide exclusivity, sublicensing, and training rights require separate confirmation; they are not included in this discussion.' For audit resistance, say: 'We can narrow inspection to records relevant to the licensed activity, protect confidential material, and use an independent accountant. We still need a reproducible royalty base and a remedy for material underpayment.' For an opt-out, reply: 'Your address has been removed from our marketing outreach. We will retain only the suppression information needed to prevent further marketing messages.' Keep required contract administration separate from promotional follow-up. Before adding calls or texts, review 47 U.S.C. § 227 and 47 CFR § 64.1200 against the number type, technology, message purpose, applicable consent requirements, and federal or state restrictions. Do not assume a business relationship authorizes prerecorded marketing or that manual dialing eliminates every restriction. The full-time operator owns outbound accuracy; counsel reviews new channels, the analyst controls claim substantiation, and the accountant validates quoted economics. Keep the 6–24 month first-deal horizon visible: disciplined qualification protects the $25,000–$250,000 startup capital range better than unsupported promises intended to accelerate a signature.
