## 1. Protect the reserve before buying the platform  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Allocate capital against the expected delay to signed, collectible licenses, not against the apparent size of the intellectual property portfolio. The prescribed startup range is $25,000 to $250,000, while the first deal can take six to twenty-four months. Hold 60% in a separately tracked working-capital reserve: $15,000 at the lean entry point, $75,000 at a $125,000 midpoint, and $150,000 at the upper end. Commit the remaining 40% through small purchase orders for rights diligence, contracting, demand validation, infrastructure, formation, and insurance. These are spending ceilings, not instructions to exhaust each envelope. At $2,000 monthly burn, the smallest reserve buys only 7.5 months before collections; it cannot support a twenty-four-month wait. At $5,000 monthly burn, the midpoint reserve buys fifteen months; at $15,000 monthly burn, the upper reserve buys ten. Extend runway through narrower rights packages, deferred nonessential purchases, paid pilots, or additional committed capital before taking obligations that outlast available cash. Keep customer advances and royalties payable separately visible because restricted or contractually owed money does not enlarge the operator's freely spendable reserve. Forecast collections weekly and refresh a thirteen-week cash schedule every Friday.

The budget matrix uses the same allocation percentages at every capital level so procurement decisions remain comparable. A patent-heavy portfolio may need more title review than a small owned-image catalog; move funds between deployment envelopes without reducing the reserve below 50%. Buy an initial counsel scope that answers specific questions: who owns the asset, who can license it, which fields and territories are available, and what prior grants constrain exclusivity. Do not purchase an expensive patent valuation merely to decorate a pitch deck. Require a buyer interview and a plausible permitted-use package before commissioning extensive claim mapping. For catalogs and datasets, sample provenance records before paying to ingest every item. Release the contracting envelope after the first qualified buyer confirms a use case and purchasing authority. Release the infrastructure envelope against a tested reporting workflow rather than vendor demonstrations. Insurance budgets are allowances, not insurer quotations; obtain actual terms before accepting indemnities. Startup cash classifications also differ from accounting treatment: an acquisition, prepaid premium, and current professional expense may land differently in the books. Have the bookkeeper map each payment to its proper account instead of treating all deployment as recurring overhead.

Reconcile operating ambition to the canonical scorecard before adding staff. Conservative revenue of $50,000 less $37,500 of total annual costs leaves $12,500, exactly 25%. Base revenue of $425,000 less $244,375 leaves $180,625, exactly 42.5%. Scaled revenue of $800,000 less $320,000 leaves $480,000, exactly 60%. A common $2,000 monthly fixed operating floor uses $24,000 annually, leaving cost allowances of $13,500, $220,375, and $296,000 respectively for everything outside that floor. Those residuals must cover the applicable contractor, delivery, audit, collection, and other expenses rather than being presented as additional profit. Conversely, a $15,000 monthly fixed stack costs $180,000 annually and cannot fit the Conservative case. Team counts mean one full-time operator plus three contractors in Conservative and Base, then two full-time people plus four contractors in Scaled; they do not imply six salaried employees. Keep contractor hours variable until contracted receipts justify recurring commitments. Reserve funding itself is neither revenue nor an expense. Avoid counting a prepaid annual subscription again when cash leaves the reserve; maintain separate cash and accrual views with a single underlying transaction ledger.

## 2. Configure twelve layers without twelve enterprise subscriptions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The infrastructure matrix names twelve functional layers, not twelve mandatory paid subscriptions. Its exact dollar figures are planning inputs for the named tiers, excluding tax, domain registration, equipment, hosting, and implementation labor; confirm current regional checkout terms before purchasing. Google Workspace Business Starter is budgeted at $7 per user monthly on an annual commitment, Bitwarden Teams at $4 per user monthly billed annually, and Amazon S3 Standard at $0.023 per gigabyte monthly for the first storage tier in US East, Northern Virginia. For two users and 100 gigabytes, those components total $24.30 monthly before request, retrieval, transfer, and other charges. That small subtotal is not the business's monthly operating cost; counsel, bookkeeping, insurance, hosting, and contractor support dominate the $2,000 to $15,000 envelope. Free software does not supply free administration. Prefer a single managed workspace and a minimal deployment over maintaining several servers simply to avoid a small subscription. Keep customer source files out of issue comments and email attachments. Record each vendor's data location, administrator, renewal date, export format, and offboarding procedure in the same obligation register used for customer licenses.

Build around an immutable agreement identifier that links the signed instrument, asset manifest, permitted uses, rate schedule, reporting period, invoice, and collection record. PostgreSQL can hold normalized royalty lines while Paperless-ngx indexes executed documents and supporting title records. Use DocuSeal Community for signatures only after testing signer identity evidence, timestamps, completion records, and exports against the transaction's requirements. An electronic signature tool does not establish that the signatory actually controls the rights. Use Python decimal arithmetic for monetary calculations, explicit currency codes, and stored exchange-rate sources; binary floating-point totals invite avoidable reconciliation differences. Preserve original licensee reports and calculate adjustments in separate versioned records. A changed rate should create a dated amendment, not overwrite history. For a 3% royalty on $120,000 of contract-defined eligible sales, record $3,600 before applying any separately documented credits or offsets. Reject duplicate report periods and quarantine reports containing unexplained negative quantities. Notify the operator thirty, sixty, and ninety days before renewal or notice deadlines, with a second person receiving unresolved exceptions. Automation should surface contractual obligations; it should never silently expand permitted uses or waive a reporting breach.

Security expenditure should follow the value of the evidence and the harm of an erroneous license. Require multifactor authentication for email, storage, signatures, banking, and administrator access; use individual accounts rather than a shared founder password. Separate read-only contractor access from authority to change rates or bank details. GitHub Free can track schema and calculation changes in private repositories, but raw customer datasets and privileged correspondence belong in controlled storage rather than source control. S3 versioning and carefully configured Object Lock can preserve selected evidence, although retention settings require deliberate cost and deletion planning. Maintain an independent backup and perform a quarterly restore into an isolated location. A successful backup job is insufficient evidence that records are recoverable. Make one test agreement traverse intake, signature, obligation creation, report import, calculation, invoice export, and archive before onboarding a paying buyer. Have a contractor follow the written recovery procedure without the founder's assistance. Stop purchasing features when the critical path works; add integrations only when measured error reduction or labor savings exceed their total cost. Keep the manual spreadsheet fallback operational so an integration outage does not suspend invoicing or contractual notice delivery.

## 3. Fund four rights-specific plays with explicit downside limits  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Treat the four mapped plays as distinct underwriting paths, not interchangeable inventory. Patent field-of-use licensing starts with an executed assignment chain, maintenance status, claim relevance, and a carefully bounded field and territory. An illustrative $25,000 access license less $8,000 of title and technical diligence and $4,000 of contracting contributes $13,000 before shared overhead; it is not a replacement for the canonical profit cases. If claim coverage or buyer demand fails, stop that patent package and offer separately identified know-how only where the operator actually controls it. Brand minimum-guarantee licensing requires product-category boundaries, quality specifications, specimen review, and a functioning inspection process. At 5% of $600,000 eligible licensee sales, earned royalties equal $30,000. A $20,000 recoupable guarantee therefore leaves $10,000 beyond the guarantee, not $50,000 of combined revenue. If projected sell-through does not support the guarantee, reduce the territory or offer a short paid pilot rather than accepting broad exclusivity for an uneconomic advance. Review patent term limitations and competitive restrictions with specialist counsel before drafting long-lived royalty obligations. An attractive royalty percentage cannot repair a grant the licensor lacked authority to make.

Dataset subscription licensing turns provenance and permitted processing into the product. Four customers paying $12,000 annually produce $48,000 of illustrative subscription revenue; if curation costs $8,000 and delivery costs $4,000, contribution before shared overhead is $36,000. Define whether the license permits internal analytics, redistribution, model training, derived outputs, or only a specified evaluation. Keep personal information outside the initial package unless collection authority and the proposed processing have been established. Facts alone do not become exclusively owned copyright merely because they were assembled into a file; distinguish protectable selection or arrangement, contractual access restrictions, and third-party rights. If provenance fails, remove affected records or substitute a demonstrably authorized corpus rather than promising rights the operator cannot substantiate. Catalog licensing similarly requires asset-level grants and releases, including separate analysis of musical compositions and sound recordings. Price the support burden: four quarterly reports from four customers create sixteen report reviews per year; at ninety minutes each, budget twenty-four review hours before exception handling. This modest workload is a better reason to retain a spreadsheet fallback than to commission a custom portal before customer demand is established.

The required copyright_phash_reverse_image_statutory_settlement play belongs to TRACK_2 and starts with authorized monitoring for a represented rights holder, not automated accusations. Perceptual image hashes and audio fingerprints identify candidates; similarity does not establish infringement, ownership, or statutory-damages eligibility. In an illustrative monthly batch, 1,000 machine matches might yield forty human reviews at fifteen minutes each, or ten hours. At $75 per hour, review costs $750; two additional counsel hours at $350 add $700, for $1,450 before acquisition and storage. Budget zero settlement receipts until an executed resolution and the applicable recognition requirements support revenue. Counsel checks assignments, existing licenses, public-domain status, exceptions including fair use, registration timing, and standing before any communication asserting liability. Under 17 U.S.C. 412, registration timing can foreclose statutory damages and attorney fees even where a claim otherwise exists. Withdraw unsupported demands and retain matching only for authorized monitoring and voluntary licensing. Preserve original files, retrieval dates, source locations, and review decisions; a hash score alone is inadequate evidence. Route claimant receipts and operator compensation separately, recognizing only amounts the operator earns under the representation agreement and the applicable accounting treatment.

## 4. Verify the entity, money path, and first live report  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Create a clean boundary between the operator, represented rights holders, and licensees before sending the first invoice. Form the appropriate entity, obtain an EIN directly from the IRS, document ownership and signing authority, and open a business deposit account using the bank's required identity and formation records. Keep personal spending outside that account and reconcile activity monthly. If the operator licenses assets it owns, document the transfer into the contracting entity; if it acts for another owner, maintain an executed agency or representation agreement defining negotiation authority, collection authority, compensation, and termination. A deposit account title cannot cure a broken copyright assignment. Verify wire instructions through an independently established contact channel, especially after an emailed change request. Check current federal and state filing requirements for the actual entity type and ownership rather than copying a stale formation checklist. Royalty payments to foreign persons can require withholding and documentation analysis before payment. Obtain appropriate tax forms and route cross-border structures to the accountant before quoting a net payment amount. Maintain a rights-holder payable ledger whenever collections include money owed to someone else.

Insurance selection follows contract exposure rather than a generic small-business shopping list. Ask a broker to compare professional liability, media liability, cyber coverage, and general liability against the actual activities and license forms. Examine intellectual-property exclusions, territorial limits, defense-cost treatment, retention amounts, and whether claims-made coverage needs continuity or a reporting extension. A policy described as media coverage may still exclude patent claims. Do not promise an uncapped infringement indemnity because a buyer's template requests one. Align warranties with documented rights, negotiate appropriate remedies, and allocate review funds before accepting unusually broad downstream use. Title diligence should distinguish ownership from freedom to operate: a patent owner can still encounter another party's blocking rights, and a photograph's copyright does not automatically supply publicity or trademark permissions. Under 15 U.S.C. 1055, controlled use by related companies is relevant to trademark ownership and use; operational quality control must therefore survive beyond signature. Under 15 U.S.C. 1, restrictive licensing terms warrant competition review where they restrain trade. These issues become concrete contract schedules, inspection duties, and escalation assignments rather than decorative policy statements.

Pre-launch readiness is demonstrated through artifacts, not a meeting where everyone declares confidence. Assemble one complete deal folder containing the title chain, asset manifest, permitted-use schedule, pricing definition, executed agreement, insurance analysis, and collection instructions. Reperform the first royalty calculation independently from the source report. For the Base bridge, five separate $25,000 earned access fees provide $125,000 and 3% of $10 million in eligible licensee sales provides $300,000; together they equal $425,000. For Scaled, eight such fees provide $200,000 and 3% of $20 million provides $600,000; together they equal $800,000. These bridges assume access fees are separately earned and royalties are incremental, rather than recoupable advances counted twice. Recognition timing still follows the actual performance obligations and accounting policy. Test a late report, a disputed deduction, a currency conversion, and a termination notice through the workflow. Require a named owner and response deadline for each exception. Launch only the rights package that passes these checks; leave uncertain assets outside the schedule. Review reserve coverage, receivable aging, and upcoming notice deadlines weekly during the first quarter of operation.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Open the reserve and spending register
   - Assign 60% of startup capital to working reserves and approve scoped deployment envelopes.
   - Record committed cash separately from paid cash and model collections under six-, twelve-, and twenty-four-month delays.
2. Package only documented rights
   - Build the title chain, asset manifest, permitted-use schedule, and buyer-specific commercial offer.
   - Remove unsupported assets and route patent, trademark, copyright, and dataset limitations to the appropriate specialist.
3. Run the agreement through the lean stack
   - Execute a test document, import a sample report, calculate royalties, and export an invoice.
   - Test duplicate periods, negative adjustments, guarantee recoupment, reporting exceptions, and thirty-, sixty-, and ninety-day reminders.
4. Release the verified package and monitor cash
   - Confirm execution authority, verified collection details, appropriate coverage, and a successful evidence restore.
   - Review receivable aging and runway weekly; expand tools or contractor hours only against documented workload and collectible contracts.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four checks before assets or invoices leave the business**
> 
> Each check produces retained evidence and a named exception owner. Passing one asset package does not authorize unrelated rights.
> - Verify the signed title chain or representation authority against every asset, territory, field, and proposed sublicense permission.
> - Reconcile the cash forecast to a reserve of at least 50% of initial capital before accepting discretionary startup commitments.
> - Recompute one invoice independently, including guarantee recoupment, deductions, currency treatment, and rights-holder payables.
> - Restore the executed deal folder and test renewal notices, contractor access removal, and independently verified bank instructions.
