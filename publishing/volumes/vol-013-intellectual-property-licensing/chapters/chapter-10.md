## 1. Build One Evidence Ledger for Four Licensing Plays  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with four explicit operator plays rather than one generic licensing funnel: patent field-of-use licenses, brand category licenses, catalog reproduction or distribution licenses, and dataset access licenses. Each play shares an asset identifier, counterparty identifier, executed agreement, territory, permitted use, effective dates, reporting calendar, and evidence bundle. Each also needs different proof. Patent records require ownership documents, claim scope, jurisdiction, and remaining term; brand records require category restrictions and quality-control duties. Catalog records distinguish composition, recording, image, and other relevant rights instead of assuming one clearance covers everything. Dataset records distinguish ownership assertions from source permissions, privacy restrictions, and redistribution limits. Store normalized records in PostgreSQL or, for an initial single-writer implementation, SQLite through Python's sqlite3 module. Keep signed documents in restricted object storage and record their SHA-256 digests. A search result establishes a lead, not authority to license. USPTO Assignment Center and Copyright Office records help locate evidence, but public recordation alone does not establish a complete chain of title. The full-time operator owns commercial decisions; three contractors cover rights review, automation and royalty operations, and channel development. Their scopes must state response times, review limits, and who can substitute during a reporting deadline.

Implement intake as a durable event pipeline, not a chain of unrecoverable spreadsheet edits. An HTTPS receiver accepts uploads or supported vendor notifications, verifies the provider's signature over the original request bytes, and persists an event before acknowledging receipt. Use hmac.compare_digest where the provider specifies HMAC, a documented timestamp tolerance, and a unique provider event identifier to reject replayed deliveries. A Python worker validates the envelope with json, checks required fields, and writes a transaction containing the event, asset version, and pending work item. Store a content digest alongside the event identifier because duplicate documents can arrive through different channels. The LLM receives only approved document excerpts and returns proposed fields with page references, quoted evidence, and an explicit review-needed flag. Validate types, permitted values, date formats, and reference locations before displaying the proposal. Treat document text as evidence, never as instructions that can change tools or permissions. Reject embedded requests to publish files, reveal credentials, or modify counterparties. Extraction can prepare territory and renewal fields, but a person confirms their contractual meaning. Keep the accepted value, rejected proposal, reviewer, timestamp, and source version so later amendments remain explainable.

Define the automation denominator before promising 70%+. Measure recurring research, qualification, fulfillment preparation, and acquisition administration in minutes, while tracking negotiation and substantive rights review separately. In an illustrative 100-item batch, manual execution consumes ten minutes per item, or 1,000 minutes. Eighty routine items need one minute of human review after automation; twenty exceptions need nine minutes each. Retained work is 260 minutes, a 74% reduction within the defined repetitive workload. This does not mean that 74% of licensing decisions become autonomous. Instrument the first four weeks with started_at, completed_at, active_review_seconds, exception_reason, and rework_seconds. Report the median and slowest decile by play, because dataset permissions and patent scope can dominate exceptions. The rights contractor reviews new asset authority and material amendments; the operations contractor resolves report mappings; the channel contractor validates buyer fit. The full-time operator approves price, exclusivity, contract issuance, and dispute responses. Consolidate routine reviews into scheduled batches, but route expiring notice rights and suspected unauthorized use immediately. Automation earns additional scope only after sampled outputs remain accurate and the exception queue stays within its assigned response window. Count correction time against the claimed savings rather than hiding it in another person's workload.

## 2. Automate Royalty Reporting Without Automating Contract Interpretation  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Turn each executed agreement into a versioned royalty specification with an effective interval. Required fields include currency, royalty base, rate tiers, permitted deductions, minimum guarantee period, advance recoupment rules, reporting deadline, audit rights, and payment terms. Use decimal.Decimal for monetary operations and preserve the contract's rounding sequence; binary floating point should not decide invoices. A Python adapter reads CSV reports with csv.DictReader, normalizes product identifiers, and rejects unsupported currencies or periods before calculation. Require source totals, row counts, and a report digest so a corrected file cannot silently overwrite a settled period. For an illustrative credited annual minimum, a $30,000 guarantee and $24,000 earned royalty produce $30,000 total consideration for that period, not $54,000. If earned royalty reaches $42,000, the incremental amount above the credited guarantee is $12,000. Different agreements can make minimums noncreditable or advances recoupable across periods, so never generalize this example into a universal rule. Separate amounts earned, billed, received, and recognized under the applicable accounting treatment. The scorecard revenue line contains operator license fees and royalties, never the licensee's underlying product sales. A reviewer signs off the contract specification before the calculation engine handles its first live report.

Design reconciliation around independent evidence rather than assuming a clean spreadsheet is truthful. Compare reported units with contractually available shipment summaries, distributor statements, approved SKU lists, prior periods, and return schedules. Where the agreement permits access, ingest Shopify exports or NetSuite reports through a licensee-authorized connection; collect only fields needed for the reconciliation. An unexpected rate drop, omitted territory, newly active product, or deduction spike becomes a reason-coded exception, not an automatic accusation. Store report versions and calculation outputs so an audit can reproduce the original invoice. LLMs can classify narrative deduction explanations and draft clarification requests, but deterministic code calculates money. Before an invoice leaves the queue, the operations contractor checks the source period, minimum credit, prior payments, and material variances; the operator approves disputed charges. For failures, distinguish transport errors from semantic errors. Retry a temporary connection failure with bounded exponential backoff and jitter; quarantine an invalid royalty base without repeatedly executing it. After the retry ceiling, retain the payload and alert an assigned person. A provider timeout after invoice submission requires checking the external invoice identifier before resending, preventing duplicate demands on a licensee and preserving a coherent collection history.

Renewal alerts and obligation tracking should share the same contract version as the royalty engine. Record expiry, notice lead time, delivery method, recipient address, cure periods, insurance evidence, product approvals, attribution, and audit windows as separately owned obligations. A scheduled Python worker uses datetime and zoneinfo to prepare reminders from reviewed date rules, then creates internal tasks at 120, 90, 60, and 30 days where those intervals fit the agreement. Those internal reminders do not replace contractual notice requirements. If a notice must arrive by registered mail, an email task alone does not satisfy delivery. Store dispatch evidence and receipt evidence separately, and escalate when either remains missing. Amendment intake must invalidate affected future jobs and regenerate them against the new version; preserve completed actions for auditability. Trademark licenses need actual quality-control activity, not merely a clause stored in a database. Patent royalty schedules need review around expiration rather than automatic extension of the same payment basis. On scheduler failure, export the next ninety days of obligations into a dated CSV and assign daily manual checks until recovery. Rebuild the queue from accepted contract records, compare counts and dates, and close the incident only after missed deadlines have named owners.

**Royalty, Renewal, and Obligation Failure-Recovery Matrix**

| Operational event requiring a reproducible processing record | Deterministic test before moving the workflow forward | Assigned human review for material business consequences | Recovery evidence retained after a processing failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quarterly royalty report arrives through authenticated licensee upload | Validate reporting period, currency, row counts, and source totals | Operations contractor reviews unexplained differences against independent permitted evidence | Preserve original file, digest, rejection reasons, and corrected submission |
| Credited minimum guarantee intersects earned royalties within one period | Apply reviewed credit rules without counting the guarantee twice | Operator approves exceptions involving disputed credits or cross-period recoupment | Retain prior calculations and reconcile every revised invoice balance |
| Licensee submits returns or deductions exceeding historical operating patterns | Compare deduction categories against expressly permitted contract line items | Operations contractor requests supporting schedules before accepting material adjustments | Quarantine unsupported rows while preserving uncontested amounts for review |
| Renewal deadline approaches under contract-specific notice delivery requirements | Calculate reviewed deadlines and confirm recipient and delivery method | Operator approves commercial renewal position and actual notice content | Retain dispatch receipts, delivery evidence, and manually assigned follow-up |
| Executed amendment changes reporting rates or future obligation dates | Version effective intervals and regenerate only affected pending jobs | Rights specialist confirms amendment scope before revised calculations activate | Compare old and new schedules and preserve completed historical actions |
| Invoice provider times out after receiving an issuance request | Query the provider identifier before retrying the issuance operation | Operations contractor resolves ambiguous outcomes before contacting the licensee | Record external status, retry history, and duplicate-prevention decision evidence |

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the accepted source package
   - Record the executed contract version, report digest, period, and licensee identifier.
   - Reject mismatched periods and unsupported source formats before any monetary calculation starts.
2. Calculate and reconcile deterministically
   - Apply Decimal-based rate tiers, permitted deductions, minimum credits, and reviewed rounding rules.
   - Compare totals with authorized independent evidence and attach reason codes to every material difference.
3. Review consequential outputs
   - Present the invoice draft and exception evidence to the assigned contractor and operator.
   - Block external issuance until required reviews are recorded against the exact calculation version.
4. Issue once and preserve the trail
   - Use a stable issuance identifier, record the provider response, and schedule collection follow-up.
   - Resolve ambiguous provider outcomes by status lookup; retain both source evidence and accepted results.

## 3. Compound Verified Rights into Qualified Distribution  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build programmatic distribution from the accepted rights ledger, not from scraped descriptions that imply ownership. Generate public landing-page drafts for meaningful combinations of asset family, permitted use, territory, and buyer problem. Patent pages can describe an application area without asserting that a prospect infringes; brand pages can show approved categories and the sample-review process. Catalog pages should expose cleared preview material with media and territory limits, while dataset pages should publish schemas, provenance summaries, and synthetic samples where appropriate. Python renders structured records into templates, checks required fields, escapes untrusted text, and submits drafts for human publication review. Avoid thousands of near-identical pages: each published page needs distinct buyer utility, current availability, and an accountable owner. Track qualified inquiries, accepted briefs, diligence completion, and signed licenses rather than celebrating impressions. Add a request form that captures intended use, geography, distribution channel, expected volume, and decision timing. Feed that structured demand back into packaging decisions. Repeated requests for a territory outside the current grant indicate an acquisition opportunity, not permission to sell rights the operator lacks. Archive or revise pages immediately when exclusivity, ownership, or permission changes make their published offers inaccurate.

Use outbound automation to prioritize a small, defensible prospect list, not to manufacture volume. The channel contractor defines buyer signals for each play: manufacturers entering a relevant application, category operators with distribution capability, publishers needing identifiable catalog rights, or data teams with a permitted use case. Python collects authorized public facts and records the source URL, retrieval date, and reason for fit. An LLM drafts a concise use-case hypothesis from those facts; it must not invent funding, partnerships, infringement, or purchasing intent. Review initial messages and every new campaign pattern before sending. For U.S. commercial email, implement 15 U.S.C. 7704 requirements concerning accurate headers, nondeceptive subjects, required sender information, opt-out mechanisms, and timely suppression. Treat other jurisdictions as separate routing rules rather than assuming the U.S. process applies everywhere. Maintain a shared suppression list across contractors and tools, and stop sequences when someone replies or opts out. Integrate a CRM such as HubSpot through least-privilege credentials, with a local outbox recording proposed sends and delivery status. Cap daily releases to the pod's capacity to answer substantive rights questions. A positive reply creates a review task, not an automatic license offer or unrestricted sample delivery.

Create a referral loop only where sharing is itself permitted. After a successful catalog placement, ask the licensee for permission to publish a narrowly described case study and provide a tracked introduction link for another producer. For brand deals, approved launch materials can direct qualified category partners to the availability page without implying open sublicensing. For datasets, a reproducible evaluation notebook can demonstrate schema and permitted analysis using synthetic records; never embed restricted source data in a public demo. For patents, an approved technical application note can attract implementers without disclosing confidential claim analysis. Attribute referrals through campaign identifiers and record any agreed partner compensation separately from royalty deductions. The flywheel is specific: clearer evidence improves qualification, better qualification reduces diligence friction, successful licenses produce approved proof, and approved proof creates more relevant inquiries. Measure conversion by rights family and acquisition cohort because one large deal can distort averages. Rights packaging should also respect competition constraints under 15 U.S.C. 1 and 2; have qualified counsel assess sensitive territorial, exclusivity, tying, and pricing provisions. Do not let a recommendation model coordinate downstream prices or treat competitor disclosures as a shortcut to commercial strategy. Share only approved, counterparty-appropriate evidence.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Make Every Completed License Improve the Next One**
> 
> Veteran operators reuse verified evidence and workflow learning, not confidential terms or unchecked ownership assertions.
> - Publish a field-of-use intake worksheet for patent prospects; it separates application fit from infringement analysis before specialist time is spent.
> - Build brand sample-review calendars into onboarding so quality evidence accumulates throughout the license rather than just before renewal.
> - Attach stable work identifiers and clearance versions to catalog previews; downstream inquiries then refer to the exact rights package reviewed.
> - Distribute synthetic dataset examples with realistic schema constraints; reserve actual records for authorized, scoped evaluation delivery.

## 4. Price the Automation, Prove the Savings, and Rehearse Recovery  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Budget automated runs explicitly instead of calling software labor free. For an illustrative routine work item, allow $0.018 for model usage, $0.006 for execution and storage, and $0.010 for allocated messaging and monitoring, totaling $0.034 of machine cost. These are planning assumptions, not vendor quotations; measure actual token counts, invocations, storage, and subscription allocations. Using the earlier batch, retained human effort averages 2.6 minutes per item. At an illustrative loaded review rate of $45 per hour, that adds $1.95, producing $1.984 per completed item before separately allocated build costs. One thousand monthly items therefore consume $34 of machine expense and $1,950 of review capacity, or $1,984 combined. The ten-minute manual baseline costs $7.50 per item at the same rate, a modeled capacity difference of $5,516 across that monthly volume. Retained salaries and contractor commitments mean capacity savings do not automatically become cash savings or additional owner profit. Add failed runs, rework, specialist escalation, and subscription minimums to the measured ledger. Cache accepted extractions by document digest and schema version, but invalidate them when source documents or interpretation rules change. Optimize expensive exceptions before chasing fractions of a cent in routine model usage.

Reconcile the operating plan to the canonical scorecard without inserting automation savings as a second profit stream. Conservative revenue is $50,000, with $37,500 of total costs and $12,500 owner net profit at a 25% margin. Base revenue is $425,000, with $244,375 of costs and $180,625 profit at 42.5%. Startup capital remains $25,000–$250,000, and first-deal timing remains 6–24 months. Build the evidence ledger and one reporting adapter before funding elaborate acquisition infrastructure. A possible Base revenue composition is $250,000 of recognized license fees plus $175,000 of incremental royalties, provided credited minimums are not counted twice. That composition illustrates the revenue basis; it does not replace the canonical case. Test whether collected cash covers contractor commitments during long diligence cycles, and keep contract pipeline value separate from recognized revenue and bank receipts.

Operate the system with a weekly exception review and a monthly recovery rehearsal. Track unmatched reports, overdue notices, unsupported extracted fields, duplicate issuance attempts, suppression failures, and aging rights questions. Sample accepted outputs as well as rejected ones; otherwise the dashboard only shows errors the system already knows about. Preserve a small reviewed test set for each play, including a disputed deduction, a changed territory, an expired right, and a dataset redistribution restriction. Run that set before changing prompts, parsers, rate rules, or vendor integrations. A failed test freezes the affected automated action while unrelated workflows continue. Back up the ledger and evidence references, then prove restoration into an isolated environment with outgoing messages disabled. Check record counts, document digests, pending obligations, and external issuance identifiers before resuming. If the LLM becomes unavailable, use manual extraction templates; if the CRM fails, retain the outbox and suspend sends; if reporting ingestion fails, accept reviewed CSV submissions. Add the second full-time role and fourth contractor for the Scaled case only when sustained review demand justifies the commitments within its cost envelope. The target is dependable throughput with intact rights controls, not the largest possible number of unattended actions. Record each incident's cause, affected contracts, corrective action, and verification evidence.
