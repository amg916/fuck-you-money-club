## 1. Convert four rights plays into one evidence-controlled production queue  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an asset register, not a pitch deck. Candidate #19's four mapped plays are patent field-of-use licensing, brand licensing, catalog licensing, and dataset licensing. Assign every intake an asset identifier, rights holder, evidence digest, territory, permitted application, excluded application, expiration condition, and prospective buyer segment. Patent intake needs assignment instruments, maintenance status, claim mapping, and existing encumbrances; an issued patent alone does not establish commercial demand or freedom to operate. Brand intake needs ownership records and an executable product-quality inspection schedule. Catalog intake distinguishes composition, recording, image, text, and performance permissions rather than selling a vague content bundle. Dataset intake separates ownership, contractual access, personal-information restrictions, and downstream training or redistribution rights. Store signed instruments in versioned S3 or an equivalent controlled repository; store their hashes and object identifiers in the operating ledger. USPTO Assignment Center, Patent Center, and Copyright Office records support investigation but do not replace the underlying instruments. Score packaging readiness and buyer evidence separately from verified ownership. A commercially attractive asset with broken title stays blocked, even when a buyer offers an advance. That separation prevents the sales funnel from silently becoming the authority to grant rights.

Keep the Base pod at one full-time operator and three fractional contractors: specialist rights counsel, a technical or commercial diligence analyst, and a royalty bookkeeper. The operator owns prospect selection, commercial terms, exception decisions, and final cash review. Counsel owns title exceptions, grant language, territorial limitations, and competition analysis. The analyst builds patent claim-to-product evidence, brand inspection packets, catalog clearance manifests, or dataset provenance tests according to the play. The bookkeeper imports statements, challenges deductions, maintains receivables, and proposes reconciled postings. Route all four plays through the same identifiers and document requirements, but never pretend their permission structures are interchangeable. Under 35 U.S.C. § 261, patent assignments require written instruments; under 17 U.S.C. § 204, copyright ownership transfers generally require signed writing, while a nonexclusive license raises different formalities. Brand deals need actual quality control, not merely inspection language that nobody executes. Dataset deals need source-by-source rights analysis because facts, copyrightable selection, confidential material, and personal information create different constraints. Cap active negotiations at four per operator and use weekly contractor batches. This is a capacity rule for the illustrated pod, not an assertion that every contractor supplies full-time labor or can cover every specialty.

Turn each verified asset into a license packet containing a permission matrix, priced use cases, reporting specification, inspection rights, remediation schedule, and termination mechanics. Define royalty-bearing sales precisely: included products, permitted deductions, related-party transfers, bundles, returns, currencies, and reporting periods. For a 5% illustrative royalty, $200,000 of qualifying licensee sales produces $10,000 of operator royalty revenue, not $200,000. If a contract has a $12,000 annual minimum credited against the same annual royalties, $10,000 of earned running royalties plus a $2,000 earned minimum true-up produces $12,000, not $22,000. Accounting treatment still follows the actual performance obligations and enforceable payment terms; an advance can remain a liability before it is earned. Specify whether minimums reset annually, carry forward, or apply by territory. Negotiate audit access before signing, including source records, retention periods, sampling rights, and the contractual threshold for shifting audit costs. Use 15 U.S.C. §§ 1–2 to frame competition review of exclusivity, tying, and coordinated restrictions. Counsel must evaluate the actual market and restraint rather than treating a patent or brand as automatic permission to impose any commercial condition. Finish intake only when the packet can survive a skeptical licensee review.

## 2. Install the six-module ledger and serialized execution boundary  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The six reference modules below form one runnable Python script when concatenated in order. They use only the standard library and create a local SQLite database. Every business mutation enters BEGIN IMMEDIATE before inspecting state, so competing writers cannot both consume a revision, activate the same asset, or allocate the same customer credit. A unique event key binds retries to a canonical payload digest; reusing the key with different content fails. Separate unique economic keys prevent a second event identifier from posting the same invoice or bank transaction. Posted journals are balanced before release and protected against subsequent edits by database triggers. Run one connection per worker on a local filesystem, keep transactions short, and retry lock timeouts with the original event identifier. Do not place the database on a shared network filesystem or share one connection across threads. These controls remove check-then-write races inside the stated database boundary; they do not make an external bank, email service, or escrow provider transactional with SQLite. External collection remains statement-driven, and the reference deliberately initiates no transfers. Restrict database write access to the application identity and preserve encrypted backups outside the working directory.

The schema keeps operational verification separate from accounting. An asset moves from intake to verified, optionally to escrow_pending, and then to active; any existing state can be blocked. Activation requires a signed-document digest, a bounded date range, a scope digest, and a verified rights packet. Escrow here means evidence of a third-party arrangement required by the contract, not an operator pretending to be a custody institution. A digest proves which bytes were reviewed, not that their statements are true; the reviewer must authenticate the documents and counterparties. The compact reference permits one contract per asset package, so split nonoverlapping fields or territories into separately identified packages before activation. Its intake score measures readiness and is unrelated to Candidate #19's fixed 88/100 portfolio score. Store sensitive documents outside event payloads and use durable evidence references. Document access requires separate authenticated application controls. The Python functions assume their caller has already been authenticated and authorized; a reviewer name supplied by an arbitrary caller is insufficient authorization. Production deployment must bind that field to the authenticated principal and keep counsel's findings, inspection reports, and signed contracts in the protected evidence repository.

Revenue posting accepts an approved earned amount in integer cents; it does not decide when a license obligation has been satisfied. The bookkeeper supplies a unique invoice reference after checking the agreement, statement, minimum-credit schedule, and recognition memo. Receipts first clear outstanding receivables, then become customer credit rather than automatic revenue. Later earned invoices consume that credit before creating new receivables. Bank identifiers must combine institution, account, and immutable transaction identifier, because statement row numbers can change between exports. The reconciliation view proves earned revenue minus collected cash minus receivables plus customer credit equals zero for each contract. It covers a single USD book; reject foreign-currency amounts until an explicit exchange-rate and realized-difference design is added. Correct mistakes with separately identified reversing and replacement events, never by editing posted lines. The generic posting primitive supports those journals, but the application must provide their authorization and business validation. Run the QA module against a consistent read snapshot and compare its cash totals with independently imported bank records. The reference has not been executed here; test replay, competing revisions, duplicate bank identifiers, rollback after exceptions, and backup restoration in an isolated database before connecting live operational inputs.

## 3. Run the reporting, exception, renewal, and cash-close operating cycle  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Operate a weekly collection cycle and a monthly close, with contract deadlines driving the calendar rather than a generic subscription schedule. On Monday, the analyst imports licensee reports into a staging area and checks the contract identifier, statement period, revision number, source-file digest, currency, and covered products. Tuesday is the bookkeeper's recalculation batch: recompute qualifying sales, approved deductions, royalty rates, minimum credits, and prior corrections from source rows. Wednesday belongs to exceptions, including missing territories, abrupt return spikes, changed bundle allocations, related-party discounts, and unreported channels. Thursday, the operator resolves commercial disputes while counsel handles questions about grant scope or enforcement. Friday, the bookkeeper posts approved earned amounts and imports actual bank transactions; the operator independently reviews the cash bridge. Use pandas-free CSV processing, SQLite queries, and versioned spreadsheet exports if the pod does not need a dedicated royalty platform. A spreadsheet can present results, but it should not be the sole record of corrections or payment identities. Keep original statements immutable and link every replacement to its predecessor. Never overwrite the evidence that explains why an earlier invoice, deduction, or cash allocation was reasonable when it was approved.

Design audit tests around each play's characteristic leakage. Patent licensees can omit product variants or route sales through affiliates; compare reported part numbers with approved product schedules and available shipment evidence. Brand licensees can report sales accurately while degrading quality, so revenue testing does not replace physical or documented product inspection. Catalog users can expand into new channels, territories, edits, or derivative works that the original grant never covered. Dataset users can exceed seat counts, export limits, model-training permissions, or permitted downstream recipients. Instrument access logs where technically appropriate, but do not equate a download count with contractual royalty-bearing use without checking the definition. Escalate discrepancies through the agreement's notice, cure, inspection, and dispute process. For datasets containing California personal information, evaluate whether the actual relationship meets the requirements of Civil Code § 1798.100 and applicable contract rules; labeling a buyer a service provider does not settle the issue. For patents, review 35 U.S.C. § 287 when marking affects potential damages. Preserve contemporaneous findings and the sampling method. An audit conclusion without its population, selection criteria, and source records is difficult to defend and expensive to reproduce during a later dispute.

Automation should shorten evidence handling, not manufacture rights judgments. Schedule renewal alerts at contract-specific intervals, with separate reminders for notice deadlines, minimum adjustments, audit windows, exclusivity milestones, and evidence expiration. Route missing reports into a dated exception queue before escalating payment reminders. A failed import must retain its source digest, error category, and assigned owner; silently skipping malformed rows can look like successful automation while hiding revenue leakage. Maintain a daily backup and periodically restore it into a disposable environment, then rerun reconciliation checks and compare evidence references with repository objects. Use append-only corrections, narrow document permissions, and separate reviewer access for bank-instruction changes. The owner reviews the oldest five receivables, the largest five deductions, all blocked rights packages, and every pending expiration each week. The Base cost model supports fractional specialist work, not unlimited litigation or bespoke engineering. Escalate an infringement dispute into a separately budgeted decision with expected recovery, remaining term, evidence quality, and enforcement cost. Preserve the four-person operating constraint by rejecting deals whose reporting complexity or inspection burden exceeds the expected license contribution, even when their underlying product-sales headline appears impressive.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the reporting evidence
   - Archive the original statement and record its contract, period, revision, and SHA-256 digest.
   - Reject duplicate economic submissions with changed identifiers; preserve revised statements as linked versions rather than replacements.
2. Recompute and resolve exceptions
   - Recalculate royalties, deductions, minimum credits, and source totals before approving an earned amount.
   - Assign scope questions to counsel and numerical discrepancies to the bookkeeper; attach both findings to the recognition memo.
3. Post and allocate actual cash
   - Submit the approved invoice and imported bank transaction through their respective replay-safe event functions.
   - Use immutable bank references; retain excess collections as customer credit until supported earned amounts consume them.
4. Close and schedule the next obligation
   - Run QA, match receipts with bank evidence, review receivables, and refresh contract-specific deadlines.
   - The operator signs the close packet only after differences have assigned owners and material unresolved items are visibly excluded from completed status.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Four tactics that protect a small licensing pod**
> 
> The most valuable automation usually prevents a familiar exception from consuming another specialist review.
> - Negotiate a sample royalty report before signing; load its actual columns and test deductions against one worked invoice.
> - Partition patent fields and catalog territories into distinct asset packages so exclusivity checks operate on explicit boundaries.
> - Require separate minimum-credit schedules by contract period; carry-forward language should never be inferred from a payment memo.
> - Buy targeted audit work after a measurable anomaly, such as missing product identifiers, rather than commissioning broad reviews without a testable hypothesis.
