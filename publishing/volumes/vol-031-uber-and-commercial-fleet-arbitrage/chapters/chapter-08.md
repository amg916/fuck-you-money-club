## 1. Build an evidence spine before enriching a single vehicle  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with three durable entities: vehicle, driver, and operating agreement. A vehicle record carries the normalized VIN, plate plus issuing jurisdiction, plate effective dates, investor account, registration expiry, and permitted service class. A driver record carries an internal identifier and separately protected license references; a lease record links the parties, service territory, billing calendar, and responsibility for tolls, deductibles, and downtime. Never join on a driver name alone or treat a plate as permanent: plate transfers create false toll allocations and misleading permit histories. Store each observation with source name, retrieval timestamp, source effective date, raw response digest, and the identifier actually queried. Keep the original file immutable, then write normalized facts into SQLite for a small desk or an existing managed database as volume grows. A public listing establishes what that registry publishes, not insurance coverage, platform eligibility, ownership, or authority to obtain personal records. Under 18 U.S.C. 2721, access to motor vehicle personal information requires a permitted purpose; broad lead generation is not a substitute. Keep public eligibility research separate from permissioned driver screening so an analyst cannot accidentally turn a fleet prospect list into a personal-data collection campaign.

Run enrichment from cheapest decisive evidence to expensive verification. First validate VIN length and format, decode it through NHTSA vPIC, and compare the returned model year and body class with the proposed Black SUV service. Next query the relevant TLC or CPUC record, preserving its published update time rather than assuming that today's download means today's status. Then inspect recall evidence and order an NMVTIS report through an approved provider when title history could change the acquisition or co-hosting decision. NHTSA's public recall campaign API is not a VIN-specific repair-completion certificate; use the manufacturer or dealer channel for completion evidence. NICB VINCheck is a limited screening input, not a complete ownership, theft, or damage history. Finish with authorized driver records and carrier-issued coverage evidence before assigning a revenue shift. For illustration, avoiding one $180 title report and inspection bundle on each of five clearly ineligible prospects saves $900; that saving is an acquisition expense reduction, not additional lease revenue. If sources disagree, retain both observations, open an exception, and hold the assignment until the issuing authority, owner, or insurer resolves the relevant fact in writing.

The first eight directory entries cover official records and low-cost verification. Their cost descriptions identify charging structures, not invented vendor quotes; capture the current price and permitted use during onboarding. For Socrata, locate the named dataset, record its resource identifier, and use its Export or API documentation rather than copying an identifier from an old blog. Page large responses, select only necessary columns, and compare record counts with the published export. A zero-row response can mean a bad filter, renamed field, or stale identifier; it cannot independently prove that a vehicle lacks authority. Check dataset metadata and run a known-valid control lookup before escalating an empty result. Cache stable VIN decoding, but refresh status-sensitive permit evidence at dispatch and after a relevant alert. California TCP authority and New York TLC licensing are jurisdiction-specific regimes, not interchangeable national credentials. Airport operating rights require separate verification with the airport involved. If a portal blocks automation, use its permitted manual lookup or published download and record the operator's evidence; do not bypass challenges or borrow another account. Give every fallback an expiry so a saved screenshot never quietly becomes permanent operating authority.

## 2. Connect authorized APIs without confusing access with entitlement  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Permissioned integrations begin with an authority register, not an API key pasted into a spreadsheet. Record the account owner, approved purpose, allowed fleet identifiers, scopes, credential custodian, renewal date, and revocation procedure. Uber supplier tools expose capabilities according to geography, relationship, and account entitlement; do not assume a public endpoint can read every driver's earnings or enforce every lease waterfall. Use the authorized portal export until the relevant supported integration is available. Samsara and Geotab can provide vehicle telemetry through contracted interfaces, but location access does not confer authority to command hardware. Keep command credentials outside the reporting pipeline. Starter-interrupt equipment requires a separate documented process addressing local restrictions, contract disclosures, stationary-state verification, emergency access, and vendor safety controls; a billing exception must never directly trigger immobilization. Stripe events show payment lifecycle changes, not final earned revenue or irrevocable collection. Match processor settlements, fees, refunds, and disputes against the bank deposit before closing a billing period. The commercial directory below names eight channels worth evaluating; none implies that this desk already has credentials, a negotiated price, production access, or the right to retrieve another operator's records.

Use an append-only ingestion envelope containing source, account identifier, event identifier, event time, receipt time, schema version, and raw-object digest. Deduplicate webhooks by source account and event identifier, then apply updates through an idempotent transaction. Providers can retry messages, deliver them late, or deliver a refund before a delayed payment event; the current business state must come from a supported object lookup or an explicit state transition policy. Poll overlapping time windows where a feed supports them, and advance the watermark only after the full page set commits. Respect Retry-After when supplied; otherwise use bounded exponential backoff with jitter and a retry ceiling. Persistent failures go to an exception queue instead of spinning indefinitely. Preserve raw timestamps and normalize a separate field to UTC, retaining local service dates for toll disputes and shift billing. Encrypt driver reports separately from telemetry and settlement records, and give dispatch only the eligibility result needed for assignment. Consumer-report use requires a permissible purpose under 15 U.S.C. 1681b; employment-purpose reports bring additional disclosure, authorization, and adverse action duties when applicable. Calling a driver an independent contractor does not settle those questions.

A small fleet needs a freshness budget for each decision. A historical VIN decode can remain useful for months, while a device's last GPS fix must carry its actual event time before anyone uses it to coordinate a pickup. Establish a local operating threshold, such as flagging location samples older than fifteen minutes for dispatch review, without pretending the vendor promises that latency. Toll charges may arrive days after a shift; keep the lease ledger open for the contractually stated reconciliation period and retain the event-time vehicle assignment. Use Google Ads API reporting only for accounts the client has authorized through the appropriate manager relationship. Developer-token access and OAuth authorization are separate requirements, and neither proves card-reward eligibility. Review account quotas in the provider console rather than hard-coding folklore about requests per minute. Every integration should have a manual continuity route: signed export, secure portal download, or a documented provider support case. During an outage, label stale observations explicitly, stop decisions that require fresh evidence, and continue unaffected collections or bookkeeping. This preserves useful operations without turning a temporary data failure into an unjustified driver rejection or an unsafe vehicle assignment.

## 4. Instrument the desk so stale data cannot become a cash leak  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A useful control panel shows decisions and exposure, not just successful HTTP requests. Track source freshness, unmatched vehicle identities, unassigned toll amounts, unresolved coverage intervals, pending driver reports, settlement differences, and vehicle downtime hours. Give each metric a denominator: five unmatched tolls out of six is different from five out of six thousand. Use Python logging for structured job events, sqlite3 for durable local state, hashlib for evidence digests, and unittest for parsers and reconciliation rules. Keep credentials out of logs and sample payloads. Add tests for duplicate webhooks, plate reuse, daylight-saving transitions, negative adjustments, partial refunds, canceled shifts, and late toll posting. A job should fail visibly if a required column disappears or a monetary field changes format; silently substituting zero turns a schema change into fabricated performance. Compare daily row counts and total amounts against historical ranges, but route unusual results to review rather than deleting them. For a one-person full-time desk supported by contractors, assign one exception owner and one backup per feed. Contractors receive only the records required for their task, while access revocation is part of offboarding rather than an annual cleanup.

Coverage continuity and driver monitoring deserve independent exception queues because a cash ledger cannot prove either. For each vehicle, align policy effective intervals with the actual service: off-app rental use, app-on waiting, accepted trips, passenger transport, and commercial livery assignments. California Public Utilities Code section 5433 addresses transportation network company insurance, but it does not replace the coverage analysis for a TCP livery arrangement or an investor-owned vehicle rented to a driver. Ask the carrier or authorized broker to resolve exclusions, vehicle schedules, driver restrictions, and cancellation notices; a certificate alone does not rewrite the policy. Driver monitoring likewise needs a defined response to a new record, a dispute, and an incomplete report. Where employment reporting rules apply, build the required pre-adverse and adverse action steps into the case workflow rather than letting an automated score terminate access. Apply a written retention schedule tied to the relevant purpose and dispute obligations. The business objective is timely, defensible assignment, not collecting every available personal detail. During a coverage or eligibility conflict, use a separately verified replacement or pause that assignment while continuing unrelated administrative work.

Close the week with an exception-to-cash bridge. Begin with issued billings, subtract credits and reversals, separate collected cash from open receivables, then allocate investor distributions and pass-through fleet costs once. Measure toll recovery against contractually recoverable posted charges, not every camera event or provisional telematics inference. A $34 toll recovered exactly from the responsible driver can settle a receivable without creating $34 of retained profit. For maintenance, compare actual odometer evidence with the service schedule and book repairs into lower-demand windows. Avoiding two days of downtime at $550 divided by seven days preserves about $157.14 gross lease capacity, not that amount of owner profit; apply retention and incremental repair costs before claiming a benefit. Track advertising rewards only after the issuer and rebate provider settle them, with reversals assigned to the original campaign. Review the ten mappings monthly and retire feeds that do not change a decision, prevent a loss, or accelerate collection. Capital remains constrained by the canonical $12,000-$65,000 startup range, and first-deal planning remains one to three months. Buying more dashboards does not shorten vehicle qualification, insurance placement, or a legitimate client authorization process.
