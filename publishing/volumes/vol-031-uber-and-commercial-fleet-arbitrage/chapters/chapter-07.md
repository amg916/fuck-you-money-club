## 1. Turn a lead into a permitted, insured, collectible vehicle assignment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a route-specific operating packet rather than a vehicle shopping list. Capture the investor identity, VIN, title or lease authority, lienholder restrictions, garaging address, proposed driver, dispatch territory, airport use, and intended service class. Separate ordinary rideshare leasing from commercially licensed Black SUV work; an eligible vehicle does not establish an eligible operator, driver, or trip. Obtain the actual insurance forms, endorsements, exclusions, deductibles, cancellation notice terms, and driver schedule from the broker. In New York City, verify TLC vehicle, driver, and applicable base licensing against the intended dispatch arrangement. Airport permission is another record, not an inference from platform activation. Score commercial readiness using documented route eligibility, usable insurance pricing, and funded operating capacity; never use demographic proxies. The intake score prioritizes review but cannot override a failed coverage requirement. Reject a mismatched insurance class before accepting keys, deposits, or investor promises. Store document references and expiry dates, while restricting access to licenses and consumer reports. Every accepted packet receives one durable application identifier and one proposed VIN reservation.

Convert the accepted packet into a contract-defined waterfall. Specify who invoices the driver, who collects platform settlements, which party owns receivables, and which amounts are refundable deposits rather than revenue. Uber Fleet arrangements and payment features vary by market and account; obtain the actual enabled settlement terms instead of assuming universal automatic lease deductions. Where a supported split is unavailable, use a separately authorized collection method and match its settlement reference to the weekly obligation. Record driver fare earnings separately from operator lease, dispatch, and management charges. For this chapter's agency presentation, every $100 of modeled gross billings produces $55 retained revenue and $45 owner or pass-through payables. ASC 606 principal-versus-agent conclusions still depend on the contracts and control of each promised service; the 55% factor is the supplied scorecard assumption, not a universal accounting rule. A $1,000 refundable security deposit creates $1,000 bank cash and $1,000 deposit liability, with zero operating revenue. Never distribute that balance as profit. Obtain ACH authorization with the processor's required language, record revocation handling, and price chargebacks into collection procedures rather than silently transferring them to an investor.

Release the vehicle only after independent evidence supports driver eligibility, continuous coverage, current permits, and signed commercial terms. Automated MVR retrieval through an authorized provider can shorten review, but report use must follow the actual permissible purpose. **SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the candidate and route
   - Create one application with stable identifiers and document references.
   - Record owner authority, VIN, driver, service class, territory, proposed prices, and the precise source of the lead.
2. Verify and reserve
   - Complete evidence review before creating the exclusive VIN assignment.
   - Resolve coverage and permit mismatches, sign the waterfall, and reserve capacity within one database transaction.
3. Fund and hand over
   - Confirm actual bank settlement and complete physical condition acceptance.

4. Close the settlement cycle
   - Match invoices, processor settlements, bank entries, and owner statements.
   - Clear exceptions before distributions, retain the settlement identifiers, and reconcile receivables and liabilities independently.

## 2. Build the six-module ledger and make retries economically harmless  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use SQLite for a single-host small-team deployment, with the database on durable local storage rather than a shared network filesystem. The six reference modules below concatenate in numerical order into one Python 3.9 script. They use integer cents, foreign keys, unique business keys, explicit transactions, immutable journal rows, and a unique active reservation per VIN. BEGIN IMMEDIATE serializes competing writers before they read balances or change assignments, eliminating check-then-write races inside this deployment boundary. A reused request key with a different payload fails instead of silently overwriting history. Journal creation and its lines commit together; a process crash cannot leave a partially posted entry through the provided write functions. Keep raw database access behind the trusted service account because direct SQL can bypass application-level business rules. Store encrypted documents outside the ledger and retain only evidence references here. Configure encrypted backups, restoration tests, access logging, and monitored disk capacity before live collection. These snippets are a reference implementation, not a tested installation report. Their assertions must pass against the deployment's actual adapter, retention policy, and permission model before real vehicles or money depend on them.

The verification and funds-held state machine deliberately separates evidence from cash. A reviewer records coverage, permit, driver, and contract evidence with a shared earliest expiry timestamp. Funding requires a confirmed bank reference; a submitted payment instruction is insufficient. The same funding reference cannot credit two applications because its global request key includes the immutable payload digest. Activation checks the evidence deadline, deposit threshold, and exclusive VIN reservation inside the same write transaction. Suspension blocks new invoice generation without deleting the assignment or releasing refundable funds. Closing requires a confirmed deposit return after suspension and releases the reservation atomically. This is an internal restricted-funds workflow, not a representation that the operator offers regulated escrow services. Where a contract requires genuine escrow, substitute the authorized custodian's confirmed ledger events and segregated accounts. The sample conservatively treats held deposits as unavailable for operating payouts. Collision deductions, disputes, partial refunds, and contract amendments require separately designed adjustment events rather than editing posted journals. Extend the state machine explicitly for those events, with two-person review and reversal entries. Never repair a production discrepancy by changing old amounts until the bank balance appears convenient.

External work needs a separate correctness boundary. The transactional outbox records a provisioning intent alongside activation, but it does not claim that a database transaction can make an unrelated provider execute exactly once. The adapter must support durable idempotency keys, retrieval by that key, and payload agreement. Two workers may attempt delivery concurrently; both must receive the same provider receipt, and the database records that receipt once. A timeout leaves the intent pending for lookup and retry with the original key. If a provider lacks those semantics, stop automatic delivery and reconcile manually; do not generate a fresh key. Invoices recognize retained revenue and owner liabilities, collections reduce receivables, and payouts reduce liabilities without recognizing revenue again. The reconciliation view exposes bank cash, receivables, retained revenue, operating expense, refundable deposits, and owner payables independently. Daily QA checks balanced journals, exclusive reservations, nonnegative restricted-fund coverage, and stale active evidence. Run additional deployment tests for simultaneous activation, repeated funding callbacks, crash-after-provider-success, and duplicate settlement files. Platform exports, bank confirmations, and owner statements remain the external sources of truth; a balanced internal ledger can still contain a correctly balanced but commercially wrong transaction.

## 3. Walk the first 90 days from scrubbed leads to the Base run rate  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Define the cohort unit before discussing growth. These are illustrative blended prices, not quoted Uber rates or passenger fares. Dispatch charges exclude underlying trip GMV, and management charges cannot duplicate services already included in the lease. An investor-facing management invoice remains an explicit charge even if settlement permits offsetting it against that investor's distribution.
