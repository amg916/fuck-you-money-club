## 1. Map ten plays onto one measurable fleet event spine  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the operating spine around a vehicle, a driver, an investor, and a contract version, rather than around disconnected spreadsheets. The ten mapped plays below are this chapter's implementation map; public_record_api_intelligence preserves the supplied catalog identifier, while the other labels describe local workflow components. Assign the full-time operator responsibility for cash reconciliation, insurance exceptions, and release decisions. Allocate the three contractors to driver onboarding, dispatch and vehicle coordination, and bookkeeping with acquisition support. These are work assignments, not assumptions that contractor classification follows automatically from a label. Record each task's baseline handling time before automating it. A sample week containing 1,000 repetitive tasks at three minutes each represents 50 hours. Completing 750 without manual re-entry removes 37.5 baseline hours, but reviewing 100 exceptions for four minutes and auditing 50 outputs for two minutes consumes 8.33 hours. Net time reduction is therefore 29.17 hours, or 58.3%, despite 75% straight-through task completion. Report both measures and exclude driving, physical inspections, and collision response from the repetitive-task denominator. The 70% target concerns verified task completion, not an assertion that a four-person operation can ignore safety or eliminate judgment.

Implement ingestion with Python's urllib.request, json, sqlite3, hashlib, hmac, and logging modules. Use a production HTTPS receiver supplied by the hosting environment; Python's basic development server is not the public production endpoint. Verify each vendor's documented signature against the original request bytes, check its timestamp and replay rules, then persist the event before acknowledging receipt. Where no webhook exists, fetch an authorized report with a scheduled job and hash the file. Give every event a provider identifier, external event identifier, vehicle identifier, observed timestamp, contract version, and processing state. Enforce uniqueness on the provider and event identifiers in SQLite, and keep monetary values as integer cents. An event marked received becomes validated, proposed, reviewed when necessary, posted, and reconciled. Separate the proposed state from ledger posting so an LLM cannot move money merely by generating convincing text. Use a transactional outbox for downstream requests, with an idempotency key where the receiving service supports one. After a timeout, query the provider's transaction status before retrying a payment. A single-writer queue suits the initial footprint; migrate storage only when measured lock contention or recovery objectives justify the operational burden.

Run public_record_api_intelligence against fleet-relevant business triggers: new transportation permits, municipal surplus notices, commercial entity registrations, and published airport operator directories. Fragmented county dockets, probate registries, and unclaimed-property ledgers require narrower handling. A record may suggest a business ownership change or an asset requiring an authorized representative, but it does not establish an uncontested claim, available vehicle title, or permission to contact beneficiaries. Resolve identity and business relevance before outreach; exclude household distress targeting and do not substitute public records for a driving report. Illustratively, processing 2,000 permitted records at $0.015 each costs $30. If 80 records become plausible business matches and 20 survive human verification, the data cost is $1.50 per verified lead, before labor. Add 40 minutes of review at $30 per hour and the combined cost becomes $2.50 per verified lead. Attach the official source URL, retrieval date, permitted use, and entity matching evidence to each record. When access changes, stop the connector rather than bypassing restrictions. Switch to scheduled bulk FOIA/public-record exports, official data feeds, and low-frequency cached queues; mark stale records ineligible for outreach until a fresh business-status check succeeds.

## 2. Automate qualification and telemetry without delegating consequential decisions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Treat driver qualification as evidence assembly followed by an accountable decision. Capture the applicant's identity, license jurisdiction, intended vehicle class, operating geography, and contract type before requesting an MVR through a properly contracted provider. The Driver's Privacy Protection Act, 18 U.S.C. 2721, restricts disclosure and use of personal information from motor vehicle records; the workflow must record the actual permitted purpose rather than a generic business justification. When a report is a consumer report, apply the relevant Fair Credit Reporting Act provisions, including 15 U.S.C. 1681b. Employment-purpose reports trigger specific disclosure, authorization, and pre-adverse-action requirements; do not assume every driver lease is employment or that a contractor label removes applicable duties. Configure the report provider and reviewer checklist for the actual relationship. An LLM may extract expiration dates and organize cited findings, but it must not invent disqualifying events or decide eligibility. Store the original report in a restricted repository, pass only necessary fields to the model, and maintain an access log. An identity mismatch stops the workflow. The reviewer requests correction, records the disposition, and follows the applicable notice process before an adverse decision is implemented.

Make coverage and vehicle authority machine-readable, but keep binding interpretations with the broker and responsible operator. A certificate upload alone does not prove that a particular driver, vehicle, use, and territory are covered. Store the policy identifier, named insured, scheduled vehicle, authorized-driver conditions, effective times, deductibles, and relevant endorsements. California Insurance Code 5433 addresses transportation network company insurance requirements across operating periods; it does not automatically satisfy every commercial livery assignment. California charter-party authority under Public Utilities Code 5371 and New York City TLC licensing requirements must be evaluated for the service actually sold. Airport permissions are a separate operating check, not a synonym for TCP or TLC status. The dispatch engine should require affirmative, current evidence matching the proposed shift and decline automatic assignment when evidence expires. Illustratively, reviewing 40 proposed shifts for two minutes each uses 80 minutes; verifying only eight flagged exceptions for five minutes uses 40 minutes, provided the remaining 32 have complete validated evidence. The saved 40 minutes is the benefit, not an assumed reduction in insurance expense. During a carrier or licensing-system outage, preserve existing records and route new assignments to manual verification.

Connect authorized OBD-II GPS and diagnostic feeds from a contracted telematics vendor such as Geotab or Samsara where the purchased hardware and service expose the required data. Never assume a GPS subscription includes starter-interrupt capability or that a location fix proves a vehicle is safely parked. Use telemetry first for maintenance triage, overdue return investigation, and dispatch readiness. Require freshness, device health, and vehicle association checks before promoting an event into a staff task. A low-voltage event should generate a battery inspection proposal, not an accusation against a driver. Starter-interrupt equipment, where lawful and contractually authorized, remains outside autonomous model control. Require documented human authorization, manufacturer-prescribed safeguards, verified safe stationary conditions, an emergency escalation route, and applicable notice requirements; never command a moving vehicle to stop or treat uncertain status as permission. The safest default on stale telemetry, conflicting sensors, or an unresolved emergency is no remote command. For an illustrative 400 daily events, $4 processing spend plus 20 five-minute reviews at $30 per hour produces $54 total handling cost, or $0.135 per event. Track false alerts and repeat causes so sensor noise cannot silently consume the team's entire operating day.

>

Remaining costs are an annual reconciliation envelope, not an assertion that every dollar is fixed monthly overhead. Allocate labor, insurance exposure, software, repairs, downtime, and other costs according to the actual contract, avoiding double counting costs already removed in the 45% share. For presentation, preserve the scorecard's ASC 606 retained-share convention while documenting the actual principal-versus-agent assessment for each revenue stream. Neither passenger fares earned by drivers nor investor vehicle values become operator revenue simply because they appear in imported reports. The Scaled team grows to 2 FT + 4 C; do not quietly keep the Base staffing assumption.

Build the Uber Fleet Partner waterfall from actual agreements and authorized reporting access, not an imagined universal payout API. Where the account supports a relevant export or integration, import its settlement records; otherwise use controlled report downloads and reviewer-confirmed bank matching. Top-of-waterfall driver leases describe a negotiated collection position, not a blanket right to seize driver earnings or reorder platform payments. Model the contract's lease charge, investor entitlement, permitted pass-through costs, credits, and disputed amounts as separate ledger lines. At the scorecard's aggregate ratio, $1,000 gross billings leaves $550 retained after $450 allocated to investor splits and pass-through fleet costs; an individual contract can differ, so reconcile the portfolio rather than forcing every vehicle into the same split. Match tolls against plate history, transponder, trip timestamp, and the responsible lease window. Explain any permitted administrative fee in the contract and customer statement. Maintenance billing needs an approved work order, vendor invoice, vehicle identity, and contractual responsibility. Missing evidence creates a disputed item rather than an automatic debit. Reconcile the bank, platform settlement, driver receivable, and investor payable daily, and require a second review for changed payout destinations or material manual adjustments.

Price each automated run as vendor charges plus model usage, infrastructure allocation, retries, and expected human review. Illustratively, a document workflow costing $0.02 extraction, $0.01 inference, and $0.01 queue and storage totals $0.04 before review. At a 12% exception rate, four minutes per exception, and $30 hourly labor, expected review adds $0.24, making the run $0.28. Ten thousand monthly runs therefore cost $2,800, not $400. These are planning assumptions rather than vendor quotations, and must fit within the canonical cost envelope rather than being added after declaring profit. Exclude MVR purchase charges from this document example and budget them separately to avoid hiding a material input. Record model identifier, prompt version, token counts, outcome, and reviewer corrections without logging unnecessary sensitive documents. Validate output against required keys, permitted values, source references, and arithmetic constraints; low confidence is not the only reason to review. Use bounded retries with exponential delay for transient read failures, then move exhausted jobs to a visible exception queue. A failed monetary write receives status reconciliation, not a blind replay. Restore a backup into a separate environment monthly and prove that ledger totals and event identifiers survive recovery.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the source batch
   - Import authorized fleet settlements, bank records, toll statements, and approved maintenance invoices.
   - Hash each source, record its retrieval time, and preserve immutable event identifiers before proposing ledger entries.
2. Construct contract-specific proposals
   - Match vehicles, drivers, lease windows, investor terms, and charge evidence using integer-cent calculations.
   - Separate collectible amounts from disputed items and compare aggregate retained contribution with the scorecard model.
3. Review consequential exceptions
   - Assign changed payout destinations, unsupported fees, identity conflicts, and unusual adjustments to named reviewers.
   - Record the evidence and decision; unresolved exceptions remain held rather than being silently netted against another party.
4. Post and reconcile safely
   - Submit authorized postings with supported idempotency controls and reconcile resulting balances against external records.
   - Investigate timeouts before retrying, retain adjustment history, and publish only reconciled driver and investor statements.

## 4. Compound verified distribution while constraining acquisition and recovery risk  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build programmatic distribution from verified operating facts rather than mass-produced location pages. Maintain structured records for service area, available vehicle class, weekly lease components, deposit conditions, driver requirements, and staffed response hours. Publish a useful page only when the business genuinely serves that location and a reviewer checks its claims. Google Search Console can measure discovery and query relevance; a first-party intake form should attribute qualified inquiries without exposing driver documents to advertising systems. Refresh availability from reconciled inventory and withdraw stale offers automatically. An LLM can draft explanations of lease mechanics, but statutory statements need a cited primary source and human verification. Illustratively, ten maintained pages yield 100 visits each, 20 qualified inquiries in total, and two activated leases. At $300 combined production and review cost, acquisition cost is $150 per activation, before ongoing maintenance. Do not describe 1,000 visits as demand proven profitable until collections and downtime are observed. Each successful onboarding should improve a reusable requirements page and an intake checklist. This feedback loop compounds useful content and reduces repeated questions without manufacturing testimonials, copying competitors, or representing that Uber endorses an independently operated leasing or co-hosting business.

Combine permissioned referrals with narrow business outreach to investors, licensed operators, repair partners, and genuinely relevant fleet owners. Give each introduction a referral identifier and publish clear eligibility and payment terms; reward a verified commercial milestone rather than an unsupported earnings claim. Obtain permission before sharing a driver's story or identifiable vehicle performance records. For commercial email, implement accurate sender information, nondeceptive subjects, required identification and address information, and a working opt-out process under 15 U.S.C. 7704. Honor opt-outs within the statutory period and suppress them immediately in the operating system. Calls and texts require a separate analysis under 47 U.S.C. 227 and 47 CFR 64.1200; an email relationship does not automatically authorize automated promotional texts. Illustratively, $600 spent on referral administration and reviewed outreach produces three activated relationships, for $200 acquisition cost each. If each generates $120 weekly gross operator billings, the model retains $66 before remaining costs. With $26 incremental weekly servicing cost, weekly contribution toward acquisition is $40 and simple payback is five collected weeks. Track missed collections, early exits, and vehicle downtime by cohort; pause expansion when realized contribution cannot recover acquisition spending within the planned holding period.

Close the flywheel with an investor evidence packet that turns reliable execution into additional authorized vehicle supply. Report collected operator billings, investor distributions, maintenance history, days available, days off-road, and unresolved claims separately. Never substitute gross passenger trip value for operator revenue or imply that vehicle depreciation disappears because the operator owns no metal. An investor still bears or allocates that exposure through the agreement, and poor asset economics eventually damage retention. Ask for introductions only after a reviewed statement and resolved service issues demonstrate execution. Run a weekly operating review covering straight-through completion, net minutes saved, exception aging, collection accuracy, downtime, acquisition payback, and vendor spend. A useful initial test processes 200 representative historical events in shadow mode, deliberately including duplicates, reassigned plates, expired documents, delayed webhooks, and payment timeouts. Compare every proposed monetary entry with an independently reviewed result before live posting expands. Maintain a staffed manual queue, restricted offline dispatch roster, and documented vendor escalation contacts. When automation fails, stop the affected transition instead of the whole business. Resume from reconciled checkpoints after correcting the cause, and report recovered transactions separately from new activity so apparent growth cannot conceal a backlog being replayed.
