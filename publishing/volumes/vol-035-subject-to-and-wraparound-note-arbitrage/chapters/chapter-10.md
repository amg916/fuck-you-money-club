## 1. Build an evidence ledger before automating distressed-property research  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an event ledger, not an autonomous deal finder. The full-time principal owns acquisition economics and exception decisions; three contractors cover licensed RMLO underwriting, licensed servicing, and title or transaction coordination within their actual credentials. These are staffing equivalents, not permission for one contractor to perform every regulated function. Map eleven plays explicitly: public-record trigger ingestion, lien-and-rate screening, document extraction, seller qualification, RMLO packet assembly, servicer boarding, autopay exception routing, underlying-payment reconciliation, escrow surveillance, performance-content distribution, and social_to_search_ping_post_arbitrage. The first ten are this chapter's workflow labels; only the last is the supplied catalog identifier. Each workflow needs an input owner, an evidence pointer, a maximum processing cost, and a human exception destination. Use county recorder and court records where access permits, ATTOM data under contract, and seller-authorized statements rather than assuming a marketing database contains current loan terms. A foreclosure filing is a dated event, not proof of available equity or seller intent. Preserve jurisdiction, recording date, retrieval date, source identifier, and correction history. Suppress withdrawn matters promptly and never publish a person's distress status as promotional content.

Implement ingestion with Python's urllib.request, json, sqlite3, hashlib, hmac, and decimal modules, using a managed HTTPS endpoint for production webhooks. Validate the provider's documented signature over the original request bytes, enforce a timestamp window when supported, and compare digests with hmac.compare_digest. Store a unique provider-and-event key inside the same transaction as the received payload reference; acknowledge only after durable receipt. A separate worker advances received, validated, enriched, reviewed, and released states. Never treat an HTTP success response as proof that a mortgage payment posted. Use bounded retries with jitter for transient transport failures, a dead-letter queue for schema failures, and a daily provider-export comparison for missed events. For low-equity screening, compute estimated value less verified senior balances, junior liens, arrears, and transaction costs; label every unverified estimate visibly. A $210,000 estimate minus $198,000 debt, $4,000 arrears, and $9,000 costs produces negative $1,000 screening equity, not an acquisition instruction. Stop the workflow when lien priority, bankruptcy status, ownership, or reinstatement figures conflict. The coordinator resolves those conflicts against primary documents before the principal receives a price recommendation.

Keep language models downstream of evidence collection and upstream of human judgment. Send redacted statement extracts through an approved API account with contractual data controls, then demand structured fields containing value, source page, quoted evidence, and extraction status. Treat text inside uploaded documents as data, never as instructions to change recipients, release money, or retrieve additional files. Validate dates, currency, account suffixes, interest rates, and totals with deterministic Python checks; a plausible sentence cannot repair a failed balance reconciliation. A specimen run using 8,000 input tokens at $3 per million and 1,000 output tokens at $15 per million costs $0.039 for model usage. Add $0.020 extraction and $0.011 orchestration for $0.070 per completed run before review labor; these are planning rates, not vendor quotations. At 300 runs, machine cost is $21. If 15% require six minutes of review at $40 per hour, review adds $180. Reconcile the $201 combined cost to invoices and time logs. Route unreadable pages to manual entry with a second-person check, rather than repeating expensive prompts until an answer looks confident.

## 2. Automate packet preparation while humans retain origination and transfer decisions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Automate qualification logistics without automating a consumer credit decision. The seller bridge asks four factual questions: property location, ownership role, desired transaction timing, and permission for a specified response channel. Request loan statements only through an authenticated document link after explaining the transaction being evaluated. Keep a separate buyer application workflow supervised by a licensed RMLO; the chapter's operating standard routes every owner-occupant wrap buyer through that professional even where a particular statutory exemption might otherwise be considered. The SAFE Act licensing framework, 12 U.S.C. 5103, and Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.43, require transaction-specific analysis rather than a universal exemption checkbox. Python may assemble income documents, calculate proposed payment schedules, and flag missing obligations. It must not infer income from social profiles, rank protected characteristics, or tell an applicant they qualify. The RMLO verifies the applicable ability-to-repay treatment, reliable income evidence, debts, payment obligations, and underwriting disposition. Record who made each decision and which document version supported it. For credit applications, assign responsibility for Regulation B notices and timing explicitly; a stalled automation queue must never silently become an uncommunicated adverse decision.

Subject-to acquisition leaves the existing borrower obligated unless the lender actually releases them. A deed transfer does not rewrite the senior note, and an autopay authorization does not neutralize a due-on-sale clause. Under 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3, certain transfers receive statutory protection, but ordinary investment acquisitions cannot assume they qualify. Have transaction counsel examine the actual instruments and transfer facts before commitments, and document a response plan for acceleration using available liquidity, refinancing, sale, or another agreed resolution. Do not build a workflow whose success depends on concealing ownership changes from the lender or insurer. In Texas, route covered conveyances through the notice requirements of Property Code 5.016 and separately examine wrap mortgage requirements under Finance Code Chapter 159. These provisions have different scopes; satisfying one does not establish satisfaction of the other. The coordinator records recipients, delivery evidence, required timing, exceptions relied upon, and counsel's determination. Before closing, compare the final note, deed, servicing instructions, insurance arrangement, and settlement statement against the approved term sheet. Any changed payment date, balloon, lien position, or escrow responsibility returns the packet for substantive review.

Make the human review boundary executable through permissions and state transitions. A model-generated packet can enter prepared status but cannot enter authorized-to-close status without named reviewer records. Separate document preparation from approval and restrict bank-detail changes to an independent callback using a previously verified number. A seller's emailed replacement account cannot override the closing agent's verification procedure. Maintain an append-only decision log with document hashes, timestamps, reviewer identities, and reasons for overrides; hashes support integrity checks but do not prove that the original statements were accurate. Use a checklist that blocks completion when signatures, statutory delivery evidence, insurance confirmation, or servicing acceptance are missing. For a specimen 20-packet month, reducing assembly from 45 to 12 minutes saves 660 minutes, or 11 hours, while a required 20-minute human review per packet remains 400 minutes. At $40 per hour, assembly savings equal $440 before software cost; they do not justify removing review. When the RMLO or counsel is unavailable, pause commitments and send a factual scheduling update. Do not substitute an LLM explanation for the absent professional's decision.

## 3. Reconcile borrower cash, senior debt service, and escrow before recognizing spread  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Board each closed account with a licensed third-party loan servicer whose authority, jurisdictional coverage, contract, and operational controls match the loan. Ask prospective providers such as FCI Lender Services or Evergreen Note Servicing to substantiate coverage rather than assuming a brand name supplies every required authorization. Capture the wrap amortization schedule, senior loan schedule, escrow terms, payment destinations, borrower authorizations, and statement delivery obligations. Recurring electronic debits need appropriate authorization and a borrower copy under 12 CFR 1005.10; offer an alternative payment method where required instead of making recurring electronic repayment an improper condition of credit. Python listens for collection, return, allocation, and disbursement events, but the servicer controls actual money movement. Reconcile three distinct facts: borrower funds cleared, the underlying lender credited the correct installment, and tax or insurance obligations remain funded and current. A servicer's sent flag proves neither lender application nor continuing insurance coverage. Obtain authorized lender statements or transaction evidence monthly, reconcile shortages independently, and investigate suspense-account postings. When integrations fail, use secure exports and documented manual reconciliation; never recreate a debit merely because a webhook arrived late.

Separate cash movement from earned operator revenue with a component ledger. Suppose an illustrative wrap installment contains $1,450 interest and $250 principal, while the senior installment contains $950 interest and $350 principal. The interest spread is $500, but the payment cash difference is only $400 before servicing and other costs because principal amortization differs. A further $300 borrower escrow collection belongs in the escrow ledger, not spread revenue. This simplified example excludes fees and timing differences; an accountant determines the proper recognition and classification from the actual instruments. Apply the scorecard's retained-contribution convention so pass-through senior payments never inflate operator revenue, while separately evaluating ASC 606 for relevant services and applicable accounting guidance for loan interest. Do not invent a retained-share multiplier when none was supplied. For annual scenario reconciliation, $100,000 revenue less $72,000 expenses yields $28,000 profit; $475,000 less $285,000 yields $190,000; $850,000 less $408,000 yields $442,000. These are the canonical totals, not projected mortgage principal. Operational forecasts must reconcile net upfront spreads and retained interest to those totals without booking refundable deposits or acquisition debt proceeds as earned income.

Design exception handling around deadlines and liquidity, not dashboard color. An ACH return opens an incident linking the borrower account, original collection, underlying installment deadline, and authorized contact procedure. The servicer determines permitted retry handling and borrower communications; the principal decides whether available reserves must cover the senior payment. Never assume the buyer's default permits withholding payment on the underlying obligation. If six accounts each require $1,300 monthly senior debt service, three months of that obligation equals $23,400 before property taxes, insurance, repairs, and foreclosure expense. This is an illustrative liquidity requirement, not an extra scorecard expense or a universal reserve standard. Model reserve funding separately from recognized operating costs and confirm affordability within the $12,000–$65,000 startup capital range for the actual portfolio. Escrow surveillance compares bills, due dates, paid receipts, renewal declarations, and servicer balances, with alerts before the earliest actionable deadline. During a provider outage, assign one person to the exception register and another to payment verification. Resume automated processing only after exported balances, bank activity, and ledger totals agree. Record recovery time and financial exposure for every incident.
