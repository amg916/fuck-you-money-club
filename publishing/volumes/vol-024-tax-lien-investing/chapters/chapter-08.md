## 1. Build the authoritative source map before buying enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with the legal instrument, not a national property search. A Florida tax certificate, an Arizona certificate of purchase, and a Texas redeemable tax deed require different clocks, cash-flow fields, and resolution procedures. Create one jurisdiction profile per county, recording the collecting officer, auction contractor, registration requirements, current rules, payment cutoff, redemption contact, and source of recorded instruments. Florida Statutes sections 197.432 and 197.472 govern certificate sales and redemption mechanics; Arizona Revised Statutes sections 42-18114 and 42-18153 address interest bidding and redemption. Texas Tax Code section 34.21 instead supplies property-dependent redemption periods and redemption-premium mechanics. Store those provisions beside the county instructions rather than flattening everything into an interest-rate column. For Candidate #3, this chapter maps four operator plays: certificate auction screening, bid-down underwriting, redemption servicing, and post-Tyler resolution. Each play consumes the same evidence ledger but produces a different decision. Screening removes unsuitable instruments; underwriting prices the surviving exposure; servicing verifies cash and deadlines; resolution determines the lawful next action when redemption does not arrive. The full-time owner controls that ledger, while the two contractors supply parcel research and jurisdiction-specific title or procedural work.

The directory gives starting addresses and access paths, not a promise that every county exposes a stable API. Follow links from the current county website before entering bidder credentials or wiring funds; a familiar vendor name does not authenticate a payment destination. Record retrieval time separately from the source's effective date. An auction CSV downloaded today can still reflect yesterday's cancellations, while a recorder index can lag the receipt of a document. The refresh intervals below are desk polling targets unless a publisher supplies a release timestamp; they are not publisher service commitments. Treat prices similarly: free public access can coexist with charges for certified copies, bulk delivery, commercial reuse, or contractor labor. Contract-priced services require a written quote covering query volume, retained fields, storage rights, and historical coverage. Use conditional requests only where supported, honor published quotas and Retry-After headers, and stop automation when access controls prohibit it. A manual download is often the correct production interface for a three-person desk. The fallback must preserve authority: a collector's redemption confirmation outranks an aggregator's status flag, and an instrument image outranks a summarized ownership label.

Maintain a source register with owner, purpose, authentication method, permitted use, update evidence, retention period, and fallback contact. Separate discovery from dispositive evidence. ATTOM and Regrid can accelerate parcel matching, but neither replaces the certificate ledger, legal description, or recorded chain. OpenCorporates can suggest an entity identifier; the relevant secretary of state supplies the authoritative entity record. FEMA flood layers and EPA ECHO records flag exposure, not a complete physical or environmental evaluation. PACER reveals federal litigation and bankruptcy filings, but a name hit needs identity resolution and a current docket review. Build a short source packet for each shortlisted certificate: original sale row, parcel map, collector balance, recorded deed reference, relevant instruments, and unresolved exceptions. The research contractor prepares it; the owner checks instrument identity; the title or procedural contractor handles escalated defects. Save public records only to project storage with role-based access, and avoid distributing owner contact details beyond the task. This division keeps the Conservative and Base pod at 1 FT + 2 C while preserving a defensible trail from raw source to capital decision. At scale, add capacity without changing the evidence hierarchy.

## 2. Run an evidence-first enrichment waterfall with reversible joins  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a small, inspectable data model rather than a sprawling property warehouse. A SQLite database can hold jurisdictions, parcels, parcel aliases, certificates, source documents, events, cash movements, and exceptions. Scope every certificate key to its issuing jurisdiction and preserve the issuer's identifier verbatim. A tax year and parcel number alone may not distinguish supplemental assessments, multiple instruments, or historical numbering changes. Store raw identifiers as text so leading zeros survive spreadsheet exports. Normalize spacing and punctuation into separate matching fields, never over the evidence itself. For each imported row, record source URL, retrieval timestamp, effective date when published, document hash, parser version, and original row number. Python's csv, json, hashlib, datetime, decimal, and sqlite3 modules are sufficient for this foundation. Store money as integer cents or exact decimal text; do not let binary floating-point rounding drive settlement comparisons. Keep the immutable downloaded file beside the normalized tables under a controlled retention policy. An updated collector record creates a new observation, not a silent replacement. The owner must be able to reconstruct what the desk knew when a bid was authorized and what changed afterward.

The waterfall begins with free authoritative exclusions: withdrawn certificates, incompatible instrument types, missing parcel identity, unresolved sale eligibility, and payment deadlines the desk cannot meet. Next, join assessor and GIS records to examine land use, improvements, parcel size, and boundary continuity. Match the county identifier first; use legal descriptions and recorded references to investigate splits or mergers. Address similarity is supporting evidence, not a primary key. Only then buy commercial attributes that can change a bid or reduce expensive manual work. A property value estimate may support triage, but it cannot establish recoverable equity without senior interests, costs, condition, and the governing distribution rules. Escalate survivors to recorded-document review, bankruptcy checks, and targeted environmental or access investigation. Stop enrichment when a decisive defect appears; retain the rejection reason so the next sale does not trigger the same expense. Measure cost per accepted candidate and cost per funded certificate, not merely API cost per thousand records. For a 1 FT + 2 C pod, the research contractor works a bounded exception queue while the owner controls the purchase threshold and the specialist resolves instrument-level uncertainty.

Attach freshness requirements to decisions rather than pretending that one daily refresh makes every field current. Parcel geometry changes infrequently, but withdrawals and redemption postings can invalidate a bid or notice immediately. Before submitting an auction instruction, recheck the sale status and the county's current settlement directions. Before spending on a collection or foreclosure step, recheck the certificate balance, redemption status, relevant docket, and procedural dates. Configure source-specific retry ceilings, bounded concurrency, and explicit timeouts. On an HTTP 429 response, honor Retry-After when present; otherwise use conservative exponential backoff with jitter and a stop condition. On authentication failure, stop and repair access rather than rotating identities or bypassing restrictions. Quarantine HTML error pages returned where a CSV was expected. Validate required headers, row counts, duplicate keys, monetary signs, date formats, and totals before promoting an import. A surprisingly clean empty file is a failure until confirmed. The production dashboard should show last successful retrieval, source effective date, rejected rows, unresolved joins, and the oldest actionable exception. Send the owner a compact failure digest instead of burying substantive breaks beneath routine success notifications.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture an immutable sale snapshot
   - Download the permitted collector export and save its instructions alongside it.
   - Record retrieval time, published effective date, file hash, sale identifier, and source URL before parsing.
2. Validate and reconcile the import
   - Load rows into staging tables and compare counts, identifiers, and monetary totals.
   - Reject malformed dates, duplicated instrument keys, missing required fields, and unexpected schema changes into a visible exception queue.
3. Resolve identity before purchasing attributes
   - Join county parcel evidence first, then buy only the enrichment needed for surviving decisions.
   - Preserve aliases, confidence reasons, original documents, and a named reviewer for every consequential manual override.
4. Publish a decision packet and refresh its critical facts
   - Deliver the candidate packet to the owner and recheck time-sensitive sources before execution.
   - Freeze the reviewed version, record subsequent changes as events, and block affected actions when authoritative evidence conflicts.

## 3. Make bid-down diagnostics reconcile to realized revenue  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Keep the underwriting engine separate from the source parser. The parser reports the advertised assessment, permitted bid increment, statutory rate framework, and auction-specific charges; the engine applies a versioned jurisdiction rule set to a proposed bid. Florida's certificate auction uses interest-rate bidding under section 197.432, while section 197.472 includes redemption mechanics that require careful treatment of minimum charges and zero-percent certificates. Arizona has its own rate and accrual rules; do not copy Florida's calculation into an Arizona workbook. A Texas redemption premium under section 34.21 is not a quoted annual coupon, and its application depends on property classification, timing, and the statutory payment components. For every jurisdiction, test immediate redemption, partial calendar periods, the permitted zero-rate outcome, late posting, subsequent taxes, and reimbursable versus absorbed expenses. Use an independently checked example drawn from the current authority or a collector statement. Preserve both the contractual result and the cash date used for economic evaluation. An attractive nominal rate can produce poor realized economics after research costs and idle cash; a high redemption premium can coexist with substantial duration, title, and capital-loss exposure.

Use a deliberately simple diagnostic fixture before testing jurisdiction-specific cases. Suppose a hypothetical instrument permits simple annual interest, has $10,000 of principal, earns 8%, and redeems after exactly one-half year without additional charges. Gross interest is $400; the $10,000 principal return is a balance-sheet recovery, not revenue. If unreimbursed research and servicing cost $125, the illustrative contribution is $275 before shared overhead and other portfolio outcomes. This fixture tests accounting classification only; it is not a Florida, Arizona, or Texas statutory calculator. Candidate #3's canonical annual cases remain $15,000 revenue and $4,500 owner profit at 30%; $97,500 revenue and $41,438 owner profit at 42.5%; and $180,000 revenue and $99,000 owner profit at 55%. The Base calculation produces $41,437.50 before whole-dollar rounding. Do not multiply certificate face value by a take rate and label the result recognized operator revenue. Revenue comprises realized interest, penalties, and disposition gains. Maintain separate fields for contributed capital, principal deployed, principal returned, earned receipts, reimbursed advances, and realized losses. Portfolio projections must reconcile those fields rather than treating every bank deposit as operating income.

Budget tooling against the canonical expense envelope instead of selling automation as free margin. Conservative revenue less owner profit leaves $10,500 of annual costs; Base leaves $56,062.50 before display rounding; Scaled leaves $81,000. Those are total modeled cost envelopes, not permissions to spend the same amounts on subscriptions. The stated monthly operating range of $500 to $4,000 annualizes to $6,000 to $48,000, but transaction costs, contractor work, and other modeled expenses must also reconcile within each scenario rather than being counted twice. The $100,000 to $1,500,000 startup capital range describes funding needs, not revenue or a software budget. Likewise, the six-to-thirty-six-month first-deal window argues for reversible subscriptions and limited paid experiments. Track cash tied up by cohort, redemption duration, loss severity, contractor hours, and tool spending per funded certificate. Retain the 1 FT + 2 C structure for Conservative and Base; the Scaled case uses 2 FT + 3 C. These headcounts do not establish salary rates or spare capacity. Expand automation only when measured queue reduction exceeds its integration and maintenance burden without weakening source verification or procedural control.

## 4. Operate redemption tracking and post-Tyler resolution as evidence queues  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build redemption tracking as an event ledger with explicit states: held, redemption reported, collector remittance pending, cash received, reconciliation complete, and exception open. A portal label is not bank cash, and bank cash is not automatically interest. Link each remittance to its certificate and split principal, earned receipts, and recoverable advances using the collector's statement. Reconcile the bank ledger to the certificate subledger on every remittance cycle, with a weekly unresolved-item review by the owner. The research contractor can assemble statements and investigate identifiers, but the owner authorizes cash classification and resolves discrepancies. Keep county-specific calendars for application eligibility, expiration, notices, and required subsequent payments. Compute dates from verified statutory events, not from the date a spreadsheet row was created. Arizona Revised Statutes section 42-18201 supplies a foreclosure timing framework; Florida section 197.502 addresses tax-deed applications. Neither provision should become a national default. Configure reminders sufficiently early for document retrieval and specialist review, then confirm the live rule before taking action. Escalate approaching deadlines with the source citation, triggering event, proposed action, and named responsible person. Calendar automation should expose uncertainty, not conceal it behind a green status.

When redemption stalls, the fourth play is disciplined resolution, not automatic acquisition of every dollar of property equity. In Tyler v. Hennepin County, 598 U.S. 631 (2023), the Supreme Court held that the owner's pleaded claim concerning retained surplus stated a Fifth Amendment takings claim. The decision makes surplus ownership a central diligence question; it does not supply one uniform private-investor foreclosure procedure across states. Track current statutory distribution rules, relevant state decisions, sale proceeds, debt, allowable costs, and potential surplus claimants in the resolution packet. Florida section 197.582 supplies a concrete distribution framework for tax-deed sale proceeds. Separately, 11 U.S.C. section 362 can stay collection and foreclosure activity when applicable; a docket hit requires prompt specialist assessment, not an assumption that every tax-related action is prohibited or exempt. Federal liens can implicate 26 U.S.C. section 7425 or 28 U.S.C. section 2410 depending on the proceeding. Route these issues to the procedural contractor with instrument images and current docket evidence. Do not let an API's lien count substitute for the analysis of parties, priority, notice, discharge, redemption rights, and the actual method of sale.

Use diagnostics that a three-person pod can actually maintain. SQLite integrity checks test database structure; foreign-key checks expose orphaned events; duplicate-key reports detect repeat imports; and unit tests protect monetary classification and date rules. In QGIS, verify coordinate reference systems before interpreting a parcel overlay, then inspect apparent slivers or road-only parcels against recorded descriptions. Keep a small regression set containing a split parcel, a withdrawn certificate, a same-name bankruptcy mismatch, a zero-rate bid, a delayed remittance, and a disputed surplus allocation. Replay it after parser or rule changes. Back up source files and the database to controlled storage, and test restoration rather than merely counting backup jobs. Keep API credentials outside datasets and logs; restrict contractor access to the evidence needed for their assigned queue. Every week, the owner reviews stale sources, unresolved joins, upcoming statutory dates, cash mismatches, and paid enrichment that failed to change a decision. Every month, compare realized revenue, tool expense, contractor effort, and deployed capital with the selected canonical case. A useful system makes nonredeeming liens, title defects, and capital-loss exposure visible earlier; it does not claim to remove them.
