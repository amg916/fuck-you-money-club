## 1. Fund the collection delay before funding the workstation  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Start with a cash envelope, not a subscription list. The authorized startup range is $5,000–$35,000, while the first deal may take 3–12 months; neither fact establishes when the first service fee will clear. Separate formation spending, recoverable operating runway, and client principal in both the budget and bank records. The allocation below preserves $2,500 of a $5,000 launch, $12,000 of a $20,000 launch, and $21,000 of a $35,000 launch as working capital. Those reserves represent 50%, 60%, and 60% of starting cash. They are operating reserves, not money for advancing a claimant's expected distribution. At $1,500 monthly fixed overhead, the smallest reserve funds only 1.67 months, the middle reserve funds eight months, and the largest funds fourteen months. Therefore the $5,000 version is a constrained validation launch requiring separate earned income or additional committed funding before sustained production. Do not describe that envelope as a fully funded twelve-month business. Preserve the reserve in a separate business savings account, reconcile transfers weekly, and require a written case-level justification before consuming it for evidence purchases. Unspent setup allocations stay in cash rather than becoming permission to upgrade equipment.

Use the seven allocation categories as spending ceilings, not vendor quotations. Formation includes state filing charges, registered agent needs, and bookkeeping setup; obtain the actual state schedule before committing the amount. Legal configuration buys review of finder eligibility, contract timing, fee limits, and corporate signatory authority for the initial jurisdiction. It does not buy nationwide coverage. The equipment allowance assumes an existing usable computer at the smallest tier and encrypted replacement hardware only where necessary. Security setup includes authentication keys and recovery preparation rather than a decorative monitoring dashboard. Outreach funds a narrow batch of verified corporate contacts; evidence funds certified records, postage, and permitted document retrieval. When a jurisdiction requires registration, a bond, or insurance exceeding the envelope, reallocate discretionary setup cash or postpone that jurisdiction without reducing the reserve below half of starting capital. Review errors-and-omissions and cyber coverage for social engineering, identity-document handling, contractor acts, and any exclusion affecting recovery services. Request written coverage explanations rather than relying on a generic business-policy label. A home office and scheduled contractor hours are usually more useful here than leased premises, dedicated sales seats, or prepaid enterprise data.

Reconcile the operating plan to the canonical scorecard before setting a hiring budget. Conservative revenue of $60,000 less $45,000 total modeled costs yields $15,000 owner net profit. Base revenue of $255,000 less $159,375 costs yields $95,625. Scaled revenue of $450,000 less $225,000 costs yields $225,000. These are annual recognized service fees and annual owner profit, not recovered asset balances or monthly cash collections. For an illustrative cost bridge, assign fixed monthly overhead of $1,500, $3,500, and $8,000 respectively. Annual fixed costs then equal $18,000, $42,000, and $96,000, leaving $27,000, $117,375, and $129,000 for all remaining modeled costs. Those residuals are budget capacity, not permission to exclude labor or duplicate contractor charges. Keep 1 FT + 2 C in the first two cases and 2 FT + 3 C in Scaled; contractor counts do not imply full-time salaries. Record actual hours and payment terms. At $8,000 monthly overhead, even the $21,000 reserve lasts only 2.625 months before variable spending. Delay that burn rate until collected fees or additional committed capital support it. An unsigned prospect list contributes nothing to available runway.

## 2. Assemble twelve narrow layers without twelve paid subscriptions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the production stack around a case identifier that survives every export. Use a jurisdiction prefix, source property identifier, and internal sequence; keep owner names out of filenames that could appear in email previews. Python and SQLite perform record normalization and candidate scoring locally, while Airtable Free holds a limited operational queue containing case identifiers, dates, and task ownership rather than identity documents. Microsoft 365 Business Basic provides business email and controlled document storage. Choose one authoritative document repository instead of copying passports into the CRM, inbox, and contractor chat. The twelve layers below are functions, not twelve mandatory paid accounts. Several are free utilities, and the archive and OCR layers remain disabled until a concrete workload justifies them. Treat every stated price as an exact planning input for the named tier, not a verified current offer. Confirm checkout pricing, billing commitment, taxes, regional availability, and contractual data terms before purchase. Model annual commitments as cash obligations even when a vendor advertises a monthly equivalent. No paid tool should become a prerequisite for inspecting a public ledger, calculating a lawful fee, or telling an owner how to claim directly.

Use the matrix to distinguish license arithmetic from total operating cost. One Microsoft seat at $6 per month, one Bitwarden Teams seat at $4, and one Backblaze Computer Backup subscription at $9 produce a $19 monthly-equivalent software baseline under the listed assumptions. Add a $15 Dropbox Sign Essentials seat only after confirming the receiving agency accepts the resulting execution method. The baseline then becomes $34, before domain registration, taxes, transaction charges, or optional usage. Microsoft and Bitwarden annual billing alone implies $120 of yearly license commitment for one seat each; budget the actual payment schedule rather than assuming monthly cancellation. For optional Amazon S3 storage, 100 GB at $0.023 per GB-month creates $2.30 of storage charges before requests and transfer. For Amazon Textract Detect Document Text, 1,000 pages at $0.0015 per page creates $1.50 of processing charges under the stated regional tier. These calculations are procurement assumptions, not the whole $1,500–$8,000 operating envelope. Most spending belongs to people, evidence, owner acquisition, insurance, and administration. Add contractor licenses only when access is necessary, and remove those accounts when the assignment ends.

Automation should produce reviewable evidence, not impersonate the claimant or assert ownership. Store each downloaded ledger with source URL, retrieval timestamp, permitted-use notes, and a SHA-256 digest. Preserve source spelling alongside normalized names so punctuation cleanup cannot erase an important distinction. A SQLite uniqueness constraint on jurisdiction and property identifier prevents duplicate outreach; a separate ownership table records the Secretary of State entity number and evidence supporting each link. GitHub Free stores code and synthetic test fixtures only, never live claimant documents or credentials. Require multifactor authentication on business accounts, encrypt workstations, and restrict contractor access to assigned folders. Test a backup restoration before trusting a successful upload indicator. If OCR fails on a warrant image, compare the original manually instead of allowing the extracted amount to drive a fee quote. If a portal lacks an authorized automation route, use scheduled manual checks rather than bypassing access controls or flooding endpoints. Maintain a documented retention schedule that reconciles agency requirements, contractual needs, disputes, and applicable privacy duties. Cheap infrastructure remains useful only when an operator can explain where each fact originated and who changed it.

## 3. Underwrite the recovery play before scaling record matching  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The priority implementation is unclaimed_property_escheatment_arbitrage, the TRACK_1 State Treasury Escheatment & Unclaimed Corporate Warrant Recovery play. Ingest permitted state controller or treasury ledgers and uncashed municipal or corporate warrant registries, prioritizing amounts above $25,000. Separate a warrant still payable by its issuer from property already held by a state treasury; the correct obligor determines the evidence route. Cross-match corporate officers through Secretary of State filings, then establish entity continuity through name changes, mergers, dissolutions, and assignments. An officer-name match creates a research lead, not authority to sign a recovery agreement. The play's commercial fee range is 10–25%, but that range never overrides state law. California Code of Civil Procedure §1582 supplies agreement requirements and a 10% ceiling for covered recovery agreements; Texas Property Code §74.507 also controls covered recovery assistance agreements and limits compensation. Read each provision's scope and timing before configuring intake. Store the governing jurisdiction, eligible signing date, maximum permitted compensation, required disclosures, and reviewer in the case record. When a timing restriction is unresolved, defer solicitation or execution as applicable instead of relabeling the fee as research.

Use a $30,000 eligible claim and a lawful 10% contingent fee as the transparent unit model. Successful recovery produces $3,000 of operator service fees and leaves $27,000 with the owner before any separately applicable owner obligations. The $30,000 principal never becomes operator revenue. Twenty such paid recoveries generate the Conservative $60,000 revenue case; eighty-five generate Base revenue of $255,000; one hundred fifty generate Scaled revenue of $450,000. These are arithmetic equivalents, not asserted throughput or conversion rates. A $100,000 claim at 10% produces $10,000 of fees, while a $30,000 claim at 25% would produce $7,500 only where that rate and agreement are lawful. Do not build the business around the higher rate without jurisdiction-specific support. For triage, suppose an eligible $3,000 fee has a 40% collection probability, $500 of remaining direct costs, and a $300 collection-delay allowance. Its planning contribution is $1,200 minus $800, or $400 before fixed overhead. Document those assumptions rather than presenting them as historical performance. Reject low-value cases when evidence costs consume expected contribution; combine records only when ownership and agency procedures genuinely support aggregation.

Customer distrust is an operating constraint, not a copywriting problem to solve with urgency. Disclose prominently that the owner can claim directly from the relevant state without paying a recovery service, provide the official claim URL, and explain the paid work actually offered. Avoid government styling, invented deadlines, or claims of exclusive access to public records. Verify a corporate contact through an independently obtained business channel before requesting identity documents. Have distributions sent directly to the verified owner whenever the agency process permits; invoice the contractual service fee separately after the agreed earning event. If a process requires custody of client money, resolve its legal, banking, and accounting requirements before accepting that workflow. Obtain an EIN through the IRS without paying a reseller merely to submit the application. Keep the entity account, reserve account, and bookkeeping ledger distinct from personal spending. California Civil Code §1798.81.5 requires reasonable security for covered personal information; translate applicable duties into restricted folders, secure transmission, vendor review, and documented deletion. Identity mismatch sends the case back to evidence review. It never becomes an invitation to submit a more persuasive but unsupported ownership narrative.

## 4. Prove one complete claim workflow before increasing overhead  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Pre-launch readiness means a complete evidence trail can survive handoff, correction, and recovery from failure. Select one jurisdiction, one corporate claimant profile, and a small permitted sample of public records. Run the intake through deduplication, ownership review, statutory configuration, outreach drafting, agreement preparation, evidence indexing, and claim-status tracking. Use synthetic identity documents during rehearsal and do not submit a fictional claim to an agency. Confirm that every agreement states the correct fee basis, treats timing restrictions correctly, and explains the owner's direct claim option. Recalculate the fee independently of the template so a stale percentage cannot silently survive. Have the evidence contractor explain the corporate ownership chain to the principal operator without access to unrelated cases. Then restore the rehearsal folder from backup and compare its inventory with the original. Record missing permissions, broken links, incomplete signatures, and ambiguous amount fields as defects with an assigned owner. No claim enters production merely because the CRM shows a green status. Readiness is the ability to reconstruct the entire case from source records without relying on someone's memory or an undocumented conversation with the claimant.

Install a thirteen-week cash forecast before signing recurring contracts. Separate beginning cash, collected fees, fixed payments, case-variable payments, taxes reserved from actual activity, and ending unrestricted cash. Keep prospective recoveries in a separate schedule until the contractual earning and collection conditions are satisfied. A state acknowledging a claim does not establish that the operator's invoice is earned or paid. At the $1,500 fixed-overhead planning level, one useful monthly allocation is $120 for software and connectivity, $500 for scheduled support, $250 for evidence and delivery, $200 for outreach, $180 for insurance and administration, and $250 for contingency. These six amounts total $1,500; replace them with actual quotes and avoid charging the same expense again to a variable-cost line. During each weekly review, compare committed obligations with reserve cash and the next eight weeks of unavoidable payments. If the cash floor is threatened, pause discretionary evidence purchases, reduce outreach, and defer additional jurisdictions before missing essential security or filing obligations. Owner distributions follow funded operating needs, not the headline amount of a newly discovered property. A promising claim remains illiquid until the relevant cash actually arrives.

Expand only after measuring the bottleneck with paid-case data. Track eligible records reviewed, confirmed ownership matches, owner responses, signed lawful agreements, complete submissions, agency deficiency requests, recoveries, earned fees, and cash receipts as separate events. Record elapsed days between events instead of collapsing them into one conversion percentage. A high deficiency rate calls for better evidence preparation, not additional prospect volume. A low response rate calls for verified contacts and clearer direct-claim disclosure, not escalating pressure. The first two scorecard cases retain 1 FT + 2 C: the principal controls economics and claimant relationships while narrowly scoped contractors support research and document preparation. The Scaled case permits 2 FT + 3 C, but additional access follows demonstrated workload and documented responsibilities. Require confidentiality terms, least-privilege access, and deletion confirmation when assignments end. Expand matching automation only after a reviewed sample demonstrates acceptable false-positive handling. Expand claim-status tracking through permitted interfaces or disciplined reminders, not unattended assumptions about agency progress. Maintain the same revenue boundary at every size: client assets measure recovered value, collected contractual fees support cash planning, and recognized service fees feed the canonical profit model. These are related but different ledgers.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the launch envelope
   - Allocate starting cash and transfer the reserve into a separate business account.
   - Use the exact budget columns, replace allowances with quotes, and retain at least half of starting capital as working capital.
2. Configure one lawful intake lane
   - Build one jurisdiction-specific record schema, fee rule, timing rule, and agreement template.
   - Include source identifiers, corporate authority evidence, direct-claim disclosure, and a documented review path for ambiguous cases.
3. Rehearse and restore the case
   - Run a synthetic claim packet through matching, preparation, permissions, and backup recovery.
   - Do not file the rehearsal with an agency; independently check fee arithmetic and the complete evidence inventory.
4. Release a measured live cohort
   - Begin narrowly scoped authorized outreach and update the cash forecast every week.
   - Track ownership errors, agency deficiencies, collection delays, earned fees, and receipts separately before increasing fixed overhead.
