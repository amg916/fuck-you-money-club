## 1. Build an eighteen-source directory around custody, ownership, and permitted access  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with the custodian, not the enrichment vendor. State treasury systems establish that a property record exists; they do not establish that your prospect owns it, that the displayed amount is complete, or that a finder may charge immediately. Create one source register with jurisdiction, official landing URL, documented access method, retrieval timestamp, publication date when available, and the current finder rule reference. MissingMoney provides useful participating-state discovery, while California, Texas, Florida, and New York supply jurisdiction-specific search and claim instructions. Coverage differs, so a missing aggregator result never closes a lead. Corporate uncashed warrants need an additional classification: some remain with the issuing agency before eventual treasury custody. Record the warrant issuer, issue date, stale-date procedure, and replacement route before calling the item escheated property. The directory below gives operating check intervals rather than promising publisher refresh schedules. Check terms and current official links at onboarding, because portal vendors and export permissions change. A browser search box is not permission to replay private endpoints or bypass interactive access controls. Prefer an agency-approved extract when recurring manual search becomes the bottleneck.

Use entity evidence to turn an attractive name into a defensible claimant chain. SEC submissions identify registrants and filing histories; secretary-of-state records establish entity status and filed name changes; merger documents connect predecessor names to surviving corporations. None independently proves entitlement to a particular treasury item. A dissolved company may require a winding-up representative, and an asset purchase may exclude cash receivables that a stock acquisition would leave inside the same legal entity. Retrieve the operative document rather than inferring succession from matching websites. For an estate, use the relevant county court's authorized records channel and obtain the appointment instrument needed by the custodian; a death notice does not appoint a representative. For a warrant, confirm the payee's original legal identity with the issuing agency before building a treasury claim. Store public business evidence separately from owner identity documents, with narrower access for the latter. PACER, county clerks, and state filing offices are fallback evidence sources, not universal entitlement APIs. Measure evidence completeness by missing required instruments, not by the number of commercial databases queried during research.

The directory deliberately mixes official sources, commercial APIs, and diagnostic tools without pretending they have equivalent authority. OpenCorporates can accelerate entity discovery, Melissa can normalize an address, and Twilio Lookup can describe a telephone number; none proves ownership or permission to contact. Use commercial results as leads back to authoritative records. For sources without published quotas, choose low-volume manual research or obtain a written extract arrangement instead of inventing a safe requests-per-second number. For documented APIs, record the account quota, identify the client where required, cache stable responses, and honor throttling responses. Cost entries distinguish free public search from paid copies and contracted usage; procurement must capture current prices before activation. The eleven recovery mappings in this chapter are local operating labels: treasury discovery, warrant tracing, predecessor resolution, merger succession, estate authority, address normalization, contact verification, property deduplication, claim packaging, status monitoring, and fee reconciliation. The twelfth mapping is the specified transferable-credit adjacency. This distinction keeps an incomplete cross-volume roster from becoming invented candidate metadata while still giving the desk twelve concrete workflows to instrument and evaluate.

## 2. Turn discovery into a provenance-preserving enrichment waterfall  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the ingestion layer with Python's urllib.request, csv, json, sqlite3, hashlib, and datetime before purchasing an orchestration platform. Use only documented API or export access authorized for the source; browser-only portals stay in a manual research queue. Preserve each permitted source artifact with retrieval time, original URL, content hash, parser version, and source publication date when supplied. A hash supports change detection and integrity checks, not confidentiality. Keep raw artifacts separate from normalized records so a parser correction can be replayed without silently rewriting evidence. Normalize business suffixes, punctuation, whitespace, and address components into comparison fields while retaining the original strings. Use the custodian's property identifier as the primary deduplication anchor when available; otherwise build a composite candidate key and flag collisions for review. Two records sharing a name and amount can still represent different properties. A disappearing search result is not proof of payment. Represent it as a source observation requiring confirmation. SQLite transactions should commit artifact metadata and normalized records together, preventing an apparently complete lead from pointing to evidence that was never actually saved.

Run enrichment in a strict waterfall: property existence, entity identity, historical address, successor authority, claimant contact, then document collection. Spend on the next stage only when the prior stage supports a plausible claim. Illustratively, reviewing 200 records at three minutes each requires ten hours; at a $35 loaded hourly rate, discovery costs $350. If twenty records survive, assign a $0.25 budget ceiling per initial commercial address check, adding $5 rather than enriching all 200. Those are internal planning assumptions, not quoted vendor prices. If five prospects then require twenty minutes of authority review, another $58.33 brings cohort research cost to $413.33 before outreach and claim preparation. Stop when expected collectible fees cannot cover the remaining workload. Matching scores must expose evidence: exact historical address, documented predecessor name, official entity identifier, and corroborated representative authority. A common surname plus a nearby city should never trigger automatic claimant selection. Route conflicting names, competing successors, estate disputes, and multiple plausible owners into an exception queue. The fallback is a direct custodian inquiry or additional owner evidence, not more aggressive personal-data collection from unrelated commercial sources.

Keep sensitive claimant documents out of enrichment payloads and ordinary CRM notes. Store identification, tax identifiers, estate instruments, and bank instructions in a restricted document repository with access logs and a defined retention schedule. Contractors receive only the records needed for their assigned review; the owner handles disputed authority and payment instructions. Public availability does not erase contractual restrictions or make every personal-data product suitable for this purpose. Do not obtain consumer reports without a qualifying purpose under 15 U.S.C. § 1681b, or motor-vehicle personal information outside 18 U.S.C. § 2721's permitted-use framework. Prefer business filings and claimant-supplied evidence. Outreach should identify the recovery business, explain the specific property lead without exposing unnecessary personal details, disclose the owner's ability to claim directly, and state the proposed fee basis. A commercial phone lookup is neither contact permission nor proof that the person answering owns the asset. Maintain suppression requests across vendors and campaigns. Measure false matches, unresolved authority conflicts, document rework, and accepted claims by source. Those diagnostics reveal whether the waterfall is improving decision quality rather than merely producing more completed database fields.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the source and permitted access
   - Record the official URL, access method, cost basis, and source-specific restrictions.
   - Assign a custodian and review cadence; use manual research when no authorized automated interface exists.
2. Capture and normalize without overwriting evidence
   - Save the permitted artifact, compute its hash, and write normalized comparison fields.
   - Retain original names and addresses; commit evidence references and parsed records in one database transaction.
3. Resolve identity and representative authority
   - Collect authoritative business or estate instruments and document conflicting candidate matches.
   - Permit contact enrichment only after the record passes the defined evidence threshold and expected-fee screen.
4. Submit, monitor, and reconcile the fee
   - Track authorized claim milestones and link owner receipt to the contractual fee calculation.
   - Recognize earned service fees under the desk's accounting policy; keep client asset values outside operator revenue.

## 3. Instrument the desk against the canonical fee economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Anchor every dashboard to recognized service fees, not the face value of property discovered or money returned to owners. The canonical Conservative case is $60,000 revenue, $45,000 implied total expense, and $15,000 owner net profit at 25%. Base is $255,000 revenue, $159,375 expense, and $95,625 profit at 37.5%. Scaled is $450,000 revenue, $225,000 expense, and $225,000 profit at 50%. Conservative and Base each use one full-time person plus two contractors; Scaled uses two full-time people plus three contractors. These are annual scorecard outcomes, not promises that this month's leads will produce next month's collections. The $1,500–$8,000 monthly operating-spend range does not replace the scenario's total implied expense envelope; staffing, variable processing, and other modeled costs must reconcile inside that envelope. Budget $5,000–$35,000 startup capital separately and plan for three to twelve months before the first deal. A source subscription that accelerates discovery but not document acceptance may deepen the cash trough. Track committed spend and expected collection dates weekly so delayed claims do not force premature fee invoices or careless acceptance of weak matches.

Use an explicitly illustrative ten-percent fee only where the applicable state rules, waiting period, registration, contract form, and property category permit it. At that rate, the Conservative revenue corresponds to $600,000 recovered client assets, Base to $2,550,000, and Scaled to $4,500,000. Those principal amounts stay outside recognized desk revenue. If an average successful claim produces $2,000 of collectible fees, the Conservative case requires thirty successes annually; Base can comprise 127 successes at $2,000 plus one at $1,000; Scaled requires 225 successes at $2,000. These decompositions explain arithmetic without asserting a universal claim-size distribution. For prospect screening, a $10,000 property with a permitted ten-percent fee and a twenty-percent probability of collectible success yields $200 expected fees. Subtract $70 research, $40 documentation, and $15 communication to leave $75 before fixed overhead and delay costs. If the property is only $1,000, the same probability produces $20 expected fees and fails that cost screen. Batch related claims with shared authority documents where the custodian permits it, but never combine owners or omit property-specific evidence merely to improve apparent unit economics.

Make pipeline diagnostics explain lost time rather than decorate a dashboard. Use SQLite integrity checks, foreign-key enforcement, unique property constraints, and unittest fixtures for parser regressions. A saved HTML example should demonstrate what happens when a portal returns a maintenance page instead of search results. Test missing amount fields, multiple owner rows, punctuation changes, and withdrawn records without silently converting absence into zero value. Structured logs should record source, batch, parser version, response status, elapsed time, and retry count while excluding claimant identifiers and document contents. Honor Retry-After where supplied, use bounded exponential backoff, and stop repeatedly failing jobs instead of hammering a source. Alert on abrupt count changes, stale active claims, duplicate submissions, and fees without supporting recovery evidence. Separate submitted, accepted, paid-to-owner, invoiced, collected, and recognized-fee milestones; cash collection and revenue recognition may occur on different dates. Reconcile the fee ledger to payment records and the accounting ledger monthly. The owner reviews exceptions while contractors process documented research tasks. This division protects the small-team model because automation reduces repetitive observation without delegating disputed ownership or payment authority to a matching score.

## 4. Qualify the transferable-credit adjacency without confusing leads, credits, and fees  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The specified adjacent play is transferable_tax_credit_brokerage_syndication, Transferable Federal & State Tax Credit Brokerage & Syndication (IRA § 6418 / Film / Historic), TRACK_1. Reuse the desk's provenance and entity-resolution capabilities, not its unclaimed-property fee assumptions. DOE and EPA publications can identify projects, developers, and contaminated-land opportunities, but there is no single universal DOE/EPA interconnection queue that establishes transferable credits. Actual queue research belongs with ISO, RTO, and utility sources such as PJM; queue position does not prove construction, placed-in-service status, credit amount, or tax eligibility. Georgia and Louisiana film offices, state revenue agencies, and historic preservation programs provide separate lead channels whose certification and transfer rules must be examined individually. Federal historic rehabilitation credits under IRC § 47 are not automatically transferable under § 6418. State historic and brownfield programs may have their own transfer mechanics. Record the exact credit statute, issuing authority, project entity, tax year, certification stage, and buyer-use constraints before soliciting offers. When the public record cannot establish eligibility, request seller documentation or discard the lead instead of converting a project listing into an asserted tax asset.

The supplied commercial spread assumes origination at $0.82–$0.88 per dollar of credit and placement at $0.92–$0.96. On $1,000,000 face value, those ranges imply $820,000–$880,000 seller proceeds and $920,000–$960,000 buyer outlay, with a paired gross spread of $40,000–$140,000 before insurance, diligence, escrow, and intermediary costs. At $0.85 and $0.94, the illustrative spread is $90,000. These are supplied pricing assumptions, not current market quotations or automatically recognized broker revenue. Crucially, IRC § 6418(e)(2) prevents a transferee from making a subsequent transfer election for the acquired credit. Therefore, do not implement the federal workflow as the desk buying a credit and reselling it. Structure a direct eligible transfer between the originating taxpayer and eligible unrelated buyer, with separately documented intermediary compensation and transaction expenses. A buyer-funded escrow can coordinate that direct transfer and disbursements without making the desk an intermediate credit owner. Corporate and high-net-worth buyers still require tax-capacity analysis; passive-activity restrictions can materially constrain an individual's ability to use purchased credits. For state credits, verify whether resale is permitted rather than importing federal mechanics or pricing.

The risk fallback is strict: never hold unhedged credit inventory or advance the seller's proceeds from the recovery desk's operating cash. Require buyer-funded escrow, synchronized document delivery, and non-cancelable tax-credit insurance as the specified play's execution condition, while examining the actual policy's exclusions, limits, retention, representations, and claim procedures. Non-cancelable wording does not erase fraud exclusions or establish coverage for every recapture event. If suitable coverage or an eligible funded buyer cannot be secured, decline execution or remain a disclosed origination-only intermediary without principal exposure. For federal credits, verify required pre-filing registration, transfer-election documentation, cash consideration, and the seller's supporting credit calculation under the applicable regulations. Keep credit face value, seller proceeds, buyer outlay, escrow balances, and earned intermediary compensation in distinct ledger fields. None of this illustrative adjacency changes Volume 007's $60,000, $255,000, and $450,000 canonical service-revenue cases. Launch it only with separate workflow ownership and transaction expertise. The transferable skill is disciplined evidence handling; the dangerous assumption is that finding a public project creates a freely tradable asset or an immediately collectible brokerage fee.
