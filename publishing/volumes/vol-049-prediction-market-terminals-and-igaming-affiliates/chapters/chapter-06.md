## 1. Mine observable buying friction instead of broad gambling interest  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with two separate prospect ledgers: organizations purchasing non-custodial analytics, and licensed operators purchasing attributable customer acquisition. For analytics, inspect public Kalshi documentation, ForecastEx access arrangements through authorized intermediaries, published market schedules, permitted developer communities, and job advertisements seeking market-data engineers. A release that changes an API field, a documented disconnect problem, or a research team manually matching contracts is stronger intent than a large social following. For gaming, use NJ DGE, PA PGCB, and MI MGCB operator and supplier records to establish identity and licensing status before studying public affiliate-program terms. Add operator earnings presentations, state revenue reports, affiliate-manager hiring, and dated promotion changes as business signals, not evidence that a particular budget exists. Record source URL, observation date, jurisdiction, suspected problem, reachable business contact, and the exact evidence supporting outreach. Review twenty new records each weekday and reject records without a concrete operational problem. Use SQLite for the evidence ledger and a small CRM for contact history; neither requires expensive enrichment subscriptions. Preserve only necessary business contact information. Do not harvest bettor identities, private community messages, self-exclusion lists, or account-level transaction histories to manufacture an audience. Public availability does not override platform access terms or redistribution rights.

Translate each signal into a testable offer before finding an email address. A terminal buyer with intermittent feeds receives a timestamped reliability diagnostic and a proposed paid dashboard pilot; an affiliate manager receives a state-specific comparison-page sample and an attribution test plan. Separate market probability, executable price, fees, spread, settlement definitions, and observation time in every sample. Two contracts with similar titles may resolve differently, so a cross-venue price gap is not automatically an executable edge. Use WebSocket subscriptions only where supported and contractually permitted; otherwise use approved polling or licensed feeds. Promise a measured service level after testing rather than describing every source as sub-second. Mine Google Search Console queries on owned pages for recurring workflow questions, then publish instrumented answers with a business-demo invitation. Prioritize queries about reconciliation, contract matching, latency, and jurisdiction coverage over generic betting tips. A page receiving 200 qualified visits and six demo requests has a 3% visitor-to-demo rate; it has not generated six customers. Record subscriptions, permitted builder payments, and licensed gaming commissions in different CRM opportunity types. This prevents a promising analytics pilot from silently becoming compensated transaction solicitation without the necessary registration analysis and contractual permissions.

The twelve-play matrix below is this chapter's operating map, not a claim that twelve separate upstream identifiers were supplied. Its named credit play, cloud_ai_startup_credit_aggregation_arbitrage, supports the other eleven through lower eligible infrastructure cash expense. Form a Delaware or Wyoming entity only for a genuine standalone product with its own records, ownership disclosures, and business purpose. Check current AWS Activate, Azure Founders Hub, Google Cloud for Startups, Cloudflare, OpenAI, and Anthropic program availability, eligibility, partner verification, expiration, and permitted use directly; published headline amounts and eligibility change. A hypothetical $25,000 award against $1,000 of eligible monthly usage offsets only $12,000 during a twelve-month validity period. It does not create $25,000 of sales or authorize combining incompatible awards. The play's advertised $50,000–$350,000 aggregate range is a research target, not a budget assumption. Apply through verified founder or incubator channels and retain written award terms. Never create shell startups to repeat introductory offers, resell API credentials, or assume credits transfer between entities. Maintain an exportable extraction pipeline and test a licensed open-weight Llama or DeepSeek model through vLLM where hardware supports it. A rehearsed failover reduces interruption risk; it cannot establish zero downtime without measured capacity and recovery tests.

## 2. Qualify permission, buyer intent, and collectible unit economics  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build qualification around five scored dimensions worth twenty points each: lawful service scope, evidenced operational pain, reachable purchasing authority, technical readiness, and collectible contribution. Require eighty points and a named budget owner for a paid pilot; require complete permission evidence regardless of total score. Analytics publishing is not automatically outside CFTC registration merely because software never touches money. The commodity trading advisor definition and exclusions in 7 U.S.C. § 1a(12), registration requirements in 7 U.S.C. § 6m, and applicable exemptions in 17 CFR § 4.14 must be evaluated against the actual service. Compensated solicitation or order handling can raise introducing-broker questions under 7 U.S.C. § 1a(31) and 17 CFR § 1.3; accepting customer funds raises additional FCM issues. Document whether outputs are impersonal, whether compensation depends on transactions, and whether links, onboarding, recommendations, or routing change the activity. Do not hold funds, pool capital, collect trading credentials, or direct United States users to unlicensed offshore, crypto, or unlawful sweepstakes venues. If the classification remains unresolved, restrict the offer to the reviewed reporting scope and defer the disputed compensation feature. A customer signature does not establish regulatory permission.

For gaming prospects, verify the operator's exact contracting entity, permitted products, jurisdictions, supplier requirements, and permitted marketing channels. New Jersey casino-service requirements under N.J.S.A. 5:12-92 warrant specific review; Pennsylvania and Michigan classifications require their own regulator checks rather than borrowing a New Jersey determination. West Virginia and Ontario are separate expansion workstreams with local rules, not extra boxes on a United States campaign. This project uses a 21+ audience restriction; Ontario's legal gambling age is nineteen, so twenty-one is a stricter product choice there. Pair age screening with jurisdiction-aware presentation, accurate responsible-gaming resources, and operator-side eligibility verification. A website geolocation estimate cannot replace an operator's regulated location checks. Ask how self-exclusion suppression and prohibited targeting are handled without obtaining protected lists yourself. FTC endorsement requirements under 16 CFR § 255.5 make material affiliate relationships relevant to the comparison page itself, not merely an obscure footer. Use an obvious compensation disclosure beside ranked offers and keep paid placement distinct from measured product attributes. Archive dated operator authorization, regulator status evidence, creative versions, and allowed-channel terms. Recheck these records before launching a new jurisdiction or changing the acquisition method.

Underwrite each prospect with an acceptance-adjusted worksheet, not the advertised commission. An illustrative sportsbook cohort produces twenty accepted first-time depositors at $150 CPA, or $3,000 of operator revenue. Deduct $1,400 traffic acquisition, $300 content expense, $200 attribution expense, and a $150 contract-supported reversal allowance to leave $950 contribution before shared overhead. If only fifteen depositors qualify, revenue falls to $2,250 and the same expenses leave $200. Confirm whether duplicate customers, jurisdiction mismatches, delayed identity checks, or deposit thresholds cause rejection. For revenue sharing, define the contractual NGR starting point before subtracting anything. If $20,000 excludes neither bonuses nor administration, $3,000 bonuses and $1,000 administration leave a $16,000 commission base; twenty-five percent pays $4,000. If those expenses already reduce reported NGR, subtracting them again understates revenue. A prior $5,000 negative balance applied before the split reduces this example to $2,750; a different carryover convention produces different results. Obtain the actual formula and a worked invoice. Model half the acquisition volume during a budget contraction and a full settlement-cycle payment delay. Reject contracts whose deductions, carryover grouping, caps, or dispute rights cannot be expressed as reproducible arithmetic.

> 
> A high qualification score cannot compensate for missing permissions, unsafe access, or unreconciled commercial terms.
> - Verify the precise analytics, referral, or affiliate activity against applicable registration requirements and retain the written scope determination.

## 3. Run a permission-aware cadence that sells a bounded first outcome  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Use a six-month prospect cohort with explicit stage definitions. Start with 300 raw business records, retain 120 evidence-qualified accounts, reach forty-eight live conversations, complete twenty-four discovery sessions, issue twelve scoped proposals, and sign six paid agreements. Those transitions are 40%, 40%, 50%, 50%, and 50%; the complete raw-record-to-signature rate is 2%. These are planning assumptions, not observed industry benchmarks. A plausible six-win mix is three analytics subscriptions, two licensed affiliate agreements, and one publisher integration. An affiliate signature counts as a commercial win but contributes no commission until qualifying activity occurs under the contract. Track cash collection as a separate stage. During month one, finish entity records, scope review, source permissions, and the first sixty qualified accounts. Months two and three should produce initial discovery and bounded paid pilots; months four through six emphasize referenceable delivery and repeatable conversion. Three wins at the same assumed conversion rate require 150 raw records; ten require 500. Capacity and segment quality may change those rates, so expand only after measuring a completed cohort. When conversion misses, identify the failing transition rather than declaring that the entire market lacks demand or purchasing a much larger contact list.

Run a five-touch business cadence across twenty-one days, stopping immediately after an objection to further contact. On day one, send a short individual email naming the observed workflow defect and offering one relevant artifact. On day four, use a professional-network message only when the contact's role clearly matches the problem. On day eight, send the artifact with an explanation of its limitations and one measurable pilot objective. On day fourteen, request a brief working session through the established business channel; avoid unsolicited automated telephone or text campaigns. On day twenty-one, close the sequence with a useful checklist and explain that further contact will stop unless requested. Apply CAN-SPAM requirements in 15 U.S.C. § 7704 to commercial email, including accurate identity, required address information, and functioning opt-out handling. Maintain suppression records rather than deleting objections and accidentally reimporting contacts. A concise opening is: 'Your public integration note describes stale contract timestamps. We built a permitted-data comparison showing where freshness diverges. Would a fifteen-minute review with your feed owner help determine whether a fixed-price monitoring pilot is useful?' Do not claim customer results, operator relationships, latency measurements, or registrations that the evidence ledger cannot substantiate.

Sell a narrow acceptance test rather than an ambitious platform replacement. For an analytics prospect, offer a $500 diagnostic followed by a thirty-day $1,000 team pilot, with separately defined feed permissions, supported contracts, refresh measurements, and support hours. These are illustrative offer prices, not additions to the canonical annual cases. A five-hour diagnostic at $50 per contractor hour leaves $250 before overhead; a pilot requiring twenty hours at that rate has consumed its entire fee before hosting. Limit scope or change price instead of hiding the labor. For an affiliate prospect, offer a synthetic click-to-postback demonstration and a small approved placement test after contractual permissions are complete. Specify accepted event definitions, deduplication keys, attribution windows, reversal handling, and the reconciliation owner. For publishers, define display rights and revenue-share treatment before installing a widget. End discovery by asking which single outcome would justify renewal and who can authorize it. Send a one-page scope within one business day, including deliverables, dependencies, acceptance criteria, price, and invoicing milestones. Never exchange a discount for a favorable endorsement; negotiate a smaller scope or longer commitment instead. Record refusal reasons verbatim and use the objection matrix to distinguish fixable uncertainty from a structurally bad deal.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture a reproducible buying signal
   - Create one evidence record before creating an outreach task.
   - Store the public source, date, exact problem, jurisdiction, business owner, and permitted artifact that demonstrates relevance.
2. Score the prospect and verify permissions
   - Complete all five qualification dimensions and resolve mandatory scope checks.
   - Require at least eighty points, a named budget owner, and documented permissions; defer any activity whose classification remains unresolved.
3. Execute the bounded business cadence
   - Run the five-touch sequence while honoring suppression and channel restrictions.
   - Offer one measurable outcome, use accurate claims, and request a discovery session involving both the buyer and technical owner.
4. Close, reconcile, and request a relevant introduction
   - Complete the acceptance test and record both signature and collected cash.
   - Reconcile costs and commissions before requesting a peer introduction; expand only the offer whose delivered contribution supports repetition.

## 4. Turn first wins into collectible contribution and a repeatable pipeline  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Instrument every first deal so delivery produces the next sourcing signal. For SaaS, record activation date, active seats, supported instruments, measured freshness, support time, renewal date, and collected subscription cash. Keep forecast revenue separate from invoices and receipts. For affiliate traffic, use an opaque click identifier, allowed campaign metadata, jurisdiction classification, and server-to-server event identifiers; do not place personal information in URLs. Authenticate postbacks, prevent replay, deduplicate events, and distinguish registration, deposit, accepted FTD, reversed FTD, and paid commission. A browser conversion is not proof of an accepted depositor. Reconcile weekly against operator exports and monthly against the settlement statement. Investigate differences by campaign and event age before blaming the entire tracking stack. Give the operator a compact exception file with identifiers and timestamps, not an unnecessary customer dossier. When a pilot meets its acceptance criteria, ask for an introduction to one peer with the same workflow problem. Obtain permission before naming the customer or publishing results. Turn recurring support questions into owned documentation and search pages, then measure whether those pages produce qualified business requests. This closes the loop between delivered utility, inbound demand, and lower acquisition expense without relying on unsupported performance claims.

Manage acquisition spending against settled economics and seasonal capacity. After football season, an operator may lower CPA caps while search and content expenses remain unchanged. Confirm current caps before each paid campaign and maintain a subscription offer that solves a year-round research problem. Do not present casino acquisition as an automatic replacement; product permissions, audience suitability, and commission rules differ. If an affiliate manager offers a larger percentage but refuses a deduction schedule, request a worked settlement and dispute procedure before discussing volume. If a SaaS buyer asks for automated trading, explain that the current product delivers reviewed analytics and keep execution outside the agreed scope until separately assessed. If a publisher requests raw data resale, provide only the licensed display format. Protect cash by limiting each unproven paid cohort to a predetermined loss budget, such as $500, and stopping when acceptance-adjusted contribution becomes negative. That budget is illustrative and must fit available working capital. A $1,400 traffic invoice due today against commissions payable next month creates a funding gap even when eventual contribution is positive. Maintain a settlement calendar, unresolved-deduction register, and cash runway view alongside the lead pipeline; signed contracts alone cannot fund delivery.

Reconcile the operating plan to Formula v2.0 without changing its annual scorecard. Base uses $216,000, $12,500, $96,000, and $38,000 respectively: $362,500 revenue less $224,750 costs produces $137,750 at 38.0%, again with 1 FT + 2 C. These channel allocations are planning decompositions, not additional canonical inputs or first-six-month forecasts. Costs must include acquisition, contractors, hosting, licensing work, servicing, and settlement losses without double-counting deductions already reflected in commission revenue. Assess principal-versus-agent presentation under ASC 606 for publisher arrangements; use retained operator consideration where net presentation applies, rather than gross partner billings or an invented retained_share multiplier. Credits never become sales. Customer trading volume, wagers, deposits, and loan principal never enter these revenue totals. Reforecast timing and channel mix when evidence changes while preserving the distinction between targets and observed results.
