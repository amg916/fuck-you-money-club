## 1. Days 1–7: Fix the Operating Boundary, Twelve-Play Map, and Cash Envelope  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The pod remains one full-time person plus two contractors, with specialist consultations purchased as bounded services rather than hidden permanent headcount. Separate the analytics product from affiliate landing pages, contracts, event schemas, and ledgers. A user must never fund an operator wallet, delegate trading credentials, pool capital, or submit an order through this launch. Non-custody alone does not settle registration: solicitation, compensation, advice, and order-related activity also matter under 7 U.S.C. § 1a, 7 U.S.C. § 6d, and 17 CFR § 1.3. Obtain a written assessment of the actual functions and compensation before enabling them. A builder program agreement does not override federal registration requirements. Record excluded features explicitly, including personalized trade recommendations and execution links carrying order instructions. Public analytics must remain within the assessed scope; calling software a publisher does not create an exemption. Any unresolved classification stays disabled while ordinary engineering continues.

Use a local execution map for all twelve plays, so the chapter does not imply twelve simultaneous product launches. P01 is the non-custodial research terminal; P02 is authorized event-feed ingestion; P03 is contract and settlement normalization; P04 is cross-venue probability comparison; P05 is fee-adjusted positive-EV alerting; P06 is paid analytics subscriptions. These identifiers are this sprint's operational mapping, not an assertion that every partner offers every commercial rail. Activate P01–P06 first where authorized; treat P07–P11 as contract-dependent modules and use P12 across both businesses. The founder owns P06–P10, C1 owns P02–P05 and P11, and C2 owns P01 publishing and P12 reporting. Every handoff needs a named reviewer and a retained test artifact. Prioritize one event category, one subscription offer, and one permitted affiliate jurisdiction. NJ, PA, MI, WV, and ON are separate operating territories, not interchangeable license extensions. Ontario also has distinct advertising restrictions; a uniform 21+ product policy does not describe Ontario's statutory minimum.

These are spending limits, not vendor quotes. Include prepaid services, filing payments, taxes on purchases, and founder cash draws when checking the ceiling; separately record unpaid founder hours. Cut acquisition and optional interfaces before consuming the reserve. The stated $1,500–$10,000 monthly operating range is an early operating envelope, not a ceiling on mature all-in scenario expenses. First-deal timing remains two to six months, so Day 30 can produce a verified prototype and partner pipeline without producing an authorized affiliate commission.

## 2. Days 8–14: Prove Data Integrity, Eligible Referrals, and Separate Revenue Ledgers  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the feed service around permissioned access, replayability, and economic comparability. Kalshi access and ForecastEx access through an authorized broker or data arrangement must be assessed separately; do not assume identical WebSocket availability, redistribution rights, or builder terms. Use an available licensed streaming interface where supported and approved polling otherwise. C1 stores source timestamps, receipt timestamps, market identifiers, settlement sources, fee versions, and sequence information when supplied. A sub-second internal target means p95 receipt-to-screen latency below 1,000 milliseconds across 10,000 measured updates, not a promise that upstream quotes are always current. Flag missing heartbeats and suppress alerts when quote age exceeds two seconds during active trading, with product-specific exceptions documented for slower sources. Compare contracts only when event definitions, settlement timing, cancellation rules, and payout units align. A binary quote of $0.54 is not directly comparable with sportsbook decimal odds without accounting for payout structure, fees, and differing outcomes. Store the transformation alongside the displayed number. Positive-EV labels require an explicit probability estimate, timestamp, and fee assumption; they are model outputs, not executable returns. Test suspended markets, corrections, reconnects, and settlement changes before allowing any automated alert to reach subscribers.

For affiliate attribution, define an eligible first-time depositor using the signed operator contract, not a universal marketing definition. Required conditions may include a new verified account, territory eligibility, qualifying deposit, minimum activity, attribution window, and exclusion of duplicate or prohibited users. C1 creates an opaque click identifier; C2 maintains the partner-specific conversion dictionary. Send no trading credentials or unnecessary identity documents through the affiliate system. Where supported, authenticate callbacks with signatures, enforce replay protection, and make conversion processing idempotent. A duplicate callback must not become a second CPA. Enforce jurisdiction restrictions before the outbound link, but recognize that IP location is a preliminary control and does not replace the operator's required geolocation and identity checks. Display material affiliate relationships clearly under 16 CFR § 255.5, near recommendations and comparison links. Apply the 21+ product policy and relevant responsible-gaming resources consistently; do not publish prohibited inducements or target excluded people. Reconcile click, accepted conversion, reversal, and paid settlement identifiers separately. An operator dashboard can report a pending depositor without creating an earned receivable. Where a partner supplies no test callback, use a clearly isolated synthetic fixture and require subsequent production reconciliation before increasing acquisition.

The accounting design must prevent throughput from masquerading as operator revenue. Subscription revenue follows delivered service and applicable refund treatment; annual prepayments are not automatically first-month earned revenue. Builder payments enter their own ledger only when the program actually exists, the operator is eligible, and the contractual earning conditions are satisfied. Affiliate economics use the commission entitlement after contractual deductions, not deposited funds, betting handle, or customer winnings. For an illustrative NGR contract, $30,000 of operator-defined gaming revenue less $6,000 of allowable bonus and administration deductions produces $24,000 eligible NGR. A 25% commission creates $6,000 before any affiliate-level carryover; a documented $1,000 carryover offset leaves $5,000 before acquisition and servicing expenses. Whether deductions apply before or after the percentage depends on the signed schedule. Preserve that ordering in executable tests. Under an ASC 606 principal-versus-agent assessment, any retained_share bridge uses the operator's actual retained consideration, not customer stakes as gross billings. Do not deduct the same bonus twice through both revenue and expense. Maintain accrued revenue, cash received, deferred service obligations, and disputed commissions as distinct fields. Day 14 ends only after the founder can reproduce each dollar from contract to event to ledger.

## 3. Days 15–21: Test Paid Demand Without Corrupting the Canonical Economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Days 15–21 test whether eligible users will pay for a narrow analytical job, not whether advertising can generate impressive account counts. Interview twenty prospects who match the chosen use case and record their existing tools, decision frequency, and willingness to purchase without promised winnings. Define an activated trial as a person who completes onboarding, views a correctly normalized comparison, and returns on a second day. Use a single illustrative $49 monthly subscription during the experiment; this test price does not reconstruct the annual scorecard's customer mix. Five paying accounts would produce $245 of monthly recurring subscription value before refunds, rather than evidence of the Base annual case. Track CAC separately for subscription buyers and accepted affiliate depositors. Allocate shared creative and contractor work by recorded hours or another documented driver. Affiliate contribution equals earned net commissions less acquisition, variable servicing, and allocated operating costs. If acquisition is paused for permissions, do not manufacture demand using employee deposits or incentivized accounts that breach partner terms. Continue interviews, interface tests, and partner negotiations instead. C2 reviews every comparison claim, while the founder approves spending in daily increments against the remaining week-three envelope. C1 fixes reliability before adding features.

Reconcile the annual cases before interpreting any launch conversion. The canonical Conservative case is $75,000 revenue less $55,500 total modeled expenses, yielding $19,500 owner profit and a 26% margin. Base is $362,500 less $224,750, yielding $137,750 and 38.0%. Scaled is $650,000 less $325,000, yielding $325,000 and 50%. An illustrative stream allocation that preserves those totals is Conservative $50,000 subscriptions, $5,000 permitted builder payments, and $20,000 net affiliate commissions; Base $240,000, $12,500, and $110,000 respectively; Scaled $420,000, $30,000, and $200,000 respectively. Those allocations are planning bridges, not additional canonical assumptions. Every affiliate figure is already net of contractual revenue deductions. Each scenario excludes trading volume, merchant handle, deposits, loan principal, and customer assets. Conservative and Base use one full-time operator plus two contractors; Scaled uses two full-time operators plus three contractors. The five-person case cannot be claimed while silently outsourcing equivalent work outside its expenses. Annual expenses exceed early monthly fixed overhead where acquisition, delivery, and other modeled costs expand. Neither a successful launch week nor an annualized subscription snapshot establishes these profit outcomes. Review the expense bridge by stream before deciding which marginal acquisition dollar deserves funding.

For a Base-compatible affiliate worksheet, start with $160,000 in annual commission accruals before contractual adjustments. Subtract $25,000 of documented administration and bonus adjustments, $10,000 of applicable carryover offsets, and $15,000 of reversals to reach $110,000 recognized affiliate revenue. This worksheet assumes those adjustments are commission-level amounts; an NGR-level contract requires recalculation before applying the revenue-share percentage. The remaining $252,500 of subscription and builder revenue carries $170,750 of expenses and $81,750 profit, so combined profit remains exactly $137,750. For seasonality, stress-test an isolated CPA cohort with forty accepted depositors: $200 per depositor yields $8,000 earned commission, while $120 CAC and $800 servicing leave $2,400 contribution. At a $100 off-season CPA, unchanged spending produces a $1,600 loss. That sensitivity is not a replacement scorecard scenario. Pause that cohort rather than averaging its loss into profitable subscriptions. Maintain NGR carryover separately by the contractual pooling unit, and record settlement delays in runway calculations. A positive accrued margin with cash arriving after payroll can still exhaust the sprint reserve.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the evidence window
   - Export billing, acquisition, partner events, and settlement records with one cutoff.
   - Retain event dates and receipt dates separately so late callbacks cannot silently improve an earlier cohort.
2. Calculate each rail independently
   - Reconcile subscriptions, builder payments, CPA, and NGR before combining totals.
   - Apply each contract's deduction order and distinguish earned revenue, cash collection, and disputed entitlements.
3. Charge the real operating burden
   - Allocate CAC, contractor effort, feed costs, and servicing with documented drivers.
   - Record founder hours alongside cash expenses; preserve the canonical profit definition rather than silently adding or removing compensation.
4. Authorize one bounded experiment
   - Select Kill, Fix, or Scale for each rail and name the next review date.
   - Apply spending and eligibility constraints before performance criteria; save the failed cases as well as successful cohorts.

## 4. Days 22–30 and Reviews at 60/90: Allocate Cash Through Explicit Kill, Fix, or Scale Decisions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The final nine days turn measurements into an operating decision. Day 22 freezes the cohort dictionary; Day 23 exercises stale-feed and duplicate-callback failures; Day 24 reconciles collections; Day 25 retests territory restrictions; Day 26 reviews retention; Day 27 closes contract issues; Day 28 reruns seasonal sensitivity; Day 29 prepares the evidence packet; Day 30 assigns each rail a disposition. A critical incident means custody or order-taking outside scope, a prohibited territory referral, exposed credentials, or unauthorized data distribution. Any single such incident stops the affected function immediately; repair and re-verification precede resumption. For analytics, paid retention means eligible starting subscribers still paying after thirty days, excluding newly added accounts from the numerator. CAC payback equals acquisition cost per new paying subscriber divided by monthly subscriber contribution before acquisition cost. Use observed contribution after refunds, payment fees, variable data, and support. For affiliates, contribution margin uses recognized net commissions as its denominator and includes acquisition plus allocated servicing in its costs. Do not combine CPA depositor counts with subscription accounts. A small sample receives a Fix disposition with a bounded measurement task, not a fabricated confidence claim. Evaluate cash runway using unrestricted funds and committed outflows, not unpaid commissions.

Day 30 Scale means permission to expand a verified experiment, not permission to adopt the Scaled annual forecast. Require zero open critical incidents, all blocking-route tests passing, at least twenty qualified interviews, ten activated trials, and five paying analytics subscribers where sales are permitted. Increase the next thirty-day discretionary experiment budget by at most 25%, only when a thirteen-week cash forecast preserves the reserve. Without completed commercial permissions, keep acquisition at zero and assess the partner pipeline instead. Require at least two documented partner discussions and one written list of remaining requirements with an accountable counterparty. A slow decision within the stated two-to-six-month deal window is not itself a failed business. Day 60 requires thirty paying subscribers and at least twenty renewal-eligible accounts showing 80% thirty-day paid retention before subscription acquisition expands. Affiliate expansion needs thirty settled accepted depositors, at least 20% cohort contribution margin, and settlement reconciliation within 1% by value. An unresolved larger difference pauses spending until explained. Subscription and affiliate rails can receive different decisions on the same day. Never use subscription cash to hide an affiliate cohort that fails its own contribution test.

At Day 90, require sixty paying subscribers, at least forty renewal-eligible accounts, 85% thirty-day paid retention, and CAC payback no longer than six months before scaling subscription acquisition again. Affiliate expansion needs sixty settled accepted depositors spanning two complete cohorts, each contributing at least 20% after acquisition and allocated servicing, with nonnegative contribution under the 50% CPA reduction stress. NGR-heavy rails need two complete settlement cycles and explicit carryover reconciliation instead of borrowing a CPA depositor test. These are management thresholds, not scorecard inputs or industry benchmarks. Kill means retire a channel or feature whose evidence meets the failure condition; it does not automatically close a profitable independent rail. Fix means a named owner, a fourteen-day deadline, and spending restricted to remediation and existing service. Scale means at most another 25% discretionary budget increase while the same pod can meet support and reconciliation service levels. Require 95% of support requests answered within one business day and weekly closes completed within two business days. If either fails for two weeks, stop expansion and repair workload first. Move to two full-time staff plus three contractors only through an explicit capacity and expense plan, never by treating automation as evidence that review work disappeared.
