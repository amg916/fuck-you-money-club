## 1. Sell to the person who controls the payable, not merely the partnership conversation  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Three different buyers write checks, and their motivations should never share one undifferentiated pitch. An individual terminal subscriber spends discretionary research money to reduce stale prices, fragmented screens, and missed information; a professional research desk buys repeatable coverage, exportable evidence, and team access. Neither buys an entitlement to profitable trading. A permitted DCM builder program pays only for activity expressly covered by its current written program; an exchange listing or API connection creates no rebate entitlement. A licensed sportsbook or casino acquisition manager buys qualified incremental customers, but procurement, affiliate operations, and accounts payable may control different parts of the commitment. Ask who can sign the insertion order, who can increase the monthly cap, who validates first-time depositors, and which legal entity owes payment. A friendly affiliate manager cannot bind an unidentified parent company. Statutory pressure creates demand for accurate location-specific distribution, substantiated marketing, and auditable eligibility, but it does not force any operator to purchase your service. Make the commercial offer measurable: fewer invalid referrals, documented source quality, explicit campaign limits, and a reconciliation package that their finance team can approve without reconstructing your traffic history.

## 2. Protect the fee with jurisdiction evidence, bounded attribution, and a reviewable NGR waterfall  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Non-custodial design is necessary but does not by itself establish a publisher exclusion or eliminate registration questions. Under 7 U.S.C. § 1a and 17 CFR § 1.3, introducing-broker and futures-commission-merchant analysis turns on actual functions, including solicitation, order acceptance, and receipt of customer property. Commodity trading adviser analysis also matters when selling advice about covered instruments; relevant publisher exclusions and exemptions have conditions rather than a universal software exception. Document whether the terminal only displays analytics, sends personalized recommendations, supplies execution functionality, or receives transaction-linked compensation. Keep brokerage credentials and customer trading permissions outside the affiliate ledger, and obtain a function-specific registration assessment before adding solicitation or execution features. Never hold user funds, pool capital, or route American users to unlicensed offshore or crypto venues. Kalshi and ForecastEx connectivity, where authorized and available, must use documented interfaces and licensed data rights; do not assume identical protocols or builder compensation. Compare settlement definitions, fees, cancellation rules, and contract availability before labeling a price difference actionable. A positive expected-value estimate is an analytical output, not proof that two instruments have matching payoffs or that an executable arbitrage exists.

Write CPA acceptance around observable events: a permitted jurisdiction and product, eligible age, a genuinely first-time customer under the agreed deduplication scope, a qualifying deposit, and any lawful activity requirement. Specify attribution windows, last-click treatment, cross-device limitations, excluded traffic sources, self-referrals, fraud evidence, and reversal deadlines. A signed server-to-server callback should include a unique event identifier, campaign identifier, timestamp, jurisdiction result, and status without passing unnecessary personal information. Verify message signatures, reject replays, and make processing idempotent; ten retries must not create ten commissions. Record clicks and accepted conversions separately, because a tracked click is neither an eligible player nor an earned fee. For NGR, start with the contract's defined gaming revenue and enumerate bonuses, applicable gaming taxes, administrative fees, chargebacks, and other permitted deductions. For example, $80,000 less $15,000 bonuses, $8,000 taxes, $5,000 administration, and $2,000 chargebacks leaves $50,000 eligible NGR; a 20% share earns $10,000. If the contract carries a prior $10,000 negative NGR balance forward, the current commission becomes $8,000. Specify whether carryover operates at player, brand, product, or jurisdiction level, whether it expires, and whether the contract carries NGR or commission balances.

Fee protection is mostly definition discipline plus enforceable records. Negotiate a reconciliation deadline, a reasonable objection window, access to event-level explanations, and payment of undisputed amounts while disputed items are reviewed. Where commercially achievable, prohibit retroactive rate changes, limit unrelated setoff, and require notice before reducing an active campaign cap. A narrow anti-circumvention provision can protect specifically introduced commercial accounts, but it should not claim ownership of every consumer or override privacy and gaming rules. Preserve the introduction record, signed rate schedule, and applicable attribution terms instead of relying on an expansive clause alone. In New Jersey, N.J.S.A. 5:12-92 illustrates why casino service relationships require classification rather than a blanket assumption that all affiliates need the same credential. Check Pennsylvania PGCB, Michigan MGCB, West Virginia Lottery, and Ontario AGCO requirements separately for the activity and compensation structure. FTC endorsement disclosures under 16 CFR § 255.5 should make material commercial relationships apparent alongside recommendations. Maintain product-specific age controls, geofencing, and current responsible-gaming helpline information. A broken location service should disable monetized offers rather than default to unrestricted availability, and an unresolved registration change should freeze new referrals while preserving legitimate settlement records.

## 3. Choose a settlement rail that pays your business without touching player money  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The payment rail should move consideration owed to the publisher, never customer wagering balances or exchange collateral. For subscriptions, use a processor or merchant-of-record arrangement that has expressly accepted the actual analytics business and its adjacent affiliate activities. Stripe or another familiar checkout provider is not automatically available merely because the product is called software; restricted-business rules, acquiring bank policies, and geography still govern acceptance. Describe the activity accurately, separate product descriptors, and retain written underwriting approval when required. Recognize prepaid annual service over the service period rather than treating every checkout receipt as immediately earned. For operator commissions, ACH credit or wire usually provides cleaner invoice linkage than consumer checkout. Confirm bank instructions through an independently obtained telephone contact, not the number supplied in a change-request email. Match the beneficiary to the contracted debtor or documented payment agent and investigate unexplained third-party payers. An escrow arrangement, where appropriate and lawful, must use an independent authorized provider with objective release conditions; the publisher should not become the escrow holder for player funds. The eight-rail comparison distinguishes payment mechanisms from contractual structures and exceptional statutory distributions rather than pretending they are interchangeable bank products.

Budget authority is not escrow authority. An acquisition manager may authorize a $30,000 monthly campaign without possessing authority to prefund an account or release a reserve. Obtain the release instruction chain, the authorized signatories, and the independent provider's acknowledgement before counting escrow as protection. For a commercial reporting retainer, a $6,000 advance covering three months of $2,000 services improves cash timing but remains unearned until the corresponding obligations are satisfied. For milestone work, tie a $4,000 release to delivered integration tests and a $6,000 release to an accepted reporting package, with a defined review deadline and dispute process. Do not condition ordinary analytics delivery on a customer achieving gambling winnings. Factoring is a liquidity purchase against eligible business receivables, not additional revenue: selling a $20,000 accepted invoice with a $600 fee leaves $19,400 before other reserves or costs. Review recourse, notification, anti-assignment provisions, and applicable state UCC Article 9 rules before transferring rights. A factor's willingness to advance money does not validate the underlying gaming arrangement, and a provider's reserve should never be confused with an independent escrow trust or a promise of final collection.

Build one settlement packet per counterparty and accounting period. Include the purchase order or executed agreement, invoice, accepted conversion count, rate version, NGR waterfall where relevant, credits, tax documentation, and bank details already verified through the standing procedure. Reconcile three different dates: the event date, contractual acceptance date, and cash receipt date. An operator's dashboard can report $18,000 while only $15,000 is accepted and $12,000 has reached the bank; those are different states, not competing descriptions of the same cash. Keep currency separate as well. A CAD 10,000 Ontario invoice is not USD 10,000 of revenue; record the applicable accounting exchange rate, settlement rate, and conversion fees without silently changing the scorecard's USD assumptions. ACH credits, ACH debits, cards, and wires have different return, dispute, and reversal characteristics, so confirmed receipt is not a universal finality rule. Reject requests to disguise the merchant category or split payments to evade restrictions. Under 31 U.S.C. §§ 5363–5364 and applicable implementing rules, restricted gambling transactions require controls; a different rail does not cure an unlawful underlying transaction. Escalate unexplained shortfalls with transaction references and collect uncontested balances while the documented dispute proceeds.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Establish the debtor and permitted activity
   - Verify the contracting entity, signatory authority, jurisdiction coverage, compensation permissions, and accepted payment provider.
   - Record official register evidence, the registration assessment, executed terms, and independently verified banking contacts before activating monetized offers.
2. Capture attributable earning evidence
   - Log signed conversion events, campaign versions, qualifying conditions, subscription delivery, and applicable NGR inputs in separate ledgers.
   - Use idempotent event processing and pseudonymous identifiers; reconcile retries, duplicates, reversals, and product-specific eligibility without collecting unnecessary player information.
3. Invoice the accepted consideration
   - Prepare a settlement packet distinguishing pending, accepted, disputed, invoiced, and collected balances for each currency and revenue stream.
   - Apply the signed rate and deduction waterfall, request explanations for variances, and preserve the right to collect the undisputed balance.
4. Reconcile cash and limit new exposure
   - Match bank receipts to invoices, record fees and exchange differences, and suspend new paid acquisition when credit limits are exceeded.
   - Maintain permitted factual content and service obligations while escalating overdue amounts through finance contacts and the agreed contractual dispute mechanism.

## 4. Underwrite the debtor and reconcile the three owner-profit cases  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Counterparty screening starts with the entity that owes money, not the consumer brand on the landing page. Match the contract to official corporate and gaming registers, identify any paying agent, and request accounts-payable references or available financial statements. Public groups may provide useful filings through SEC EDGAR, but a parent company's cash does not secure a subsidiary's invoices without an enforceable support arrangement. Review late-payment reports critically, verify material claims, and start unfamiliar counterparties with a small accepted invoice before scaling traffic. Set exposure as unpaid accepted fees plus unbilled earned consideration and committed acquisition spend that cannot be canceled. For example, $18,000 receivable, $6,000 earned but unbilled, and $4,000 unavoidable spend create $28,000 exposure against a $25,000 limit. Stop new paid acquisition immediately rather than waiting for a bank default. A practical illustrative escalation policy contacts accounts payable five business days after the due date and pauses additional discretionary spend at fifteen; contract-specific rights still control. Termination, setoff, negative carryover, and campaign cancellation can damage collection before formal insolvency. Preserve audit exports outside the counterparty dashboard so access termination does not erase the evidence needed to establish the debt.
