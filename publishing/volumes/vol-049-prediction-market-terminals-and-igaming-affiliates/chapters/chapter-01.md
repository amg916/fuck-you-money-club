## 4. Build the small-team moat around evidence, attribution, and graceful shutdown  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The technical moat begins with reliable licensed information, not an impressive animation. Use venue-supported streaming connections where actually offered and authorized; do not assume Kalshi, ForecastEx, and every sportsbook expose equivalent public WebSocket products. Record source timestamps, receipt timestamps, sequence identifiers, and instrument versions. Measure sub-second latency only across the observable path and publish stale-state indicators when source age exceeds the product's stated tolerance. PostgreSQL can hold canonical event definitions and immutable conversion records, while Redis can maintain current permitted market snapshots. Store raw feed payloads only where retention rights permit. The comparison engine should reject mismatched settlement dates, ambiguous outcome wording, suspended markets, and missing fee schedules rather than manufacturing a probability ranking. Every alert needs a reproducible calculation and a visible explanation of relevant limits. Run scheduled tests against archived fixtures so a venue schema change cannot silently invert a probability. When a feed fails, disable affected calculations and keep unaffected research accessible. This architecture turns operational restraint into a product feature: customers know which information remains usable instead of discovering that a beautiful dashboard has been recycling old prices. Reliability supports renewal more consistently than an unsupported promise of universal real-time coverage.

Acquisition automation needs equally strict event semantics. A server-to-server attribution pipeline should associate campaign, jurisdiction, partner, creative version, and pseudonymous click identifier with each accepted conversion. Verify signatures where supported, reject replayed events through idempotency keys, and preserve the partner's original status history. Keep email addresses and identity documents out of tracking URLs; the gambling operator, not the publisher, performs account verification and holds customer funds. Store only the information needed for attribution, support, and applicable retention duties. A daily exception report should identify missing callbacks, duplicate first-time-depositor claims, unexplained deductions, changed CPA schedules, and conversions outside the permitted geography. Reconcile monthly statements against event records rather than treating a postback as the final settlement record. Define separate statuses for received, provisionally eligible, accepted, reversed, invoiced, and paid. Revenue estimates should reflect the observed reversal history and contract terms, while cash planning respects settlement timing. Test the system with documented synthetic events or a partner sandbox, never fabricated production players. If attribution breaks, pause incremental paid acquisition and retain educational pages. Buying more traffic into an unmeasurable funnel compounds losses and makes later commission disputes substantially harder to resolve.

A small team remains durable by narrowing ownership, not by pretending every process runs without supervision. In the Base case, the full-time owner controls product direction, partner economics, and monthly reconciliation; one contractor can maintain data engineering and another can produce jurisdiction-specific editorial work. Obtain specialist legal and accounting review as scoped services and budget them explicitly rather than assuming contractors replace regulated counterparties. In the Scaled case, a second employee can own acquisition and retention while three contractors support engineering, content, and operations. These are staffing interpretations of the canonical headcounts, not assumptions that every contractor works full time. Track subscription churn, support minutes per account, licensed-data cost per subscriber, accepted acquisition cost, deduction-adjusted NGR yield, and partner concentration. Before expanding, run a downside month with no builder payments, a reduced sportsbook CPA, and a delayed affiliate settlement. If the terminal cannot retain customers without promotional subsidies, improve the product before adding jurisdictions. Keep feature-level shutdown controls and clear restoration criteria. The strongest business can lose one partner, one feed, or one seasonal campaign while continuing to serve paying customers with useful, lawfully supported information and a reconciled revenue ledger.
