**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the cohort evidence
   - Export subscription invoices, affiliate event receipts, and operator settlement statements each week.
   - Join records by immutable transaction identifier, contract version, jurisdiction, and earning month; restrict access to customer-level attribution evidence.
2. Reconcile earned consideration
   - Apply credits, eligibility scrubs, NGR deductions, and carryover before recording recognized revenue.
   - Separate pending, rejected, earned, billed, collected, and clawed-back amounts; retain supporting reasons for every movement between states.
3. Project unrestricted collections
   - Populate thirteen weekly cash buckets using actual earning-to-bank settlement terms.
   - Include contractor due dates, professional costs, processor restrictions, refund exposure, and a thirty-day affiliate delay sensitivity.
4. Authorize spending within funded capacity
   - Reduce acquisition commitments whenever forecast cash breaches the chosen continuity reserve.
   - Release owner distributions only after preserving service delivery capacity and documented clawback funding; never supplement business float with participant money.
