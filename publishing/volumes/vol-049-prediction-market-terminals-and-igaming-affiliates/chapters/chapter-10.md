## 4. Operate the exception queue and scale only verified loops  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Design recovery before increasing event volume. The ingestion worker writes a durable receipt before acknowledging delivery; downstream handlers make state changes through transactional outbox records and idempotent consumers. Duplicate callbacks should produce one ledger effect, not two commissions. Partition retries by cause: transient network failures receive bounded exponential backoff with jitter, authentication failures disable the affected connection, and schema changes enter a dead-letter queue for engineering review. A poison message cannot occupy the only worker indefinitely. Preserve the original payload under restricted access and attach a redacted diagnostic record to the incident. For market data, prioritize current state recovery over replaying every stale alert; the dashboard shows a freshness warning until the snapshot and incremental stream agree. For attribution, replay historical events because accounting completeness matters, but never overwrite an accepted event without a traceable correction. An illustrative incident objective is detection within two minutes and initial classification within fifteen minutes during staffed coverage. Outside coverage, automatically suppress affected alerts or offers rather than implying continuous human supervision. Test restoration from backups and replay a known callback batch every month. A backup that has never reconstructed a ledger is merely an untested storage expense.

Set review intensity according to consequence and novelty. Humans inspect every new jurisdiction rule, venue-equivalence pair, personalized feature, promotional claim, and material contract change before release. Stable extraction jobs receive stratified samples that include each source, model version, and jurisdiction; exceptions receive complete review. As an illustrative policy, inspect thirty of 600 routine extraction outputs daily, plus all outputs with missing sources or changed eligibility language. At thirty seconds per routine sample, the sample consumes fifteen minutes, but complex statutory review requires additional separately budgeted time. Sampling does not validate the uninspected population individually. Track defect classes, severity, correction minutes, and how many customers received an incorrect result. Any consequential eligibility or payment defect pauses the affected automation class until the root cause is fixed and replay tests pass. Use a versioned evaluation set containing settlement cancellations, ambiguous timestamps, duplicate FTD callbacks, carryover reversals, and misleading source instructions. Compare candidate model releases against the current version on these cases before switching. Keep a deterministic extraction fallback for essential fields and a manual queue for unstructured material. The owner signs off on consequential decisions rather than treating a confidence score as delegated authority.

Scale the narrow loops that produce retained contribution, not the loops with the largest impression counts. A weekly operating sheet joins subscription cohorts, source-level acquisition costs, affiliate statement adjustments, automation expense, and correction labor without mixing customer trading volume into revenue. Compare paid, programmatic, referral, and citation-led channels using the same attribution window and explicitly record assisted touches rather than counting one customer several times. For the $30 subscription with $8 incremental monthly delivery cost, a six-month retained customer contributes $132 before acquisition and fixed expenses. Spending $66 leaves $66 over those six months; a customer leaving after two months contributes only $44 and does not recover that acquisition cost. Gaming CPA offers need their own accepted-event and reversal assumptions, especially after NFL budgets contract. Promote one change at a time through shadow calculation, limited exposure, and measured expansion, with a recorded rollback owner. Move from the Base three-person configuration to the Scaled five-person configuration only when contracted revenue, queue age, and review workload support the additional capacity. Automation succeeds when each additional customer requires less repetitive handling while evidence quality remains intact. The flywheel is verified data becoming useful distribution, then retained revenue funding better data and faster corrections.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture and classify the evidence
   - Register entitled sources, contract versions, jurisdiction policies, and baseline task minutes.
   - Assign one owner per source and preserve the exact documents supporting every eligibility, settlement, and revenue decision.
2. Run a shadow batch before public delivery
   - Process recorded market events, payment webhooks, and affiliate callbacks without changing customer-facing state.

   - Enable a limited customer cohort after checking consequential outputs and documenting the rollback owner.

4. Reconcile and expand only the verified loop
   - Match subscription records and affiliate statements to their respective ledgers before increasing acquisition volume.
   - Expand only when contribution, queue age, correction effort, and replay tests support the next cohort; otherwise restore the prior configuration.
