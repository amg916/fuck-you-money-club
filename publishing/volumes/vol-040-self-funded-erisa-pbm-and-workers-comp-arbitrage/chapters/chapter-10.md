## 1. Build a Renewal Radar That Produces Evidence, Not Prospect Fiction  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with a tenant-separated evidence ledger, not a conversational agent allowed to browse every employer document. The first three mapped plays are Schedule A/C renewal radar, sponsor entity resolution, and renewal readiness scoring. Download Department of Labor Form 5500 public datasets through documented distribution endpoints; use Python urllib.request, csv, zipfile, hashlib, and sqlite3 to preserve source checksums, filing years, amendments, and extraction versions. Index sponsors by EIN plus plan number, then distinguish legal entities from trade names before joining CRM records. Schedule A can reveal insurance contracts and reported commissions; Schedule C can identify reportable service-provider compensation subject to its reporting rules. Neither schedule is a complete current broker ledger, and self-funded arrangements do not uniformly produce a useful Schedule A insurance record. A plan year-end is a targeting clue, not proof of the renewal date. Store inferred dates separately from employer-confirmed dates. For an illustrative batch of 200 filings, suppose 160 pass parsing, 120 resolve to distinct sponsors, and 30 match the agency's geography and employee band. Those 30 are research candidates, not qualified opportunities. Route ambiguous EIN matches, amended returns, and conflicting provider names to a contractor before any outreach references them.

Make every ingestion event reproducible. A webhook receiver validates an HMAC signature against the raw request body with hmac.compare_digest, rejects stale timestamps, and records a tenant-scoped event identifier before acknowledging receipt. Place accepted jobs in a durable queue; a small deployment can use SQLite transactions with a single dispatcher, moving to a managed queue when concurrency exceeds that design. Keep source documents immutable and write derived records under separate versioned keys. A structured extraction request asks the language model for field values, page references, and quoted supporting text, never permission to contact a prospect or alter a policy. Treat retrieved documents as evidence rather than instructions. Python validates dates, currency units, and required fields after the model responds. A model-generated confidence score cannot establish correctness; compare a labeled sample against human extraction and measure field-level accuracy. For example, 198 correct renewal-date classifications in 200 reviewed records yields 99% sample accuracy, while the two errors still require investigation. Retry transient service failures with capped exponential backoff and jitter. After three unsuccessful attempts, park the job with its source reference and owner. Do not silently manufacture a date to keep the funnel moving. An unavailable model should leave deterministic parsing and manual review operational.

Assign responsibility before increasing throughput. In the 1 FT + 3 C configuration, the full-time principal owns licensed recommendations and commercial relationships; contractors cover benefits and PBM analysis, workers' compensation auditing, and automation plus research operations. These are work allocations, not a claim that every contractor needs equal hours or identical credentials. Verify applicable Life & Health, Property & Casualty, agency, appointment, and TPA requirements against the actual activities and jurisdictions. Keep employer prospect data outside the protected claims workspace. Where the operator is a business associate, execute the required agreement and apply HIPAA access, disclosure, and security requirements before handling protected health information. Public filing availability does not authorize access to employee claims. Use role-based access, tenant-specific exports, encrypted storage, and an approved retention schedule. Renewal scoring should favor confirmed decision timing, accessible plan documents, employer size fit, and an identifiable decision-maker, rather than diagnoses or individual claimant characteristics. A 40-point readiness score might allocate ten points to each of those four dimensions, with a documented rule for every awarded point. Publish the evidence links beside the score. When the employer corrects a filing-derived assumption, preserve the correction history and replace the active value instead of overwriting the original source. That provenance prevents stale automation from repeatedly reopening settled questions.

## 2. Automate PBM and E-Mod Arithmetic While Reserving Judgment for Specialists  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The next four mapped plays are the NADAC comparator, rebate reconciliation, E-Mod replay, and payroll-code audit. Build the PBM pipeline around an authorized claim extract, current contract, eligibility periods, formulary terms, and rebate settlement statements. Normalize NDC identifiers only with a documented conversion rule; retain the original identifier because package and product confusion can destroy unit math. Match CMS NADAC observations to the dispensing date and compatible units, recording publication date and effective date separately. NADAC measures surveyed retail community pharmacy acquisition costs; it does not automatically establish the contracted price for every specialty, mail, or excluded product. Suppose 100 eligible fills contain 30 tablets each, the matched benchmark is $0.12 per tablet, the illustrative dispensing allowance is $1.50, and employer-paid cost after member cost-sharing adjustments is $9.00 per fill. The comparator is $5.10 per fill and the observed difference is $390 across the cohort. Call that a benchmark difference, not recoverable spread or promised savings. A specialist must reconcile taxes, fees, channel, reversals, member liability, and contractual definitions before presenting an actionable finding. Unmatched products stay outside the calculated cohort and appear in a coverage report, so a small clean subset cannot masquerade as a whole-book conclusion.

Rebate reconciliation requires a separate settlement model because a low ingredient price and a high rebate can describe different economic arrangements. Parse the contract's eligible claims, exclusions, minimum guarantees, allocation periods, administrative deductions, and payment deadlines into a reviewed rule sheet. For an illustrative quarter, 1,000 eligible claims at an $18 contractual minimum produce an $18,000 minimum settlement before contract-specific adjustments. If $14,000 has been credited and no adjustment applies, the $4,000 difference is a reconciliation item requiring evidence, not immediately recognized agency revenue. The employer's recovered amount and the operator's earned fee occupy different ledger accounts. Under a hypothetical 20% earned recovery split, a collected $4,000 recovery produces $800 of operator fee revenue, subject to the actual agreement and recognition conditions. Never add the $4,000 employer recovery to the $800 fee. Use Decimal for money, explicit rounding rules, and tests for reversed claims and duplicated settlement files. Models can classify clauses and draft discrepancy letters, but a benefits specialist verifies every contract citation and the licensed principal releases the communication. If eligible-claim counts disagree, suspend the dollar demand and send a narrow request for the missing settlement detail. Preserve the original calculation so later evidence changes the estimate transparently rather than rewriting the audit trail.

For workers' compensation, obtain the employer-authorized rating worksheet, policy documents, loss runs, payroll detail, and applicable NCCI or independent-bureau rules. E-Mod replay means reproducing the bureau's calculation using the correct state, experience period, expected-loss rates, split points, credibility components, and rule effective dates. It does not mean subtracting an inconvenient claim from total losses. Licensed access terms govern proprietary manuals and data; automation must use permitted exports rather than circumventing access controls. The payroll-code audit separately compares payroll records, actual duties, classification rules, officer treatment, and permitted payroll divisions. Consider a simplified premium component with $1,000,000 payroll, a $2.00 rate per $100 payroll, and a 1.20 modifier. That component is $24,000; substituting a verified 1.10 modifier yields $22,000, a $2,000 difference before other policy charges and adjustments. At a hypothetical 25% earned refund split, a collected $2,000 refund generates $500 operator revenue. Label this as illustrative component arithmetic, not a complete premium quotation. A workers' compensation specialist checks state-specific correction procedures and deadlines before submission. If the replay cannot match the issued worksheet, freeze the correction package and reconcile inputs with the carrier or bureau. Never reclassify hazardous duties solely because a cheaper code improves the displayed result.

## 3. Measure Automation by Completed Work and Reconcile Every Dollar  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The eighth mapped play, exception routing, converts the three analysis pipelines into a controlled service operation. Establish a fixed taxonomy of repetitive tasks before claiming 70% automation. In an illustrative week containing 100 comparable task instances, let 30 concern research, 20 qualification, 35 fulfillment preparation, and 15 distribution administration. If 25, 16, 26, and seven respectively finish without manual rework, the automation rate is 74%. Count an instance once; a failed extraction retried four times is not four successes. Record human review minutes separately, including final reviews of automatically prepared work. This task-count measure does not imply 74% labor savings or 74% autonomous client decisions. Estimate time reduction using paired baseline and observed minutes for the same task classes, and include exception handling. If a baseline week requires 40 repetitive-work hours and the instrumented week requires 18, measured time reduction is 55%, even though task automation is 74%. Review a random sample of passing records as well as every exception to detect silent drift. The principal owns the weekly error budget, while contractors own correction queues. Stop increasing intake when unresolved high-impact exceptions exceed the team's documented capacity, rather than letting attractive dashboard volume hide a growing service backlog.

Price each run as an operating unit with a defined boundary. An illustrative screening run handles one sponsor's bounded document packet and produces one reviewable research record; it excludes a full claims audit or policy placement. Budget $0.12 for compute and queue activity, $0.30 for language-model usage, $0.05 for storage allocation, and three reviewer minutes at $55 per hour, or $2.75. Total unit cost is $3.22. The model allowance could represent 60,000 input tokens at $3 per million plus 8,000 output tokens at $15 per million; these are planning rates, not a vendor quotation. Enforce token and document-size limits, and replace allowances with invoice observations monthly. At 180 runs per month, this workload costs $579.60 monthly or $6,955.20 annually. If review time doubles to six minutes, unit cost rises to $5.97 and monthly cost to $1,074.60. This sensitivity makes reviewer effort more important than shaving pennies from model tokens. Route oversized packets into a separately priced audit queue instead of absorbing unlimited work into the screening unit. Track completed, failed, and abandoned runs in the same cost denominator so unsuccessful processing remains visible. Allocate shared software and supervision separately to avoid pretending that variable run cost equals total delivery cost.

Reconcile automation spending to Formula v2.0 rather than inventing a new profit story. Conservative revenue of $120,000 less $81,600 total costs yields $38,400 owner net profit at 32%; Base revenue of $535,000 less $294,250 yields $240,750 at 45.0%; Scaled revenue of $950,000 less $399,000 yields $551,000 at 58%. These are the supplied annual scenario totals. Their revenue basis is retained PEPM advisory fees, stop-loss or TPA commissions, and earned contingent E-Mod refund splits, excluding employer claim funds and insurance premiums. Although the scorecard labels its top line gross revenue, that line is not gross employer billings or premium volume. Assess principal-versus-agent presentation under ASC 606 from the actual arrangement; where retained-share treatment applies, do not apply a second retention haircut to these already retained operator figures. The illustrative $6,955.20 annual screening budget fits inside Base costs, leaving $287,294.80 for all other included costs rather than increasing the scorecard expense total. Conservative and Base retain 1 FT + 3 C; Scaled uses 2 FT + 4 C, six people. The $2,000–$11,000 monthly operating-overhead range is not a substitute for fully loaded annual scenario costs. Likewise, $8,000–$45,000 startup capital and a 2–5 month first-deal interval require a separate cash runway schedule, not premature revenue recognition.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze the measured task taxonomy
   - Define research, qualification, fulfillment preparation, and distribution administration instances.
   - Record baseline minutes and acceptance criteria; count retries under the original instance identifier rather than creating artificial completions.
2. Run a bounded shadow batch
   - Process authorized packets without automatically releasing any consequential output.
   - Compare extracted fields and calculations with specialist-reviewed answers; retain source hashes, model versions, prompts, and deterministic rule versions.
3. Release only reviewed deliverables
   - Assign exceptions and require the appropriate specialist to approve recommendations or submissions.
   - Use a named reviewer, timestamp, source-linked checklist, and immutable released version; corrections create a superseding version rather than erasing history.
4. Close the operating and accounting loop
   - Reconcile weekly task outcomes and monthly costs against earned retained revenue.
   - Include failed runs, reviewer time, shared overhead, and unresolved receivables; preserve the canonical scenario totals when allocating automation expense.

Each page needs an editorial owner, version date, calculation tests, and a concrete next step. Do not generate hundreds of thin locality pages or publish employer-level claims to manufacture search traffic. Use synthetic worked examples by default; any actual benchmark release requires appropriate rights, aggregation, and disclosure-risk review. A useful distribution loop starts when a CFO saves the checklist, shares it with a controller, and requests the source worksheet. Record that request as a warm signal, not permission for unrelated broadcasts. Partner routing sends an employer-authorized introduction to the appropriate licensed specialist based on state, renewal timing, and service need. Check referral compensation restrictions before promising payments to accountants or payroll firms. Keep partner attribution attached to earned operator fees rather than employer premium volume, so the loop optimizes valuable relationships instead of impressive but economically irrelevant transaction totals.

Implement high_velocity_dm_outbound_systems as an explicit signal state machine, not blind cold mass messaging. A prospect comments with the requested keyword, uses a clearly explained hand-raiser reaction, or sends an inbound message; only then may an approved integration create a pending response. A generic reaction without a clear invitation to receive a message is insufficient. Confirm platform permissions and applicable messaging rules, deduplicate the person and trigger, and send one contextual acknowledgment without an external link. Dispatch the requested link only after the prospect replies. Multi-inbox routing assigns warm conversations to the relevant authorized specialist; it must not rotate accounts to evade limits or multiply contact frequency. For an illustrative cohort, 100 valid triggers produce 60 replies, 18 qualified conversations, six meetings, and one signed client. At 100 covered lives and $8 retained PEPM, that client has a $9,600 annual fee run rate, assuming all twelve months are active; it is not $9,600 earned on signature. If content costs $200 and handling costs $300, the cohort's acquisition outlay is $500 before sales labor and onboarding. Measure those additional costs before claiming full acquisition efficiency. Reconcile trigger counts to actual delivered messages, since silent Message Request placement can make attempted dispatch look much healthier than real engagement.

Design the growth loop to fail safely. PeerFloodError, platform rate-limit responses, revoked permissions, and unexpected delivery deterioration should pause automated dispatch and alert the owner, not trigger account rotation or alternate identity creation. Preserve the prospect's requested action and route the conversation to the platform's permitted manual workflow, or wait for a fresh inbound message when no permitted continuation exists. A failed send is not permission to contact the same person through a newly discovered channel. Where commercial email is used, apply CAN-SPAM identification, postal-address, and opt-out requirements and propagate suppression across connected systems. Test webhook replay rejection, duplicate suppression, revoked CRM access, model outages, stale NADAC files, and revised bureau inputs during a monthly recovery exercise. A rollback should restore the last reviewed rule package while preserving pending work and the reason for suspension. Feed only approved, non-sensitive issue categories back into content planning: frequent renewal-date confusion can justify a better checklist, while a claimant's diagnosis cannot become marketing material. Track qualified meetings per valid trigger, retained revenue per activated client, review minutes per delivered analysis, and correction rates by source version. Expand traffic only when these measures remain stable and service capacity supports the resulting obligations. The scalable asset is a repeatable chain from evidence to trust to earned fees, not an unattended messaging machine.
