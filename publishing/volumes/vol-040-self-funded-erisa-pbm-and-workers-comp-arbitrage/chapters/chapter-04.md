## 1. Protect the reserve before buying production capacity  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Allocate startup money around the two-to-five-month first-deal window, not around a software shopping list. The three capital envelopes below are purchase limits, not vendor quotations or additional revenue scenarios. An $8,000 launch places $4,000 in reserve and spends $4,000 establishing a narrow advisory operation; the $25,000 envelope protects $15,000; the $45,000 envelope protects $27,000. Those reserve shares are 50%, 60%, and 60%. Hold the reserve in a separate business deposit account with an insured institution, verify ownership-category coverage, and reconcile transfers weekly. It belongs to the operator and must never contain employer claim money, pharmacy inventory proceeds, or premium remittances. At $2,000 monthly fixed overhead, the smallest reserve buys only two months before collections. A five-month delay requires $10,000 of fixed-cost coverage, leaving a $6,000 reserve shortfall in that envelope. Resolve the shortfall through additional committed owner liquidity, a smaller operating scope, or legitimately earned advance advisory fees with explicit deliverables. Do not bridge it by borrowing from a client's benefit account. Treat the $8,000 entry point as conditional on existing credentials and a short sales cycle, rather than sufficient funding for every jurisdiction or service line.

Reconcile overhead to Formula v2.0 before selecting a staffing plan. Conservative retained revenue of $120,000 less $38,400 owner profit implies $81,600 total annual costs. Base revenue of $535,000 less $240,750 implies $294,250; Scaled revenue of $950,000 less $551,000 implies $399,000. These totals produce the required 32%, 45.0%, and 58% margins without changing the supplied revenue basis. For planning, assign fixed monthly operating envelopes of $2,000, $6,000, and $11,000 respectively. Their annual totals are $24,000, $72,000, and $132,000, leaving $57,600, $222,250, and $267,000 for all remaining scenario expenses. Those residuals must absorb contractor delivery, payroll where applicable, acquisition costs, transaction charges, and other modeled expenses; they are not extra owner distributions. The team remains one full-time operator plus three contractors in Conservative and Base, then two full-time staff plus four contractors in Scaled. Contractor counts describe available specialists, not three guaranteed full-time retainers. Fund work orders against signed assignments and measured collection cycles. Do not subtract a fresh assumed owner salary from the published profit and still label the result the canonical scorecard. Track any alternate compensation presentation separately, with its bridge to these exact totals.

Translate sales assumptions into collectible units before releasing discretionary cash. An illustrative account with 250 enrolled employees paying a retained $8 PEPM produces $2,000 monthly operator revenue and $24,000 over twelve fully billable months. Five such full-year accounts produce the Conservative $120,000 revenue figure, but five accounts signed late in the year do not. Apply the actual contract definition of eligible or enrolled lives, effective dates, and billing cutoffs. For recovery work, a documented $40,000 workers' compensation premium refund at a 25% contractual split produces $10,000 operator revenue after the agreed collection trigger, not $40,000. Exclude disputed estimates until the applicable recognition criteria are met. A 13-week cash schedule should distinguish earned fees, invoices, carrier commission statements, bank receipts, and refundable advances. Release outreach spending in weekly increments rather than annual lead-platform contracts. Keep commission reversals and contingent-fee disputes visible as reserve demands. At the upper $11,000 fixed-overhead setting, $27,000 covers only about 2.45 months; holding five months requires a fixed burn no greater than $5,400 unless additional liquidity exists. Buy the next capacity layer only when collected contribution supports it, not because pipeline premiums look impressive on a presentation.

## 2. Assemble twelve layers without creating twelve data silos  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build one identity boundary, one controlled document repository, and one auditable calculation path. The twelve-layer matrix provides exact reference pricing assumptions for named tiers, not verified current offers; confirm checkout prices, minimum seats, regional charges, and contractual terms before committing. Annual-billing rates are monthly equivalents and can create upfront cash requirements. Microsoft 365 Business Premium at the $22 reference rate buys the primary productivity and endpoint-control layer; Bitwarden Teams at $4 supplies shared secrets without spreadsheets. Two seats of each produce $52 monthly before tax at these assumptions. HubSpot Free receives employer contacts and renewal dates, never member claims. Calendly Standard receives meeting logistics, not diagnoses. Adobe Acrobat Standard handles redacted proposals and document preparation; its listed price does not purchase a specialized health-record workflow. PostgreSQL, Python, and Metabase provide a reproducible calculation stack without per-claim software fees. Open-source licensing does not eliminate patching, database administration, backups, or contractor expense. Keep source-control repositories free of claim extracts, production credentials, and live employer census files. Purchase commercial seats for actual operators rather than every occasional reviewer, and explicitly verify whether a contractor's access is permitted by the selected license.

Treat protected health information as a separate deployment decision. A vendor's brand, encryption claim, or enterprise marketing page does not establish that a particular service and configuration can receive it. Map each disclosure to the plan, covered entity, business associate, or other applicable role; execute required business associate agreements and document minimum-necessary access before loading identifiable claims. Microsoft services require confirmation of the applicable agreement and service scope. AWS requires an executed agreement and eligible services configured according to the assigned responsibilities. The EC2 and S3 rates below omit storage attachments, keys, requests, transfer, monitoring, and recovery copies; the compute reference is not an all-in production quote. Bind PostgreSQL to private connectivity, encrypt volumes and backups, disable public buckets, and use short-lived application credentials. Require phishing-resistant multifactor authentication for privileged users, separate administrator accounts from daily mail accounts, and remove contractor access at assignment closure. Synthetic claims should populate development environments. A limited data set remains regulated information under HIPAA and is not interchangeable with properly de-identified data. If the necessary contracts or controls cannot be established economically, leave claims with the authorized administrator and accept aggregate outputs sufficient for the contracted analysis.

Automate calculations only after documenting their inputs and exceptions. Use Department of Labor EFAST2 public filings for prospect research, record the filing year, and distinguish Schedule A insurance information from Schedule C service-provider reporting; neither schedule is a complete current renewal calendar. Confirm renewal dates directly before outreach. For PBM analysis, import CMS NADAC reference files with effective dates, normalize NDC representations, and compare equivalent package quantities. NADAC is an acquisition-cost benchmark, not a specialty reimbursement promise or a complete measure of net plan cost. Reconcile dispensing fees, rebates, member cost sharing, exclusions, and invoice timing separately. For workers' compensation, ingest authorized payroll registers, class-code assignments, loss runs, and the applicable experience-rating worksheet. NCCI rules apply only where relevant; independent bureaus and jurisdiction-specific filing practices require different adapters. Store each source version and transformation checksum with the output. Run a known-case regression suite before updating any pricing file or rating assumption. An analyst should be able to reproduce an employer's invoice from frozen inputs without opening a private chat thread. If records disagree, suspend the affected savings assertion and route the discrepancy to the carrier, administrator, or pharmacy partner instead of silently substituting a favorable number.

## 3. Separate entity authority, client money, and pharmacy economics  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Establish the operating entity, tax identifier, bank accounts, bookkeeping categories, and written signing authority before binding engagements. Verify individual and business-entity producer requirements in every relevant state, including Life and Health authority for benefits work and Property and Casualty authority for workers' compensation brokerage. Carrier appointments, referral restrictions, consultant rules, and TPA licensing depend on the actual activity; holding one producer license does not authorize all administrative services. California Insurance Code section 1631 illustrates the prohibition on unlicensed insurance solicitation and negotiation, but the operating checklist must follow the client's jurisdictions. Purchase professional liability and cyber coverage for the stated services, check exclusions for pharmacy operations and benefit-plan fiduciary acts, and obtain subcontractor coverage evidence. Record commissions and retained advisory fees in separate ledger categories. Do not route employer premiums or claim payments through the ordinary operating account. Where handling such money is unavoidable and authorized, implement the applicable separate-account and reconciliation requirements first. For covered group-health brokerage or consulting arrangements expecting at least $1,000 in compensation, build the ERISA section 408(b)(2)(B) disclosure process around services, direct and indirect compensation, payer relationships, and timing before entering, extending, or renewing the arrangement. Maintain evidence of delivery to the responsible fiduciary.

The mapped play section_340b_contract_pharmacy_spread_arbitrage requires a separate contracting lane, not a casual extension of employer benefits brokerage. Partner only with an eligible, registered covered entity, such as a qualifying Federally Qualified Health Center, Ryan White provider, or eligible hospital category; rural location alone does not establish eligibility. Section 340B, codified at 42 U.S.C. 256b, governs ceiling prices, duplicate discounts, diversion, and covered-entity obligations. A modeled 25–50% acquisition discount must be validated by actual eligible-drug invoices; it is not a universal statutory percentage. Commercial reimbursement depends on the payer contract and adjudicated claim, rather than an entitlement to collect an assumed full price. For a deliberately simplified eligible prescription, $10,000 collected reimbursement minus $6,000 acquisition cost, $400 pharmacy fulfillment expense, and a $250 operator administrative fee leaves $3,350 with the covered entity before its other expenses. The operator recognizes only its $250 fee; $150 of attributable delivery expense leaves $100 contribution before shared overhead. One hundred comparable completed prescriptions would yield $25,000 operator revenue and $10,000 contribution, not $1 million of operator revenue. This sensitivity example does not supplement or replace the three canonical annual cases.

Design flat administrative or dispensing compensation around documented services and supportable market value; the word flat does not resolve referral incentives or federal Anti-Kickback Statute exposure. Assess 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b), applicable state restrictions, payer contracts, and the real patient-care relationship before accepting tele-referral or specialty capture arrangements. Require encounter evidence, prescription linkage, prescriber attribution, Medicaid duplicate-discount controls, reversal handling, and auditable accumulator logic. A telehealth encounter alone does not establish 340B patient eligibility. The covered entity retains oversight even when a vendor performs split billing. If contract-pharmacy restrictions block an NDC, assess a genuinely entity-owned specialty pharmacy alongside contract pharmacies, with separate licensing, payer enrollment, staffing, inventory, and capital underwriting. That ownership fallback is outside this chapter's $8,000–$45,000 advisory launch envelope. Automate 340B ESP uploads only when applicable manufacturer terms, lawful disclosure authority, contractual permissions, and data minimization support them; submission alone does not ensure access to restricted products. Reconcile uploaded records to claims and inventory without transmitting unnecessary identifiers. If an eligible dispensing pathway remains unavailable, suspend that product's capture and continue the ordinary benefits advisory business. Never convert an unresolved eligibility question into projected spread revenue merely to justify the software purchase.

## 4. Prove readiness with one complete transaction before scaling  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run pre-launch readiness as a series of evidence tests, not a collection of purchased subscriptions. Start with one consenting employer, one defined service, one authorized data source, and one signed compensation schedule. For a self-funded transition, the case file must identify current plan terms, proposed specific and aggregate stop-loss attachment points, exclusions, lasers, contract basis, run-out obligations, and funding responsibilities. An attractive premium comparison does not settle shock-claim liquidity or coverage gaps between policies. Record who evaluates reference-based pricing balance-bill exposure and who provides any contracted legal defense; do not imply that a repricing algorithm supplies representation. For a PBM review, obtain rebate definitions, reconciliation timing, audit rights, exclusions, and guarantees from executed contracts. For an E-Mod assignment, confirm the bureau, experience period, payroll classifications, correction procedures, and carrier refund process. A projected modifier change is not a received refund. Build the invoice from the resulting authorized service evidence and reconcile it to the contractual trigger. If the first deal cannot be explained from source document through calculation to bank receipt, more prospect volume will multiply exceptions rather than improve the operating model. Keep that first transaction small enough to review completely.

Test controls using synthetic cases before any live claim import. Include a duplicate prescription, a reversed claim, a mismatched NDC package, an ineligible encounter, a missing payroll classification, and a stale loss valuation. The expected behavior is an exception record with an assigned owner, not a silently completed savings calculation. Restore a backup into an isolated environment and prove that document versions, calculation inputs, access permissions, and invoice evidence remain usable. Test contractor offboarding by removing access and checking active sessions, shared links, service tokens, and local synchronization. Maintain an incident contact sheet covering the customer, insurer, hosting provider, and specialist response support. HIPAA security requirements at 45 CFR 164.308 include risk analysis, workforce controls, contingency planning, and evaluation where applicable; 45 CFR 164.502(e) addresses business-associate arrangements. Translate each applicable requirement into an owner and retained evidence. Do not send production claim files through ordinary scheduling or prospect automation accounts. Use an access review to verify that aggregate dashboards cannot expose underlying member rows through exports or drill-through features. A successful screenshot is insufficient when the actual export path remains unrestricted or the recovery copy cannot be restored.

Release additional capacity only after reconciling delivery economics and cash timing for the completed pilot. Track enrolled lives actually invoiced, retained PEPM, commission receipts, contractor hours, correction cycles, refunds collected, and days from completed work to cash. Maintain a separate register for employer savings estimates so they cannot inflate operator revenue. For principal-versus-agent presentation under ASC 606, document which services the operator controls and which receipts merely pass through; the supplied scorecard uses retained operator revenue and excludes premiums and claim funds. Do not apply a second retained-share haircut to amounts already supplied on that basis. Review the 13-week forecast every week and compare actual spending with the fixed-overhead envelope plus remaining scenario costs. At $535,000 Base revenue, allowing annual costs above $294,250 necessarily reduces the stated $240,750 profit unless revenue also changes. Preserve that arithmetic when considering another contractor or application. Expand one dimension at a time: more employers, another jurisdiction, or another service line. If collection slows, freeze discretionary acquisition, renegotiate delivery timing, and return to the lowest-cost validated workflow. Scaling should purchase measured throughput while preserving source traceability, not conceal unresolved exceptions behind a larger team.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Freeze scope and reserve
   - Choose one service line and fund the protected working-capital account.
   - Record startup allocations, weekly burn limits, collection assumptions, and the specific liquidity response to a five-month first-deal delay.
2. Contract authority and data access
   - Complete licensing checks, engagement terms, disclosures, and applicable data agreements.
   - Identify the responsible fiduciary or covered-entity contact, permitted data recipients, subcontractors, compensation sources, and termination duties.
3. Rehearse the production calculation
   - Run synthetic exceptions, independent calculation review, and backup restoration.
   - Preserve test inputs, expected outputs, reviewer signoff, recovery evidence, and remediation records before importing authorized live information.
4. Close the cash-and-evidence loop
   - Deliver one bounded engagement and reconcile earned fees to actual receipts.
   - Compare measured labor, correction time, collection delay, and contribution with the scenario cost bridge before increasing recurring commitments.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four proofs required before the first live production file**
> 
> The principal retains the evidence and assigns a named owner to every unresolved exception; a purchased subscription is not evidence of readiness.
> - Authority proof: verify applicable producer and entity authority, appointments, TPA scope, signed engagement terms, and required compensation disclosures.
> - Data proof: document permitted purposes, applicable business associate agreements, least-privilege access, private storage, and tested backup restoration.
> - Economic proof: reconcile one sample invoice to retained fees, delivery expense, cash timing, and a reserve forecast that excludes all client funds.
> - Delivery proof: independently reproduce one stop-loss comparison, PBM reconciliation, E-Mod correction, or eligible 340B fee calculation from preserved source evidence.
