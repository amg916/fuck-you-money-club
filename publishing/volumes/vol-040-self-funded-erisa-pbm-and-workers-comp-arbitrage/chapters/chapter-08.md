## 1. Build the public-record spine before buying enrichment  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an employer-plan identity spine, not a purchased contact list. Download the Department of Labor Form 5500 datasets, retain each original archive, and key observations by sponsor EIN, plan number, filing year, and amendment status. Preserve sponsor names as reported while keeping a separate normalized matching field. A sponsor can maintain several welfare plans, change administrators, or file an amended return; merging everything under a similar trading name manufactures false renewal opportunities. Schedule A can identify reported insurance contracts and compensation, while Schedule C contains qualifying service-provider reporting where applicable. Neither schedule provides a complete current contract inventory. Filing deadlines, extensions, reporting exemptions, and processing time make these historical discovery records rather than live renewal calendars. Compare two or three filing years, inspect material changes in funding answers and service relationships, and then request the current plan document and renewal schedule from an authorized employer representative. Record the observation date separately from the coverage period. Use SEC submissions for public-company identity corroboration and SAM.gov for relevant contractor entities, but never infer that a corporate subsidiary shares its parent's benefit arrangements. A clean identity crosswalk prevents expensive repricing work on the wrong population.

Organize the eleven mapped plays as explicit work queues: Form 5500 renewal targeting; broker compensation reconstruction; self-funded transition screening; captive participation screening; stop-loss attachment and laser review; run-out exposure reconstruction; NADAC-based PBM repricing; rebate recovery reconciliation; reference-based pricing defense review; Workers' Comp classification and E-Mod correction; and saas_technographic_churn_pixel_scraping. These work-queue labels describe this chapter's operating decomposition rather than additional supplied play identifiers. Every queue needs an entry document, a calculation, an authorized reviewer, and an exit artifact. For example, renewal targeting starts with a filing observation, calculates days until a verified policy anniversary, and exits with a dated meeting brief. Compensation reconstruction starts with engagement letters and carrier statements, separates direct employer fees from indirect remuneration, and exits with an ERISA section 408(b)(2) disclosure package where the health-plan brokerage or consulting provisions apply. Captive screening requires participation terms and collateral requirements, not just an attractive quote. Reference-based pricing review requires actual provider-dispute and defense terms, not a generic savings percentage. Give each queue a reason to stop: unavailable authority, unmatched policy, incomplete claim period, or unresolved allocation. Such stops preserve analyst capacity for evidence that can support a transaction.

Use the first nine directory entries as a low-cost discovery and reference layer. CMS NADAC provides acquisition-cost benchmarks, not a complete measure of an employer's contractual drug cost. Preserve effective dates, units, package identifiers, and the original extract before joining it to an authorized pharmacy claim. RxNav and the FDA NDC Directory help resolve identifiers, but neither substitutes for the PBM's paid-claim ledger or establishes therapeutic equivalence. NPPES identifies providers; an NPI does not establish network participation, credentialing status, or payment entitlement. Transparency in Coverage files can support selected negotiated-rate comparisons, yet their size, reporting relationships, and plan-specific applicability make broad downloads wasteful. Fetch a relevant index first, isolate the required payer and service family, and cap storage before expanding. A five-gigabyte partition downloaded twice consumes ten gigabytes of transfer without adding evidence; content hashes and conditional requests avoid that duplication. Treat every published cadence as the publisher's intended schedule, with observed release timestamps controlling analysis. Public availability also does not imply permission to redistribute every assembled product. Store source terms alongside each connector, and send stale or incomplete observations into a document-request queue rather than silently substituting a more convenient benchmark.

## 3. Reconcile diagnostic tools to claims, rating documents, and retained revenue  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

A useful PBM diagnostic separates paid ingredient cost, dispensing fees, administrative charges, member cost sharing, rebates, and other contractual remuneration. Obtain an employer-authorized, appropriately protected claim extract and a data dictionary before calculating anything. Match reversals and resubmissions, preserve service dates, and normalize quantities only with a documented conversion. For an illustrative fill of thirty units, a $2.10 NADAC benchmark produces $63 of benchmark ingredient cost; a comparable $78 ingredient charge leaves a $15 diagnostic difference before dispensing fees, administrative charges, and rebate treatment. One thousand comparable fills would produce $15,000 of benchmark difference, not established recoverable savings and not desk revenue. Actual recovery requires contractual entitlement, suitable comparability, and a settled employer credit. Rebate analysis must match covered drug periods, eligible utilization, guarantee definitions, exclusions, and reconciliation dates. If an employer receives $40,000 and the desk's enforceable retained split is 20%, the associated fee basis is $8,000 when recognition conditions are met; the remaining $32,000 never becomes operator revenue. Where specialty products lack suitable benchmarks, segregate them and request contract-specific evidence. Do not extrapolate the clean generic sample across excluded specialty utilization merely to enlarge the proposal.

Workers' Comp diagnostics need an equally disciplined distinction between an indicative model and an issued correction. With $1,000,000 of payroll and a hypothetical manual rate of $4 per $100, the manual premium component is $40,000. Multiplying that component by 1.20 rather than 1.10 changes it by $4,000 before schedule factors, credits, assessments, minimums, and other policy adjustments. This sensitivity calculation does not establish the actual corrected premium or prove that the experience modifier should change. Reproduce the applicable bureau rules, valuation dates, split points, payroll classifications, and loss treatment using authorized records; then submit documented discrepancies through the proper carrier or bureau process. Stop-loss analysis needs a separate worksheet containing specific and aggregate attachment points, named lasers, contract basis, aggregating deductibles, and run-in or run-out treatment. A $250,000 specific attachment with a $500,000 claimant laser leaves an additional $250,000 of claimant-specific exposure under otherwise comparable terms; model that exposure rather than counting premium reduction alone. If claim detail or policy wording remains incomplete, obtain a binding clarification or compare documented alternatives. Captive collateral and reference-based pricing defense obligations belong in the employer risk model, never in the desk's sales-revenue column.

Keep the scorecard ledger separate from all employer savings and insurance flows. Formula v2.0 defines Conservative revenue of $120,000 at 32%, producing $38,400 owner profit and $81,600 costs; Base revenue of $535,000 at 45.0%, producing $240,750 profit and $294,250 costs; and Scaled revenue of $950,000 at 58%, producing $551,000 profit and $399,000 costs. Teams remain 1 FT plus 3 C in Conservative and Base, and 2 FT plus 4 C in Scaled. Although the scorecard calls the topline gross revenue, its stated basis is already net retained operator fees, commissions, and refund splits. Do not apply an invented retained-share discount a second time. An illustrative Base composition is 2,500 covered employees times $10 retained PEPM times twelve months, or $300,000, plus $175,000 retained stop-loss/TPA commissions and $60,000 collected refund splits. The last component could represent a 25% retained share of $240,000 settled employer refunds. These components total exactly $535,000 and are a reconciliation example, not extra revenue assumptions. Apply ASC 606 principal-versus-agent presentation to the actual arrangements and evaluate variable consideration and performance obligations. Cash collection, signed engagement, and revenue recognition are distinct ledger events.

## 4. Operate a reproducible pipeline with bounded access and evidence-backed handoffs  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a three-zone pipeline: immutable source objects, normalized working records, and approved employer-level outputs. A small desk can implement control tables with SQLite, transformations with Python's csv, decimal, datetime, hashlib, and json modules, and scheduled execution through its existing job runner. These choices avoid unnecessary subscription sprawl, but the standard library alone does not provide encrypted databases or managed secrets. Place files on appropriately encrypted storage, keep tokens in an approved credential service, and apply account-level access controls. Every ingestion manifest should contain source URL or delivery channel, retrieval time, effective period, content hash, authorization reference, schema version, row count, and control totals. Use Decimal for money and explicit currency fields; binary floating-point arithmetic can create avoidable penny mismatches across large claim files. Record rejected rows with a specific reason and reconcile accepted plus rejected counts to the source. Idempotent loading should recognize an identical object and skip duplication while preserving a new amendment as a separate version. Separate network failures from legitimate empty datasets. An empty response after a timeout is not evidence that a broker relationship disappeared or a claim population fell to zero.

Build protections around the actual data role. Public plan filings belong in the discovery zone; member-level claim records belong in a restricted analytical workspace with access limited to the engagement team. Determine whether the desk acts as a business associate and execute required arrangements under 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e) before receiving protected health information in that role. Apply minimum-necessary requirements where applicable under 45 CFR 164.502(b), and implement access, audit, integrity, and transmission safeguards under 45 CFR 164.312. Hashing member identifiers alone does not establish HIPAA de-identification; use a supported method under 45 CFR 164.514 when that outcome is required. Keep claims out of CRM notes, ordinary email attachments, public issue trackers, and commercial technographic APIs. Give contractors engagement-specific access and remove it at assignment end. Establish retention periods from contractual duties, applicable rules, and litigation holds, then verify deletion across working copies and backups according to the approved schedule. For an outbound report, inspect the actual recipient list and deliver through the agreed secure channel. Employer-level aggregate findings still require review for small-cell disclosure and contractual restrictions; aggregation is a technique, not an automatic permission to publish.

Run the desk on an exception dashboard rather than a pile of connector success messages. Track source age, unmatched employer identities, missing authorization references, claim-total differences, unresolved product conversions, stale license checks, and proposals lacking a current renewal document. Give every exception an owner and an explicit next action. Test connectors with saved, authorized fixtures so a vendor outage cannot silently change the analytical result. A schema change that renames a paid-amount column must stop the affected calculation, while an optional website-description change should not halt unrelated work. Use bounded retries with exponential backoff and jitter, honor Retry-After where provided, and stop after the connector's configured retry budget. Review a small sample of accepted records as well as rejected records; perfectly reconciled totals can still hide a wrong join. At weekly handoff, the analyst supplies the evidence manifest, calculation version, open assumptions, and exact client question. The licensed producer confirms service scope and commercial disclosures before recommendations leave the desk. Measure useful throughput as verified employer briefs and resolved discrepancies per analyst hour, not harvested rows. This discipline supports the scorecard's small teams without pretending automation eliminates underwriting uncertainty, carrier judgment, or client-specific professional review.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the source and authority
   - Classify each connector as public, licensed, or employer-authorized before scheduling retrieval.
   - Record permitted fields, source terms, responsible owner, expected cadence, cost ceiling, and the applicable account or engagement authorization.
2. Capture and reconcile the original
   - Store the unmodified object, create its content hash, and reconcile row counts and monetary control totals.
   - Load amendments separately, retain rejected-row explanations, and distinguish transport errors from valid empty results.
3. Run scoped diagnostics
   - Apply versioned employer matching, dated PBM benchmarks, issued rating inputs, and retained-fee calculations.
   - Use saved test fixtures, decimal arithmetic, explicit unit conversions, and independent review of high-value findings.
4. Release the evidence-backed handoff
   - Deliver a reviewed employer brief through the agreed secure channel and reconcile resulting commercial events.
   - Include source age, unresolved assumptions, verified renewal dates, compensation terms, and the next counterparty action without exposing unnecessary member information.
