## 1. Sell an auditable administrative service, never a slice of litigation  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Mass-tort case acquisition begins with attracting potential claimants, but the defensible business is a contracted operating system around a law firm's intake desk. The non-lawyer management services organization supplies campaign administration, scripted intake, document routing, identity checks, record retrieval, and evidence indexing. The law firm decides whom to represent, what legal theories to pursue, which deadlines control, and whether to settle. Those boundaries belong in the agreement, staff permissions, call scripts, and invoice descriptions. A service described as administrative becomes a different proposition when compensation follows settlement size or staff promise a claimant that a lawsuit qualifies. Applicable state versions of ABA Model Rule 5.4, solicitation restrictions, and unauthorized-practice rules control; the ABA model text itself is not a nationwide operating license. California Business and Professions Code sections 6151 and 6152 illustrate why a paid runner model cannot be repaired merely by renaming referral payments. Start with a documented fair-market monthly scope, defined capacity, and separately priced change orders. Preserve attorney supervision without letting the vendor choose legal outcomes. The first durable asset is a clean allocation of authority that a replacement employee can follow without interpreting litigation strategy.

The primary mapped play is aba_compliant_law_firm_mso_and_hitech_emr_triage, designated TRACK_1. Its unit is a service obligation: for example, an illustrative $10,000 monthly desk contract buys defined staffing coverage, secure routing, and a stated retrieval workload, rather than ten successful lawsuits. Twelve delivered months produce $120,000 of service billings before credits, collection losses, or principal-versus-agent adjustments. The same arrangement can use documented cost-plus pricing, such as $8,000 of eligible monthly costs plus a 25% markup, provided the result remains supportable and is not a disguised recommendation payment. Patient access and medical chronology are operational services, not diagnoses or legal sufficiency opinions. A licensed clinician handles clinical ambiguity where the engagement requires that expertise; counsel determines whether evidence supports representation. An actually licensed Arizona alternative business structure offers another ownership path under Arizona Supreme Court Rule 31.1 and ACJA section 7-209. That license does not export permission into every state. If the compensation design, marketing, or authority fails review, suspend the affected work, restrict improperly obtained records, route legal decisions to counsel, and resume only the services supported by corrected contracts, permissions, and jurisdictional authority.

For this chapter, the 13 mapped plays are an editorial operating map, not thirteen independently supplied scorecards: the core MSO play; duplicate-claimant screening; retainer administration; patient-access retrieval; pharmacy-proof retrieval; exposure chronology; deficiency remediation; attorney-advertising operations; CRM integration; service reporting; Arizona ABS administration; secure matter archiving; and the separately specified CTV supply play. The five archetypes below group these functions into businesses a small team can actually supervise. A founder should select one anchor contract and attach adjacent services only when the same evidence and permissions support them. Selling every function immediately creates incompatible turnaround promises and several distinct professional boundaries. The operational moat comes from provider-specific retrieval knowledge, reproducible identity matching, source-linked chronologies, and reliable replacement handling. It does not come from keeping claimants in a proprietary black box or making counsel dependent on inaccessible documents. Clients should receive exportable records and an intelligible audit trail. Price the cost of that portability into the service. Startup capital of $12,000–$50,000 funds initial systems, contract work, staffing deposits, and retrieval float; the expected first deal takes one to four months. Buying traffic before establishing these mechanics consumes capital without creating a usable operating asset.

> **INSIDER PRO-TIPS — Build the evidence trail before expanding paid intake**
> 
> Experienced operators buy fewer ambiguous handoffs and make every administrative decision reproducible from source evidence.
> - Store the exact landing-page text and TrustedForm certificate reference together; a certificate without the actual disclosure cannot explain what the claimant saw.

> - Use duplicate matches as review flags, not automatic accusations; twins, shared phones, changed surnames, and household addresses create legitimate collisions.
> - Negotiate a precise replacement window and evidence standard before launch; reserve expected rework costs when the original service is delivered.

## 2. Reconcile recognized service revenue to the conversion waterfall  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Recognized operator revenue is earned flat or documented cost-plus MSO administrative, marketing, intake, and records-triage fees. It excludes law-firm trust funds, settlement awards, claimant recoveries, and the firm's own legal fees. Under ASC 606, identify the customer contract, promised services, transaction price, allocation, and when each obligation is satisfied. A stand-ready desk commonly recognizes service revenue over the covered period; a distinct completed records packet may follow a different delivery pattern. Cash received in advance is not automatically earned revenue, while an earned receivable is not cash available for payroll. Track accrual revenue and collected fees separately. Evaluate whether the MSO controls media or retrieval services before transfer: principal treatment can support gross presentation, while an agent generally recognizes its fee or retained share rather than reimbursed spend. The canonical cases already specify operator service revenue; do not multiply them by an invented retained_share or add client advertising budgets on top. If $20,000 of client media funds passes through an agency arrangement alongside a $3,000 earned administration fee, the operating revenue is $3,000, not $23,000. Record credits and constrained variable consideration consistently with the underlying contract.

Use the full waterfall: raw inquiry -> signed law-firm retainer -> usable HITECH EMR/pharmacy proof -> deficiency-free accepted case -> collected MSO/vendor fee. An illustrative monthly cohort starts with 1,000 inquiries at $20 each, costing $20,000. Duplicate screening removes 200, and counsel-directed intake converts 25% of the remaining 800 into 200 signed retainers. Records work costs $60 per signed file, or $12,000. Usable proof arrives for 140 files, and 100 ultimately satisfy counsel's documented acceptance criteria without outstanding deficiencies. Suppose a separate fixed-capacity contract earns $50,000 that month and the client pays $48,000 initially; the remaining $2,000 is a receivable, not additional collected revenue. Another $10,000 of labor and systems produces $42,000 of direct operating costs, leaving $8,000 before other overhead on the earned basis and $6,000 on the immediate cash basis. These are illustrative cohort economics, not a replacement scorecard. Dividing the fee by 100 accepted cases gives a diagnostic $500 service allocation per accepted file, not permission to charge for recommendations. If proof yield falls from 70% to 50%, only 100 files reach the proof stage, while the original retrieval outlay remains $12,000.

The canonical annual cases reconcile exactly: $160,000 less $118,400 of total expenses equals $41,600 owner net profit; $705,000 less $437,100 equals $267,900; and $1,250,000 less $625,000 equals $625,000. Their margins are respectively 26%, 38.0%, and 50%, with teams of one full-time employee plus three contractors, one plus three, and two plus four. These figures are the complete scenario envelope, not proof that any traffic channel can produce those results. The stated $2,500–$14,000 monthly operating-expense range is a planning input rather than a cap on all-in scenario costs; the annual totals also have to absorb variable delivery and acquisition expenses. An illustrative revenue bridge uses four $40,000 annual contracts for Conservative, five $141,000 contracts for Base, and ten $125,000 contracts for Scaled. Actual scopes must justify those prices. Stress the cohort again with 300 duplicates, 175 signed files, and $10,500 of retrieval costs at the same signing rate and unit cost. Add twenty replacement files at $60 each: another $1,200 leaves the business before any additional collection. Preserve a cash reserve for provider invoices, replacements, and client payment delays rather than treating uncollected invoices as available owner distributions.

Demand source pages and event dates rather than treating extracted text as an established fact. Configure AWS Textract or another approved extraction service only after determining applicable privacy obligations, contractual protections, and access controls. ## 4. Turn permissions, source evidence, and replacement handling into the moat  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

HITECH is not a universal command to send any law-firm request cheaply and immediately. Classify the request before submitting it. Individual access under 45 CFR section 164.524 concerns the designated record set and carries specific access, timing, form, and fee rules. Covered entities ordinarily must act within thirty days; one additional thirty-day extension requires a timely written explanation and completion date. Electronic production depends on the requested form being readily producible or another agreed readable electronic format. A HIPAA authorization under section 164.508 is a different instrument, and third-party-directed requests require attention to HITECH's electronic-health-record provision and the Ciox litigation's effect on HHS interpretation. Do not extend the individual-access fee limitation automatically to third-party transmission. Track request type, patient signature, recipient designation, record scope, requested format, submission evidence, provider response, and assessed charges. State requirements may add applicable rights or restrictions. Separate the provider's permissible copying charge from the MSO's contracted administrative work. When a provider disputes the request, correct the routing or authority rather than impersonating the patient or using their portal credentials. Escalate contested access and pricing questions through the appropriate documented process.

Determine each participant's HIPAA role from actual functions and relationships. A law firm holding claimant records is not automatically a covered entity, and every vendor handling health information is not automatically its business associate. Where business-associate status applies, execute the required agreements and flow obligations to qualifying subcontractors under 45 CFR sections 164.502(e) and 164.504(e). Apply appropriate access restrictions and security even when another privacy regime governs. Separate client workspaces, require multifactor authentication, log exports, and restrict raw identifiers to personnel with a defined task. Do not promise cross-firm duplicate-retainer detection without lawful access to the relevant data; no generic identity tool establishes every existing representation. Use verified claimant statements, permitted client records, and counsel-directed conflict processes. Keep extraction output distinct from reviewed chronology. Professional reviewers should sample both accepted and rejected files because false rejections silently destroy usable evidence. Set retention and deletion instructions by contract, applicable obligations, and preservation needs; an access termination must not erase material subject to a litigation hold. The operator's security moat is repeatable evidence custody, not a dashboard badge or an unsupported claim that a tool solves privacy.

Finally, underwrite litigation concentration as a service-continuity risk rather than a settlement forecast. Federal Rule of Evidence 702 requires the proponent to establish the rule's admissibility conditions more likely than not; adverse general-causation rulings can collapse demand across an MDL. That can strand advertising commitments and retrieval work even when every inquiry was genuine. Diversify contracted services and clients, limit advance media exposure, and specify what happens to work in progress when counsel pauses a campaign. Avoid promising replacement claimants after scientific or legal criteria change; distinguish vendor defects from a new litigation decision. Run a weekly ledger linking invoice lines to service periods, completed tasks, credits, provider charges, and actual collections. Review aging by client, record source, and delivery cohort so that slow payment cannot hide behind aggregate lead volume. Scale only after the desk repeatedly delivers its contracted scope without founder-only interventions. The six-person Scaled case requires narrow workflows, bounded exceptions, and credible contractor capacity, not unlimited automation. If authority, evidence provenance, or compensation fails, stop that activity, preserve the audit trail, correct the defect, and restart the lawful service rather than buying more volume.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Define and price the service boundary
   - Execute a jurisdiction-reviewed administrative scope with measurable capacity.
   - List excluded legal decisions, eligible cost-plus inputs, replacement terms, change-order pricing, and invoice evidence.
2. Establish the authorized claimant record
   - Capture provenance, resolve identity exceptions, and route retainer decisions to the firm.
   - Preserve contact permissions and duplicate-review rationale; do not infer representation from an advertising inquiry.
3. Retrieve and review source-linked proof
   - Submit correctly classified requests and build a cited chronology.
   - Record provider charges and response dates, distinguish filled prescriptions from orders, and escalate clinical ambiguity.
4. Close the service and collection loop
   - Reconcile delivered obligations, client deficiencies, credits, and actual payments.
   - Update the full conversion waterfall, fund outstanding replacement exposure, and recognize revenue under the contract's performance obligations rather than projected case value.

> **OPERATOR GATE — Four pre-flight verifications before accepting live intake**
> 
> Proceed only when the desk can demonstrate its authority, source permissions, operational readiness, and cash coverage.
> - Verify the executed MSO agreement uses supportable fixed or documented cost-plus compensation and expressly reserves representation, legal advice, and settlement decisions to authorized lawyers.
> - Verify each acquisition source preserves the actual disclosure, firm-specific contact permission, suppression process, and applicable attorney-advertising requirements.
> - Verify records-request classification, valid patient authority, recipient details, applicable privacy contracts, and tested tenant-separated storage before receiving live records.
> - Verify the budget funds retrieval outlays and contracted replacements through the expected collection delay; exclude projected settlement receipts from available cash.
