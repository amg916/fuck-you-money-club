## 1. Convert opportunities into evidence-backed, bounded commitments  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Run one opportunity register, not separate spreadsheets for merchandise, rewards, and seats. The eleven mapped operating plays are committed BG fulfillment, insured shipment spread, supplier early-payment discounts, commercial-card rebates, statement float, primary ticket allocations, secondary-exchange repricing, stadium seat-license brokerage, corporate-suite subdivision, corporate allocation concierge, and free_llc_fintech_bounty_stacking. These are operating labels rather than eleven independent revenue additions: insurance protects an existing spread, and float changes timing rather than creating sales. Every record needs a source reference, counterparty, contractual buyer, expected gross collection, landed basis, reward eligibility, tax treatment, event date when relevant, and an evidence locator. A $5,000 BG reimbursement against $4,500 landed basis offers $500 before fees; a verified 2% reward on eligible $4,500 spend adds $90 once, not another $4,500 of revenue. Underwrite the trade without that reward until eligibility and redemption terms are documented. Require a written purchase commitment before ordering inventory, and retain the exact SKU, quantity, acceptance standard, and payment trigger. A vague chat promise cannot establish a collectible receivable. A counterparty accepting Net-3 after inspection is different from one promising Net-3 after warehouse delivery, so encode the triggering event explicitly.

Separate acquisition authority from resale feasibility. For tickets, record the original channel, permitted purchase quantity, transfer activation date, seat identifiers, and whether the venue permits the proposed allocation or suite subdivision. The BOTS Act, 15 U.S.C. §45c, prohibits specified circumvention of ticketing controls and covered resale of tickets obtained through that circumvention; ordinary automation must not bypass queues, CAPTCHA, or purchase limits. SkyBox or Automatiq can support inventory distribution where the operator has an authorized account and supported integration, but neither replaces venue transfer rules. A $1,000 ticket lot projected to collect $1,350 after mandatory buyer charges has only $350 before seller fees and losses. If the exchange deducts $135 and delivery costs $15, the usable spread is $200. Record those deductions separately from face value. For a $6,000 suite divided into eight permitted allocations, a $900 selling price produces $7,200 gross and $1,200 before service costs; six sales instead produce a $600 basis shortfall. If subdivision is prohibited or transfer remains locked, broker the intact allocation under written authority or decline it. Never substitute speculative availability for actual inventory control.

The formation play needs a distinct eligibility and commission subledger. Under free_llc_fintech_bounty_stacking, charge a $0 service fee while the founder pays mandatory state filing fees directly or through a clearly identified pass-through. Offer the post-formation 'Required Business Infrastructure Checklist' as an operational sequence, explicitly explaining that individual banking, corporate-card, payroll, bookkeeping, and merchant offers are voluntary and subject to provider eligibility. Do not claim every company must buy every product. Three earned $200 commissions produce $600; five earned $360 commissions produce $1,800, before servicing costs and reversals. These are underwriting examples, not quoted provider offers. Pre-qualify founders on documented 30-day operating revenue intent and genuine product need. The fallback is to condition optional operating-agreement and bookkeeping templates on verified funding of an eligible business bank account, with that condition disclosed before signup and essential formation documents delivered regardless. Never request account credentials or manufacture funding transactions. Disclose paid referral relationships under 16 CFR §255.5, track each provider's actual payout and reversal window, and recognize only amounts supported by the applicable contract. No formation commissions enter the worked Base cohort; adding them without additional costs would silently change the canonical model.

## 2. Install a six-module ledger with serialized transitions and replay-safe receipts  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

The reference implementation below uses integer cents, SQLite foreign keys, unique source references, immutable receipt fingerprints, and BEGIN IMMEDIATE transactions. Save each code cell under its stated filename in one directory; run the schema first, then intake, workflow, dispatch, receipts, and reporting through the six modules. Schema creation and reporting share the first and sixth modules respectively. The workflow serializes capacity checks with state changes, eliminating the local check-then-purchase race. Two workers cannot claim the same ready instruction because claiming updates its state inside the same write transaction. A repeated receipt with identical content returns harmlessly; reusing its identifier with different content fails. The double-entry representation stores one debit and one equal credit per posting, making unbalanced individual postings unrepresentable. Production access must restrict direct database writes to these controlled processes. Use local persistent storage rather than a shared network filesystem, take SQLite-aware backups, and retain source documents outside the database under immutable object identifiers. This reference deliberately stops at an external instruction envelope: it does not claim that a terminal printout executes a purchase or verifies a bank deposit. An authorized adapter or documented operator confirmation must establish the actual external result.

Exactly-once effects across an external issuer, exchange, and local database cannot be established by a UNIQUE constraint alone. The dispatcher prints a stable instruction identifier and then refuses to issue that instruction again automatically. If the process stops after claiming but before printing, the item remains claimed for investigation. Query the external provider using that identifier before any recovery; reuse the identifier only where the provider contract supports durable idempotency. Otherwise reconcile manually and never infer failure from a timeout. VERIFIED means the operator has attached acquisition authority and delivery evidence; ESCROW_READY means funds are reserved internally, not that a licensed escrow relationship exists. SUBMITTED means an instruction has been claimed, FUNDED requires a supported purchase receipt, and CLOSED requires collected proceeds plus repayment of any associated card principal. The funding receipt selects cash or card treatment explicitly. A card purchase credits card payable, never cash; its later repayment debits that payable. Credit limits are not cash reserves. Actual escrow providers can replace the internal reserve only through a documented custody agreement and corresponding external evidence. Keep authorization documents separate from bank settlement proof so one cannot masquerade as the other.

The ledger treats the scorecard's 35% retained share as the economic presentation for this chapter, not a universal ASC 606 conclusion. Principal-versus-agent presentation follows the actual control analysis for each contract. Here, a settled collection credits retained revenue for gross less basis and clears the corresponding basis advance through a customer-funds payable bridge. The gross collection remains visible in the deals table rather than being mislabeled retained revenue. Provider rewards included in a deal's gross must be posted only after they become supported proceeds; otherwise create a separate reward record when earned and avoid double counting. The reporting view reconciles cash movement, retained revenue, operating expense, open advances, and card liabilities. The included QA checks prove trial-balance equality, state completeness, card reconciliation, and settled-deal arithmetic; they do not prove that uploaded evidence is authentic. Require a second person to compare high-value receipts against bank or issuer exports. Match SkyBox or Automatiq settlement identifiers to the same deal references used for acquisition. Keep credentials out of database rows and command arguments. Exercise concurrent dispatch, duplicate receipts, failed transitions, and process interruption in a test copy before enabling any external purchasing adapter.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Initialize and prove the local cash lane
   - Save the six reference files and run python schema.py in an isolated test directory.
   - Post a capital receipt, intake one $5,000 gross and $4,500 basis deal, then retain the returned deterministic deal identifier.
2. Verify evidence and reserve exposure
   - Run workflow.py with the deal identifier, verify, and two immutable evidence locators; then run its reserve action.
   - Use real authorization and delivery documents in production; a string locator alone does not establish authenticity or purchasing permission.
3. Claim and confirm the external instruction
   - Run dispatch.py once and execute only through an explicitly authorized purchasing channel.
   - Post funded evidence with cash or card rail; a card receipt additionally requires the issuer transaction reference and actual payment due date.
4. Settle, repay, replay, and reconcile
   - Post settled evidence with exact gross cents, post card_paid when applicable, then run qa_report.py.
   - Replay the identical receipt to confirm no duplicate posting; alter its content under the same identifier and confirm rejection in the test database.

## 3. Work the 90-day cohort from scored leads to collected owner contribution  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Treat this as a worked target-economics cohort, not observed trading history. Each month begins with 60 screened opportunities: 24 BG shipments, 12 ticket lots, and four suite or allocation packages execute; 20 opportunities are scrubbed before purchase. Ten fail commitment or counterparty checks, six fail transfer or venue rules, and four fail liquidity or fee-adjusted spread tests. Monthly gross throughput comprises $60,000 BG reimbursements, $1,000 earned rewards, $18,000 ticket proceeds, and approximately $6,416.67 allocation proceeds. BG basis is $42,000, ticket basis is $10,000, and allocation basis is approximately $3,520.83. The implied BG unit is $2,500 reimbursement against $1,750 basis; the reward yield is 2.38% of the $42,000 eligible merchandise spend. Those merchandise discounts and ticket spreads are demanding procurement assumptions calibrated to the supplied 35% retained-share model. Ordinary retail purchases may not support them. Reject the cohort if executable quotes cannot produce the aggregate spread after acquisition costs; do not disguise the shortfall with additional float. Recognize rewards separately from issuer principal, and include them in gross only once. Cent adjustments in the matrix make the quarter exact without overstating its annualized run rate.

The monthly retained contribution is approximately $29,895.83, followed by $8,937.50 of direct operating costs and $9,000 of fixed overhead. The write-offs and dispute losses represent incremental cash losses outside the delivered units' stated basis; they must not also be deducted from gross proceeds or included in that basis. Ticket markdowns are already reflected in realized selling prices, so there is no second markdown expense. Fixed overhead covers the operator's staffing and operating structure at the canonical monthly range ceiling; it is not the complete cost stack. The one-FT, two-contractor team divides acquisition, fulfillment, and reconciliation without assuming free contractor labor. Owner profit is the model's residual before owner distributions, not an additional salary charge invented for this chapter. At the annual level, $107,250 direct costs plus $108,000 fixed overhead equal $215,250. Subtracting that from $358,750 retained revenue yields exactly $143,500.
