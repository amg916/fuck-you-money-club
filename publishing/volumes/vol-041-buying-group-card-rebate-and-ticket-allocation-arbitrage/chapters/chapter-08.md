## 1. Build an evidence waterfall before extending counterparty exposure  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with an evidence waterfall, not a purchased contact list. For each buying group, capture the contracting entity, formation jurisdiction, filing number, remittance instructions, authorized signer, and a purchase-order acceptance that names the merchandise and rejection rules. Search the formation registry first; then search applicable UCC records under the exact debtor name and appropriate filing jurisdiction. A registered entity can still be insolvent, and a financing statement describes a claimed security interest rather than a current payoff balance. Review sanctions results using identifying attributes instead of treating a similar name as a definitive match. Ask the counterparty for resale documentation and verify it through the relevant state's available process. Bank-account ownership and payout reliability require separate evidence; neither a Secretary-of-State record nor a SAM registration establishes them. Obtain payment changes through a callback to a previously verified contact, never through the changed email thread alone. Store the source URL, retrieval time, document hash, reviewer, and next review date. When a portal offers only interactive search, use a documented manual lookup rather than inventing an API or scraping around access restrictions. An unavailable registry becomes an open exposure exception, not a fabricated clean result.

The chapter's eleven mapped plays are committed buying-group fulfillment, counterparty routing, commercial-card rebates, statement-float scheduling, insured shipment recovery, secondary-ticket resale, event-decay repricing, stadium seat-license brokerage, corporate-suite allocation, resale-tax reconciliation, and sportsbook promo conversion. These are operating mappings, not additional catalog identifiers. Give each play a measurable unit and a source that can contradict the trade thesis. A valid exemption on a $10,000 taxable purchase at an illustrative 8% rate avoids an $800 acquisition tax payment; it is not automatically $800 of revenue. If documentation fails, price the actual tax treatment or decline the order. For Net-3 and Net-5 buyers, define when the clock starts: carrier delivery, inspection acceptance, or invoice approval can produce materially different cash dates. Record settlement percentiles from the desk's own receipts and cap new commitments against the slower observed outcome. Public records support identity; historical settlements determine usable credit capacity.

Run enrichment in increasing order of expense and sensitivity. Normalize the entity name and address, resolve the official filing identifier, retrieve relevant filings, request the signed contract and resale certificate, then validate payout changes and insurer eligibility. Use domain and telephone checks only as corroboration, because a polished website and working number are inexpensive to create. ## 2. Connect contracted inventory feeds without mistaking access for trading rights  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use Ticketmaster Discovery to identify events, venues, attractions, and published event status, not to obtain privileged inventory or bypass ticket purchase controls. Its developer key and documented endpoints provide metadata within the account's permitted limits; availability of a discovery record says nothing about transferable ownership. Resolve the venue, local start time, timezone, event identifier, seating description, and postponement status before merging a seller's stock. Then connect SkyBox or Automatiq only through the commercial access actually granted to the desk. Product names do not establish API entitlement, exchange coverage, webhook availability, or a guaranteed synchronization interval. Obtain written interface documentation and test listing creation, cancellation, partial fills, and duplicate delivery in a nonproduction workflow where offered. A $150 ticket sale against $100 face basis, a $22.50 selling fee, and $5 fulfillment cost leaves $22.50 before shared operating costs. If the same seat sells twice, replacement costs can erase many such units. Assign each owned ticket a stable inventory key and lock that key while a sale is being resolved. When feed freshness exceeds the desk's threshold, suspend affected listings rather than treating cached availability as permission to continue selling.

Card tooling must distinguish authorization, posting, statement close, payment due, rebate accrual, and rebate receipt. A transaction feed can help reconcile merchant spend, but it does not establish a universal sixty-day interest-free period. Actual usable float depends on purchase timing, issuer terms, grace eligibility, and payment execution. On $10,000 of qualifying spend, a contractual 3% rebate contributes $300 before fees, exclusions, returns, and issuer adjustments. An illustrative 40-day funding alternative at 12% simple annual cost is $131.51, calculated as $10,000 multiplied by 0.12 and 40/365. That avoided cost is a comparison, not an additional cash rebate to book. Stress the ledger for an ACH receipt arriving after the card due date, an issuer review that freezes new spending, and a reward reversal after a return. Use statement exports as the final payment-schedule reference and bank settlement evidence for receipts. Plaid coverage and product support vary by institution and account type; confirm commercial-card compatibility before buying a connection. Never promise uncapped 2%–4% economics without checking the actual agreement, qualifying categories, annual caps, and redemption conditions for that specific account.

The second nine resources connect event truth, contractual inventory, money movement, shipments, and local diagnostics. Refresh intervals in the directory are operating targets, not vendor service commitments. Prefer an authorized webhook where available, but keep a scheduled reconciliation because callbacks can arrive late, repeat, or disappear. For polling, honor documented quotas and Retry-After responses, apply bounded exponential backoff with jitter, and stop retrying authentication failures until credentials are repaired. Store secrets outside exported datasets and restrict connector accounts to the minimum read or trading scope required. Shipment protection deserves its own evidence chain: a $20,000 shipment with an illustrative 0.5% insurance premium costs $100, but the policy's exclusions and declared-value rules determine whether the merchandise is covered. Carrier tracking proves a recorded scan, not contents, acceptance quality, or insurance recovery. Preserve serials, packing photographs, weights, purchase invoices, and the insurance certificate. If delivery stalls, pause replacement purchasing until the original exposure is understood. Run SQLite integrity checks and HTTP diagnostics against sanitized examples, not live credential dumps. The fallback for a broken integration is a controlled export and reconciled exception queue, not silent manual edits to production balances.

## 3. Reconcile immutable events into exposure, inventory, and cash decisions  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Design the pipeline around immutable source events and reversible derived views. A compact SQLite deployment can hold counterparties, commitments, purchase lines, shipments, tickets, card postings, cash receipts, and exception records without requiring a data warehouse. Give every imported item a source identifier, account identifier, retrieval timestamp, source event time, currency, and provenance hash. Use integer cents or Decimal for money, never binary floating-point totals that drift during reconciliation. Store event timestamps in UTC while preserving the venue timezone and original local representation. Distinguish an absent value from a verified zero; missing fee information should prevent a profitability calculation instead of silently increasing the spread. Idempotency keys should reflect the source's stable transaction identifier and event version where available. Do not deduplicate two legitimate purchases merely because their amounts and merchant names match. Retain raw payloads only when licensing and privacy requirements permit, with access controls and a defined retention schedule. For restricted payloads, preserve permitted normalized fields and retrieval evidence. A scheduled backup must include restoration testing; an intact database file is insufficient if connector credentials, schema migrations, and reconciliation checkpoints cannot be recovered on another authorized workstation.

Separate receipt of a feed message from permission to change economic state. A purchase-order acknowledgment creates committed demand; a card authorization creates provisional spend; a posted charge creates a payable; an accepted delivery creates the contractual reimbursement claim; and a settled ACH creates cash. Build explicit transitions and retain reversals rather than overwriting the prior state. For tickets, distinguish owned, listed, reserved, sold, transferred, accepted, and paid. A transfer email without recipient acceptance may leave fulfillment risk outstanding. Use a reconciliation queue that identifies the oldest unmatched dollar amount and the person responsible for resolving it. Alert when buyer cash is forecast after the issuer's actual payment deadline, not merely when an invoice looks overdue. Maintain a daily exposure report by buyer, issuer, event, carrier, and bank rail. Twenty tickets with a planned $25 markdown loss each imply a $500 event risk budget; a cancellation can exceed that budget, so the model also needs refund and unrecoverable-fee cases. Apply pre-event markdown curves from executable market evidence, with explicit minimum proceeds and inventory-review times. Asking prices alone do not prove that the desk can liquidate its seats.

Build the operator screen around decisions, not decorative charts. Show available settled cash, issuer payments due, accepted-but-unpaid buying-group receivables, inventory that can actually transfer, and exceptions requiring human action. Every figure should open its component transactions and evidence trail. A stale event-status feed should disable automated repricing for that event until an official source is checked; it should not disable unrelated shipment reconciliation. Give write-enabled connectors separate permissions and audit trails from read-only research connectors. Replay sanitized fixtures for duplicate webhooks, partial refunds, a timezone change, a postponed event, a returned ACH, and a card-reward reversal. Validate both the expected balance and the expected operational restriction. For a stadium seat-license brokerage engagement, an illustrative $1,000 earned fee less $250 of direct diligence cost leaves $750 before shared costs, but only the governing license agreement can establish assignment rights and required transfer charges. If rights cannot be verified, stop marketing that allocation and retain the documentary exception. Review access logs weekly, remove departed contractors promptly, and keep participant identity material outside broadly shared analytics. The best automation reduces unresolved exposure rather than merely reducing keystrokes.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Register the source and its permission boundary
   - Record access owner, supported interface, pricing basis, documented quota, and permitted data uses.
   - Test a read-only retrieval and archive a sanitized example before allowing production ingestion or any listing writes.
2. Normalize and quarantine conflicting evidence
   - Resolve entity, event, shipment, and transaction identifiers before calculating exposure or margin.
   - Reject duplicate economic events while retaining legitimate equal-value transactions; route incomplete fee or currency fields to an exception queue.
3. Reconcile state transitions and settlement dates
   - Match commitments to purchases, acceptance to receivables, and posted cash to the relevant obligation.
   - Recompute issuer coverage and event exposure after each material update; preserve reversals rather than replacing historical records.
4. Prove recovery before enabling automation
   - Replay outage and duplicate-message fixtures, restore a backup, and reconcile the resulting balances.
   - Enable bounded automation only for verified paths; keep unrelated workflows operational when one source becomes stale or unavailable.

## 4. Bound promo claims and reconcile every play to retained owner economics  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

Its advertised certainty needs correction: bonuses, opposing wagers, and sweepstakes credits do not produce guaranteed risk-free cash. Eligibility, stake-return rules, odds movement, market suspension, payout restrictions, tax treatment, and conflicting settlement rules can all change proceeds. A theoretical fifteen offers averaging $300 at 70% conversion produces $3,150 before costs; twenty-five offers averaging $400 at 80% produces $8,000 before costs. Neither example establishes that those offers coexist for a resident or remain available. Treat the supplied 70%–85% band as a conditional modeling assumption, never as an observed constant. Maintain an offer ledger containing the official terms, eligibility date, bonus type, minimum odds, expiry, qualifying-wager cost, and withdrawal conditions. Do not mix regulated sportsbook authorization with sweepstakes eligibility; their state availability and governing rules require separate checks. If terms prohibit coordinated play or the economics require someone else's credentials, exclude that offer. An information platform should calculate participant-specific expected outcomes and downside cases, not manufacture certainty by hiding losing legs, taxes, or disqualified promotions.

Use the requested client-side fallback as an ownership control: each participant acts only on their own personal device, account, and cellular connection, without location spoofing, credential sharing, or delegated wager placement. This does not override operator terms or state restrictions. Prefer high-liquidity mainlines when permitted, but recognize that hedge availability can disappear between executions.
