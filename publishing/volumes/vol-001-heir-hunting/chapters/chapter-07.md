## Executive Summary

Separate genealogical identity, legally qualifying relationships, and distribution math. Use the Genealogical Proof Standard as a research discipline, not as a substitute for the jurisdiction's evidentiary rules or a court's determination of heirship.

## Build a proof packet, not a name list  `🏷️ PROFESSIONAL RESEARCH METHOD; NOT A STATUTORY PROOF RULE.`

Start with the decedent's identity, domicile, death record, and probate file. Document each parent-child and marital link with source citations, record images where permitted, and an explanation of what each record actually establishes.

Apply the Genealogical Proof Standard: reasonably exhaustive research, complete and accurate citations, analysis and correlation, resolution of conflicting evidence, and a sound written conclusion. An index or family tree is a lead, not conclusive proof.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Define the question
   - Record the relevant identity, relationship, jurisdiction, and missing evidence.
2. Correlate independent sources
   - Compare dates, locations, relatives, and informants; record negative searches.
3. Resolve and review
   - Write the conflict analysis and obtain independent review before labeling a link supported.

## Calculate only after the legal rule is selected  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE ARITHMETIC UNDER EXPRESSLY ASSUMED BRANCH REPRESENTATION.`

Assume a $180,000 net intestate estate, no surviving spouse, and three child branches. Two children survive; the third predeceased the decedent leaving two qualifying children. Under the assumed branch-based rule, each branch receives $60,000 and each grandchild in the third branch receives $30,000.

Do not apply this example automatically. Representation rules, the generation at which division begins, survival periods, adoption, disclaimers, and spouse shares vary. Have counsel identify the governing statute and facts before treating a calculation as a distribution proposal.

## Package uncertainty explicitly  `🏷️ INTERNAL QUALITY-CONTROL POLICY.`

Give every relationship an evidence status and every exhibit a stable identifier. Preserve contrary evidence rather than deleting inconvenient records. Distinguish an unsupported relationship from one disproved by reliable evidence.

Deliver a relationship chart, source register, unresolved-issues log, and versioned arithmetic sheet. Counsel or the authorized fiduciary should decide which material belongs in a filing and which additional proof the court requires.

> **WARNING — Research confidence is not legal entitlement**
> 
> A plausible family tree does not establish a payable claim.
> - Flag missing vital records.
> - Reconcile namesakes and date conflicts.
> - Obtain review of the applicable succession rule.
