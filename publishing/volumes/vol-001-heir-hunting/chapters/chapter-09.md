# Chapter 09: Ethical Outreach, Contracting & Jurisdictional Restrictions

*Separate probate assignments, unclaimed-property recovery, licensing, and legal representation.*

## Executive Summary

Use truthful, low-pressure outreach and a jurisdiction-specific contract review. Probate interests and state-held unclaimed property are different regimes; a fee term acceptable in one is not automatically lawful in another.

## Map the asset and jurisdiction first  `🏷️ ISSUE-SPOTTING REFERENCES, NOT A CURRENT-LAW FEE SCHEDULE OR LEGAL OPINION.`

California Probate Code § 11604.5 addresses specified agreements involving estate interests. Florida Statutes § 717.135 and Texas Property Code § 74.507 concern unclaimed-property recovery arrangements. New York SCPA § 2225 concerns the determination of distributees in its statutory setting, not a universal finder-fee authorization.

Have local counsel confirm statutory scope, current text, amendments, waiting periods, fee limitations, contract formalities, and any court review before offering services. Record the effective date and asset category. Do not copy a fee cap or contract across probate and unclaimed-property matters.

| Jurisdiction | Reference | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| California | Probate Code § 11604.5 | Does this estate-interest agreement fall within the provision? |

| Texas | Property Code § 74.507 | Which timing, compensation, and agreement restrictions apply? |
| New York | SCPA § 2225 | What distributee-determination procedures apply in this proceeding? |

## Make the first contact verifiable  `🏷️ ETHICAL OPERATING POLICY; CHANNEL-SPECIFIC LAWS REQUIRE SEPARATE REVIEW.`

Identify your business and explain that you conduct private genealogical research. Say a possible relationship is under investigation rather than promising money. Offer independently verifiable contact details and explain that recipients may seek independent advice or pursue available direct claim routes.

Do not request payment, banking credentials, or sensitive identity documents in an unsolicited first message. Check applicable calling, texting, email, and privacy requirements before choosing a channel. Record consent where required, contact preferences, and opt-outs without pressuring grieving families.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Approve the message
   - Remove guarantees, artificial urgency, and implications of government affiliation.
2. Offer verification
   - Provide public business details and a way to verify the underlying proceeding independently.
3. Document the response
   - Record permission, questions, and suppression requests before any follow-up.

## Keep licensing and legal practice separate  `🏷️ JURISDICTION-DEPENDENT LEGAL BOUNDARY; OBTAIN QUALIFIED ADVICE.`

Paid locating and investigative work may trigger private-investigator licensing, registration, bonding, or exemptions that vary by location and activity. Check both where work occurs and where clients or subjects are located; a genealogy label does not itself establish an exemption.

Researchers may organize evidence but should not give individualized legal advice, select legal remedies for clients, or represent others in court without authorization. Route legal interpretation, contested heirship, and client-specific contract advice to licensed counsel. Clearly state services, compensation, expenses, cancellation terms, and complaint routes in reviewed agreements.

> **WARNING — Stop at the UPL boundary**
> 
> Unauthorized practice of law and PI licensing are distinct checks; satisfying one does not satisfy the other.
> - Obtain a written licensing assessment.
> - Use counsel-approved agreements for the exact asset regime.
> - Escalate disputed relationships and legal advice requests.
