# Chapter 06: Finding Your First Qualified Opportunities

*A repeatable official-source workflow beats an indiscriminate lead list.*

> THE BIG MONEY PLAYBOOKS · THE FUCK YOU MONEY CLUB

## Executive Summary

Choose one claim lane, screen a small batch of official records and advance only leads with plausible entitlement, net value and a feasible evidence path. Keep discovery separate from permission to contact, contract or file. The output is a qualified research queue, not a list of guaranteed recoveries.

## Choose sources that match the lane  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

For probate, begin with accessible court dockets and official public-administrator information in the selected jurisdiction. For custodial property, use the state's official search and claim instructions. For sale surplus, identify the responsible court, trustee or county and its specific official records.

A listing's existence does not establish that the case is open, the money remains available or your proposed client is eligible. Respect access terms, record restrictions and privacy rules. Do not bypass access controls or use a commercial lead vendor's eligibility claim as a substitute for official confirmation.

| Source lane | Capture | Verify next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Probate docket | Case number, decedent and fiduciary | Current status and governing instruments |
| State property record | Property identifier and custodian | Owner identity and successor requirements |
| Sale-surplus record | Sale identifier and holding authority | Priority, deadlines and remaining balance |

## Run four qualification gates  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

First verify status and current availability. Second identify a plausible claimant and the authority they would need. Third estimate net distributable value after known claims, costs and uncertainty. Fourth assess whether the necessary records can be obtained within the pilot budget.

For a suggested 20-record batch, time-box preliminary screening and escalate only documented survivors. The separate 100-record funnel is an invented teaching example, not a conversion target. Track observed outcomes in your own batch before making acquisition or staffing decisions.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Save the official source URL, retrieval date and identifier.
2. Confirm case status, availability and deadlines.
3. Document the claimant hypothesis and missing authority.
4. Estimate net value with unresolved claims explicitly flagged.
5. Approve a bounded evidence search or close the lead.

## Approach qualified people without manufacturing urgency  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a clear introduction identifying your business, the general nature of the matter and an independent way to verify your identity. Do not expose sensitive family details to an unverified recipient or demand identity documents through an insecure channel. A plausible match is not permission to reveal someone else's personal records.

Before presenting an agreement, explain the scope, fee basis, available direct-claim route and limitations. Check contact, privacy and consumer-protection rules for the channel you use. Declined assistance is a valid outcome; log it and respect any request not to be contacted again.

> **NOTE — A qualified opportunity is not a signed client**
> 
> Keep lead qualification, identity verification, informed consent and lawful representation as separate gates.
