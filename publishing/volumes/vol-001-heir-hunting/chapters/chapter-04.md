# Chapter 04: Realistic Startup Requirements

*A bounded research pilot, professional review and enough cash to wait.*

> THE BIG MONEY PLAYBOOKS · THE FUCK YOU MONEY CLUB

## Executive Summary

The essential startup investment is a reliable evidence and compliance process. Begin in one jurisdiction with a defined research budget, secure records, reviewed contracts and a reserve. Do not confuse inexpensive access to records with low business risk.

## Buy capacity, not a stack of subscriptions  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

An illustrative $5,000 pilot allocates $1,500 to setup and review, $600 to tools, $900 to pilot case costs and $2,000 to reserve. Actual legal, insurance and document costs vary substantially; obtain quotes before relying on these amounts.

Purchase only the sources your initial cases require. Free official indexes may identify a lead, while certified records and specialist work can determine whether the relationship chain is usable. Budget for the evidence rather than collecting software subscriptions.

## Build defensible operating controls  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use a case register, source log, budget tracker and access-controlled document store. Capture record provenance and maintain a clear distinction between verified identities, possible matches and disproved hypotheses.

Collect only the personal information needed for the claim and use secure transfer channels. Confirm licensing, registration, insurance and unauthorized-practice boundaries with qualified local advisers before offering services or accepting sensitive client records.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Define the lane and permitted services.
2. Obtain local professional review and actual quotes.
3. Create case, consent and evidence templates.
4. Set retention, access and deletion policies.

## Validate demand before committing overhead  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A small pilot tests whether official leads can be screened, evidence can be obtained and eligible people value assistance at a lawful price. Set a fixed amount of time and cash you can afford to lose; an unresolved pilot is not a reason to keep spending indefinitely.

Avoid commitments that depend on rapid distributions. A home-based researcher can still face extended collections and failed files. Continue only when the pilot shows a repeatable process and economics that survive realistic unsuccessful cases.

> **NOTE — Stop rule**
> 
> Pause when the approved research budget is exhausted, a legal gate fails or the evidence no longer supports a plausible claim.
