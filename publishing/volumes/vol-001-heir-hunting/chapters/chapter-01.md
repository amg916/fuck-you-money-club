# Chapter 01: What Exactly Is Heir Hunting?

*Three adjacent businesses with different claimants, rules and evidence.*

## Executive Summary

Heir hunting is paid identity and relationship research used to locate people who may have inheritance rights. It is not a universal license to recover every kind of abandoned money. Start by identifying the legal lane and the decision-maker.

## Probate research follows the estate  `🏷️ STATUTORY_FACT`

A probate researcher locates potential heirs and documents relationships. Intestacy statutes govern succession where applicable; a will, trust, spousal rights and other facts can change who receives property.

The court or authorized fiduciary determines the administration path. Researchers should describe evidence and uncertainty, not promise a share or represent that discovering a relative proves entitlement.

| Lane | Typical decision-maker | Central evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Probate heir research | Court / estate fiduciary | Vital records, instruments and kinship chain |
| State-held unclaimed property | State custodian | Owner identity and statutory successor authority |
| Foreclosure or tax surplus | Court / trustee / county under local law | Title history, liens, sale record and priority |

## Custodial property is a different workflow  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

State-held accounts, checks and other property are claimed through the relevant custodian. A deceased owner's claimant may need estate authority, but that does not turn every custodial claim into an open probate lead.

Owners can generally use official state claim channels without hiring a finder. Disclose that option, check finder-agreement restrictions and never imply affiliation with the government.

> **NOTE — Name the lane**
> 
> Write the asset holder, claim type and governing jurisdiction at the top of every intake record.

## Sale surplus follows its own priority rules  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Foreclosure and tax-sale surplus concerns proceeds remaining after legally specified payments. The claimant may be an owner, successor or lienholder, and the priority analysis differs from identifying an intestate heir.

Before outreach, identify the specific sale process and current filing procedure. A large posted surplus can produce no payable amount for the person you found once senior claims and eligibility are considered.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Record the official case or property identifier.
2. Classify the asset and the authority handling it.
3. List unresolved entitlement and priority questions.
4. Refer legal questions before drafting an offer.
