# Chapter 12: The First 30 Days: Step-by-Step Implementation Plan

*Build one reviewed pilot workflow with explicit stop and continuation criteria.*

> THE BIG MONEY PLAYBOOKS · THE FUCK YOU MONEY CLUB

## Executive Summary

The first month should produce one traceable operating experiment, not a promise of quick collections. Choose a single jurisdiction and recovery lane, establish the lawful scope of work, and protect the evidence before sourcing leads. Screen twenty official records through four explicit tests, then develop the strongest survivors into documented kinship packets with independent review. Finish with verified outreach or a recorded dry-run, an honest cost ledger, and a written continuation decision. Progress means learning which cases can be pursued accurately and economically, including learning when to close a weak lead.

## Days 1–7: select the lane and establish the workspace  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

On Day 1 choose one state and one legal lane: a defined probate research service or a specific custodial unclaimed-property recovery process. Write the selection on the pilot cover sheet, including the agency or court, target record series, proposed payer, and intended service. Do not mix tax-sale proceeds, probate distributions, and state-held property merely because all involve missing owners. On Day 2 retrieve the governing statutes, official forms, fee schedules, and agency instructions, recording their URLs and retrieval dates. On Day 3 give local counsel the actual proposed tasks and sample communications. Ask who may investigate, contact potential heirs, negotiate compensation, prepare filings, and represent a claimant; a generic business description will not resolve those questions.

On Day 4 document counsel's conclusions on private-investigator licensing, unauthorized practice of law, fee limits, contract timing, and any applicable exemptions. California Business and Professions Code section 6125 restricts practicing law in California to active State Bar licensees unless otherwise provided by law; factual genealogy does not authorize legal representation. Texas Property Code section 74.507 addresses agreements to assist recovery of property reported under that chapter, including timing and compensation restrictions that must be read against the specific facts. Neither citation supplies a national rule for every recovery lane. Record the current rule, its scope, and counsel's interpretation without importing a fee percentage from another state. If required authority is unresolved, continue source research only within the permitted scope and use a simulated outreach exercise.

On Days 5 and 6 configure the encrypted case vault, individual accounts, multifactor authentication, role-specific folders, and a backup recovery test. Create a case identifier format and separate source images, research notes, legal queries, correspondence, and financial records. Use a fictional case to test upload, redaction, restricted sharing, and restoration; do not upload live identity documents simply to discover whether access controls work. On Day 7 freeze the pilot budget and weekly hours, nominate an independent reviewer, and reserve counsel time for specific questions. Define what stops further spending: unavailable operating authority, exhausted cash budget, inaccessible evidence, or a critical unresolved identity conflict. The first week's deliverable is a usable work system with named owners, not a decorative business plan.

## Days 8–14: screen twenty records with four explicit tests  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

On Days 8 and 9 pull twenty official probate or custodial records belonging to the selected lane. For probate, capture the docket number, decedent, filing court, relevant orders, personal representative if listed, and current procedural posture. For custodial property, capture the official property identifier, holder information, listed owner, amount if disclosed, and agency claim instructions. Deduplicate repeated entries before counting the sample, and record records that cannot be accessed rather than inventing missing amounts. Keep a source image or durable reference with retrieval date and distinguish the original entry from your transcription. A public listing is a lead, not proof that funds remain available or that a person with a similar name is entitled to them.

On Days 10 and 11 apply the four tests consistently. Gate 1 asks whether the asset and current custodian are verified through an official source. Gate 2 asks whether the jurisdiction, service scope, deadlines, and compensation route permit the proposed work. Gate 3 asks whether there is a plausible claimant identity or kinship chain that can be proved with accessible evidence. Gate 4 asks whether the likely collectible fee can justify remaining costs under conservative assumptions. Mark each pass, hold, or fail with a source reference and a reason. An unknown amount does not pass the economics test by default; it remains a hold until an approved bounded inquiry supplies enough information for a decision.

On Days 12 and 13 rank only the survivors, using estimated remaining effort and evidence quality rather than the largest advertised asset. A hypothetical ten-thousand-dollar potential fee with a twenty-percent collection probability has a two-thousand-dollar probability-weighted receipt before cost and timing adjustments; spending three thousand more cannot be justified by its headline value alone. These estimates are assumptions to test, not measured probabilities from a twenty-record sample. On Day 14 reconcile all twenty entries: survivors plus holds plus closed leads must total twenty. Use dead-lead reason codes such as duplicate, already paid, wrong identity, unavailable evidence, barred route, or uneconomic remainder. Retain a minimal audit record of closure so the same failed lead is not purchased again next month.

**Four-Gate pilot screening matrix**

| Test | Evidence | Hold or fail trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1: Asset | Current official record and custodian | Stale, duplicate, or already disbursed entry |
| 2: Route | Applicable law and documented service authority | Unresolved licensing, deadline, or fee restriction |
| 3: Proof | Identity anchors and attainable relationship records | Name-only match or inaccessible critical link |
| 4: Economics | Collectible-fee assumptions and remaining-cost budget | Downside exceeds approved experiment budget |

## Days 15–21: build a proof packet that another person can challenge  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

On Day 15 select the strongest one or two survivors that fit available research capacity, not every lead that passed screening. Build a relationship question for each claimed link: whether the named child is the child of the relevant parent, whether two records describe the same individual, or whether a branch contains additional descendants. On Days 16 and 17 collect and correlate birth, marriage, death, probate, census, and other suitable records with full citations. Record access restrictions and use authorized channels for restricted vital records. A census household can suggest a relationship but may not establish the legal link needed for distribution. Preserve spelling variants, dates, residences, informants, and contradictions so the final conclusion is reproducible rather than dependent on the researcher's memory.

On Day 18 assemble a Genealogical Proof Standard, or GPS, packet: reasonably exhaustive research appropriate to the question, complete source citations, analysis and correlation, resolution of conflicting evidence, and a reasoned written conclusion. GPS is a genealogical method, not a substitute for the court's evidentiary rules or counsel's determination of heirship. Include negative search results with jurisdiction, date range, index searched, and search terms; absence from one index does not establish that a person never existed. Separate source quality from relationship confidence, and explain why a later informant's statement does or does not outweigh a contemporaneous record. Escalate unresolved conflicts instead of smoothing them out in a polished family tree.

On Days 19 and 20 prepare a provisional per-stirpes share table under counsel's identified governing rule. For a purely illustrative one-hundred-twenty-thousand-dollar distributable pool, assume three eligible child branches, one surviving child in each of two branches, and two eligible grandchildren representing the deceased child in the third. Under that assumed branch allocation, the surviving children receive forty thousand each and the grandchildren twenty thousand each. This illustration assumes away a spouse, debts, priority claims, and other complications; actual statutes may select another representation method. State every assumption, include all branches, and confirm the fractions sum to one. On Day 21 obtain independent QC sign-off covering identity links, citations, conflict resolution, and arithmetic, with counsel addressing legal distribution questions separately.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Frame the proof
   - Write one question per relationship link.
   - Assign source IDs and record both supporting and conflicting evidence.
2. Reconcile shares
   - Apply the identified distribution rule to explicit assumptions.
   - Show branch fractions, distributable pool, unresolved persons, and arithmetic totals.
3. Review independently
   - Obtain a dated reviewer disposition.
   - Close or explicitly retain every objection; do not call an author review independent.

> **OPERATOR GATE — No outreach from an unresolved proof packet**
> 
> An attractive potential fee cannot cure a broken identity link.
> - Keep unresolved relationships marked as hypotheses.
> - Record reviewer identity, packet version, and corrections.
> - Use a dry-run if lawful outreach or proof remains unresolved.

## Days 22–30: test contact, reconcile downside, and decide  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

On Days 22 and 23 prepare outreach only for reviewed cases within the documented permitted scope. Verify the contact route against appropriate records and minimize sensitive details in the initial message. Identify the firm accurately, avoid claiming government affiliation, and explain the service without promising an award or implying that a fee is required to contact the public custodian directly. Have counsel review the proposed agreement for the selected lane, including compensation basis, applicable limits, timing, scope, cancellation provisions where required, and who provides legal services. Do not send a recipient another family's identity documents. If a condition remains unresolved, conduct a dry-run using fictional contact details and record the exact reason live contact did not occur.

On Days 24 through 26 execute the permitted outreach or simulation and log the date, channel, approved message version, response, and next action. Respect a request to end contact and keep factual status updates separate from distribution advice. On Days 27 and 28 reconcile the time ledger, supplier invoices, record fees, counsel costs, and any remaining commitments. Model zero collections explicitly: if cash outlay is twelve hundred dollars and unpaid owner work is forty hours valued at fifty dollars, the pilot has twelve hundred cash exposure and thirty-two hundred economic cost before other omitted expenses. A signed contingent contract does not reduce that cash downside. Compare actual spend with the budget authorized on Day 7 and explain each variance.

On Day 29 prepare a decision memo that reports all twenty lead outcomes, reviewed packets, unresolved legal or evidence issues, actual hours, cash spend, and the next experiment's capped cost. On Day 30 choose Continue only when at least one reviewed pilot workflow is usable, the operating route is established, and the next bounded cycle fits available cash and capacity. Choose Revise when a specific repairable issue has an owner, deadline, and affordable test; identify whether the next cycle pauses outreach or changes sourcing. Choose Stop when the route is unavailable, proof cannot be obtained economically, or the loss budget is exhausted. No collections in thirty days alone do not establish failure, but absence of collections must remain visible in the cash plan rather than being replaced by optimistic pipeline value.
