# Chapter 12: The First 30 Days: Step-by-Step Implementation Plan

*Build one reviewed pilot workflow with explicit stop and continuation criteria.*

> THE BIG MONEY PLAYBOOKS · THE FUCK YOU MONEY CLUB

## Executive Summary

Use the first month to establish legal boundaries, source access, evidence quality, and cash discipline. A completed pilot may end in a documented no-go decision; a distribution or paid engagement is not a thirty-day deliverable.

## Days 1–7: establish lawful operating boundaries  `🏷️ SUGGESTED IMPLEMENTATION SCHEDULE; EXTERNAL APPROVALS MAY TAKE LONGER.`

Choose one jurisdiction and distinguish probate research from state-held unclaimed-property recovery. Prepare a concise scope for local counsel covering PI licensing, unauthorized-practice boundaries, outreach channels, contracts, privacy, and fees. Do not launch regulated work while these questions remain unresolved.

Inventory approved sources, establish a restricted case store, and create a source register and retention policy. Use synthetic records to test intake and evidence capture if access or licensing is not yet cleared. Assign owners and dates to unresolved decisions instead of assuming silence means approval.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Days 1–2: scope
   - Record jurisdiction, asset regime, intended services, budget, and counsel-review questions.
2. Days 3–4: controls
   - Configure access, backup, retention, incident escalation, and source authorization records.
3. Days 5–7: readiness review
   - Approve only the activities whose legal and operational prerequisites are satisfied.

## Days 8–21: run an evidence-first pilot  `🏷️ OPERATIONAL PLAN, CONDITIONAL ON PERMISSIONS AND REVIEW CAPACITY.`

During days 8–14, collect permitted pilot records, build the relationship chart, and test page-linked transcription. Track search time and record costs. Keep unresolved identity questions in a hold queue rather than optimizing for a complete-looking tree.

During days 15–21, obtain independent proof review and a jurisdiction-specific legal review of proposed outreach and terms. If cleared, conduct limited truthful outreach with appropriate channel compliance; otherwise rehearse internally. Record the outcome even when no contact or contract is appropriate.

| Window | Deliverable | Advance only if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 8–10 | Source register and initial chart | Access is permitted and claims are source-linked |
| Days 11–14 | Conflict log and cost baseline | Missing evidence is visible and costs are recorded |
| Days 15–18 | Independent review packet | Reviewer competence and independence are adequate |
| Days 19–21 | Approved outreach or internal rehearsal | Licensing, channel rules, and wording are cleared |

## Days 22–30: decide whether to continue  `🏷️ MANAGEMENT DECISION FRAMEWORK; NO EARNINGS OR SETTLEMENT GUARANTEE.`

Reconcile actual hours, records expense, review cost, defects, and unresolved legal questions. Compare the revised economic model with a zero-collection scenario. Estimate working-capital needs for delays without assuming an estate will settle on your calendar.

At day 30, choose continue, revise, or stop. Continue only with an approved scope, a reviewable evidence packet, lawful outreach controls, and an affordable downside. Carry unanswered questions into the next plan with named owners; do not present pilot projections as realized income.

> **DECISION — The day-30 gate**
> 
> A documented stop can be the correct result of a well-run pilot.
> - Continue when prerequisites and budget are satisfied.
> - Revise when a remediable control or evidence gap remains.
> - Stop when legality, evidence, or affordable economics cannot be established.
