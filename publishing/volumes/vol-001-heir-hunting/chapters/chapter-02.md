# Chapter 02: Who Pays, Why They Pay & Where Estate Capital Sits

*Find the payer, the asset holder and the authority to act before discussing price.*

## Executive Summary

People pay for documented research, coordination and reduced administrative burden, not for secret access to public funds. Payment depends on a lawful engagement and its actual terms. A claimant, fiduciary and asset custodian are different roles.

## Who can authorize and who can pay  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A potential heir might retain a researcher; a fiduciary might commission genealogy within their authority. Those arrangements have different approval, expense and conflict considerations, so the client and payer must be explicit.

Do not charge an estate merely because a relative signed a contract. Determine who has authority, whether approval is required and whether a fee is payable from a claimant's recovery or under a separate professional engagement.

| Role | Typical responsibility | Do not assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claimant | Provide identity and authorize permitted assistance | Authority over the whole estate |
| Fiduciary | Administer within legal authority | Unlimited power to approve expenses |
| Custodian | Evaluate claims and release property | Liability for your private fee |

## Where capital actually sits  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Estate assets may sit in bank accounts, real property or investments controlled through administration. State unclaimed property is held by a statutory custodian; sale surplus is held under a separate distribution process.

An inventory is a snapshot, not a fee base. Verify liquidity, ownership, restrictions and whether the listed amount is still available. Treat an old docket entry or commercial lead list as a research pointer until the official record confirms it.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Identify the current asset holder.
2. Check the record's date and status.
3. Separate gross value from likely distributable value.
4. Document who can sign and who would pay.

## Value without pressure  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A credible offer describes the records to be obtained, the work excluded, the applicable fee arrangement and the client's alternatives. Give people time to verify your identity through independently located contact details.
