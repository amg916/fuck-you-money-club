## Executive Summary

Build a small stack around official dockets, lawful vital-record access, historical collections, and a restricted case register. Availability online does not make a record accurate, reusable, or unrestricted.

## Use a source hierarchy  `🏷️ RESEARCH GUIDANCE; AVAILABILITY AND ACCESS RULES VARY.`

Begin with the court clerk's official docket and the responsible vital-record agency. Use county land records and historical census collections to corroborate identity and geography. Certified copies may be required even when an online image is available.

Treat obituaries, cemetery listings, user-contributed family trees, and search-engine snippets as leads. Corroborate material statements and note whether the source creator had firsthand knowledge. Do not present a commercial directory match as a verified heir.

| Resource | Best use | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official probate docket | Case posture and fiduciary details | Confirm current docket and access terms |
| Vital-record agency | Birth, marriage, and death links | Check eligibility and certification requirements |
| Archives and census collections | Historical household context | Preserve image and collection citation |
| Obituaries and family trees | Generate hypotheses | Corroborate independently |

## Keep a reproducible research register  `🏷️ OPERATIONAL RECOMMENDATION.`

Use a case register with stable case, person, source, and relationship identifiers. Store retrieval dates, exact collection references, search variants, and access restrictions. A saved URL alone is insufficient when a portal uses expiring links.

Keep originals separate from working transcriptions and redacted exports. Use an encrypted document store with access logging and tested backups. Record a retention decision for each category instead of keeping sensitive records indefinitely.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Capture
   - Save permitted source material with provenance and retrieval date.
2. Link
   - Associate the source with specific claims and competing interpretations.
3. Review access
   - Remove obsolete permissions and verify backup restoration on a recurring schedule.

## Reject shortcuts with hidden legal costs  `🏷️ COMPLIANCE SCREENING; LEGAL ADVICE REQUIRED FOR SPECIFIC ACCESS QUESTIONS.`

Do not bypass logins, CAPTCHAs, sealed-record restrictions, or portal limits. Prefer official exports or licensed access. Public availability does not authorize impersonation, pretexting, or unrestricted redistribution of living people's information.

Screen specialty databases for permissible-purpose requirements and contract restrictions before purchase. Motor-vehicle information is subject to specific federal restrictions; do not assume heir research is a qualifying purpose. Use a documented approval gate for restricted datasets.

> **CAUTION — A subscription is not universal authorization**
> 
> Evaluate the intended use, data source, and disclosure rules before importing records.
> - Record license terms.
> - Exclude unnecessary sensitive identifiers.
> - Escalate restricted-source requests for legal review.
