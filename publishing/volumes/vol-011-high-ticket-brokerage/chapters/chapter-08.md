## 1. Resolve the seller, asset, and authority before buying another lead  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start every exclusive mandate with an identity spine, not a contact spreadsheet. Assign a deal identifier, record the seller's exact legal name and formation jurisdiction, then attach the state entity number, asset identifier, signing authority, and expected closing venue. Separate registered agent, officer, beneficial owner, and authorized seller; a registry hit establishes neither ownership of the offered asset nor permission to sell it. Obtain the engagement letter, organizational documents, and seller authorization directly, then compare them with official records. For equipment, preserve serial numbers and invoice chains; for aircraft, use the registration and airframe identifiers; for property, use parcel and recorded instrument references. For business sales, distinguish an asset purchase from a securities transaction before selecting the broker's operating structure. Exchange Act registration and the conditional M&A broker exemption in 15 U.S.C. 78o(b)(13) require a transaction-specific assessment; the exemption does not erase state requirements or permit custody of client funds. Place every conflict into an exception queue with a named resolver. The first deliverable is a defensible identity packet that an escrow officer, buyer, and transaction adviser can independently follow without relying on the salesperson's memory.

Treat each registry as an instrument with a narrow measurement range. A Secretary of State record supports entity existence and filing status, while a UCC search supports a particular debtor-name and jurisdiction inquiry. Neither substitutes for a lien release, title opinion, or ownership representation. Apply the adopted state versions of UCC Sections 9-301, 9-307, 9-503, and 9-506 when deciding where and under which name to search; include former names and merger history when the facts require them. County recorder indexes can lag document submission, and asset registries can lag a recent transfer. Request certified searches or specialist title work when closing exposure justifies the expense. PACER supplies federal court records, not a complete national judgment or litigation search; add the relevant state courts and bankruptcy review. OFAC screening likewise requires identity resolution, not a binary decision from a similar surname. Record search parameters, retrieval time, source update date when exposed, and coverage limits. Recheck material facts before accepting a mandate and again before releasing closing instructions. If the record conflicts with the seller's story, stop distributing that assertion rather than silently overwriting the evidence with a more convenient commercial database entry.

Use the directory below as a research routing map, not a promise that every source has a supported API or immediate publication. Its refresh entries distinguish source publication from the desk's polling policy. Assign one owner to maintain endpoint documentation, permitted use, account requirements, and current prices quarterly. Public access does not imply permission for unlimited extraction, commercial redistribution, or unrestricted personal-data use. Where only a browser portal exists, use authorized manual retrieval or an approved records provider rather than defeating access controls. Download a reusable official bulk file once when offered, retain its publication date, and update only the changed records. Conditional requests and shared caching reduce load and prevent several contractors from paying for the same document. For an illustrative 40-mandate research queue, a $15 initial records allowance per mandate creates a $600 procurement envelope, not a claim about registry pricing. Escalate certified searches against specific closing exposure instead of purchasing them indiscriminately. When a source is unavailable, label the fact as awaiting evidence, identify the alternate official source, and assign a deadline. A stale record may support historical analysis; it must not masquerade as current clearance for a high-value transfer.

## 2. Buy enrichment only after the mandate survives primary-source checks  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Build the enrichment waterfall around an identified company and a documented purpose. Begin with the entity spine and company website, then add commercial company identifiers, relevant executives, and permitted business contact channels. OpenCorporates can improve cross-jurisdiction discovery, D&B Direct+ can supply licensed business identifiers and attributes, and Crunchbase can suggest ownership or funding events. None should silently replace a primary document. For each attribute, store the provider, retrieval date, matching inputs, confidence, and redistribution permission. A matching name is weaker than agreement across jurisdiction, address, domain, and registration number. Distinguish a buyer's announced fund size from available acquisition capital, and distinguish a portfolio company's employee count from the parent organization's purchasing authority. Contact enrichment follows qualification rather than preceding it. People Data Labs and Hunter can support permitted discovery and verification under their current terms, but a deliverable address does not establish permission, relevance, or buyer interest. Route ambiguous records to a human researcher instead of spraying outreach across possible identities. Maintain suppression records and apply channel-specific rules, including CAN-SPAM for commercial email and applicable restrictions on automated calls or texts. The desired output is a small, explainable buyer shortlist, not a maximal collection of personal records.

Price the waterfall by accepted evidence, not nominal API credits. Consider an illustrative batch of 200 company candidates: official checks eliminate 80, commercial company matching resolves 90 of the remaining 120, and human review accepts 60 for outreach research. At assumed internal budgeting rates of $0.20 for each of 120 company lookups and $0.10 for each of 60 contact verifications, variable API spend is $30. These are planning assumptions, not vendor quotations, and exclude subscriptions, minimum commitments, registry fees, and researcher time. If 12 recipients become qualified buyer conversations, that narrow variable cost is $2.50 per conversation; the fully loaded acquisition cost remains higher. Log every lookup against a deal or research campaign so an unused annual subscription cannot disappear inside overhead. Purchase a second provider only when its incremental matches justify the incremental expense and permitted usage. When two sources disagree, preserve both claims and seek stronger evidence rather than averaging identities. Cache only for the contractually allowed period, respect deletion obligations, and remove unnecessary personal attributes. The cheapest reliable fallback is often a targeted call through the company's published switchboard, not a third bulk-data contract.

The mapped play aged_high_karma_social_account_brokerage needs an explicit asset-rights test before anyone prices audience access. Account age, karma, followers, and verification badges are observations, not transferable property rights or proof of authentic influence. Reddit, LinkedIn, X, and TikTok impose different account, identity, advertising, and transfer restrictions; inspect the current rules for the exact proposed transaction. This desk excludes fabricated personas, manufactured reputation, proxy-based filter evasion, and covert coordinated promotion. It does not operationalize account fleets or promise search placement or model citations. Instead, evaluate permitted creator-business acquisitions, licensed content, disclosed sponsorships, or platform-supported transfers with written authority. An illustrative $80,000 permitted business acquisition at a 10% retained commission produces $8,000 broker revenue, not $80,000. If expected diligence costs are $2,000, the remaining $6,000 is transaction contribution before shared overhead and other costs, not the chapter's annual owner profit. Require seller-authorized analytics, audience-quality sampling, content-rights schedules, and an escrow condition covering the supported transfer method. If platform permission or identity continuity cannot be established, exclude the account from the sale and value independently transferable assets instead. If those assets cannot support the economics, decline the mandate rather than substituting a concealment technique.

## 3. Build an auditable evidence pipeline instead of a fragile scraping stack  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

A small desk can operate a durable pipeline with Python's urllib.request, json, csv, sqlite3, hashlib, and logging modules, plus an authorized CRM or spreadsheet for workflow visibility. Keep raw evidence separate from normalized assertions. A source record should contain a source identifier, request parameters with secrets removed, retrieval timestamp in UTC, response status, publication date when available, content hash, and permitted retention period. An assertion should link an entity or asset to a field, value, effective date, evidence reference, and reviewer. A SHA-256 hash helps detect later file changes; it does not prove that the original statement was true. Use stable keys and unique constraints to prevent repeated imports from creating duplicate buyers or duplicated milestone tasks. Do not place API keys, full bank details, or unnecessary identity documents in logs. Protect stored evidence with account-level access controls and encrypted storage, and keep client-specific workspaces separate. Test backup restoration, not merely backup creation. For an illustrative 300 evidence packets averaging 2 MB, raw storage is about 600 MB before versions and backups; document quality, permission management, and reviewer time dominate the economics, not disk capacity.

Design connectors to fail visibly and narrowly. Set connection and read timeouts, bound response sizes, validate content types, and reject schema changes that would silently move an address into an ownership field. Use per-provider concurrency limits and exponential backoff with jitter; honor Retry-After and never rotate identities to defeat quotas. Quarantine authentication failures until the account owner resolves them. A daily scheduled run is a desk policy, not evidence that the underlying registry updates daily. Preserve the last successful retrieval and display its age beside every material assertion. Build contract tests from permitted fixtures covering empty results, multiple matches, revoked credentials, expired pagination tokens, and truncated downloads. Use sqlite3 transactions so a failed import does not leave half a buyer cohort marked as verified. Track fetched records, accepted matches, exceptions, cost units, and human review minutes as separate counters. If a provider disappears, route affected cases to the documented manual procedure rather than allowing automated buyer matching to continue on stale attributes. Reconciliation should expose disagreements between sources, not smooth them away into a deceptively confident score. A useful automation result is an accurate exception list with owners and deadlines, even when no record qualifies for unattended acceptance.

Connect evidence to a closing state machine: mandate signed, identity resolved, buyer qualified, diligence opened, definitive documents executed, funding confirmed, escrow conditions satisfied, and commission settled. Each transition needs a responsible person and a supporting artifact. Buyer-seller matching ranks fit against documented criteria; it does not invent capacity. Document checklists must distinguish received, reviewed, accepted, expired, and superseded materials. For an NCNDA, record the covered parties, introduced opportunity, effective period, exceptions, and acknowledged delivery; a generic template cannot establish every commission claim. Keep underlying purchase money outside the desk's ordinary operating account, using properly authorized escrow and asset-specific handling arrangements. Verify escrow instructions through independently obtained contact details and a second communication channel; an email thread alone cannot authenticate changed bank details. Diagnostic tools test systems, not counterparties' honesty: a valid TLS certificate proves neither escrow authority nor account ownership. Before closing, rerun the material entity, sanctions, lien, and authority checks under the transaction team's required timetable. After settlement, reconcile the escrow statement, commission entitlement, co-broker allocations, and bank receipt. Preserve the evidence needed to explain the retained amount while deleting personal information that no longer has a justified retention purpose.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Create the identity and authority packet
   - Capture legal names, jurisdictions, asset identifiers, seller authority, and signed mandate scope.
   - Assign one deal identifier and record which official sources can corroborate each material assertion before purchasing enrichment.
2. Retrieve and normalize permitted evidence
   - Fetch authorized official records, then enrich only unresolved attributes that affect qualification.
   - Store retrieval dates, source dates, match inputs, usage rights, and evidence references; quarantine conflicts for named human review.
3. Advance milestones through documented review
   - Match buyers against recorded criteria and connect every diligence milestone to an accepted artifact.
   - Keep licensing, transferability, sanctions, lien, and escrow exceptions visible; do not let a CRM status substitute for evidence.
4. Recheck and reconcile the closing
   - Refresh material records, authenticate escrow instructions independently, and reconcile retained commission after settlement.
   - Archive the supporting settlement packet under the retention schedule and record failed-closing causes for the next pipeline review.

## 4. Screen allocation opportunities and reconcile tooling to retained commissions  `🏷️ ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`

The mapped play section_179d_municipal_deduction_allocation_arbitrage concerns documented allocations to eligible designers, not a marketplace for freely transferable deductions. Under 26 U.S.C. 179D, first identify the qualifying building owner, eligible property, designer contribution, and applicable construction and placed-in-service dates. The statute terminates eligibility for property whose construction begins after June 30, 2026; document the applicable construction-start analysis rather than assuming every public retrofit remains eligible. Government ownership and specified tax-exempt ownership require their own eligibility analysis. IRS organization records, public procurement awards, building permits, and owner-authorized Portfolio Manager data help identify candidates, but none establishes a deduction amount. Obtain the design contract, scope of services, allocation authorization, qualifying technical calculations, and certification required under applicable rules. Determine whether prevailing-wage and apprenticeship requirements affect the available amount, and retain the substantiation supporting that determination. Municipal staff may possess useful records without possessing authority to sign the allocation. Confirm the owner's delegation and procurement requirements before proposing compensation. Where a federal contract is implicated, examine the actual solicitation and contract clauses, including applicable contingent-fee restrictions, rather than treating public-sector access as permission to charge a success fee. Every eligibility conclusion belongs to a documented project review.

Use unit math to prevent deduction amounts from leaking into brokerage revenue. An illustrative 100,000-square-foot building multiplied by an independently substantiated hypothetical $2.00-per-square-foot applicable amount produces a $200,000 deduction before other applicable limitations. That assumed rate is not a published rate for a particular tax year, and the deduction is neither a $200,000 cash payment nor operator revenue. Actual tax benefit depends on the recipient's tax position and applicable limitations. A separately contracted $6,000 fixed documentation fee would compensate defined services, subject to eligibility, procurement, and professional-scope requirements. It is outside this volume's retained-transaction-commission scorecard and must not be inserted into the canonical cases. If a proposed contingent arrangement is impermissible or the allocation is doubtful, pause the allocation, obtain independent tax review, and offer fixed-fee documentation support only for eligible projects. Do not cure an ineligible project by renaming the fee. Record who prepares the calculation, who independently certifies the technical work, who authorizes allocation, and who claims the deduction. If those responsibilities collapse into one unsupported broker assertion, the opportunity has failed diligence regardless of the potential headline deduction or enthusiasm of the lead source.

Keep the canonical operating model fixed while using local assumptions to test throughput. Conservative retained commissions are $100,000, with $75,000 total costs and $25,000 owner profit at 25%, supported by 1 FT + 3 C. Base retained commissions are $550,000, with $343,750 total costs and $206,250 owner profit at 37.5%, also with 1 FT + 3 C. Scaled retained commissions are $1,000,000, with $500,000 total costs and $500,000 owner profit at 50%, supported by 2 FT + 4 C. Illustrative mixes of four $25,000 retained commissions, eleven $50,000 commissions, and twenty $50,000 commissions reproduce those revenue totals without counting asset consideration. A Base desk that provisionally budgets $12,000 annually for data and tooling has allocated part of its $343,750 cost envelope, not created an additional profit deduction outside the model. Track failed-closing expense and commission collection timing separately from lead volume. Startup capital of $15,000–$100,000, monthly operating expenditure of $3,000–$18,000, and a 3–12 month first-deal horizon require cash planning; the monthly range does not describe every annual variable cost. Approve subscriptions against verified research capacity and expected closing contribution, then cancel tools that merely make an unqualified pipeline look larger.
