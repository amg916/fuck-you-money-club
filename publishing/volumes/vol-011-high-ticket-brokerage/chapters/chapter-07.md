## 1. Move Each Mandate from Verified Authority to Earned Commission  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Start with one asset class, one buyer population, and one defensible mandate rather than advertising universal deal access. Assign a permanent mandate identifier before collecting documents, then record the seller entity, authorized signer, beneficial ownership evidence, asset description, jurisdiction, expected consideration, fee basis, and existing intermediary claims. The Base operating unit is a $27,500 retained commission; twenty completed units produce $550,000 annual operator revenue. A $2,750,000 asset transaction charging one percent illustrates that unit, but the asset consideration never enters revenue. If a cooperating broker receives $7,500 from a $35,000 contractual commission, this model records the $27,500 retained portion and documents the settlement allocation separately. Have the accounting reviewer confirm the presentation against the actual contractual role. The owner handles qualification, pricing, negotiation, and closing accountability; three contractors cover research, document coordination, and specialist review. Contractors receive bounded work orders and evidence access, not authority to bind clients or redirect settlement proceeds. Reject an intake that cannot identify who owns the asset or who may appoint the intermediary. A signed introduction agreement cannot repair missing authority, and an attractive asking price cannot substitute for transferable title.

Classify the transaction before circulating a teaser. An asset sale, a securities transfer, and a real-property conveyance can require different registrations, disclosures, and compensation arrangements even when the same company owns everything. For qualifying private-company control transactions, examine 15 U.S.C. 78o(b)(13), including buyer control and active management, eligible-company size, disqualifications, and excluded activities. Its federal M&A broker exemption does not erase state requirements, permit custody of transaction funds, or cover every minority investment introduction. In California, Business and Professions Code section 10131 reaches specified real-estate and business-opportunity brokerage activities; section 10137 restricts payments for licensed acts to unlicensed persons. Record the actual license or exemption analysis for the particular role, not a generic company label. Then execute an exclusive mandate defining scope, term, fee trigger, retained share, named exclusions, reporting duties, termination, and a carefully bounded tail. Use an NCNDA to define protected introductions and permitted disclosures, with dated introduction evidence and clear exceptions for existing relationships. It cannot create licensing authority or ensure collection by itself. Route ownership conflicts, duplicate mandates, and uncertain fee entitlement to specialist review before spending buyer credibility on the opportunity.

Run buyer matching as controlled disclosure, not indiscriminate distribution. Share a redacted teaser first, verify buying authority and plausible funding, execute the required confidentiality documents, and only then release a permissioned diligence package through ShareFile, Box, or an equivalent controlled repository. Record each version, recipient, download permission, and material correction. Require the seller to distinguish documented facts from forecasts, especially adjusted earnings, customer concentration, title restrictions, and infrastructure claims. A letter of intent starts diligence; it does not earn the success commission unless the contract expressly creates a different, separately reviewed entitlement. Select an independent escrow or closing professional appropriate to the asset and jurisdiction. Verify settlement instructions by calling a previously established number, never the number supplied inside a last-minute wire-change message. The broker tracks conditions and fee allocation without receiving buyer principal. At closing, compare the signed settlement statement, contractual earning trigger, retained commission calculation, and escrow disbursement advice. Record earned revenue and a receivable only after the entitlement is established. Clear that receivable only against settled bank evidence, preserving the distinction between an executed transaction, an issued invoice, a promised wire, and cash actually received.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**

1. Create and classify the mandate
   - Assign a canonical source key and verify the transaction category.
   - Capture authority evidence, jurisdiction, retained-fee cents, screening inputs, and the reviewer responsible for role classification.
2. Qualify and contract the opportunity
   - Confirm buyer fit and execute the exclusive mandate before identifiable circulation.
   - Preserve the signed fee trigger, introduction exclusions, agency disclosures, and document-access instructions under the mandate identifier.
3. Coordinate diligence and independent closing
   - Track conditions without taking custody of client principal.
   - Require source-linked diligence findings, authenticated settlement instructions, and a closing statement identifying the retained commission.
4. Recognize, collect, and reconcile
   - Post earned commission separately from settled cash receipt.
   - Match an independently imported bank reference, reject duplicate use, and investigate every remaining receivable or unmatched bank observation.

## 3. Build a Six-Module Ledger with Serialized State Changes and Replay-Safe Posting  `🏷️ OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`

Use the reference below as a small, explicit execution core rather than pretending a CRM is an accounting system. Concatenate the six code cells, in displayed order, into broker_ledger.py and run it with Python 3.9 or later; the final module executes an isolated self-test. The code uses only the standard library and creates its test database inside a temporary directory. Production deployment initializes a local SQLite database once, then gives every worker its own connection. BEGIN IMMEDIATE serializes write transactions before reads that decide state changes; the version check rejects stale commands instead of overwriting intervening work. A unique command key plus a canonical request digest makes identical replays return the original result and rejects altered replays. Unique bank references prevent one settlement from paying two invoices. This concurrency model requires a supported local filesystem, not a shared network drive, and all application writers must use these functions. Database administrators remain trusted. Run schema changes through one controlled migration process, restrict database file permissions, and keep encrypted backups. SQLite locking protects ledger transitions; it does not authenticate an escrow officer or validate a seller's representations without the documented human verification process.

The six modules intentionally separate intake, evidence-based workflow, bank observations, and accounting presentation. Screening points prioritize work but never establish licensing eligibility or asset quality. Intake stores the retained fee in integer cents and refuses a reused source key carrying different facts. Every successful transition appends an immutable event and advances the mandate version within the same transaction. Closing posts a debit to commission receivables and a credit to retained-commission revenue; payment posts cash against that receivable only after an independently imported settled bank observation matches the exact amount. Each journal record contains equal debit and credit amounts by construction, avoiding a temporary half-posted entry. The reconciliation view shows receivables, cash movement, earned commission, expense, bank movement, unmatched observations, and the signed ledger balance. Expense posting uses the same atomic event and unique bank-reference discipline. Evidence strings should identify immutable document versions or content hashes, not mutable shared-folder labels. Their presence proves that a reviewer supplied a reference, not that software independently verified the underlying document. Keep that responsibility assigned to a named reviewer in the surrounding task system and preserve the review history with the referenced evidence packet.

Keep the supported scope narrow enough to test completely. This reference handles fixed retained fees, full settled receipts, and cash-paid operating expenses; it rejects partial receipts and does not implement refunds, taxes, foreign exchange, fee amendments, or accounting reversals. Route those exceptions to a reviewed extension with new journal events rather than editing posted records. The ledger contains operator cash only, never escrow principal. Bank observations must come from a separately authenticated statement import; granting a user access to that importer is a distinct permission from approving a closing. The included test proves concurrent identical closing commands produce one event, altered replays fail, a bank reference cannot fund duplicate expenses, and ledger totals remain balanced. It also checks that failed posting rolls back its event. Lock contention can still produce a timeout; callers retry the exact same command key after bounded backoff, without changing its payload. No function sends outreach or initiates a transfer, so the transaction boundary contains no external side effect. If those capabilities are added, introduce a transactional outbox and recipient-side deduplication; do not claim that a committed database row alone makes an external email or wire execute exactly once.
