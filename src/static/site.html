EPISTEMIC RESEARCH STANDARDS
  • 01 / Experience is not proof.

    Practitioner consensus is labeled as such. Repeated advice is not independently verified evidence or a representative outcome.

  • 02 / Local law comes first.

    Jurisdiction-dependent claims need current local statutes and qualified review. This is educational publishing focused on documented research and jurisdiction-specific sources.

  • 03 / Keep the source trail (150–200+ expert YouTube links per book).

    Every volume enforces a minimum floor of 150–200+ timestamped [HH:MM:SS] expert YouTube deep links across all 12 chapters (minimum 12 per chapter).

  • 04 / Permissions are not optional.

    No third-party video frames, portal screenshots, or long verbatim transcript passages in commercial exports without appropriate written permission. A short excerpt is not automatically lawful.
