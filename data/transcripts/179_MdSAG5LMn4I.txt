Regrid demonstrates self-service acquisition of statewide standardized parcel data at store.regrid.com, covering more than 155 million US parcels. Select a state, such as Ohio with approximately 5.29 million parcels across 88 counties. Inspect the County Coverage Report spreadsheet and Parcel Schema / Data Dictionary. Fields and tier distinctions include APN, owner name, mailing address, situs address, land use, valuation, building footprints and secondary addresses, across Standard and Premium offerings. Add a statewide dataset to the cart, complete checkout under the Data License Agreement and download the full state bundle or individual county files. Available formats include CSV, GeoJSON, Shapefile (SHP), File Geodatabase (GDB), GeoPackage (GPKG) and KML. Support is available at parcels@regrid.com.
