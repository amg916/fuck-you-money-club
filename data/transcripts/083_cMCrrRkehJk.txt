Welcome to this tutorial on how to complete the Table of Heirship Form located on the California State Controller's Unclaimed Property Website. To navigate to the Table of Heirship form from the State Controller's Office website, click on Unclaimed Property. Next, click the link for Claim Filing Instructions and Forms. Lastly, scroll down to the bottom of the page, and click the link for the Table of Heirship form. The Table of Heirship Form is used for Heir claims, where the owner passed intestate, with no Will, Trust, or other Estate Plan, and Probate was not required. This form provides detail about the family of the deceased owner, to determine how any funds that belonged to the owner should be distributed. At the top of the form, above Section 1, list the name of the deceased owner. Next, list the date of their passing. Just below that, list the property ID number you are claiming. If there are multiple Property ID Numbers, list one of the Property ID Numbers. These can be found on your claim form, or on the property details screen on the Unclaimed Property website. In section 1, list the name of the spouse of the deceased owner, or none, if they were never married. If the deceased owner had more than one spouse, list the names in chronological order, and be sure to include the year the marriage occurred. If the owner was divorced at the time of death, write *divorced* next to the spouse's name. Next, list the birthdate and date of death for the spouse if known. In section 2, list all of the deceased owner's children, whether they are living or deceased. If the owner did not have any children, write *none* in this field. Be sure to list the name of each child's parent from section one under Parent's Name. Next, list the birthdate and date of death for each child, if known. In section 3, list all of the grandchildren of the deceased owner, whether living or deceased. Be sure to include their parent's name in this section under Parent's Name. Next, list the birthdate and date of death for each grandchild, if known. Section 4 list the name of the parents of the deceased owner, including their date of birth and their date of death, if applicable. In section 5 you will list the names of all siblings of the deceased owner, whether they are living or deceased. Next, list the birthdate and date of death for each sibling, if known. If the owner did not have any siblings, write *none*. In section 6, list the nieces and nephews of the deceased owner, whether living or deceased. Next, list the birthdate and date of death for each niece or nephew, if known. If the owner did not have any nieces or nephews, write *none*. For any of the previous sections on the Table of Heirship, if there is not enough room to list everyone you need to in any section, you can attach a second Table of Heirship Form to add the additional names. At the bottom of the form, print your name in the first box, and sign in the second box. In signing this form, you declare the information provided on this form is true under penalty of perjury. A notary is not required on this form. If you have any questions, please contact the State Controller's Office by phone at 800-992-4647. Our Call Center is available 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific Time, Monday through Friday, excluding state holidays. The California State Controller's team is committed to helping rightful owners receive their property.
