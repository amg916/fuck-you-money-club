Controller Malia M. Cohen introduces ClaimIt.ca.gov and explains California Unclaimed Property Law. After three years of dormancy, covered property can include bank accounts, stocks, uncashed checks, insurance benefits, wages and safe deposit box contents. Real estate is excluded. Search by Last or Business Name, First Name and City or ZIP, or by Property ID. Active escheated properties have a +CLAIM button. Pre-escheat Notice properties have an INFO button showing holder contact information and a June 1 transfer deadline; an owner can contact the holder directly before transfer. Add multiple properties to the Claim Cart. Select the Claimant Relationship: Myself; Business Officer/Dissolving/LLC/Sole Prop; Heir; Heir-Trust; Heir-Will; Heir-Court Appointed Rep; Heir-Final Decree Distribution; Holder Reimbursement; Government Agency; or Bankruptcy Trustee. Complete the three-step eClaim form. Step 1 collects contact information, SSN and date of birth. Step 2 is an electronic signature under penalty of perjury. Step 3 presents a claim summary with Claim ID and a confirmation email within 24 hours. Upload verification documents through Upload Claim Documentation, with up to five files per batch in PDF, JPG or JPEG format, each between 1 KB and 15 MB. Document types include ID, SSN, Proof of Reported Address, Death Certificate, Trust/Will/Probate Decree and Investigator Agreement. Check status at ClaimIt.ca.gov or call 1-800-992-4647.
